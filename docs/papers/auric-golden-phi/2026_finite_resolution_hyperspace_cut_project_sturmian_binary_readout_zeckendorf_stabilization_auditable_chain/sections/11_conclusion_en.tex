\section{Conclusion}

This paper provides an auditable unified chain: the six-dimensional cut-and-project scheme provides the ontological model of quasiperiodic structure; internal phase parameterizes the observer; the one-dimensional factor of torus rotation provides the implementable sampling mechanism under finite resources; binary partition readout generates Sturmian mechanical words providing minimal complexity invariants; the Zeckendorf stabilization kernel compresses raw binary readout to the golden-mean legal language; time is rigorously formalized as the stepwise refinement of stabilization-kernel preimage fibers under observation history, with information rate given by metric entropy. Observer difference manifests significantly at the local patch layer, while under unique-ergodic conditions the statistical laws can be recovered independent of phase. The appendices further provide multiscale, visibility projection, and effective dimension frameworks, as well as interfaces between symbol dynamics $\zeta$ generating functions and arithmetic structure, providing explicit verifiable entry points for future connection of this protocol with broader arithmetic/spectral structures.
