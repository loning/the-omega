\begin{conclusion}[\texorpdfstring{$(4)$}{(4)}-division weight image: \(y\)-values of the critical set governed by an irreducible degree-12 polynomial]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-4division-weight-image}
In the same elliptic-curve model
\[
E:\ Y^{2}=X^{3}-X+\frac14,
\qquad
y:=X^{2}+Y-\frac12
\]
and its induced quartic spectral plane curve
\[
\Pi(X,y):=X^{4}-X^{3}-(2y+1)X^{2}+X+y(y+1)=0
\]
set
\[
N_{6}(X):=2X^{6}-10X^{4}+10X^{3}-10X^{2}+2X+1
\]
(its zeros give \(x\bigl(E[4]\setminus E[2]\bigr)\), equivalently the \(X\)-coordinate representation of the \(4\)-division certificate polynomial \(\psi_{4}/(2Y)\) in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-lattes-4division-certificate}).
Define
\[
Q_{4}(y):=\operatorname{Res}_{X}\bigl(N_{6}(X),\Pi(X,y)\bigr)\in\ZZ[y].
\]
Then \(Q_{4}\) is an irreducible degree-12 polynomial with integer coefficients, and
\[
y\bigl(E[4]\setminus E[2]\bigr)=\{\,u\in\overline{\QQ}:\ Q_{4}(u)=0\,\}.
\]

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Since \(P\in E[4]\setminus E[2]\) iff \([2]P\in E[2]\setminus\{O\}\), its \(X\)-coordinate satisfies the sextic \(4\)-division certificate \(N_{6}(X)=0\).
On the other hand, eliminating \(Y\) from \(y=X^{2}+Y-\tfrac12\) gives \(\Pi(X,y)=0\) (Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-normalization}).
Therefore \(y(E[4]\setminus E[2])\) is exactly the parametric image after eliminating \(X\), and the resultant \(\operatorname{Res}_{X}(N_{6},\Pi)\) provides its minimal integer-coefficient elimination certificate.
Irreducibility and discriminant factorization can be verified by \texttt{scripts/exp\_fold\_zm\_elliptic\_4division\_weight\_image\_audit.py}. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}
