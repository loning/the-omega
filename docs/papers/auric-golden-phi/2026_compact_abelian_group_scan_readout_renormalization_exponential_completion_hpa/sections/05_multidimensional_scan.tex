\section{Multidimensional Joint Scan: Degeneration Mechanism and Non-Resonant Construction}

\subsection{Diagonal Degeneration}

On $\TT^d$, if we choose synchronous equal increments $\alpha=(\delta,\dots,\delta)$, the orbit lies on the diagonal subtorus
\[
\{x\in\TT^d: x_1-x_2=\cdots=x_{d-1}-x_d\}
\]
and cannot be jointly dense. This degeneration is a structural obstruction that must be explicitly corrected in multidimensional extensions.

\subsection{Kronecker--Weyl Condition}

\begin{theorem}[Kronecker--Weyl joint equidistribution]\label{thm:kronecker-weyl}
If $(1,\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_d)$ is linearly independent over $\QQ$, then the orbit $x_n=x_0+n\alpha$ is jointly equidistributed on $\TT^d$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{A Realizable Class of \texorpdfstring{$\varphi$}{varphi}-anchored Non-Resonant Vectors}

To provide a verifiable construction, choose pairwise distinct square-free integers $p_1,\dots,p_d$, and set
\[
\beta_i=\sqrt{p_i},\qquad \alpha_i=\varphi\,\beta_i.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Rational linear independence of square-root families]\label{lem:sqrt-independence}
If $p_1,\dots,p_d$ are pairwise distinct square-free positive integers, then $(1,\sqrt{p_1},\dots,\sqrt{p_d})$ is linearly independent over $\QQ$.
\end{lemma}
Hence $(1,\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_d)$ is also linearly independent and satisfies Theorem \ref{thm:kronecker-weyl}. The proof is given in Appendix \ref{app:multidim-indep}.

This construction realizes a ``$\varphi$-anchored'' cross-dimensional unified scale: multiplying by the global factor $\varphi$ does not alter linear independence, while it keeps parameter families coherent under resolution scaling.
