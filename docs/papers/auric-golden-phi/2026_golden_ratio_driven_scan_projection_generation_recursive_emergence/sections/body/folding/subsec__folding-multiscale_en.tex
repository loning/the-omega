\subsection{Multiscale Consistency: Restriction Inverse System and Compatibility Caliber}\label{subsec:folding-multiscale}

When comparing outputs across different window lengths $m$, ``consistency'' should be precisely defined as cross-scale compatibility: after projecting a high-resolution output to lower resolution via a natural truncation (restriction), it should reproduce the content contained in the low-resolution output. Below we give the standard construction at the object level (complete proof in Appendix G).

\subsubsection{Core Theorems of Folding and Factorization (Summary Statement)}\label{subsec:fold-theorem-suite}
To unify the auditability requirements of the folding interface, this subsection summarizes the key structures induced by folding into a single summary theorem: it provides the precise definition of folding, reduction-order independence (termination/confluence), idempotence/surjectivity, multiscale restriction inverse system compatibility, the sequence-level sofic factor and minimal right-resolving cover, and the quantitative chain from discrepancy certificates to the folding histogram TV deviation.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-Window Folding: Confluence, Inverse System Compatibility, Sofic Factor, and Quantitative Certificates]\label{thm:fold-suite}
Let $\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m$, $X_m=\{w\in\{0,1\}^m:\text{no subword }11\}$, and let $\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m$ be as in Definition \ref{def:fold-word}. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\normalfont
  \item \textbf{(Reduction-order-independent normalization)} Folding can be realized by a value-preserving local rewriting system that is strongly terminating and confluent; therefore the implementation of $\Fold_m$ is independent of the reduction order.
  \item \textbf{(Idempotence and surjectivity)} \(\Fold_m(\omega)\in X_m\); moreover \(\Fold_m|_{X_m}=\mathrm{id}\) and \(\Fold_m:\Omega_m\twoheadrightarrow X_m\).
  \item \textbf{(Restriction inverse system and inverse limit)} For any $m_2\ge m_1$, the truncation $\pi_{m_2\to m_1}:X_{m_2}\to X_{m_1}$ forms a restriction inverse system, and there exists a natural isomorphism \(X_\infty\cong\varprojlim X_m\) (Theorem \ref{thm:inverse-limit-golden}).
\item \textbf{(Cross-resolution compatible commutative diagram)} Direct microstate truncation generally does not commute with folding; however, the fold-aware restriction \(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)=\pi_{m+1\to m}(\Fold_{m+1}(\omega))\) of Definition \ref{subsec:fold-omega-restriction} makes the commutative diagram hold (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-omega-commute}).
  \item \textbf{(Sequence-level factor and explicit sofic image)} The map \(\Phi_m\) induced by \(\Fold_m\) along sliding windows is a sliding block code with memory $0$ and anticipation $m-1$ (Proposition \ref{prop:Phi_m-radius}); its image space \(Y_m\subset X_m^{\ZZ}\) is a sofic subshift given by the explicit labeled graph \(G_m\) (Theorem \ref{thm:Phi_m-sofic-graph}).
  \item \textbf{(Minimal right-resolving cover)} For \(Y_m\), a right-resolving representation can be obtained by subset determinization (Proposition \ref{prop:Phi_m-right-resolving}); further minimization by follower sets yields the right Fischer cover, whose minimality can be found in \cite{LindMarcus1995,Kitchens1998SymbolicDynamics}.
  \item \textbf{(Quantitative certificate: discrepancy \(\Rightarrow\) folding histogram TV deviation)} Under the rotational scan (Sturmian) model, for any $N\ge 1$,
  \[
  D_{\mathrm{TV}}(\hat{\pi}_{m,N},\pi_m^{\mathrm{fold}})\ \le\ (m+1)\,D_N^*(\alpha;x_0),
  \]
  and the error decomposition to the Parry baseline satisfies Corollary \ref{cor:tv-decomposition-parry}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(1) By Proposition \ref{prop:fold-rewrite-newman} (strong termination + confluence) and Corollary \ref{cor:foldm-order-indep}.

(2) By Proposition \ref{prop:fold-basic}.

(3) Restriction consistency follows from Lemma \ref{lem:pi-functorial-golden}; the inverse limit isomorphism from Theorem \ref{thm:inverse-limit-golden} (complete proof in Appendix G, Theorem \ref{app:multiscale-inverse-limit}).

(4) By the definition in Section \ref{subsec:fold-omega-restriction} and Proposition \ref{prop:fold-omega-commute}.

(5) The radius follows from Proposition \ref{prop:Phi_m-radius}; the sofic image and explicit labeled graph from Theorem \ref{thm:Phi_m-sofic-graph}.

(6) By Proposition \ref{prop:Phi_m-right-resolving} and the minimality of the right Fischer cover (see \cite{LindMarcus1995,Kitchens1998SymbolicDynamics}).

(7) By Theorem \ref{thm:tv-certificate-hist} in Section \ref{sec:experiments} (whose proof gives the explicit coefficient $m+1$) and Corollary \ref{cor:tv-decomposition-parry} for the Parry baseline error decomposition.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Scale Projection (Restriction)]\label{def:pi-m2-m1-golden}
For any $m_2\ge m_1$, define the truncation projection
\[
\pi_{m_2\to m_1}:X_{m_2}\to X_{m_1},\qquad
\pi_{m_2\to m_1}(x_1,\dots,x_{m_2})=(x_1,\dots,x_{m_1}).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Consistency]\label{lem:pi-functorial-golden}
For any $m_3\ge m_2\ge m_1$,
\[
\pi_{m_3\to m_1}=\pi_{m_2\to m_1}\circ \pi_{m_3\to m_2}.
\]
Moreover, for any infinite legal sequence $c=(c_1,c_2,\dots)$,
\[
\pi_{m_2\to m_1}\bigl(\pi_{m_2}(c)\bigr)=\pi_{m_1}(c).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}[Inverse Limit Isomorphism (Resolution-Independent Object)]\label{thm:inverse-limit-golden}
Let
\[
X_\infty:=\{(c_1,c_2,\dots)\in\{0,1\}^{\mathbb{N}}:\ c_kc_{k+1}=0\ \forall k\ge 1\}.
\]
Then there exists a natural isomorphism
\[
X_\infty\ \cong\ \varprojlim X_m,
\]
and $X_m$ can be viewed as a finite truncation factor of $X_\infty$: the difference across different $m$ lies only in the ``visible prefix length,'' not in the unified object itself.
\end{theorem}

\subsubsection{On Compatibility of Microstate Truncation and Folding: The Correct Commutative Diagram}\label{subsec:fold-omega-restriction}
The above inverse system structure occurs on the stable syntax sets $\{X_m\}$, with the restriction maps being the truncations $\pi_{m_2\to m_1}:X_{m_2}\to X_{m_1}$ of Definition \ref{def:pi-m2-m1-golden}. A question that needs clarification is: can one directly form a commutative diagram using ``drop the last bit'' truncation on the microstate space $\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m$ together with the fold $\Fold_m$?

\paragraph{Direct Truncation Generally Does Not Commute with Folding}
Let
\[
r_{m+1\to m}:\Omega_{m+1}\to\Omega_m,\qquad
r_{m+1\to m}(\omega_1\cdots\omega_m\omega_{m+1}):=\omega_1\cdots\omega_m
\]
be the direct truncation on microstates. In general,
\[
\Fold_m\circ r_{m+1\to m}\ \neq\ \pi_{m+1\to m}\circ \Fold_{m+1}.
\]
For example, take $m=1$ and $\omega=11$: then $r_{2\to 1}(11)=1$, so $(\Fold_1\circ r_{2\to 1})(11)=\Fold_1(1)=1$; on the other hand, $N(11)=F_2+F_3=3$ has Zeckendorf normal form $3=F_4$, so $\Fold_2(11)=00$, whence $(\pi_{2\to 1}\circ \Fold_2)(11)=0$.

\paragraph{Fold-Aware Restriction (Achieving Commutativity on $\Omega$)}
To obtain a strictly commutative formalization at the microstate level, we define a restriction compatible with folding:
\[
\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}:\Omega_{m+1}\to \Omega_m,\qquad
\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega):=\pi_{m+1\to m}\bigl(\Fold_{m+1}(\omega)\bigr).
\]
Note that $\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\in X_m\subset\Omega_m$, so this definition indeed gives a map $\Omega_{m+1}\to\Omega_m$ (it is not the ``raw truncation'' but an auditable coarsening via ``fold first, then truncate'').

\begin{proposition}[Cross-Resolution Compatible Commutative Diagram]\label{prop:fold-omega-commute}
For any $m\ge 1$, the following diagram commutes:
\[
\begin{CD}
\Omega_{m+1} @>{\Fold_{m+1}}>> X_{m+1}\\
@V{\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}}VV @VV{\pi_{m+1\to m}}V\\
\Omega_m @>{\Fold_m}>> X_m.
\end{CD}
\]
That is, for any $\omega\in\Omega_{m+1}$,
\[
\Fold_m\bigl(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\bigr)
\ =\
\pi_{m+1\to m}\bigl(\Fold_{m+1}(\omega)\bigr).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By definition, $\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)=\pi_{m+1\to m}(\Fold_{m+1}(\omega))\in X_m$. The idempotence of Proposition \ref{prop:fold-basic} gives: for any $x\in X_m$, $\Fold_m(x)=x$. Therefore
\[
\Fold_m\bigl(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\bigr)
=\Fold_m\!\left(\pi_{m+1\to m}(\Fold_{m+1}(\omega))\right)
=\pi_{m+1\to m}(\Fold_{m+1}(\omega)).
\]
\qed
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{Gauge Anomaly: Worst-Case Global Deviation Between Direct Truncation and Fold-Aware Restriction}\label{subsec:fold-gauge-anomaly}
\paragraph{Minimal Correction Between Two Resolution-Lowering Outputs}
At the microstate level, we distinguished the direct truncation \(r_{m+1\to m}\) and the fold-aware restriction \(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}\) (Section \ref{subsec:fold-omega-restriction}).
Below, their discrepancy is quantified as a computable scalar in purely combinatorial terms.

\begin{definition}[Gauge Anomaly \texorpdfstring{$G_m(\omega)$}{Gm(omega)}]\label{def:fold-gauge-anomaly}
For \(m\ge 1\) and any \(\omega\in\Omega_{m+1}\), define the length-\(m\) gauge anomaly
$$
G_m(\omega)
:=\bigl|\,r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\ \oplus\ \widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\,\bigr|_0,
$$
where \(\oplus\) is bitwise XOR (modulo-$2$ addition on \(\{0,1\}^m\)) and \(|\cdot|_0\) is the Hamming weight (count of nonzero positions).
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Semantics: Lower Bound on ``Temporal Projection'' Under Resolution Lowering]
\(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}\) is by definition the coarsening ``fold first, then truncate,'' while \(r_{m+1\to m}\) is the direct dimension reduction ``discard the last bit.''
The quantity \(G_m(\omega)\) precisely answers: if starting from the direct truncation \(r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\) and wishing to recover the low-resolution output consistent with folding \(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\), how many output bits must be changed at minimum (in the Hamming sense)?
It is therefore an auditable temporal projection certificate in the ``upscale--project'' language: it measures the support size of the output touched by resolution lowering, rather than the delay length of the local causal cone.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Worst-Case Gauge Anomaly Is Full-Length: Existence of Global-Flip Inputs]\label{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-max}
For any \(m\ge 1\), there exists \(\omega^\star(m)\in\Omega_{m+1}\) such that
$$
G_m\bigl(\omega^\star(m)\bigr)=m.
$$
In other words, the direct truncation and the fold-aware restriction differ \emph{at every position} in the first \(m\) bits under the worst-case input.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By definition, \(0\le G_m(\omega)\le m\).
We construct an \(\omega^\star(m)\) achieving the upper bound.
\par
Let the first \(m\) bits of \(\omega^\star=\omega^\star(m)\in\Omega_{m+1}\) follow the periodic pattern \(110\):
$$
\omega^\star_k=
\begin{cases}
1,& k\equiv 1,2\pmod 3,\ 1\le k\le m,\\
0,& k\equiv 0\pmod 3,\ 1\le k\le m,
\end{cases}
$$
and set the last bit as
$$
\omega^\star_{m+1}:=\mathbf{1}_{\{m\equiv 1\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 3)\}}.
$$
Then \(r_{m+1\to m}(\omega^\star)\) is the length-\(m\) prefix of \(110110\cdots\).
\par
Embed \(\omega^\star\) into the configuration space \(a:=\iota_{m+1}(\omega^\star)\in\mathcal D\) (Definition \ref{def:fold-word}).
For each \(j\ge 0\) with \(3j+1\le m\), we have \(a_{3j+1}=a_{3j+2}=1\), so the adjacent merging rule (\ref{rule:fold:adj}) can be applied simultaneously at these pairwise disjoint positions (see Definition \ref{def:fold-word}):
$$
a\ \to\ a':=a-\sum_{3j+1\le m}\bigl(e_{3j+1}+e_{3j+2}\bigr)+\sum_{3j+1\le m}e_{3j+3}.
$$
By the Fibonacci identity \(F_{k+1}+F_{k+2}=F_{k+3}\), each step preserves \(\mathrm{Val}\), so \(\mathrm{Val}(a')=\mathrm{Val}(a)=N(\omega^\star)\).
\par
By construction, for \(1\le k\le m\), \(a'_k\) is the length-\(m\) prefix of the periodic pattern \(001001\cdots\); this prefix contains no adjacent \(11\), so \(a'\) is an irreducible form (belonging to \(X_\infty\)).
By the confluence and Zeckendorf uniqueness of Proposition \ref{prop:fold-rewrite-newman}, \(a'\) must equal the normal form \(\mathrm{NF}(a)\).
Hence
$$
\Fold_{m+1}(\omega^\star)=\pi_{m+1}(\mathrm{NF}(a))=\pi_{m+1}(a'),
\qquad
\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega^\star)=\pi_{m+1\to m}(\Fold_{m+1}(\omega^\star))=\pi_m(a').
$$
Thus \(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega^\star)\) is the length-\(m\) prefix of \(001001\cdots\).
The bitwise XOR of the two gives the all-ones string, so \(G_m(\omega^\star)=m\). \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Any ``Modify Only a Constant Number of Tail Bits'' Resolution-Lowering Patch Is Incomplete]\label{cor:fold-tail-patch-incomplete}
Fix a constant \(R\ge 0\). Suppose an alternative map \(T_{m,R}:\Omega_{m+1}\to\Omega_m\) satisfies: for any \(\omega\in\Omega_{m+1}\), the string \(T_{m,R}(\omega)\) can differ from the direct truncation \(r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\) only in the last \(R\) coordinates (i.e., at most the last \(R\) bits are modified).
Then for all \(m>R\), there exists \(\omega\in\Omega_{m+1}\) such that \(T_{m,R}(\omega)\ne \widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Take the \(\omega^\star(m)\) of Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-max}.
Since \(r_{m+1\to m}(\omega^\star)\) and \(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega^\star)\) differ at every position in the first \(m\) bits, and \(T_{m,R}\) can modify at most the last \(R\) bits, \(T_{m,R}(\omega^\star)\) differs from \(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega^\star)\) in at least the first \(m-R\) positions, so they cannot be equal. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Finite Tail Patch Incompleteness and the Non-Locally-Eliminable \texorpdfstring{$\mathsf{NULL}$}{NULL} Paradigm]\label{rem:fold-tail-patch-null-nonlocal}
Corollary \ref{cor:fold-tail-patch-incomplete} shows that in cross-resolution lowering, the fold-consistent restriction \(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}\) cannot be recovered by a ``direct truncation \(r_{m+1\to m}\) + modify only a constant number of tail bits'' protocol.
More precisely, the gauge anomaly \(G_m(\omega)\) (Definition \ref{def:fold-gauge-anomaly}) gives the minimum Hamming correction size from \(r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\) to \(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}(\omega)\); when only the last \(R\) bits are allowed to be touched, \(m>R\) guarantees the existence of inputs for which this correction \emph{necessarily} overflows, thereby conflicting with the fold compatibility checker (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-omega-commute}).
\par
Under the ``address precedes concept value'' semantic convention (see Section \ref{sec:spg}), this gives a strict non-locally-patchable paradigm:
if one refuses to introduce sufficient orthogonal recording axes (e.g., externalizing the non-local information needed for resolution lowering as a residual ledger) while simultaneously imposing ``fold consistency/value preservation'' as a hard audit semantic constraint, then any implementation allowing only finite tail patches will lose addressability on certain input families;
by the conventions of this paper, such ``addressless/undefined readout'' is equivalently treated as \(\mathsf{NULL}\).
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Multi-Step Resolution Lowering: Gauge Anomaly Density Invariance]\label{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-kstep-invariance}
Fix an integer \(k\ge 1\).
For any \(\omega\in\Omega_{m+k}=\{0,1\}^{m+k}\), define the multi-step direct truncation and fold-aware restriction
\[
r_{m+k\to m}(\omega):=\omega_{1}\cdots\omega_{m},
\qquad
\widetilde r_{m+k\to m}(\omega):=\pi_{m+k\to m}\bigl(\Fold_{m+k}(\omega)\bigr)\in X_m,
\]
and define the \(k\)-step gauge anomaly
\[
G_m^{(k)}(\omega):=\bigl|\,r_{m+k\to m}(\omega)\ \oplus\ \widetilde r_{m+k\to m}(\omega)\,\bigr|_0,
\]
where \(\oplus\) is bitwise XOR and \(|\cdot|_0\) is the Hamming weight.
Under the uniform baseline \(\omega\sim\mathrm{Unif}(\Omega_{m+k})\),
\[
\boxed{\ \lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}\,\mathbb E\!\left[G_m^{(k)}\right]=\frac49\ },
\qquad
\boxed{\ \frac{G_m^{(k)}-\frac49 m}{\sqrt m}\Rightarrow \mathcal N\!\left(0,\frac{118}{243}\right)\ }.
\]
Moreover, for any \(\theta\in\RR\), its per-step log moment generating function limit equals that of the \(k=1\) case: there exists a pressure function \(P_G(\theta)\) independent of \(k\) such that
\[
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}\log\mathbb E\exp\!\bigl(\theta\,G_m^{(k)}\bigr)=P_G(\theta)=\log\rho(A_\theta),
\]
where \(A_\theta\) is the same \(4\times 4\) weighted adjacency matrix of Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure}.
Therefore, by Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-ldp}, the large deviation rate function of \(G_m^{(k)}/m\) has endpoint closed forms \(I(0)=\log(2/\varphi), I(1)=\log 2\) that also do not change with \(k\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch (structural)]
The fold \(\Fold_\infty\) admits a finite-state online implementation: under IID Bernoulli\((1/2)\) input, the output and per-bit mismatch indicator can be represented as an additive observable on a primitive finite-state Markov chain (proof sketches of Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-clt} and Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure}).
For fixed \(k\), the finite word \(\omega\in\Omega_{m+k}\) only changes the right boundary condition of this two-sided synchronization implementation; by primitivity of the chain, the right boundary condition affects interior-point statistics with exponentially decaying influence, so its impact on the sum \(G_m^{(k)}\) in terms of expectation, variance, and exponential moments is only an \(O(1)\) boundary correction.
Dividing this boundary correction by \(m\) (or normalizing by \(\sqrt m\)) makes it vanish, so the density limit, CLT constant, and pressure function \(P_G\) (and hence the LDP rate function) all agree with the \(k=1\) case. \qed
\end{proof}

\end{remark}
