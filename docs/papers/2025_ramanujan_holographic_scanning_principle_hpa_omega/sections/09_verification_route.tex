\section{Auditable intermediate claims and a verification route}
\label{sec:verification_route}

To keep the constitution auditable, we restrict ourselves to intermediate claims that are mathematically standard and numerically verifiable without any new experimental apparatus. The goal is not to prove a physical ontology, but to demonstrate that the proposed dependency chain closes \emph{internally} on canonical arithmetic objects.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Golden rotation window readout $\Rightarrow$ Fibonacci/Sturmian words and discrepancy scaling.}
  We verify that a golden-branch irrational rotation with an interval window produces a Sturmian word shift-equivalent to the Fibonacci word, and we compute basic uniformity diagnostics and star discrepancy behavior.

  \item \textbf{Discriminant form $\Delta$ $\Rightarrow$ Ramanujan $\tau(n)$ obeys Hecke multiplicativity and prime-power recursion.}
  We compute $\tau(n)$ from the Euler product definition of $\Delta(q)$ and verify (i) multiplicativity on coprime indices and (ii) the prime-power recursion induced by the Hecke algebra.

  \item \textbf{$j$-invariant $\Rightarrow$ numerical $S$-invariance.}
  Using $q$-series for Eisenstein series $E_4,E_6$, we evaluate $j(\tau)$ and check $j(\tau)\approx j(-1/\tau)$ at representative points in $\HH$.
\end{enumerate}

Reference implementations (standard library only) are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:repro_code}.
