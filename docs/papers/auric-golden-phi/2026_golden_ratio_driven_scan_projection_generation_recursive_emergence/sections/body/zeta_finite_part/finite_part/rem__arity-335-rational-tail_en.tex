\begin{conjecture}[Coboundary defect $\Rightarrow$ spectral gap (quantitative GRH certificate)]\label{conj:coboundary-defect-gap}

\begin{remark}[Proof roadmap: from energy functionals to discrete Poincar\'e inequalities]\label{rem:coboundary-defect-proof}
