\section{Reflectors and Induction Commutation: Factor Systems and Categorical Template}

\subsection{Kronecker Reflector and Idempotent Comonads}

Let $\mathbf{Erg}$ be the category of ergodic measure-preserving systems and factor maps. Let $\mathbf{Kron}\subset\mathbf{Erg}$ be the full subcategory of systems with discrete spectrum (equivalently, rotations of compact abelian groups). The Kronecker factor gives a reflector
\[
K:\mathbf{Erg}\to \mathbf{Kron},
\]
satisfying idempotence
\[
K\circ K\simeq K.
\]
Universality is expressed: for any $(X,T)\in\mathbf{Erg}$ and $(Y,S)\in\mathbf{Kron}$, any factor map $X\to Y$ factors uniquely through $X\to K(X)$.

\subsection{Windowed Category and Induction Functor}

Define the windowed category $\mathbf{Erg}^{\ast}$: objects are triples $(X,T,E)$ where $E\subset X$ is measurable with $\mu(E)>0$. A morphism $(X,T,E)\to(Y,S,F)$ is a factor map $\pi:X\to Y$ satisfying the window pullback condition
\[
E=\pi^{-1}(F).
\]
On this category we define the induction functor
\[
\mathrm{Ind}:\mathbf{Erg}^{\ast}\to\mathbf{Erg},\qquad (X,T,E)\mapsto(E,T_E).
\]
The window pullback condition ensures that the pushforward of return times and the induced factor structure naturally exist.

\subsection{Commutation Theorem Template: Base System Closure and Fiber Stability Decomposition}

Let $\pi:X\to K(X)$ be the Kronecker factor map, and take $F\subset K(X)$ with $E=\pi^{-1}(F)$. Then induction yields a natural factor map
\[
\pi_E:\mathrm{Ind}(X,E)\to \mathrm{Ind}(K(X),F).
\]
To compare $K(\mathrm{Ind}(X,E))$ with $\mathrm{Ind}(K(X),F)$, we introduce two types of conditions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Base System Closure:} $\mathrm{Ind}(K(X),F)\in\mathbf{Kron}$.
  \item \textbf{Fiber Stability:} The non-isometric part of the extension $X\to K(X)$ does not produce new isometric factors under induction (ensured by relative weak mixing and other randomness conditions, see Appendix \ref{app:relative-product}).
\end{itemize}

\begin{theorem}[Reflector--Induction Commutation Template]\label{thm:reflector-induction-template}
Let $(X,T)\in\mathbf{Erg}$, and let $\pi:X\to K(X)$ be the Kronecker factor map. Take $F\subset K(X)$ and $E=\pi^{-1}(F)$. If
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $\mathrm{Ind}(K(X),F)\in\mathbf{Kron}$;
  \item The extension $X\to K(X)$ preserves relative randomness under induction, so it does not produce new non-trivial isometric factors;
\end{enumerate}
then there exists a measure isomorphism
\[
K(\mathrm{Ind}(X,E))\cong \mathrm{Ind}(K(X),F).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof Outline]
By condition 2, the Kronecker factor of $\mathrm{Ind}(X,E)\to \mathrm{Ind}(K(X),F)$ is controlled by the base system; by condition 1 the base system itself lies in the reflector class $\mathbf{Kron}$, so $K(\mathrm{Ind}(X,E))$ must align with $\mathrm{Ind}(K(X),F)$. A rigorous proof uses relative direct-product ergodicity and induction commutation (Appendix \ref{app:relative-product}). Proven.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Characterization of Inducible Closure Windows in One-Dimensional Rotations}

In the one-dimensional rotation $(\TT,T_\theta)$, if $F=E$ is an interval, then Theorems \ref{thm:two-value-criterion}--\ref{thm:two-value-iet} give a computable necessary and sufficient criterion for base system closure:
\[
\mathrm{Ind}(T_\theta,E)\in\mathbf{Kron}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
r_E\ \text{two-valued}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
b=\delta_{i-1}-c\delta_i,\ 0\le c<a_{i+1}.
\]
Thus ``inducible closure windows'' in the rotation prototype have a completely arithmetizable classification.

\subsection{Higher-Order Generalization Problems}

Replacing $K$ with higher-order structure factors such as Host--Kra factors $Z_s$ leads to a generalization: ``induction commutes with higher-order characteristic factors''. The proof would require replacing relative weak mixing with higher-order relative randomness, and controlling the induction behavior of cubic systems under the window pullback condition. This direction constitutes future work.
