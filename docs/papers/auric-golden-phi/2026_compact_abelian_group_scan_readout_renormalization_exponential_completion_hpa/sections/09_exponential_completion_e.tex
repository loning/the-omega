\section{Exponential Completion and the Structural Appearance of \texorpdfstring{$e$}{e}}

\subsection{Discrete Iteration and Continuous Flows}

Let $G$ be a Lie group and $A$ an element of its Lie algebra. The continuous flow is generated by
\[
U(t)=\exp(tA).
\]
The discrete scan operator $U=\exp(A)$ satisfies $U^n=\exp(nA)$.

\subsection{Euler Limit}

The exponential map admits the Euler-limit representation
\[
\exp(A)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(I+\frac{A}{n}\right)^n.
\]
Therefore, the constant $e$ is not an externally imposed parameter; it is fixed by the normalization structure of ``discrete increments $\to$ continuous completion.''
