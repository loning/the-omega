\section{Symbolic Readout and Renormalization Closure: Sturmian, Fibonacci, and Frequency Consistency}

\subsection{Sturmian Property of Rotation Coding}

Let $\alpha\in(0,1)\setminus\QQ$, and choose the partition $W=[0,\alpha)$. The readout $u_n=\ind_W(x_0+n\alpha)$ is Sturmian and has minimal complexity
\[
p(n)=n+1.
\]
This property makes ``minimal alphabet + minimal complexity'' a canonical principle at the readout layer.

\subsection{Fibonacci Special Case and the Perron--Frobenius Origin of \texorpdfstring{$\varphi$}{varphi}}

When $\alpha=\varphi^{-1}$, the Sturmian sequence is in the same equivalence class as the Fibonacci substitution fixed point. The substitution matrix
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1\\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix}
\]
has dominant eigenvalue $\varphi$, and its Perron vector yields symbol frequencies.
On the other hand, unique ergodicity guarantees that window measure yields symbol frequencies.
Therefore we obtain a closed consistency loop:
\[
\text{window-measure frequency}\ \equiv\ \text{substitution fixed-point frequency}\ \equiv\ \text{PF dominant eigenvector}.
\]

\subsection{A Testable Axiom Form for Golden Weights}

At the readout level one may state a binary-weight axiom:
\[
\mathbb{P}(E=1)=\frac{1}{\varphi},\qquad \mathbb{P}(E=0)=\frac{1}{\varphi^2},
\]
with normalization guaranteed by $\varphi^2=\varphi+1$. This statement is not presented as a theorem; it is falsifiable via window-readout frequencies and symbolic statistics (see Section 11 and the sparse-subsequence theorem in Appendix \ref{app:sparse-gating}).
