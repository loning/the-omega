\subsection{Higgs--\texorpdfstring{$Z$}{Z} depth-offset rigidity at bounded denominator}

\begin{proposition}[Higgs--$Z$ depth-offset rigidity at bounded denominator]\label{prop:higgs_z_offset_rigidity}
Let $m_Z$ and $m_H$ denote the PDG reference masses used elsewhere in this paper (Table~\ref{tab:mass_spectrum}).
Define the resolution-depth offset
\[
\Delta r_{HZ}:=\log_\varphi\!\left(\frac{m_H}{m_Z}\right).
\]
Within the bounded candidate family of reduced rationals $\Delta r=p/q$ with $1\le q\le 20$ and $0\le p\le q$, selecting the unique minimizer of the absolute log mismatch
\[
e(p/q):=\left|\log\!\left(\frac{m_Z\,\varphi^{p/q}}{m_H}\right)\right|
\]
by deterministic tie-break rules yields the stabilized minimizer $\Delta r=2/3$ (Table~\ref{tab:higgs_z_offset_sweep}).
Concretely, we minimize $e$ and then break ties by choosing the smallest denominator $q$ and then the smallest numerator $p$ (equivalently: lexicographic minimization of $(e,q,p)$), matching the generator script \texttt{scripts/exp\_higgs\_z\_offset\_rigidity.py}.
Equivalently, the Higgs scale is captured at low complexity by the closed relation
\[
m_H\approx m_Z\,\varphi^{2/3}.
\]
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Uplift microtexture at \texorpdfstring{$m=10$}{m=10} and a Higgs-doublet representative (2D/3D Hilbert screens)}
\label{subsec:higgs_doublet_uplift_geometry}

\paragraph{Scope and status.}
\AuditTag This subsection records a deterministic, geometry-first certificate for the scalar-sector uplift narrative at $m=10$ used in the main text (Section~\ref{subsec:scalar_sectors_interface}).
It constructs protocol-local parity-even scalar observables from the uplift microtexture and bundles them into a canonical complex doublet representative, displayed on both a balanced $2$D Hilbert screen and a $3$D Hilbert screen.
The purpose is to provide an auditable, machine-checkable representative at the interface layer; it is not used as a premise for the theorem-level folding core.

\paragraph{Protocol objects.}
\AuditTag Fix $m=10$ and, for each scan index $k\in\{0,\dots,2^{10}-1\}$, write
$w=\mathrm{Fold}_{10}(k)\in X_{10}$.
On a chosen Hilbert screen (2D or 3D), each $k$ occupies a site $x$ under the corresponding addressing map, and we consider the suffix bits
$w_{7..10}$ as the uplift microtexture beyond the $X_6$ prefix label.
Define four site fields $b_j(x)=w_{6+j}(x)\in\{0,1\}$ and coarse-grain them by fixed block averaging on the screen:
in 2D we use $4\times 4$ blocks on the balanced $(m,n)=(10,5)$ screen; in 3D we use $2\times 2\times 2$ blocks on the $(m,n_3)=(10,4)$ cube, averaging over occupied sites in each block.

\paragraph{Deterministic doublet bundling.}
\AuditTag The generator selects a canonical bundling of the four coarse-grained scalar channels into a complex doublet by a fixed deterministic rule:
it contrasts the parity-even coarse scalar channels against a parity-odd traversal-turn sign field on the same screen and selects the pairing that minimizes a declared correlation-based objective (no free parameters).
For the present $m=10$ instance, the selected representative is
\[
H_1=\overline b_0+\iu\,\overline b_3,
\qquad
H_2=\overline b_1+\iu\,\overline b_2,
\]
agreeing between the 2D and 3D displays (Figures~\ref{fig:higgs_doublet_structure_m10_2d} and \ref{fig:higgs_doublet_structure_m10_3d}).
