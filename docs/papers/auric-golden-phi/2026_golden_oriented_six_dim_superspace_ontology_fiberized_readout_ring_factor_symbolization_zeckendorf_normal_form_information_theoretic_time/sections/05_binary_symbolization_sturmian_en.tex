\subsection{Sturmian Complexity Theorem}

The core property of Sturmian words can be stated as: a Sturmian infinite word on a binary alphabet has exactly $n+1$ distinct factors of length $n$ for each $n\ge 0$; binary encoding of irrational rotations is Sturmian; the Fibonacci word is a special case of Sturmian words \cite{CambridgeSturmianWordsLothaire}.
Based on this, define complexity
\[
p(n)=\card{\{\text{distinct factors of length }n\}},
\]
and use $p(n)=n+1$ as a verifiable invariant.
