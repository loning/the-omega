\subsection{Triangle audit: phase$\to$delay proxy vs linewidth proxy (audit)}
\label{app:scattering_delay_linewidth_triangle_audit}

This appendix records a small triangle-style consistency audit that relates three interface objects already present in the manuscript:
scattering phase data, a phase-derivative delay proxy, and a coarse linewidth proxy.
It is audit-only and does not claim a unique resonance model.

\paragraph{Input and conventions.}
\AuditTag We reuse the vendored registry \texttt{data/k4\_matching/scattering\_phase\_registry.json}.
For datasets that include a coarse resonance metadata entry $(E_0,\gamma)$, we compute a phase-derived delay proxy
$\tau_{\mathrm{phase}}(E_0)\approx 2\,\mathrm{d}\delta/\mathrm{d}E$ (bounded local-linear window family) and compare it to the linewidth proxy $\tau_\gamma\approx 4/\gamma$ as recorded in the registry notes.

\paragraph{Scope note.}
\AuditTag This audit only checks internal consistency of two transparent proxies under a bounded estimator family.
It does not fix the physical energy variable (matching-layer) nor does it assert a single-channel Breit--Wigner interpretation for the empirical datasets.

\paragraph{Match boundary (unit/variable dictionary).}
\AuditTag The registry contains heterogeneous abscissa conventions (e.g.\ $T_{\mathrm{lab}}$, $W_{\mathrm{cm}}$, invariant mass) and heterogeneous units (e.g.\ MeV versus a synthetic \texttt{proxy\_E} benchmark).
This triangle audit is formulated \emph{within} each dataset's declared coordinate and therefore does not require cross-dataset unit normalization.
However, any attempt to use these datasets to CAP-select a single toy carrier $S(\omega)$ across datasets would require an explicit matching-layer dictionary that maps the registry energy variable to the toy frequency variable (including unit normalization and kinematic conventions).
In the absence of such a declared Match input, the paper treats cross-unit aggregation as out of scope and restricts any CAP-selected toy carrier anchoring to the \texttt{proxy\_E} benchmark only.
