\begin{proposition}[Semantic correctness of the exponential law \texorpdfstring{$(\mathrm{RMOM})$}{RMOM} (fiber-product associativity)]\label{prop:pom-rmom-sound}
Under the \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) semantics of Definition \ref{def:pom-projword-2cat}, the rule
\[
\mathrm{MOM}_p\circ \mathrm{MOM}_q\ \Rightarrow\ \mathrm{MOM}_{pq}
\]
is strictly faithful in the underlying ``bitwise co-output'' fiber-product compilation framework: both sides compile to the same \(pq\)-fold collision kernel (the same counting language), and hence yield identical fingerprints for all readout targets (\(S_{pq}(m)\), Perron principal scale, and the pressure/finite part derived therefrom).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By the \(q\)-fold collision relation of Definition \ref{def:pom-moment-q}, the semantics of \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) compiles the ``\(q\) trajectories with bitwise co-output'' synchronization constraint onto a finite kernel (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-kernel-exists}).
For any length \(m\), first applying \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) then \(\mathrm{MOM}_p\) is equivalent to: partitioning the paths into \(p\) groups of \(q\) each, and requiring that the output words of these \(p\) groups coincide; this is precisely equivalent to requiring all \(pq\) trajectories to have bitwise co-output in a single step.
Therefore both sides count the same set
\[
R_m^{(pq)}=\Bigl\{(\omega^{(1)},\dots,\omega^{(pq)})\in\Omega_m^{pq}:\ \Fold_m(\omega^{(1)})=\cdots=\Fold_m(\omega^{(pq)})\Bigr\}
\]
and generate the same sequence \(S_{pq}(m)=|R_m^{(pq)}|\); the corresponding finite kernel realizations can be chosen to be isomorphic (differing only in realization choice), and hence the rule \((\mathrm{RMOM})\) holds semantically. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Lift--Moment Beck--Chevalley exchange law (group-axis replication)]\label{prop:pom-mom-lift-beck-chevalley}
For any finite group \(G\) and integer \(q\ge 2\), under the lift semantics where ``each copy carries one group coordinate'' via juxtaposition, there is a natural isomorphism (serving as a rewritable certificate):
\[
\boxed{\;
\mathrm{LIFT}_G\circ \mathrm{MOM}_q\ \simeq\ \mathrm{MOM}_q\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_{G^{\times q}}\; }.
\]
Equivalently, when extracting \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) as a left prefix, the lift branch of \((\mathrm{RMOM\text{-}BUBBLE})\) can be taken as
\[
\mathrm{LIFT}_G\circ \mathrm{MOM}_q\ \Rightarrow\ \mathrm{MOM}_q\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_{G^{\times q}}.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) extends the underlying state to ``original state \(\times\) group coordinate'', accumulating a cocycle on the group coordinate at each step (the group extension interface of Section \ref{sec:zeta-finite-part}).
When subsequently applying the \(q\)-fold synchronous product of \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\), the state space is naturally \((Q\times G)^q\cong Q^q\times G^q\), and the ``bitwise co-output'' screening constrains only the output symbols without coupling the group coordinates of different copies.
Therefore, this composite is equivalent to: first performing the \(q\)-fold synchronous product on the underlying layer to obtain \(Q^q\), then lifting the label axis to \(G^q\) in a single step (i.e., \(\mathrm{LIFT}_{G^{\times q}}\)), and finally applying the same output-consistency screening; this corresponds precisely to the semantics of the right-hand side \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_{G^{\times q}}\). \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Wreath product and unlabeled replication]\label{def:pom-wreath-product}
For any group \(G\) and integer \(q\ge 2\), define the wreath product
\[
G\wr S_q\ :=\ G^{\times q}\rtimes S_q,
\]
where \(S_q\) acts on \(G^{\times q}\) by permuting the \(q\) factors.
In the moment-kernel semantics of this paper, the ``symmetric power quotient kernel'' realization of \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-symmetric-power}) compresses the \(q\) copies from the \emph{labeled} direct product \(Q^q\) to the \emph{unlabeled} occupation vector space;
hence when the lift labels are also carried into this unlabeled replication, the correct label symmetry group necessarily includes the permutation symmetry \(S_q\), and thus naturally falls within \(G\wr S_q\).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Moment--Lift wreath exchange law (type-correct exchange object)]\label{prop:pom-mom-lift-wreath-exchange}\label{prop:pom-mom-lift-wreath}
For any finite group \(G\) and integer \(q\ge 2\), there exists a canonical isomorphism in the sense of natural isomorphisms of \(\mathbf{PW}\):
\[
\boxed{\;
\mathrm{MOM}_q\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_G
\ \simeq\
\mathrm{LIFT}_{G\wr S_q}\circ \mathrm{MOM}_q\; }.
\]
Intuitively: the ``unlabeled replication + permutation symmetry'' of \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) upgrades the \(q\) copies of the lift label axis from \(G^{\times q}\) to \(G^{\times q}\rtimes S_q\), i.e., the wreath product.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Regard \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) as the compilation operator for ``\(q\) parallel copies + output-consistency screening + symmetric power quotient compression'' (Definition \ref{def:pom-projword-2cat} and Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-symmetric-power}).

Therefore, the correct type for the lift is no longer the plain direct product \(G^{\times q}\), but its semidirect product with the permutation symmetry, \(G^{\times q}\rtimes S_q\), i.e., \(G\wr S_q\).
Equivalently, this yields the canonical natural isomorphism from the ``lift then moment'' compilation to the ``moment then lift (wreath product)'' compilation.
\end{proof}

(when needed)
(ii) if one chooses to apply \((\mathrm{RMOML})\) to the prefix \(\mathrm{MOM}_Q\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_{G'}\) for character-domain expansion, then the higher-order anomaly signature tower \(\mathsf{Anom}^{(Q)}_{G'}(K;\theta)\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-signature-tower}),
and (when needed)
(iii) the moment-anomaly label \(\mathsf{Anom}^{(Q)}_{\mathrm{mom}}(K;\theta)\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-moment-anom-signature}) from the \(E_m\)--\(\mathrm{MOM}\) extraction commutation.
Here \(Q\) is obtained by merging via \((\mathrm{RMOM})\), and \((G',u',m')\) are determined by three-generator normalization (with consistent updates to lift parameters according to the type promotion during extraction).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Merge all \(\mathrm{MOM}\) factors in the original word into a single \(\mathrm{MOM}_Q\) using \((\mathrm{RMOM})\), then use \((\mathrm{RMOM\text{-}BUBBLE})\) to swap it stepwise to the leftmost position, synchronously updating the necessary type parameters (e.g., the juxtaposition promotion of the lift axis).
The remaining fragment without \(\mathrm{MOM}\) can be reduced by Proposition \ref{prop:pom-three-gen-interface-normal-form} to the form \(\mathrm{LIFT}\circ\mathrm{PROJ}\circ E\), outputting the anomaly signature.
Concatenating both reduction segments yields the stated normal form and the accompanying residual outputs. \qed
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{Symmetric Power Compression: Compiling Moment-Kernels from Exponential to Polynomial State Spaces}\label{subsec:pom-moment-symmetric-power}

Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-kernel-exists} above provides the fiber-product construction for arbitrary \(q\)-fold collisions: directly taking the \(q\)-fold synchronous product produces an intermediate state space of exponential size (\(|Q|^q\), where \(Q\) is the state set of the underlying online kernel).
However, since the \(q\) copies are completely symmetric with respect to the counting objective (permuting the \(q\) trajectories does not alter the ``co-image'' constraint), one can take the quotient of the direct product kernel under the symmetric group action, thereby obtaining an equivalent counting kernel of \emph{polynomial size}.

\begin{proposition}[Symmetric power quotient kernel: polynomial-state realization of \texorpdfstring{$q$}{q}-fold collisions]\label{prop:pom-moment-symmetric-power}
Let \(\mathcal{T}\) be the deterministic online kernel realizing \(\Fold_\infty\) (in the framework of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-delay-min}), with finite internal state set \(Q\), reading one input symbol and outputting one stable address symbol at each step.
Fix \(q\ge 2\). After taking the synchronous direct product of \(q\) independent copies and applying the ``output-consistency'' screening, the resulting \(q\)-fold collision kernel (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-kernel-exists}) possesses a natural \(S_q\)-permutation symmetry at the state level: permuting the \(q\) copies does not alter reachability or the counting objective.

Let
$$
\mathcal{N}_{q}:=\Bigl\{(n_s)_{s\in Q}\in\ZZ_{\ge 0}^{Q}:\ \sum_{s\in Q}n_s=q\Bigr\}
$$
be the symmetric quotient space (``occupation number vectors''). Then there exists a nonnegative integer matrix \(\widetilde A_q\) (indexed by \(\mathcal{N}_q\)) and integer readout vectors \(\widetilde u_q,\widetilde v_q\) such that
$$
S_q(m)=\widetilde u_q^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_q^{\,m-m_0}\widetilde v_q\qquad(m\ge m_0).
$$
Moreover, the number of states satisfies the polynomial upper bound
$$
|\mathcal{N}_q|=\binom{q+|Q|-1}{|Q|-1}.
$$
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
In the direct product kernel on \(Q^q\), permuting the \(q\) coordinates does not alter the ``bitwise output-consistency'' screening condition, so the transition relation and counting objective are \(S_q\)-invariant.
Taking the quotient of \(Q^q\) by the orbits of the \(S_q\) action, each orbit is uniquely determined by the occupation number vector \((n_s)_{s\in Q}\) indicating ``how many times each internal state appears''; hence the quotient space is \(\mathcal{N}_q\), whose cardinality is the multiset coefficient \(\binom{q+|Q|-1}{|Q|-1}\).
For any single-step input symbol and output-consistency screening, the number of feasible transitions from one orbit to another can be explicitly given by polynomial counting (multinomial coefficients/polynomial expansion), yielding a nonnegative integer adjacency matrix \(\widetilde A_q\) with orbits as nodes and ``inter-orbit transition counts'' as edge weights.
Path counting on this weighted kernel yields the same \(S_q(m)\) as a matrix power readout expression. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Implementation: coefficient extraction formula for the symmetric power moment-kernel]\label{rem:pom-moment-symmetric-power-coefficient-extraction}
The ``polynomial counting'' of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-symmetric-power} can be written as a directly implementable coefficient extraction formula.
Following the deterministic transducer \(\tau(s,a)=(s',b)\) of Definition \ref{def:pom-collision-count-bisim}, and allowing one-step weights (edge weights after potential/temperature gate synthesis)
\(
w(s,a)\in\RR_{\ge 0}
\)
(the unweighted case takes \(w\equiv 1\) to yield strict integer counts).
Let \(\mathbf{x}=(x_t)_{t\in Q}\).
For each output symbol \(b\in A_{\mathrm{out}}\) and each internal state \(s\in Q\), define the \textbf{transition polynomial}
\[
T_{s}^{(b)}(\mathbf{x})
:=\sum_{a\in A_{\mathrm{in}}:\ \mathrm{out}(s,a)=b}\ w(s,a)\,x_{\mathrm{next}(s,a)},
\]
where \(\mathrm{next}(s,a)\) and \(\mathrm{out}(s,a)\) are given by \(\tau(s,a)=(\mathrm{next}(s,a),\mathrm{out}(s,a))\).
\par
For any occupation vector \(\nu\in\mathcal{N}_q\), let
\[
G_{\nu}^{(b)}(\mathbf{x})
:=\prod_{s\in Q}\bigl(T_s^{(b)}(\mathbf{x})\bigr)^{\nu(s)}.
\]
Then the entries of the symmetric power quotient kernel can be taken as
\[
\boxed{\;
\widetilde A_q(\nu,\nu')
\ =\
\sum_{b\in A_{\mathrm{out}}}\ [\mathbf{x}^{\nu'}]\;G_{\nu}^{(b)}(\mathbf{x})\;},
\qquad
[\mathbf{x}^{\nu'}]\ \text{denotes the coefficient of}\ \prod_{t\in Q}x_t^{\nu'(t)}.
\]
Combinatorial interpretation: the coefficient \(\widetilde A_q(\nu, \nu')\) equals the weighted sum over input choices satisfying the output-consistency constraint (i.e., all \(q\) copies in their respective current states produce the same output symbol \(b \in A_{\mathrm{out}}\)). For a given \(b\), the coefficient \([\mathbf{x}^{\nu'}]\) of the polynomial \(G_{\nu}^{(b)}(\mathbf{x})\) counts the weight contributions of all input letter combinations such that the \(\nu(s)\) copies transitioning from state \(s\) yield a successor-state distribution that is exactly the occupation vector \(\nu'\).
\par
Under the pre-compression framework of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-col-lumping}, one simply replaces \(Q\) with \(Q/{\sim_{\mathrm{col}}}\) and rewrites \(T_s^{(b)}\) as the class-wise counting/weighting \(T_C^{(b)}\), to obtain the same-type coefficient extraction formula directly implementable on the histogram state space.
\end{remark}

\subsubsection{Two-Dimensional Pressure Surface: Unifying the \texorpdfstring{$q$}{q}-Moment and Temperature Gate \texorpdfstring{$u$}{u} into a Computable Phase Diagram}\label{subsec:pom-pressure-surface-qu}

The coefficient extraction formula of Remark \ref{rem:pom-moment-symmetric-power-coefficient-extraction} above allows one-step edge weights
\(
w(s,a)\in\RR_{\ge 0}
\).
When this edge weight comes from the tempered gate \(\mathrm{PROJ}_u\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-proj-temp}), the ``spatial axis'' (multiplicity moment order \(q\)) and the ``temporal axis'' (temperature parameter \(u\)) can be organized into the same two-dimensional spectral surface.

\paragraph{Weighted collision kernel}
Take a nonnegative additive cost potential \(E:Q\times A_{\mathrm{in}}\to\ZZ_{\ge 0}\), and let
\[
w_u(s,a):=u^{E(s,a)},\qquad u\in(0,1].
\]
Replacing \(w(s,a)\) in Remark \ref{rem:pom-moment-symmetric-power-coefficient-extraction} with \(w_u(s,a)\) yields a family of \(u\)-dependent symmetric power quotient moment-kernels
\(
\widetilde A_q(u)
\)
(whose entries are still given by the same-type coefficient extraction formula).
Denote the Perron root as
\[
r_q(u):=\rho\!\bigl(\widetilde A_q(u)\bigr).
\]
When \(u=1\), \(w_u\equiv 1\) degenerates back to the unweighted counting kernel \(\widetilde A_q\) and the principal scale \(r_q\) of this paper.

\paragraph{Two-dimensional pressure surface}
Let \(|A_{\mathrm{out}}|\) denote the output alphabet size (typically \(|A_{\mathrm{out}}|=2\) for the folding readout in this paper).
Define the two-dimensional pressure as
\[
\boxed{\;
P(q,u):=\log r_q(u)-q\log|A_{\mathrm{out}}|\;}.
\]
\begin{remark}[Temperature parameter reparametrization: \texorpdfstring{$u=e^{-\beta}$}{u=exp(-beta)} and the pressure surface \texorpdfstring{$P(q,\beta)$}{P(q,beta)}]\label{rem:pom-pressure-beta-reparam}
Let \(\beta:=-\log u\in[0,\infty)\), and define
\[
P(q,\beta):=P\!\bigl(q,e^{-\beta}\bigr)=\log r_q(e^{-\beta})-q\log|A_{\mathrm{out}}|.
\]
Under the binary readout framework \(|A_{\mathrm{out}}|=2\) of this paper, this gives \(P(q,\beta)=\log r_q(e^{-\beta})-q\log 2\).
This notation is purely a coordinate rewrite: it reformulates the ``temperature axis'' from \(u\in(0,1]\) to the cost strength \(\beta\ge 0\), and is compatible with the two-dimensional convex dual formulation of Remark \ref{rem:pom-psi-2d-legendre-front}.
\end{remark}
It is compatible with the existing one-dimensional fingerprints of this paper in two limiting directions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Spatial endpoint)} \(P(q,1)=\log r_q-q\log|A_{\mathrm{out}}|=P(q)\) (the moment pressure curve of this paper);
  \item \textbf{(Temporal endpoint)} Formally setting \(q=1\), the ``collision constraint'' becomes trivial, and \(\widetilde A_1(u)\) degenerates to the adjacency matrix of the underlying weighted kernel (the \(M_E(u)\) framework of Definition \ref{def:pom-proj-temp}), so \(r_1(u)=\lambda_1(u)\) recovers the single-axis temperature pressure \(P(1,u)=\log\lambda_1(u)-\log|A_{\mathrm{out}}|\).
\end{enumerate}
Therefore, \(P(q,u)\) serves as a unified phase diagram coordinate for ``space (collision moment spectrum)--time (temperature tilting)'', providing a computable spectral-side base for the \((u,q,\varepsilon)\) three-knob scheduling of Remark \ref{rem:pom-hardening-u-vs-q}.

\begin{definition}[Normalized mixed pressure \texorpdfstring{$\Psi(q,u)$}{Psi(q,u)} (temperature scaling of the Renyi/collision spectrum)]\label{def:pom-psi-mixed-pressure}
On the basis of the two-dimensional pressure surface \(P(q,u)\), define
\[
\boxed{\;
\Psi(q,u):=P(q,u)-qP(1,u)
=\log r_q(u)-q\log r_1(u)\;}
\qquad(q\ge 2,\ u\in(0,1]).
\]
It is equivalent to the ``normalized difference of the \(q\)-collision principal scale relative to the baseline temperature free energy \(r_1(u)\)''.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Temperature scaling law: the \texorpdfstring{$q$}{q}-collision spectrum of \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{PROJ}_u$}{PROJ_u} readout is given by \texorpdfstring{$\Psi(q,u)$}{Psi}]\label{prop:pom-proj-mom-psi-law}
Following Definition \ref{def:pom-proj-temp}, take the stepwise readout \(R\) and denote by \(\nu_{u,m}\) the pushforward distribution of the equilibrium measure \(\mu_u\) on the length-\(m\) output word space \(A_{\mathrm{out}}^m\).
Then for any integer \(q\ge 2\),
\[
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}\log\sum_{x\in A_{\mathrm{out}}^m}\nu_{u,m}(x)^q
=\Psi(q,u).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Expand \(\sum_x\nu_{u,m}(x)^q\) as a sum over \(q\) independent paths, and use ``bitwise co-output'' to restrict the summation to reachable paths on the \(q\)-fold synchronous product (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-kernel-exists}).
The numerator is given by the path weight count of the weighted moment-kernel \(\widetilde A_q(u)\), whose exponential growth rate is \(r_q(u)\); the denominator comes from the Perron--Frobenius normalization of \(\mu_u\): the probability of each length-\(m\) path carries a factor \(r_1(u)^{-m}\), so the \(q\)-th power contributes \(r_1(u)^{-qm}\).
By the Perron--Frobenius principal scale,
\[
\sum_{x\in A_{\mathrm{out}}^m}\nu_{u,m}(x)^q=\Theta\!\left(\left(\frac{r_q(u)}{r_1(u)^q}\right)^{\!m}\right),
\]
taking \(\frac1m\log\) and letting \(m\to\infty\) yields the conclusion. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Second-order collision probability and anti-collision exponent]\label{def:pom-col-u-h2}
Following the notation of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-proj-mom-psi-law}, define the second-order collision probability
\[
\mathrm{Col}_u(m)
:=
\sum_{x\in A_{\mathrm{out}}^m}\nu_{u,m}(x)^2.
\]
and define the anti-collision exponent
\[
\boxed{\;
h_2(u):=-\Psi(2,u)=2\log r_1(u)-\log r_2(u)\;\ge\;0\; }.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Collision threshold and addressing capacity: the \texorpdfstring{$1/2$}{1/2} rule governed by \texorpdfstring{$h_2(u)$}{h2(u)}]\label{thm:pom-collision-threshold-capacity}
Fix \(u\in(0,1]\).
Following Definition \ref{def:pom-col-u-h2},
\[
\mathrm{Col}_u(m)=\exp\!\bigl(-h_2(u)\,m+o(m)\bigr),\qquad m\to\infty.
\]
Let \(X_1,\dots,X_{N(m)}\) be independent and identically distributed \(A_{\mathrm{out}}^m\)-valued random variables, each with distribution \(\nu_{u,m}\), and denote the event
\[
\mathsf{Inj}_m:=\{\,X_1,\dots,X_{N(m)}\ \text{are pairwise distinct}\,\}.
\]
Then there exists a phase transition threshold:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item If \(\displaystyle \limsup_{m\to\infty}\frac{2\log N(m)}{m}<h_2(u)\), then \(\mathbb{P}(\mathsf{Inj}_m)\to 1\);
  \item If \(\displaystyle \liminf_{m\to\infty}\frac{2\log N(m)}{m}>h_2(u)\), then \(\mathbb{P}(\mathsf{Inj}_m)\to 0\).
\end{enumerate}
Therefore, \(h_2(u)/2\) is the exponential capacity for ``collision-free addressing achievable at resolution budget \(m\)''; and \(h_2(u)\) is computable from the Perron roots of the finite kernel \((r_1(u),r_2(u))\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-psi-mixed-pressure}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Proposition \ref{prop:pom-proj-mom-psi-law} with \(q=2\),
\[
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}\log \mathrm{Col}_u(m)
=\Psi(2,u)
=-h_2(u),
\]
so \(\mathrm{Col}_u(m)=\exp(-h_2(u)m+o(m))\).
\par
Let
\(
I_{ij}:=\mathbf{1}\{X_i=X_j\}
\)
and denote the collision pair count
\[
Y:=\sum_{1\le i<j\le N} I_{ij},\qquad (N:=N(m)).
\]
Then \(\mathsf{Inj}_m=\{Y=0\}\).
\par
\textbf{(1) Sub-threshold: collision-free becomes certain.}
By Markov's inequality,
\[
\mathbb{P}(Y\ge 1)\le \mathbb{E}Y=\binom{N}{2}\mathrm{Col}_u(m).
\]
When \(\frac{2\log N}{m}<h_2(u)\) holds strictly in the limit,
\(
\binom{N}{2}\mathrm{Col}_u(m)\to 0
\), so \(\mathbb{P}(\mathsf{Inj}_m)=1-\mathbb{P}(Y\ge 1)\to 1\).
\par
\textbf{(2) Super-threshold: collision-free becomes impossible.}
Using Chebyshev's inequality,
\[
\mathbb{P}(Y=0)
\le
\mathbb{P}\bigl(|Y-\mathbb{E}Y|\ge \mathbb{E}Y\bigr)
\le
\frac{\mathrm{Var}(Y)}{(\mathbb{E}Y)^2}.
\]
Note that \(\mathbb{E}I_{ij}=\mathrm{Col}_u(m)\) and for disjoint index pairs \((i,j)\cap(k,\ell)=\varnothing\), \(I_{ij}\) and \(I_{k\ell}\) are independent, hence
\[
\mathrm{Var}(Y)
\le
\sum_{i<j}\mathbb{E}I_{ij}
 + 6\!\!\sum_{1\le i<j<k\le N}\!\!\mathbb{E}\bigl[I_{ij}I_{ik}\bigr].
\]
Here
\(
\mathbb{E}[I_{ij}I_{ik}]
=\mathbb{P}(X_i=X_j=X_k)
=\sum_x \nu_{u,m}(x)^3
\).
By \(\ell^p\)-norm monotonicity (or H\"older's inequality),
\[
\sum_x \nu_{u,m}(x)^3
\le
\left(\sum_x \nu_{u,m}(x)^2\right)^{3/2}
=\mathrm{Col}_u(m)^{3/2}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\mathrm{Var}(Y)\ \le\ \binom{N}{2}\mathrm{Col}_u(m)\ +\ 6\binom{N}{3}\mathrm{Col}_u(m)^{3/2}.
\]
Since \(\mathbb{E}Y=\binom{N}{2}\mathrm{Col}_u(m)\),
\[
\frac{\mathrm{Var}(Y)}{(\mathbb{E}Y)^2}
\ \lesssim\
\frac{1}{\mathbb{E}Y}
\ +\
\frac{1}{N\sqrt{\mathrm{Col}_u(m)}}.
\]
When \(\frac{2\log N}{m}>h_2(u)\) holds strictly in the limit, \(N^2\mathrm{Col}_u(m)\to\infty\), so \(\mathbb{E}Y\to\infty\) and \(N\sqrt{\mathrm{Col}_u(m)}\to\infty\). Thus the right-hand side \(\to 0\), and \(\mathbb{P}(\mathsf{Inj}_m)=\mathbb{P}(Y=0)\to 0\).
\qed
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Two-dimensional Legendre--Fenchel front: inverting the joint spatial--energy spectrum from \texorpdfstring{$\Psi(q,u)$}{Psi} (conditional formula)]\label{rem:pom-psi-2d-legendre-front}
Let \(\gamma_m(x)=\frac{1}{m}\log d_m(x)\) be the fiber log-multiplicity density (Definition \ref{def:pom-fiber-spectrum}), and let
\[
\mathcal{E}_m:=\frac{1}{m}\sum_{t=1}^m E(e_t)
\]
be the energy density of the potential (Definition \ref{def:pom-proj-temp}).
Under standard thermodynamic formalism conditions, \(\Psi(q,u)\) can be viewed as the limit of the mixed log-moment generating function of \((\gamma_m,\mathcal{E}_m)\); hence the joint front (rate function) can be given by a two-dimensional Legendre--Fenchel transform:
\[
I(\gamma,\mathcal{E})
=\sup_{q\ge 2,\ u\in(0,1)}
\Bigl((q-1)\gamma+(\log u)\mathcal{E}-\Psi(q,u)\Bigr).
\]
This formula upgrades the ``spatial loss spectrum'' from a single-parameter \(q\) to a joint spectral front simultaneously involving the temperature axis \(u\), and all input quantities are computable from the Perron roots of the finite kernel; hence even if computable only on a discrete \(q\) and sampled \(u\) grid, it can still output an auditable two-dimensional upper/lower envelope as a front certificate for the compiler and audit chain.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[Pressure front-driven hardening time lower bound (prototype)]\label{cor:pom-pressure-delay-lower-bound}
Under the framework where the order gate readout employs the twisted spectral gap scale (Corollary \ref{cor:pom-order-mixing}; Remark \ref{rem:pom-order-mixing-scale}), denote
\[
\Gamma(u)
:=
\log\!\Bigl(\frac{\lambda_1(u)}{\Lambda(u)}\Bigr)
=\frac{1}{\tau_{\mathrm{mix}}(u)},
\qquad
\lambda_1(u):=\rho(M_1(u)),\quad
\Lambda(u):=\sup_{\chi\neq 1}\rho(M_\chi(u)).
\]
Let \(\varepsilon_m\) be the total failure probability budget allowed under the reference sampling framework at resolution \(m\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-eps-sound-rewrite}).
If a ``soft order gate \(\Rightarrow\) hard order gate'' hardening/comparison readout requires achieving
\(
\varepsilon_m\le \exp(-\alpha m)
\)
exponential reliability (\(\alpha>0\)), then its minimum iteration depth \(n_{\min}(m)\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{\;
n_{\min}(m)\ \gtrsim\ \frac{\log(1/\varepsilon_m)}{\Gamma(u)}\ \ge\ \frac{\alpha}{\Gamma(u)}\,m\;}
\]
(constant factors absorbed into \(\gtrsim\)).
In particular, when the reliability exponent \(\alpha\) is given by the rate function of the pressure front (e.g., taking \(\alpha=I(\gamma,\mathcal{E})\), see Remark \ref{rem:pom-psi-2d-legendre-front}), the minimum time growth for ``order projection hardening'' is rigidly constrained by the joint spatial--energy spectral front: the steeper the front (\(I\) larger), the greater the minimum number of time steps required at fixed \(\Gamma(u)\).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Corollary \ref{cor:pom-order-mixing} gives the mixing time scale
\(
n\gtrsim \tau_{\mathrm{mix}}(u)\log(1/\varepsilon_m)
\);
rewriting with \(\Gamma(u)=1/\tau_{\mathrm{mix}}(u)\) yields the first inequality.
The second follows immediately by taking the logarithm of \(\varepsilon_m\le e^{-\alpha m}\). \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Hardening--endpoint swap anomaly \texorpdfstring{$\Delta_{\mathrm{swap}}$}{Delta_swap} (irreducible certificate primitive)]\label{def:pom-hard-tail-swap-anomaly}

\boxed{\;
\Delta_{\mathrm{swap}}
:=
\Bigl(\lim_{u\downarrow 0}\ \limsup_{q\to\infty}\ \frac{1}{q}\Psi(q,u)\Bigr)
-
\Bigl(\limsup_{q\to\infty}\ \frac{1}{q}\ \lim_{u\downarrow 0}\Psi(q,u)\Bigr).\;}

\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Interpretation: \texorpdfstring{$\Delta_{\mathrm{swap}}$}{Delta_swap} nonzero means ``the boundary cannot be simultaneously absorbed into the normal form'']\label{rem:pom-hard-tail-swap-anomaly}

\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[Collision count equivalence (\texorpdfstring{$\sim_{\mathrm{col}}$}{~col})]\label{def:pom-collision-count-bisim}
Let \(\mathcal{T}\) be a deterministic online kernel/transducer: state set \(Q\), input alphabet \(A_{\mathrm{in}}\), output alphabet \(A_{\mathrm{out}}\), with deterministic transition-output
\[
\tau:Q\times A_{\mathrm{in}}\to Q\times A_{\mathrm{out}},\qquad (s,a)\mapsto (s',b).
\]
Call an equivalence relation \(\sim_{\mathrm{col}}\) a \textbf{collision count equivalence} if it is the maximal (coarsest) equivalence relation satisfying the following ``bucket by output, count by successor class'' condition:
for any \(s,t\in Q\), if \(s\sim_{\mathrm{col}} t\), then for any output symbol \(b\in A_{\mathrm{out}}\) and any equivalence class \(C\in Q/{\sim_{\mathrm{col}}}\),
\[
N_{s}(b,C):=\#\{a\in A_{\mathrm{in}}:\ \tau(s,a)=(s',b)\ \text{and}\ s'\in C\}
\ =\
N_{t}(b,C).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Pre-compression before symmetric power: replacing \texorpdfstring{$|Q|$}{|Q|} with \texorpdfstring{$r_{\mathrm{col}}=|Q/{\sim_{\mathrm{col}}}|$}{rcol}]\label{prop:pom-moment-col-lumping}
Following the setting of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-symmetric-power}.
Let \(\sim_{\mathrm{col}}\) be the collision count equivalence of Definition \ref{def:pom-collision-count-bisim}, and denote
\(
r_{\mathrm{col}}:=|Q/{\sim_{\mathrm{col}}}|.
\)
Then for any \(q\ge 2\), there exists a polynomial-state moment-kernel strictly equivalent to \(\widetilde A_q\) (generating the same \(S_q(m)\)), whose state space can be taken as ``class occupation number vectors''
\[
\widetilde{\mathcal{N}}_{q}:=\Bigl\{(n_{C})_{C\in Q/{\sim_{\mathrm{col}}}}\in\ZZ_{\ge 0}^{r_{\mathrm{col}}}:\ \sum_{C}n_{C}=q\Bigr\},
\]
so that the state count satisfies the stronger upper bound
\[
|\widetilde{\mathcal{N}}_q|=\binom{q+r_{\mathrm{col}}-1}{r_{\mathrm{col}}-1}.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The one-step transition count of the moment-kernel depends only on: the internal state of each trajectory at the current time and, under the given output-consistency screening, ``how many input letters send it to which successor state''.
If states in the same \(\sim_{\mathrm{col}}\) class have identical input counts \(N_s(b,C)\) for all output symbols \(b\) and all successor classes \(C\), then after the \(q\)-fold synchronous product with ``output-consistency'' screening, any computation of feasible transition counts depends only on ``how many trajectories are in each equivalence class'' (i.e., the class occupation vector), not on the specific state names within each class.
Therefore one can first take the quotient \(Q\to Q/{\sim_{\mathrm{col}}}\) on the underlying states, then perform symmetric power histogram compression, to obtain the stated \(\widetilde{\mathcal{N}}_q\) and the same \(S_q(m)\) matrix power readout. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Executable interface: partition refinement and histogram-DP]\label{rem:pom-moment-col-lumping-impl}
The \(\sim_{\mathrm{col}}\) of Definition \ref{def:pom-collision-count-bisim} can be computed in polynomial time via partition refinement;
the script module \texttt{scripts/common\_moment\_kernel\_hist.py} implements this partition solver (function \texttt{collision\_count\_lumping\_partition}), and allows performing a \(\sim_{\mathrm{col}}\) pre-compression when constructing the histogram moment-kernel (parameter \texttt{lump\_by\_collision\_counts}, and demo \texttt{scripts/exp\_moment\_kernel\_histogram\_dp\_demo.py --lump-col}).
This pre-compression stacks with the symmetric power quotient compression of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-symmetric-power}: first reduce the dimension using \(|Q/{\sim_{\mathrm{col}}}|\), then use histogram/orbit representation to compress the exponential state space \(Q^q\) to polynomial states.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Implementation suggestion: treat \texorpdfstring{$\widetilde A_q$}{Aq} as ``compiler back-end'' rather than minimal realization]\label{rem:pom-moment-symmetric-power-impl}
Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-symmetric-power} provides a structural guarantee: an equivalent counting kernel can be constructed without enumerating the exponential state space \(Q^q\).
However, within the \(q\le 16\) range verified in this paper, smaller realizations can be recovered directly by Hankel/recursion methods (Table \ref{tab:fold_collision_moment_recursions} and Corollary \ref{cor:pom-moment-minreal}), with dimensions far below \(|\mathcal{N}_q|\).
Therefore the recommended engineering path is: \(\widetilde A_q\) provides the ``back-end kernel generatable from first principles'', while the minimal-order recursion/minimal realization serves as the ``front-end compression and certificate''.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Symmetric power functor viewpoint: \texorpdfstring{$\widetilde A_q$}{Aq} is the representation of the substitution operator on \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{Sym}^q$}{Symq}]\label{rem:pom-moment-symmetric-power-substitution-operator}
Following the transition polynomials \(T^{(b)}_s(\mathbf{x})\) of Remark \ref{rem:pom-moment-symmetric-power-coefficient-extraction}.
Let \(R:=\RR_{\ge 0}[\,x_s:\ s\in Q\,]\) be the polynomial semiring, and for each output symbol \(b\in A_{\mathrm{out}}\) define a \textbf{substitution operator}
\[
\mathcal{T}_b:R\to R,\qquad
x_s\longmapsto T^{(b)}_s(\mathbf{x}),
\]
extended as an algebra endomorphism by the semiring structure; then let \(\mathcal{T}:=\sum_{b\in A_{\mathrm{out}}}\mathcal{T}_b\) (summation by coefficient addition).
Then for each \(q\ge 2\), the matrix representation of \(\mathcal{T}\) on the homogeneous degree-\(q\) subspace \(R_q\) (with basis \(x^\nu=\prod_s x_s^{\nu(s)}\), \(\nu\in\mathcal{N}_q\)) is precisely the symmetric power quotient kernel \(\widetilde A_q\) of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-symmetric-power}:
\[
\mathcal{T}(x^\nu)=\sum_{\nu'\in\mathcal{N}_q}\widetilde A_q(\nu,\nu')\,x^{\nu'}.
\]
Therefore, in the language of ``gates'', \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) can be viewed as the composable operator obtained by applying the \(\mathrm{Sym}^q\) (symmetric power) functor to the underlying deterministic kernel, and \(\widetilde A_q\) is merely one realization representation of this operator in the monomial basis.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[From integer \texorpdfstring{$q$}{q} to real parameter: projective pressure operator]\label{rem:pom-moment-q-to-real-projective-operator}
Remark \ref{rem:pom-fiber-index-ldp} requires upgrading the ``integer-order moment fingerprint'' to a ``real-parameter pressure curve''; the key is to define, for any real \(q\ge 1\), a positive operator that strictly degenerates to \(\widetilde A_q\) at integer points.
Appendix \ref{app:pom-projective-pressure-operator} provides an executable strictification: the output-bucketed transition matrices \(B_b\) induce projective dynamics \(\Phi_b\) on the simplex, and the projective transfer operator \(\mathcal{L}_q f=\sum_b g_b^q\cdot f\!\circ\!\Phi_b\) is defined (Definition \ref{def:pom-projective-transfer-operator}).
Theorem \ref{thm:pom-projective-operator-degenerates-to-moment-kernel} proves that for integer \(q\ge 2\), the restriction of \(\mathcal{L}_q\) to the homogeneous polynomial subspace \emph{is} the symmetric power moment-kernel \(\widetilde A_q\) of this paper.
\end{remark}
