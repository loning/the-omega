\section{Appendix B: Proof of Linear Independence in the Multidimensional Non-Resonant Construction (Lemma \ref{lem:sqrt-independence})}
\label{app:multidim-indep}

Let $p_1,\dots,p_d$ be pairwise distinct square-free positive integers. Suppose there exist rational numbers $q_0,q_1,\dots,q_d$ such that
\[
q_0+\sum_{i=1}^d q_i\sqrt{p_i}=0,
\]
we must prove all $q_i=0$.

Let $K=\QQ(\sqrt{p_1},\dots,\sqrt{p_d})$. Its Galois group contains, for each $i$, an automorphism $\sigma_i$ with $\sigma_i(\sqrt{p_i})=-\sqrt{p_i}$ and $\sigma_i(\sqrt{p_j})=\sqrt{p_j}$ for $j\neq i$. Applying $\sigma_i$ to the identity above gives
\[
q_0+\sum_{j\neq i}q_j\sqrt{p_j}-q_i\sqrt{p_i}=0.
\]
Subtracting the original identity yields $2q_i\sqrt{p_i}=0$, so $q_i=0$. Since this holds for every $i$, the original identity gives $q_0=0$. The proof is complete.
