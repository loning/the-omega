\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The paper develops one primitive-orbit determinant calculus at the
Perron pole with two complementary faces. On the abelian side, the
finite-part constant and the cyclic-lift constants recover the reduced
spectral polynomial from root-of-unity data. On the finite-group side,
class-function logarithms and Adams--M\"obius inversion yield explicit
primitive Peter--Weyl formulas, explicit conjugacy-class Mertens
constants, and a non-abelian Fourier reconstruction of the twisted
primitive values.

\Cref{sec:shadows} makes this parallel exact. The quotient theorem
shows that abelianisation gives the precise one-dimensional shadow of
the finite-group theory, while the higher-dimensional Peter--Weyl
channels measure the genuine non-abelian defect. The explicit
\(S_3\)-example then exhibits the phenomenon that closes the main
story: even a channel with identically vanishing periodic traces may
carry non-zero primitive bias, because the primitive extraction is
governed by Adams operations rather than by bare M\"obius inversion.
