\begin{remark}[M\"obius renormalization and generating closure of the finite-window probability spectrum]\label{rem:pom-max-fiber-prob-spectrum-mobius-closure}
For even support, take a phase branch and let
\(
p_k:=\frac{F_{k+1}}{F_{k+2}}\in(1/2,1)
\) (\(k\ge 4\)).
Then by the Fibonacci recursion \(F_{k+3}=F_{k+2}+F_{k+1}\), one has the strict M\"obius recursion
\[
\boxed{\;
p_{k+1}=\frac{F_{k+2}}{F_{k+3}}=\frac{1}{1+\frac{F_{k+1}}{F_{k+2}}}=\frac{1}{1+p_k}.\;}
\]
The unique fixed point of this recursion is \(p=\varphi^{-1}\) (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-golden-bias-variance-equals-bias}), and the complementary involution \(S(p)=1-p\) gives the complementary limit \(\varphi^{-2}\).
The two-phase probabilities for the odd-support bias are, by the simplified formula of Remark \ref{rem:pom-max-fiber-achievers-bsplit}, precisely
\(
\{\frac{F_k}{F_{k+1}},\frac{F_{k-1}}{F_{k+1}}\}=\{p_{k-1},1-p_{k-1}\}
\),
with an additional balanced fixed point \(p=\tfrac12\).
Thus, the finite-window probability spectrum of the maximum achievers within the stability window can be viewed as the truncated union of the discrete orbit generated by
\[
S(p)=1-p,\qquad T(p)=\frac{1}{1+p}
\]
on \((0,1)\); the limiting accumulation points are immediately given by the fixed-point classification as the ternary rigidity
\(
p_1\in\{\tfrac12,\varphi^{-1},\varphi^{-2}\}
\)
(Corollary \ref{cor:pom-max-fiber-achievers-hidden-bit-prob-spectrum}).
\end{remark}
