\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Translation Flows, Window Observations, and Induction}

Let $G$ be a compact abelian group and $m$ the Haar measure. Take $a\in G$. Define the translation
\[
T_a(x)=x+a,\qquad x\in G.
\]
Given a measurable window $E\subset G$ with $m(E)>0$, define the first-return time
\[
r_E(x)=\min\{n\ge 1:\ T_a^n(x)\in E\},
\]
and define the induced map
\[
T_{a,E}(x)=T_a^{r_E(x)}(x),\qquad x\in E,
\]
together with the normalized window measure $m_E(A)=m(A\cap E)/m(E)$. The induced system $(E,m_E,T_{a,E})$ captures the dynamics ``visible only within the window'' and manifests arithmetic and geometric constraints through the return-time spectrum and piecewise structure.

\subsection{Research Questions}

This paper addresses the following structural problems:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item How is the return-time spectrum of interval windows on circle rotations determined by continued-fraction arithmetic, and when does the induced map remain in the rotation class?
  \item On two-value stable window families, how does the parameter update from induction and rescaling form integer-matrix cocycles and align with continued-fraction tail updates? How do quadratic irrationals and the golden parameter correspond to periodic points and fixed points?
  \item In higher-dimensional torus translations, how do algebraic constraints on the translation vector limit the effective dimension of orbit closure, thereby limiting the dimension that ``single-step scanning'' can cover?
  \item How can we organize the ``observation--induction--observable compression'' as a commutation structure between reflectors and induction functors using factor systems and categorical language, and propose checkable generalization propositions?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Contributions and Improvements}

\begin{itemize}
  \item We organize the three-value structure of interval return times, the characterization of two-value degeneration, the equivalence of induced 2-IET with rotation, the read-off of the induced rotation number, and its fractional linear expression into a closed chain of theorems, yielding a computable ``return-spectrum--induction-parameter'' algorithmic formula.
  \item We introduce the ``window-induced renormalization operator'' and provide, on two-value stable window families, continued-fraction tail formulas for parameter updates and matrix action representations. We characterize periodic renormalization orbits for ultimately periodic continued-fraction parameters; the golden parameter yields a fixed point and self-induced closure chain.
  \item We give an exact formula for the closure dimension of higher-dimensional torus translations, correcting the misconception that ``single-step scanning in a fixed low-degree number field can fill high-dimensional space'': if all coordinates belong to a fixed quadratic field, the closure dimension is at most 1.
  \item We propose the reflector--induction commutation theorem template: under window pullback and fiber randomness conditions, we align the Kronecker factor after induction with induction on the Kronecker factor, formalizing ``inducible closure windows'' as stable objects within the reflector class.
\end{itemize}
