\paragraph{Boundary as non-analytic point of the pressure function: unified coordinate for spectral radius, dimension spectrum, and phase transition}
Theorem \ref{thm:pom-real-q-pressure-analytic-interpolation} promotes the integer-\(q\) moment fingerprint to a real-parameter pressure curve, solidifying pressure as a spectral object:
\[
\tau(q)=\log\lambda(q)=\log\rho(\mathcal L_q).
\]
Within this framework, ``boundary'' can be institutionalized as the non-analytic frontier of pressure; its mechanism arises not from intuitive geometric discontinuities, but from the destruction of the analytic perturbation regime by the competability of the principal spectral point (spectral gap collapse or principal spectral branch switching).

\begin{definition}[Pressure boundary set (non-analytic frontier)]\label{def:pom-pressure-nonanalytic-boundary}
Following the notation of Theorem \ref{thm:pom-real-q-pressure-analytic-interpolation}, on the open interval \(J\subset(0,\infty)\) let
\(
\tau(q):=\log\rho(\mathcal L_q)
\)
be the real-parameter pressure.
Define the pressure boundary set as
\[
\partial\tau:=\{q\in J:\ \tau\ \text{is not real-analytic at}\ q\}.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Analyticity under spectral gap regime and boundary trigger mechanism]\label{rem:pom-nonanalyticity-as-spectral-gap-closure}
If \(q_0\in J\) satisfies the uniform spectral gap hypothesis of Theorem \ref{thm:pom-real-q-pressure-analytic-interpolation} (the Perron principal eigenvalue is an isolated spectral point of algebraic multiplicity \(1\) in some neighborhood), then by the analytic perturbation theory of spectral projections, \(\lambda(q)\) and \(\tau(q)\) are real-analytic in a neighborhood of \(q_0\).
Therefore the occurrence of \(\partial\tau\) can only be triggered by spectral structure degeneracy: typical mechanisms include spectral gap collapse, loss of isolation of the principal spectral point, or principal spectral branch switching caused by equal-mode competition.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Spectralization of dimension spectrum via spectral radius: alignment with ``fractal dimension-matrix-phase transition'']\label{rem:pom-dimension-matrix-pressure-unified}

First, the Hausdorff dimension of the subshift under the prefix ultrametric is determined by topological entropy, thus degenerating to the logarithmic scale of the spectral radius for finite graphs/adjacency operators (Corollary \ref{cor:Ym-amb-hausdorff-codim}).
Second, for the Rényi dimension spectrum of the fold pushforward measure \(w_m=d_m/2^m\), under geometric scaling \(\varepsilon_m=\varphi^{-m}\), a closed form is given by
\[
D_q=\frac{q\log 2-\log r_q}{(q-1)\log\varphi}
\]
(Proposition \ref{prop:pom-renyi-dimension-spectrum}), and at integer anchor points satisfies \(\tau(q)=\log r_q\) (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-real-q-pressure-analytic-interpolation}).
Therefore, ``structural transitions in the dimension spectrum'' can be equivalently rewritten in the same coordinate system as spectral mechanism changes of \(\tau\): when pressure non-analyticity or significant spectral gap weakening appears in the \(q\) direction, the multifractal Legendre geometry and large deviation spectral equality (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-ldp-uniform-and-wm-exact-spectrum}) induced by \(\tau\) will synchronously exhibit auditable boundary effects.

\end{remark}
