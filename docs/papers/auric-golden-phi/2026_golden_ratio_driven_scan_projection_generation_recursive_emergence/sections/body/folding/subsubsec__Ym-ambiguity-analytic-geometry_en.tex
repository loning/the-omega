\subsubsection{Analytic and geometric fingerprints of the ambiguity shell: principal singularity star clusters and fractal codimension}\label{subsubsec:Ym-ambiguity-analytic-geometry}
The previous subsection compressed the time-layer semantics of ``unsynchronized i.e.\ statistical \(\mathsf{NULL}\)'' into a finite-state counting object: the non-singleton induced subgraph \(H_m^{\mathrm{amb}}\) and its adjacency matrix \(A_m^{\mathrm{amb}}\).
In the specific Zeckendorf folding of this work (\(m\ge 3\)), we have \(Y_m^{\mathrm{amb}}=\varnothing\) (Theorem \ref{thm:Ym-ambiguity-shell-dag}; Remark \ref{rem:Ym-amb-empty}), so this layer terminates at finite level;
however, in general sofic protocols, once the ambiguity shell subsystem is nonempty, both its analytic-side and geometric-side fingerprints can be completely characterized by the same Perron--Frobenius data.

\begin{corollary}[Period modulation \texorpdfstring{$\Longleftrightarrow$}{<->} principal singularity star cluster]\label{cor:Ym-unsync-star-pole-cluster}
Assume \(Y_m^{\mathrm{amb}}\neq\varnothing\), and follow the notation of Theorem \ref{thm:Ym-sync-tail-pf-sharp} and Definition \ref{def:Ym-unsync-prefix-count}.
Let \(A_m^{\mathrm{amb}}\) denote the edge-weighted adjacency matrix, \(\rho_{\mathrm{amb}}(m)=\rho(A_m^{\mathrm{amb}})\) its spectral radius, and suppose the maximal essential irreducible component has period \(p\ge 1\).
Define the ordinary generating function for unsynchronized prefix counts
\[
\mathcal{G}_m^{\mathrm{unsync}}(z)
:=\sum_{n\ge 0}N_m^{\mathrm{unsync}}(n)\,z^n
=e_{V_m}^{\mathsf T}(I-zA_m^{\mathrm{amb}})^{-1}\mathbf 1.
\]
Then the principal singularities of \(\mathcal{G}_m^{\mathrm{unsync}}\) form an equimodular pole cluster in the complex plane
\[
z_r=\rho_{\mathrm{amb}}(m)^{-1}e^{2\pi i r/p},\qquad r=0,1,\dots,p-1.
\]
In particular, if \(p=1\) (primitive case), there is a unique principal singularity at \(z_\star=\rho_{\mathrm{amb}}(m)^{-1}\in(0,1)\);
whereas when \(p>1\), the leading term of \(N_m^{\mathrm{unsync}}(n)\) must exhibit strict \(n\bmod p\) period modulation (Theorem \ref{thm:Ym-sync-tail-pf-sharp} and Theorem \ref{thm:Ym-unsync-prefix-pf-sharp}).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Since \(A_m^{\mathrm{amb}}\) is a finite matrix, \(\mathcal{G}_m^{\mathrm{unsync}}(z)\) is a rational function whose singularities are contained in the zero set of \(\det(I-zA_m^{\mathrm{amb}})=0\), namely the reciprocals of the eigenvalues of \(A_m^{\mathrm{amb}}\).
When the maximal essential irreducible component has period \(p\), Perron--Frobenius theory yields \(p\) eigenvalues on the spectral circle
\(
\rho_{\mathrm{amb}}(m)e^{2\pi i r/p}
\) (\(0\le r\le p-1\)), whose reciprocals give the equimodular point set
\(
z_r=\rho_{\mathrm{amb}}(m)^{-1}e^{2\pi i r/p}
\).
Since \(\mathbf 1\) is a strictly positive vector and the initial vector \(e_{V_m}\) can reach the essential component, the principal mode does not vanish under the readout \(e_{V_m}^{\mathsf T}(\cdot)\mathbf 1\), so these points constitute the principal singularity cluster.
The correspondence between period modulation and the multiple eigenvalues on the spectral circle is given by the same Perron decomposition (see Theorem \ref{thm:Ym-sync-tail-pf-sharp}). This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Fractal codimension calibrated by entropy gap: \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{codim}=\varepsilon_m/\log 2$}{codim=eps/log2}]\label{cor:Ym-amb-codim-from-entropy-gap}
Assume \(Y_m^{\mathrm{amb}}\neq\varnothing\).
Under the one-sided prefix ultrametric
\(
d(y,y'):=2^{-N}
\)
(\(N\) being the minimal index at which the two sequences differ in their prefixes), we have
\[
\dim_{\mathrm H}(Y_m^{\mathrm{amb}})
=\frac{h_{\mathrm{top}}(Y_m^{\mathrm{amb}})}{\log 2}
=\frac{\log\rho_{\mathrm{amb}}(m)}{\log 2}
=1-\frac{\varepsilon_m}{\log 2},
\qquad
\varepsilon_m:=\log 2-\log\rho_{\mathrm{amb}}(m)>0.
\]
Hence the fractal codimension of the ambiguity shell is strictly \(\varepsilon_m/\log 2\).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For any subshift \(Z\), under the prefix ultrametric, the diameter of length-\(n\) cylinder sets is \(2^{-n}\), and their \(n\)-word count satisfies
\(
N_Z(n)=\exp(h_{\mathrm{top}}(Z)\,n+o(n))
\).
Standard covering estimates yield \(\dim_{\mathrm H}(Z)=h_{\mathrm{top}}(Z)/\log 2\).
Substituting \(Z=Y_m^{\mathrm{amb}}\) and using
\(
h_{\mathrm{top}}(Y_m^{\mathrm{amb}})=\log\rho_{\mathrm{amb}}(m)
\)
from Theorem \ref{thm:Ym-sync-tail-pf-sharp} and \(\varepsilon_m=\log 2-\log\rho_{\mathrm{amb}}(m)\) yields the conclusion. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}
