\section{Problem 7: Uniform Lattices, Torsion, and $\mathbb Q$-Acyclic Universal Covers}

\subsection*{Problem}
Let $\Gamma$ be a uniform lattice in a real semisimple Lie group, and assume $\Gamma$ contains $2$-torsion.
Can $\Gamma$ be the fundamental group of a closed manifold $M$ with
\[
\widetilde H_*(\widetilde M;\mathbb Q)=0?
\]

\subsection*{What is rigorously known}

\begin{theorem}[Odd-torsion obstruction]
If $\Gamma$ contains torsion of odd prime order, then there is no closed manifold $M$ with
\[
\pi_1(M)=\Gamma,\qquad \widetilde H_*(\widetilde M;\mathbb Q)=0.
\]
In fact, no closed ANR $\mathbb Q$-homology manifold with these properties exists.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
This is a published obstruction theorem in the Fowler / Banagl--Weinberger framework (via controlled signature and $\rho$-invariant methods away from the prime $2$). Since closed manifolds are ANR $\mathbb Q$-homology manifolds, the manifold case is an immediate corollary.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Necessary consequence of existence]
If such a closed manifold $M$ existed, Cartan--Leray gives
\[
H^*(M;\mathbb Q)\cong H^*(\Gamma;\mathbb Q),
\]
and Poincar\'e duality on $M$ would force $\Gamma$ to satisfy rational Poincar\'e duality constraints. This is necessary but not sufficient for existence.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{Status for the stated hypothesis (2-torsion)}
The pure $2$-torsion case (no odd torsion) is currently \textbf{open} in the cited literature.
Therefore, under the exact hypothesis of Problem~7 (only assuming the presence of $2$-torsion), there is no complete unconditional YES/NO theorem recorded here.

\subsection*{Conclusion}
For Problem~7 as stated:
\begin{itemize}
\item odd torsion present: \textbf{NO} (obstructed);
\item only 2-primary torsion known: \textbf{open}.
\end{itemize}
So the full question is \textbf{not closed} in this manuscript.
