\paragraph{Horizon fiber capacity and Rényi-\texorpdfstring{$4$}{4} collision-spectrum critical tangency: area-law saturation and finite-dimensional bridging of square-root stability threshold}
In the externalization/horizon-projection semantics of this paper, black-hole entropy is strictly placed as the logarithmic capacity of typical fibers under external-interface constraints: given fixed external algebra and boundary geometry (area \(A\)), the typical fiber logarithmic size under \(\pi_{\mathrm{ext}}\) has a maximal admissible upper bound, saturating in Planck units at the canonical
\(
S_{\mathrm{BH}}=A/4
\)
(Proposition~\ref{prop:dephys-horizon-fiber-capacity-area-law}; equation~\eqref{eq:dephys-bh-entropy-area-quarter}).
In the operator-algebra caliber, this capacity is uniquely locked by the logarithm of the finite-index conditional expectation \(\log\mathrm{Ind}(E)\): it equals the worst relative-entropy loss externalization can incur, depending only on the external interface and embedding structure while being state-independent (Proposition~\ref{prop:op-algebra-dmax-capacity-equals-log-index}; see also Proposition~\ref{prop:fold-condexp-index-maxfiber} for the fold discrete realization).
\par
In parallel, the \(q\)-order moment-kernel of the collision spectrum serves as a computable probe of the same fiber structure in the Rényi direction: taking \(q=4\) as an example,
\(
S_4(m)=\sum_{x\in X_m} d_m(x)^4
\)
is equivalently the probability \(S_4(m)/16^m\) that ``four independent uniform windows fold and completely collide,'' precisely realized by the five-state non-negative integer kernel \(A_4\) whose exponential growth rate is controlled by the Perron root \(r_4=\rho(A_4)\) (Proposition~\ref{prop:pom-s4-recurrence}).
Under the square-root stability bound \(\Lambda\le \sqrt{\rho}\) of kernel-version RH (Definition~\ref{def:kernel-rh}), \(A_4\) first exhibits critical-level crossing: \(\Lambda_4>\sqrt{r_4}\) with crossing magnitude \(10^{-3}\), dominated by a stable negative real slow mode \(\mu_2<0\) (Remark~\ref{rem:pom-collision-rh-break-a4-spectrum}).
Treating the multiple edge weights of \(A_4\) as continuous parameters, this critical crossing can be organized as a Newman-type threshold given by resultant elimination as a complete algebraic certificate (Remark~\ref{rem:pom-collision-rh-break-a4-threshold}), with threshold semantics homologous in regime interpretation to ``information-recovery threshold induced by the horizon as scrambler/encoder'' (Remark~\ref{rem:dephys-horizon-scrambler-recovery-threshold}).
On the same kernel family, the even-spectrum quintic curve \(E_t(u)=\det(I-uA_4(t)^2)=0\) provides a shortest even-sector closed-form entry; its \(u\)-discriminant factorization excludes even-spectrum degeneracy on \(t\ge 0\) (except the isolated point \(t=1\)), re-characterizing the threshold certificate \(P_4(t)\) as the resultant of intersection elimination between the Perron curve and the even-spectrum embedding curve (Propositions~\ref{prop:pom-a4t-even-zeta-quintic},~\ref{prop:pom-a4t-newman-resultant-curve-intersection}).
At the discriminant resolution level, the characteristic polynomial of the fourth-order kernel itself induces the companion imaginary quadratic field \(K_4^{\mathrm{disc}}=\QQ(\sqrt{-985219})\) with class number \(195\), juxtaposing the \(q=4\) near-RH critical phase with an auditable constant-level arithmetic invariant chain (Proposition~\ref{prop:pom-a4-disc-quadratic-class-number}).
\par
Thus, the near-RH crossing at \(q=4\) can be understood as: when auditing externalization fiber structure with the fourth-order Rényi collision probe, the system first manifests a critical slow-mode channel on the spectral side, providing an auditable finite-dimensional bridge between ``area-law-saturating fiber capacity'' and ``critical stability threshold tangency.''
\par
In the same replica-statistics free-energy notation, let \(F_q:=\log r_q\), \(G_q:=\log\Lambda_q\), and write
\(\delta_q:=2G_q-F_q=\log(\Lambda_q^2/r_q)\) (see Remark~\ref{rem:pom-collision-kernel-rh-phase}).
If the fold-pushforward probability is \(w_m(x)=d_m(x)/2^m\), then
\(
S_q(m)/2^{qm}=\sum_{x\in X_m}w_m(x)^q
\)
yields a completely discrete, auditable integer replica partition function realization.
In this caliber, \(F_q\) gives the action density of the ``disconnected'' leading contribution, while the connected two-point correction generated by sub-spectral modes is measured exponentially at \(\exp(m\cdot 2G_q)\); thus the competition strength relative to the disconnected leading term \(\exp(mF_q)\) is controlled by \(\exp(m\delta_q)\).
From the sign change \(\delta_2<0,\delta_3<0,\delta_4>0\) in Table~\ref{tab:fold_collision_kernel_rh_scan_q2_8}, one identifies \(q_c=4\) as the minimal crossing point of the Fold collision spectrum in the replica-order direction.
The same table also shows that \(\beta_q:=\log\Lambda_q/\log r_q\) first satisfies \(\beta_q>\tfrac12\) at \(q=4\), so ``square-root suppression'' is first allowed to be breached at the exponential level by slow-mode channels at this order; this first passage can be viewed as a kind of semi-entropy criticality.
In the black-hole Rényi-entropy analogy, the above crossing can be interpreted as the first equal-order competition in action density between the connected saddle (replica wormhole) and the disconnected saddle; and the criterion for this competition falls entirely within the finite-dimensional computable spectral invariant \(\delta_q\), without relying on any external continuous limits or thermal-flow limit hypotheses.
Corollary \ref{cor:pom-deltaq-sample-complexity-threshold} further reads this crossing exponent as the minimal sample-exponent threshold in the sampling caliber.
\par
Finally, define the critical amplifiability constant \(\kappa_4:=|\delta_4|^{-1}\). Since \(\delta_4\) is of order \(10^{-3}\) (Table~\ref{tab:fold_collision_kernel_rh_scan_q2_8}), \(\kappa_4\) falls in the \(10^2\) range and gives the minimal scale in the ``replica evolution depth'' direction at which relative weights undergo an order-of-magnitude jump: when \(m\asymp \kappa_4\), the relative weight of connected/disconnected channels undergoes an order-of-magnitude transition in the exponential sense; moreover, for any spectral perturbation \(\delta_4\mapsto \delta_4+\Delta\), the multiplicative drift of this relative weight is precisely \(\exp(m\Delta)\), so the structural stability radius of the near-RH critical zone in the depth direction is forced to compress to the \(O(1/m)\) level. This phenomenon promotes ``spectral tangency'' from a static inequality to an auditable dynamic amplification mechanism: the critical zone is not merely ``close,'' but exhibits ``high-gain'' response.
On the other hand, the spectral-side critical depth measured by \(\kappa_4\) is not equivalent to the optimal moment-order breakpoint of the Chernoff quantile certificate: at \(\varepsilon=10^{-6}\), Table~\ref{tab:fold_collision_qstar_breakpoints_eps1e6_q2_20} gives the \(4\leftrightarrow 5\) breakpoint at \(m\approx 1241.89\), while the \(3\leftrightarrow 4\) breakpoint is at \(m\approx 2128.30\) (Remark~\ref{rem:pom-qstar-phase-diagram-breakpoints}). Therefore, a strict misalignment occurs under auditable parameters: the spectral tangency of \(q=4\) can be amplified at the depth scale \(m\sim\kappa_4\), while in the ``optimal tail bound/scanning strategy'' caliber, the depth at which \(q=4\) becomes optimal is significantly delayed; this misalignment indicates that spectral phase transitions and strategy phase transitions are controlled by two irreducible critical structures, both directly verifiable within the same finite window.
