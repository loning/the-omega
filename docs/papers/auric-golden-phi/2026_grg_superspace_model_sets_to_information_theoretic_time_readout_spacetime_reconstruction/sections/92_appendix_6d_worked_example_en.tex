\section{A worked 6D example: icosahedral splitting, probabilistic readout, and fiber statistics}\label{sec:appendix:6d}

This appendix provides a complete, auditable toy chain in the canonical ``6D to 3D'' setting used in Demo~C (Section~\ref{sec:falsifiability:demo}). The emphasis is on a concrete specification of $(E,H,\Gamma)$, a window family and a resolution kernel, a scan rule, a probabilistic readout, and the resulting symbolic/fiber statistics that can be regenerated by a single command.

\subsection{Object layer: a 6D lattice and an icosahedral splitting}
We take $G=\RR^6$ with lattice $\Gamma=\ZZ^6$. We choose a $(3+3)$ decomposition $G=E\oplus H$ with $E\cong\RR^3$ (physical) and $H\cong\RR^3$ (internal) via two projection matrices
\[
P_E:\ZZ^6\to E,\qquad P_H:\ZZ^6\to H,
\]
implemented explicitly in \texttt{scripts/demo\_6d\_icosa\_fingerprints.py} by the function \texttt{icosa\_matrices()}. The matrices use the golden ratio $\varphi$ and its Galois conjugate $\varphi^\star=1-\varphi=-1/\varphi$ to realize a standard icosahedral embedding: the physical columns use $\varphi$ while the internal columns use $\varphi^\star$. (Overall scaling is normalized for numerical conditioning.)

Given a finite enumeration of lattice points
\[
\Gamma_L:=\ZZ^6\cap[-L,L]^6,
\]
the projected point clouds are
\[
x=\gamma P_E\in E,\qquad u=\gamma P_H\in H,\qquad \gamma\in\Gamma_L.
\]

\paragraph{Explicit numeric matrices (reference run).}
For auditability, we record the exact $(6\times 3)$ matrices used in the reference run (rounded to $10$ decimals). Writing $\gamma\in\ZZ^6$ as a row vector, the projections are implemented as
\[
P_E(\gamma)=\gamma A_T,\qquad P_H(\gamma)=\gamma A_T^\star.
\]
\[

\normalsize
\]

\subsection{Protocol layer: a compact window and a finite-resolution kernel}
For the sharp model-set patch we use a compact internal window $W\subset H$ with boundary of Haar/Lebesgue measure zero. In Demo~C the sharp window is the ball
\[
W=\{u\in H:\ \|u\|\le R\}.
\]
To model finite resolution, we replace the sharp indicator by a smooth boundary layer weight $w=\kappa_\epsilon$ with scale parameter $\epsilon>0$. Concretely, the demo uses a logistic boundary layer
\[
\kappa_\epsilon(u):=\frac{1}{1+\exp\!\left(-\frac{R-\|u\|}{\epsilon}\right)}\in(0,1),
\]
with the sharp limit $\kappa_\epsilon\to\mathds{1}_W$ as $\epsilon\to 0$.

\subsection{Geometric outputs: diffraction slices and visibility curves}
From the accepted points $\{x_i\}\subset E$ (selected by the sharp or weighted window), Demo~C forms a binned projected density image (a 2D slice of the projected cloud) and computes an FFT-based intensity estimate $I(k)$ on a finite grid. The resulting diffraction slice and a resolution-dependent visibility curve $V(\epsilon)$ are saved under:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \texttt{sections/generated/demo\_6d\_diffraction\_slice.png},
  \item \texttt{sections/generated/demo\_6d\_visibility\_curve.png}.
\end{itemize}
The visibility statistic in the current demo is the coefficient of variation of the binned density image (variance over mean), phase-averaged over internal shifts.

\subsection{Symbolic scan: a torus factor, probabilistic readout, and induced stationarity}
To produce a symbolic record whose entropy rate depends on resolution, Demo~C scans an internal phase on a $3$-torus representation:
\[
h_t=h_0+t\beta\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 1)\in\TT^3,
\]
represented numerically in $[-1/2,1/2)^3$ by componentwise wrap. Given a radius parameter $R_{\mathrm{scan}}$ and the same boundary-layer rule, define the kernel
\[
p_t=\kappa_\epsilon(h_t):=\frac{1}{1+\exp\!\left(-\frac{R_{\mathrm{scan}}-\|h_t\|}{\epsilon}\right)}.
\]
The readout is a \emph{probabilistic window} (Section~\ref{sec:readout:probabilistic-template}): conditioned on $h_t$, the protocol samples
\[
A_t\sim \mathrm{Bernoulli}(p_t),
\]
independently across $t$ given the scan. This yields a stationary ergodic symbolic process under the skew-product construction (Proposition~\ref{prop:prob-window-ergodic}), so SMB applies and the refinement functional $\tau(t)$ is meaningful at the protocol level (Section~\ref{sec:info-time}).

For each $\epsilon$ in a fixed grid, Demo~C computes:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item a block-entropy proxy $H(A_0^{n-1})/n$ (with fixed $n$) as a crude entropy-rate estimator,
  \item a plug-in prefix statistic $\widehat{\tau}(t)=-\log\widehat{\mathbb{P}}(A_0^{t-1})$ estimated by counting prefix occurrences in the record (finite-data proxy for $\tau(t)$).
\end{itemize}
The $\epsilon\mapsto \widehat h(\epsilon)$ plot is saved as \texttt{sections/generated/demo\_6d\_entropy\_rate\_proxy.png}.

\subsection{A 1D ring-factor toy: Sturmian complexity and fuzzy two-interval readout}
For an explicit ``zero KS base, positive protocol entropy'' cross-check, the 6D demo also records a one-dimensional factor toy: an irrational rotation $x_t=x_0+t\alpha\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 1)$ with a two-interval readout $[0,I)$.
At $\epsilon=0$ the symbolic record is Sturmian and therefore has block complexity $p(n)=n+1$ (irrational $\alpha$); the empirical curve is saved as \texttt{sections/generated/demo\_6d\_ring\_factor\_complexity.png}.
For $\epsilon>0$ the readout is fuzzified via a smooth kernel $\kappa_\epsilon$ at the interval boundary and the protocol samples $A_t\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\kappa_\epsilon(x_t))$, yielding a strictly positive entropy-rate proxy; the resulting $\epsilon$-curve is saved as \texttt{sections/generated/demo\_6d\_ring\_entropy\_rate\_proxy.png}.
All intermediate numeric values (including $p(n)$ and the $\epsilon$-grid proxies) are archived in \texttt{artifacts/demo\_6d\_fingerprints.json}.

\subsection{Stabilization and fiber statistics (Fold, types, transitions)}
At a fixed stabilization depth $m$, the demo applies the Zeckendorf-based stabilization map $\Fold_m$ (Section~\ref{sec:stabilization:zeckendorf}) as a sliding-window factor to the raw bit record, producing a stabilized type sequence. The resulting reproducible statistics include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item the exact degeneracy histogram $\{|\mathcal{F}_m(x)|:x\in X_m\}$ computed combinatorially for the chosen $\Fold_m$,
  \item empirical stabilized type frequencies and their entropy $H(\text{type})$,
  \item empirical transition edge counts for successive stabilized types, and an exact Fold transition graph for the chosen depth $m$ computed combinatorially.
\end{itemize}
All numeric outputs for Demo~C are archived in \texttt{artifacts/demo\_6d\_fingerprints.json} together with the full parameter set (scan slope, window radii, $\epsilon$ grid, and stabilization depth). The exact transition graph is saved separately as \texttt{artifacts/demo\_6d\_fold\_graph\_m12.json}.
For a compact visual audit, the adjacency heatmap (log edge counts) is saved as \texttt{sections/generated/demo\_6d\_fold\_graph\_m12\_heatmap.png}.

\subsection{A box-window variant (polytope window) and closed-form Fourier envelopes}
For protocol audits that require an explicit closed-form $\widehat w$, one can replace the ball window by a box (or more general polytope) window in internal space. In particular, for an axis-aligned box in $H=\RR^3$ one has a closed-form product-sinc formula for $\widehat w$ under the convention of Remark~\ref{rem:fourier-convention}. This yields a direct link between predicted envelopes $|\widehat w(k^\star)|^2$ and the relative intensity profile across Fourier-module points (Section~\ref{sec:falsifiability:diffraction-formula}).
