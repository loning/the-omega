\subsection{Inverse consistency audit: phase$\to$delay$\to$phase (audit)}
\label{app:scattering_inverse_consistency_audit}

This appendix turns the scattering interface modules into a deterministic audit that checks an elementary inverse-consistency loop on vendored phase-shift point clouds.
It does not upgrade scattering theory to theorem-level closure; it only validates a bounded numerical pipeline on declared inputs.

\subsubsection{Setup and bounded estimator family}
\paragraph{Input.}
\AuditTag We use the registry file \texttt{data/k4\_matching/scattering\_phase\_registry.json}, which stores small vendored phase-shift point clouds $(x_i,\delta_i)$.
Here $x$ is a dataset abscissa (e.g.\ $T_{\mathrm{lab}}$ or a proxy energy), and $\delta$ is the scattering phase in radians.

\paragraph{Estimator family.}
\AuditTag We estimate $\dd\delta/\dd x$ via a bounded family:
\begin{itemize}
\item central differences (CD) on interior points;
\item local-linear fits on a symmetric window of radius $k\in\{1,2,3\}$ (LL);
\item optional moving-average smoothing of $\delta$ with window $w\in\{1,3,5\}$ (with $w=1$ meaning no smoothing).
\end{itemize}
We then define the paper's delay proxy $\tau(x)=2\,\dd\delta/\dd x$ and invert the map by integrating $\dd\delta/\dd x$ back to a reconstructed $\widehat\delta$ on the same interior grid, anchored at the first interior point (fixing the additive constant).

\subsubsection{Coordinate transforms and a Jacobian sign gate (audit)}
\AuditTag For datasets whose abscissa is a laboratory energy coordinate (e.g.\ $T_{\mathrm{lab}}$), one may compare $\mathrm{d}\delta/\mathrm{d}x$ to $\mathrm{d}\delta/\mathrm{d}y$ under transparent monotone coordinate transforms $y(x)$.
When $y(x)$ is monotone increasing on the sampled grid ($\mathrm{d}y/\mathrm{d}x>0$), the sign of the derivative must be preserved: $\mathrm{sign}(\mathrm{d}\delta/\mathrm{d}y)=\mathrm{sign}(\mathrm{d}\delta/\mathrm{d}x)$.
We record a bounded family of transforms and report a sign-mismatch fraction on interior points as a gate-style diagnostic.

\paragraph{Scope note.}
\AuditTag This audit checks numerical stability and internal consistency of a bounded estimator family on declared inputs.
It does not claim that the chosen abscissa $x$ is the physical frequency $\omega$ entering the Wigner--Smith definition, nor does it fix the unit dictionary; those remain matching-layer conventions already made explicit in Appendix~\ref{app:orbit_dynamics_and_force_scattering_bridge} and Appendix~\ref{app:time_mass_delay}.
