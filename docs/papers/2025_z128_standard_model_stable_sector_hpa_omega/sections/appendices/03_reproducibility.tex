\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:reproducibility}

This paper includes auditable scripts that reproduce the finite combinatorics, folding statistics, and the Hilbert chirality-index checks.
All scripts are written in Python and live under:
\[
\texttt{docs/papers/2025\_z128\_standard\_model\_stable\_sector\_hpa\_omega/scripts/}.
\]
Some extended audit and plotting utilities use a small scientific stack recorded in \path{requirements.txt}.

\subsection{What is reproduced}
The scripts reproduce:
\begin{itemize}
\item enumeration of $X_6$ and verification $|X_6|=21$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:xm_fib});
\item Hamming-weight distribution of $X_6$ and the $18\oplus 3$ cyclic/boundary split (Proposition~\ref{prop:cyc_bdry_6});
\item a tick-trap protocol-horizon illustration fragment (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol_horizon_tick_trap});
\item a leakage-kernel demo fragment and a $m=6$ trap/exit audit table that reports exit-channel decompositions into the three $m=6$ boundary channels (Appendix~\ref{app:leakage_kernel});
\item a low-leakage phase signature fragment (Appendix~\ref{app:protected_low_leakage_phase});
\item computation of $\mathrm{Fold}_6(N)$ for $N=0,\dots,63$, including surjectivity and the degeneracy histogram (Theorem~\ref{thm:fold6_stats});
\item generation of a complete Fold$_6$ table (Appendix~\ref{app:fold6_full_table});
\item a bounded counterfactual fold-family sensitivity sweep at $m=6$ (Appendix~\ref{app:fold_family_sensitivity}).
\item computation of the Hilbert chirality index $\chi_H$ at order $n=3$ and its sign flips under reflection and traversal reversal (Proposition~\ref{prop:chi_flip}).
\item an addressing-basis audit (Hilbert vs.\ a row-major counterfactual) that makes the tick-first locality choice explicit at the minimal anchor (Table~\ref{tab:addressing_selection}).
\item a bounded sensitivity sweep for gauge-factor selection under alternative complexity labels (Appendix~\ref{app:gauge_complexity_sensitivity}).
\item a holonomy-driven bounded candidate-family closure certificate for the three-factor gauge identification (Appendix~\ref{app:gauge3_holonomy_candidate_closure}).
\item a coarse-grained scalar parity check on the $n=3$ Hilbert grid that contrasts the parity-odd chirality index with parity-even block-averaged intrinsic observables (Table~\ref{tab:scalar_coarse_grain}).
\item reproduction of the closed $21\to$SM labeling table (Table~\ref{tab:sm_labeling_table}), whose underlying labeling map is uniquely fixed by Theorem~\ref{thm:labeling_unique} once the declared ordering keys are chosen (Remark~\ref{rem:labeling_script_status});
