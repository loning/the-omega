\begin{remark}[Discriminant-Induced Companion Quadratic Fields: Quadratic Resolution of Recurrence Certificates]\label{rem:pom-discriminant-quadratic-field-tower}
For each integer \(q\ge 2\), let \(P_q(\lambda)\in\ZZ[\lambda]\) denote the minimal integer-coefficient recurrence characteristic polynomial for \(m\mapsto S_q(m)\).
Denote
\[
\Delta_q^{\mathrm{disc}}:=\mathrm{sqf}\!\bigl(\mathrm{Disc}(P_q)\bigr)\in\ZZ
\]
as the square-free part (with sign) of its discriminant, and define the companion quadratic field
\[
K_q^{\mathrm{disc}}:=\QQ\bigl(\sqrt{\Delta_q^{\mathrm{disc}}}\bigr).
\]
In the three low-order explicitly realizable cases \(q=2,3,4\), the recurrence characteristic polynomials are respectively
\[
P_2(\lambda)=\lambda^3-2\lambda^2-2\lambda+2,\qquad
P_3(\lambda)=\lambda^3-2\lambda^2-4\lambda+2,\qquad
P_4(\lambda)=\lambda^5-2\lambda^4-7\lambda^3-2\lambda+2,
\]
thus their quadratic resolution fingerprints can be directly read out as
\[
\Delta_2^{\mathrm{disc}}=37,\qquad
\Delta_3^{\mathrm{disc}}=3\cdot 47,\qquad
\Delta_4^{\mathrm{disc}}=-985219
\]
(where the discriminant for \(q=4\) is given by Proposition \ref{prop:pom-a4-galois-s5}).
Therefore the fourth-order collision kernel induces at the discriminant level a completely new imaginary quadratic direction
\(
K_4^{\mathrm{disc}}=\QQ(\sqrt{-985219})
\),
and is arithmetically strictly separated from the discriminant quadratic subfield \(K_2=\QQ(\sqrt{-1151})\) of the Newman edge-weight threshold certificate (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-a4t-newman-k2-class-number}).
This companion quadratic field sequence provides a unified interface for "the minimal recurrence not only determines the real spectral growth constant, but also fixes quadratic subdirections at the discriminant level"; its local fingerprints can be directly projected and audited via the splitting type over finite fields of \(P_q\) (Frobenius permutation type) (see the modular \(p\) splitting certificate chain in Proposition \ref{prop:pom-a4-galois-s5}).
\end{remark}
