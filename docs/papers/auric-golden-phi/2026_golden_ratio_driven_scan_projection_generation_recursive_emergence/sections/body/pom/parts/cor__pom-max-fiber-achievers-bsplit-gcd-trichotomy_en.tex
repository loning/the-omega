\begin{corollary}[\texorpdfstring{$\gcd$}{gcd} trichotomy and phase type readout for the maximal achiever splitting pairs]\label{cor:pom-max-fiber-achievers-bsplit-gcd-trichotomy}
Within the stable threshold interval, the greatest common divisor of the maximal achiever splitting pair \((d_{m,0},d_{m,1})\) satisfies a three-valued law:
\[
\boxed{\;
\gcd(d_{2k,0},d_{2k,1})=1\qquad(m=2k\ge 10),\;}
\]
\[
\boxed{\;
\gcd(d_{2k+1,0},d_{2k+1,1})
=\begin{cases}
F_{k+1},& (d_{2k+1,0},d_{2k+1,1})=(F_{k+1},F_{k+1})\quad(\text{balanced phase}),\\
2,& (d_{2k+1,0},d_{2k+1,1})\in\{(2F_{k-1},2F_k),(2F_k,2F_{k-1})\}\quad(\text{biased phase}).
\end{cases}}
\]
Therefore, from the splitting pair alone one can perform, at a purely arithmetic level: even/odd branch discrimination, balanced/biased phase type discrimination within the odd branch, and orientation readout of the bias exchange involution \(\tau(a,b)=(b,a)\) (determined by comparing the magnitudes of the two components).
\par
Furthermore, the sum of the splitting pair satisfies \(d_{m,0}+d_{m,1}=D_m\) (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-max-fiber}), which is strictly increasing in \(m\) (\(D_{2k}<D_{2k+1}<D_{2k+2}\)); hence within the stable window a single splitting pair can unambiguously recover the scale parameter \(m\) (equivalently \(k\)) and complete a phase--scale invertible readout.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For the even branch, Remark \ref{rem:pom-max-fiber-achievers-bsplit} gives \((d_{2k,0},d_{2k,1})\in\{(F_{k+1},F_k),(F_k,F_{k+1})\}\).
By the Euclidean algorithm,
\(
\gcd(F_{k+1},F_k)=\gcd(F_k+F_{k-1},F_k)=\gcd(F_{k-1},F_k)=\cdots=\gcd(F_1,F_0)=1
\),
yielding the first identity.
\par
For the odd branch, the balanced phase gives trivially \(\gcd(F_{k+1},F_{k+1})=F_{k+1}\);
the biased phase, by the simplified form from Remark \ref{rem:pom-max-fiber-achievers-bsplit}, is equivalent to \((2F_{k-1},2F_k)\) (or its swap), and \(\gcd(F_{k-1},F_k)=1\), hence \(\gcd(2F_{k-1},2F_k)=2\).
\par
For the final assertion: the closed forms \(D_{2k}=F_{k+2}\), \(D_{2k+1}=2F_{k+1}\) from Theorem \ref{thm:pom-max-fiber} immediately yield
\(
D_{2k}<D_{2k+1}<D_{2k+2}
\),
so the map \(m\mapsto D_m\) is strictly increasing, and hence \(D_m\) uniquely determines \(m\).
\end{proof}
