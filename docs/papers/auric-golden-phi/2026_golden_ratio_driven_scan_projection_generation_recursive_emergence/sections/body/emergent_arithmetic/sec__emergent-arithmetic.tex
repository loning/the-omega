\section{Stable Arithmetic: Value-Preserving Rewriting, Normalized Transversals, and Resolution-Module Structure}\label{sec:emergent-arithmetic}

Building on the folding/normal-form mechanisms of Section \ref{sec:folding}, this section develops arithmetic organization aligned with stable-type interfaces. To avoid postulating ``natural numbers, addition, multiplication'' as primitive external objects, we first introduce Fibonacci congruences and value invariants on a free commutative monoid of scale counters, and prove that the quotient structure is isomorphic to \((\NN,+)\). We then construct an ``orbit-transversal (Zeckendorf)'' structure through value-preserving local rewrites in a groupoid embryo, define stable addition and stable multiplication on that transversal, and provide computable implementations with complexity interfaces. Finally, we place several parallel adder kernels (\(9/13/21\)-local) into a unified comparison framework as inputs for later spectral fingerprints and audit interfaces.
