\subsubsection{Invertible fingerprinting of the golden constant: recovering \texorpdfstring{$\varphi$}{phi} from constant-scale nonuniformity and phase-diagram ledgers under resolution deficiency}\label{subsubsec:discussion-phi-inversion-phase-ledger}
Most ``auditable interfaces'' in this work do not rely on long-range fitting or full-distribution detail; instead, they compress structure into \emph{constant-scale} verifiable objects: these serve both as minimal fingerprints for mechanism equivalence and as hard anchors for budget/compression narratives.
This subsection supplements and emphasizes five especially high-value structures: they elevate \(\varphi\) from a background constant to an identifiable parameter \emph{invertible from a single scalar}, and rewrite ``resolution deficiency'' from explanatory language into computable phase diagrams and ledgers.

\paragraph{Single-scalar inversion of \texorpdfstring{$\varphi$}{phi}: strict monotone invertibility of the power-divergence limiting constant}
Let \(\mu_m\) be the pushforward output distribution of bin-fold, and let \(u_m\) be the uniform distribution on the stable layer (notation as in Corollary \ref{cor:fold-bin-two-point-limit-law} and Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-kl-constant}).
For any \(\alpha>1\), take the strictly convex function
\[
f_\alpha(t):=t^\alpha-1-\alpha(t-1)\qquad(t>0).
\]
Then its Csisz\'ar \(f\)-divergence limit
\(
D_{f_\alpha}^\infty=\lim_{m\to\infty}D_{f_\alpha}(\mu_m\Vert u_m)
\)
not only exists (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-f-divergence-collapse}), but is also closed-form with strict monotone invertibility: Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-phi-identifiable-power-divergence} states
\[
D_{f_\alpha}^\infty
=\frac{1}{5^{\alpha/2}}\Bigl(\varphi^{2\alpha-1}+\varphi^{\alpha-2}\Bigr)-1
=:\mathcal{D}_\alpha(\varphi),
\]
where \(\mathcal{D}_\alpha:(1,\infty)\to\RR\) is strictly increasing.
Hence, in this system, \(\varphi\) is not an externally imposed constant: it is uniquely recoverable by inversion from the single-scalar certificate \(D_{f_\alpha}^\infty\), becoming an \emph{auditable identifiable fingerprint} of mechanism parameters.

\paragraph{Quantile thresholds and R\'enyi-rate collapse: two-state degeneration at exponential scale, with all structure reduced to \texorpdfstring{$O(1)$}{O(1)} drift}
The two-state uniform asymptotics of bin-fold (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-bin-two-state-asymptotic}) and the two-point limit law (Corollary \ref{cor:fold-bin-two-point-limit-law}) show that, at leading exponential order, fiber size is determined only by the terminal two states.
This yields a piecewise-constant limit for tail-quantile thresholds (Corollary \ref{cor:fold-bin-quantile-threshold-constant}): the scaled quantile
\(
(\varphi/2)^m B^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(\varepsilon)
\)
jumps only between \(\{1,\varphi^{-1}\}\) as \(\varepsilon\) crosses the threshold \(\varepsilon_c=\varphi/\sqrt5\).
\par
A stronger compression fact is ``R\'enyi-rate collapse'': for any-order R\'enyi entropy \(H_q(\mu_m)\) of \(\mu_m\) (including Shannon), one has the common limit
\[
\frac{1}{m}H_q(\mu_m)\to\log\varphi
\qquad(q>0),
\]
with only constant-scale deviations (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-bin-renyi-rate-collapse}; see also the KL constant law in Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-kl-constant}).
Therefore, on the ``exponential scale,'' all detail degenerates to the same slope \(\log\varphi\), and auditable structure survives only in \(O(1)\) drift and two-state splitting; this provides narrative-independent hard anchors for subsequent budget/compression interpretation.

\paragraph{Rigid kinks of ``visible capacity--tail spectrum'': non-removable phase transitions at \texorpdfstring{$s=\varphi^{-1}$}{s=phi^{-1}} and \texorpdfstring{$s=1$}{s=1}}
To write ``how much information remains visible under resolution deficiency'' as a computable curve, one may use the degeneracy tail count
\(
N^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(t)
\)
and the continuous capacity curve
\(
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{bin,cont}}_m(T)
\)
which satisfy the strict identity
\(
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{bin,cont}}_m(T)=\int_0^T N^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(t)\,\dd t
\)
(Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-degeneracy-tail-capacity-kinks}).
Under the scaled variable \(s\), the tail-spectrum process and capacity curve converge to piecewise-constant/piecewise-linear limits, with two rigid kinks at
\[
s=\varphi^{-1},\qquad s=1
\]
(same proposition).
These two kinks provide a ``phase-diagram style'' minimal certificate: they are unforgeable boundary points of the mechanism, elevating visible capacity from an ``empirical phenomenon'' to an auditable piecewise-structural object.

\paragraph{Two-scale expansion of gauge-volume density \texorpdfstring{$g_m$}{gm}: exponentially small corrections in the volume--information ledger}
The fold-gauge group volume of bin-fold
\(
|\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m|=\prod_{w\in X_m}d_m^{\mathrm{bin}}(w)!
\)
induces gauge-volume density \(g_m:=2^{-m}\log|\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m|\);
on the other hand, the fiber information term
\(
\kappa_m=\mathbb{E}_{\mu_m}[\log d_m^{\mathrm{bin}}(W_m)]
\)
is the average logarithmic cost of ``resolution deficiency'' (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-kl-constant}).
The relation between them is not merely heuristic: Stirling with remainder gives a strict second-order expansion with explicit remainder bounds (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-bin-gauge-volume-stirling-second-order}), further reducible to a two-scale leading term (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-gauge-density-two-scale}):
\[
g_m=\kappa_m-1+\Theta\!\Bigl(\Bigl(\frac{\varphi}{2}\Bigr)^m\Bigr),
\]
with exponentially small errors that are offline verifiable.
Thus the difference between ``combinatorial volume (factorial product) \(\leftrightarrow\) information term (expected \(\log d\))'' is itself an auditable ledger: the main gap is fixed at \(-1\), and the remaining structure appears as exponentially small corrections.

\paragraph{Functoriality of general \texorpdfstring{$\alpha$}{alpha}-fold: embedding the golden branch into an auditable parametric category}
This work does not bind folding to the single point \(\varphi\): the general Ostrowski fold
\(\Fold_m^{(\alpha)}\)
is defined by ``weighted valuation \(N_\alpha\)\(\to\) Ostrowski normal form \(Z_\alpha\)\(\to\) truncation \(\pi_m^{(\alpha)}\)'' (Definition \ref{def:fold-alpha}), and satisfies idempotence, surjectivity, and multiscale compatibility
\[
\pi^{(\alpha)}_{m_2\to m_1}\circ \Fold^{(\alpha)}_{m_2}
=\Fold^{(\alpha)}_{m_1}\circ \pi^{(\alpha)}_{m_2\to m_1}
\qquad(m_2\ge m_1)
\]
(Proposition \ref{prop:fold-alpha-basic}-(4)).
More importantly, this commutativity has definability and uniqueness under the order ``normalize first, then truncate'' (Theorem \ref{thm:normalize-then-truncate-functorial}), fixing the normalization choice in cross-resolution comparisons as a verifiable functorial constraint.
\par
Accordingly, the constant certificates above (divergence-limit inversion, quantile thresholds and R\'enyi-rate collapse, tail-spectrum kinks, and the volume--information ledger) can be organized into an \(\alpha\)-parametrized family of audit-kernel objects; the golden branch is merely the simplest specialization with the most accessible closed forms, and can therefore be used as a protocol-layer normalization anchor.
