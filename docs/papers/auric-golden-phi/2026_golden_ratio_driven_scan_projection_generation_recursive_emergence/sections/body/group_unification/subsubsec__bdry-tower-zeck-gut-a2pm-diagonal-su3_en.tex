\paragraph{\texorpdfstring{$A_2(\pm)$}{A2(±)} Equal-Weight and Color Double-Copy Exclusion: Forcing of the Diagonal \texorpdfstring{$\mathfrak{su}(3)$}{su(3)}}\label{par:bdry-a2pm-isotropy-diagonal-su3}
Theorem \ref{thm:boundary-m10-b3c3-rigid-four-block-split} rigidly splits the \(21\) modes of the \(m=10\) boundary layer under the \(B_3/C_3\) root--Cartan dictionary into
\[
21\ =\ 6\ (\mathrm{short})\ \oplus\ 6\ (A_2(+))\ \oplus\ 6\ (A_2(-))\ \oplus\ 3\ (\mathrm{Cartan}),
\]
where the two copies \(A_2(\pm)\) are distinguished solely by their sign structure (Remark \ref{rem:boundary-m10-longroot-double-a2-sign-rigidity}).
Under the isotropic scan caliber (\S\ref{subsec:group-unification-audit-pointers}, the root-type splitting-ratio audit chain), define \(R_{pm}(\theta)\) as the ratio of effective length statistics of the two copies \(A_2(\pm)\) with respect to the Hessian metric \(g=\Sigma(\theta)^{-1}\) (see the corresponding paragraph in \S\ref{subsec:bdry-tower-zeck-gut}), and adopt \(R_{pm}(\theta)\approx 1\) as the ``equal-weight'' criterion.

\begin{proposition}[\texorpdfstring{$A_2(\pm)$}{A2(±)} Equal-Weight Involution and the Unique Visibility of the Diagonal \texorpdfstring{$\mathfrak{su}(3)$}{su(3)}]\label{prop:a2pm-isotropy-diagonal-su3}
Under the notation of Theorem \ref{thm:boundary-m10-b3c3-rigid-four-block-split}, let
\(
V_{+},V_{-}
\)
be the subspaces spanned by the root directions of \(A_2(+)\) and \(A_2(-)\), respectively (each of dimension \(6\)).
If at some unification point \(\theta^\ast\) the ``\texorpdfstring{$A_2(\pm)$}{A2(±)} equal-weight'' condition is promoted to a protocol symmetry constraint---namely, there exists a metric- and root-type-invariant isometric involution
\(
\iota:V_{+}\oplus V_{-}\to V_{+}\oplus V_{-}
\)
that exchanges the two blocks (\(\iota(V_{+})=V_{-}\), \(\iota(V_{-})=V_{+}\)), and the admissible continuous envelope depends only on the aforementioned invariants---then
in the strongest scenario where both blocks are endowed with \(\mathfrak{su}(3)\)-type structure,
the exchange involution induces an automorphism \((X,Y)\mapsto (Y,X)\) on
\(
\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)
\),
whose fixed-point subalgebra is the diagonal embedding
\[
\mathfrak{su}(3)_{\mathrm{diag}}
:=\{(X,X):X\in\mathfrak{su}(3)\}\ \cong\ \mathfrak{su}(3).
\]
Therefore, when the equal-weight symmetry is required to be an observable invariant, the ``color-symmetric double copy'' \(\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)\) cannot be maintained as two independent factors at the visible level; its only visible non-abelian part degenerates to the diagonal \(\mathfrak{su}(3)\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The fixed-point set of the exchange involution \((X,Y)\mapsto(Y,X)\) is given by the equation \((X,Y)=(Y,X)\), i.e., \(\{(X,X)\}\), hence it is isomorphic to \(\mathfrak{su}(3)\) at the Lie algebra level.
If the observable structure of the continuous envelope depends only on the metric and root-type invariants, then \(A_2(+)\) and \(A_2(-)\) no longer carry distinguishable labels under the equal-weight symmetry; consequently, the independence of the two copies of \(\mathfrak{su}(3)\) cannot be audited through invariants, and their visible part is compressed to the fixed-point subalgebra of the exchange involution.
\end{proof}
