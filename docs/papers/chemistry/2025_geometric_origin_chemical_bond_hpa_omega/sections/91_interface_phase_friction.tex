\section{An optional interface template: phase stabilization under finite readout}
\label{app:interface_phase_friction}

This appendix records an \emph{interface-level} model that connects the scan--readout viewpoint to chemistry language (bonding, reactions, and free-energy descent). It is not used in the closed-theory derivations of $(\alpha_{\mathrm{geo}},\mu_{\mathrm{geo}})$, nor in the stability and scaling arguments of the main text; it is included as a constrained, falsifiable interface consistent with HPA phase thermodynamics.

\subsection{A minimal mismatch-augmented functional}
Let $R$ denote nuclear coordinates (collectively). Let $\psi$ be an electronic state normalized on the electronic Hilbert space at fixed $R$. Define the functional
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chem_free_energy_template}
\mathcal{F}[\psi;R]
:=
\braket{\psi|\hat H_{\mathrm{Coul}}(\alpha_{\mathrm{em}};R)|\psi}
\;+\;\lambda\,\mathcal{D}_\varepsilon[\psi;R]
\;-\;T_c\, S_{\mathrm{pf}}[\psi;\varepsilon],
\end{equation}
where $\hat H_{\mathrm{Coul}}$ is the standard Coulomb electronic Hamiltonian, $\mathcal{D}_\varepsilon$ is a resolution-dependent mismatch penalty, and $S_{\mathrm{pf}}$ is a phase-friction entropy proxy (e.g.\ discrepancy-based) \cite{Ma2025HPT}.
For fixed $R$, define the corresponding effective surface
\begin{equation}
E_{\mathrm{eff}}(R)=\inf_{\psi}\mathcal{F}[\psi;R].
\end{equation}
In the formal limit $\lambda\to 0$ and when the entropy term is negligible, $E_{\mathrm{eff}}$ reduces to the standard BO surface used in quantum chemistry.

\subsection{Bonding and reaction direction (interpretation)}
In standard chemistry language, a bond forms when the electronic energy is lowered by delocalization and exchange at an intermediate nuclear separation, producing a minimum in $E_{\mathrm{BO}}(R)$. In the HPA interface language, the same phenomenon can be phrased as:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Bond formation}: reconfiguration that lowers the total protocol cost by stabilizing phases (reducing mismatch accumulation) under finite readout.
\item \textbf{Reaction direction}: evolution toward configurations that decrease the effective functional \eqref{eq:chem_free_energy_template}, aligning with a free-energy descent structure but expressed via phase-friction entropy and mismatch penalties \cite{Ma2025HPT,Ma2025CAP}.
\end{itemize}
The purpose of \eqref{eq:chem_free_energy_template} is falsifiability: once a concrete $\mathcal{D}_\varepsilon$ and $S_{\mathrm{pf}}$ are specified, the implied corrections to molecular levels must exhibit structured parameter dependence tied to readout resolution and discrepancy, rather than arbitrary higher-order operators.

\subsection{A minimal computable toy choice (for falsifiability bookkeeping)}
To make \eqref{eq:chem_free_energy_template} operational without committing to a full microscopic derivation, we record a minimal interface that is explicit, low-parameter, and auditable:
\begin{itemize}
\item Choose a finite readout depth $N$ associated with resolution $\varepsilon$ (e.g.\ $N\sim \varepsilon^{-1}$ as a bookkeeping proxy).
\item Let the scan orbit be $x_n=x_0+n\nu\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 1)$ from Axiom~\ref{ax:O6}.
\item Define a phase-friction proxy by $S_{\mathrm{pf}}(N)=k_B\,E_N$ with $E_N=N D_N^\ast$ as in Section~\ref{sec:minimal_interface}.
\item Define a mismatch penalty as a scalar multiple of the same mismatch accumulation,
\[
\mathcal{D}_\varepsilon := E_N,
\]
so that the interface correction is controlled by a single effective coefficient $(\lambda - T_c k_B)$ at fixed $(\varepsilon,N)$.
\end{itemize}
This minimal choice fixes a concrete residual template so that any spectroscopy-facing comparison can be phrased in terms of an explicit mismatch variable $E_N$ rather than an unspecified functional; more detailed interfaces should reduce to an equivalent low-parameter form at leading order.
