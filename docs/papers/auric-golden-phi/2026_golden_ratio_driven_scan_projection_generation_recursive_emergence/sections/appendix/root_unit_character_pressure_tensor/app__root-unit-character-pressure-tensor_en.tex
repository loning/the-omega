\subsection{Completion Orbit Module at Roots of Unity and Character Spectral Decomposition}\label{subsec:root-unit-completion-orbit-character}

Take the unit circle twist \(u=e^{\mathrm{i}\theta}\), the half-angle variable \(r=e^{\mathrm{i}\theta/2}\), and the completion coordinate
\(s=r+r^{-1}=2\cos(\theta/2)\in[-2,2]\), and set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chebyshev-adams-Cm}
C_m(s):=r^m+r^{-m}=2\cos(m\theta/2)\in\ZZ[s],
\end{equation}
which is the Chebyshev--Adams operation (consistent with \S\ref{subsec:unit-disk-witt-chebyshev} and Corollary \ref{cor:primitive-completion-hatp}).

For any integer \(q\ge 3\), define the finite \textbf{completion orbit at roots of unity}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:root-unit-orbit-Sq}
\mathcal{S}_q:=\Bigl\{\, s_a:=2\cos\!\Bigl(\frac{\pi a}{q}\Bigr) : a=1,2,\dots,q-1 \Bigr\}\subset[-2,2].
\end{equation}
Then \(\mathcal{S}_q\) is closed under all maps \(s\mapsto C_m(s)\) and satisfies the permutation law
\begin{equation}\label{eq:root-unit-Cm-permutation}
C_m(s_a)=2\cos\!\Bigl(\frac{\pi m a}{q}\Bigr)=s_{\langle ma\rangle},
\end{equation}
where \(\langle ma\rangle\) denotes the unique representative under the identification \(a\sim 2q-a\) (evenness of cosine).

Let \(\CC^{\mathcal{S}_q}\) be the function space on \(\mathcal{S}_q\), and define the finite-dimensional representation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:root-unit-Rm}
(\mathsf{R}_m F)(s):=F(C_m(s)),\qquad F\in\CC^{\mathcal{S}_q}.
\end{equation}
Then \(\{\mathsf{R}_m\}_{m\ge 1}\) is a finite-dimensional representation induced by a multiplicative semigroup action, whose irreducible decomposition is diagonalized by the even Dirichlet characters modulo \(2q\) (those satisfying \(\chi(-1)=1\)).

\begin{theorem}[Character diagonalization at roots of unity]\label{thm:root-unit-character-diagonalization}
For any \(\sigma>0\), define the ``completion--Witt dilation'' regularized operator on \(\CC^{\mathcal{S}_q}\) by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:root-unit-W-sigma}
(\mathsf{W}_{\sigma,q}F)(s):=\sum_{m\ge 1}\frac{1}{m^{1+\sigma}}\,F(C_m(s)).
\end{equation}
Then for each even Dirichlet character \(\chi\) modulo \(2q\), there exists a nonzero vector \(v_\chi\in\CC^{\mathcal{S}_q}\) such that
\[
\mathsf{W}_{\sigma,q}v_\chi=L(1+\sigma,\chi)\,v_\chi,
\]
where \(L(1+\sigma,\chi)=\sum_{m\ge 1}\chi(m)m^{-(1+\sigma)}\) is the Dirichlet \(L\)-series. Moreover, \(\CC^{\mathcal{S}_q}\) decomposes as a direct sum of these even-character eigenspaces (under the equivariant subspace identification \(a\sim -a\)).
\end{theorem}

This result yields a systematic generation law for discrete orthogonal channels within the framework: when the continuous phase is compressed to the set of roots of unity, the dilation chain no longer exhibits the endpoint tristate degeneracy (the set \(\mathcal{S}_\star=\{0,2,-2\}\) of Appendix \ref{app:unit-circle-phase-gate}), but automatically lifts to a complete character spectrum, producing controllable finite-dimensional orthogonal channels as \(q\) increases.

\subsection{Algebraic Spectral Curve of the Gauge Anomaly Pressure Function}\label{subsec:gauge-pressure-algebraic-curve}

The manuscript expresses the exponential moment-generating function of the bitwise gauge anomaly mismatch as the spectral radius of a finite-dimensional weighted adjacency matrix (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure}): there exists a matrix \(A_\theta\) such that the pressure function satisfies
\[
P_G(\theta)=\log\rho(A_\theta),
\]
where \(A_\theta\) is given in explicit form in the main text.

\begin{theorem}[Quartic algebraic equation of the pressure spectral curve]\label{thm:gauge-pressure-quartic}
Set \(x=e^\theta>0\) and let \(y=\rho(A_\theta)\). Then \(y\) is the largest positive real root of the following quartic equation:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gauge-pressure-quartic}
8y^4-4y^3-(4x+2)y^2+(2x-x^3)y+x=0.
\end{equation}
Consequently, \(P_G(\theta)\) is the logarithm of an algebraic function branch; its global regularity and convexity can be determined at the level of this algebraic curve via its discriminant and branching-point structure.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Closed-form expression of the tilted mean mismatch rate]\label{cor:gauge-pressure-tilt-derivative}
Let \(F(y,x)=8y^4-4y^3-(4x+2)y^2+(2x-x^3)y+x\). Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gauge-pressure-P-prime}
P_G'(\theta)=-\frac{x\,\partial_x F(y,x)}{y\,\partial_y F(y,x)}
=\frac{x\bigl(4y^2-(2-3x^2)y-1\bigr)}{y\bigl(32y^3-12y^2-(8x+4)y+(2x-x^3)\bigr)}.
\end{equation}
This expression reduces the ``tilted mean of the gauge anomaly'' entirely to an algebraic function of \((x,y)\), thereby unifying the statistical output of the gauge anomaly (mean, variance, deviation law) within an auditable algebraic spectral curve mechanism.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Orthogonal Double-Residual Decomposition of the Dilation Chain: Tensorization Closed Loop of Character Spectral Channel and Precision Potential Ledger Channel}\label{subsec:dilation-character-precision-tensor}

The manuscript introduces the Witt--dilation operator on Dirichlet variables (\S\ref{app:real-input-40-dirichlet}):
\[
(Wf)(s):=\sum_{m\ge 1}\frac{1}{m}\,f(ms),
\]
and establishes the strict identity \(\log\mathcal{Z}(s)=(W\mathcal{P})(s)\) together with the M\"obius inversion
\(\mathcal{P}(s)=\sum_{m\ge 1}\frac{\mu(m)}{m}\,\log\mathcal{Z}(ms)\).

Interpreting the ``scale chain'' as translational convolution along the depth line and combining it with the character representation of \S\ref{subsec:root-unit-completion-orbit-character} yields a tensorization closed loop requiring no additional structure.

\begin{theorem}[Character--precision orthogonal tensorization]\label{thm:character-precision-tensor}
Take the depth coordinate \(y\in\RR\) with translation representation \(T_{\log m}g(y)=g(y+\log m)\), and the character representation \(\mathsf{R}_m\) on the completion orbit module at roots of unity (Theorem \ref{thm:root-unit-character-diagonalization}). For \(\sigma>0\), define the joint dilation operator
\[
\mathcal{K}_{\sigma,q}:=\sum_{m\ge 1}\frac{1}{m^{1+\sigma}}\,T_{\log m}\otimes \mathsf{R}_m
\quad\text{acting on}\quad L^2(\RR,\dd y)\otimes \CC^{\mathcal{S}_q}.
\]
Then, under the character direct-sum decomposition of Theorem \ref{thm:root-unit-character-diagonalization}, \(\mathcal{K}_{\sigma,q}\) decomposes into a direct sum of scalar convolution operators:
\[
\mathcal{K}_{\sigma,q}\ \simeq\ \bigoplus_{\chi(-1)=1}\ \Bigl(\sum_{m\ge 1}\frac{\chi(m)}{m^{1+\sigma}}\,T_{\log m}\Bigr),
\]
and for each even-character component, the ``zero-frequency'' eigenvalue is \(L(1+\sigma,\chi)\).

Consequently, the residual space of the dilation chain admits a verifiable orthogonal bisection within the framework:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Character spectral channel} (finite-dimensional): controlled by \(q\), producing the discrete spectrum of \(L(1+\sigma,\chi)\);
  \item \textbf{Precision potential ledger channel} (infinite-dimensional): the continuous channel of depth translations, carrying the compressed precision budget.
\end{enumerate}
This orthogonal decomposition is consistent with the manuscript's layering principle of ``integer-layer audit first, spectral-layer index second'': the integer layer (e.g., Frobenius defect and Adams structure) locks in realizability and consistency, while the dilation residuals of the spectral layer are separated into the character spectral channel and the precision potential ledger channel for verifiable channelized analysis.
\end{theorem}
