\begin{abstract}
General Relativity, based on Riemannian geometry and continuous manifolds, encounters an essential singularity crisis in extreme gravitational fields (such as $r \to 0$ in black holes). This paper applies the \textbf{Holographic Polar Arithmetic (HPA)} established in Paper I to spacetime physics, proposing a non-perturbative quantum gravity geometric model—\textbf{Omega Dynamics}. Under this framework, we re-derive the Schwarzschild metric, demonstrating that gravity is not merely spacetime curvature but a ``Phase Mismatch'' between high-dimensional $\Theta$ scanning and low-dimensional projection grids. We prove that the singularity within the Schwarzschild radius is not a geometric collapse but a conformal inversion of spacetime topology from the ``South Pole'' (physical origin) to the ``North Pole'' (holographic boundary). The center of a black hole is a topological throat leading to the holographic horizon.
\end{abstract}
