\subsection{Epstein--Glaser causal perturbation theory (EG) as the strong-closure carrier}
\label{app:eg_causal_perturbation_framework}

\AuditTag This appendix fixes the mathematical carrier framework for the \emph{strong-closure} layer of EFT quantization/renormalization tasks (T1S).
We adopt the Epstein--Glaser (causal) perturbative construction as the default proof-bearing framework, because it packages renormalization as a controlled extension problem (with explicit freedom) and supports algebraic gauge/BRST constraints at the level of time-ordered products.

\subsubsection{Local fields, test functions, and time-ordered products}
\paragraph{Local functionals and smeared interaction.}
Let $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ denote the space of local polynomial functionals in the classical fields (including ghosts and auxiliary fields as needed), modulo total derivatives.
For $F\in \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ and a test function $g\in \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{M}^{1,3})$ (compact support), write the smeared functional as
\[
F(g):=\int_{\mathbb{M}^{1,3}} F(x)\,g(x)\,\dd^4 x.
\]

\paragraph{Time-ordered products.}
EG starts from a family of multilinear maps (time-ordered products)
\[
T_n:\ \underbrace{\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{loc}}\times\cdots\times\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{loc}}}_{n}\ \longrightarrow\ \mathcal{F}
\qquad (n\ge 0),
\]
where $\mathcal{F}$ is a suitable algebra of (renormalized) local functionals or operator-valued distributions after quantization.
We use the convention $T_0=1$ and $T_1(F)=F$.

\subsubsection{Causality and the inductive construction}
\paragraph{Causal factorization (core axiom).}
For $F_1,\ldots,F_n\in\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ with supports separated by a Cauchy surface such that $\mathrm{supp}(F_1,\ldots,F_k)$ is later than $\mathrm{supp}(F_{k+1},\ldots,F_n)$, EG imposes
\[
T_n(F_1,\ldots,F_n)=T_k(F_1,\ldots,F_k)\,T_{n-k}(F_{k+1},\ldots,F_n).
\]
This axiom allows an inductive construction of $T_n$ off the total diagonal $x_1=\cdots=x_n$.

\paragraph{Extension problem and renormalization freedom.}
At each order $n$, the inductive step reduces to extending a distribution defined on configuration space with the diagonals removed to a distribution on all of configuration space.
The ambiguity of extension is local and is parameterized by finite-dimensional counterterm data at each $n$ (the ``finite renormalization freedom'').

\subsubsection{Power counting via scaling degree}
\paragraph{Scaling degree (Steinmann).}
Let $t$ be a distribution on $\mathbb{R}^d\setminus\{0\}$.
Its scaling degree at $0$ is
\[
\mathrm{sd}(t):=\inf\Bigl\{\delta\in\mathbb{R}\ \Bigm|\ \lim_{\lambda\to 0^+}\lambda^\delta\, t(\lambda \cdot)=0\Bigr\}.
\]
If $\mathrm{sd}(t)<d$, the extension to $\mathbb{R}^d$ is unique; if $\mathrm{sd}(t)\ge d$, extensions exist but are non-unique with a finite-dimensional ambiguity.

\paragraph{EFT truncation interface.}
\InterfaceTag In this paper, the EFT representative is truncated by operator dimension and/or loop order (Appendix~\ref{app:eft_representative_construction}).
The EG scaling-degree bookkeeping provides a theorem-facing meaning for ``finite counterterm freedom at fixed truncation order'' and is the carrier for strong-closure statements of the form ``there exists a renormalized time-ordered product consistent with the truncation and constraints''.

\subsubsection{S-matrix as a formal power series}
\paragraph{Formal S-matrix.}
Given a local interaction functional $V\in\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{loc}}$, define the formal S-matrix (as a power series in the coupling constant) by
\[
S(V):=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\iu^n}{n!}\,T_n(V,\ldots,V).
\]
In the EFT setting we only require this expansion up to a fixed order consistent with the truncation $N$.

\subsubsection{Strong-closure role for this manuscript}
\paragraph{Carrier choice.}
\MathTag We will use EG to replace ``assume a BRST-invariant quantization map'' by a concrete theorem chain: existence of $T_n$ satisfying causality plus additional normalization conditions encoding symmetries.

\paragraph{Mapping to the paper's EFT representative.}
\InterfaceTag The input interaction functional $V$ is taken from the finite candidate family defined by the CAP-minimal representative selection in Appendix~\ref{app:eft_representative_construction}.
The strong-closure tasks (Appendices~\ref{app:eg_brst_st_consistency}--\ref{app:eg_anomaly_counterterm_restoration}) upgrade gauge/BRST and anomaly constraints from interface filters to theorem-level constraints on the admissible renormalization freedoms in EG.

\subsubsection{Scope boundary (explicit)}
\AuditTag This appendix fixes the framework and core objects.
It does \emph{not} by itself prove existence of a nonperturbative 4D interacting QFT, nor asymptotic completeness, nor any constructive continuum limit outside perturbation theory.
Those remain separate strong-closure targets (see the roadmap and Appendix~\ref{app:renormalization_dictionary_and_boundaries}).
