\section{Variation--limit exchange and Yang--Mills equations (theorem-facing layer)}
\label{app:variation_limit_exchange_and_yang_mills_eom}

This appendix records the theorem-facing step that connects variational convergence (e.g.\ $\Gamma$-limits) to Euler--Lagrange equations.
It is the point where one can legitimately say that the continuum Yang--Mills equations arise as limits of discrete stationary points/minimizers, rather than as a representative dictionary.

\subsection{A standard $\Gamma$-convergence consequence}
\begin{theorem}[Minimizers under $\Gamma$-convergence (standard template)]
\label{thm:gamma_convergence_minimizers_template}
\MathTag Let $S_k$ be a sequence of functionals on a configuration space $\mathcal{X}$ that $\Gamma$-converges to a functional $S$.
Assume equi-coercivity so that minimizer sequences are precompact in the chosen topology.
If $u_k$ is a (near-)minimizer of $S_k$, then any limit point $u$ of $(u_k)$ is a minimizer of $S$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Reference]
This is standard in the calculus of variations; see, e.g., \cite{DalMaso1993GammaConvergence}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{From limit minimizers to Euler--Lagrange equations}
\begin{assumption}[Differentiability/weak-EL bundle]
\label{ass:weak_el_bundle}
\MathTag Assume the limit functional $S$ is sufficiently regular on the declared function space (or admits a well-posed weak first-variation) so that the condition $\delta S=0$ yields the Euler--Lagrange equations in the intended weak sense.
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[Yang--Mills equations as limit stationarity (template)]
\label{prop:ym_eom_as_limit_stationarity_template}
\MathTag Assume:
\begin{itemize}
\item the holonomy-to-connection convergence bundle (Appendix~\ref{app:holonomy_to_connection_convergence_theorems}),
\item the Wilson-to-Yang--Mills $\Gamma$-limit theorem (Theorem~\ref{thm:gamma_limit_wilson_to_yang_mills_template}),
\item the differentiability/weak-EL bundle (Assumption~\ref{ass:weak_el_bundle}).
\end{itemize}
Then the continuum Yang--Mills Euler--Lagrange equations recorded in Proposition~\ref{prop:ym_equation} follow as the stationarity condition of the $\Gamma$-limit action, and are consistent with the limit of discrete (near-)stationary points in the declared refinement family.
\end{proposition}

\AuditTag Proposition~\ref{prop:ym_eom_as_limit_stationarity_template} is the theorem-level bridge that upgrades ``dictionary correspondence'' to ``limit consequence''.
If any prerequisite fails, the manuscript must downgrade the Yang--Mills equations to representative-level consequences of the CAP-selected action skeleton (Appendix~\ref{app:cap_continuum_action_closure}).
