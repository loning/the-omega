\begin{conclusion}[Alignment of the Mordell--Weil free generator and low multiples on branch fibers in the minimal model: a unified representation of \texorpdfstring{$(R,Q_0,Q_0',P)$}{(R,Q0,Q0',P)}]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-mw-generator-alignment}
Consider the minimal integral Weierstrass model
\[
E_0:\ y_0^2+y_0=x^3-x,
\]
which corresponds to the short model \(E\) of Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-normalization} via \(X=x\), \(Y=y_0+\tfrac12\).
From the standard Mordell--Weil data of this curve (Cremona label \(37\mathrm{a}1\), see \cite{LMFDBEllipticCurve37a1}),
\(E_0(\QQ)\) is torsion-free with \(\mathrm{rank}=1\), and one may take the free generator \(R_0=(0,0)\).
Denote its corresponding point in \(E(\QQ)\) by
\[
R=(0,\tfrac12).
\]
Then the following low-multiple relations are completely explicit:
\[
[2]R=\left(1,\tfrac12\right)=-Q_0,\qquad
[3]R=\left(-1,-\tfrac12\right)=-Q_0',\qquad
[4]R=\left(2,-\tfrac52\right)=P,
\]
where \(Q_0=(1,-\tfrac12)\), \(Q_0'=(-1,\tfrac12)\), and \(P=(2,-\tfrac52)\) is the physical base point in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-rational-points-denominator-law}.
Since \(E(\QQ)\) is torsion-free, the equation \([4]G=P\) has the unique solution \(G=R\) in \(E(\QQ)\).
\par
By Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-lattes-doubling}, the duplication Latt\`es self-map \(\Phi\) (in the \(x\)-coordinate) satisfies, for every \(G\in E(\overline{\QQ})\),
\(
x([2]G)=\Phi\bigl(x(G)\bigr)
\);
hence
\[
x(P)=x([4]R)=\Phi^{\circ 2}\!\bigl(x(R)\bigr),
\qquad
x(R)=0,\ \Phi(0)=1,\ \Phi(1)=2.
\]
Moreover,
\(
y(Q_0)=0,\ y(Q_0')=1
\),
and \(-Q_0=(1,\tfrac12)\), \(-Q_0'=(-1,-\tfrac12)\) provide the other rational point on the fibers \(y=1\) and \(y=0\), respectively.
Combined with the double-fiber points in \(\mathrm{div}(y)\) and \(\mathrm{div}(y-1)\) from Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-normalization}, one sees that
\(Q_0\) and \(Q_0'\) are precisely the rational branch points of the cover \(y:E\to\mathbb P^1_y\) at \(y=0,1\).
Therefore \((R,Q_0,Q_0',P)\), as low multiples on the orbit of a single free generator, provides a unified representation of ``generator--branch fiber--physical base point''.
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The displayed coordinate identities follow directly from duplication/addition formulas; branch-point identification follows from the divisor certificate in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-normalization} (\(y=0\) is double at \(X=1\), and \(y=1\) is double at \(X=-1\)).

\end{proof}

\begin{conclusion}[Halving and \(2\)-divisibility of the physical base point: uniqueness of the rational half-point]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-physical-basepoint-halving-uniqueness}
Retain the notation of Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-mw-generator-alignment}, with physical base point \(P=(2,-\tfrac52)\).
Then there exists a unique \(Q\in E(\QQ)\) such that \([2]Q=P\), namely
\[
Q=\Bigl(1,\frac12\Bigr)=-Q_0.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
[2]^{-1}(\{\pm P\})\cap E(\QQ)=\{\pm Q\}.
\]
Hence \(P\in 2E(\QQ)\), and \(P\) is not primitive in the Mordell--Weil group \(E(\QQ)\).
Since \(E(\QQ)_{\mathrm{tors}}=\{O\}\) (Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-rational-points-denominator-law}), \(Q\) has infinite order.
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
From Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-mw-generator-alignment}, let \(Q:=[2]R=(1,\tfrac12)\) and \(P=[4]R\), so \([2]Q=[4]R=P\).
If \(Q'\in E(\QQ)\) also satisfies \([2]Q'=P\), then \(Q'-Q\in E[2](\QQ)\subseteq E(\QQ)_{\mathrm{tors}}\), hence \(Q'=Q\).
The case \([2]Q'=-P\) similarly gives \(Q'=-Q\).
Finally, \(E(\QQ)_{\mathrm{tors}}=\{O\}\) implies \(Q\neq O\) has infinite order, and the \(2\)-divisibility of \(P\) immediately yields its non-primitivity. QED.
\end{proof}
