\section{PF/Parry and zeta interfaces}\label{app:pf-parry-zeta}

This appendix records standard derivations used for the cover interface in Section~\ref{sec:sofic-cover}.

\subsection{Entropy of SFTs}\label{app:sft-entropy}
Let $X_A$ be an irreducible SFT presented by a $0$--$1$ adjacency matrix $A$.
Then the topological entropy is
\[
h_{\mathrm{top}}(X_A)=\log\rho(A),
\]
where $\rho(A)$ is the Perron--Frobenius eigenvalue \cite{LindMarcus1995,Kitchens1998SymbolicDynamics}.

\subsection{Parry measure}\label{app:parry-measure}
Let $l,r>0$ be PF eigenvectors normalized by $l^{\mathsf T}r=1$.
The Parry measure is the stationary Markov measure on edges defined by the weights and transition kernel given in Proposition~\ref{prop:parry}.
It is the unique measure of maximal entropy on an irreducible SFT.

\subsection{Zeta determinant formula}\label{app:zeta-det}
For an SFT $X_A$, periodic point counts satisfy $\#\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^n)=\mathrm{Tr}(A^n)$.
The Artin--Mazur zeta function is
\[
\zeta_{X_A}(z)=\exp\!\left(\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{\#\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^n)}{n}z^n\right)
=\exp\!\left(\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{\mathrm{Tr}(A^n)}{n}z^n\right)
=\frac{1}{\det(I-zA)},
\]
where the last identity follows from $\log\det(I-zA)=-\sum_{n\ge 1}\mathrm{Tr}(A^n)z^n/n$ for $\abs{z}$ sufficiently small.
See \cite{LindMarcus1995} for details.
