\begin{theorem}[Perturbative (EG/pAQFT) identification of the gauge theory (template)]
\label{thm:perturbative_identification_eg_template}
\MathTag Fix a truncation order (operator dimension and/or loop order) and a local interaction functional $V$ from the declared EFT candidate family (Appendix~\ref{app:eft_representative_construction}).
Assume:
\begin{itemize}
\item the causal perturbation framework hypotheses of Appendix~\ref{app:eg_causal_perturbation_framework},
\item BRST/ST consistency to the declared order (Appendix~\ref{app:eg_brst_st_consistency}),
\item anomaly filters hold for the declared gauge group and chiral content (Appendix~\ref{app:anomaly_theorem_filters}),
\item admissible counterterm freedom is characterized within the declared normalization basis (Appendix~\ref{app:eg_normalization_freedom_basis}) and remainder budgets are tracked (Appendix~\ref{app:strong_eft_remainder_bounds}).
\end{itemize}
Then the perturbative time-ordered products can be chosen so that the Slavnov--Taylor identities are satisfied to the declared order, and the resulting perturbative dynamics agree with the continuum gauge-theory representative in the sense of the EG/pAQFT carrier.
\end{theorem}
