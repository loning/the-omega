\section{Appendix E: Complete Enumeration Properties for Base Case \texorpdfstring{$m=6$}{m=6} (Usable for Full Enumeration Verification)}
\label{app:m6-enum}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Steady-state scale for $m=6$}{Steady-state scale for m=6}}

\[
\card{X_6^{\mathrm Z}}=F_8=21,\qquad \card{\{0,1\}^6}=64.
\]

\subsection{Boundary States and Twofold Preimages (Integer Folding Version)}

Define boundary states as legal words with both ends being $1$:
\[
X^{\mathrm{bdry}}_6=\{100001,\ 101001,\ 100101\}.
\]
Under the integer folding version (Zeckendorf first 6 bits of $N\in\{0,\dots,63\}$ as output), the above three each have twofold preimages, and the two preimages differ by $34$ (Fibonacci weight $F_9$).

\subsection{Preimage Size Spectrum (Finite Enumeration Invariant)}

For $m=6$, preimage sizes of stable states take only values in $\{2,3,4\}$, satisfying fixed counts:
\[
\card{\{x\in X_6^{\mathrm Z}:\ \card{\mathcal{F}_6(x)}=2\}}=8,\quad
\card{\{x\in X_6^{\mathrm Z}:\ \card{\mathcal{F}_6(x)}=3\}}=4,\quad
\card{\{x\in X_6^{\mathrm Z}:\ \card{\mathcal{F}_6(x)}=4\}}=9.
\]
The above facts can be directly verified by exhaustive computation of $\mathrm{Fold}_6$ over all $2^6$ inputs.
