\subsection{Quantum Gap and Projection Discrepancy}
In Paper I, we established that the superposition of two polar states $\Psi_A + \Psi_B$ is not perfectly closed under unitary $\Theta$-scanning. According to the Theory of Uniform Distribution \cite{Weyl1916, KuipersNiederreiter1974}, for a sequence of $N$ points, the discrepancy scales as:
\begin{equation}
    D_N \propto \frac{\log N}{N}
\end{equation}

Unlike Entropic Gravity \cite{Verlinde2011}, which relies on thermodynamic statistics, Phase Pressure is a number-theoretic force driving matter toward regions of maximal scanning density to minimize projection error.

\begin{equation}

This implies that in Omega Dynamics, mass is not continuous but quantized in units of the Planck mass, consistent with the hypothesis of Primordial Black Holes as the fundamental constituents of matter.
\end{remark}

A massive object is thus a \textbf{Phase Monopole}. It introduces a permanent phase lag $\delta \Phi = 2M$ in the $\Theta$-scanning of its local coordinate patch. This lag forces the surrounding projection grid to compress radially, yielding the spatial metric deformation:
\begin{equation}
