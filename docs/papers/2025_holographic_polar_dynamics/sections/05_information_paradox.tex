\subsection{Hawking Radiation as Decrypted Phase}
Hawking radiation \cite{Hawking1975} is traditionally viewed as thermal noise, leading to the information loss paradox. In Omega Dynamics, it represents the \textbf{readout} of the stored phase information. As the black hole evaporates (radius shrinks), the stored angular phase is projected back into linear space.
The sequence of emitted photons follows a \textbf{Sturmian Sequence} of phases, which encodes the precise clear-text of the matter that formed the black hole. As shown in symbol dynamics \cite{MorseHedlund1940}, Sturmian sequences have minimal complexity among non-periodic sequences, making them the optimal carrier for maximum entropy compression. Thus, the information is preserved in the phase correlations of the radiation, consistent with the Page curve \cite{Page1993}, resolving the paradox.
The Black Hole is not a trash compactor; it is a \textbf{Holographic Hard Drive}.
