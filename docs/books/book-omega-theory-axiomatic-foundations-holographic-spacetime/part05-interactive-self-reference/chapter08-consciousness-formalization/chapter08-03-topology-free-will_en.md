### 8.3.1 Energy Degeneracy and Causal Gap

Recalling Section 8.1, when the cosmic QCA evolves to a Gödelian deadlock point, the system Hamiltonian $\hat{H}$ exhibits energy level degeneracy. Let $|\Psi_t\rangle$ be the current state, with two possible evolution branches $|A\rangle$ and $|B\rangle$ for the next step, satisfying:

$$\langle A | \hat{H} | A \rangle = \langle B | \hat{H} | B \rangle = E$$

In standard physics, since $\Delta E = 0$, the system has no dynamical reason to favor either one. This is a **symmetry point**.

However, in Omega Theory's discrete Penrose tiling, although $|A\rangle$ and $|B\rangle$ have the same energy, their **holographic topological structures** are fundamentally different.

* State $|A\rangle$ may correspond to a tiling pattern that causes the grid to develop "cracks" or infinite loops $N$ steps in the future.
* State $|B\rangle$ may correspond to a tiling pattern that can maintain Fibonacci growth and logical self-consistency.

Due to the locality of QCA, the Hamiltonian $\hat{H}_{local}$ cannot perceive this long-range topological difference. This is the **Causal Gap**: physical laws (algorithms) fail here. This leaves operational space for non-algorithmic **free will**.

Free will does not create energy "out of nothing" (which would violate the first law of thermodynamics) but performs **geometric navigation on isoenergy surfaces**.

### 8.3.2 Selection Mechanism of Homotopy Classes

We treat evolution paths in Hilbert space as curves on manifolds. Two paths $\gamma_1, \gamma_2$ belong to the same **homotopy class** if they can be continuously deformed into each other.
However, due to **topological defects** in the Omega grid (such as black hole singularities or logical paradox points), phase space is not simply connected. This means there exist different, topologically inequivalent evolution sectors.

The observer's (consciousness's) choice in the present is actually reading boundary conditions of future holographic screens. This explains the "readiness potential" paradox observed in Benjamin Libet's experiments—consciousness seems to "decide" after neural activity, but in Omega Theory, this reflects that consciousness operates **at the slice level of the four-dimensional block universe**, not at a single instant of linear time.
