\section{A Discrete Minimal Model Amenable to Exhaustive Verification: Reversible Ontology and Irreversible Observation}

This section provides a completely discrete and exhaustively verifiable prototype to demonstrate the auditable mechanism of ``microscopic reversibility + non-injective observation $\Rightarrow$ macroscopic multi-valuedness and stable frequency laws.'' This prototype serves not as an inevitable law at the object layer, but merely as a reproducibly verifiable platform.

\subsection{Primitive State Space and Observation Kernel (D8.1)}

Fix $m$ and let the raw window label space be $Y_m:=\{0,1\}^m$. The observation kernel is $\Fold_m:Y_m\to X_m$. Define the fiber degeneracy
\[
d(x):=\card{\mathcal{F}_m(x)},\qquad x\in X_m.
\]

\subsection{Reversible Update and Pushforward Distribution (D8.2, T8.2)}

Let the microscopic update $U:Y_m\to Y_m$ be a permutation (implementable via Feistel-type local construction, see Appendix \ref{app:feistel}), hence $U$ preserves uniform distribution. Let $u$ be the uniform distribution on $Y_m$; then the visible distribution
\[
q:= (\Fold_m)_*(u)
\]
satisfies
\[
q(x)=\frac{d(x)}{2^m}.
\]
Furthermore, for any microscopic distribution $\pi$, total variation of pushforward satisfies non-increase:
\[
\big\|(\Fold_m)_*(\pi)-q\big\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
\le \|\pi-u\|_{\mathrm{TV}}.
\]
Hence, under the condition of ``microscopic near-uniform mixing,'' the steady-state frequency is dominated by the degeneracy spectrum. This conclusion provides a null model reference for subsequent experimental templates: if the observed system frequency significantly deviates from the degeneracy spectrum prediction, it must be explained by mechanisms such as non-uniform mixing, non-permutation dynamics, or observation kernel deviation.
