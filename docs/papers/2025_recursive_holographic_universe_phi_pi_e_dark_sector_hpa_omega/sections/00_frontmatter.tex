
\begin{abstract}
We propose a discrete-readout cosmology in which spacetime is not a prior continuum, but an emergent geometry produced by a one-dimensional scan projected onto a Hilbert-addressed grid \cite{Hilbert1891,Sagan1994}.
At the minimal testable local resolution, the readout hardware is modeled by a $6$-bit microstate space $\Omega_6:=\{0,1\}^6$ with $|\Omega_6|=64$ and linearization $\mathcal{H}_6:=\ell^2(\Omega_6)$.

Stability is organized by three commuting constraint channels, denoted $\varphi$--$\pi$--$\e$.
The $\varphi$-channel is a Zeckendorf (golden-mean) grammar constraint forbidding adjacent ones, selecting a $21$-dimensional stable sector $X_6\subset\Omega_6$ \cite{Zeckendorf1972,LindMarcus1995}.
The $\pi$-channel refines $X_6$ by cyclic closure and produces a canonical split $21=18\oplus 3$ into cyclic states and boundary states.
The $\e$-channel expresses analytic stability via an Abel--zeta pole-barrier viewpoint and connects naturally to a pressure-like weighting mechanism at higher resolution \cite{ArtinMazur1965,ParryPollicott1990,Ruelle1978}.

The key bridge to cosmology is a computable resolution-folding map $\mathrm{Fold}_6:\{0,\dots,63\}\twoheadrightarrow X_6$ with fully explicit preimages and a rigid $(2,3,4)$ degeneracy histogram.
We interpret the $64-21=43$ hidden degrees of freedom as ghost microstates: they do not form stable visible outputs after projection, yet they occupy hardware state space and induce routing/compilation overhead, whose minimal disambiguation cost is information-theoretically constrained \cite{CoverThomas2006}.
We propose that dark matter is an effective gravitational source arising from the spatial statistics of folding degeneracy and overhead, while dark energy originates from grid-tension and information potential induced by resolution refinement.
Black-hole horizons appear as computational boundaries formed by channel saturation, and singularities correspond to recursive resolution resets.

Finally, we formulate falsifiable predictions: dark-sector clustering should correlate with Hilbert-address aggregation and folding degeneracy; coupling ``constants'' should drift in high-overhead regions; and early-universe readout should carry scanline artifacts and spectral-template biases tied to Hilbert recursion.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Conventions.}
Unless otherwise stated, $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm.
We write $w=w_1\cdots w_m$ for a finite binary word with letters $w_i\in\{0,1\}$, reserve $n$ for Hilbert resolution order, and reserve $m$ for local readout window length.
Fibonacci numbers are denoted $F_k$ with $F_1=F_2=1$.
The standard Abel path refers to the limit process $r\uparrow 1$ with $r\in(0,1)$, applied to Abel generating functions defined (and holomorphic) for $|r|<1$.
