\begin{theorem}[Elliptic-geometric characterization of Lee--Yang ramification: fixed elliptic curve, critical-value structure, and maximal monodromy group]\label{thm:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-elliptic-structure}
Retain the spectral quartic polynomial
\[
\Pi(\lambda,y):=\lambda^4-\lambda^3-(2y+1)\lambda^2+\lambda+y(y+1)\in\ZZ[\lambda,y].
\]
Set
\[
x:=\lambda,\qquad u:=y-\lambda^2,\qquad w:=2u+1=2y+1-2\lambda^2,
\]
and denote the affine spectral curve \(\mathcal C:=\{(\lambda,y)\in\mathbb A^2:\Pi(\lambda,y)=0\}\).
Then the substitution \(y=x^2+u\) gives a \(\QQ\)-isomorphism
\[
\mathcal C\ \cong\ E_{\min}:\quad u^2+u=x^3-x,
\]
whose inverse is \((x,u)\mapsto(\lambda,y)=(x,u+x^2)\); hence the branching structure of \(\Pi(\lambda,y)=0\) is not an accidental feature of an arbitrary quartic cover, but is strictly governed by a fixed rational elliptic curve independent of the parameter \(y\).
Writing \(E_{\min}\) as \((2u+1)^2=4x^3-4x+1\) and setting \(w=2u+1\), one obtains the equivalent short Weierstrass model
\[
\mathcal E:\quad w^2=4x^3-4x+1\qquad(g_2=4,\ g_3=-1),
\]
with discriminant \(\Delta(\mathcal E)=g_2^3-27g_3^2=37\).
Equivalently, the spectral branch \(\lambda(y)\) can be viewed as local inversion of the rational function \(y=x^2+u\) on \(E_{\min}\), thereby lifting the original multibranch spectral inversion to a rational-function inversion problem on an elliptic curve.
This elliptic curve satisfies
\[
\Delta(E_{\min})=37,\qquad j(E_{\min})=\frac{110592}{37},
\]
so it has bad reduction only at the prime \(37\) and conductor \(37\); consequently, Lee--Yang-type analytic boundaries can be uniformly formulated on the same arithmetic elliptic object together with period-lattice/modularity anchors.
\par
Let \(\pi_y:E_{\min}\to\mathbb P^1_y\) be the degree-\(4\) map induced by the rational function
\(
y=x^2+u=\frac{w+2x^2-1}{2}
\)
Then \(y\) has a unique pole of order \(4\) at the point at infinity \(O\in E_{\min}\), so \(\infty\in\mathbb P^1_y\) is a totally ramified critical value.
Its finite critical points are characterized by \(\dd y=0\); equivalently on the short Weierstrass model \(\mathcal E\),
\[
2xw+3x^2-1=0,
\]
and they split exactly into two classes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item two rational critical points
  \[
  (x,u)=(1,-1)\mapsto y=0,\qquad (x,u)=(-1,0)\mapsto y=1;
  \]
  \item three irrational critical points whose \(x\)-coordinates are the roots of
  \[
  16x^3-9x^2+1=0
  \]
  and whose corresponding \(u\)-coordinates are uniquely determined by
  \[
  u=\frac{1-3x^2-2x}{4x}
  \]
  so that the critical values satisfy the explicit formula
  \[
  y=x^2+u=\frac{4x^3-3x^2-2x+1}{4x}.
  \]
\end{itemize}
The three nontrivial finite critical values above are exactly the roots of the irreducible cubic
\[
P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y):=256y^3+411y^2+165y+32
\]
with unique negative real root \(y_{\mathrm{LY}}\).
Moreover, the discriminants satisfy
\[
\mathrm{Disc}(16x^3-9x^2+1)=-2^{2}\cdot 3^{3}\cdot 37,
\qquad
\mathrm{Disc}\bigl(P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)\bigr)=-3^{9}\cdot 31^{2}\cdot 37,
\]
so the prime \(37\) appears simultaneously in the square-free parts of both critical-point and critical-value polynomials, aligned with the bad-reduction prime in \(\Delta(E_{\min})=37\).
Hence the ramification set identified by discriminant factorization is interpreted as the critical-value structure of \(\pi_y\), not as accidental factor splitting in \(\ZZ[y]\).
\par
For any finite critical value \(y_*\in\{0,1\}\cup\{P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)=0\}\), let \(\lambda_*\) be the coalescing spectral value of \(\Pi(\lambda,y_*)=0\) (the corresponding double root).
As \(y\to y_*\), the two coalescing spectral branches have a square-root Puiseux expansion
\[
\lambda(y)=\lambda_*\pm \kappa_*(y-y_*)^{1/2}+O(y-y_*),
\]
whose leading coefficient satisfies the explicit algebraic formula
\[
\kappa_*^{2}
=-\,\frac{2\,\partial_y\Pi(\lambda_*,y_*)}{\partial_{\lambda}^{2}\Pi(\lambda_*,y_*)}
=\frac{3\lambda_*^{2}-1}{8\lambda_*^{3}-3\lambda_*^{2}-1}.
\]
In particular, at \(y_*=0\) and \(y_*=1\), respectively,
\[
\lambda(y)=1\pm \sqrt{\frac{y}{2}}+O(y),
\qquad
\lambda(y)=-1\pm \frac{i}{\sqrt6}(y-1)^{1/2}+O(y-1),
\]
whereas at the real Lee--Yang boundary \(y_*=y_{\mathrm{LY}}\),
\[
y_{\mathrm{LY}}\approx -1.1344520030,\qquad
\lambda_*=\lambda_{\mathrm{LY}}\approx-0.2734348106524542,\qquad
\kappa_{\mathrm{LY}}:=\kappa_*\approx 0.7476109845904598.
\]
Therefore any analytic quantity built from this coalescing spectral branch (e.g., \(\log\lambda\) on a chosen branch) exhibits a strict square-root Lee--Yang singularity at \(y_{\mathrm{LY}}\), with amplitude algebraically fixed by \(\kappa_{\mathrm{LY}}\).
\par
The ramification type of \(\pi_y\) consists of five finite simple branchings and one totally ramified point at infinity (Hurwitz passport \((2,2,2,2,2,4)\)); thus its geometric monodromy contains a \(4\)-cycle and a transposition, so
\par
in Nielsen-class terms, this Hurwitz passport provides a discrete homotopy invariant of the covering in Hurwitz space, making the ramification cycle type a stable fingerprint for cross-model comparison.
\[
\mathrm{Mon}(\pi_y)\cong S_4.
\]
Equivalently, the Galois group of the Galois closure of the function-field extension \(\QQ(y)\subset \QQ(y,\lambda)\) is \(S_4\).
Hence any attempt to compress its ramification structure into smaller monodromy groups such as \(A_4\), \(D_4\), or \(V_4\) is incompatible with the ramification cycle type.
At the algebraic level, this maximal monodromy states that the spectral quartic cover attains the least-degenerate symmetric complexity, and supplies the group-theoretic skeleton for global analytic continuation of spectral branches, branch-cut selection, and irreducibility analysis of zero-condensation structures.
On the other hand, by the standard isomorphism \(S_4\simeq \mathrm{PGL}_2(\FF_3)\), it is identified with a finite octahedral monodromy image;
on the punctured curve \(U:=\mathbb P^1_y\setminus\{0,1,P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)=0,\infty\}\),
the monodromization equivalence above yields a finite-image \(\mathrm{PGL}_2(\FF_3)\)-projective local system, whose existence and branch set are fully fixed by the discriminant mechanism of \(\Pi(\lambda,y)\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Ellipticized entry point for zero-condensation hierarchies]\label{rem:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-zero-measure-outlook}
Within the elliptic monodromization framework, the zero sets of the finite-volume polynomial family \(\{Z_m(y)\}\) can be rewritten as level sets/phase-locking sets of certain elliptic-function sequences under \(\pi_y\);
this enables a strictly algebraic characterization of zero-condensation curves and further motivates analytic formulas for the associated densities and pushforward measures.
\end{remark}
