\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, \emph{``Holographic Polar Arithmetic: Multiplicative Ontology, Unitary Scanning, and the Geometric Origin of Quantum Uncertainty,''} for publication as an Article in \emph{Foundations of Physics}.

The paper proposes \emph{Holographic Polar Arithmetic} (HPA) as a foundational re-framing in which multiplication is treated as ontological and classical addition arises as a readout/projection rather than a primitive microscopic law. The technical core includes: (i) a no-go theorem showing that no nontrivial embedding can preserve both multiplication and linear addition, motivating an intrinsic projection residual (``quantum gap''); (ii) a unitary scan operator model (irrational rotation/Koopman) yielding a Weyl pair and a structural uncertainty tradeoff; (iii) a minimal binary readout leading to Sturmian/Fibonacci structure and canonical Zeckendorf/Ostrowski encodings; and (iv) an orbit-wise ``cyclic calculus'' (difference/trace/finite part) that yields a rigid discrete dispersion relation and a careful causality discussion. The discussion section explicitly separates rigorous results from interpretation-layer hypotheses.

I am submitting this work to \emph{Foundations of Physics} because the manuscript is written to engage the journal's readership on foundational issues at the interface of geometry, quantization, and the meaning of probability and measurement. I also hope the work can be widely visible: if the framework is useful, it should be available to a broad community for scrutiny, refinement, and application.

This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere and has not been published previously. I have no conflicts of interest to declare.

\closing{Sincerely,}
