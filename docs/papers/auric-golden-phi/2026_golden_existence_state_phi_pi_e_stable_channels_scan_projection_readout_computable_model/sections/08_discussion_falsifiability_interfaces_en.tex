\section{Discussion: Falsifiable Boundaries, Interface Assumptions, and Verifiable Predictions}

\subsection{Stratified falsifiability structure}

This paper stratifies falsifiability into three layers:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{M layer (Mathematical layer):} Counting theorems, folding map properties, cycle/boundary decomposition, degeneracy distributions, etc., are completely determined by finite constructions.
  \item \textbf{P layer (Protocol layer):} Interprets scan--projection readout as an ``observation protocol'', and the stable subspace conditioning as a formal expression of ``only existence states can be observed''. The objects of this layer are protocol equivalence classes and repeatable statistics.
  \item \textbf{I layer (Interpretation layer):} Maps $\varph$--$\pi$--$e$ channels to concrete physical/informatic semantics (such as ``closed phase'', ``analytic stability domain'', ``logarithmic impedance''). This layer is not a premise in the theorem chain but a verifiable modeling choice.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Verifiable predictions (examples)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Rigid growth law for stable type counts:} Under the minimal syntax with local prohibition law $11$, the number of stable types for window length $m$ is strictly $F_{m+2}$.
  \item \textbf{Cycle/boundary decomposition:} Endpoint admissibility gives $\card{X_m^{\mathrm{cyc}}}=F_{m-1}+F_{m+1}$ and $\card{X_m^{\mathrm{bdry}}}=F_{m-2}$.
  \item \textbf{Verifiable degeneracy of fold compression:} The average degeneracy of $\mathrm{Fold}_m$ is $2^m/F_{m+2}$ and grows exponentially at scale $(2/\varph)^m$ with $m$.
  \item \textbf{Rigid structure of minimal case $m=6$:} The $64\to 21$ compression, $3\oplus 18$ decomposition, and boundary two-fold-preimage structure constitute the minimal ``golden existence'' instance, verifiable by exhaustive enumeration.
  \item \textbf{Channel separation condition:} In longer windows or weighted syntax, analytic stability ($e$ channel) can separate from syntactic legality ($\varph$ channel); the presence and scale of this separation form direct falsification points for model extension.
\end{enumerate}
