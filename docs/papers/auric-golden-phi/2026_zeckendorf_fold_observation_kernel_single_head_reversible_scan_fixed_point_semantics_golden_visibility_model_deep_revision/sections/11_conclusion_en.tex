\section{Conclusion and Future Directions}

This paper constructs and verifies a minimal model system that is auditable, exhaustively computable, and empirically reproducible:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item We define and analyze the Zeckendorf window observation kernel $\mathrm{Fold}_m$, prove stable language counting and fixed-point structure;
  \item We define fibers and closure operators, objectifying invisible degrees of freedom and reality set structure;
  \item For $m=6$, we give an analytically provable degeneracy spectrum theorem and linear-cyclic consistency splitting;
  \item We construct Feistel single-head reversible microscopic dynamics $U$, and verify the degeneracy law and graph spectrum mixing properties at the observation layer.
\end{enumerate}

Future work includes: generalizing to degeneracy spectrum analysis for $m=6k$, systematic screening of round function families $f$ (targeting mixing time, spectral radius, and boundary artifact fraction), and explicitly incorporating tail variables $\mathrm{Tail}_m$ into microscopic state to study the possibility of ``dynamicizing time fibers.''
