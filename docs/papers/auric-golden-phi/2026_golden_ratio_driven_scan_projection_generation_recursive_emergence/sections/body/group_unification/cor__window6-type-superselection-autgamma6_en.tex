\begin{corollary}[Superselection decomposition at the type layer: \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma_6)=\{1\}$}{Aut(Gamma6)=1} forces continuous symmetries to act only within fibers]\label{cor:window6-type-superselection-autgamma6}
Let \(\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{obs}}\) be the observable representation space decomposed by stable types
\[
\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{obs}}\ \cong\ \bigoplus_{x\in X_6}\mathcal{H}_x.
\]
Let a compact group \(\mathcal{G}\) act via unitary representation \(U:\mathcal{G}\to \mathrm{U}(\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{obs}})\), and assume this action preserves the type adjacency semantics: for each \(g\in\mathcal{G}\), there exists a permutation \(\pi_g\in\mathrm{Sym}(X_6)\) such that
\(
U(g)\mathcal{H}_x=\mathcal{H}_{\pi_g(x)}
\), and \(\pi_g\) preserves the adjacency relations of \(\Gamma_6\).
Then \(\pi_g=\mathrm{id}\) for all \(g\in\mathcal{G}\), hence the action of \(\mathcal{G}\) is block-diagonal in the above direct sum decomposition:
\[
U(g)\ =\ \bigoplus_{x\in X_6}U_x(g),
\qquad
U_x(g):\mathcal{H}_x\to\mathcal{H}_x.
\]
Therefore, the type layer admits no nontrivial continuous mixing degrees of freedom; any continuous coupling across types must be explicitly realized by introducing additional channels/degrees of freedom, and cannot be absorbed by ``type relabeling.''
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By assumption, \(\pi_g\) is a graph automorphism of \(\Gamma_6\), i.e., \(\pi_g\in \mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma_6)\).
By Proposition \ref{prop:terminal-gamma6-rigidity}, \(\mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma_6)=\{1\}\), hence \(\pi_g=\mathrm{id}\).
Therefore \(U(g)\mathcal{H}_x=\mathcal{H}_x\) for all \(x\), which is equivalent to \(U(g)\) being block-diagonal in the direct sum decomposition. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}
