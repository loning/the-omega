\begin{corollary}[Application to Fold: square-root upper bound for hidden label algebra]\label{cor:dephys-fold-hidden-label-cap-sqrt}
For fixed resolution \(m\), let \(E_m:A_m\to B_m\) be the fiber-uniform conditional expectation induced by the folding \(\Fold_m\) (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-condexp-index-maxfiber}), with index satisfying
\(\mathrm{Ind}(E_m)=D_m:=\max_{x\in X_m}d_m(x)\).
Then for any discrete hidden label projection family that is externally indistinguishable yet internally perfectly distinguishable, the number of pairwise orthogonal options satisfies the hard upper bound
\[
\#\{\text{perfectly distinguishable hidden labels}\}\ \le\ \mathrm{Ind}(E_m)=D_m.
\]
Moreover, since \(|X_m|=F_{m+2}\sim \varphi^{m}/\sqrt5\) and \(D_m\) satisfies the two-step recurrence \(D_m=D_{m-2}+D_{m-4}\) (Corollary \ref{cor:pom-D-rec}, with leading root \(\sqrt{\varphi}\)), there is the asymptotic scaling
\[
D_m\ \asymp\ \varphi^{m/2}\ =\ |X_m|^{\,1/2+o(1)}.
\]
\end{corollary}
