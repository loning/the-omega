\section{Padé pole-barrier audit for the confinement proxy (HPA viewpoint; audit)}
\label{app:qcd_confinement_pade_pole_barrier_audit}

This appendix records an \AuditTag{} analytic-continuation audit in the spirit of the Abel-first / pole-barrier discipline used elsewhere in this paper.
It is intended as a computationally transparent consistency check and does not constitute a proof of confinement or of a Yang--Mills mass gap.
\AuditTag In the broader manuscript spine, pole barriers are treated as analyticity-domain certificates for resolvent/determinant packages, with the dominant barrier located at the inverse spectral radius (Corollary~\ref{cor:pole_barrier_equals_exp_minus_pressure}) and recorded in the operator mother-space bookkeeping language (Appendix~\ref{app:operator_mother_space}).
The present Pad\'e audit is a deliberately finite, short-series instantiation of that same certificate template (and therefore remains diagnostic-only).

\paragraph{Input series.}
We extract the finite loop-size Wilson-loop style magnitudes $|W_k|$ from Table~\ref{tab:holonomy_wilson_loop} (Appendix~\ref{app:continuum_yang_mills_from_holonomy}).
We form a sparse power series
\[
F(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{36} c_n z^n,
\qquad
c_0=1,\ \ c_{k^2}=|W_k|\ (k\in\{1,\dots,6\}),
\]
with all other coefficients set to $0$.

\paragraph{Padé/pole-barrier heuristic.}
\AuditTag We compute a bounded family of Padé approximants $[L/M]$ to $F(z)$ and audit whether the resulting denominator polynomial $Q_{L,M}(z)$ has numerical roots in the open unit disk $|z|<1$.
Such roots correspond to interior poles of the rational approximant.
Because Padé approximants can introduce spurious poles/zeros for short or sparse series, this check is recorded only as an audit-facing diagnostic.

\paragraph{Relation to the finite area/perimeter proxy.}
\AuditTag Appendix~\ref{app:qcd_confinement_proxy_audit} records a bounded area/perimeter proxy fit $|W_k|\approx \exp(-\sigma k^2-\mu k)$ to the same finite Wilson-loop magnitude diagnostics.
The present Padé check probes a complementary analytic-continuation viewpoint (interior poles of rational approximants to a sparse series built from $|W_k|$).
The two audits are reported side-by-side as a consistency bridge (Appendix~\ref{subsec:qcd_proxy_pade_bridge}), while the strict confinement/mass-gap problem remains explicitly not closed.

\paragraph{Minimal failure points (Pad\'e-specific).}
\AuditTag The Pad\'e pole-barrier diagnostic is sensitive to (i) short/sparse coefficient sets and (ii) the bounded $(L,M)$ order family.
As an audit hook, future extensions should include: stability checks under modest order-family enlargement, and a comparison to alternative analytic-continuation heuristics on the same coefficient family, while keeping the confinement/mass-gap problem explicitly not closed.
\AuditTag Any enlargement of the bounded $(L,M)$ family is an explicit look-elsewhere enlargement and should be recorded as such; for the manuscript-wide cross-family accounting rule used when aggregating across registries, see Appendix~\ref{app:global_model_selection_mdl}.
