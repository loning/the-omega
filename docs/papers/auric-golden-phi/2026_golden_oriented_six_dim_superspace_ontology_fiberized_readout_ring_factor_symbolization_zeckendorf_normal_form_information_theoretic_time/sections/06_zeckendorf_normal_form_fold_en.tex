\section{Zeckendorf Normal Form and Stabilization Kernel \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{Fold}_m$}{Fold\_m}}
\label{sec:zeckendorf}

\subsection{Formalized Zeckendorf Theorem}

Formal proof libraries provide mechanically verified proofs of the Zeckendorf theorem: every positive integer can be uniquely represented as a sum of non-adjacent Fibonacci numbers \cite{AFPZeckendorf}.
This uniqueness is used to support this paper's ``uniqueness of normal form''.

\subsection{Legitimate Language and Counting}

Define
\[
\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^{m},\qquad
X_m^{\mathrm Z}=\{x\in\{0,1\}^{m}:\text{contains no subword }11\}.
\]
Combinatorial proofs give that the count of Fibonacci indicator strings with no adjacent $1$s satisfies Fibonacci recursion, leading to $\card{X_m^{\mathrm Z}}=F_{m+2}$ \cite{PlanetMathZeckendorfCombinatorial}.
Therefore $\card{\Omega_6}=64$ and $\card{X_6^{\mathrm Z}}=F_8=21$.

\begin{remark}
The above count $2^6\to F_8$ serves as the minimum exhaustive verification platform for fixed $m=6$ in the main text: it permits exhaustive verification of the output types and fiber structure of $\mathrm{Fold}_6$ without introducing additional semantic claims.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Stabilization Kernel}

Define the surjection
\[
\mathrm{Fold}_m:\Omega_m\twoheadrightarrow X_m^{\mathrm Z},
\]
which reduces any window word to a unique normal form. Appendix \ref{app:zeckendorf-fold} provides a class of local rewrite constructions and sketches of termination/uniqueness proofs, with logical foundation in Zeckendorf unique representation \cite{AFPZeckendorf}.
