\begin{conclusion}[The second-order collision cubic and the cubic coordinate field of \texorpdfstring{$E[2]$}{E[2]}: explicit isomorphism via a Tschirnhaus generator transform]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-collision-s2-e2-field-isomorphism}
Retain the second-order collision cubic from Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-37-squarefree-coupling}, with variable \(\Lambda\):
\[
P_2(\Lambda):=\Lambda^3-2\Lambda^2-2\Lambda+2\in\ZZ[\Lambda].
\]
On the other hand, in the short Weierstrass model of Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-normalization},
\(
E:\ Y^2=X^3-X+\tfrac14
\)
the \(X\)-coordinates of nontrivial \(2\)-torsion points are described by the \(2\)-division cubic
\[
D_2(X):=4X^3-4X+1\in\ZZ[X]
\]
Take \(\theta\) to be any root of \(D_2(X)=0\), and define the Tschirnhaus transform
\[
\boxed{\;\Lambda:=-2+2\theta+4\theta^2\;}
\]
Then \(P_2(\Lambda)=0\) holds in \(\QQ(\theta)\), inducing the field isomorphism
\[
\boxed{\ \QQ(\Lambda)\cong \QQ(\theta)=:\QQ(E[2])\ }.
\]
Hence the cubic algebraic number field of the second-order collision spectrum and the \(2\)-torsion coordinate field of the weighted-spectral elliptic curve are arithmetically identified as the same object;
moreover,
\(
\mathrm{Disc}(P_2)=4\cdot 37
\)
and
\(
\mathrm{Disc}(D_2)=2^{4}\cdot 37
\)
have matching square-free parts, uniformly explained by this explicit isomorphism.
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Substitute \(\Lambda=-2+2X+4X^2\) into \(P_2(\Lambda)\), then perform polynomial division with remainder by \(D_2(X)=4X^3-4X+1\) in \(\QQ[X]\): the remainder is identically zero, so \(D_2(\theta)=0\) implies \(P_2(-2+2\theta+4\theta^2)=0\).
Equivalently, the resultant provides a strict elimination certificate
\[
\mathrm{Res}_X\!\bigl(D_2(X),\ \Lambda-(-2+2X+4X^2)\bigr)=16\,P_2(\Lambda),
\]
so \(\Lambda\) satisfies the same cubic minimal polynomial in \(\QQ(\theta)\), yielding the stated cubic-field isomorphism.

\end{proof}

\begin{conclusion}[Splitting type of second-order collisions and Frobenius action on \texorpdfstring{$E[2]$}{E[2]}: verifiable equivalence modulo \texorpdfstring{$p$}{p}]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-collision-s2-splitting-e2-torsion}
Retain the notation of Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-collision-s2-e2-field-isomorphism}, and let \(p\neq 2,37\) be prime.
Then the following conditions are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the second-order collision cubic \(P_2(\Lambda)\) splits completely over \(\FF_p[\Lambda]\);
  \item the \(2\)-division cubic \(D_2(X)=4X^3-4X+1\) splits completely over \(\FF_p[X]\);
  \item all three nontrivial \(2\)-torsion points of the elliptic curve \(E/\FF_p\) are \(\FF_p\)-rational, i.e.,
  \[
  E(\FF_p)[2]\cong (\ZZ/2\ZZ)^2.
  \]
\end{enumerate}
Moreover, the splitting type of \(P_2\) (equivalently \(D_2\)) over \(\FF_p\) matches the Frobenius conjugacy class in
\(
S_3\cong \mathrm{Aut}\bigl(E[2]\setminus\{O\}\bigr)
\)
so the mod-\(p\) splitting behavior of the second-order collision spectrum is precisely decoded as the Galois representation type on \(E[2]\).
\par
In contrast, the Lee--Yang irrational ramification orbit described by the cubic \(16X^3-9X^2+1\) in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-ramification-critical-values} belongs to a different \(S_3\)-type extension, with quadratic resolvent field \(\QQ(\sqrt{-111})\) (see Conclusions \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-cubic-arithmetic} and \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-cubic-field-isomorphism}), and generally does not merge with the splitting mechanism of \(E[2]\).
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
By the Tschirnhaus isomorphism of Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-collision-s2-e2-field-isomorphism}, \(P_2\) and \(D_2\) generate the same cubic number field over \(\QQ\).
For primes \(p\nmid 2\cdot 37\), this field is unramified at \(p\), so the splitting type at \(p\) can be read from the factorization type over \(\FF_p\) of any integer-coefficient defining polynomial, yielding \((1)\Longleftrightarrow(2)\).
On the other hand, in short Weierstrass form over characteristic \(\neq 2\), nontrivial \(2\)-torsion points correspond exactly to \(Y=0\), with \(X\)-coordinates given by the three roots of \(D_2(X)=0\).
Hence \(D_2\) splits completely over \(\FF_p\) iff all three nontrivial \(2\)-torsion points are \(\FF_p\)-rational, i.e., \((2)\Longleftrightarrow(3)\).
Finally, the correspondence between Frobenius permutation type on nontrivial \(2\)-torsion and cubic-extension splitting type is standard.

\end{proof}
