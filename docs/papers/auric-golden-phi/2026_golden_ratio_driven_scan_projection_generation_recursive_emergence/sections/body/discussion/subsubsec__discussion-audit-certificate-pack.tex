\subsubsection{Auditable certificate-pack interface: an algebraic criterion for mechanism equivalence}\label{subsubsec:discussion-audit-certificate-pack}
Across multiple levels, this work repeatedly uses the same strategy: compress seemingly ``mechanistic complex differences'' into a very small set of computable and verifiable algebraic objects, thereby rewriting mechanism-equivalence questions as consistency tests over a finite certificate set.
Here, certificates are short objects uniquely induced by the mechanism under fixed readout and baseline conventions (spectral radius, characteristic polynomial, Jordan residual, Hankel rank, endpoint exponents, etc.).
Under this convention, the falsifiable meaning of ``same mechanism'' is not narrative similarity, but auditable consistency of these objects; any stable mismatch in any component serves as a hard interface for refuting equivalence.

\paragraph{Gauge discrepancy as a minimal prototype: low-order constants and endpoint exponents locked by spectral radius}
The noncommutativity of resolution reduction can be quantitatively characterized by the per-position mismatch indicator of gauge discrepancy, \(g_t=\mathbf 1\{X_t\neq Y_t\}\) (\S\ref{subsubsec:insight-gauge-anomaly}; Definition \ref{def:fold-gauge-anomaly}).
Under the uniform baseline, the limiting pressure of its exponential moment generating function is fully characterized by the spectral radius of a \(4\times 4\) weighted adjacency matrix (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure}):
\[
P_G(\theta)=\log\rho(A_\theta),\qquad
A_\theta=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}
1 & e^\theta & 0 & 1\\
0 & 0 & e^\theta & 0\\
e^\theta & 2 & 0 & 0\\
1 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Hence at \(\theta=0\), the mean density, variance rate, and third cumulant are jointly locked by closed forms of \(P_G'(0),P_G''(0),P_G^{(3)}(0)\) (same proposition), and endpoint rare-event exponents are also fully closed-form (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-ldp}).
Moreover, this chain of density/fluctuation/pressure constants remains invariant under fixed-step multistep resolution reduction (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-kstep-invariance}), extending certificate stability from one-step reduction to finite-step lookahead readout.
Further, the Jordan defect at \(-\tfrac12\) appears rigorously in related closed forms as an ``exponential decay \(\times\) linear polynomial'' residual (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-A0-jordan}), inducing finite-sample-verifiable Hankel-rank certificates and fixed-order recurrence constraints (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-hankel-rank3}; see \S\ref{par:fold-gauge-anomaly-spectral-recurrence-triangle}).
The rate curve of the same mechanism is also pinned by an integer-coefficient elimination certificate: eliminating stationary-point parameters yields the resultant fingerprint \(R(\alpha,u)\in\mathbb Z[\alpha,u]\) (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-rate-curve-param}; Table \ref{tab:fold_gauge_anomaly_rate_curve_resultant_degree}).
This yields a typical ultra-short ``spectrum--constant--endpoint'' certificate chain: short objects, independent of interpretive narrative, and independently reproducible.

\paragraph{Three output classes of the certificate pack: algebraic relations, low-order statistics, and endpoint exponents}
At the level of organizing auditable interfaces, the prototype above can be abstracted into three complementary classes of certificate output, covering formalization, black-box auditing, and robustness, respectively:
\par
\textbf{Algebraic certificates:}
Compress the mechanism into elimination-derived integer-coefficient polynomial relations and branch-selection rules.
Such certificates use ``coefficient table + admissible branch'' as the full storage payload: e.g., the quartic algebraic equation for \(\mu(u)\) (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure}) and the resultant \(R(\alpha,u)\) of the rate curve (Table \ref{tab:fold_gauge_anomaly_rate_curve_resultant_degree}).
Under this convention, any implementation claiming equivalence must reproduce the same algebraic relation (or lie in the explicitly specified same Perron branch), otherwise certificate validation fails.
\par
\textbf{Low-order statistical certificates:}
Use a small number of stably estimable statistics as the falsification interface, e.g., constant-scale fingerprints of \(P_G'(0),P_G''(0),P_G^{(3)}(0)\) (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure} and Corollary \ref{cor:fold-gauge-anomaly-cumulant3}), supplemented by finite-order recurrences/low-Hankel-rank constraints to compress the degrees of freedom of ``correlation structure'' (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-hankel-rank3}).
The key use of this class is black-box auditing: without entering implementation internals, finite samples suffice for a falsifiable rejection of ``non-identical mechanism.''
\par
\textbf{Endpoint-exponent certificates:}
Close the far-field limit via endpoint exponents of rare events, forming a two-sided anchor together with local-derivative constants.
The endpoint closed forms of gauge discrepancy, \(I(0)=\log(2/\varphi)\), \(I(1)=\log 2\) (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-ldp}), give a canonical example: the exponential rates at ``full match/full mismatch'' are fixed as pure algebraic constants, yielding unavoidable robustness anchors for far-field behavior under mechanism replacement.

\paragraph{Modular organization of a unified audit pack: converging cross-section invariants into a single output interface}
Reorganizing the verifiable interfaces established across the paper by output type yields a modular certificate-pack view; each component corresponds to a reproducible object chain:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Curvature certificates (noncommutativity obstruction and error bars):}
  quantify the obstruction between ``truncate then fold'' and ``fold then restrict'' via the gauge-curvature indicator \(\mathcal K_{m+1\to m}\) for resolution reduction (Definition \ref{def:pom-gauge-curvature}), and provide measurable error bars for any bounded observable via curvature probability (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-gauge-curvature-shadow-bound}; Table \ref{tab:fold_truncation_curvature_stats}).
  \item \textbf{Spectral certificates (spectral radius and defect modes of finite-state operator families):}
  use \(P(\theta)=\log\rho(A_\theta)\), or equivalent characteristic-polynomial/power-spectrum rational fingerprints, as auditable spectral objects (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure}; Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-psd-rational}), and characterize rigid projections of non-semisimple defects onto fluctuation/correlation via Jordan residuals (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-A0-jordan}).
  \item \textbf{Elimination certificates (integer-coefficient resultants of rate curves):}
  use \(R(\alpha,u)\in\mathbb Z[\alpha,u]\) as an unforgeable fingerprint of ``stationary point--rate curve--branch selection'' (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-rate-curve-param}; Table \ref{tab:fold_gauge_anomaly_rate_curve_resultant_degree}).
  \item \textbf{Volume certificates (closed-form link between gauge-volume density and folding-defect potential):}
  use second-order Stirling expansion of gauge-group volume density \(g_m\) and folding-defect potential \(\kappa_m\) as a reproducible bridge object, with exponentially small error bars (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-bin-gauge-volume-stirling-second-order}).
  \item \textbf{Arithmetic certificates (Hankel principal minors and \texorpdfstring{$p$}{p}-adic rigidity):}
  pin down the ``visible state count'' via Hankel rank/minimal realization dimension, and use \(\mathsf{Stiff}_p(k)=v_p(\det(H_k))\) as an internal arithmetic-rigidity fingerprint (\S\ref{subsec:pom-moment-visibility}; Remark \ref{rem:pom-hankel-stiffness-p5}).
  \item \textbf{Graph-polynomial certificates (reconstructible internal fiber decomposition and \texorpdfstring{$\bmod 3$}{mod 3} obstruction):}
  fully reconstruct fiber generating functions \(Z_x(t)\) from the independent-set system of the induced graph \(\Gamma_x\) (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-fiber-indset-factorization}), and derive the \(\bmod 3\) obstruction from vanishing of the signed index \(\mathfrak{s}_m(x)=Z_x(-1)\in\{0,\pm 1\}\) (\S\ref{subsec:pom-fiber-signed-index}; Theorem \ref{thm:pom-fiber-signed-index-periodicity}).
\end{itemize}
Under this organization, any subsequent cross-domain bridge (e.g., horizon positivity in unitary-slice, spectral-gap conditions for kernel RH/GRH variants, and certificate rewriting chains for POM) can be uniformly localized as follows: output verifiable certificate objects before interpretive statements, and use certificate consistency as the primary criterion of mechanism equivalence.
In a reproducible experimental pipeline, this is equivalent to a unified audit interface: input a black-box output sequence (or its low-order statistical summary), output certificate objects such as minimal polynomials/spectral fingerprints/recurrence constraints, and return a verifiable pass/fail decision.
