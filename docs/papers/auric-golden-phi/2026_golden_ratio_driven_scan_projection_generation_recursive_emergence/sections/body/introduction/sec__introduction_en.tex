\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

Finite-window observation provides a unified interface across dynamical systems, symbolic dynamics, and automata/coding theory: given a singly generated action \(T^t\) and a binary projection readout \(s_t=\ind_W(T^t x)\), the observation data are naturally organized as sliding-window microstates \(\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m\) of length \(m\). The fundamental difficulty with this perspective is that as \(m\) increases, the microstate space grows exponentially; directly treating microstates as ``concepts/types'' leads to sparsity and instability, thereby obstructing auditable cross-resolution reasoning chains.
\par
The goal of this paper is to construct an auditable generative chain: starting from scan--projection readout, obtaining stable grammar, fold normal forms, and realizable arithmetic structures through statistical stability and recursive addressing. To ensure verifiability, this paper strictly separates proof chains from explanatory narrative: all conclusions are anchored to previously numbered definitions/theorems/propositions, and explanatory terminology does not serve as a proof premise. Throughout the paper, we fix the golden branch \(\alpha=\varphi^{-1}\) as the anti-resonance canonical representative under the Hurwitz/Markov worst-case rational approximation caliber (\cite{Cassels1957,KuipersNiederreiter1974}), and employ \(T\)-invariant measures and ergodic tools (\cite{WaltersErgodicTheory}) in frequency/stability statements.

\subsection{Contribution List: Auditable Interfaces}\label{subsec:contrib-scope}
\textbf{The mathematical contributions of this paper} are concentrated in a set of independently auditable structural conclusions, which can be summarized as the following ``auditable interfaces'':
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Minimality of recursive addressing:} Recursive addressing is rigorously formalized as an endogenous mechanism of ``readable prefixes'': when the address object is not given, the evaluation function \(\ind_{C_a}\) does not hold at the referential/type level, the readout partial function is undefined and represented by \(\mathsf{NULL}\) to indicate non-addressability; once an address is given, the derived cylinder event family does not expand the existing visible \(\sigma\)-algebra, but merely reselects and reorganizes visible events within the old layer \(\mathcal{G}^{(L)}\), so that \(\mathcal{G}^{(L+1)}\subseteq \mathcal{G}^{(L)}\) (Proposition \ref{prop:null-as-local-section-obstruction}, \ref{prop:recursive-addressing-refinement}; Corollary \ref{cor:recursive-addressing-visible-sigma-nonexpansion}).
  \item \textbf{Resolution folding and cross-scale compatibility:} We construct finite-window folding \(\Fold_m\) and its computable implementation, and prove unified conclusions on confluence, inverse system compatibility, and sofic factor interfaces (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-suite}; see also Definition \ref{def:fold-word} and Proposition \ref{prop:fold-basic}).
  \item \textbf{Quantitative law of scan length versus clarity:} On the ``address precedes concept value'' interface, we characterize resolution decidability as clopen-ness in the cylinder topology, and give the exponential law for ``deeper scans yield greater clarity'' via the optimal error profile and boundary cylinder dimension (Proposition \ref{prop:spg-decidable-clopen}, Definition \ref{def:spg-scan-error-profile}, Theorem \ref{thm:spg-clarity-boundary-dimension}; see also the fold visibility and two-sided error bound Theorem \ref{thm:pom-visibility-to-error-two-sided}). Under address semantics, this exponential clarity can be further decomposed into two independent failure modes---``statistical-type \(\mathsf{NULL}\)'' and ``de-collision''---unified by the double-budget addressing capacity law (Theorem \ref{thm:spg-double-budget-address-capacity}).
  \item \textbf{Projection Ontology Mathematics and information ledger:} We define scan--projection generators and projection word calculus (Definitions \ref{def:pom-spg}, \ref{def:pom-projection-word}, \ref{def:pom-projword-2cat}), and provide the closed-form information ledger identity for relative entropy defects and its corollaries (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-kl-ledger}, Corollary \ref{cor:pom-ent-increase-equals-kl}).
  \item \textbf{Readout tower rewriting and decidable closure:} We prove termination and confluence on local readout tower slices and give normal forms (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-rewrite-termination-confluence}), yielding decidability of the word problem and an audit closure (Propositions \ref{prop:pom-projword-decidable}, \ref{prop:pom-rewrite-audit-loop}).
  \item \textbf{Resonance mechanism and Hankel rank collapse:} For arbitrary-order collision moments, we give finite-state moment-kernel compilation and Perron principal spectral characterization (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-collision-kernel-ak}), and provide sufficient criteria for Hankel rank collapse induced by modal constraints, thereby locating resonance as a verifiable mechanism (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-moment-resonance}).
  \item \textbf{Sync-subtracted Chebotarev capacity law:} Under the kernel-version GRH / uniform spectral gap caliber, we lift finite-layer Chebotarev counts along the profinite axis and give equidistribution errors for cylinder sets (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-profinite-axis}); under online semantics, we incorporate the synchronization threshold into the capacity inequality via explicit deduction terms, thereby compiling ``synchronize first, then equalize'' into an auditable family of inequalities (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-sync-subtracted-chebotarev-capacity}).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Scope boundaries:}
\begin{itemize}

  \item \textbf{Explanatory terms do not enter the proof chain:} All derivations rely on previously numbered conclusions and standard external theorems.
\end{itemize}
