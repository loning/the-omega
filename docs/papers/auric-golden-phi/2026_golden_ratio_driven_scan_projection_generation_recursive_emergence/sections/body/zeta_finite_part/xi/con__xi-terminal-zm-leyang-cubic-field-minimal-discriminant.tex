\begin{conclusion}[Minimal discriminant and ramification type of the Lee--Yang cubic field: \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{Disc}(K)=-999$}{Disc(K)=-999}]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-cubic-field-minimal-discriminant}
Using the notation of Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-cubic-field-isomorphism}, let \(\beta\) be any root of the Lee--Yang cubic
\[
P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)=256y^{3}+411y^{2}+165y+32\in\ZZ[y]
\]
and write \(K:=\QQ(\beta)\) (equivalently \(K=\QQ(\alpha)\), where \(\alpha\) is a root of \(16X^{3}-9X^{2}+1=0\)).
Then:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \(K\) is a complex cubic field of signature \((r_1,r_2)=(1,1)\), with field discriminant
  \[
  \boxed{\ \mathrm{Disc}(K)=-999=-3^{3}\cdot 37\ }.
  \]
  \item Therefore the ramified-prime set of \(K/\QQ\) is exactly \(\{3,37\}\).
  Here \(3\) is wildly and totally ramified, while \(37\) is tamely ramified with decomposition
  \[
  \boxed{\ 3\mathcal O_K=\mathfrak p_3^{3},\qquad 37\mathcal O_K=\mathfrak p_{37}^{2}\mathfrak q_{37}\ }.
  \]
  \item The unique quadratic resolvent subfield of the \(S_3\)-splitting field of \(K\) is
  \[
  \boxed{\ \QQ(\sqrt{-111})\ }.
  \]
\end{enumerate}
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Take the integral generator \(\theta:=256\beta\) (hence \(\QQ(\theta)=\QQ(\beta)\)); then \(\theta\) satisfies the monic integral polynomial
\(
\theta^{3}+411\theta^{2}+42240\theta+2097152=0
\).
Computing the maximal order of this integral model and the field discriminant gives \(\mathrm{Disc}(K)=-999\), and the discriminant criterion implies ramification only at \(3\) and \(37\).
Prime-ideal decomposition for \(p\in\{3,37\}\) then yields the displayed decomposition types.
Reproducibility audit script: \texttt{scripts/exp\_fold\_zm\_elliptic\_leyang\_arithmetic\_audit.py}. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}
