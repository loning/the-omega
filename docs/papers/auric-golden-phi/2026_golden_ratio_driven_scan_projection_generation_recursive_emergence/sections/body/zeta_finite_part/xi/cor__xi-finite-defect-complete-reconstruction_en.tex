\begin{corollary}[Complete reconstruction of off-critical zeros under finite defect: finite data is complete]\label{cor:xi-finite-defect-complete-reconstruction}
Under the finite defect setting of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-basepoint-scan-inversion-uniqueness}, the scan profile
\(
H(x)=\sum_{k=1}^{\kappa}\frac{4\delta_k}{(x-\gamma_k)^2+(1+\delta_k)^2}
\)
is a rational Herglotz boundary value object with \(2\kappa\) poles; therefore there exists a "finite data is complete" reconstruction mechanism:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Defect degree is readable)} \(\kappa\) can be completely audit-read from the stable value of the Toeplitz truncation negative inertia:
  \(
  \nu_-(\mathbf{T}_N)=\kappa\ (N\gg 1)
  \)
  (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-toeplitz-negative-squares-equals-blaschke-defect} and Theorem \ref{thm:xi-tate-maslov-pontryagin-unification});
  \item \textbf{(Finite sampling \(\Rightarrow\) pole recovery)} Given \(\kappa\), taking \(4\kappa\) general position sample points \(x_j\) with values \(H(x_j)\), one can uniquely recover its meromorphic continuation's pole set via a single linear-algebraic rational interpolation (equivalent Pad\'e/Prony elimination calibration), thereby uniquely recovering the parameter multiset \(\{(\gamma_k,\delta_k)\}_{k=1}^{\kappa}\) (counted with multiplicity);
  \item \textbf{(Moment/series calibration)} Equivalently, if a sufficiently long finite segment of the far-field moment chain \(\{M_n\}\) can be obtained, one can recover the denominator polynomial \(Q\) from the minimal-order \(2\kappa\) recurrence and reconstruct all poles (Proposition \ref{prop:xi-far-field-moment-recursion-order-2kappa}).
\end{enumerate}
Furthermore, this reconstruction is gauge-independent under constant-layer strictification: pure constant counterterm \(\mathscr{C}\mapsto \mathscr{C}+\mathrm{i}\eta\) does not alter the Toeplitz negative inertia sequence (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-counterterm-invariance-kappa}), nor move the pole set of the rational continuation; therefore the recoverable point-spectrum data is a strict invariant under this calibration.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By Proposition \ref{prop:xi-basepoint-depth-closed-form}, \(H\) is a linear combination of finitely many Poisson kernels (Cauchy kernels), thus its complex continuation is a rational meromorphic function with \(2\kappa\) poles, and by Theorem \ref{thm:xi-basepoint-scan-inversion-uniqueness} we obtain the unique correspondence "pole set \(\Longleftrightarrow\) parameter multiset".
\par
The unknowns for rational interpolation can be taken as the numerator-denominator coefficients of \(H=P/Q\) (with unknown dimension \(4\kappa-1\) under the monic normalization of \(Q\)), which can be linearly determined from \(4\kappa\) general position sample equations, yielding the pole set; this gives clause (2).
Clause (1) is directly derived from the equivalence chain of Toeplitz negative inertia stable readout of \(\kappa\).
Clause (3) is a direct consequence of Proposition \ref{prop:xi-far-field-moment-recursion-order-2kappa}.
Finally, the gauge independence comes from combining "constant translation does not move poles" with Theorem \ref{thm:xi-counterterm-invariance-kappa}.
\end{proof}
