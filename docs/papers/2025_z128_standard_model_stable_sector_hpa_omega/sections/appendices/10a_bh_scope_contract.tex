\section{Black-hole information closure contract (scope, targets, and failure points)}
\label{app:bh_scope_contract}

\paragraph{Purpose.}
\AuditTag This appendix records a reader-facing contract for any future attempt to close
black-hole evaporation and information recovery inside the tick\,+\,CAP discipline.
It is a \emph{scope and gatekeeping} module: it introduces no new theorem-level premises
into the folding core.

\paragraph{Context.}
This paper already contains three black-hole-facing infrastructure modules:
(i) the protocol-horizon / tick-trap notion (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol_horizon_tick_trap}),
(ii) a deterministic capacity calibration that links black-hole boundary information to
protocol resolution parameters $(m,n)$ (Appendix~\ref{app:bh_planck_capacity_calibration}),
and (iii) a capacity-first uplift/fusion/horizon staging vocabulary (Appendix~\ref{app:resolution_uplift_fusion_horizon_unification}).
In addition, a finite-family leakage-kernel vocabulary unifies decay and evaporation as
exits from tick traps (Appendix~\ref{app:leakage_kernel}).

\paragraph{What is \emph{not} closed in this paper.}
\AuditTag This manuscript does \emph{not} close black-hole evaporation dynamics or the
black-hole information paradox (Page curve, islands/QES, replica wormholes). These topics
are explicitly kept beyond-scope in the main limitations section
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations_related_work}).
The present appendix defines what it would mean to close them in a protocol-auditable form.

\subsection{Contract C0: external record algebra (what the outside can access)}
\label{subsec:bh_contract_record_algebra}

\paragraph{Record-algebra requirement.}
\AuditTag Any black-hole information statement must begin by declaring the externally accessible
record algebra $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{out}}$.
In the paper's protocol language, this can be implemented by one of the following concrete choices:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Window-word record.} A record stream of finite window outcomes (Section~\ref{sec:hpa_readout}).
\item \textbf{Stable-type record.} A record stream of stable labels $w_t\in X_m$ induced by fixed-resolution
coarse readout, with $w_t=\mathrm{Fold}_m(N_t)$ for microstates $N_t\in\{0,\dots,2^m-1\}$.
\item \textbf{Exit-channel record.} A record stream of coarse exit-channel symbols, as in the leakage-kernel
module (Appendix~\ref{app:leakage_kernel}).
\end{itemize}
The record choice is an \InterfaceTag interface declaration and must be audited as a finite protocol specification.

\paragraph{Causal inaccessibility is a statement about $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{out}}$.}
\AuditTag ``Causal inaccessibility'' in this program is formalized as: the exterior observer's
predictions and reconstructions are functions only of $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{out}}$ (and declared matching inputs),
with no privileged access to internal bookkeeping variables.

\subsection{Contract C1: unitarity target is a \texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{out}}$}{Aout}-level recovery statement}
\label{subsec:bh_contract_unitarity_target}

\paragraph{Unitarity target (interface form).}
\InterfaceTag The unitarity target for black-hole evaporation is not ``the spectrum is thermal'',
but a recovery statement about information in the \emph{external record}:
even if low-order statistics of $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{out}}$ appear approximately thermal/featureless for a long
epoch, the full record must contain sufficient higher-order correlations to allow information recovery
in the long-time limit (within declared error tolerance and budget constraints).

\paragraph{Page behavior as a record-entropy surrogate (audit form).}
\AuditTag In this paper's discipline, the Page-curve content should be expressed through a
protocol-computable surrogate functional $S_{\mathrm{sur}}(R_{0:t})$ on finite record prefixes,
for example an MDL/compression-rate proxy or an explicitly declared recovery error bound.
The surrogate must be tied to a declared finite hypothesis class and must provide
clear failure conditions (Appendix~\ref{app:minimal_failure_point_templates}).

\subsection{Contract C2: CP\texorpdfstring{$\to$}{->}PT gate (what must be proved vs.\ assumed)}
\label{subsec:bh_contract_cp_to_pt_gate}

\paragraph{Why a CP gate is unavoidable.}
\AuditTag Black-hole information recovery in full quantum gravity typically relies on strong inputs
(nonperturbative control, replica saddles, or duality assumptions).
In the present paper, the admissible approach is to spell out the minimal assumption package and its
failure points (CP), and to promote individual steps to theorem-level statements (PT) whenever possible.

\paragraph{Minimum CP package (recommended).}
\AuditTag Any CP-level black-hole information closure claimed within this project must explicitly include:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(CP1) A declared protocol state $(m,n,K)$.} (Appendix~\ref{subsec:tick_cap_joint_protocol_state}).
\item \textbf{(CP2) A record algebra $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{out}}$ and its reconstruction target.}
(Contract C0).
\item \textbf{(CP3) A resource ledger.} A discrete mass/energy/overhead budget that decreases under emission,
preventing perpetual emission without a source (required for physical interpretability).
\item \textbf{(CP4) A stability/spectrum surrogate.} If scattering/LSZ-style interfaces are invoked,
the windowed spectrum-surrogate contract must be declared (Appendix~\ref{app:spectrum_surrogate_contract}).
\item \textbf{(CP5) A recovery surrogate with a falsifiable failure point.} (Contract C1) together with an
evidence-map pointer (Appendix~\ref{app:minimal_failure_point_templates}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{PT promotion checklist.}
\AuditTag Steps are eligible for PT promotion if they can be stated as one of the rigidity-bridge certificates
of Appendix~\ref{app:rigidity_bridge_checklist} (finite-family minimization, obstruction, counting, or robustness),
with all finite families and tie-breaks declared and reproducible.

\subsection{Contract C3: fallbacks and failure recording}
\label{subsec:bh_contract_failures}

\paragraph{Failure recording rule.}
\AuditTag If any CP item cannot be discharged (e.g.\ no viable emission ledger, no recovery surrogate,
or no stable record-algebra choice), the manuscript must downgrade the claim to interface-only language
and record the failure in the evidence map (Appendix~\ref{app:minimal_failure_point_templates}).

\paragraph{Allowed fallbacks (conservative).}
\AuditTag The following downgrades are acceptable and must be explicitly labeled:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Delay-only closure.} Keep only delay/lapse and trap-leakage diagnostics (Appendix~\ref{app:leakage_kernel})
without claiming information recovery.
\item \textbf{Capacity-only closure.} Keep only area/capacity mapping (Appendix~\ref{app:bh_planck_capacity_calibration})
without claiming evaporation dynamics.
\item \textbf{Pointer-only closure.} Keep only external rigidity targets and observational hooks
(Appendix~\ref{app:bh_wormholes_pointer}).
\end{itemize}
