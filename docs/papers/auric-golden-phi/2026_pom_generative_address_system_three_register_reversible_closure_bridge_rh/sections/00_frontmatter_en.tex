\begin{abstract}
This paper restates object existence using auditable semantics based on "address prior to value": we define $\Read:\Addr\rightharpoonup \mathrm{ValDom}$ as a partial read function, where "undefined" is strictly distinguished from any numeric value (including $0$); we use $\NULL$ solely as notation for undefined ($\NULL\notin\mathrm{ValDom}$). An object "exists" if and only if there exists an address such that the read is defined and auditable.\par
Under the minimal axiom system POMmin, we construct stable syntax and fold normalization $\Fold_m:\Omega_m\twoheadrightarrow X_m$ and provide mechanically auditable uniqueness of normal form and equality judgment mechanisms. Within the stable address space, we generate in situ definitions of operations $(\oplus,\otimes)$ and prove their isomorphism with the semiring $(\NN,+,\times)$. Further, we introduce ledger-axis injectification: by externally placing fold residuals into the prime register (prime-register), we establish lifting/replay and establish information ledger identities to decompose visible entropy and residual information; within the decidable fragment of projection words (projection word, PW), we prove uniqueness of normal form and equality decidability; under the strong type auditing protocol of unitary slice, we obtain soundness: any zero-point certificate passing auditing can only lie on $\Re(s)=\tfrac12$, and nearby points read undefined ($\NULL$) within the closure.\par
Finally, under the assumptions that BridgeRH and address completeness, injectability, and certificate completeness hold, we provide a conditional reduction theorem $\mathrm{BridgeRH}\Rightarrow\mathrm{RH}$, and formalize each bridge premise as a falsifiable failure witness.
\end{abstract}
