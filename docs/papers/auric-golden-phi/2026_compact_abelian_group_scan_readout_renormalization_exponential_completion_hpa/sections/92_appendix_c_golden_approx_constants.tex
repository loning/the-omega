\section{Appendix C: Golden Parameters and the Approximation-Constant Chain for Suppressing Short Returns}
\label{app:golden-approx}

Let $\alpha\in(0,1)\setminus\QQ$. Its continued-fraction convergents $p_k/q_k$ form a best-approximation sequence and control recurrence and discrepancy of the Kronecker sequence $\{n\alpha\}$. The golden ratio $\varphi$ has continued fraction $[1;1,1,\dots]$, and is therefore extremal in the sense of ``slowest denominator growth among convergents and approximation errors hardest to further improve.'' This extremality supports a parameter-selection rule for suppressing short pseudo-periods: under fixed finite sample length $N$, avoid small-denominator approximations $q\alpha\approx p$, thereby delaying significant near-return times.
