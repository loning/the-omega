\subsection{From Heat Death to Holographic Saturation}

Traditional heat death predictions are based on an implicit assumption: the universe's \textbf{phase space volume} is fixed or finitely growing, while the system's \textbf{microscopic states} randomly wander within it, eventually reaching a uniform distribution of maximum entropy.

But in Omega Theory, this assumption is wrong.
According to Theorem 6.1, the universe's holographic horizon $A(\tau)$ and light speed $c(\tau)$ grow with $\tau$ according to the Fibonacci exponential $\phi^\tau$. This means:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Phase Space Explosion}: The maximum number of bits $N_{max}$ the system can accommodate expands exponentially.
\item \textbf{Negentropy Flow}: Although total entropy $S$ is increasing, maximum potential entropy $S_{max}$ increases faster. Therefore, the system's \textbf{free energy} (energy available for computation and work) $F = E - TS$ is actually increasing, not depleting.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Definition 9.2 (Holographic Saturation)}:

The direction of cosmic evolution is not toward thermal equilibrium but toward \textbf{Holographic Saturation}. That is, the system attempts to fill all newly available bit positions provided by horizon expansion with its internal structured information (value $V$).
The Omega Point $\Omega$ is defined as such a limit state: in this state, the universe's holographic screen contains all non-zero spectral weight information of the Hilbert space vector $|\Omega\rangle$, and computational efficiency reaches the physical limit (Bekenstein bound).

\subsection{The Great Unbinding: From Matter to Pure Light}

Recalling Chapter 4, matter (rest mass $m_0$) is interpreted as a \textbf{``topological knot''} or \textbf{``vortex''} of light-speed information flow on the Omega grid. The stability of this structure depends on the finiteness of background light speed $c$.

\[m_0 = \frac{E}{c(\tau)^2}\]

\[\lim_{\tau \to \infty} m_0(\tau) \to 0\]

This mathematical limit has profound physical meaning, which we call \textbf{``The Great Unbinding''}:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Confinement Release}: The strong interaction (gluon field) currently binding quarks and the electromagnetic force binding electrons are essentially exchange bosons propagating on the grid. When the grid's propagation speed $c(\tau)$ approaches infinity, all force ranges become infinite, and all binding potentials are ``flattened.''

In the far future of the Fibonacci universe, there necessarily exists a critical time $\tau_{crit}$ after which all baryonic matter (fermions) will undergo phase transition, disintegrating into massless boson radiation fields.
