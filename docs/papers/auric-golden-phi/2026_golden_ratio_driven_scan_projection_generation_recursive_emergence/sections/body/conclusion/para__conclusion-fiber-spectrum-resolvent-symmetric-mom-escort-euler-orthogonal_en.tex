\paragraph{Rational completeness of fiber spectrum and commutatively symmetric MOM coordinates: finite reconstruction, uniform approximation, zero-temperature concentration, and Euler defect energy}
At the fixed-resolution \(m\) level, the collision moments and escort mechanism in this paper not only provide exponential-scale pressure/spectral readouts but also yield a suite of strict ``finite certificatization'' interfaces: they unify the discrete spectrum of fiber multiplicity, the learnable space of commutatively symmetric observables, and the cross-energy of Euler defects into computable and auditable finite objects. The conclusive interface can be summarized as:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Rational structure and complete reconstruction of fiber-spectrum resolvent: finitely many power-sum moments determine all poles and multiplicities.}
  For fixed \(m\), the power-sum moment generating function \(H_m(u)=\sum_{q\ge 0}S_q(m)u^q\) has a strict partial-fraction expansion whose pole set and residues encode the distinct fiber multiplicity values \(\{a_j\}\) and their multiplicities \(\{c_j\}\), respectively (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-fiber-spectrum-resolvent-rational}).
  Furthermore, if \(R\) is the number of distinct multiplicity values at this level, then at most \(2R\) power-sum data points \(\{S_q(m)\}_{q=0}^{2R-1}\) uniquely reconstruct \(H_m\), and generically one fewer datum loses uniqueness (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-fiber-spectrum-finite-reconstruction-sharp}).

  \item \textbf{Uniform approximation of commutatively symmetric observables: \texorpdfstring{$\{p_q\}$}{pq} generate all learnable symmetric statistics.}
  On the probability simplex \(\Delta(X_m)\), the power-sum features \(p_q(w)=\sum_x w(x)^q\) (\(2\le q\le |X_m|\)) generate an algebra dense in the uniform norm in the space of all commutatively symmetric continuous observables (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-mom-symmetric-observables-stone-weierstrass}).
  Thus in a learning/audit caliber ``insensitive to type labels,'' finitely many collision-moment features suffice to uniformly approximate any symmetric statistic.

  \item \textbf{Collision-moment coordinate chart: in generic position, finite power sums yield local coordinates for symmetric distribution space.}
  On the open dense set of simplex interior points with pairwise distinct weights, the map \(w\mapsto(p_2(w),\dots,p_n(w))\) has Jacobian controlled by the Vandermonde determinant, thus becoming a local coordinate chart in the commutative-symmetric sense (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-power-sum-vandermonde-jacobian}; Corollary~\ref{cor:pom-power-sum-local-chart}).

  \item \textbf{Quantitative zero-temperature limit: escort exhibits \texorpdfstring{$m^2$}{m^2}-order super-exponential concentration at \texorpdfstring{$q\sim m$}{q~m} scaling.}
  The escort distribution \(w_{m,q}(x)\propto d_m(x)^q\) for sets ``below worst exponent \(\alpha_m-\varepsilon\)'' satisfies the explicit upper bound \(w_{m,q}(A_m(\varepsilon))\le |X_m|e^{-q\varepsilon m}\), so when \(q=\Theta(m)\) we have \(\exp(-\Theta(m^2))\) super-exponential contraction (Corollary~\ref{cor:pom-escort-zero-temperature-superexp-concentration}).

  \item \textbf{Oracle inversion budget curve: explicit readout of success exponent, capacity exponent, and thermal-phase cut.}
  Under \(w_m\), the exponential leading term of optimal inversion success rate under restricted bit budget is strictly given by \(I_w\) via a folded variational formula and can be recast as a two-branch extremum (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-oracle-success-variational-laplace}).
  The corresponding capacity exponent admits the variational representation \(\sup_{\alpha}(f(\alpha)+\min(\alpha,a))\), dominated in the thermal-phase regime by the single level \(\alpha=a\), so the multifractal counting spectrum can be read out directly from the capacity curve (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-oracle-capacity-thermal-cut}; Corollary~\ref{cor:pom-oracle-capacity-slope-readout}).
  Simultaneously, microcanonical conditioning and escort have zero relative-entropy rate on level sets (information equivalence), the critical budget window satisfies a Gaussian limit and yields a second-order inversion formula (Theorems~\ref{thm:pom-microcanonical-escort-kl-rate-zero},~\ref{thm:pom-oracle-critical-window-gaussian}; Corollary~\ref{cor:pom-oracle-critical-window-bit-inversion}).

  \item \textbf{Orthogonal energy of Euler defect: cross-term \texorpdfstring{$L^2$}{L2} energy equals minimal distance to additive subspace.}
  Under the uniform measure on \(\widehat G=\prod_i\widehat{G_i}\), coordinate averaging operators induce a Hoeffding-type orthogonal decomposition and yield a Pythagorean identity (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-euler-defect-orthogonal-pythagoras}).
  Accordingly, the total energy of second- and higher-order cross-components strictly equals the minimal \(L^2\) distance to the ``constant \(\,+\) axis-additive'' subspace \(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{add}}\) (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-euler-defect-min-action-gauge}), providing a canonical Euler defect energy gauge.
\end{enumerate}
