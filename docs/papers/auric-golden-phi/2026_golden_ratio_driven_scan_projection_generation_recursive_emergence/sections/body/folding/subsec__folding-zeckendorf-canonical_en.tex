\subsection{Zeckendorf Normal Form}\label{subsec:folding-zeckendorf-canonical}

Zeckendorf's theorem asserts that for any $N\in\NN$, there exists a unique representation (\cite{Zeckendorf1972})
\[
N=\sum_{k\ge1}c_k F_{k+1},\qquad c_k\in\{0,1\},\quad c_kc_{k+1}=0.
\]
We regard $(c_k)_{k\ge1}$ as the Zeckendorf digit sequence of $N$ and denote it by $Z(N)=(c_1,c_2,\dots)$.

Define the truncation map
\[
\pi_m(c_1,c_2,\dots)=(c_1,\dots,c_m)\in X_m.
\]
