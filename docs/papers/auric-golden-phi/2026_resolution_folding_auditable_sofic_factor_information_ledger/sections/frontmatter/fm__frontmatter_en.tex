\begin{abstract}
We study a finite-window normalization map induced by Fibonacci (Zeckendorf) numeration and use it as a single organizing interface that closes an auditable loop:
\[
\Omega_m \xrightarrow{\ \Fold_m\ } X_m \xrightarrow{\ \Phi_m\ } Y_m
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{computable covers, fiber statistics, and an entropy ledger}.
\]
At resolution $m$, microstates are $\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m$ and stable types are the golden-mean language $X_m$ (no adjacent $11$).
The \emph{resolution folding} $\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m$ is defined as ``value $\to$ Zeckendorf normal form $\to$ truncate'' and admits an order-independent local rewriting implementation; hence it produces a unique canonical type and is compatible with a fold-aware restriction across resolutions.
\par
We place the construction in the broader \emph{Ostrowski design space}: for any irrational $\alpha$, the corresponding normalization-then-truncation fold $\Fold_m^{(\alpha)}$ is functorial across $m$, and the \emph{binary} alphabet constraint forces the golden endpoint $\alpha=\varphi^{-1}$.
In the binary case, naive truncation does not commute with folding; we quantify the mismatch by a \emph{gauge anomaly} functional $G_m$, show it can be full length in the worst case, and compute its uniform-baseline density $g_\ast=4/9$ with a finite-state (synchronizing-kernel) certificate.
\par
At the sequence level, the induced sliding block code $\Phi_m$ yields a sofic shift $Y_m$ with an explicit bi-resolving presentation and a computable Fischer-cover interface.
Beyond cover-level invariants, we introduce a \emph{fiber spectrum} through collision moments
\(
S_k(m):=\sum_{x\in X_m} d_m(x)^k
\)
of the fiber multiplicity $d_m(x)=|\Fold_m^{-1}(x)|$.
For each fixed $k\ge 2$ we construct a finite nonnegative integer \emph{moment kernel} $A_k$ such that $S_k(m)$ is a matrix coefficient of $A_k^m$ for all sufficiently large $m$; hence $\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}\log S_k(m)=\log\rho(A_k)$, and a symmetry quotient yields polynomial-size compressed kernels.
We also give an explicit \emph{online} normalizer: $\Fold_\infty$ is computed by a deterministic finite-state transducer with delay $3$, and this delay is provably minimal.
\par
Finally, we record an \emph{entropy ledger identity} for arbitrary $\mu\in\Delta(\Omega_m)$:
\[
H(\mu)=H(P_m\mu)+\mathbb{E}[\log d_m]-D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|Q_mP_m\mu),
\]
decomposing microstate entropy into visible type entropy, fiber volume, and a residual KL term. Combined with exact independent-set factorization of fibers, this yields auditable certificates (constants, spectral radii, tail bounds) and a finite-sample verification protocol.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.}
Ostrowski numeration; golden mean shift; Zeckendorf normal form; online normalization; local rewriting system; sliding block code; sofic shift; Fischer cover; Perron--Frobenius measure; collision moments; entropy ledger; auditable certificate.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Main contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Design-space rigidity (Ostrowski families and the golden endpoint).} We formalize a general normalization-then-truncation fold $\Fold_m^{(\alpha)}$ for Ostrowski numeration systems and prove functorial multiscale compatibility. Requiring a binary alphabet at all resolutions forces $\alpha=\varphi^{-1}$, hence the golden-mean syntax as the minimal binary member of the design space.
  \item \textbf{Canonical folding and quantified multiscale obstruction.} In the golden-mean case we realize $\Fold_m$ as a confluent local rewriting normalizer producing a unique canonical form in $X_m$, and we prove idempotence and fold-aware multiscale compatibility (Theorem~\ref{thm:fold-normal-form-multiscale}). We quantify the non-commutativity of naive truncation by the gauge anomaly $G_m$, prove a worst-case full-length obstruction, and compute its uniform-baseline density $g_\ast=4/9$ with a finite-state certificate (Propositions~\ref{prop:gauge-anomaly-max} and \ref{prop:gauge-anomaly-density-49}, Appendix~\ref{app:gauge-anomaly-transducer}).
  \item \textbf{Online normalization with optimal delay.} We construct an explicit deterministic finite-state transducer computing $\Fold_\infty$ with delay $3$ and prove that no delay-$2$ online normalizer exists; this isolates an intrinsic \emph{time defect} of Zeckendorf normalization.
  \item \textbf{Sofic factor and computable cover interface.} We construct $Y_m=\Phi_m(\{0,1\}^{\ZZ})$ via an explicit suffix-state labeled graph and prove one-step distinguishability (right- and left-resolving). This yields an auditable Fischer-cover interface for entropy/PF/Parry data and cover-level zeta determinants (Theorem~\ref{thm:phi-sofic-fischer} and Appendix~\ref{app:pf-parry-zeta}).
  \item \textbf{Fiber spectra via collision moments.} We elevate the fiber multiplicity profile to a spectrum of collision moments $S_k(m)=\sum_x d_m(x)^k$ and construct, for each fixed $k\ge 2$, a finite nonnegative integer moment kernel $A_k$ whose Perron root controls the exponential growth rate of $S_k(m)$. A symmetry quotient yields polynomial-size compressed kernels, providing a computable interface to a family of auditable invariants beyond cover-level PF data.
  \item \textbf{Ledger identity and auditable certificates.} We record an entropy ledger identity that isolates the residual KL term beyond folded types and fiber volume (Theorem~\ref{thm:information-ledger}). We also give an exact fiber factorization into path independent sets, yielding a closed product formula $d_m(x)=\prod_i F_{\ell_i+2}$ and explicit universal bounds (Appendix~\ref{app:fiber-multiplicity} and Proposition~\ref{prop:fiber-multiplicity-bound}), together with finite-sample total-variation certificates (Theorems~\ref{thm:tv-certificate-hist} and \ref{thm:markov-tv-envelope}).
\end{itemize}
