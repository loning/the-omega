\section{Causality Constraint: Light-Cone Clipping in the Observation-Theory Expression}

The entry on relativistic causality notes: in special relativity, information and influence cannot exceed the speed of light; light propagation determines the spacetime causal structure and can divide events into causally influenceable and non-influenceable sets via light cones \cite{EinsteinOnlineCausal}.
At the semantic level, this paper absorbs this by constraining the readout accessible domain: the observation operator $q_m$ permits only data from within the causal past light cone to enter, thereby expressing the invariance of light speed as ``light-cone clipping of reachable information'' without extending to ontological equivalence claims.
