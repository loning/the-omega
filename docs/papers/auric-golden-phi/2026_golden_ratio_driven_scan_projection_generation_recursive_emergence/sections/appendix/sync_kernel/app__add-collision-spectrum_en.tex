\subsubsection{Addition as Projection: The Collision Spectrum of the 10-State Synchronization Kernel vs.\ the 40-State Real-Input Kernel}\label{app:add-collision-spectrum}

\paragraph{Addition = lifting to \texorpdfstring{$\{0,1\}^2$}{\{0,1\}^2} then projecting back to \texorpdfstring{$X_\infty$}{X\_infty}}
At the stable address level, Zeckendorf addition can be written as
\[
c\oplus d=\Fold_\infty(c+d),
\]
where \((c+d)_j:=c_j+d_j\in\{0,1,2\}\) is the digitwise sum and \(\Fold_\infty\) is the online normalization gate that maps \(\{0,1,2\}\) back to \(\{0,1\}\), which can be realized by the 10-state synchronization kernel \(K_{\mathrm{sync}}\) (Table \ref{tab:sync-kernel-A-compare}).
Thus the mechanism of ``addition as a single projection'' can be viewed as:
\[
\{0,1\}^{\ZZ}\times\{0,1\}^{\ZZ}\xrightarrow{\ (x,y)\mapsto x+y\ }\{0,1,2\}^{\ZZ}\xrightarrow{\ K_{\mathrm{sync}}\ }\{0,1\}^{\ZZ}\supseteq X_\infty,
\]
i.e., first lifting the input to two bit streams (the ontological field), then projecting back to the stable address surface via the normalization kernel.

\paragraph{Collision moments and spatial loss spectral constants}
Viewing the above formula as a deterministic transducer (input alphabet \(\{0,1\}^2\), output alphabet \(\{0,1\}\)),
for window length \(m\) and output block \(y\in\{0,1\}^m\), let
\[
d^{\mathrm{add}}_m(y):=\#\{(x,x')\in(\{0,1\}^m)^2:\ \Fold_m(x+x')=y\},
\qquad
S^{\mathrm{add}}_q(m):=\sum_{y\in\{0,1\}^m}d^{\mathrm{add}}_m(y)^q\quad(q\ge 2).
\]
The exponential growth rate of \(S^{\mathrm{add}}_q(m)\) is controlled by a family of finite non-negative integer moment-kernels \(A^{\mathrm{add}}_q\), with
\[
r^{\mathrm{add}}_q:=\rho\!\left(A^{\mathrm{add}}_q\right),
\qquad
S^{\mathrm{add}}_q(m)=\Theta\!\left((r^{\mathrm{add}}_q)^m\right).
\]
In particular, when \(q=2\), there is a probabilistic interpretation: if two pairs of inputs \((x_1,x'_1),(x_2,x'_2)\) are drawn uniformly and independently from \(\{0,1\}^{2m}\), then
\[
\mathbb{P}\bigl[\Fold_m(x_1+x'_1)=\Fold_m(x_2+x'_2)\bigr]=\frac{S^{\mathrm{add}}_2(m)}{16^m},
\qquad
h^{\mathrm{add}}_2=\log\!\left(\frac{16}{r^{\mathrm{add}}_2}\right).
\]

\paragraph{Real-input constraint: the 40-state lifting}
If one requires that both input streams themselves be legal Zeckendorf orbits (each forbidding adjacent \(11\)), one can incorporate this constraint into the state, obtaining the 40-state real-input synchronization kernel \(K_{\mathrm{real}}\) (Table \ref{tab:sync-kernel-A-compare}).
Performing the same collision-moment compilation for the ``addition projection'' on this kernel yields the real-input values \(r^{\mathrm{add,real}}_q\).
