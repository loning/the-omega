\section{Holographic equivalence closure contract (scope, PT carriers, and failure points)}
\label{app:holo_scope_contract}

\paragraph{Purpose.}
\AuditTag This appendix records a reader-facing contract for using the phrase \emph{holographic equivalence}
inside the tick\,+\,CAP discipline of this manuscript.
It is a \emph{scope and gatekeeping} module: it introduces no new theorem-level premises into the folding core.

\paragraph{What is and is not meant by ``holographic equivalence'' here.}
\AuditTag The manuscript does \emph{not} claim an unconditional AdS/CFT duality, nor does it assume any specific
asymptotic boundary (AdS) or large-$N$ limit.
Instead, ``holographic'' is used in the operational sense already implicit in the paper:
finite readout on a declared screen/window family generates a finite (boundary) observable algebra,
and certain bulk-facing reportables are constrained or reconstructible from that algebra within a declared
window, hypothesis class, and error budget.

\subsection{Holo0: declared objects and the minimal dictionary}
\label{subsec:holo0_declared_objects}

\paragraph{Boundary object.}
\InterfaceTag The boundary object is a region-indexed algebra family
\[
R\ \longmapsto\ \mathcal{A}_{\partial}(R),
\]
constructed from the protocol's addressing/screen layer.
Concretely, we take $\mathcal{A}_{\partial}(R)$ to be the protocol-induced local algebra
restricted to screen regions (Appendix~\ref{app:holo_boundary_algebra_from_screens}).

\paragraph{Bulk reportables.}
\InterfaceTag The bulk side is \emph{not} an ontic continuum.
It is a declared registry of reportable quantities $\mathfrak{Obs}_{\mathrm{bulk}}$
that the manuscript already closes (or treats as interface representatives),
such as $\chi$-based weak-field proxies, determinant/pressure certificates, and selected EFT-representative data
(Appendix~\ref{app:holo_bulk_observables_registry}).

\paragraph{Holographic dictionary.}
\AuditTag A holographic dictionary in this paper is a triple
\[
\mathfrak{D}_{\mathrm{holo}}:=
\bigl(\mathcal{A}_{\partial}(\cdot),\ \mathfrak{Obs}_{\mathrm{bulk}},\ \mathcal{R}\bigr),
\]
where $\mathcal{R}$ is a \emph{reconstruction claim} that maps (some) bulk reportables to boundary observables,
either exactly (PT) or up to an explicit error budget (CP).

\subsection{Holo0: the only allowed theorem-like claim format}
\label{subsec:holo0_allowed_claim_format}

\paragraph{Windowed comparability (the master format).}
\AuditTag Any statement of the form ``bulk quantity $X$ is encoded on the boundary'' is only permitted
in the following windowed form.
Fix a declared protocol state $(m,n,K)$ (Appendix~\ref{subsec:tick_cap_joint_protocol_state}),
fix a finite observable registry $\mathfrak{Obs}_{\le N}$ (Appendix~\ref{app:observable_algebra_state_base}),
and fix a reconstruction map $\mathcal{R}$ (Heisenberg picture) acting on the boundary algebra.
Then an admissible claim is:
\[
\forall\,\mathcal{O}\in\mathfrak{Obs}_{\le N},\qquad
\Bigl|\langle \mathcal{O}\rangle_{\mathrm{bulk}}-\langle \mathcal{R}(\mathcal{O})\rangle_{\partial}\Bigr|
\le \epsilon_N(m,n,K),
\]
where $\epsilon_N$ must be an \emph{auditable} budget term (finite-family envelope and/or error propagation bound),
not a verbal ``higher order'' remainder (cf.\ Appendix~\ref{app:matching_envelope_theoremization} and
Appendix~\ref{app:protocol_to_continuum_error_control}).

\subsection{Holo0: minimal failure points (mandatory downgrade rules)}
\label{subsec:holo0_failure_points}

\paragraph{Failure point H0a (hidden knobs / non-invariant verdicts).}
\AuditTag If a purported holographic verdict depends on an undeclared choice
(e.g.\ a representation, coordinate convention, unreported tie-break, or an implicit kernel family member),
then it violates the invariance/legality threshold (Proposition~\ref{prop:rigidity_legality_invariant_verdicts})
and must be downgraded to a representation-dependent diagnostic.

\paragraph{Failure point H0b (comparability class not closed).}
\AuditTag If the boundary quantity and the bulk reportable are not defined within a shared comparability class
and declared units/schemes, then the statement is not well-typed.
In that case, the manuscript must revert to a matching-layer envelope statement
(Appendix~\ref{app:scheme_invariance_audit_contract} and Appendix~\ref{app:matching_envelope_theoremization})
or record the item as \textbf{[Open]} in Appendix~\ref{app:inference_ledger}.

\paragraph{Failure point H0c (identifiability boundary).}
\AuditTag If boundary readouts are restricted to class-function summaries (e.g.\ cycle-type histograms),
then reconstruction is limited by the identifiability boundary
(Proposition~\ref{prop:cycle_type_identifiability_boundary}).
Any statement that requires distinguishing two realizations with identical class-function summaries
must be downgraded or must enlarge the observable family.
