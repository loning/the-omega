\begin{abstract}
This comprehensive monograph unifies the series of papers in \texttt{docs/papers/auric-golden-phi/} under a single minimal auditable interface: underlying dynamical systems (compact groups / torus translations, or their geometric realization via cut-and-project), finite-resolution readout (windows / binary partitions / projections), canonical stabilization (golden-mean legal languages and Zeckendorf folding $\Fold_m$), preimage fibers (invisible degrees of freedom / time fibers), and the information-theoretic time arrow provided by the KS/SMB theorems. The goal of this paper is not to replace the proof chains of individual chapters, but rather to make explicit the shared objects and reusable theorem templates, and to indicate the ``instantiation location of each paper within the interface,'' thereby organizing scattered self-contained reasoning chains into a maintainable modular theory.
\end{abstract}
