\paragraph{Audit summary (force propagation and relaxation toy).} \AuditTag We simulate $N_p=12$ particles on a $64\times64$ periodic screen with a scalar field $\chi(x,t)$ that generates an effective potential $\Phi=-\gamma c^2(\chi-\chi_0)$ and force $F=-\nabla\Phi=\gamma c^2\nabla\chi$. The field follows a damped Klein--Gordon/telegraph form $\chi_{tt}+\alpha\chi_t=c_f^2\Delta\chi-\omega^2(\chi-\chi_{\mathrm{eq}}(t))$ with a smooth equilibrium-center shift at $t_\mathrm{switch}=40$ (torus). Particles include viscous damping $-\eta v$ so the orbit family relaxes into a new stable limit cycle around the post-switch equilibrium center. The table reports an explicit energy ledger $E_\mathrm{tot}=E_\mathrm{part}+E_\mathrm{field}$ and a stability proxy (RMS field deviation and particle-radius statistics) at fixed sampling times.
