\begin{corollary}[Double Rigid Locking of the Value \texorpdfstring{$12$}{12}: Boundary Three-Tower and Window-\(6\) Most Rigid Sector]\label{cor:window6-double-lock-12}
Under the boundary word tower caliber, the boundary mode counts satisfy \(|X_m^{\mathrm{bdry}}|=F_{m-2}\) (Remark \ref{rem:fib-lie-resonance-shift-by-4}; Table \ref{tab:zeckendorf_gut_signatures}), yielding the strict identity
\[
|X_4^{\mathrm{bdry}}|+|X_6^{\mathrm{bdry}}|+|X_8^{\mathrm{bdry}}|
=F_2+F_4+F_6
=1+3+8
=12.
\]
On the other hand, under the dyadic fold \(\mathrm{Fold}^{\mathrm{bin}}_6\) of window-\(6\) (Theorem \ref{thm:terminal-foldbin6-64-to-21-hist}), define the most rigid sector
\[
X_6^{\mathrm{rig}}
:=\{\,w\in X_6:\ d^{\mathrm{bin}}_6(w)\le 3\,\}.
\]
Then its cardinality is strictly locked by the degeneracy spectrum histogram:
\[
|X_6^{\mathrm{rig}}|
=\#\{d^{\mathrm{bin}}_6=2\}+\#\{d^{\mathrm{bin}}_6=3\}
=8+4
=12.
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The first identity follows directly by evaluating \(|X_m^{\mathrm{bdry}}|=F_{m-2}\) at \(m=4,6,8\).
The second identity is obtained by substituting the degeneracy histogram from Theorem \ref{thm:terminal-foldbin6-64-to-21-hist} into the definition. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Independence and Over-Determined Consistency of the Two Locking Mechanisms]\label{rem:window6-double-lock-12-independent}
The count of \(12\) from the boundary three-tower arises from the Zeckendorf--Fibonacci boundary counting interface; whereas the count of \(12\) for \(X_6^{\mathrm{rig}}\) depends solely on the fiber degeneracy spectrum of the dyadic fold (i.e., the histogram of \(d^{\mathrm{bin}}_6\)).
The two are produced by mutually distinct finite objects, so the ``appearance of \(12\)'' can be regarded as an over-determined consistency check: it is not obtained by specifying a continuous gauge group a priori, but is simultaneously forced by discrete audit invariants at the window-\(6\) anchor point.
\end{remark}
