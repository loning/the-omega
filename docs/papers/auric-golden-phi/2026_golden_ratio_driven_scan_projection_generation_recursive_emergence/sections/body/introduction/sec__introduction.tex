\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

Finite-window observation provides a unifying interface across dynamical systems, symbolic dynamics, and automata/coding theory. Given a single-generator action \(T^t\) and a binary projection readout \(s_t=\ind_W(T^t x)\), observed data are naturally organized as length-\(m\) sliding-window microstates \(\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m\). The central difficulty is that, as \(m\) grows, microstate spaces expand exponentially; treating microstates directly as ``concepts/types'' leads to sparsity and instability, obstructing auditable cross-resolution inference chains.
\par
The goal of this paper is to construct an auditable generative chain: from scan-projection readout to stable syntax, folded normal forms, and implementable arithmetic structures via statistical stability and recursive addressing. To guarantee verifiability, we strictly separate proof chains from interpretive prose: all formal claims are anchored to numbered definitions/theorems/propositions/corollaries, and explanatory terminology is never used as a proof premise. We fix the golden branch \(\alpha=\varphi^{-1}\) as the anti-resonant canonical representative under Hurwitz/Markov worst rational-approximation scales (\cite{Cassels1957,KuipersNiederreiter1974}), and use \(T\)-invariant measures and ergodic tools for frequency/stability statements (\cite{WaltersErgodicTheory}).

\subsection{Contribution checklist: auditable interfaces}\label{subsec:contrib-scope}
\textbf{The mathematical contributions of this paper} are concentrated in independently auditable structural results, summarized as the following interface modules:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Minimality of recursive addressing:} Recursive addressing is formalized as an endogenous mechanism of ``readable prefixes.'' When an address object is not instantiated, the evaluator \(\ind_{C_a}\) is undefined at the type layer and readout partial maps return \(\mathsf{NULL}\); once addresses are instantiated, derived cylinder families do not enlarge the visible \(\sigma\)-algebra but only reselect visible events inside prior layers, yielding \(\mathcal{G}^{(L+1)}\subseteq \mathcal{G}^{(L)}\) (Propositions \ref{prop:null-as-local-section-obstruction}, \ref{prop:recursive-addressing-refinement}; Corollary \ref{cor:recursive-addressing-visible-sigma-nonexpansion}).
  \item \textbf{Resolution folding and cross-scale compatibility:} We construct finite-window folding \(\Fold_m\) with computable realization and prove unified results for confluence, inverse-system compatibility, and sofic factor interfaces (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-suite}; see also Definition \ref{def:fold-word} and Proposition \ref{prop:fold-basic}).
  \item \textbf{Scan-depth vs. clarity quantitative law:} Under the interface ``address precedes concept value,'' decidability at resolution is encoded as clopen structure under cylinder topology, and ``deeper scans, higher clarity'' is quantified by optimal error profiles and boundary-cylinder dimensions (Proposition \ref{prop:spg-decidable-clopen}, Definition \ref{def:spg-scan-error-profile}, Theorem \ref{thm:spg-clarity-boundary-dimension}; also Theorem \ref{thm:pom-visibility-to-error-two-sided}). In address semantics, this exponentiated clarity further decomposes into two independent failures, ``statistical \(\mathsf{NULL}\)'' and ``de-collision,'' unified by a dual-budget addressing-capacity law (Theorem \ref{thm:spg-double-budget-address-capacity}).
  \item \textbf{Projection Ontic Mathematics and information ledger:} We define scan-projection generators and projection-word calculus (Definitions \ref{def:pom-spg}, \ref{def:pom-projection-word}, \ref{def:pom-projword-2cat}) and derive a closed-form ledger identity for relative-entropy defects together with consequences (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-kl-ledger}, Corollary \ref{cor:pom-ent-increase-equals-kl}).
  \item \textbf{Readout-tower rewriting and decidable closure:} On local readout-tower fragments we prove termination and confluence, obtain normal forms (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-rewrite-termination-confluence}), and derive decidability of the word problem and an auditable closure loop (Propositions \ref{prop:pom-projword-decidable}, \ref{prop:pom-rewrite-audit-loop}).
  \item \textbf{Resonance mechanisms and Hankel-rank collapse:} For arbitrary-order collision matrices, we give finite-state moment-kernel compilation and Perron principal-spectrum characterization (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-collision-kernel-ak}), plus a sufficient-and-necessary criterion for Hankel-rank descent under modal constraints, turning resonance into a testable mechanism (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-moment-resonance}).
  \item \textbf{Chebotarev capacity law with synchronization subtraction:} Under kernelized GRH/uniform spectral-gap assumptions, we lift finite-layer Chebotarev counting along the profinite axis and bound equidistribution error on cylinder sets (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-profinite-axis}); in online semantics we incorporate synchronization thresholds as explicit subtraction terms in capacity inequalities, translating ``synchronize first, homogenize later'' into auditable inequality families (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-sync-subtracted-chebotarev-capacity}).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Scope boundaries:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Folding limit under rotations (Sturmian) equals Parry:} not true in general; this paper uses the Parry distribution as a maximum-entropy baseline and separates ``finite-sample error'' from ``model mismatch'' via explicit decomposition terms (Section \ref{sec:experiments}).
  \item \textbf{Interpretive language is not part of the proof chain:} all derivations are governed by numbered internal results plus externally cited standard theorems.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Verifiable outputs of this paper: quantitative targets}
The primary outputs of this paper are auditable in a purely mathematical sense:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Auditable certificates for folding histograms:} We provide explicit upper bounds for cross-resolution consistency residuals and folding-boundary perturbation events, yielding reproducible structural audit metrics (Section \ref{subsec:multiscale-residual}).
  \item \textbf{Resonance criteria:} For higher-order collision matrices we provide sufficient-and-necessary criteria linking recursion order to Hankel-rank collapse, thereby localizing resonance as a modal-constraint mechanism (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-moment-resonance}).
  \item \textbf{Spectral projection entropy-rate fingerprints:} We report \(r_q\) and \(h_q=\log(2^q/r_q)\) for \(q=2,\dots,23\) (\(q=2,3,4\) from finite-state collision kernels; \(q\ge 5\) from verified integer-coefficient recursions), see Tables \ref{tab:fold_collision_renyi_spectrum}, \ref{tab:fold_collision_moment_spectrum_k9_17}, and \ref{tab:fold_collision_moment_spectrum_k18_23}.
\end{itemize}
