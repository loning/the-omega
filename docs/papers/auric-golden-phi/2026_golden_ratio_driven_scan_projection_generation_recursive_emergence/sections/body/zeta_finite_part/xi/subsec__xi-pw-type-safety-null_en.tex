\subsubsection{\texorpdfstring{$\mathbf{PW}$}{PW} Type Safety: Addressability of Unitary Slice and NULL}\label{subsec:xi-pw-type-safety-null}
This chapter's \(\Xi_{\Delta,L}\) zero auditing criterion is built upon the fixed geometry of the unitary slice (\(|u|=1\); Remark \ref{rem:xi-critical-line-unit-circle}), using spectral crossing on the \(\theta\)-axis as an auditable interface (Proposition \ref{prop:xi-unitary-slice-spectral-crossing} and Remark \ref{rem:xi-unitary-slice-operator-audit}).
On the other hand, in the projection ontology semantics, concepts are \emph{address-first} (Section \ref{sec:recursive-addressing}): without address, readout is undefined (null); with address, readout becomes a verifiable projection/certificate.
This subsection compiles "reading out points off \(\Re(s)=\tfrac12\) as NULL (no address)" into a soundness proposition of the \(\mathbf{PW}\) type system, explicitly distinguishing it from the completeness bridge required for RH.

\paragraph{Visible Domain and Projection Gate in Unit Circle Phase Arithmetic}
Appendix \ref{app:unit-circle-phase-gate} provides the parameter-free compactification gate
\(\upsilon(x)=\pi^{-1}\arctan(x)\in(-\tfrac12,\tfrac12)\)
and its unit circle equivalent embedding
\(\iota(x)=\frac{1+\mathrm{i}x}{1-\mathrm{i}x}\in\TT\)
which can be extended to a homeomorphism \(\iota:\widehat{\RR}\to\TT\) on the projective compactification \(\widehat{\RR}:=\RR\cup\{\infty\}\) (\S\ref{subsec:unit-circle-phase-lift}).
Thus, \emph{at the coordinate gate level alone} there is no mandatory appearance of null values: the compactification gate itself is a well-defined invertible map, and any non-invertibility arises from subsequent "partial observation/projection readout" (Appendix \S\ref{subsec:unit-circle-phase-information}) and the \(\mathsf{NULL}\) convention of address semantics (Section \ref{sec:spg}).
\par
The key point of this section is: when fixing the verification interface of \(\Xi\) to the unitary slice, the verifiable continuous parameter axis is further compressed to a \emph{visible slice}; objects deviating from this slice, even though formally writable at the analytic level, have no address under our closure and their readout degenerates to \(\mathsf{NULL}\) under null value semantics.
In the gauge groupoid caliber, this visible slice can be viewed as a gauge section, and degrees of freedom deviating from the slice belong to gauge fibers; without externalizing record axes, they are treated as \(\mathsf{NULL}\) at the type layer (see subsection \ref{subsec:pom-gauge-groupoid}).

\paragraph{Phase-Type Parameter Domain (unitary slice)}
Let
\[
\mathsf{Phase}:=\{u\in\CC:\ |u|=1\}=\{e^{i\theta}:\ \theta\in\RR\}.
\]
For any \(L>1\), from the definition \(u_L(s)=L^{2s-1}\) we obtain
\[
\Re(s)=\tfrac12\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad |u_L(s)|=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad u_L(s)\in \mathsf{Phase}
\]
(Remark \ref{rem:xi-critical-line-unit-circle}).

\begin{definition}[Readout with Visible Domain Constraint and \texorpdfstring{$\mathsf{NULL}$}{NULL}]\label{def:xi-visible-read-null}
Given a state space \(\Omega\), an observation map \(\operatorname{obs}:\Omega\rightharpoonup X\) (allowing partial maps), and a visible domain \(X_{\mathrm{vis}}\subseteq X\).
Fix a closure/checker \(\mathrm{Chk}\) (in our context, take the combination of "\(\mathbf{PW}\) normalization closure + unitary-slice audit interface": its output is \(\mathsf{pass}/\mathsf{fail}\)).
Define the visible readout with visible domain constraint
\[
\mathrm{read}(\omega)=
\begin{cases}
\operatorname{obs}(\omega), &
\omega\in\mathrm{Dom}(\operatorname{obs}),\ \operatorname{obs}(\omega)\in X_{\mathrm{vis}},\ \mathrm{Chk}(\omega)=\mathsf{pass},\\
\mathsf{NULL}, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
where \(\mathrm{read}(\omega)=\mathsf{NULL}\) indicates the readout is undefined (not addressable).
This "otherwise" can be decomposed into three structural sources (all occurring at the \emph{denotational/type layer} rather than numerical layer):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Semantic null (address missing):} Address precedes value; if the address is not given, the corresponding concept value is undefined, denoted \(\mathsf{NULL}\), and is not equivalent to the numerical value \(0\) (see Section \ref{sec:spg}; also Section \ref{sec:recursive-addressing}).
  \item \textbf{Protocol null (type system prohibition):} Interpretation of the object requires degrees of freedom beyond \(X_{\mathrm{vis}}\), or requires coordinate axes explicitly prohibited by interface protocol (typically: requiring radial mode with \(|u|\neq 1\), while the visible domain only allows unitary slice), thus cannot be typed under the given closure/checker and is rejected.
  \item \textbf{Collision null (insufficient side information):} When \(\operatorname{obs}\) is a many-to-one compression and one wishes to lift the readout to (approximately) invertible with a given confidence caliber, record axes must be externalized to make the extended readout injective; if the record axis budget is insufficient, different preimages cannot be uniquely reconstructed in the error/confidence sense allowed by protocol, thus the readout degenerates equivalently to \(\mathsf{NULL}\) (see Proposition \ref{prop:xi-null-three-way}).
\end{enumerate}
In the \(\Xi\)-interface of this subsection, one can take \(\Omega=\CC\), \(\operatorname{obs}(s)=u_L(s)=L^{2s-1}\), and let \(X_{\mathrm{vis}}=\mathsf{Phase}\); thus \(\Re(s)\neq\tfrac12\Rightarrow \operatorname{obs}(s)\notin X_{\mathrm{vis}}\), and its readout directly degenerates to \(\mathsf{NULL}\) by definition.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Visible Symmetry (Readout Automorphism)]\label{def:xi-read-visible-symmetry}
Continue with the notation from Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}.
A bijection \(g:\Omega\to\Omega\) is called a \textbf{visible symmetry} if for any \(\omega\in\Omega\) we have
\[
\mathrm{read}(g\omega)=\mathrm{read}(\omega).
\]
Denote the set of all visible symmetries by \(\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{read}}(\Omega)\).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Orbits of Visible Symmetry and Readout Fibers]\label{prop:xi-read-fiber-orbits}
Continue with the notation from Definition \ref{def:xi-read-visible-symmetry}.
Then the action orbits of \(\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{read}}(\Omega)\) on \(\Omega\) are precisely the fibers of \(\mathrm{read}\): for any \(\omega\in\Omega\) we have
\[
\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{read}}(\Omega)\cdot\omega
=\{\omega'\in\Omega:\ \mathrm{read}(\omega')=\mathrm{read}(\omega)\}.
\]
In particular, under projection ontology semantics, "symmetry" can be understood as automorphism degrees of freedom within readout fibers; this is consistent with the gauge groupoid caliber in \S\ref{subsec:pom-gauge-groupoid}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By Definition \ref{def:xi-read-visible-symmetry}, for any \(g\in\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{read}}(\Omega)\) and any \(\omega\in\Omega\), we have \(\mathrm{read}(g\omega)=\mathrm{read}(\omega)\), so the orbit \(\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{read}}(\Omega)\cdot\omega\subseteq \mathrm{read}^{-1}(\mathrm{read}(\omega))\).
Conversely, if \(\omega'\) satisfies \(\mathrm{read}(\omega')=\mathrm{read}(\omega)\), define a bijection \(g\) swapping \(\omega\) with \(\omega'\) (identity elsewhere), then \(g\in\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{read}}(\Omega)\) and \(g\omega=\omega'\).
This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[\texorpdfstring{$\mathsf{NULL}$}{NULL} Three-way Decomposition and Injectivization Principle]\label{prop:xi-null-three-way}
Let \(\pi:\Omega\to Y\) be a readout (allowing many-to-one compression), and by the "address-before-value" convention take "verifiable single-valued readout" as a definitional premise at the type layer (see Section \ref{sec:spg}).
If a record axis \(r\) making the extended readout \(\pi^{\mathrm e}=(\pi,r):\Omega\to Y\times R\) injective is not provided, then the readout degenerates to \(\mathsf{NULL}\) at the type layer.
Moreover, this \(\mathsf{NULL}\) can be decomposed into three sources according to the status of record axis \(r\):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Semantic null:} The record axis is empty (address not given), hence readout is undefined;
  \item \textbf{Protocol null:} The record axis is prohibited by interface protocol/type system (e.g., under unitary slice caliber, radial modes with \(|u|\neq 1\) are rejected), hence the object cannot be typed;
  \item \textbf{Collision null:} The record axis exists but the budget is insufficient to achieve injectivization; under the interval readout/CRT externalization protocol (Definition \ref{def:pom-order-elimination-rewrite}), if one wishes to lift the folded many-to-one compression to approximately invertible in the sense of failure probability \(\le\varepsilon\), then the modulus-product budget of the prime-register must satisfy
  \[
  P_m>2B_m(\varepsilon),
  \qquad
  B_m(\varepsilon):=\exp(m\gamma_m(\varepsilon)),
  \]
  where \(\gamma_m(\varepsilon)\) is the tail quantile of the fiber exponent (Definition \ref{def:pom-gamma-quantile}).
  When \(P_m>2B_m(\varepsilon)\), the failure probability of "label overflow leading to non-unique reconstruction" can be compressed to \(\le\varepsilon\); and this threshold is sharp in the sense of this protocol (Corollary \ref{cor:pom-prime-register-quantile-budget}).
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[Register Minimality and "Tail Patch" Incompleteness]\label{rem:xi-register-minimality-no-tail-patch}
Repairing collision null is not a local patch that can be completed by "appending a few bits at the end": in resolution downgrading, the difference between naive truncation and fold-aware restriction can reach full-length flip in the worst-case input (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-max}), and any patch that "only modifies constant end bits" is incomplete (Corollary \ref{cor:fold-tail-patch-incomplete}).
Therefore, once certain information must be externalized as side information to ensure invertibility or verifiability, its record axis is structurally necessarily \emph{globally non-local}: it can only fall on the residue/inverse limit axis carried by prime-register (and finite-state memory consistent with folding), and cannot appear within our \(\mathbf{PW}\) closure interface as an additional continuous coordinate register decoupled from phase \(\theta\).
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[\texorpdfstring{$\mathbf{PW}$}{PW} Closure's No-Continuous-Hair Principle (Type—Register Minimality)]\label{thm:xi-pw-no-continuous-hair}
Under the visible domain constraint of Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null} (\(X_{\mathrm{vis}}=\mathsf{Phase}\)) and the \(\mathbf{PW}\) normalization closure audit interface adopted in this paper, the following two structural assertions hold:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Radial Non-Absorbability)}
  If the denotation/verification of an object structurally requires radial data with \(|u|\neq 1\) (equivalently requires radial–phase dual structure) to preserve its pole/main scale invariant, then under pure phase regime this object has no address within the closure, and its readout degenerates to \(\mathsf{NULL}\) by protocol; moreover, without introducing additional record axes, any constant-layer strictification cannot eliminate this radial dependence while keeping the pole/main scale invariant.
  \item \textbf{(Non-Locality and Minimality of Externalization)}
  If one allows externalizing side information to achieve injectivization of extended readout with the goal of reducing \(\mathsf{NULL}\), then this externalized axis is structurally necessarily globally non-local residue/inverse limit axis (prime-register and its consistency constraints), and cannot be realized by an additional continuous coordinate register decoupled from phase \(\theta\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\textbf{(1)} By Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}, under the visible domain \(X_{\mathrm{vis}}=\mathsf{Phase}\), any object requiring \(|u|\neq 1\) does not satisfy the visible domain filter condition, so its readout degenerates to \(\mathsf{NULL}\) by definition.
On the other hand, local invertible readout of radial–phase state \((\theta,\varrho)\) cannot be continuously compressed to a single circle register (Theorem \ref{thm:radial-dimension-obstruction}), thus under the regime that refuses externalized record axes, "eliminating radial degrees of freedom at constant layer" cannot hold while keeping pole/main scale invariant; its semantic consequence is non-addressability (\(\mathsf{NULL}\)).
\par
\textbf{(2)} By Proposition \ref{prop:xi-null-three-way}, if the readout has folding/many-to-one compression, record axes making the extended readout injective must be externalized, otherwise it degenerates to \(\mathsf{NULL}\) at the type layer; and the prime-register budget provides an auditable threshold for achieving injectivization.
Furthermore, register minimality and "tail patch" incompleteness show: any record axis sufficient to repair collision null is structurally globally non-local and can only fall on residue/inverse limit axis (Remark \ref{rem:xi-register-minimality-no-tail-patch}).
Combining the above yields the two assertions. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{\(\mathbf{PW}\) Closure and "Visible" Form of Interface Parameters}
On the three-generator fragment \((\mathrm{PROJ}_u,E_m,\mathrm{LIFT}_G)\), any projection word can be reduced to the interface normal form \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\circ\mathrm{PROJ}_u\circ E_m\), and non-commutative residuals are completely bundled as anomaly signature \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\) (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-three-gen-interface-normal-form} and Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-signature}).
When connecting this normal form to the \(\Xi\)-audit interface of this chapter, the verifiable continuous parameter axis is the phase \(\theta\) of the unitary slice (i.e., \(u=e^{i\theta}\)), rather than the general radial–phase decomposition \(u=re^{i\theta}\).
Therefore, "addressable \(\Xi\) zero certificates" can only be presented in \(\theta\)-caliber within our closure.

\begin{theorem}[\(\mathbf{PW}\) Type Safety: Non-Addressability of Offset Points (unitary slice caliber)]\label{thm:xi-offset-null-type-safety}
Fix \(L>1\) and consider \(\Xi_{\Delta,L}(s)=\Delta(L^{-s},L^{2s-1})\) (definition see \S\ref{subsec:xi-from-self-dual}).
In the closure adopted in this paper of "three-generator normalizer + anomaly signature interface + unitary slice zero audit", if \(s_0\in\CC\) satisfies \(\Re(s_0)\neq \tfrac12\), then \(s_0\) is not addressable under this closure; equivalently, the corresponding readout is null value (denoted \(\mathsf{NULL}\)).
In particular, any \(\Xi_{\Delta,L}\) zero \(s\) that is addressable and verifiable within this closure must satisfy \(\Re(s)=\tfrac12\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
From \(u_L(s)=L^{2s-1}\) we get
\[
|u_L(s_0)|=L^{2\Re(s_0)-1}\neq 1,
\]
so \(u_L(s_0)\notin\mathsf{Phase}\).
\par
On the other hand, the zero audit interface of this chapter parametrizes on the unitary slice as \(u=e^{i\theta}\) (\(\theta\in\RR\)) (Proposition \ref{prop:xi-unitary-slice-spectral-crossing}), and rewrites zero detection as an auditable spectral crossing criterion (Remark \ref{rem:xi-unitary-slice-operator-audit}).
On the projection word side, three-generator normalization bundles any candidate compilation word to the interface normal form and compresses residuals as \(\theta\)-parametrized anomaly signature (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-three-gen-interface-normal-form} and Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-signature}), thus the visible continuous parameter axis of "verifiable certificate" aligns with the \(\theta\)-caliber of the above unitary slice.
\par
Therefore, within our closure, if a point \(s_0\) can be addressed as a \(\Xi\) audit object, then its parameter \(u_L(s_0)\) must fall in the visible parameter domain \(\mathsf{Phase}\); but the above formula shows \(u_L(s_0)\notin\mathsf{Phase}\), contradiction.
Hence \(s_0\) has no address under this closure; by the null value semantics of Section \ref{sec:recursive-addressing}, its readout is undefined, i.e., null value \(\mathsf{NULL}\).
\par
Finally, "addressable zero must be on critical line" follows from the contrapositive: if \(s\) is addressable as zero within the closure, it cannot satisfy \(\Re(s)\neq\tfrac12\), thus \(\Re(s)=\tfrac12\). This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Critical Line Property of Visible Zeros (\(\Xi\)'s Internal RH)]\label{cor:xi-visible-zeros-on-critical-line}
Under the closure setting of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offset-null-type-safety}, all \(\Xi_{\Delta,L}\) zeros that are addressable and verifiable within \(\mathbf{PW}\) satisfy \(\Re(s)=\tfrac12\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Distinction of soundness vs completeness]\label{rem:xi-soundness-vs-completeness}
Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offset-null-type-safety} belongs to the soundness direction of type safety: \emph{addressable/auditable} \(\Rightarrow\) \emph{critical line property}.
The proposition
\[
\Xi_{\Delta,L}(s)=0\ \Longrightarrow\ s\ \text{is addressable (not}\ \mathsf{NULL}\text{) in}\ \mathbf{PW}
\]
is the completeness direction, whose content is not equivalent to "all visible concepts are non-null" (the latter is a definitional statement in the semantics of Section \ref{sec:recursive-addressing}).
When lifting the internal caliber to \(\Xi\)-RH, the completeness gap must be filled: provide auditable certificate conditions guaranteeing that zero events are addressable (do not trigger \(\mathsf{NULL}\)) under the closure.
\par
Under unit circle phase semantics, the above two can also be understood as the distinction of two "\(\mathsf{NULL}\)---conservation" regimes.
\emph{Pure phase regime} takes \(X_{\mathrm{vis}}=\mathsf{Phase}\) and maintains type safety with \(\mathsf{NULL}\): any object requiring radial degrees of freedom with \(|u|\neq 1\) is judged non-addressable, thus rejected within visible semantics (corresponding to the soundness of this section).
If reducing the occurrence of \(\mathsf{NULL}\) is the goal, then by the "extension-preserving" principle, the flattened degrees of freedom must be externalized (Proposition \ref{prop:unit-circle-phase-extension-preserving}): write \(u=re^{i\theta}\) and take
\[
\operatorname{proj}_{\mathsf{Phase}}(u)=\frac{u}{|u|}=e^{i\theta}\in\mathsf{Phase},
\qquad
\varrho(u)=\log|u|\in\RR,
\qquad
\operatorname{proj}^{\mathrm{e}}(u):=\bigl(\operatorname{proj}_{\mathsf{Phase}}(u),\varrho(u)\bigr).
\]
Then \(\operatorname{proj}^{\mathrm{e}}\) is injective on \(\CC^\times\), and the unitary slice is merely the section \(\varrho(u)=0\); at this point \(|u|\neq 1\) no longer necessarily triggers \(\mathsf{NULL}\), but manifests as a non-degenerate extended readout of "phase readout + radial record".
When record axes are allowed to grow unboundedly, the above externalized degrees of freedom can be carried by additional orthogonal modes of a separable Hilbert space in the limit representation of "tight phase tower" (Proposition \ref{prop:phase-tower-principal-u1}).
Therefore, the completeness gap required by RH can be located as: \emph{whether there exists a zero event that can only be explained by introducing a radial channel (or other additional visible degrees of freedom, such as minimal realization dimension exceeding bounds)}; if so, it must read out as \(\mathsf{NULL}\) under pure phase regime, while under extended regime it requires explicit carrying of its radial residual by additional record axes.
\end{remark}

Under the Hardy/Smirnov condition caliber of disk-compactified completion \(F_{\zeta}\), the boundary modulus \(|F_{\zeta}(e^{\mathrm{i}\theta})|\) only determines the outer factor \(O\), while disk interior zero data is carried by the Blaschke inner factor \(B\) (\S\ref{subsec:unit-disk-inner-outer}).
In particular, off-critical-line offset \(\delta=\tfrac12-\beta\) is strictly realized in the disk as horocycle/Busemann coordinate based at endpoint \(-1\)

\delta=\mathcal{B}_{-1}(w_{\rho})=\frac{1-|w_{\rho}|^{2}}{|1+w_{\rho}|^{2}}
\)
(Corollary \ref{cor:app-offcritical-horocycle-busemann}), thus belonging to a hidden component that is \emph{structurally invisible} to unitary slice pure phase readout.
\par
In the \(\mathbf{PW}\) closure audit of this paper, "whether hidden component can be accepted" is a type question rather than a numerical question: if externalizing record axes is refused, then by Theorem \ref{thm:xi-pw-no-continuous-hair} and Proposition \ref{prop:xi-null-three-way}, this hidden degree of freedom must trigger collision/protocol null and degenerate as \(\mathsf{NULL}\); whereas if requiring it to remain verifiable within closed loop, this inner factor data must be compiled into auditable output (e.g., resolution threshold and corner atoms of defect ledger \(D_{\zeta}(\varrho)\), or discrete spectral fingerprints such as Hankel rank/rigidity index at realization layer), making it enter the finite semantic output domain of "signature \(\times\) spectrum" (Definition \ref{def:pom-sem-functor}).

\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Auditable Decomposition of Completeness Gap: Cross-Resolution Patch and Time Layer Right Decoding]\label{rem:xi-completeness-auditable-decomposition}
Completeness can be stated as the global assertion "zero events do not trigger \(\mathsf{NULL}\)".
In the projection gate semantics of this paper, this assertion can be further decomposed into several auditable subconditions, isolating the sources of \(\mathsf{NULL}\) layer by layer.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Cross-resolution: finite tail patch incompleteness (non-locally eliminable).} Micro-state direct truncation \(r_{m+1\to m}\) and fold-aware restriction \(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}\) are generally incompatible, the gauge difference \(G_m\) (Definition \ref{def:fold-gauge-anomaly}) quantifies the minimum correction scale; moreover, any patch that "only modifies constant end bits" cannot reproduce \(\widetilde r_{m+1\to m}\) on all inputs (Corollary \ref{cor:fold-tail-patch-incomplete}; also see Remark \ref{rem:fold-tail-patch-null-nonlocal}). Thus if the protocol simultaneously refuses non-local access and orthogonal record axes, then certain input families cannot obtain fold-consistent addresses, and their readout degenerates to \(\mathsf{NULL}\) under null value semantics.

  \item \textbf{Time layer: right invertibility dichotomy and synchronization budget.} After lifting \(\Fold_m\) along sliding window to sofic factor \(\Phi_m\), statistical-type \(\mathsf{NULL}\) at the time layer can be characterized as determinization filter state being non-singleton (\(|S_t|>1\); Remark \ref{rem:Ym-stat-null-unsync}). Its behavior satisfies right invertibility dichotomy: either complete synchronization after uniform finite delay (\(Y_m^{\mathrm{amb}}=\varnothing\), Corollary \ref{cor:Ym-time-layer-dichotomy}; in Zeckendorf folding when \(m\ge 3\) given strong form by Theorem \ref{thm:Ym-ambiguity-shell-dag}), or long-term ambiguity can only reside in strictly lower entropy shell, with exponential-type synchronization tail bound under maximum entropy measure \(\nu_m(T_m>n)\le C_m e^{-\varepsilon_m n}\) (Theorem \ref{thm:Ym-sync-tail}, where \(\varepsilon_m=\log 2-h_{\mathrm{top}}(Y_m^{\mathrm{amb}})>0\)).

  \item \textbf{Constant-level closure: periodic merging and online risk table.} Cover and intrinsic \(\zeta\) main pole residue ratio \(\eta_m\in(0,1]\) (Proposition \ref{prop:Ym-zeta-residue-ratio}) and periodic compression defect \(\delta_m\in[0,\log|\mathcal{S}_m|]\) (Corollary \ref{cor:Ym-periodic-compression-ratio}) quantify the constant-level information gap of "merging but not changing exponential scale"; singleton state proportion limit \(\eta_m^{\mathrm{obs}}\in(0,1]\) (Proposition \ref{prop:eta_m_hat_ergodic_limit}) provides an online observable risk table without determinant.
\end{enumerate}
\par
Therefore, one can replace the "no \(\mathsf{NULL}\)" goal of completeness with a family of auditable criteria: once the time layer satisfies finite-delay synchronization (or synchronization tail budget meets standard) and constant-level merging is controlled (e.g., \(\eta_m^{\mathrm{obs}}\approx 1\) and \(\eta_m\approx 1\)), then if systematic \(\mathsf{NULL}\) still appears, its source should be primarily located to higher-layer mechanisms outside the geometric domain (\(|u|\neq 1\) radial degrees of freedom) or dimension threshold (minimal realization exceeds bounds), rather than time layer ambiguity or cross-resolution finite tail patch insufficiency.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[NULL's Orthogonal Trichotomy and Resource Threshold Non-Substitutability]\label{thm:xi-null-orthogonal-trichotomy-resource-nonsubstitutable}
Under the unitary slice visible domain constraint of this paper (Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}, taking \(X_{\mathrm{vis}}=\mathsf{Phase}\)), \(\mathbf{PW}\) closure type safety (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offset-null-type-safety}) and injectivization principle (Proposition \ref{prop:xi-null-three-way}), the structural sources of global readout failure (\(\mathsf{NULL}\)) can be organized into three classes of mutually inequivalent and \emph{orthogonal} witnesses in the auditable sense:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Closure/out-of-domain defect: denotational or type layer rejection)} Address not given, or object interpretation requires degrees of freedom beyond \(X_{\mathrm{vis}}\) (typically: radial mode with \(|u|\neq 1\)), thus has no address within closure and reads out as \(\mathsf{NULL}\) by protocol (Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}; Theorem \ref{thm:xi-pw-no-continuous-hair}).
  \item \textbf{(Gluing twist: second-order compatibility obstruction)} Even if local certificates exist piecewise on covers, globalization may fail due to second-order consistency constraint failure; this failure can be witnessed by a verifiable Čech \(2\)-cocycle obstruction class \([\alpha]\in H^2(\mathrm{Addr},A)\) (Definition \ref{def:prefix-site-h2-gluing-obstruction}), and thus the address fiber is empty, readout is \(\mathsf{NULL}\) (Proposition \ref{prop:null-as-h2-obstruction}).
  \item \textbf{(Hard resource insufficiency: threshold-type budget violation)} To lift many-to-one compressed readout to single-valued verifiable readout, two classes of non-substitutable hard threshold resources must be simultaneously satisfied:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Collision threshold (prime-register budget):} If modulus-product budget \(P_m\) is insufficient to exceed quantile threshold \(2B_m(\varepsilon)\), then collision null cannot be eliminated under given confidence caliber (Proposition \ref{prop:xi-null-three-way}; also see worst-case lower bound of Theorem \ref{thm:address-defect-law}).
    \item \emph{Endpoint threshold (precision/resolution budget):} If endpoint layer resolution is insufficient to carry the quadratic compression gap of off-slice signal, then off-line information cannot be certificated in a single-valued manner under verification semantics, and must appear as \(\mathsf{NULL}\) or "must extend mode axis" form (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offcritical-endpoint-resolution-lower-bound}; also see tight compactification readout bit complexity lower bound of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-radial-information-projection-lower-bound}).
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
Moreover, these three classes of witnesses are mutually non-substitutable within the system: \emph{increasing budget on one axis} cannot substitute \emph{repairing defects on another axis} (e.g., prime-register modulus-product growth cannot offset endpoint resolution threshold, and vice versa; similarly, any budget boost cannot eliminate gluing obstruction \([\alpha]\neq 0\)).
This non-substitutability is the auditable meaning of "information conservation/no leakage" in this paper: failure sources are closed under orthogonal decomposition and each carries independent threshold or invariant certificates.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Class (1) is a direct consequence of visible domain filtering and \(\mathbf{PW}\) type safety (Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}; Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offset-null-type-safety} and Theorem \ref{thm:xi-pw-no-continuous-hair}).
Class (2) is provided by the \(H^2\) gluing obstruction caliber of prefix site: if \([\alpha]\neq 0\), then no globally extendable local section exists, thus the fiber is empty and readout is \(\mathsf{NULL}\) (Proposition \ref{prop:null-as-h2-obstruction}).
Class (3) is respectively provided by collision threshold of injectivization principle (Proposition \ref{prop:xi-null-three-way}) and endpoint quadratic compression/resolution barrier (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offcritical-endpoint-resolution-lower-bound}; Theorem \ref{thm:xi-radial-information-projection-lower-bound}).
\par
Non-substitutability comes from the non-overlapping "supporting axes" of these certificates: collision threshold only constrains modulus-product budget of residue/inverse limit axis, while endpoint threshold only constrains resolution threshold induced by endpoint precision potential; both correspond to different orthogonal record axes in type semantics, thus no legal compilation path exists to cover one class gap with another class budget.
Similarly, \([\alpha]\neq 0\) is an invariant certificate of second-order consistency, which cannot be eliminated by any mere bit budget boost.
This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Notation and Semantic Gate: Fold Fiber, Maximum Entropy Lift and Invisible Capacity}\label{par:xi-null-notation-semantic-gate}
The following notation is used to unify "addressable/null" on unitary slice and the fiber information gap of window folding into the same ledger language (see subsection \ref{subsec:pom-information-ledger} and Definition \ref{def:pom-kappa}).
Fix resolution \(m\ge 1\), take microscopic phase space \(\Omega_m:=\{0,1\}^m\) and stable type set \(X_m\).
Let fold projection be \(\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m\), fiber multiplicity
\[
d_m(x):=\card{\Fold_m^{-1}(x)}\qquad(x\in X_m).
\]
For any macroscopic marginal distribution \(\pi\in\Delta(X_m)\), define its \textbf{fiber uniform lift} (maximum entropy lift)
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-null-maxent-lift}
\pi^{\mathrm{e}}(a):=\frac{\pi(\Fold_m(a))}{d_m(\Fold_m(a))},\qquad a\in\Omega_m,
\end{equation}
thus \((\Fold_m)_*\pi^{\mathrm{e}}=\pi\); it is the same as \(\widetilde\pi\) in main text Definition \ref{def:pom-maxent-lift}.
Define \textbf{thermodynamic defect/invisible capacity}
\[
\kappa_m(\pi):=\mathbb{E}_{\pi}\bigl[\log d_m(X)\bigr]
\]
(Definition \ref{def:pom-kappa}).
In the unitary slice closure of this subsection, the "addressable" continuous domain is fixed to \(\mathsf{Phase}\), additionally subject to \(\mathbf{PW}\) type closure constraint (Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}); thus if externalizing additional record axes is refused, then internal labels of fold fibers have no address in visible semantics, their "readout" is equivalently treated as \(\mathsf{NULL}\) (not addressable) at the semantic layer.

\begin{theorem}[Microscopic Entropy Window and Endpoint Realization Under Fixed Macroscopic \texorpdfstring{$\pi$}{pi}]\label{thm:xi-null-fiber-entropy-window}
Fix resolution \(m\ge 1\) and macroscopic marginal \(\pi\in\Delta(X_m)\), and let \(\pi^{\mathrm{e}}\in\Delta(\Omega_m)\) be its fiber uniform lift \eqref{eq:xi-null-maxent-lift}.
Then for any lift \(\mu\in\Delta(\Omega_m)\) satisfying \((\Fold_m)_*\mu=\pi\), the strict ledger identity holds
\[
\boxed{\;
H(\mu)=H(\pi)+\kappa_m(\pi)-D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}})\; }.
\]
Moreover:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \(\pi^{\mathrm{e}}\) \textbf{uniquely} achieves maximum entropy among all lifts, and
  \[
  H(\pi^{\mathrm{e}})=H(\pi)+\kappa_m(\pi).
  \]
  \item There exists a "fiber point mass lift" \(\mu^\star\) such that \(H(\mu^\star)=H(\pi)\) and
  \[
  D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu^\star\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}})=\kappa_m(\pi).
  \]
  Thus under fixed \(\pi\), microscopic entropy satisfies \textbf{exact window width}
  \[
  H(\mu)\in\bigl[H(\pi),\ H(\pi)+\kappa_m(\pi)\bigr],
  \qquad
  \mathrm{width}=\kappa_m(\pi).
  \]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The ledger identity is the "fixed \(\pi\)" version of Theorem \ref{thm:pom-kl-ledger} (where \(\mu^e\) is denoted as \(\pi^{\mathrm{e}}\)).
Maximum entropy endpoint and uniqueness are given by Theorem \ref{thm:pom-maxent-lift}.
\par
For point mass endpoint: take any section \(s:X_m\to\Omega_m\) satisfying \(\Fold_m(s(x))=x\) (choose one point per fiber).
Define
\(
\mu^\star(s(x)):=\pi(x)
\)
and for other \(a\notin s(X_m)\) take \(\mu^\star(a)=0\).
Then \((\Fold_m)_*\mu^\star=\pi\), and
\[
H(\mu^\star)=-\sum_{x\in X_m}\pi(x)\log\pi(x)=H(\pi).
\]
Also by \(\pi^{\mathrm{e}}(s(x))=\pi(x)/d_m(x)\), we get
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu^\star\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}})
=\sum_{x\in X_m}\pi(x)\log\frac{\pi(x)}{\pi(x)/d_m(x)}
=\sum_{x\in X_m}\pi(x)\log d_m(x)
=\kappa_m(\pi).
\]
This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Address–Defect Law: Side Information Budget Required for Injectivization]\label{thm:address-defect-law}
Fix resolution \(m\ge 1\) and fold readout \(\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m\), and let record axis \(r:\Omega_m\to R\).
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Worst case: fiberwise pointwise lower bound)} If extended readout \((\Fold_m,r):\Omega_m\to X_m\times R\) is injective, then for each \(x\in X_m\)
  \[
  \card{r(\Fold_m^{-1}(x))}\ \ge\ d_m(x),
  \]
  thus \(\card{R}\ge \max_{x\in X_m} d_m(x)\).
  In particular, as long as there exists \(x\) with \(d_m(x)>1\), then stable type \(x\) alone cannot form a single-valued verifiable "complete address"; if externalizing record axis is refused, this collision corresponds to collision-type \(\mathsf{NULL}\) under our semantics (Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null} and Proposition \ref{prop:xi-null-three-way}).

  \item \textbf{(Thermodynamic: average bit budget lower bound)} Given any macroscopic marginal \(\pi\in\Delta(X_m)\), let \(A\sim\pi^{\mathrm{e}}\in\Delta(\Omega_m)\) be its fiber uniform lift \eqref{eq:xi-null-maxent-lift}, and let \(X:=\Fold_m(A)\sim\pi\).
  If \((\Fold_m,r)\) is injective, then
  \[
  H\bigl(r(A)\mid X\bigr)\ \ge\ H(A\mid X)\ =\ \kappa_m(\pi),
  \]
  thus the record axis must carry at least \(\kappa_m(\pi)/\log 2\) bits of side information in the average sense.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\textbf{(1)} Fix \(x\in X_m\). If \(a\neq a'\) and \(\Fold_m(a)=\Fold_m(a')=x\), by injectivity of \((\Fold_m,r)\) we must have \(r(a)\neq r(a')\), so \(r\) is injective on fiber \(\Fold_m^{-1}(x)\), hence \(\card{r(\Fold_m^{-1}(x))}\ge \card{\Fold_m^{-1}(x)}=d_m(x)\). Taking maximum yields \(\card{R}\ge \max_x d_m(x)\).
\par
\textbf{(2)} Since \(\pi^{\mathrm{e}}\) is uniform on each fiber, for each \(x\) we have
\(
H(A\mid X=x)=\log d_m(x)
\), thus
\(
H(A\mid X)=\mathbb{E}_\pi[\log d_m(X)]=\kappa_m(\pi)
\).
On the other hand, \((\Fold_m,r)\) injective means there exists deterministic decoder \(D\) such that \(A=D(X,r(A))\), so \(H(A\mid X,r(A))=0\).
Thus
\[
H(A\mid X)=I(A;r(A)\mid X)\le H(r(A)\mid X),
\]
yielding the shown lower bound; dividing by \(\log 2\) for bit unit conversion. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Semantic Interpretation: Three Classes of \texorpdfstring{$\mathsf{NULL}$}{NULL} Sources Under Unitary Slice]\label{rem:xi-null-three-kinds}
In unitary slice audit semantics, if externalizing orthogonal record axes is refused, then "non-addressable/null" can be decomposed into three mutually orthogonal structural sources:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Geometric \(\mathsf{NULL}\).} Parameter domain constraint to \(\mathsf{Phase}\): any object requiring radial degrees of freedom with \(|u|\neq 1\) has no address within closure, reads out as \(\mathsf{NULL}\) by Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}.
  \item \textbf{Branch \(\mathsf{NULL}\).} Even when restricted to \(|u|=1\), if further compressing half-angle layer \(\mathfrak r\) to unique angle layer \(u=\mathfrak r^2\), then the discrete branch of double covering \(\mathfrak r\mapsto-\mathfrak r\) is erased; this branch information is invisible to the \(u\)-layer (see Appendix \S\ref{subsec:unit-disk-witt-chebyshev} and Proposition \ref{prop:completion-subring-unique-angle}).
  \item \textbf{Statistical \(\mathsf{NULL}\).} When macroscopic marginal \(\pi\) is fixed, lift is not unique; without externalizing fiber shape record, microscopic differences have no address in visible semantics. Theorem \ref{thm:xi-null-fiber-entropy-window} gives its invisible window width \(\kappa_m(\pi)\), and precisely carries "fiber shape residual" of any concrete lift as \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}})\).
\end{itemize}
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[Fiber Residual Decomposition and "Statistical \texorpdfstring{$\mathsf{NULL}$}{NULL} Radius"]\label{cor:xi-null-statistical-radius}
Continue with notation of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-null-fiber-entropy-window}, and write fiber conditional distribution \(\mu_x(\cdot):=\mu(\cdot\mid x)\) (\(x\in X_m\)) and fiber uniform distribution \(u_x(a):=1/d_m(x)\) (\(a\in\Fold_m^{-1}(x)\)).
Then we have fiber orthogonal decomposition
\[
\boxed{\;
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}})
=\sum_{x\in X_m}\pi(x)\,D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu_x\|u_x)\; }.
\]
Thus for all lifts we have explicit upper bounds
\[
0\le D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}})\le \kappa_m(\pi),
\qquad
D_{\mathrm{TV}}(\mu,\pi^{\mathrm{e}})\le \sqrt{\frac{\kappa_m(\pi)}{2}}.
\]
And for any bounded observable \(f:\Omega_m\to\RR\), the systematic bias of all lifts compatible with \(\pi\) on \(f\) relative to \(\pi^{\mathrm{e}}\) satisfies
\[
\bigl|\mathbb{E}_\mu[f]-\mathbb{E}_{\pi^{\mathrm{e}}}[f]\bigr|
\le 2\|f\|_\infty\sqrt{\frac{\kappa_m(\pi)}{2}}.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The first formula is a direct restatement of Lemma \ref{lem:pom-fiberwise-kl} (where \(\mu^e\) is denoted as \(\pi^{\mathrm{e}}\)).
The remaining inequalities follow immediately from Theorem \ref{thm:defect-limited-audit} (and Pinsker inequality). This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Microscopic Sliding Window: KL Interval Law Under Same Macroscopic]\label{cor:xi-null-micro-kl-window}
Let \(u_{\Omega_m}\) be the uniform distribution on \(\Omega_m\), and let
\(
w_m:=(\Fold_m)_*u_{\Omega_m}
\)
be its fold pushforward (Definition \ref{def:pom-wm-baseline}).
Fix any \(\pi\in\Delta(X_m)\).
Then for all lifts \(\mu\in\Delta(\Omega_m)\) satisfying \((\Fold_m)_*\mu=\pi\), the explicit sandwich holds
\[
\boxed{\;
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\|w_m)
\le D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|u_{\Omega_m})
\le D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\|w_m)+\kappa_m(\pi)\; }.
\]
Equivalently,
\(
0\le D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}})\le \kappa_m(\pi)
\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By identity \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|u_{\Omega_m})=m\log 2-H(\mu)\), and substituting Theorem \ref{thm:xi-null-fiber-entropy-window} we get
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|u_{\Omega_m})
=m\log 2-H(\pi)-\kappa_m(\pi)+D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}}).
\]
Then by Proposition \ref{prop:pom-kappa-kl-decomposition}'s
\(
\kappa_m(\pi)=m\log 2-H(\pi)-D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\|w_m)
\)
we get decomposition
\(
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|u_{\Omega_m})
=D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\|w_m)+D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}})
\).
Finally applying bounds from Corollary \ref{cor:xi-null-statistical-radius} to \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}})\). This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Endpoint Compression, Finite Precision and Second-Class \texorpdfstring{$\mathsf{NULL}$}{NULL}]\label{rem:xi-null-finite-precision}

\[
\frac{\dd x}{\dd u}=\pi(1+x^2)

In "extension-preserving" language, this corresponds to externalized record axis (or precision register) being insufficient to make extended observation injective, thus address is necessarily missing.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Auditable Alternative: Address Completeness Sufficient Criterion Given by Hankel-Rank Stability (ACC-Rank)]\label{rem:xi-acc-rank}
The so-called "address completeness" (completeness) gap can be stated as: there exist zero events that are non-addressable (readout undefined, i.e., \(\mathsf{NULL}\)) under our \(\mathbf{PW}\) closure, thus soundness alone is insufficient to deduce the conclusion that "all zeros are captured within visible domain".
In the bridge-package realization caliber of this paper, this gap can be compiled into a \emph{regression-testable} backend stability criterion: along the \(\theta\)-scan of unitary slice, if the limiting kernel family does not need to introduce new visible degrees of freedom in the minimal realization sense, then any zero event must have \(\mathbf{PW}\) address and will not read out as \(\mathsf{NULL}\).
\par
More specifically, for each finite level \(\ell\) and each phase sampling point \(\theta\), take the trace sequence of normalized kernel family \(U_{L,\ell}(\theta)\) used in Proposition \ref{prop:xi-unitary-slice-spectral-crossing}
\(
a^{(\ell)}_n(\theta):=\Tr(U_{L,\ell}(\theta)^n)
\)
(or equivalent resolvent-trace fragment), and measure its "visible degrees of freedom" with Hankel rank/minimal realization dimension
\[
d^{(\ell)}_{\min}(\theta):=\mathrm{rank}\,H\!\bigl(a^{(\ell)}_\bullet(\theta)\bigr)
\]
(Theorem \ref{thm:hankel-rank-minimal}).
If there exists constant \(d_\ast\) such that for all sufficiently large levels \(\ell\) and all phase samplings \(\theta\) we have \(d^{(\ell)}_{\min}(\theta)\le d_\ast\), and this bound remains stable when densifying \(\theta\) grid and lifting level, then "zero \(\neq\mathsf{NULL}\)" address completeness can be viewed as certificated to satisfy.
\par
This route replaces completeness from pure existential statement with an auditable "dimension stability" check; Hankel-rank realization caliber and script organization see Remark \ref{rem:two_layer_bridge_package_completed_minH}, and can refer to Table \ref{tab:fold_collision_moment_hankel_rank} (script \texttt{scripts/exp\_fold\_collision\_moment\_hankel\_rank.py}) as baseline template for backend dimension certificate.
\par
From \(\mathsf{NULL}\) semantics viewpoint, the above check corresponds to a gap orthogonal to "geometric/branch/statistical": if limiting visible audit to realizing trace/primitive exchange diagram in fixed finite-dimensional state space, then Hankel rank (Theorem \ref{thm:hankel-rank-minimal}) provides a hard lower bound on \emph{minimal record axis dimension}; any strategy attempting to compress realization dimension below \(<\mathrm{rank}(H)\) will exhibit non-eliminable ambiguity on some visible observations, whose semantic consequence is "dimension-type \(\mathsf{NULL}\)" (address must be externalized to higher-dimensional axis).
This gap can be certificated in polynomial time by Hankel normal form \(A=(H_0^{(\ell)})^{-1}H_1^{(\ell)}\) (Remark \ref{rem:pom-s2-hankel}), aligned with \(\theta\)-scan stability test.
Under further assumption of "uniform minimality + protocol closure (no unrecorded axis backflow)", the above ACC-Rank stability check can be upgraded from sufficient criterion to necessary and sufficient characterization, see Theorem \ref{thm:xi-completeness-iff-hankel-bounded}.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Mode Axis Completeness \texorpdfstring{$\Longleftrightarrow$}{<->} Hankel Rank Uniformly Bounded (Under Uniform Minimality and Protocol Closure)]\label{thm:xi-completeness-iff-hankel-bounded}
Continue with setting and notation of Remark \ref{rem:xi-acc-rank}, and consider mode axis \(a_{\mathrm{mode}}\) in multi-axis prefix (Definition \ref{def:multi_axis_address}).
Fix a confidence caliber such that residual axis \(a_{\mathrm{res}}\) budget is sufficient to exclude collision null caused by "collision/label overflow" (e.g., satisfying prime-register quantile threshold condition of Proposition \ref{prop:xi-null-three-way}, or equivalently satisfying item (2) of Proposition \ref{prop:xi-completeness-auditable-decomposition}).
\par
Further assume:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Uniform minimality:} For all sufficiently large levels \(\ell\) and all phase parameters \(\theta\), the "required visible degrees of freedom" of trace sequence \(a^{(\ell)}_\bullet(\theta)\) is uniquely characterized by its Hankel rank, i.e.,
  \[
  d^{(\ell)}_{\min}(\theta)=\mathrm{rank}\,H\!\bigl(a^{(\ell)}_\bullet(\theta)\bigr)
  \]
  and this minimal dimension is viewed as a lower bound on mode axis capacity that must be explicitly externalized within protocol closure (Theorem \ref{thm:hankel-rank-minimal}).
  \item \textbf{Protocol closure (no unrecorded axis backflow):} Any new degrees of freedom affecting visible readout/certificate, if not explicitly recorded as mode axis/register, is rejected at type layer and reads out as \(\mathsf{NULL}\) (Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}; also see "no unrecorded axis" principle of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-pw-no-continuous-hair}).
\end{enumerate}
Then the following statements are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item[(i)] \textbf{Mode axis completeness:} There exist constant \(d_\ast\) and level threshold \(\ell_0\), such that for all \(\ell\ge \ell_0\) and all phase samplings \(\theta\), the visible degrees of freedom required for unitary-slice audit can be closed within the same finite-dimensional mode type; equivalently, zero events do not degenerate to \(\mathsf{NULL}\) merely due to "need to introduce new visible degrees of freedom/mode axis" within \(\mathbf{PW}\) closure.
  \item[(ii)] \textbf{Hankel rank uniformly bounded:} There exist constant \(d_\ast\) and \(\ell_0\) such that for all \(\ell\ge \ell_0\) and all \(\theta\)
  \[
  d^{(\ell)}_{\min}(\theta)\le d_\ast.
  \]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\textbf{(ii)\(\Rightarrow\)(i).}
If there exist \(d_\ast,\ell_0\) such that \(d^{(\ell)}_{\min}(\theta)\le d_\ast\) for all \(\ell\ge\ell_0\) and \(\theta\), then for each \((\ell,\theta)\) there exists a linear realization of dimension at most \(d_\ast\), realizing all visible information of trace sequence \(a^{(\ell)}_\bullet(\theta)\) (Theorem \ref{thm:hankel-rank-minimal}).
Taking this realization (or its zero-padding extension in dimension \(d_\ast\)) as uniform mode axis type, one can complete corresponding certificate readout without introducing new visible degrees of freedom; by protocol closure assumption, any object requiring additional degrees of freedom must be explicitly externalized, otherwise will trigger \(\mathsf{NULL}\), thus no new dimension-type \(\mathsf{NULL}\) appears under current "uniform finite-dimensional" regime.
Therefore mode axis completeness holds.
\par
\textbf{(i)\(\Rightarrow\)(ii).}
Assume Hankel rank is not uniformly bounded, then for any candidate constant \(d\) and threshold \(\ell_0\) one can find \(\ell\ge\ell_0\) and some \(\theta\) such that
\(
d^{(\ell)}_{\min}(\theta)>\!d
\).
By uniform minimality and Theorem \ref{thm:hankel-rank-minimal}, any linear representation realizing this trace sequence has dimension at least \(d^{(\ell)}_{\min}(\theta)\), thus non-eliminable modal collapse occurs within any fixed \(d\)-dimensional mode type.
By protocol closure, "unrecorded axis backflow" is prohibited: the non-verifiable ambiguity induced by this collapse cannot be silently absorbed within closure, and can only trigger \(\mathsf{NULL}\) in the form of mode axis insufficiency.
This contradicts (i)'s requirement that "there exists uniform finite-dimensional mode type making events not degenerate to \(\mathsf{NULL}\) due to axis shortage".
Hence Hankel rank must be uniformly bounded, yielding (ii).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Auditable Decomposition of Completeness: Dimension Stability Certificate \(\times\) Quota Certificate]\label{prop:xi-completeness-auditable-decomposition}
Under the unitary-slice zero audit caliber of this paper, the structural sources of "zero event reading out as \(\mathsf{NULL}\)" can be consolidated into two classes: \emph{additional visible degrees of freedom} (protocol null) and \emph{collision/label overflow} (collision null, Proposition \ref{prop:xi-null-three-way}).

  P_m>2B_m(\varepsilon)=2\exp(m\gamma_m(\varepsilon))

\end{enumerate}
then under this confidence caliber, the two unique sources of zero event reading out as \(\mathsf{NULL}\) (degree of freedom instability or label collision) are both excluded, thus "address completeness" can be substitutively guaranteed by these two classes of certificates within the system; combined with Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offset-null-type-safety} (soundness), this compresses the completeness gap required by \(\Xi\)-RH into \emph{verification task of two classes of backend certificates}: dimension stability certificate (ACC-Rank) and prime-register quota certificate (quantile threshold).
\end{proposition}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Geometric out-of-domain rejection.} If \(|u|\neq 1\) (i.e., \(u\notin\mathsf{Phase}\)), then directly read out as \(\mathsf{NULL}\) by visible domain constraint (Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}).
  \item \textbf{Defect quantification of in-domain ambiguity.} When macroscopic marginal \(\pi\) is fixed, invisible window width of lift is precisely quantified by \(\kappa_m(\pi)\); fiber shape residual of any lift is carried by \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\pi^{\mathrm{e}})\) (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-null-fiber-entropy-window} and Corollary \ref{cor:xi-null-statistical-radius}).
  \item \textbf{Dimension threshold of finite-dimensional closure.} If visible audit requires closure within fixed finite-dimensional state space, then Hankel rank provides minimal realization dimension lower bound; violating this bound triggers dimension-type \(\mathsf{NULL}\) (Remark \ref{rem:xi-acc-rank}).
  \item \textbf{Priority guardrail of integer layer calibration lattice.} Frobenius defect divisibility depth at root of unity points provides discrete anchor points, enabling many pseudo-\(\mathsf{NULL}\) caused by finite precision to be primarily located as realization failure at integer layer (Remark \ref{rem:xi-null-frobenius-calibration}).
\end{enumerate}
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[Complete Address as Multi-Axis Prefix]\label{def:multi_axis_address}
Under the visible readout convention of this paper (Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}) and "address-before-value" semantics, a verifiable, denotable address should be understood as typed \textbf{multi-axis prefix}
\[
a=\bigl(a_{\mathrm{vis}},a_{\mathrm{res}},a_{\mathrm{mode}}\bigr).
\]
The three axes respectively undertake the following roles:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Visible axis (\(a_{\mathrm{vis}}\)):} Under filtering by given visible domain \(X_{\mathrm{vis}}\) and checker \(\mathrm{Chk}\), select a visible column prefix/certificate prefix (e.g., phase parameter \(\theta\) on unitary slice and its induced auditable event), thus grounding "concept" as verifiable projection readout.
  \item \textbf{Residual axis (\(a_{\mathrm{res}}\)):} Record axis/label axis used to injectivize possibly many-to-one compressed readout (Proposition \ref{prop:xi-null-three-way}). In fold readout \(\Fold_m\) scenario, worst-case capacity and average information budget of this axis are provided hard lower bounds by fiber multiplicity \(d_m\) and defect scalar \(\kappa_m(\pi)\) respectively (Theorem \ref{thm:address-defect-law}).
  \item \textbf{Mode axis (\(a_{\mathrm{mode}}\)):} Other orthogonal degrees of freedom required by protocol contract (if not externalized then prohibited at type layer and read out as \(\mathsf{NULL}\)), typical examples include: radial register/amplitude channel (Corollary \ref{cor:xi-null-second-order-radial-channel}) and minimal realization dimension/visible degrees of freedom stability axis (Remark \ref{rem:xi-acc-rank}).
\end{enumerate}
\par
Under this packing, "whether address is legal (non-\(\mathsf{NULL}\))" means three axes simultaneously satisfy: \(a_{\mathrm{vis}}\) falls within visible domain filtering, \(a_{\mathrm{res}}\) budget sufficient to avoid collision, and \(a_{\mathrm{mode}}\) not prohibited by protocol; any axis missing or prohibited triggers corresponding source of \(\mathsf{NULL}\), thus concretizing "completeness gap" as auditable axis budget/axis stability condition (Proposition \ref{prop:xi-completeness-auditable-decomposition}).
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Dual Swap Symmetry and Residual Budget Obstruction]\label{rem:xi-dual-swap-symmetry-defect}
The "three-axis prefix" characterization of Definition \ref{def:multi_axis_address} is interface direct sum: \(a_{\mathrm{vis}}\) as quotient coordinate within visible domain, \(a_{\mathrm{res}}\) as externalized label of fiber collision, their "orthogonality" is structural decomposition rather than geometric similarity.
Therefore, the statement "two orthogonal axes equivalent" can only be understood in our semantics as a stronger \textbf{dual swap symmetry} event: there exists protocol-allowed automorphism swapping "visible quotient coordinate" with "fiber label" while keeping verifiable semantics invariant.
\par
This swap generally does not hold, its failure is provided strict obstruction by residual budget.
Taking fold readout \(\Fold_m\) as example: if there exists \(x\in X_m\) with fiber multiplicity \(d_m(x)=\card{\Fold_m^{-1}(x)}>1\), then any residual axis making extended readout \((\Fold_m,r)\) injective must provide at least \(d_m(x)\) distinguishable labels on this fiber (Theorem \ref{thm:address-defect-law} item (1)); under macroscopic caliber, moreover must pay average information budget lower bound \(H(r(A)\mid X)\ge \kappa_m(\pi)\) (same theorem item (2)).
Thus, unless fiber defect vanishes on the concerned subclass (e.g., \(d_m(x)=1\) or corresponding \(\kappa_m(\pi)=0\)), otherwise the swap of "compressing fiber coordinate back to visible coordinate" cannot occur without externalizing residual axis.
\end{remark}
