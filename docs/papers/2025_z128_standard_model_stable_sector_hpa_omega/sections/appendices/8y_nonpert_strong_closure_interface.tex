\subsection{Nonperturbative strong-closure interface packages (long-range track)}
\label{app:nonpert_strong_closure_interface}

\AuditTag This appendix fixes the \emph{nonperturbative strong-closure} track as an explicit interface package.
It does not claim to solve open problems (e.g.\ 4D Yang--Mills mass gap), but it makes their dependency structure, failure points, and auditable substitutes explicit.

\subsubsection{Core nonperturbative targets}
\begin{problem}[Constructive existence of an interacting 4D gauge QFT (interface target)]
\label{prob:constructive_existence_4d_gauge_qft}
Construct an interacting four-dimensional gauge quantum field theory with the declared field content and locality/spectrum properties, in a mathematically controlled (nonperturbative) sense.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}[Yang--Mills mass gap (interface target)]
\label{prob:yang_mills_mass_gap}
Prove a positive mass gap for pure 4D Yang--Mills theory.
\end{problem}

\AuditTag Problems~\ref{prob:constructive_existence_4d_gauge_qft}--\ref{prob:yang_mills_mass_gap} are widely regarded as hard/open in full generality.
Accordingly, this manuscript treats them as explicit interface targets and does not use them as hidden premises.

\subsubsection{Decomposition into auditable subtargets}
\paragraph{NP-Existence chain (minimal).}
We decompose ``nonperturbative existence'' into subtargets that can be tracked separately:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(NP1) Algebraic existence.} A well-defined local net (Haag--Kastler type) with a vacuum representation and spectrum condition.
\item \textbf{(NP2) Control of interactions.} A nontrivial interaction content beyond free theory, with a controlled continuum limit (model-dependent).
\item \textbf{(NP3) Sector isolation.} Existence of stable gapped sectors suitable for scattering.
\item \textbf{(NP4) Asymptotic completeness (optional/strong).} Identification of a complete particle-channel basis for $S$.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Auditable substitutes and falsifiability hooks}
\paragraph{Substitute for mass gap (finite witness).}
\AuditTag When a true mass-gap theorem is unavailable, the manuscript uses finite spectral-gap witnesses (Table~\ref{tab:kernel_rg_operator_spectral_gap}) as auditable substitutes, explicitly labeled as such (Appendix~\ref{app:gapped_sector_evidence_contract}).

\paragraph{Substitute for asymptotic completeness.}
\AuditTag When asymptotic completeness is unknown, the manuscript downgrades to effective interface scattering objects (S3 failure point) and keeps claims restricted to delay/phase proxies with explicit error models.

\subsubsection{Controlled model-class strategy (sample program)}
\AuditTag A practical nonperturbative program typically proceeds through controlled classes before approaching full 4D SM:
\begin{itemize}
\item low-dimensional or superrenormalizable models with constructive control (as method templates),
\item lattice/Euclidean constructions with provable continuum limits in restricted settings,
\item incremental inclusion of gauge/matter content with explicit anomaly constraints.
\end{itemize}
This appendix fixes this as the long-range strategy and requires any future claims to specify which controlled class is being used and which NP subtargets are actually discharged.
