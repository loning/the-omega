\begin{definition}[Radial defect index and minimal off-line distance]\label{def:xi-radial-defect-index-delta-min}
Under the completion representation \(\Xi_{\Delta,L}(s)=P_L(S_L(s))\), define the radial defect index
\[
\mathfrak r(P_L)
:=\sum_{\substack{S_\star\in\RR:\\ |S_\star|>2,\ P_L(S_\star)=0}}
\mathrm{mult}(S_\star),
\]
and the minimal off-line distance
\[
\delta_{\min}(P_L)
:=\min_{\substack{S_\star\in\RR:\\ |S_\star|>2,\ P_L(S_\star)=0}}
\frac{\mathrm{arcosh}(|S_\star|/2)}{\log L}
\quad\in(0,\infty],
\]
where \(\mathrm{mult}(S_\star)\) denotes the algebraic multiplicity of \(S_\star\) as a real root of \(P_L\); if \(P_L\) has no real roots satisfying \(|S_\star|>2\), then we set \(\mathfrak r(P_L)=0\) and \(\delta_{\min}(P_L)=+\infty\).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Off-critical vertical line family enumeration and irremovability for out-of-bound roots: rigidity meaning of the radial defect index]\label{prop:xi-radial-defect-index-offcritical-lines}
Under the setting of Definition \ref{def:xi-radial-defect-index-delta-min}:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Each real root \(S_\star\) satisfying \(|S_\star|>2\) (with algebraic multiplicity \(m\)) induces two off-critical vertical line families symmetric about the critical line
  \[
  \Re(s)=\frac12\pm \frac{\mathrm{arcosh}(|S_\star|/2)}{\log L},
  \]
  and each vertical line family carries a zero tower with multiplicity \(m\) in the root tower sense.
  Therefore, the total algebraic multiplicity sum of "off-critical vertical line family pairs" equals \(\mathfrak r(P_L)\), while the total off-critical zero tower multiplicity sum on both sides equals \(2\mathfrak r(P_L)\).
  \item If \(\mathfrak r(P_L)>0\), then \(\delta_{\min}(P_L)\) equals the minimal horizontal distance from all off-critical zeros to the critical line; if \(\mathfrak r(P_L)=0\), then no off-critical zero families exist and we may take \(\delta_{\min}(P_L)=+\infty\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By Corollary \ref{cor:xi-single-scale-radial-deviation-arcosh}, for each real root satisfying \(|S_\star|>2\) there exist two zero towers with real-part offset
\(
\mathrm{arcosh}(|S_\star|/2)/\log L
\);
the algebraic multiplicity of the root is preserved along each solution branch in the composition \(P_L(S_L(s))\) (except for the degenerate case at endpoints \(S_\star=\pm 2\); here \(|S_\star|>2\) excludes this degeneracy).
Summing all roots (counted with multiplicity) yields (1), while (2) follows directly from the minimum definition.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Fake constant factor forces off-critical zeros: explicit readout of minimal distance]\label{cor:xi-fake-factor-forces-offcritical-distance}
Adopt the construction of this subsection, take any real \(S_0>2\), and let
\(
P^{\mathrm{fake}}_L(S):=P_L(S)(S-S_0)
\).
Then the new factor \((S-S_0)\) necessarily forces off-critical zero families, and its newly introduced minimal horizontal distance is exactly
\[
\delta_0=\frac{\mathrm{arcosh}(S_0/2)}{\log L}.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
For the newly introduced root \(S_0>2\), directly applying Corollary \ref{cor:xi-single-scale-radial-deviation-arcosh} yields its contributed off-critical offset as \(\mathrm{arcosh}(S_0/2)/\log L\).
Since this offset is forced by the equation \(S_L(s)=S_0\) and cannot possibly be realized on the critical line, off-critical zero families are unavoidable.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Invariance under constant-layer strictification: out-of-bound roots cannot be repaired by pure constant counterterms]\label{prop:xi-radial-defect-index-counterterm-invariant}
Under the "constant-layer strictification" calibration of this paper, the allowed counterterm gate \(\mathrm{CT}_\eta\) acts only on the finite part/constant layer and preserves the exponential-scale principal structure (Definition \ref{def:pom-finite-part-counterterm-gate}; Proposition \ref{prop:pom-counterterm-anom-cancel}).
Under this constraint, as long as the root set of the polynomial \(P_L\) obtained from completion compression remains unchanged in \(\CC[S]\), then
\[
\mathfrak r(P_L)\ \text{and}\ \delta_{\min}(P_L)
\]
are both strictification invariants; thus the existence and minimal distance of any off-critical zero families forced by out-of-bound roots \(|S_\star|>2\) cannot be eliminated by pure constant counterterms.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By Proposition \ref{prop:xi-radial-defect-index-offcritical-lines}, \(\mathfrak r(P_L)\) and \(\delta_{\min}(P_L)\) depend only on the out-of-bound real root set of \(P_L\) and their multiplicities.
Since \(\mathrm{CT}_\eta\) under this calibration does not alter the completion compression and principal scale structure, it does not change the root set hypothesis of \(P_L\).
Hence both quantities remain invariant under strictification, establishing the conclusion.
\end{proof}
