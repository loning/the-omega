\section{Statistical Holography and Single-Trajectory Statistical Reconstruction}

Birkhoff's ergodic theorem gives: for measure-preserving transformations and $f\in L^1$, time averages exist almost everywhere; under ergodicity, time averages equal space averages almost everywhere \cite{EOM_Birkhoff}.
Let $f$ be any observable defined by fiber statistics (e.g., $M(x_t^{(6)})$, information density growth rate, first-passage time moments); then the long-range statistics of typical trajectories recover global invariant statistical laws. This paper calls this property statistical holography.

It is important to emphasize: this paper uses the term ``statistical holography'' only within the above ergodic/statistical framework; if strong reversible reconstruction of microstates or strong reversible state recovery is required, then additional conditions such as reversibility and two-sided generating partitions must be introduced, and this should not be stated as a general conclusion.
