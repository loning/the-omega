\section{One-Dimensional Ring Factor Scan and Hierarchy of Rational Approximations}

\subsection{Ring Rotation Dynamics}

The ring phase $\theta_t\in S^1$ is advanced via $(\alpha,\mathcal{P})$ as
\[
\theta_{t+1}=\theta_t+\alpha\pmod 1.
\]

\subsection{Rational/Irrational and Periodic/Dense}

Linear flow theory shows: on a torus, linear flow orbits are periodic when rationally related and dense when rationally independent. Restricting to the $S^1$ factor gives: when $\alpha\in\QQ$, the orbit is periodically closed; when $\alpha\notin\QQ$, the orbit is dense (see relevant discussions and citation paths in \cite{WikipediaSturmianWord}).
Under finite time and finite precision, realizable implementation of irrational $\alpha$ can be expressed through the sequence of rational approximations $p_k/q_k$.

\subsection{Consistency with Six-Dimensional Strip Approximation Body}

The conclusion from the six-dimensional hypercubic model provides geometric-level correspondence: rational $(1/1)$ strip projection produces periodic structure, while irrational $(\tau/1)$ strip projection produces quasicrystal structure \cite{PRB46_6091}.
This work treats it as a comparison between ``rational approximation-to-irrational limit'' at both geometric and symbolic levels.

\subsection{Rational Approximation--Periodic Orbit--Finite Sample Statistics (Bridging Lemma)}

\begin{lemma}[Periodicity of Rational Parameters]\label{lem:rational-periodic}
If $\alpha=p/q\in\QQ$ (in lowest terms), then the symbolic sequence $w_t=\mathbf{1}_{I_1}(\theta_t)$ of the ring rotation orbit $\theta_{t+1}=\theta_t+\alpha\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 1)$ is a periodic sequence with period dividing $q$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proposition}[Approximate Hierarchy of Finite Samples (Verifiable Proposition)]\label{prop:finite-sample-approx-hierarchy}
Let $\alpha\notin\QQ$ and take the continued fraction convergent sequence $\alpha_k=p_k/q_k\to\alpha$. For any fixed verification scale (e.g., factor frequencies of length $n$, complexity estimates, or balance deviations), the periodic sequence generated by $\alpha_k$ can serve as an approximate hierarchy for the irrational limit statistics within sufficiently large yet finite observation windows. This proposition serves as an experimentally verifiable bridging interface in this work and does not claim uniform convergence rates in generality.
\end{proposition}
