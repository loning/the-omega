\subsubsection{Certificate Chain Closure under Fixed-Slice Loading: Finite Frontier, Defect Ledger, and Endpoint Degeneration}\label{subsubsec:xi-fixed-slice-audit-chain}
In \S\ref{subsubsec:selfdual-blaschke-defect-fixed-slice}, this paper has already strictified the ``discrete orthogonal residual axis deviating from the fixed slice'' as the Blaschke defect in the disk factorization, and interpreted it as the point spectral residual of the minimal realization.
Furthermore, \S\ref{subsubsec:xi-offslice-quantitative-indicators} compresses off-slice point-wise data into residual dimension and residual weight, and provides exact summation laws for the origin modulus encapsulation and real-part deviation.
The present subsection, within the same ``auditable calculus'' regime, further extracts a collection of structural results: the core is not to pursue zeros directly at the analytic level, but rather to establish a closed, realization-independent certificate chain between \emph{conservative semantics on finite slices} and \emph{cross-scale defect functionals}, and to provide combinatorial endpoints consistent at both the ``average'' and ``worst-case'' ends.

\paragraph{Bounded Slice, Conservative Semantics, and Cost Preorder}
In the projection word calculus of this paper, the bounded-complexity slice \(\mathbf{PW}^{\ast}_{\le}\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-bounded-slice}) is generated by the minimal fragment
\(
\mathbf{PW}^{\ast}=\langle E_m,\mathrm{PROJ}_u,\mathrm{LIFT}_G,\mathrm{TWIST}(\chi),\mathrm{MOM}_q\rangle
\)
within parameter bounds and finite sampling grids.
The auditable semantics on the slice is given by the conservative functor
\(
\mathsf{Sem}:\mathbf{PW}^{\ast}_{\le}\to \mathbf{Sig}_{\le}\times\mathbf{Spec}_{\le}
\)
(Definition \ref{def:pom-sem-functor}; Theorem \ref{thm:pom-sem-conservative}).
On the resource side, the cost domain is equipped with the pointwise preorder on the tropical semiring \(\mathbb{T}^{(2)}\), and the cost functor \(\mathcal{C}:\mathbf{PW}\to\mathbf{Res}\) solidifies the monotonicity of ``compilation-type rewriting'' into a verifiable certificate (Definition \ref{def:pom-cost-functor}; Remark \ref{rem:pom-cost-invariant-vs-monotone}).

\begin{theorem}[Finite Frontier Principle on Bounded Slices: Certificate Search Collapses to Finite Enumeration at Each Resolution]\label{thm:xi-finite-frontier-certificate}
Within any fixed slice \(\mathbf{PW}^{\ast}_{\le}\) of Definition \ref{def:pom-bounded-slice}, take the conservative semantic functor \(\mathsf{Sem}\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-sem-functor}) and any cost functor \(\mathcal{C}\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-cost-functor}).
Then for any projection word \(w\in \mathbf{PW}^{\ast}_{\le}\), its semantic equivalence class
\(
[w]=\{w'\in\mathbf{PW}^{\ast}_{\le}:w'\simeq w\}
\)
has an image \(\mathcal{C}([w])\subseteq \mathbb{T}^{(2)}\) with a finite Pareto frontier, which can be constructed by finite enumeration.
Therefore, any certificate objective whose verification output consists of ``interface normal-form parameters -- anomaly signature -- spectral fingerprint'' (including the extraction targets of \(\zeta\)/primitive/finite-part constants) necessarily degenerates at each fixed resolution level to finite enumeration and finite verification; the difficulty is strictly concentrated in the mathematical question of ``how the slice upper bound grows with resolution,'' independently of realization details.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
This follows directly from the slice conservativity of \(\mathsf{Sem}\) (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-sem-conservative}) and Proposition \ref{prop:pom-cost-optimization-decidable}, which asserts that the Pareto frontier on bounded slices is finite and constructible by enumeration.
\end{proof}

Theorem \ref{thm:xi-finite-frontier-certificate}'s ``finite enumeration collapse'' is isomorphic to the discrete curvature reduction in \S\ref{subsec:dephysicalized-horizon-timefiber-nohair} (Theorem \ref{thm:dephys-offline-hair-curvature-reduction}): at each resolution level, viewing anomaly-curvature strictification (or its support minimization) as the target of certificate-driven rewriting, the result can be expressed within the semantic bucket as a \(\mathbb{T}^{(2)}\) Pareto minimal realization, and can therefore be completely covered by finite search.

\begin{theorem}[Auditable Uniqueness of the \texorpdfstring{$\zeta$}{zeta}-Interface: Trace \texorpdfstring{$\to$}{->} Primitive \texorpdfstring{$\to$}{->} Euler/\texorpdfstring{$\zeta$}{zeta} \texorpdfstring{$\to$}{->} Finite Part as a Fixed Compilation Target]\label{thm:xi-zeta-interface-auditable-uniqueness}
Under the determinant/trace regime of the two-variable \(\zeta_K(z,u)\) (Definition \ref{def:zetaK-zu}), a strictly commuting bridge package exists among the trace sequence, primitive stratified spectrum, and Euler product (Proposition \ref{prop:zetaK-mobius-primitive}; Remark \ref{rem:zetaK-commuting-diagram}).
Within the bounded slice, any projection word can be reduced to an interface normal form (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-three-gen-interface-normal-form}, Proposition \ref{prop:pom-mom-interface-normal-form}), thereby fixing ``trace \(\to\) primitive \(\to\) Euler/\(\zeta\)'' together with the finite-part fingerprint derived from \(\zeta\) (e.g., the Abel finite-part constant \(\log\mathfrak{M}(\theta)\); see Appendix \ref{app:real-input-40-logm-theta}) as a reproducible invariant family.
Consequently, under the regime where ``exact equality is delegated to signature \(\times\) spectral output,'' if two realizations agree on this fingerprint family after normalization, then their difference can only lie in compilation-class rewrites that preserve semantics while allowing monotone cost changes (Remark \ref{rem:pom-cost-invariant-vs-monotone}), and cannot alter conclusions at the \(\zeta\)/finite-part level.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Interface normal forms collapse candidate realizations into a finite enumerable family within the slice (Propositions \ref{prop:pom-three-gen-interface-normal-form}, \ref{prop:pom-mom-interface-normal-form}; see also Corollary \ref{cor:pom-three-gen-normal-form-decidable}).
Remark \ref{rem:zetaK-commuting-diagram} shows that, under the finite-kernel regime, \(\zeta\) and its Euler/primitive expansion are strictly determined by the trace sequence.
Combining with slice conservativity (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-sem-conservative}) and the monotone certification of the cost functor for compilation rewrites (Definition \ref{def:pom-cost-functor}), the conclusion holds.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Unique Carrier of Invisible Capacity: Exact Entropy Ledger for the Window Defect \texorpdfstring{$\kappa_m$}{kappa_m}]\label{thm:xi-kappa-unique-carrier}
Fix resolution \(m\ge 1\) and the folding readout \(\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m\), with fiber multiplicity \(d_m(x)=|\Fold_m^{-1}(x)|\).
For any macroscopic marginal distribution \(\pi\in\Delta(X_m)\), define the invisible capacity
\[
\kappa_m(\pi):=\mathbb{E}_{\pi}[\log d_m(X)]
\]
(Definition \ref{def:pom-kappa}).
Under the visible-domain constraint of the unitary slice and the \(\mathbf{PW}\) closure type constraint (Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}), if external recording axes are refused, then the labels internal to the folding fiber have no address in the visible semantics; in this case, for fixed \(\pi\), the maximum amount of information that any realization can ``fold away'' is exactly \(\kappa_m(\pi)\), and this quantity can be reproduced via an entropy ledger identity in a manner completely independent of realization details.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
This is a direct restatement of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-null-fiber-entropy-window}: for fixed macroscopic \(\pi\), the microscopic entropy window width is strictly equal to \(\kappa_m(\pi)\), and is given by an exact KL ledger identity in a realization-independent reproducible regime.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cross-Scale Defect Isomorphism: A Unified Interface for Window Defect and Orbit Finite Part]\label{thm:xi-defect-window-orbit-bridge}
Above the ``trace \(\to\) primitive \(\to\) Euler'' strictly commuting bridge package of the \(\zeta\)-interface (Remark \ref{rem:zetaK-commuting-diagram}), the fiber reflector \(K_m\) provides an auditable ``reflection compression'' interface: for any microscopic operator \(T\), the stable-layer compression \(\overline T_m=P_mTQ_m\) and the reflected visible part \(T_m^{\mathrm{aud}}=K_mTK_m\) share the same nonzero spectrum and the same trace sequence (Remark \ref{rem:reflected-witt-euler-bridge}).
Therefore:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Commuting bridge package)}
  Any \(\zeta\)/primitive/Euler fingerprint derived from the functoriality of the trace sequence is preserved under the reflection compression interface;
  \item \textbf{(Realization-independence of the finite-part fingerprint)}
  The Abel finite-part constant \(\log\mathfrak{M}(\theta)\) is a functorial finite-part output of \(\zeta_\theta\) (Appendix \ref{app:real-input-40-logm-theta}; see also \S\ref{subsec:logM-analyticity}), and hence its output is isomorphic under the auditable regime in the sense of trace-sequence agreement;
  \item \textbf{(Verifiable carrier of defects)}
  Consequently, for ``hidden degrees of freedom'' to persist across scales and affect audit conclusions, they must manifest as a verifiable shift in the invisible capacity \(\kappa_m\) at the window end or in the finite-part fingerprint \(\log\mathfrak{M}(\theta)\) at the orbit end; if both remain unchanged, then the degree of freedom is absorbed by reflection compression in the slice semantics and cannot enter the auditable output as a realization detail.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Point (1) is obtained by composing the trace-sequence preservation given by Remark \ref{rem:reflected-witt-euler-bridge} with the strict commutativity of Remark \ref{rem:zetaK-commuting-diagram}.
Point (2) follows from the definition of \(\log\mathfrak{M}(\theta)\) (Appendix \ref{app:real-input-40-logm-theta}) and its functoriality as a finite-part output of \(\zeta_\theta\) in the finite-dimensional regime.
Point (3) is a combined corollary of the first two points and the \(\mathsf{NULL}\) semantics of ``refusing external recording axes \(\Rightarrow\) fiber-internal labels have no address'' (the interpretation regime of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-null-fiber-entropy-window}).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Zero-Temperature Comparability: Tropicalization at the Endpoint Forces Average Semantics to Degenerate into Min-Plus Combinatorial Eigenvalues]\label{thm:xi-zero-temp-comparability}
Along the matrix realization of the temperature-ordered projection \(\mathrm{PROJ}_u\), take the tropicalized cost of the weighted adjacency matrix with potential kernel \(M_E(u)\):
\(
c_{\mathrm{trop}}(E)=\lim_{u\downarrow 0}\frac{\log\rho(M_E(u))}{\log u}
\)
(Definition \ref{def:pom-proj-tropical-cost}).
Then, under the setting of finite graph kernels and non-negative integer edge potentials, the zero-temperature limit exists and satisfies
\[
c_{\mathrm{trop}}(E)=\min_{\gamma\ \mathrm{cycle}}\frac{E(\gamma)}{|\gamma|}
\]
(Proposition \ref{prop:pom-proj-tropical-minmean}).
Therefore, any audit chain using the Perron root/spectral radius as its spectral fingerprint admits a combinatorial degeneration in the \(u\downarrow 0\) limit: its principal scale slope is pinned by \(c_{\mathrm{trop}}(E)\), thereby unifying the ``average--worst-case'' comparison semantics at the endpoint into a combinatorial invariant verifiable in polynomial time.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Proposition \ref{prop:pom-proj-tropical-minmean} gives the existence of the tropicalized limit and its min-mean-cycle representation; the ``average--worst-case'' unification interpretation follows directly from Remark \ref{rem:pom-proj-avg-worst-unified}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Two-Dimensional Frontier and Hardening Time Lower Bound: Impassable Resource Rigidity from the Joint Rate Function]\label{thm:xi-hardening-time-frontier-rigidity}
Under the mixed pressure \(\Psi(q,u)\) regime (Definition \ref{def:pom-psi-mixed-pressure}), the two-dimensional Legendre--Fenchel transform yields the spatial--energy joint spectral frontier (rate function) \(I(\gamma,\mathcal{E})\) (Remark \ref{rem:pom-psi-2d-legendre-front}).
If the order gate readout uses the twisted spectral gap scale \(\Gamma(u)=\log(\lambda_1(u)/\Lambda(u))\) (Corollary \ref{cor:pom-pressure-delay-lower-bound}), and the failure probability budget at resolution \(m\) satisfies \(\varepsilon_m\le e^{-\alpha m}\), then viewing the ``soft order gate \(\Rightarrow\) hard order gate'' hardening as a necessary step of the audit chain, the minimal iteration depth satisfies the linear lower bound
\[
n_{\min}(m)\ \gtrsim\ \frac{\log(1/\varepsilon_m)}{\Gamma(u)}\ \ge\ \frac{\alpha}{\Gamma(u)}\,m,
\]
and when \(\alpha\) is provided by the rate function frontier (e.g., taking \(\alpha=I(\gamma,\mathcal{E})\)), this minimal time growth is rigidly constrained by the joint spectral frontier.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
This is the ``frontier-driven'' restatement of Corollary \ref{cor:pom-pressure-delay-lower-bound}; the connection between \(\alpha\) and the rate function \(I\) is given by Remark \ref{rem:pom-psi-2d-legendre-front}.
\end{proof}
