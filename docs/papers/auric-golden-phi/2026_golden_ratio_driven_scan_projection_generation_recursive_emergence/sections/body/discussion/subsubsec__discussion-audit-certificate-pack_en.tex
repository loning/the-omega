\subsubsection{Auditable Certificate Pack Interface: Algebraic Decisibility Regime for Mechanism Equivalence}\label{subsubsec:discussion-audit-certificate-pack}
This manuscript repeatedly employs the same strategy at multiple levels: compress what appear to be "mechanistic complex differences" into a minimal set of computable and verifiable algebraic objects, thereby rewriting the mechanism-equivalence question as a consistency determination over a finite set of certificates.
Certificates here refer to short objects (spectral radius, characteristic polynomial, Jordan residual, Hankel rank, endpoint exponents, etc.) uniquely induced by the mechanism under a fixed readout and baseline regime.
Under this regime, the falsifiable meaning of "same mechanism" is not similarity of narrative explanations, but consistency of these objects in the auditable sense; any stable mismatch of a single component provides a hard interface for equivalence refutation.

\paragraph{Gauge Discrepancy as Minimal Prototype: Locking Low-Order Constants and Endpoint Exponents by Spectral Radius}
The non-commutativity of resolution reduction can be quantitatively characterized by position-wise mismatch indication of the gauge discrepancy \(g_t=\mathbf 1\{X_t\neq Y_t\}\) (\S\ref{subsubsec:insight-gauge-anomaly}; Definition \ref{def:fold-gauge-anomaly}).
Under the uniform baseline, its exponential moment generating function's limiting pressure is completely characterized by the spectral radius of a \(4\times 4\) weighted adjacency matrix (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure}):
\[
P_G(\theta)=\log\rho(A_\theta),\qquad
A_\theta=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}
1 & e^\theta & 0 & 1\\
0 & 0 & e^\theta & 0\\
e^\theta & 2 & 0 & 0\\
1 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus at \(\theta=0\), the mean density, variance rate, and third cumulant rate are all locked by the closed forms of \(P_G'(0),P_G''(0),P_G^{(3)}(0)\) (same proposition), and endpoint rare-event exponents also yield complete closed forms (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-ldp}).
Moreover, the above density/fluctuation/pressure constant chains remain invariant under the fixed-step-length multi-step reduction regime (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-kstep-invariance}), thereby extending certificate stability from single-step reduction to finite-step lookahead readout.
Furthermore, the Jordan defect at \(-\tfrac12\) strictly manifests in the relevant closed forms as "exponential decay \(\times\) linear polynomial" residual (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-A0-jordan}), inducing finite-sample verifiable Hankel rank certificates and fixed-order recurrence constraints (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-hankel-rank3}; see \S\ref{par:fold-gauge-anomaly-spectral-recurrence-triangle}).
The rate curve of the same mechanism can also be pinned down by an integer-coefficient elimination certificate: eliminating the stationary parameter yields a resultant fingerprint \(R(\alpha,u)\in\mathbb Z[\alpha,u]\) (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-rate-curve-param}; Table \ref{tab:fold_gauge_anomaly_rate_curve_resultant_degree}).
This yields a typical "spectral—constant—endpoint" ultra-short certificate chain: though short, it is independent of interpretive narratives and independently verifiable.

\paragraph{Three Types of Certificate Pack Outputs: Algebraic Relations, Low-Order Statistics, and Endpoint Exponents}
At the organizational level of auditable interfaces, the above prototype can be abstracted into three complementary types of certificate outputs, covering formalization, black-box, and robustness aspects:
\par
\textbf{Algebraic certificates:}
Compress the mechanism into integer-coefficient polynomial relations after elimination and branch selection rules.
Such certificates have "coefficient table + feasible branches" as the entire storage payload: e.g., the quartic algebraic equation for \(\mu(u)\) (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure}) and the resultant \(R(\alpha,u)\) for the rate curve (Table \ref{tab:fold_gauge_anomaly_rate_curve_resultant_degree}).
Under this regime, any claimed-equivalent implementation must reproduce the same algebraic relation (or fall into the explicitly specified same Perron branch), otherwise it cannot pass certificate verification.
\par
\textbf{Low-order statistic certificates:}
Use a small number of stably estimable statistics as refutation interfaces, e.g., the constant-level fingerprints \(P_G'(0),P_G''(0),P_G^{(3)}(0)\) (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure} and Corollary \ref{cor:fold-gauge-anomaly-cumulant3}), supplemented by finite-order recurrence/low Hankel rank constraints to compress the degrees of freedom of "correlation structure" (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-hankel-rank3}).
The key use of this class of certificates is black-box auditing: without entering implementation details of the mechanism, finite samples suffice to issue falsifiable rejection of "non-identical mechanism".
\par
\textbf{Endpoint exponent certificates:}
Seal far-field limits by endpoint exponents of rare events, forming a two-sided anchoring together with local derivative constants.
The gauge discrepancy endpoint closed forms \(I(0)=\log(2/\varphi)\), \(I(1)=\log 2\) (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-ldp}) provide a typical example: they fix the exponential rates at both "full-match/full-mismatch" ends as pure algebraic constants, thus providing unavoidable robustness anchor points for far-field behavior under mechanism substitution.

\paragraph{Modular Organization of Unified Audit Pack: Collecting Cross-Chapter Invariants into a Single Output Interface}
Reorganizing the established verifiable interfaces throughout the manuscript by output type yields a modular certificate pack perspective; each component corresponds to a verifiable object chain:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Curvature certificate (non-commutative obstruction and error bars):}
  Quantify the "truncate-then-fold" vs.\ "fold-then-restrict" non-commutative obstruction via the gauge curvature indicator \(\mathcal K_{m+1\to m}\) of resolution reduction (Definition \ref{def:pom-gauge-curvature}), providing measurable error bars for any bounded observable via curvature probability (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-gauge-curvature-shadow-bound}; Table \ref{tab:fold_truncation_curvature_stats}).
  \item \textbf{Spectral certificate (spectral radius and defect modes of finite-state operator families):}
  Use \(P(\theta)=\log\rho(A_\theta)\) or equivalent characteristic polynomial/power spectral rational fingerprints as auditable spectral objects (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-pressure}; Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-psd-rational}), characterizing the rigid projection of non-semisimple defects to fluctuations and correlations via Jordan residual (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-A0-jordan}).
  \item \textbf{Elimination certificate (integer-coefficient resultant of rate curves):}
  Use \(R(\alpha,u)\in\mathbb Z[\alpha,u]\) as the unforgeable fingerprint of "stationary-point—rate-curve—branch-selection" (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-gauge-anomaly-rate-curve-param}; Table \ref{tab:fold_gauge_anomaly_rate_curve_resultant_degree}).
  \item \textbf{Volume certificate (closed-form connection of gauge volume density and folding defect):}
  Use the second-order Stirling expansion of the gauge group volume density \(g_m\) and folding defect potential \(\kappa_m\) as verifiable bridging objects, with exponentially small error bars (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-bin-gauge-volume-stirling-second-order}).
  \item \textbf{Arithmetic certificate (Hankel principal minors and \texorpdfstring{$p$}{p}-adic rigidity):}
  Pin "visible state count" via Hankel rank/minimal realization dimension, using \(\mathsf{Stiff}_p(k)=v_p(\det(H_k))\) as internal arithmetic rigidity fingerprint (\S\ref{subsec:pom-moment-visibility}; Remark \ref{rem:pom-hankel-stiffness-p5}).
  \item \textbf{Graph polynomial certificate (reconstructible factorization within fibers and \texorpdfstring{$\bmod 3$}{mod 3} obstruction):}
  Completely reconstruct fiber generating functions \(Z_x(t)\) via independent set systems of the induced graph \(\Gamma_x\) (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-fiber-indset-factorization}), yielding \(\bmod 3\) obstruction via signed index \(\mathfrak{s}_m(x)=Z_x(-1)\in\{0,\pm 1\}\) vanishing criterion (\S\ref{subsec:pom-fiber-signed-index}; Theorem \ref{thm:pom-fiber-signed-index-periodicity}).
\end{itemize}
Under this organizational mode, any subsequent cross-domain bridging (e.g., horizon positivity of unitary-slice, spectral-gap conditions for kernel-version RH/GRH, and POM's certificate rewrite chains) can be uniformly positioned as: outputting verifiable certificate objects before interpretive statements, and using certificate consistency as the primary criterion for mechanism equivalence.
Under the reproducible experiment pipeline regime, this equals a unified audit interface: input a black-box output sequence (or its low-order statistical digest), output certificate objects such as minimal polynomials/spectral fingerprints/recurrence constraints, and return pass/fail verifiable determination.
