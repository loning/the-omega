
\section{Discussion (interpretation-layer mappings and falsifiable directions)}
\label{sec:discussion}

The closed layer of this paper formalizes Wish as protocol-stable period data and derives a certified finite-resource variational dynamics on protocol space.
This section records cautious interpretation-layer mappings and concrete falsifiable directions that do not enter any proof.

\subsection{A precise sense of ``teleology''}
\label{sec:discussion_teleology}

In the closed layer, ``teleology'' is not a psychological claim.
It refers to the existence of a target functional $U_N(\theta)$---built from certified discrepancy to a Wish and a cost penalty---whose gradient generates a dissipative dynamics \eqref{eq:teleology_ode}.
In this strict sense, teleological behavior is nothing more than the dynamics of minimizing an explicit, auditable energy.

\subsection{Learning-language correspondence (structure, not identity)}
\label{sec:discussion_learning}

If one interprets protocol parameters $\theta$ as ``model parameters'' and $U_N(\theta)$ as a finite-horizon loss, then \eqref{eq:teleology_ode} is the continuous-time heavy-ball (Polyak) dynamics with damping \cite{Polyak1964}.
The correspondence is structural:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Target object}: Wish $\leftrightarrow$ supervised target period datum.
\item \textbf{Loss}: certified discrepancy and cost $\leftrightarrow$ audit-compatible loss with regularization.
\item \textbf{Training/time}: protocol evolution $\theta(t)$ $\leftrightarrow$ gradient-driven learning dynamics.
\end{itemize}
No closed theorem identifies physical time with optimization time; the analogy serves only as a vocabulary bridge once the protocol space is chosen as the effective degrees of freedom.

\subsection{Observers and readout kernels}
\label{sec:discussion_observers}

In the protocol definition, the readout kernel $f$ and regularization $R$ specify the semantic projection from an orbit to a number.
Thus, at the protocol level, changing ``what is observed'' is precisely changing $(f,R)$ and hence changing which Wish potential $\widehat V_N$ is induced.
Any further interpretation of this choice as ``consciousness'' or ``agency'' is external to the closed layer and should be stated only as a testable model mapping.

\subsection{Falsifiable directions}
\label{sec:discussion_falsifiable}

The framework suggests at least two falsifiable empirical signatures, expressed purely at protocol level:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Stability preference for low discrepancy.}
If stable constants correspond to low $J_N(\mathcal{P};W_0)$ in Conjecture~\ref{conj:selection} (with $J_N$ as in Definition~\ref{def:certified_selection_functional}), then protocols implementing them should preferentially exhibit low star discrepancy (or sharp discrepancy certificates), e.g.\ constant-type scan slopes in one dimension.
\item \textbf{Rigidity gaps under bounded description.}
If a constant admits a large uniqueness gap in a bounded hypothesis class (as in Proposition~\ref{prop:alpha_rigidity}), then the corresponding minimizer should be robust under target perturbations and noise; this is testable within the same bounded class and does not require a physical identification.
\end{itemize}
