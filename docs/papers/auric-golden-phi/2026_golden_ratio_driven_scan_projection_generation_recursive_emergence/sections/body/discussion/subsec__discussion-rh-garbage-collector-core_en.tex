\subsection{Core Conclusion Cluster: Strictification of the ``Critical Line as Garbage Collector'' (Witt--Euler--Fredholm--Unitary Slice--CMV Limit)}\label{subsec:discussion-rh-garbage-collector-core}
This subsection presents a set of structural results that can directly serve as the ``core conclusion cluster'' of the entire paper, used to strictify the phenomenon that ``RH/the critical line acts as a garbage collector.''
The term ``garbage collector'' is used only as a metaphor: its mathematical substance consists of two strictly commuting compression mechanisms that recycle any auditable object into the same class of verifiable events.
The first mechanism is \textbf{conjugacy forgetting (Witt--Euler commutative diagram)}: it reads out an endomorphism only through conjugacy invariants (trace sequences/primitive sequences/Euler products);
the second mechanism is \textbf{completion to single-point reduction (spectral crossing on the unitary slice)}: it compresses a critical line zero event to the single-point spectral event of ``whether the spectrum crosses \(1\)'' on the unitary slice.
Both mechanisms have reproducible realizations on trace-class Fredholm determinants and CMV stabilization limits, thereby forming a closed, auditable, and invertible compression framework.

\begin{theorem}[Strictly commutative Witt--Euler diagram: the ``forgetful functor'' of conjugacy invariants]\label{thm:discussion-witt-euler-gc-functor}
Consider the category \(\mathrm{End}^{\prime}\) that retains only similarity-conjugacy invariants (objects are \((V,T)\), morphisms are similarity transformations), the sequence category \(\mathrm{Seq}\), and the Euler power series category \(\mathrm{Euler}=1+z\CC[[z]]\).
Define the functors
\[
\mathrm{TrSeq}(T)=(a_n)_{n\ge 1},\qquad a_n:=\Tr(T^n),
\]
\[
\mathrm{Mob}(a)=p,\qquad p_n:=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)\,a_{n/d},
\]
\[
\mathrm{Euler}(p)(z):=\prod_{n\ge 1}(1-z^n)^{-p_n},
\qquad
\mathrm{ZetaDet}(T)(z):=\det(I-zT)^{-1}.
\]
Then the following strictly commutative identity always holds:
\[
\boxed{\ \mathrm{ZetaDet}=\mathrm{Euler}\circ\mathrm{Mob}\circ\mathrm{TrSeq}\ }.
\]
In the notation of this paper, this is the same statement as \(\mathcal Z=\mathcal E\circ\mathcal M\circ\mathcal T\), where \(\mathcal T=\mathrm{TrSeq}\), \(\mathcal M=\mathrm{Mob}\), \(\mathcal E=\mathrm{Euler}\), \(\mathcal Z=\mathrm{ZetaDet}\).
Equivalently,
\[
\det(I-zT)^{-1}
=\exp\!\Bigl(\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{\Tr(T^n)}{n}z^n\Bigr)
=\prod_{n\ge 1}(1-z^n)^{-p_n}\in 1+z\CC[[z]].
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
See Remark \ref{rem:zetaK-commuting-diagram} (composition of Proposition \ref{prop:zetaK-mobius-primitive} and Definition \ref{def:fredholm-determinant}). This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Complete characterization of the GC equivalence relation]\label{cor:discussion-gc-equivalence-characterization}
For finite-dimensional operators (matrices) \(T_1,T_2\), the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \(\det(I-zT_1)=\det(I-zT_2)\);
  \item \(\Tr(T_1^n)=\Tr(T_2^n)\) for all \(n\ge 1\);
  \item \(\mathrm{Mob}(\mathrm{TrSeq}(T_1))=\mathrm{Mob}(\mathrm{TrSeq}(T_2))\).
\end{enumerate}
Therefore, ``being regarded as the same object by GC'' can be completely characterized by the full trace sequence (ghost) or the full primitive sequence.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem \ref{thm:discussion-witt-euler-gc-functor},
\(
\det(I-zT)^{-1}
=\exp(\sum_{n\ge 1}\Tr(T^n)z^n/n)
\);
the two sides are equal if and only if the coefficients in the exponent agree term by term, i.e., (1)\(\Longleftrightarrow\)(2).
(2)\(\Longleftrightarrow\)(3) follows immediately from the invertibility of M\"obius inversion (Proposition \ref{prop:zetaK-mobius-primitive}). This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Reverse section theorem from Euler product to trace-class Fredholm \texorpdfstring{$\zeta$}{zeta}: any atomic Euler product can be constructively realized]\label{thm:discussion-traceclass-fredholm-euler-surjection}
Let \(\Pi_n\) be the standard \(n\)-cycle permutation matrix. Then
\[
\det(I-t\Pi_n)=1-t^n
\]
always holds (Proposition \ref{prop:cycle-permutation-determinant}).
Therefore, for \(C(n,\alpha)=\alpha\Pi_n\), we have
\(\det(I-rC(n,\alpha))=1-(\alpha r)^n\).
\par
More generally, given arbitrary Euler-type product data \(\mathcal D=\{(n_j,\beta_j,m_j)\}_{j\in J}\) satisfying the trace-class executability condition \eqref{eq:tc-executability}, there exists an explicit trace-class operator \(L_{\mathcal D}\in\mathcal{S}_1(H_{\mathcal D})\) (Definition \ref{def:traceclass-primitive-data}) whose Fredholm \(\zeta\)-interface
\[
\zeta_{\mathcal D}(r):=\det(I-rL_{\mathcal D})^{-1}
\]
strictly reproduces the given Euler product on \(|r|<1\):
\[
\boxed{\ \zeta_{\mathcal D}(r)=\prod_{j\in J}\bigl(1-\beta_j r^{n_j}\bigr)^{-m_j}\ }.
\]
Thus, any Euler-type product satisfying this executability condition lies in the image of the trace-class Fredholm \(\zeta\)-interface, and the ``primitive factors'' in this image possess integer spectral multiplicities and automatic NonCancel certification.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem \ref{thm:cyclic-fredholm-witt} and Corollary \ref{cor:cyclic-euler-product}, using Proposition \ref{prop:cycle-permutation-determinant}. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Euler factor = single-basepoint unit root orbit (primitive atom)]\label{cor:discussion-euler-factor-is-basepoint-orbit}
Each factor \((1-\beta r^{n})^{-m}\) is equivalent to a cyclic block of length \(n\), \(C(n,\alpha)\otimes I_m\), where \(\alpha^n=\beta\).
The spectrum of this block is the \(n\)-th root of unity orbit of the single basepoint \(\alpha\):
\(
\{\alpha\omega:\omega^n=1\}
\)
with algebraic multiplicity \(m\) at each spectral point (Theorem \ref{thm:cyclic-fredholm-witt}).
Therefore, ``primitive/prime-like'' in this paper's caliber precisely corresponds to \textbf{conjugacy invariant of a cyclic orbit}: a primitive factor compresses a multi-point spectrum into ``the orbit of a single basepoint,'' and serves as the minimal recycling unit of \(\mathrm{ZetaDet}\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral flow of the two-layer bridge: critical line zeros compressed to ``eigenvalue crossing \(1\)'' as a single-point event]\label{thm:discussion-critical-line-single-point-crossing}
Under the self-dual completion setting (\(\Delta(z,u)=\Delta(uz,1/u)\)), if furthermore \(\Delta(z,u)=\det(I-zB(u))\), then along the completion section
\(
\Xi_{\Delta,L}(s)=\Delta(L^{-s},L^{2s-1})
\)
and under the unitary slice parameter \(\theta=2t\log L\), there exists a normalized kernel family
\(
U_L(\theta):=L^{-1/2}e^{-i\theta/2}B(e^{i\theta})
\)
such that
\[
\boxed{\ \Xi_{\Delta,L}\!\left(\tfrac12+it\right)=\det\!\bigl(I-U_L(\theta)\bigr)\ },
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{\ \Xi_{\Delta,L}\!\left(\tfrac12+it\right)=0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ 1\in\mathrm{Spec}(U_L(\theta))\ }.
\]
Consequently, after completion and unitary slice reduction, the critical line zero event is compressed to a spectral crossing event at the single point \(1\) on the unit circle.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
See Proposition \ref{prop:xi-unitary-slice-spectral-crossing}. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Pointwise stabilization of finite CMV certificates \texorpdfstring{$\Rightarrow$}{=>} infinite-dimensional CMV unitary spectral realization]\label{thm:discussion-cmv-stabilization-core}
Let \(\{U_L^{\mathrm{CMV}}\}_L\) be a family of finite-dimensional CMV-like companion matrix realizations whose Verblunsky/Schur parameters \(\alpha_n(L)\) stabilize pointwise at each fixed \(n\) to \(\alpha_n\).

\widetilde U_L\to U_\infty\quad\text{in the strong operator topology},
\qquad
\mu_L\Rightarrow \mu_\infty,
\]
and the corresponding Carath\'eodory functions converge locally uniformly on compact subsets of \(\mathbb{D}\).
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Concluding synthesis: strictification of the ``garbage collector'' property of RH}
Combining Theorems \ref{thm:discussion-witt-euler-gc-functor}--\ref{thm:discussion-cmv-stabilization-core}, the phenomenon that ``any concept ultimately exhibits an RH-type decision'' can be rigorously localized as a three-step compression chain:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Conjugacy forgetting (first layer):} The auditable output of \((V,T)\) is strictly recycled via \(\mathrm{ZetaDet}\) into Euler product data (trace/primitive/Euler), and this recycling is controlled by a strictly commutative diagram (Theorem \ref{thm:discussion-witt-euler-gc-functor}).
  \item \textbf{Cyclic atomization (trace-class surjection):} Any executable Euler-type product can be reproduced by the Fredholm determinant of an explicit trace-class operator \(L_{\mathcal D}\); primitive factors correspond to unit root orbits of single basepoints, thereby compressing multi-point spectra into combinations of single-basepoint orbits (Theorem \ref{thm:discussion-traceclass-fredholm-euler-surjection} and Corollary \ref{cor:discussion-euler-factor-is-basepoint-orbit}).
  \item \textbf{Completion to single-point crossing (second layer):} Self-dual completion reduces ``the critical line'' to the unitary slice, and compresses critical line zero events to the single-point event ``eigenvalue crossing \(1\)''; pointwise stabilization of finite CMV certificates further ensures that this single-point mechanism closes on infinite-dimensional unitary spectral objects (Theorem \ref{thm:discussion-critical-line-single-point-crossing} and Theorem \ref{thm:discussion-cmv-stabilization-core}).
\end{enumerate}
Therefore, ``black-hole-like compression/garbage collection'' in this paper is not a logical equivalence between objects, but a homomorphization and single-point reduction enforced by the two-layer bridge: internal structure is recycled via the first layer into an Euler/primitive ledger, while the completion further compresses visible anomalies into a crossing audit at the single point \(1\) on the unitary slice.
