\paragraph{Two-point finitization of the anomaly axis and finite probe limits of RH-sharp combs: coprimality discrimination, evasion mechanisms, and resolution scaling}
In the \(\mathbf{PW}\) semantics of this paper, the anomaly class \([\mathrm{Anom}]\) serves both as a path invariant for rewrite closure loops and as a logical trigger axis determining whether ``infinite-family constraints admit finite certificates''; within the cyclic lift closure of RH-sharp, the imaginary structure of critical-line poles further provides a quantifiable ``finite sampling'' limit. The conclusive interface can be summarized as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Coprime two-point discrimination (without requiring torsion-free): two-point completeness of all-moment-vanishing anomaly.}
  In the anomaly cohomology group \(H\), the moment amplification law
  \(
  [\mathrm{Anom}(\mathrm{MOM}_q\circ w)]=q\,[\mathrm{Anom}(w)]
  \)
  implies that any two coprime-point vanishing test suffices to deduce \([\mathrm{Anom}(w)]=0\) (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-anom-coprime-two-point-test}), which equivalently finitizes the proposition ``anomaly vanishes at all moment orders \(q\ge 2\)'' (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-anom-all-moments-collapse-to-two}; Corollary~\ref{cor:pom-anom-oracle-collapse-two-point-trigger}).

  \item \textbf{Finite-probe non-falsifiability: cyclic lifts evade any finite sampling set.}
  If there exists a saturating mode \(\mu=\sqrt{\lambda}e^{i\theta}\), then the cyclic lift \(M^{\langle q\rangle}\) generates a \(q\)-equidistributed comb on the critical line;
  except for trivial alignment cases, any finite sampling set \(S\) can be simultaneously evaded by infinitely many lift orders \(q\) (Theorem~\ref{thm:zeta-cyclic-lift-finite-probe-evasion}).
  Thus ``finite-probe auditing'' cannot exclude the existence of RH-sharp critical-line poles in the lift closure sense.

  \item \textbf{Resolution of addressable grids: \texorpdfstring{$Q^{-1}$}{Q^-1} for general phase, improvable to \texorpdfstring{$Q^{-2}$}{Q^-2} for aligned phase.}
  Let \(Q\) be the upper bound on admissible cyclic lift orders; then the worst-case resolution \(\varepsilon_Q\) induced by the union of RH-sharp combs over the fundamental period satisfies the generic scaling
  \(
  \varepsilon_Q\asymp (Q\log\lambda)^{-1}
  \)
  (Corollary~\ref{cor:zeta-cyclic-lift-resolution-law}), and the point-counting lower bound yields a universal \(Q^{-2}\) rate (Proposition~\ref{prop:zeta-cyclic-lift-covering-radius-counting-lb}).
  In the aligned case \(\theta\equiv 0\), the grid further degenerates to a standard Farey-type rational grid, achieving the improved worst-case covering radius of \(\Theta(Q^{-2})\) (Remark~\ref{rem:zeta-cyclic-lift-aligned-farey-q2}).
\end{enumerate}
