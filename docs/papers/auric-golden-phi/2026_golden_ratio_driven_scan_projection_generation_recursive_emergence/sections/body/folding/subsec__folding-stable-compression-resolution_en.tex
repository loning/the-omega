\subsection{Stable Compression Ratio and Formalization of Resolution Insufficiency}\label{subsec:folding-stable-compression-resolution}

The folding compression ratio is characterized by
\[
\frac{|\Omega_m|}{|X_m|}=\frac{2^m}{F_{m+2}}.
\]
Since $F_{m+2}\asymp \varphi^m$, the average folding multiplicity has exponential growth rate $(2/\varphi)^m$. Therefore, although the number of stable types grows exponentially with $m$, the number of microstates grows even faster, and folding is necessarily many-to-one: distinct microstates become indistinguishable at finite resolution and are assigned to the same stable type. This conclusion provides a rigorous formulation of ``resolution insufficiency prevents readout of regularities'': the failure to read out is not a lack of regularity, but rather the stable projection compresses a large number of micro-differences into a single class, thereby losing distinguishable information at the microscopic level.

\subsubsection{Information Dichotomy and Fiber Residuals}
Under the Zeckendorf syntax constraint, the fold $\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m$ decomposes observable microstate information into two classes: the first is the stable type $x:=\Fold_m(\omega)\in X_m$, serving as the canonical representative of the equivalence class; the second is the fiber residual $r\in \Fold_m^{-1}(x)$, which characterizes the degrees of freedom that are compressed away but must be externally carried within a reversible reconstruction caliber. The compression ratio $|\Omega_m|/|X_m|=2^m/F_{m+2}$ indicates that the second class of degrees of freedom cannot be neglected as resolution increases; the corresponding fiber size is quantified by $d_m(x):=|\Fold_m^{-1}(x)|$, and enters the entropy decomposition in the information ledger of Section \ref{subsec:pom-information-ledger} in the form $\mathbb{E}_{\pi}[\log d_m(X)]$. More refined fiber internal structure can be read out in closed form directly from the Projection Ontology Mathematics part: the component decomposition of fibers and the ``inverse substitution count distribution'' are given in Theorems \ref{thm:pom-fiber-indset-factorization}--\ref{thm:pom-path-indset-poly-closed}; the signed index and \(\bmod 3\) obstruction are given in Theorem \ref{thm:pom-fiber-signed-index-periodicity}; the thermodynamic completion (LDP/CLT) of fiber indices is given in Theorems \ref{thm:pom-fiber-index-ldp-thermo}--\ref{thm:pom-fiber-index-clt-thermo}.
