\paragraph{Multiplicative chain rule and entropy-loss field: multiplicative decomposition of pressure spectrum, geometric telescope, and freezing criterion}
In the escort caliber, the integer pressure spectrum \(\{P_q\}_{q\ge 1}\) (where \(P_q=\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}\log S_q(m)\)) and R\'enyi entropy rates admit a suite of strict ``multiplicative consistency'' identities: they precisely decompose the pressure jump on the multiplicative scale \(q\mapsto ab\) into entropy-loss terms, thereby deriving geometric telescope convergence, freezing criterion, and the constraint cone of realizable spectra. The conclusive interface can be summarized as:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Multiplicative chain rule: exact decomposition identity of pressure spectrum on multiplicative scale.}
  At the finite-level, the R\'enyi entropy of the escort distribution \(\pi_{m,a}=w_{m,a}\) yields the strict identity
  \(\log S_{ab}(m)=b\log S_a(m)-(b-1)H_b(\pi_{m,a})\) (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-multiplicative-chain-rule-finite-m});
  at the limit level it yields the multiplicative decomposition
  \(P_{ab}=bP_a-(b-1)h^{\mathrm{esc}}_b(a)\) (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-pressure-entropy-factorization-multiplicative}).

  \item \textbf{Multiplicative refinement monotonicity: geometric telescope convergence of \texorpdfstring{$\alpha_\ast$}{alpha*} and explicit error bounds.}
  The multiplicative refinement sequence \(\alpha_{a,b}(n)=P_{ab^n}/(ab^n)\) is monotone non-increasing, with a telescope expansion and computable error upper bound
  \(0\le \alpha_{a,b}(N)-\alpha_\ast\le (\log\varphi)/(ab^N)\) (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-multiplicative-refinement-telescope-alpha}).

  \item \textbf{Observability: integer pressure spectrum completely determines all escort R\'enyi entropy rates.}
  The entropy-rate surface can be point-wise inverted from the pressure spectrum:
  \(h^{\mathrm{esc}}_b(a)=(bP_a-P_{ab})/(b-1)\), and the baseline distribution satisfies \(h^{\mathrm{esc}}_b(1)=(b\log 2-P_b)/(b-1)\) (Corollary~\ref{cor:pom-escort-renyi-rate-inversion}).

  \item \textbf{Entropy-loss \(1\)-cocycle: flat structure on multiplicative semigroup and decomposition formula.}
  The entropy-loss field \(\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{loss}}(a,b):=bP_a-P_{ab}\) satisfies the strict \(1\)-cocycle equation
  \(\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{loss}}(a,bc)=c\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{loss}}(a,b)+\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{loss}}(ab,c)\), inducing factor-chain expansion and flatness identities for commutative consistency (Definition~\ref{def:pom-entropy-loss-cocycle}; Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-entropy-loss-1cocycle-flat}; Corollary~\ref{cor:pom-entropy-loss-factor-chain-expansion}).

  \item \textbf{Zero-temperature identity: escort min-entropy rate equals \texorpdfstring{$P_a-a\alpha_\ast$}{Pa-a alpha*}.}
  The min-entropy rate of the escort satisfies \(h_\infty^{\mathrm{esc}}(a)=P_a-a\alpha_\ast\), yielding the freezing criterion: \(h_\infty^{\mathrm{esc}}(a)=0\Longleftrightarrow P_{ab}=bP_a\) (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-escort-minentropy-zero-temperature-identity}; Corollary~\ref{cor:pom-escort-freezing-criterion}).

  \item \textbf{Constraint cone for entropy-spectrum realizability: non-trivial feasible region derived from bounds, flatness, and freezing limits.}
  The non-negativity/upper bounds of \(\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{loss}}\), the \(1\)-cocycle flatness, and freezing-limit compatibility jointly define the feasible region \(\mathfrak{C}\) of pressure spectra, providing falsifiable necessary constraints for any candidate pressure spectrum (Theorem~\ref{thm:pom-entropy-loss-feasibility-cone}).
\end{enumerate}
