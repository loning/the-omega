\begin{remark}[Conditional Flux as Conditional Volume: \texorpdfstring{$V_{\partial}=\prod_m\sqrt{\pi_m}$}{Vpartial=prod sqrt(pi_m)}]\label{rem:xi-pick-poisson-schur-conditional-volume}
Under the setting of Proposition \ref{prop:xi-pick-poisson-schur-flux-ledger}, define the boundary parallelepiped volume
\[
V_{\partial}:=\sqrt{\det\mathsf P}.
\]
Then from the Schur chain factorization \(\det\mathsf P=\prod_{m=1}^{\kappa}\pi_m\), we obtain the strict product law
\[
\boxed{\;
V_{\partial}=\prod_{m=1}^{\kappa}\sqrt{\pi_m},\qquad
S_{\mathrm{gen}}=-\log\det\mathsf P=\sum_{m=1}^{\kappa}(-\log\pi_m).\;}
\]
Thus each increment \(-\log\pi_m\) can be interpreted as the "conditional volume loss on the existing defect spanning space"; moreover \(0<\pi_m\le p_m\) yields an explicit squeeze where the stepwise loss is controlled from above by the endpoint self-energy.
\end{remark}
