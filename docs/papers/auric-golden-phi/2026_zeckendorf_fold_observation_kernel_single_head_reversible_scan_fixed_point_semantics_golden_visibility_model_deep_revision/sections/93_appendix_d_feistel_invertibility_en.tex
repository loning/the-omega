\section{Appendix D: Invertibility of Feistel Single-Head Update}

The local transformation $(h,c)\mapsto(h',c')=(c,\ h\oplus f(c))$ has inverse
\[
c=h',\qquad h=c'\oplus f(h').
\]
Therefore, for any $f$, the local update is a bijection, and composing with position permutation makes the global update $U$ a permutation.
