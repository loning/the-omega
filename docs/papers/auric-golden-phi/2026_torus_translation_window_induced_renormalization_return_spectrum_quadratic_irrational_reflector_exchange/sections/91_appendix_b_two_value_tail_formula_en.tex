\section{Appendix B: Equivalent Forms of Two-Value Degeneration and Rigorous Treatment of the Tail Formula}

\subsection*{B.1 Endpoint Coincidence and Linear Equation}

In the three-piece structure, the middle piece degenerates when its left and right endpoints coincide. By construction, the coincidence condition is
\[
b-\delta_i=K\delta_i+\delta_{i+1},
\]
hence
\[
b=(K+1)\delta_i+\delta_{i+1}.
\]

\subsection*{B.2 Representation in Terms of $\delta_{i-1}$}

Using $\delta_{i-1}=a_{i+1}\delta_i+\delta_{i+1}$ and setting $c=a_{i+1}-(K+1)$, we obtain
\[
b=\delta_{i-1}-c\delta_i,\qquad 0\le c<a_{i+1}.
\]

\subsection*{B.3 Tail Formula}

From $b=(a_{i+1}-c)\delta_i+\delta_{i+1}$ we get
\[
\rho(E)=\frac{\delta_i}{b}=\frac{1}{(a_{i+1}-c)+\delta_{i+1}/\delta_i}.
\]
On the other hand, $\delta_{i+1}/\delta_i$ is equivalent to the continued-fraction tail $[0;a_{i+2},a_{i+3},\dots]$; substituting both into the above yields
\[
\rho=[0;\ a_{i+1}-c,\ a_{i+2},\ a_{i+3},\dots]
\]
or its reversal conjugate $1-\rho$.
