\begin{abstract}
This paper proposes an auditable cut--projection--readout formal framework for unifying the generation of three-dimensional visible structures, the coherent superposition corresponding to intensity reconstruction, and the definition of temporal arrow at the level of measurement protocols. The framework strictly constrains six-dimensional hyperspace representation to geometrically closed model sets of rank $6$, and characterizes observers as operator families carrying internal phases and readout parameters; realizable sampling under finite resources is characterized through toroidal factors and rational approximation hierarchies. At the symbolic level, the circular bipartition yields Sturmian/Fibonacci-type processes, and finite-window outputs are normalized via Zeckendorf-legal language and stabilization kernel $\Fold_m$; the main text fixes bandwidth $m=6$, and objectifies indistinguishable degrees of freedom through the preimage fiber objects of $\Fold_6$, with the exponential contraction rate of posterior cylinder-set measures (SMB/KS calibration) rigorously defining the temporal arrow and conditional uncertainty of ``the future.'' Furthermore, internal gauge degrees of freedom are extended via $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ fiber without raising the hyperspace rank, and provide verifiable reference calibrations and a set of falsifiable statistical fingerprints for fixed-bandwidth recursive readout (segmented refinement).
\end{abstract}
