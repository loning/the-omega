\begin{proposition}[\texorpdfstring{$n$}{n}-th Power of Stereographic Jacobian and Linearization of Entropy Density]\label{prop:xi-stereographic-jacobian-entropy-density}
Let \(\sigma:S^{n}\setminus\{N\}\to\RR^{n}\) be the stereographic projection from the north pole \(N\), denote the Euclidean norm of \(x\in\RR^{n}\) by \(|x|\), and denote the spherical and Euclidean volume forms by \(dV_{S^{n}}\) and \(dV_{\RR^{n}}\) respectively.
Then the pullback of the spherical volume form satisfies the strict identity
\[
\boxed{\;
(\sigma^{-1})^\ast dV_{S^{n}}
=\Bigl(\frac{2}{1+|x|^{2}}\Bigr)^{n}\,dV_{\RR^{n}}\;}
\]
thus for \(n=2\) this recovers
\(
(\sigma^{-1})^\ast dA_{S^{2}}=\frac{4}{(1+|x|^{2})^{2}}\,dA_{\RR^{2}}
\)
(consistent with the quadratic-power constant in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-four-as-universal_conformal_square}).
\par
Define the logarithmic potential of volume density (entropy density)
\[
s_{n}(x):=-\log\frac{(\sigma^{-1})^\ast dV_{S^{n}}}{dV_{\RR^{n}}},
\]
then the explicit closed form holds
\[
\boxed{\;
s_{n}(x)=n\log(1+|x|^{2})-n\log 2.\;}
\]
Thus dimension \(n\) enters the entropy density in a strictly linear manner: dimension is the exponential coefficient of logarithmic volume density; while constant factors (such as \(2^{n}\)) only alter the additive zero-point, not any difference-type observables.
\end{proposition}
