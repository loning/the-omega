\section{Thermodynamic bounds: geometric Landauer, predictive gain, and a survival inequality}
\label{sec:bounds}

An AEC agent must pay for error correction; otherwise Definition~\ref{def:agent_aec} would contradict the second law.
This section summarizes the relevant lower bounds and yields a necessary survival inequality in terms of predictive information rate.

\subsection{Geometric Landauer principle: correction costs depend on architecture}
\label{sec:geometric_landauer}

Landauer's principle bounds the minimal heat generated by logically irreversible operations such as erasure \cite{Landauer1961,Bennett1973}.
In HPA--$\Omega$, the relevant operational temperature is the computational temperature $T_c$ (Section~\ref{sec:computational_temperature}).
Moreover, erasure and re-encoding occur on a constrained physical network (locality, transport, routing), so there is an additional architecture-dependent cost \cite{Ma2025HPT}:
\begin{equation}
  W_{\mathrm{erase}}\ \ge\ \kB T_c\ln 2\ +\ Z_{\mathrm{geom}}.
  \label{eq:geom_landauer}
\end{equation}
Here $Z_{\mathrm{geom}}$ is a geometric impedance term that accounts for routing overhead, localization constraints, and reconfiguration costs.
For biology, Eq.~\eqref{eq:geom_landauer} means that the energetic price of repair depends not only on bit counts but also on physical organization: transport distances, congestion, connectivity topology, and control latency all affect the minimal dissipation required for maintaining reliable structure.

\subsection{Predictive gain upper bound from information thermodynamics}
\label{sec:predictive_gain}

Measurement--feedback thermodynamics bounds the maximum work/free-energy advantage obtainable from information \cite{SagawaUeda2010,ParrondoHorowitzSagawa2015}.
At the operational level, we write the predictive free-energy gain rate as $\dot F_{\mathrm{pred}}$ and the predictive mutual information rate between internal state $M$ and future readout outcomes as $\dot I_{\mathrm{pred}}$.
Then a generic bound of the form
\begin{equation}
  \dot F_{\mathrm{pred}}\ \le\ \kB T_c\, \dot I_{\mathrm{pred}}
  \label{eq:Fpred_bound}
\end{equation}
holds when $\dot I_{\mathrm{pred}}$ is measured in nats per unit time (our convention $\log=\ln$). If $\dot I_{\mathrm{pred}}$ is measured in bits per unit time, the corresponding bound is
\begin{equation}
  \dot F_{\mathrm{pred}}\ \le\ \kB T_c\ln 2\, \dot I_{\mathrm{pred}}^{(\mathrm{bits})}.
\end{equation}
Eq.~\eqref{eq:Fpred_bound} makes ``prediction'' an accountable resource: without sufficient predictive information rate, no agent can extract a sustained free-energy advantage to fund repair.

\subsection{A necessary survival inequality and predictive efficiency}
\label{sec:survival_inequality}

Let $\dot W_{\mathrm{diss}}$ denote the dissipation rate required to counter phase friction and maintain structure (including the geometric impedance costs implicit in $Z_{\mathrm{geom}}$).
A necessary condition for sustained existence of the AEC phase is that predictive gain exceeds dissipation:
\begin{equation}
  \dot F_{\mathrm{pred}}\ >\ \dot W_{\mathrm{diss}}.
  \label{eq:survival_basic}
\end{equation}
\paragraph{Minimal dissipation imposed by phase-friction entropy flow.}
Independent of any particular biological mechanism, if phase-friction entropy is produced at rate $\dd S_{\mathrm{pf}}/\dd t$ and is exported through a channel characterized by computational temperature $T_c$, then the second law requires a minimal heat/work outflow of order $T_c\,\dd S_{\mathrm{pf}}/\dd t$.
Using Eq.~\eqref{eq:Spf_rate_t} and the definition \eqref{eq:Sigma_def}, this yields the protocol-level lower bound
\begin{equation}
  \dot W_{\mathrm{diss}} \ \ge\ T_c\,\frac{\dd S_{\mathrm{pf}}}{\dd t}
  \ =\ \kB T_c\,\Sigma(x,t).
  \label{eq:Wdiss_lower}
\end{equation}
We interpret this as a Clausius-type bound at the readout interface \cite{Callen1985,Seifert2012}.

\paragraph{Information-rate threshold (explicit form).}
Combining \eqref{eq:Fpred_bound}, \eqref{eq:survival_basic}, and \eqref{eq:Wdiss_lower} yields the predictive-information-rate threshold
\begin{equation}
  \dot I_{\mathrm{pred}}\ >\ \frac{\dot W_{\mathrm{diss}}}{\kB T_c}
  \ \ge\ \Sigma(x,t).
  \label{eq:Idot_threshold}
\end{equation}
We define the \emph{predictive efficiency}
\begin{equation}
  \eta_{\mathrm{pred}} := \frac{\dot F_{\mathrm{pred}}}{\dot W_{\mathrm{diss}}}.
  \label{eq:eta_pred}
\end{equation}
In this interface language, biological ``purpose'' becomes a computable engineering objective: maximize $\eta_{\mathrm{pred}}$ under resource constraints so that $\eta_{\mathrm{pred}}>1$ is sustainable over the relevant time scales.
