\begin{abstract}
This paper develops a unified three-layer framework. The ontic layer takes translation scans on compact Abelian groups (in particular, on tori) as the fundamental dynamics. The readout layer uses finite-resolution window kernels and conceptual observable families as the observational interface, thereby formalizing ``existence,'' ``concepts,'' and ``closed geometric representations'' as factor-system and frequency-statistics problems. The renormalization layer characterizes cross-scale stable structures via substitution systems and block operators. Within this framework we provide: (i) scale conjugacy and unified parametrization between translation modulo $L$ and translation modulo $1$; (ii) a dense/uniquely ergodic chain for irrational translations together with a window-frequency theorem; (iii) the degeneration mechanism of multidimensional joint equidistribution and explicit non-resonant step-vector constructions; (iv) Sturmian minimal-complexity readout from rotation coding and the self-similar renormalization loop at the Fibonacci fixed point; (v) harmonic-analysis representations and discrepancy-consistent metrics for conceptual observables under Pontryagin duality; (vi) cut-and-project model sets as an interface between hidden dimensions, windows, and visible structure; and (vii) an exponential-completion account of the structural appearance of the constant $e$. We finally provide falsifiable numerical/statistical test indices and clarify the correspondence between this framework and Holographic Polar Arithmetic (HPA) under the interpretation of ``unit scan--projective readout--canonical coding--three-channel constraints $(\varphi,\pi,e)$.''
\end{abstract}
