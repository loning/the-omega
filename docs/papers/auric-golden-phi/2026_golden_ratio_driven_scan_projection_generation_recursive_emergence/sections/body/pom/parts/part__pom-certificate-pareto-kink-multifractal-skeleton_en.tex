\begin{remark}[Certificate Kink vs.\ Spectral Skeleton Phase-Transition Isomorphism: Two Types of Kinks Under the Same Duality]\label{rem:pom-certificate-pareto-kink-multifractal-skeleton}
For fixed \((m,\varepsilon)\), Corollary \ref{cor:pom-prime-register-q-quota-family} yields the certificate family
\[
\gamma^{(q)}_{m}(\varepsilon)
:=\frac{1}{q-1}\left(\frac{1}{m}\log S_q(m)-\log 2+\frac{1}{m}\log(1/\varepsilon)\right),
\qquad q\in\ZZ_{\ge 2},
\]
whose lower envelope
\(
\gamma^{\mathrm{cert,fin}}_m(\varepsilon)=\inf_{q\ge 2}\gamma^{(q)}_m(\varepsilon)
\)
is a piecewise lower envelope of \(1/m\)-type hyperbolic families (Remark \ref{rem:pom-qstar-phase-diagram-breakpoints}).
At breakpoints, two-point simultaneous optimality appears (optimal \(q^\star\) jumps discretely), which in the language of budget domain \(\mathbf{Bud}\) is equivalent to: the normal form generated by the discrete lower convex closure \(\mathbb{T}\) exhibits a kink (Definition \ref{def:pom-kink-set}).
\par
On the other hand, Theorem \ref{thm:pom-ldp-uniform-and-wm-exact-spectrum} and Proposition \ref{prop:pom-phase-transition-kink-vs-linear-face} show: the pressure \(\tau\) and level-set spectrum \(f\) are Legendre dual to each other,
and geometric phase transitions can be internalized as "optimal level set non-unique" (equivalent to a kink in \(\tau\), equivalent to a linear face in \(f\)).
Thus, within the same dual coordinate system of this manuscript:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Engineering-side kink}: Jump points of optimal \(q^\star\), where "active affine certificates" undergo branch switching in the certificate lower envelope;
  \item \textbf{Geometric-side kink}: Phase-transition points of dominant level sets/phase ensembles, where "active level sets" undergo branch switching (or become simultaneous) in \(\tau(q)=\sup_\gamma(f(\gamma)+q\gamma)\).
\end{itemize}
Both are forced to synchronize by the same discrete convex duality mechanism: the kink set of the certificate budget and the skeleton phase-transition set of multifractal spectrum are isomorphic in regime,
thus "optimal certificate/scan strategy within controllable time" and "phase-transition structure at fractal boundary" are not parallel phenomena, but equivalent images of the same convex geometric object under two interfaces (budget vs.\ spectrum).
\end{remark}
