\)。
Let \(R:=\sqrt{2\log\lambda\cdot\varepsilon}\) and note the ellipsoid \(E_R=\{x:\ x^{\mathsf T}\Sigma x\le R^2\}\).
When the angular grid is thin enough (guaranteed by \(B\,\varepsilon^{d/2}\to\infty\)), the grid point count is approximated by volume ratio:
\[
N_\varepsilon(m)
\sim
\frac{\mathrm{vol}(E_R)}{\det L(m)}
=\frac{V_d R^d}{\sqrt{\det\Sigma}}\cdot\frac{B}{(2\pi)^d},
\]
Substitute \(R^d=(2\log\lambda\cdot\varepsilon)^{d/2}\) to get \eqref{eq:pom-multi-axis-near1-density}.
The last sentence is obtained by substituting \(\varepsilon\asymp B^{-2/d}\) for the main term.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Closed inference chain (resource--spectrum--pole--finite part)}
On the basis of the near-coboundary quadratic law \eqref{eq:pom-multi-axis-near-coboundary} and the space-time conversion law (proposition \ref{prop:real-input-40-space-time-conversion-law}), a closed chain of auditable inferences can be obtained:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Budget \(\Rightarrow\) spectrum:}The Minkowski budget barrier gives \(q(m)=O(B^{-2/d})\) (theorem \ref{thm:pom-minkowski-budget-barrier}) such that the worst spectrum gap satisfies \(\Delta_{\mathrm{spec}}(m)=O(B^{-2/d})\) and enforces \(\tau_{\mathrm{mix}}(m)=\Omega(B^{2/d})\) (corollary \ref{cor:pom-multi-axis-mixing-budget-barrier}).
\item \textbf{spectrum \(\Rightarrow\) pole:} Dirichlet normalization decodes the same gap as the near-\(1\) dominant pole edge law \(1-\Re s=O(B^{-2/d})\) (corollary \ref{cor:pom-multi-axis-near1-pole-barrier}).
\item \textbf{pole \(\Rightarrow\) Finite part:} Space-time conversion linearly locks the welt and finite part drift at second-order accuracy, so the inevitable welt channel must carry the same order of finite part drift magnitude (corollary \ref{cor:pom-multi-axis-pole-drift-binding}).
\item \textbf{Constant optimization and density:} When it is allowed to optimize the lattice shape within the same common volume class, the limit constant is controlled by the Hermite constant (theorem \ref{thm:pom-hermite-constant-characterization}); at the same time, the number of edge poles satisfies the volume ratio principal term of \(N_\varepsilon\sim B\varepsilon^{d/2}\) (proposition \ref{prop:pom-multi-axis-near1-density}).
\end{itemize}
