\subsubsection{Dual reduction of horizon positivity and Ramanujan spectral bound: boundary layer compression, lattice obstruction, and phase proliferation}\label{subsubsec:xi-horizon-ramanujan-feasible-reduction}
This section has already given two complementary reductions under the unified regime of "disk completion--unitary slice--horizon positivity--certificate hierarchy":
one reduces RH to the Carath\'eodory positivity of the horizon logarithmic derivative \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) and its Toeplitz--PSD hierarchy (Appendix \ref{subsubsec:app-horizon-weyl-carath-toeplitz}), the other reduces the "critical strip geometry" in the finite kernel model to the square-root spectral bound (kernel Ramanujan) and thereby obtains a strict dictionary for toy-RH (Appendix \ref{app:real-input-40-finite-rh}).
The following six points provide a summary positioning of the \emph{auditable inputs, endpoint boundary layer compression, and structural obstructions} of these two reductions; any interpretive wording is not used as a premise for derivation, and all assertions are supported by numbered conclusions.

\paragraph{(I) Horizon compression law and finite resolution blind zone: endpoint localization of radial certificates}
The Jensen defect \(D_{\zeta}(\varrho)\) (Definition \ref{def:app-jensen-defect}) of the disk completion \(F_{\zeta}\) gives a completely finite criterion for RH
\(\bigl(D_{\zeta}(\varrho)\equiv 0\bigr)\) (Corollary \ref{cor:app-rh-iff-jensen-defect-zero}), and can be further countably encoded as a countable family of constraints \(D_{\zeta}(\varrho_n)=0\) on any approaching sequence (Theorem \ref{thm:app-jensen-countable-criterion}).
When there exists an off-critical-line zero \(\rho=\beta+\mathrm{i}\gamma\) (take \(\beta<\tfrac12\)), denote \(\delta=\tfrac12-\beta>0\) and \(w_{\rho}=\mathcal{C}(\gamma+\mathrm{i}\delta)\in\mathbb{D}\), then the endpoint depth
\(
h(\rho)=1-|w_{\rho}|^{2}
\)
satisfies the closed-form compression law \eqref{eq:app-offcritical-radius-closed}, and forms an endpoint boundary layer of thickness \(\Theta(\gamma^{-2})\) when \(|\gamma|\to\infty\) (\(\delta\) bounded) (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offcritical-quadratic-radial-compression}).
Moreover, the logarithmic radial derivative identity (Proposition \ref{prop:app-jensen-defect-log-derivative}) and kink structure (Corollary \ref{cor:app-jensen-defect-kink-structure}) of the defect show that: in-disk zeros generate localizable kink thresholds and slope jump atoms on \(D_{\zeta}\).
\par
The above facts directly give a rigorous formulation of the "finite resolution blind zone": given a finite sampling radius set \(\{\varrho_j\}_{j=1}^{M}\subset(0,1)\), let \(\varrho_{\max}=\max_j\varrho_j\). If one can only verify
\(
D_{\zeta}(\varrho_j)=0\ (1\le j\le M)
\),
then this certificate only excludes in-disk zero events within \(|w|<\varrho_{\max}\); for off-line zeros satisfying \(|w_{\rho}|>\varrho_{\max}\) (i.e., \(1-|w_{\rho}|<1-\varrho_{\max}\)), finite sampling cannot give an exclusion conclusion (Corollary \ref{cor:app-jensen-visible-threshold-offcritical}).
Therefore, "horizon redshift" is not a rhetorical device, but strictly localizes the certifiability difficulty above the resolution threshold of the endpoint neighborhood.
For finer precision potential--offset logarithmic decomposition of the endpoint layer, see Corollary \ref{cor:xi-offcritical-endpoint-depth-log-decomposition}.

\paragraph{(II) Horizon positivity and Toeplitz--PSD hierarchy: auditable semi-definite equivalence of RH}
The Herglotz positivity of the horizon Weyl function \(m_{\zeta}\) is equivalent to RH (Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-weyl-herglotz}), its Cayley pushforward is the positive real part property of the horizon Carath\'eodory logarithmic derivative \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) (Corollary \ref{cor:app-horizon-carath-rh}); this positivity is further equivalent to the entire Toeplitz--PSD hierarchy \(\mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\) holding (Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-toeplitz-lmi}).
Therefore any RH failure must generate an offline-verifiable falsifier (negative eigenvalue/negative principal minor) at some finite level \(N\).
Moreover, in the sub-disk \(|w|<1/3\), \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) has an absolutely convergent Euler product patch (Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-euler-patch}), whose Taylor coefficients \(c_n\) can be audited term-by-term in the absolutely convergent domain (Corollary \ref{cor:app-horizon-prime-moment-interface}).
Thus, the difficulty is structurally isolated as a problem of analytic continuation and positivity preservation in the endpoint neighborhood, rather than an arithmetic convergence problem away from the endpoint.

\paragraph{(III) Ramanujan (square root) spectral bound and toy-RH: strict dictionary of finite kernel model}
For a finite-dimensional kernel (matrix) \(M\), let \(\lambda=\rho(M)>1\), and define the Dirichlet-ization \(\widehat{\zeta}(s)=\det(I-\lambda^{-s}M)^{-1}\).
Then its poles in the critical strip are pointwise controlled by the modulus of eigenvalues \(\mu\), and the pole real part always satisfies
\(
\Re(s)=\log|\mu|/\log\lambda
\)
(Proposition \ref{prop:finite-rh-triangle-dict}).
Therefore the Ramanujan radius bound
\(
\max_{\mu\neq\lambda}|\mu|\le\sqrt{\lambda}
\)
is equivalent to no poles in the open half-strip \(\tfrac12<\Re(s)<1\) (Corollary \ref{cor:finite-rh-halfstrip-nopoles}), and gives an RH-sharp critical-line comb structure when saturating the bound (Remark \ref{rem:finite-rh-zero-oscillation-dict}).
This dictionary of "spectral radius bound \(\Rightarrow\) critical strip geometry" completely compresses the toy-RH mechanism in the finite-dimensional model to an auditable linear algebra spectral bound.
\par
If furthermore the spectral reciprocal closure is satisfied (\(\mu\mapsto \lambda/\mu\); Proposition \ref{prop:reciprocal-spectrum-functional-equation}), then the completed functional equation elevates the above "half-strip no-poles" to a critical strip symmetric structure about \(\Re(s)=\tfrac12\);
thus the Ramanujan spectral bound can be docked isomorphically with the upper half-plane Weyl/Herglotz positivity criterion (Corollary \ref{cor:xi-slice-weyl-herglotz} and Remark \ref{rem:xi-finite-kernel-ramanujan-herglotz-interface}).

\paragraph{(IV) Vertical lattice obstruction and \texorpdfstring{$\Theta(\log T)$}{Theta(log T)} threshold: impossibility of finite-dimensional paradigm}
The pole geometry of finite-dimensional Dirichlet-ization must be "finite vertical lines + finite phase arithmetic combs", whose structural threshold is given by Remark \ref{rem:finite-pole-lattice-barrier}.
More quantitatively, if the goal is to approximate the zero count main order \(N_{\zeta}(T)\asymp T\log T\) of \(\Xi_{\zeta}\)-type, then any realization staying long-term at fixed finite bandwidth/fixed finite scale window/fixed finite-dimensional determinant regime structurally fails at the density order: its zero count is at most \(O(T)\), so it must release a \(\Theta(\log T)\)-level degree-of-freedom budget (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-zero-statistics-log-budget-lower-bound}).
Therefore approaching real RH zero geometry from the Ramanujan mechanism must go beyond the "fixed finite dimension + single scale Dirichlet-ization" paradigm, entering approximating sequences of growing dimension or infinite-dimensional Fredholm determinant regime.

\paragraph{(V) Structural origin of RH-sharpness and phase proliferation: maintaining Ramanujan while refining critical imaginary parts}
The real input \(40\) state kernel gives an RH-sharp reproducible instance: the unique bound-attaining eigenvalue besides the Perron root is \(-\sqrt{\lambda}=-\varphi\) (Remark \ref{rem:finite-rh-40-ramanujan}), whose origin can be forced by the determinant decomposition of the hidden two-dimensional golden mean factor \((-A_0)\) (Remark \ref{rem:finite-rh-40-hidden-minusA0}).
To overcome the finite-dimensional "single-frequency comb" imaginary part rigidity, while maintaining the Ramanujan radius bound, one can use cyclic phase lifting
\(
M^{\langle q\rangle}=M\otimes P_q
\)
to achieve controllable subdivision of bound-attaining phases: spectral phases rotate equally by \(q\), while spectral radius and Ramanujan bound are preserved (Proposition \ref{prop:finite-rh-phase-lift}).
This lifting is accompanied by explicit Artin factorization
\(
\det(I-zM^{\langle q\rangle})=\prod_{k=0}^{q-1}\det(I-z\omega_q^k M)
\)
(Proposition \ref{prop:finite-rh-phase-lift-artin}), making the "role channel--twisted spectral block" decomposition fully auditable at the determinant level.

\paragraph{(VI) Conclusive reduction: feasible route indicated by horizon positivity \(\times\) Ramanujan spectral bound}
The above mechanisms merge to give a logically closed reductive route:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Horizon reduction:} RH is equivalent to full-disk positivity of \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\), equivalent to the entire Toeplitz--PSD hierarchy holding; any counterexample corresponds to some finite-level falsifier (Appendix \ref{subsubsec:app-horizon-weyl-carath-toeplitz}).
  \item \textbf{Endpoint localization:} Off-critical-line offset compresses in the endpoint boundary layer by \(\Theta(\gamma^{-2})\), finite-resolution radial/angular certificates have structural thresholds, and the difficulty is strictly concentrated at the continuation and positivity preservation near the endpoint (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offcritical-quadratic-radial-compression} and Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-toeplitz-detection-threshold}).
  \item \textbf{Role of Ramanujan mechanism:} In the finite kernel paradigm, the square-root spectral bound is fully auditable and forces toy-RH; phase lifting provides a minimal channel to increase critical imaginary part structural complexity while maintaining the spectral bound (Appendix \ref{app:real-input-40-finite-rh}).
  \item \textbf{Upgrade guardrails:} If the goal upgrades to the \(T\log T\) zero statistics of \(\Xi_{\zeta}\), one must enter a regime releasing \(\Theta(\log T)\) degrees of freedom, and simultaneously inherit completion, self-duality, and horizon positivity certificate semantics in the Fredholm determinant paradigm (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-zero-statistics-log-budget-lower-bound} and Theorem \ref{thm:bridge-paradigm-nonlocal-doublelimit-fredholm}).
\end{enumerate}
In this sense, the "black hole horizon" language in this paper corresponds to a set of computable inequalities: endpoint boundary layer compression gives visibility/budget thresholds, and the Ramanujan spectral bound gives auditable constraints on critical strip geometry; the intersection point of the two is precisely the core problem localization of "near-horizon positivity continuation".
