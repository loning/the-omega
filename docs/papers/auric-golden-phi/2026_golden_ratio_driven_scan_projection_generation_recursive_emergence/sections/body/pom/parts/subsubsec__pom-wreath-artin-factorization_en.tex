\subsubsection{Artin Factorization of the Wreath Lift: Block-Diagonal Channels for Label--Spectral Auditing}\label{subsubsec:pom-wreath-artin-factorization}
This paragraph aligns the wreath exchange of the Moment--Lift (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-mom-lift-wreath-exchange}) with the Artin factorization of covering determinants (Proposition \ref{prop:kernel-artin-factorization}),
thereby converting ``labeled higher-order collision statistics'' into a block-diagonal readout of finitely many irreducible channels.

\begin{corollary}[Artin-type factorization and twisted block spectrum of the wreath lift]\label{cor:pom-wreath-artin-factorization}
Take a finite group \(G\) and an integer \(q\ge 2\), and let \(H:=G\wr S_q=G^{\times q}\rtimes S_q\) be the wreath product (Definition \ref{def:pom-wreath-product}).
Let \(A_q\) denote a fixed realization of the \(q\)-th order collision moment-kernel (one may take the symmetric power quotient kernel \(\widetilde A_q\)), and let \(\mathrm{LIFT}_H\) denote the normal covering/permutation lift protocol compatible with the regular action of \(H\).
Write \(A_q^{(H)}\) for the resulting covering operator.

Then under the Peter--Weyl decomposition of the regular representation there exists a natural isomorphism
\[
\boxed{\;
A_q^{(H)}\ \cong\ \bigoplus_{\rho\in\widehat H}
\Bigl(\mathrm{Id}_{\dim\rho}\otimes A_{q,\rho}\Bigr)\; },
\]
where \(\widehat H\) is the family of irreducible complex representations of \(H\), and \(A_{q,\rho}\) is the corresponding \(\rho\)-twisted moment operator (channel block).
Consequently, for the covering \(\zeta\)-function (\(\zeta_{A}(z)=\det(I-zA)^{-1}\); see \S\ref{subsec:zeta-syntax-zeta}) we obtain the Artin-type factorization
\[
\boxed{\;
\zeta_{q}^{(H)}(z)
:=\det(I-zA_q^{(H)})^{-1}
\;=\;
\prod_{\rho\in\widehat H}\det(I-zA_{q,\rho})^{-\dim\rho}\; }.
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By Proposition \ref{prop:kernel-artin-factorization}, for any normal covering/permutation lift by a finite group, the covering determinant admits an Artin factorization according to the irreducible decomposition of the regular representation;
applying this general result to the group \(H=G\wr S_q\) and the lifted operator \(A_q^{(H)}\) immediately yields the stated block decomposition and multiplicative factorization.
Proposition \ref{prop:pom-mom-lift-wreath-exchange} ensures that, under the moment semantics of ``unlabeled replication + permutation symmetry,'' the type-correct object for the lift axis is precisely \(G\wr S_q\), so the above decomposition provides a label-channelized fingerprint strictly consistent with the moment compilation. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Computable shadow: character channelization and \texorpdfstring{$(\mathrm{RMOML})$}{RMOML}]\label{rem:pom-wreath-artin-computable-shadow}
The irreducible channel decomposition of Corollary \ref{cor:pom-wreath-artin-factorization} requires a complete representation classification for a general wreath product \(G\wr S_q\).
In the executable regime where \(G\) is a finite abelian group and only one-dimensional character channels are tracked,
the \((\mathrm{RMOML})\) rule given by Proposition \ref{prop:pom-rmoml-sound} and Remark \ref{rem:pom-wreath-shadow-rmoml} can be viewed as a computable shadow of this Artin decomposition:
it compresses the required twisted block readouts onto an enumerable character family, thereby forming an auditable ``label--spectral'' channel.
\end{remark}
