\section{Neutrino external audit channels (0$\nu\beta\beta$, $\Sigma m_\nu$, $m_\beta$, sterile) (supplement)}
\label{app:neutrino_external_audit_channels}

This appendix records \emph{external} neutrino audit channels and explicit fail conditions
compatible with the paper's minimal-input doctrine.
The goal is not to import a neutrino mass-generation mechanism as a premise,
but to make transparent which observational channels test which parts of the interface.

\paragraph{Layer stance (minimal input).}
\MatchTag All external constraints in this appendix (0$\nu\beta\beta$, cosmological $\Sigma m_\nu$,
beta-decay $m_\beta$, sterile searches/N$_\mathrm{eff}$ bounds) are treated as \emph{Match/Audit} inputs only.
They do \emph{not} enter any CAP selection objective in this paper.
They are used to (i) report compatibility ranges and (ii) state falsifiable failure criteria.

\paragraph{Internal inputs used.}
The only paper-internal ingredients used to \emph{compute} neutrino observables are:
(i) the closed PMNS mixing parameters (Section~\ref{sec:pmns_neutrino_closure}) and
(ii) the neutrino-scale interface bookkeeping in the resolution coordinate
(Section~\ref{subsec:neutrino_mass_interface}, Table~\ref{tab:neutrino_mass_interface}).
Majorana phases are not closed by oscillation data and remain external to the minimal closure
(Remark~\ref{rem:neutrino_scale_status} and Lemma~\ref{lem:majorana_phases_cancel}).

\subsection{Observable definitions (audit targets)}
\label{subsec:neutrino_external_observables}

Let $m_i$ denote neutrino mass eigenvalues and $U$ the PMNS matrix in the PDG convention.

\paragraph{Cosmological mass sum.}
Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sigma_mnu_def}
\Sigma m_\nu := m_1+m_2+m_3.
\end{equation}
External cosmological analyses constrain $\Sigma m_\nu$ as a matching-layer bound (model-dependent).

\paragraph{Beta-decay effective mass.}
The kinematic effective electron-neutrino mass measured in endpoint beta decay is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:m_beta_def}
m_\beta^2 := \sum_{i=1}^3 |U_{ei}|^2\,m_i^2.
\end{equation}
This definition is independent of Majorana phases.

\paragraph{Neutrinoless double beta decay (Majorana effective mass).}
If lepton number is violated by a light-neutrino Majorana mass mechanism,
the standard long-range exchange contribution is controlled by the effective mass
\begin{equation}\label{eq:m_betabeta_def}
m_{\beta\beta} := \left|\sum_{i=1}^3 U_{ei}^2\,m_i\right|.
\end{equation}
In the PDG parameterization, $m_{\beta\beta}$ depends on two additional Majorana-type phases
(equivalently, the relative phases among the three complex terms in \eqref{eq:m_betabeta_def}).
These phases do not affect oscillation probabilities and therefore are not fixed by the minimal closure.

\paragraph{A deterministic phase-agnostic bound.}
Let $a_i:=|U_{ei}|^2 m_i$.
As the phases vary, the three terms in \eqref{eq:m_betabeta_def} form a triangle (or degenerate polygon) in the complex plane.
Therefore $m_{\beta\beta}$ is bounded deterministically by the polygon inequalities:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:m_betabeta_bounds}
m_{\beta\beta}^{\min}=\max\{0,\ a_1-a_2-a_3,\ a_2-a_1-a_3,\ a_3-a_1-a_2\},
\qquad
m_{\beta\beta}^{\max}=a_1+a_2+a_3.
\end{equation}
This range uses only $(m_i)$ and the mixing moduli $|U_{ei}|$ and is therefore compatible with the minimal-input stance.

\paragraph{Sterile/extra-radiation diagnostics.}
Sterile-neutrino searches and cosmological constraints on additional relativistic degrees of freedom
(often reported via $N_\mathrm{eff}$ or $\Delta N_\mathrm{eff}$) provide external audit channels for
protocol-external neutral modes such as the $\nu_R$ label used in this paper (Prediction P1; Section~\ref{subsec:p1_rh_neutrino}).
We treat these as Match/Audit constraints whose role is to delimit the allowed coupling/visibility of protocol-external modes.

\paragraph{A minimal proxy map to $\Delta N_\mathrm{eff}$.}
\MatchTag To keep inputs minimal and avoid importing a detailed cosmological production model,
we use a standard proxy relation for additional light species:
if there are $n_R$ effectively relativistic sterile modes with temperature ratio $\xi:=T_R/T_\nu$ relative to active neutrinos,
then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:delta_neff_proxy}
\Delta N_\mathrm{eff}\approx n_R\,\xi^4.
\end{equation}
In the fully thermalized proxy ($\xi=1$) one has $\Delta N_\mathrm{eff}\approx n_R$,
while in the fully decoupled proxy ($\xi\approx 0$) one has $\Delta N_\mathrm{eff}\approx 0$.
In the present paper, the $\nu_R^{(g)}$ labels appear in three generations at the level of stable-type labeling;
as an audit-facing coarse family we therefore report the discrete proxy set
\[
\Delta N_\mathrm{eff}\in\{0,1,3\},
\]
corresponding to $\{(\xi\approx 0,\ n_R\in\{1,3\}),\ (\xi=1,\ n_R=1),\ (\xi=1,\ n_R=3)\}$.
\textbf{Inversion (audit-only):}
given an external upper bound $|\Delta N_\mathrm{eff}|<B$, the proxy \eqref{eq:delta_neff_proxy} implies
\[
\xi\le \left(\frac{B}{n_R}\right)^{1/4},
\]
so even if $n_R\in\{1,3\}$ is fixed by the labeling multiplicity, the allowed visibility is constrained through $\xi$.
\textbf{Fail condition (extra radiation, proxy form):}
an external bound $|\Delta N_\mathrm{eff}|<B$ with $B\ll 1$ excludes any fully thermalized light sterile mode in this proxy family,
and enforces an effectively decoupled (or sufficiently cold) interpretation consistent with P1.

\subsection{Mechanism families and fail conditions (audit form)}
\label{subsec:neutrino_mechanism_audit_fails}

We record two minimal mechanism families as audit-facing contrasts.
They are \emph{not} selected by CAP in this paper.

\paragraph{Family D (Dirac neutrinos; lepton number conserved).}
Assume neutrinos are purely Dirac with no lepton-number-violating interactions at accessible scales.
Then the standard light-neutrino 0$\nu\beta\beta$ amplitude vanishes, hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dirac_mbb_zero}
m_{\beta\beta}=0.
\end{equation}
\textbf{Fail condition (D):}
a confirmed 0$\nu\beta\beta$ signal inconsistent with background/systematics would falsify Family D as stated.

\paragraph{Family M (Majorana neutrinos via a low-energy Weinberg operator).}
Assume lepton number is violated so that the low-energy effective description contains
a Majorana mass term (equivalently a dimension-five Weinberg operator in an EFT embedding).
Then 0$\nu\beta\beta$ is in principle allowed and is controlled (up to nuclear physics inputs) by $m_{\beta\beta}$.
Within the minimal-input stance, the paper does not fix Majorana phases; therefore the audit output is the range
\([m_{\beta\beta}^{\min},m_{\beta\beta}^{\max}]\) from \eqref{eq:m_betabeta_bounds} for each candidate spectrum.
\textbf{Fail condition (M):}
if external 0$\nu\beta\beta$ bounds, translated to an upper bound on $m_{\beta\beta}$ under stated nuclear inputs,
exclude the entire predicted range for all candidate spectra consistent with the oscillation-scale interface,
then Family M is disfavored in that stated audit model.

\paragraph{Absolute-mass scale disclaimer.}
\MatchTag Cosmological $\Sigma m_\nu$ and kinematic $m_\beta$ constraints depend on external modeling assumptions
and are treated as audit bounds on the interface bookkeeping, not as drivers of an internal selection.

\subsection{What remains unclosed (explicit boundary)}
\label{subsec:neutrino_external_audit_boundary}

This appendix does not close a neutrino mass-generation mechanism from the tick\,+\,CAP spine.
In particular:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Majorana phases} are not determined by oscillation closures and are not selected here.
\item \textbf{Absolute mass scale} is treated at the interface level (Table~\ref{tab:neutrino_mass_interface}) and audited against external bounds.
\item \textbf{Sterile dynamics} beyond the protocol-external labeling statement (P1) is not derived; external sterile searches constrain the allowed coupling/visibility of such modes.
\end{itemize}
