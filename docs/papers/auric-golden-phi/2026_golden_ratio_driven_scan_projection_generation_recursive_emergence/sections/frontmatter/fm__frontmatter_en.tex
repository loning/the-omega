\begin{abstract}
This paper investigates the stable grammar and fold normal forms induced by finite-window observations within the scan--projection readout model. Given a singly generated action \(T^t\) and a binary readout \(s_t=\ind_W(T^t x)\), we introduce the window microstate space \(\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m\) and the golden-mean-constrained stable type space \(X_m\) (forbidding adjacent \(11\)), and construct the resolution fold operator \(\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m\).

Under the Zeckendorf grammar, we prove the well-definedness, idempotence, and cross-resolution compatibility of \(\Fold_m\), and provide the finite-graph representation and computable interface for the induced sequence-level sliding block code \(\Phi_m\). We further propose Projection Ontology Mathematics (POM) as a unifying language: within the three-projection/three-fiber framework, we establish the ledger identity for relative entropy defects, construct a projection word rewriting system, and prove its termination, confluence, and decidable closure on bounded-complexity slices. For higher-order collision moments, we provide a finite-state moment-kernel compilation and give sufficient criteria for Hankel rank collapse induced by modal constraints, thereby converting the resonance mechanism into an auditable algebraic certificate. Under the square-root bound assumption of the kernel-version GRH, we lift finite-layer Chebotarev counts along the profinite axis and provide equidistribution error bounds for cylinder sets. At the end of the paper, we present a one-click reproducible workflow consolidated by scripts, together with several baseline experiments, to verify the quantitative behavior of fold histograms and spectral fingerprints.
Under the semantic convention ``address precedes concept value / null \(\mathsf{NULL}\)'', this paper further compresses the intuition ``scan depth \(\Rightarrow\) clarity'' into an auditable error profile and boundary exponential law (Theorem \ref{thm:spg-clarity-boundary-dimension}), and decomposes ``unsynchronized (statistical-type \(\mathsf{NULL}\))'' and ``address collision'' into a double-budget addressing capacity law (Theorem \ref{thm:spg-double-budget-address-capacity}); simultaneously, the online synchronization budget is incorporated into the Chebotarev capacity inequality via explicit deduction terms, thereby separating the definitional synchronization threshold from the spectral gap equalization budget (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-sync-subtracted-chebotarev-capacity}).

Moreover, this paper provides a non-abelian channelization framework for group-labeled kernels: through Peter--Weyl block diagonalization, we obtain the Artin factorization of covering determinants, and provide the Chebotarev--Plancherel energy identity and quotient monotonicity for class function calibers. Further, at the operator algebra and spectral realization level, the periodic values of Kronecker scans are rigorously identified as canonical traces of crossed product algebras, and it is proved that the pointwise stabilization of finite CMV certificates generates a unique infinite-dimensional CMV unitary spectral object; the corresponding antilinear self-dual defect can be completely characterized by Tate/Maslov indices and boundary singular residues (degenerating to a single integer index under phase closure).

``Emergence'' in this paper refers solely to the stable types and normalization rules produced by the compression of microscopic degrees of freedom through folding/normal forms, and does not serve as a premise in any proof.
\end{abstract}
