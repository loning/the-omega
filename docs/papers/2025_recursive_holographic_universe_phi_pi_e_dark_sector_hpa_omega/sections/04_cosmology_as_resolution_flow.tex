
\section{Cosmology as resolution flow: initialization, inflation, and folding capacity}
\label{sec:cosmology_resolution_flow}

This section translates the finite-resolution folding structure into cosmological language.
The mathematical core is the growth of the stable type count $|X_m|=F_{m+2}$ and the complementary growth of mean folding degeneracy $2^m/|X_m|$.

\subsection{The big bang as resolution initialization}
\label{subsec:big_bang_init}

In a readout cosmology, the big bang is interpreted not as a singular breakdown of laws, but as a \emph{resolution initialization} of the readout hardware.
The system starts with small address order $n$ and short window length $m$, and rapidly enters a regime in which nontrivial stable types exist and can be maintained under projection.

The balanced interface $m=2n$ highlights a minimal ``bootstrappable'' coupling between a spatial grid and a local readout window:
\[
|\Lambda_n|=2^{2n}=2^m=|\Omega_m|.
\]
At $(n,m)=(3,6)$, this becomes a literal $64$-point grid coupled to a $64$-state local readout alphabet, and the nontrivial projection $64\to 21$ becomes available (Lemma~\ref{lem:Xm_fib_count}).
We treat this as the smallest computable layer at which stable matter-like readout can appear.

\subsection{Inflation as exponential growth of stable capacity}
\label{subsec:inflation_capacity}

As the window length increases from $m$ to $m+\Delta m$, the stable type count grows as
\[
|X_m|=F_{m+2}\sim \frac{\varphi^{m+2}}{\sqrt{5}},
\]
so the stable sector expands asymptotically at rate $\varphi^m$.
This provides a purely combinatorial capacity-growth mechanism: without introducing an additional inflaton field, an early phase of rapid resolution growth naturally increases the number of stable types available for macroscopic structure.
If the effective window length grows approximately linearly in an early epoch, $m(t)\approx m_0+\alpha t$, then the stable capacity grows exponentially in $t$:
\[
|X_{m(t)}|=F_{m(t)+2}\asymp \varphi^{m(t)}\asymp \e^{(\alpha\log\varphi)\,t}.
\]
This provides a quantitative readout-capacity analog of an inflationary exponential stage.
For standard inflationary motivation and phenomenological benchmarks, see \cite{Guth1981}.

\subsection{Folding degeneracy growth and dark-sector capacity}
\label{subsec:degeneracy_growth}

The folding map compresses $2^m$ microstates into $F_{m+2}$ stable types.
A basic global statistic is the mean folding degeneracy
\[
d_m:=\frac{|\Omega_m|}{|X_m|}=\frac{2^m}{F_{m+2}}.
\]
Using $F_{m+2}\asymp \varphi^{m}$, we obtain
\[
d_m\asymp \left(\frac{2}{\varphi}\right)^m,
\qquad \frac{2}{\varphi}>1.
\]
Hence the \emph{average amount of hidden redundancy per stable type grows exponentially} with resolution.
\begin{proposition}[Asymptotic dominance of the hidden sector]
\label{prop:hidden_dominance}
Let $f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m)=F_{m+2}/2^m$ and $f_{\mathrm{hid}}(m)=1-f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m)$.
Then $f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m)\to 0$ and $f_{\mathrm{hid}}(m)\to 1$ as $m\to\infty$.
Moreover, the dark-to-stable ratio satisfies
\[
\frac{f_{\mathrm{hid}}(m)}{f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m)}=\frac{2^m}{F_{m+2}}-1=d_m-1\asymp \left(\frac{2}{\varphi}\right)^m.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Since $F_{m+2}\asymp \varphi^m$ and $\varphi<2$, we have $F_{m+2}/2^m\to 0$, hence $f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m)\to 0$ and $f_{\mathrm{hid}}(m)\to 1$.
The ratio identity is algebraic, and the exponential growth follows from $d_m=2^m/F_{m+2}\asymp (2/\varphi)^m$.
\end{proof}

In the minimal case, the hidden count is already nontrivial:
\[
|\Omega_6|-|X_6|=64-21=43.
\]
The explicit preimage classification of $\mathrm{Fold}_6$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:fold6_preimages}) provides a finite, computable model of how hidden degrees of freedom are distributed among stable outputs.

\subsection{A minimal discrete fit: present-day energy budget and an effective window length}
\label{subsec:energy_budget_fit}

Define the \emph{stable fraction} and the \emph{hidden fraction} at window length $m$ by
\[
f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m):=\frac{|X_m|}{|\Omega_m|}=\frac{F_{m+2}}{2^m},
\qquad
f_{\mathrm{hid}}(m):=1-f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m).
\]
\begin{lemma}[Monotonicity of the stable fraction]
\label{lem:f_stab_monotone}
For $m\ge 1$, the function $f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m)=F_{m+2}/2^m$ is strictly decreasing in $m$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Using the Fibonacci recursion $F_{m+3}=F_{m+2}+F_{m+1}$ and $F_{m+1}<F_{m+2}$ for $m\ge 1$, we have
\[
\frac{f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m+1)}{f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m)}
=\frac{F_{m+3}}{2F_{m+2}}
=\frac{F_{m+2}+F_{m+1}}{2F_{m+2}}
<1.
\]
\end{proof}
Interpreting $f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m)$ as the fraction of degrees of freedom that survive as stable visible-sector readout and $f_{\mathrm{hid}}(m)$ as the complementary dark sector (dark matter plus dark energy), we obtain a discrete, parameter-free matching condition:
\[
f_{\mathrm{stab}}(m_\ast)\approx 1-\Omega_{\mathrm{dark},0},
\]
where $\Omega_{\mathrm{dark},0}$ is the present-day dark-sector energy fraction \cite{Planck2018CosmoParams}.
By Lemma~\ref{lem:f_stab_monotone}, this discrete matching selects a unique effective window length $m_\ast$ once a target visible fraction is fixed.
Using the Planck 2018 values (radiation negligible at $z=0$), $\Omega_{\mathrm{dark},0}\approx 0.95$, hence $1-\Omega_{\mathrm{dark},0}\approx 0.05$ \cite{Planck2018CosmoParams}.
Numerically, $\Omega_{\Lambda,0}\approx 0.68$ and $\Omega_{\mathrm{m},0}\approx 0.32$ with $\Omega_{\mathrm{b},0}\approx 0.049$, so the visible fraction is indeed at the few-percent level while the dark sector dominates the budget.
The Fibonacci ratio realizes this value at a small integer window length:
\[
\frac{F_{17}}{2^{15}}=\frac{1597}{32768}\approx 0.0487.
\]
Thus the minimal discrete fit yields
\[
m_\ast \approx 15.
\]
Equivalently, the mean degeneracy at this scale is
\[
d_{m_\ast}=\frac{2^{m_\ast}}{F_{m_\ast+2}}\approx \frac{2^{15}}{F_{17}}\approx 20.5,
\]
so the implied dark-to-stable ratio is $d_{m_\ast}-1\approx 19.5$, consistent with the observed present-day ratio $(1-\Omega_{\mathrm{b},0})/\Omega_{\mathrm{b},0}$ at the few-percent level \cite{Planck2018CosmoParams}.
The role of the $m=6$ model is not to claim that cosmic accounting is literally performed at $6$ bits, but to provide the smallest layer where the folding mechanism is fully explicit and testable; the fit indicates that the present effective readout window may be at moderate resolution while remaining in the same universality class.
