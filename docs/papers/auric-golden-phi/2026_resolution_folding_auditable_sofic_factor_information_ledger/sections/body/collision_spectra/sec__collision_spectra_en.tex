\section{Collision spectra and moment-kernel interfaces}\label{sec:collision-spectra}

This section upgrades the fiber multiplicity profile $\{d_m(x)\}_{x\in X_m}$ to a spectrum of computable invariants.
The guiding input is that Zeckendorf normalization admits a deterministic finite-state implementation with fixed delay (Appendix~\ref{app:online-normalization}); therefore, \emph{fiber-collision statistics} admit finite-state counting interfaces.

\subsection{Collision moments and their meaning}\label{subsec:collision-defs}
Fix $m\ge 1$.
Recall the fiber multiplicity
\[
d_m(x):=\card{\Fold_m^{-1}(x)},\qquad x\in X_m.
\]

\begin{definition}[Collision moments]\label{def:collision-moments}
For each integer $k\ge 2$, define the $k$-th collision moment at resolution $m$ by
\[
S_k(m):=\sum_{x\in X_m} d_m(x)^k
=\card{\Bigl\{(\omega^{(1)},\dots,\omega^{(k)})\in\Omega_m^k:\ \Fold_m(\omega^{(1)})=\cdots=\Fold_m(\omega^{(k)})\Bigr\}}.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Uniform baseline and R\'enyi fingerprints]\label{rem:collision-renyi}
Under the uniform baseline $\mu=\mathrm{Unif}(\Omega_m)$, the folded type distribution is
\(
w_m(x)=d_m(x)/2^m
\).
Hence
\[
\sum_{x\in X_m} w_m(x)^k=\frac{S_k(m)}{2^{km}},
\]
so exponential rates of $(S_k(m))_{m\ge 1}$ encode a family of R\'enyi-type fingerprints of the fold fiber spectrum.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Moment kernels: finite-state counting of collisions}\label{subsec:moment-kernel}
The next theorem provides a finite-state interface for all collision moments.

\begin{theorem}[Moment-kernel representation of collision moments]\label{thm:moment-kernel-collisions}
Fix an integer $k\ge 2$.
There exist a finite nonnegative integer matrix $A_k$ and nonnegative integer vectors $u_k,v_k$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Sk-matrix-coefficient}
S_k(m)=u_k^{\mathsf T}A_k^{\,m}v_k
\end{equation}
for all sufficiently large $m$.
In particular, the limit
\[
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}\log S_k(m)=\log r_k
\]
exists, where $r_k:=\rho(A_k)$ is the Perron--Frobenius spectral radius of $A_k$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a deterministic finite-state subsequential transducer implementing $\Fold_\infty$ with fixed delay $\delta=3$ (Appendix~\ref{app:online-normalization}).
Write its (finite) internal state set as $Q$, its one-step update/output as deterministic maps
\[
\delta:Q\times\{0,1\}\to Q,\qquad \varepsilon:Q\times\{0,1\}\to\{0,1\},
\]
and its terminal output function as a bounded-length map $\omega:Q\to\{0,1\}^{\delta}$ that flushes the delay window.
For each $m$, applying $\mathcal{T}$ to an input word $\omega\in\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m$ produces a stable output word of length $m$ (obtained from the emitted stream together with the terminal output), which coincides with $\Fold_m(\omega)$ by uniqueness of the Zeckendorf normal form (Theorem~\ref{thm:rewrite-unique-normal-form}).
\par
Fix $k\ge 2$ and form the $k$-fold synchronous product transducer $\mathcal{T}^{\times k}$: its state space is $Q^k$, it reads an input $k$-tuple
\(
(d^{(1)},\dots,d^{(k)})\in\{0,1\}^k
\),
updates each coordinate state by $\delta$, and emits the $k$-tuple of output bits
\(
(\varepsilon(q^{(1)},d^{(1)}),\dots,\varepsilon(q^{(k)},d^{(k)})).
\)
Impose the \emph{collision constraint} that the $k$ outputs agree at each step (and incorporate the finite terminal flush by enlarging the state by a constant-size buffer).
This yields a finite directed graph whose vertices are the admissible augmented states and whose directed edges correspond to those input $k$-tuples that satisfy the output-equality constraint; the edge multiplicity equals the number of admissible input $k$-tuples producing that transition.
\par
Let $A_k$ be the adjacency matrix of this finite multigraph.
Choose $u_k$ to encode the unique initial augmented state (all $k$ copies at the start state and empty buffer), and choose $v_k$ to encode the terminal acceptance weights (absorbing the finite flush constraint).
Then the number of length-$m$ admissible paths equals the number of $k$-tuples of microstates in $\Omega_m^k$ whose folded outputs agree, i.e.\ $S_k(m)$, up to a boundary correction that depends only on the fixed delay/buffer size.
Absorbing this constant boundary into the choice of augmentation and the threshold yields \eqref{eq:Sk-matrix-coefficient} for all sufficiently large $m$.
\par
Finally, Perron--Frobenius theory applied to the maximal-growth irreducible component of $A_k$ implies the existence of the exponential growth rate and identifies it with $\log\rho(A_k)$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Symmetry quotient: polynomial-size compressed kernels}\label{subsec:symmetric-quotient}
The fiber-collision constraint is invariant under permutation of the $k$ inputs.
This yields a canonical symmetry quotient that reduces the kernel size from exponential in $k$ to polynomial in $k$ (for fixed base transducer).
Appendix~\ref{app:moment-kernel} records an explicit coefficient-extraction formula for the quotient transition weights.

\begin{proposition}[Symmetric compression]\label{prop:moment-kernel-symmetric-compression}
Let $Q$ be the finite state set of the delay-$3$ online transducer from Appendix~\ref{app:online-normalization}, and let $|Q|<\infty$.
For each $k\ge 2$, there exists a quotient moment kernel $\widetilde A_k$ and vectors $\widetilde u_k,\widetilde v_k$ such that
\[
S_k(m)=\widetilde u_k^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_k^{\,m}\widetilde v_k
\quad\text{for all sufficiently large $m$},
\qquad
\rho(\widetilde A_k)=\rho(A_k).
\]
Moreover, the dimension satisfies the explicit bound
\[
\dim(\widetilde A_k)\ \le\ \binom{k+|Q|-1}{|Q|-1}=O(k^{|Q|-1}).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The $k$-fold collision construction above acts on $Q^k$ (up to a constant-size augmentation for the terminal flush).
The output-equality constraint is invariant under permuting the $k$ coordinates, and the transition multiplicities depend only on the multiset of coordinate states.
Hence the adjacency operator commutes with the natural $S_k$-action on $Q^k$ by permuting coordinates.
\par
Quotient by this action.
An orbit is uniquely represented by its \emph{occupancy vector} (histogram)
\(
\nu\in\NN^{Q}
\)
with $\sum_{q\in Q}\nu(q)=k$, where $\nu(q)$ records how many coordinates are in state $q$.
The number of such histograms is $\binom{k+|Q|-1}{|Q|-1}$, giving the stated dimension bound.
Define $\widetilde A_k(\nu,\nu')$ as the total number of admissible input $k$-tuples that, from any representative with histogram $\nu$, produce the next histogram $\nu'$ while satisfying the output-equality constraint.
This is well-defined by permutation invariance and yields a nonnegative integer matrix $\widetilde A_k$.
The corresponding quotient initial/terminal vectors $\widetilde u_k,\widetilde v_k$ are defined similarly.
By construction, path counts are preserved, hence $S_k(m)$ admits the same matrix-coefficient form on the quotient, and the Perron root is unchanged on the maximal-growth component.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Zeta/primitive interface (standard tool)}\label{subsec:moment-zeta-interface}
Once a moment kernel is fixed, standard SFT technology applies to it as a \emph{counting interface}.
In particular, the Artin--Mazur zeta function of the kernel SFT satisfies
\[
\zeta_{A_k}(z)=\frac{1}{\det(I-zA_k)},
\qquad
\#\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma^n)=\mathrm{Tr}(A_k^n),
\]
and primitive-orbit counts are extracted by M\"obius/Witt inversion (cf.\ Section~\ref{subsec:prelim-zeta}).
We emphasize that this interface is used as a reproducible packaging of collision statistics, rather than as a new invariant of the sofic factor $Y_m$ itself.
