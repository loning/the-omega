
\section{A three-operator packaging and an iterability interface}
\label{sec:three_operator}

We now package the $\varphi$--$\pi$--$\e$ channels as commuting nonnegative defect operators on $\mathcal{H}_6$.
At the minimal window length $6$, this packaging is intentionally conservative: it records what is strictly proven and isolates what becomes nontrivial only beyond the finite-window grammar.

\subsection{Defect functions and diagonal operators}
Define the $\varphi$-defect (local grammar violation count)
\[
\mathcal{D}_\varphi(w):=\#\{\,i\in\{1,\dots,5\}:\ w_i=w_{i+1}=1\,\},
\qquad w\in\Omega_6.
\]
Define the cyclic $\pi$-defect (wrap-around closure defect on the admissible sector)
\[
\mathcal{D}_\pi(w):=\ind_{\{w_1=w_6=1\}},
\qquad w\in\Omega_6,
\]
and define the \emph{finite-window} $\e$-defect by
\[
\mathcal{D}_{\e}(w):=\mathcal{D}_\varphi(w).
\]
\begin{remark}[Finite-window representative versus analytic $\e$-channel]
At window length $6$, the local forbidden-event counter $\mathcal{D}_\varphi$ already captures $\varphi$-legality, hence it can be used as a conservative finite-window representative of the $\e$-channel.
The analytically meaningful $\e$-channel discussed in Section~\ref{sec:e_constraint} is encoded by zeta/transfer-operator spectra (and, in weighted extensions, by the dependence of $\rho(A_\beta)$ on a potential), and it is not naturally a diagonal defect on $\Omega_6$.
\end{remark}

Each defect defines a diagonal self-adjoint operator on $\mathcal{H}_6$ by
\[
(\widehat{\mathcal{D}}_\star \psi)(w):=\mathcal{D}_\star(w)\,\psi(w),
\qquad \star\in\{\varphi,\pi,\e\}.
\]
They commute because they are diagonal in the same basis.
They also induce quadratic forms (constraint seminorms)
\[
\norm{\psi}_\star^2:=\langle \psi,\widehat{\mathcal{D}}_\star \psi\rangle
=\sum_{w\in\Omega_6}\mathcal{D}_\star(w)\,|\psi(w)|^2.
\]

\subsection{Stable sector and the 21-dimensional theorem}
Let $X_6\subset\Omega_6$ be the admissible set (Section~\ref{sec:phi_constraint}).

\begin{theorem}[Stable sector dimension at window length $6$]
\label{thm:stable_21}
One has
\[
\ker(\widehat{\mathcal{D}}_\varphi)=\ker(\widehat{\mathcal{D}}_{\e})=\ell^2(X_6),
\qquad \dim \ell^2(X_6)=21.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Proposition~\ref{prop:e_phi_coincide}, $\mathcal{D}_\varphi(w)=0$ if and only if $w\in X_6$, and $\mathcal{D}_{\e}=\mathcal{D}_\varphi$ at this window length.
Hence the kernels equal $\ell^2(X_6)$.
The dimension statement is Corollary~\ref{cor:x6_21}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The canonical 18+3 split inside the stable sector}
Let $X_6^{\mathrm{cyc}},X_6^{\mathrm{bdry}}$ be as in Proposition~\ref{prop:boundary_3}.
Then
\[
\ell^2(X_6)=\ell^2(X_6^{\mathrm{cyc}})\ \oplus\ \ell^2(X_6^{\mathrm{bdry}}),
\qquad \dim \ell^2(X_6^{\mathrm{cyc}})=18,\quad \dim \ell^2(X_6^{\mathrm{bdry}})=3,
\]
and $\widehat{\mathcal{D}}_\pi$ vanishes on $\ell^2(X_6^{\mathrm{cyc}})$ while acting as the identity on $\ell^2(X_6^{\mathrm{bdry}})$.

\subsection{An iterability interface: the 1+21+21+21 organization}
\label{subsec:iterability_axiom}
The arithmetic identity $64=1+21+21+21$ suggests a convenient organizational template for iterating the folding architecture across scales.
On $\mathcal{H}_6$ itself, any statement of the form ``there exists an orthogonal $1\oplus 21\oplus 21\oplus 21$ decomposition'' is purely linear-algebraic and carries no channel content.
What matters is whether such a decomposition can be realized \emph{naturally} by higher-resolution constraints in which the three channels become genuinely distinct.

\begin{definition}[A natural $1\oplus 21\oplus 21\oplus 21$ interface]
\label{def:natural_decomp_interface}
Consider a hierarchy of microstate spaces $\Omega_m$ and associated Hilbert spaces $\mathcal{H}_m=\ell^2(\Omega_m)$.
We say that a $1\oplus 21\oplus 21\oplus 21$ organization is \emph{natural} if there exist, for a distinguished scale (or in an inductive limit), orthogonal projections
\[
P_0,\ P_\varphi,\ P_\pi,\ P_{\e}:\mathcal{H}\to\mathcal{H}
\]
such that:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\rank(P_0)=1$ and $P_0$ is canonical (e.g.\ the projection onto the normalized uniform vector);
\item $\rank(P_\varphi)=\rank(P_\pi)=\rank(P_{\e})=21$;
\item each projector is defined by, or functorially determined from, its channel data (defect operators, cyclic closure, or zeta/transfer-operator spectra), rather than by an arbitrary basis partition;
\item the construction is covariant under the dihedral layout symmetries (Section~\ref{sec:state_space_addressing}) and compatible with the uplift/recursion maps relating resolutions (Section~\ref{sec:resolution_recursion}).
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Status at window length $6$]
At the minimal window length, the strictly proven content is Theorem~\ref{thm:stable_21} and the $18\oplus 3$ split.
Moreover, at $m=6$ the finite-window $\e$-defect coincides with the $\varphi$-defect (Section~\ref{sec:e_constraint}).
Thus Definition~\ref{def:natural_decomp_interface} should be viewed as an \emph{iterability interface} for higher-resolution regimes (longer windows, weighted transfer operators, or infinite-state extensions) where the three channels can become independent.
\end{remark}
