\begin{corollary}[\texorpdfstring{$\log 4$}{log4} Quantum in Full-Rank Anchor Volume Entropy: Each Visible Dimension Carries a Fixed Externalization Constant]\label{cor:xi-anchor-gram-log4-quantum}
In the full-rank anchoring case \(n=\kappa\) of Proposition \ref{prop:xi-basepoint-scan-anchor-det-cauchy-vandermonde}, define the anchor volume and its logarithmic potential
\[
V_A:=\sqrt{\det G_A},\qquad S_A:=-\log\det G_A.
\]
Then from the Cauchy--Vandermonde closed form of \(\det G_A\), its logarithm decomposes as
\[
\boxed{\;
\log\det G_A
=\kappa\log 4+\sum_{k=1}^{\kappa}\log\delta_k
+2\log|\Delta(X)|+2\log|\Delta(P)|
-2\sum_{i,k}\log|x_i-p_k|\;}
\]
equivalently,
\[
\boxed{\;
\log V_A
=\kappa\log 2+\frac12\sum_{k=1}^{\kappa}\log\delta_k
+\log\frac{|\Delta(X)|\,|\Delta(P)|}{\prod_{i,k}|x_i-p_k|}\;}
\]
thus the constant factor \(4^\kappa\) manifests as a strictly additive term \(\kappa\log 2\) (equivalently \(\tfrac12\kappa\log 4\)) in logarithmic volume.
Therefore in this full-rank externalized volume, "the constant \(4\)" is quantized in a dimension-additive manner: each introduction of one visible dimension must shift \(\log\det G_A\) by \(\log 4\) (correspondingly \(\log V_A\) shifts by \(\log 2\)), while geometric details are entirely carried by the Vandermonde/distance terms.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Taking the logarithm of the full-rank closed form in Proposition \ref{prop:xi-basepoint-scan-anchor-det-cauchy-vandermonde} and rearranging yields the result immediately. QED.
\end{proof}
