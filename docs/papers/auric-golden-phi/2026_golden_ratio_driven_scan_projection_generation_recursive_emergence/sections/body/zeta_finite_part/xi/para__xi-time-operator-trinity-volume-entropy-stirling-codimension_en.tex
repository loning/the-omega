\begin{corollary}[Equation of state for volume entropy and spectral dimension: \texorpdfstring{$h_{\mathrm{vol}}=p+1$}{h_vol=p+1}]\label{cor:xi-time-volume-entropy-state-equation}
Following the notation of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-time-spectral-triple-distance-volume} and Theorem \ref{thm:xi-time-zeta-singularity-from-growth}.

\(

\),
then the volume entropy rate \(h_{\mathrm{vol}}=\kappa\) and the spectral dimension \(p\) are dual constants under the same growth scale; under time-unit normalization one may take
\[
\boxed{\;p=\kappa-1,\qquad h_{\mathrm{vol}}=p+1.\;}
\]
Therefore, in the ``time geometry'' regime caliber of this paper: the spectral dimension determines the entropy slope, the Dixmier trace determines the canonical zero point of the volume measure, and entropy-volume-dimension are sealed by the same time operator.
\end{corollary}
