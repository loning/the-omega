\begin{proposition}[Keep-Resample Channel Representation]\label{prop:pom-diagonal-rate-keep-resample-channel}
Let \(P^\star_\delta\) be the optimal coupling achieving \(R_w(\delta)\), and write it in the exponential family form of Theorem \ref{thm:pom-diagonal-rate-explicit-param-and-derivative}
\[
P^\star_\delta(x,y)=\frac{u_xu_y\bigl(1+\kappa\,\mathbf 1_{\{x=y\}}\bigr)}{Z},
\qquad
u\in\RR_{>0}^X,\ \kappa>0.
\]
Define the conditional kernel \(K_\delta(y\mid x):=P^\star_\delta(x,y)/w(x)\) and the background distribution
\[
\pi_\delta(x):=\frac{u_x}{\sum_{z\in X}u_z}.
\]
Then for each \(x\in X\) there exists a preservation rate \(p_\delta(x)\in(0,1)\) such that
\[
K_\delta(x\mid x)=p_\delta(x),\qquad
K_\delta(y\mid x,\ y\ne x)=(1-p_\delta(x))\,\frac{\pi_\delta(y)}{1-\pi_\delta(x)}.
\]
Therefore the optimal channel can be expressed as a "keep-resample" mechanism: with probability \(p_\delta(x)\) keep \(y=x\), otherwise resample from the conditionalized distribution of the background \(\pi_\delta\) on \(X\setminus\{x\}\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write \(A:=\sum_{z\in X}u_z\).
By the exponential family representation, for \(y\ne x\) we have \(P^\star_\delta(x,y)=u_xu_y/Z\), thus
\[
K_\delta(y\mid x)=\frac{u_y}{A+\kappa u_x}\qquad(y\ne x),
\]
where we use the marginal self-consistency identity \(w(x)=\sum_y P^\star_\delta(x,y)=u_x(A+\kappa u_x)/Z\).
On the other hand,
\[
p_\delta(x):=K_\delta(x\mid x)
=\frac{(1+\kappa)u_x}{A+\kappa u_x}\in(0,1),
\]
and
\[
\sum_{y\ne x}K_\delta(y\mid x)=\frac{A-u_x}{A+\kappa u_x}=1-p_\delta(x).
\]
Writing \(u_y/(A+\kappa u_x)\) as \((1-p_\delta(x))\,u_y/(A-u_x)\), and using \(\pi_\delta(y)=u_y/A\) we get
\(
u_y/(A-u_x)=\pi_\delta(y)/(1-\pi_\delta(x))
\),
yielding the stated decomposition.
\end{proof}
