\title[Finite parts of dynamical $\zeta$-functions]{Finite parts of dynamical $\zeta$-functions, cyclic lifts,\\
and finite-group extensions for subshifts of finite type}

\address{[Affiliation]}
\email{[email]}

\date{}

\begin{abstract}
For a primitive adjacency matrix $A$ with Perron root $\lambda$ we
study the finite part of the dynamical $\zeta$-function at
$z=\lambda^{-1}$, the reduced determinant
$C(A)=\det'(I-A/\lambda)^{-1}$, and their behaviour under cyclic lifts
and finite-group extensions. We prove a primitive-orbit formula for
the Abel finite-part constant and show that the cyclic-lift constants
$C(A^{\langle q\rangle})$ determine the reduced spectral polynomial.
More precisely, one root-of-unity layer of sufficiently large order
already reconstructs the non-Perron spectrum. For prime moduli we
recover the residue-class decomposition of the reduced power sums by
character inversion, obtain equivalent forms of the square-root bound
for the reduced spectrum, recover boundary multiplicities from Cesaro
averages, and associate a Dirichlet series to the discrepancy between
$\log C(A^{\langle q\rangle})$ and $\log C(A)$.

For finite-group extensions we develop a class-function logarithm and
an Adams--M\"obius primitive inversion theory. Primitive Peter--Weyl
series are written directly in terms of twisted determinants,
conjugacy-class Mertens constants are made explicit, and these class
constants reconstruct the non-trivial twisted primitive invariants by
non-abelian Fourier inversion. A quotient theorem shows that
abelianisation yields the exact one-dimensional shadow, while the
higher-dimensional channels measure the genuine non-abelian defect. An
explicit \(S_3\)-example exhibits a sign channel with vanishing
periodic traces but non-vanishing primitive bias, showing that Adams
operations, rather than bare M\"obius inversion, govern the primitive
splitting. In this way the cyclic-lift and finite-group theories
become the abelian and non-abelian parts of a single primitive-orbit
determinant calculus.
\end{abstract}
