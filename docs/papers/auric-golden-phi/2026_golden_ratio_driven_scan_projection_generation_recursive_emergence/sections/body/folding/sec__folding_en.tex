\section{Golden Branch Folding: Multiscale Projection from Microstates to Stable Types}\label{sec:folding}

This section constructs and analyzes the resolution fold operator \(\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m\), where \(\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m\) is the window microstate space of length \(m\) and \(X_m\subseteq\Omega_m\) is the stable syntax space under the golden mean constraint (forbidding adjacent \(11\)). The goal of folding is to compress the exponentially growing microstate space to an auditable stable type layer while guaranteeing cross-resolution consistency, so that subsequent statistical auditing, addressing, and arithmetic structure can all close on the same system of canonical representatives.\par
The following subsections provide, in order: the counting structure of Fibonacci/stable syntax, the Zeckendorf normal form and well-definedness of folding, cross-resolution compatibility (inverse system/inverse limit) and multiscale residual interfaces, as well as the finite graph representation and computable implementation of the sequence-level sliding block code \(\Phi_m\) induced by \(\Fold_m\).
