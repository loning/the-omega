\section{A Page-curve surrogate in the protocol language (BH5)}
\label{app:bh_page_surrogate}

\paragraph{Scope and status.}
\AuditTag This appendix records a protocol-computable surrogate for Page-curve behavior.
It is an interface/audit module: it does not claim a quantum-gravity path-integral derivation.
Its purpose is to (i) state the unitarity/recovery target in terms of an external record and
(ii) provide deterministic, reproducible diagnostics that distinguish
``blackbody-like low-order statistics'' from ``information recovery via higher-order correlations''.
The scope contract and failure-point rules are stated in Appendix~\ref{app:bh_scope_contract}.

\subsection{Surrogate observable: remaining microstate ambiguity given a record prefix}
\label{subsec:bh5_surrogate_definition}

\paragraph{Setup.}
Fix a window length $m$ and treat the microstate index stream as $N_1,\dots,N_L$ with
$N_i\in\{0,\dots,2^m-1\}$.
Let $W_i=\mathrm{Fold}_m(N_i)\in X_m$ be the coarse stable-label record.
Let $g_m(w)=|\mathrm{Fold}_m^{-1}(w)|$ denote folding degeneracy.

\paragraph{Coarse-record ambiguity.}
If the external record includes only $W_1,\dots,W_L$, then each $N_i$ is ambiguous within
its folding fiber of size $g_m(W_i)$.
The corresponding unresolved micro-ambiguity (bits) is the additive surrogate
\[
U_{\mathrm{coarse}}:=\sum_{i=1}^{L}\log_2 g_m(W_i).
\]
This quantity is protocol-computable at fixed $m$ once the record $W_i$ is given and the fold map is fixed.

\paragraph{Recovery goal.}
\InterfaceTag A recovery mechanism is any extension of the external record that drives the
remaining ambiguity $U(t)$ to (approximately) zero as the record length increases, within stated
tolerance and under stated budget constraints.

\subsection{A deterministic two-phase toy that exhibits Page-like recovery (audit)}
\label{subsec:bh5_two_phase_toy}

\paragraph{Toy schedule (audit-only).}
\AuditTag To isolate the logic of ``coarse blackbody-like epoch'' versus ``late recovery'',
we use a two-phase record:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Phase I (coarse).} Emit only stable labels $W_i$.
\item \textbf{Phase II (recovery).} Emit an auxiliary bitstream that encodes fiber coordinates for the same
microstates, making the record information-complete at fixed $m$.
\end{itemize}
This is a protocol-level surrogate and is not claimed as a literal semiclassical Hawking model.

we also record a deterministic mixed single-stream model:
payload coarse labels and recovery digits (fiber information) are interleaved by a CAP-selected finite family,
and the record is self-describing (the decoding parameters are embedded as a short stable-label header).

\subsection{PT toy certificate: coarse loss vs.\ full-record reversibility at \texorpdfstring{$m=6$}{m=6}}
\label{subsec:bh5_pt_toy_certificate}

\AuditTag The script \texttt{scripts/exp\_black\_hole\_queue\_equivalence.py} provides a minimal PT toy:
at fixed $m$, recording only $w\in X_m$ coarse-grains away fiber information, while recording a stable label
together with a fiber coordinate $(w,j)$ makes the emission record invertible (information is conserved in the
record). This is a discrete, protocol-native model of ``information is hidden in the record correlations/side
information''.
