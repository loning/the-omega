\begin{abstract}
This paper constructs a completely discrete, exhaustively verifiable minimal model of ``reversible ontology—irreversible observation,'' unifying the structural relationships among finite-resolution readout, stable visible states, invisible degrees of freedom (time fibers), and scanning dynamics. Observation is defined as a Zeckendorf window readout map $\mathrm{Fold}_m:Y_m\to X_m$, where $Y_m=\{0,1,\dots,2^m-1\}$ is the primitive readout label space and $X_m\subset\{0,1\}^m$ is the stable language forbidding adjacent $1$'s. We prove that $\card{X_m}=F_{m+2}$ and that $\mathrm{Fold}_m$ is idempotent with $\mathrm{Fix}(\mathrm{Fold}_m)=X_m$ (under appropriate word-level lifting operators). Invisible degrees of freedom are objectified by fibers $\mathcal{F}_m(x)=\mathrm{Fold}_m^{-1}(x)$, and ``reality'' is characterized via closure operator $\Phi(S)=\mathrm{Fold}_m^{-1}(\mathrm{Fold}_m(S))$ as closure fixed points. Microscopic ontological time evolution uses single-head reversible scanning updates $U$ with Feistel-type local interactions ensuring global permutation. For the minimal exhaustively computable instance $m=6$, we give the degeneracy spectrum theorem for $\mathrm{Fold}_6$ (fiber size distribution $4\times 9,3\times 4,2\times 8$) and linear-cyclic consistency splitting ($21=18\oplus 3$), and verify under single-head scanning dynamics the predictive accuracy of the ``degeneracy law'' for visible steady-state distributions.
\end{abstract}
