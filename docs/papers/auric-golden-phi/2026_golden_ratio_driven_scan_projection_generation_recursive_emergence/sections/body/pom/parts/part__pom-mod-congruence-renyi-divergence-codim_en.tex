\begin{corollary}[Modular Congruence Holography: Geometric Dimension Equals Codimension of "R\'enyi Divergence to Uniform"]\label{cor:pom-mod-congruence-renyi-divergence-codim}
Following the setting of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-congruence-q}, denote \(F:=F_{m+2}\), and let the congruence count
\[
c_m(r):=\#\{\omega\in\Omega_m:\ N(\omega)\equiv r\ (\mathrm{mod}\ F)\},
\qquad r\in\ZZ/F\ZZ.
\]
Define the congruence distribution \(p_m(r):=c_m(r)/2^m\) and the uniform distribution \(u(r)\equiv 1/F\).
For integer \(q\ge 2\), define the R\'enyi entropy and R\'enyi divergence (with natural logarithm)
\[
H_q(p):=\frac{1}{1-q}\log\sum_{r}p(r)^q,
\qquad
D_q(p\|u):=\frac{1}{q-1}\log\sum_{r}p(r)^q\,u(r)^{1-q}.
\]
Then the strict identity holds
\[
\boxed{\;
D_q(p_m\|u)=\log F-H_q(p_m).\;}
\]
Moreover, under the exponential principal-scale regime \(S_q(m)=\Theta(r_q^m)\) (hence \(h_q=q\log 2-\log r_q\)), the divergence density limit satisfies
\[
\boxed{\;
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}D_q(p_m\|u)
=\log\varphi-\frac{h_q}{q-1}
=\log\varphi\bigl(1-D_q^{\mathrm{geom}}\bigr),\;}
\]
where \(D_q^{\mathrm{geom}}=\frac{h_q}{(q-1)\log\varphi}\) is the R\'enyi dimension spectrum of this manuscript (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-renyi-dimension-spectrum}).
Thus under the Fold's modular-congruence interface, geometric dimension can be interpreted as the "R\'enyi divergence codimension to uniform": the difference between the volume term \(\log F\) and the entropy term \(H_q\) is precisely the auditable scalar of boundary deficit.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By \(u(r)\equiv 1/F\), we have
\[
\sum_r p_m(r)^q\,u(r)^{1-q}=F^{q-1}\sum_r p_m(r)^q,
\]
substituting into the \(D_q\) definition yields
\(
D_q(p_m\|u)=\log F+\frac{1}{q-1}\log\sum_r p_m(r)^q
\).
While
\(
H_q(p_m)=\frac{1}{1-q}\log\sum_r p_m(r)^q
=-\frac{1}{q-1}\log\sum_r p_m(r)^q
\),
combining immediately gives the first identity.
\par
On the other hand, by Proposition \ref{prop:pom-moment-congruence-q},
\(
\sum_r p_m(r)^q=\sum_r c_m(r)^q/2^{mq}=S_q(m)/2^{mq}
\),
hence
\[
H_q(p_m)=\frac{1}{q-1}\bigl(qm\log 2-\log S_q(m)\bigr).
\]
Dividing both sides by \(m\) and letting \(m\to\infty\), under the regime \(\tau(q)=\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}\log S_q(m)=\log r_q\), we obtain
\(
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}H_q(p_m)=\frac{1}{q-1}(q\log 2-\log r_q)=\frac{h_q}{q-1}
\).
Then using \(\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}\log F_{m+2}=\log\varphi\) yields
\(
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{1}{m}D_q(p_m\|u)=\log\varphi-\frac{h_q}{q-1}
\).
The last identity follows by substituting \(D_q^{\mathrm{geom}}=\frac{h_q}{(q-1)\log\varphi}\). QED.
\end{proof}
