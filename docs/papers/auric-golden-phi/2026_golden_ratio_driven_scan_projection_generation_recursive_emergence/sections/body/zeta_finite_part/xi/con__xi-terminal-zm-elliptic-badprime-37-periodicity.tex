\begin{conclusion}[Nonsplit multiplicative reduction at bad prime \texorpdfstring{$37$}{37} and exact periodic laws for denominators: closed forms for first-trigger exponents and periods]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-badprime-37-nonsplit-periodicity}
Consider the minimal integral model in the same \(\QQ\)-isomorphism class as Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-normalization}:
\[
E_0:\ y_0^{2}+y_0=x^{3}-x.
\]
Its Weierstrass invariant satisfies
\(
c_6=-216
\), and therefore
\[
-c_6\equiv 31\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 37),
\qquad
\Bigl(\frac{31}{37}\Bigr)=-1,
\]
so the reduction at \(p=37\) is \emph{nonsplit} multiplicative (nonsplit Kodaira type \(I_1\)).
\par
Let \(R_0=(0,0)\in E_0(\QQ)\), and let \(P_0=(2,-3)\in E_0(\QQ)\) be the counterpart, in this minimal model, of the physical base point \(P=(2,-\tfrac52)\) in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-rational-points-denominator-law}.
For any \(n\ge 1\), write the \(x\)-coordinates in lowest terms:
\[
x(nR_0)=\frac{A_n}{B_n^{2}},
\qquad
x(nP_0)=\frac{U_n}{V_n^{2}},
\qquad
B_n,V_n\in\ZZ_{>0}.
\]
Then at the prime \(37\), there is a fully exact \(37\)-adic denominator law:
\[
v_{37}(B_n)=
\begin{cases}
0,& 38\nmid n,\\
v_{37}(n)+1,& 38\mid n,
\end{cases}
\qquad
v_{37}(V_n)=
\begin{cases}
0,& 19\nmid n,\\
v_{37}(n)+1,& 19\mid n.
\end{cases}
\]
Equivalently, since \(\mathrm{den}(x(nR_0))=B_n^2\) and \(\mathrm{den}(x(nP_0))=V_n^2\),
\[
v_{37}\!\bigl(\mathrm{den}(x(nR_0))\bigr)=
\begin{cases}
0,& 38\nmid n,\\
2\bigl(v_{37}(n)+1\bigr),& 38\mid n,
\end{cases}
\qquad
v_{37}\!\bigl(\mathrm{den}(x(nP_0))\bigr)=
\begin{cases}
0,& 19\nmid n,\\
2\bigl(v_{37}(n)+1\bigr),& 19\mid n.
\end{cases}
\]
In particular, the denominator of \(R_0\) first acquires factor \(37\) at \(n=38\), while that of \(P_0\) first acquires \(37\) at \(n=19\); thereafter they occur at every multiple of \(38\) (respectively \(19\)), and upon further multiplication by powers of \(37\), the \(37\)-adic exponent accumulates exactly by the lifting-the-exponent law.
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The congruence of \(-c_6\) and the Legendre symbol give the criterion for nonsplit multiplicative reduction.
For nonsplit multiplicative reduction, the nonsingular reduction group \(E_0^{\mathrm{ns}}(\FF_{37})\) is the \(\FF_{37}\)-points of a one-dimensional nonsplit algebraic torus,
so
\(
\#E_0^{\mathrm{ns}}(\FF_{37})=37+1=38
\).
The kernel of reduction is given by the formal group \(E_0^{1}(\QQ_{37})\).
Therefore, for any \(Q\in E_0(\QQ)\),
\[
[n]Q\in E_0^{1}(\QQ_{37})
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
n\cdot \overline{Q}=O\ \text{in}\ E_0^{\mathrm{ns}}(\FF_{37}),
\]
where \(\overline{Q}\) is reduction modulo \(37\).
For \(R_0\) and \(P_0=[4]R_0\), the toric normal form of a nodal cubic (Tate/nodal parametrization) identifies
\(
E_0^{\mathrm{ns}}(\FF_{37})
\)
with
\(
\{u\in \FF_{37^2}^\times:\mathrm N_{\FF_{37^2}/\FF_{37}}(u)=1\}
\)
as a multiplicative group of order \(38\), and direct substitution shows \(\overline{R_0}\) has order \(38\), hence \(\overline{P_0}\) has order \(19\).
Thus \(nR_0\) (respectively \(nP_0\)) lies in the formal group iff \(38\mid n\) (respectively \(19\mid n\)), giving necessary and sufficient conditions for denominator appearance.
\par
Let \(m:=\mathrm{ord}(\overline{Q})\). Then for any integer \(n\ge 1\), \([mn]Q\in E_0^{1}(\QQ_{37})\), and the \(37\)-adic valuation of its local parameter is controlled by the lifting law for multiplicative formal groups;
equivalently, in Tate-curve (or torus) parameter \(u\),
\(
v_{37}(u^{mn}-1)=v_{37}(u^{m}-1)+v_{37}(n)
\)
(LTE, since \(37\nmid m\)), with first trigger \(v_{37}(u^m-1)=1\).
From the leading term \(x\sim (u-1)^{-2}\) in formal-group parameters, one gets
\(
v_{37}(B_{mn})=v_{37}(n)+1
\);
for \(m\nmid n\), \([n]Q\notin E_0^{1}(\QQ_{37})\), hence \(v_{37}(B_n)=0\).
Substituting \(Q=R_0\) (\(m=38\)) and \(Q=P_0\) (\(m=19\)) yields the two displayed formulas.

\end{proof}
