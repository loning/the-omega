\section{Six-Dimensional Superlattice Object Layer and 3-3 Decomposition}

\subsection{Six-Dimensional Lattice and Finite Information Coloring (D2.1)}

Let the native lattice be
\[
\mathcal{L}=\ZZ^{6}\subset \RR^{6}.
\]
To allow each lattice point to carry finite internal information, introduce a finite alphabet $\mathcal{A}$ and define a coloring (or labeling)
\[
\sigma:\mathcal{L}\to \mathcal{A}.
\]
This paper does not presuppose the dynamical form of $\sigma$; $\sigma$ is merely data at the object layer available to the readout operator.

\subsection{3-3 Decomposition and Projection Operator (D2.2)}

Fix an orthogonal direct sum decomposition
\[
\RR^{6}=E_{\parallel}\oplus E_{\perp},\qquad \dim E_{\parallel}=3,\ \dim E_{\perp}=3,
\]
and denote the orthogonal projections
\[
\pi_{\parallel}:\RR^{6}\to E_{\parallel},\qquad
\pi_{\perp}:\RR^{6}\to E_{\perp}.
\]
Here $E_{\parallel}$ is used as the three-dimensional geometric carrier space, and $E_{\perp}$ as the internal space. This distinction belongs to structural definition (D); its physical semantics belong to layer (I).

\subsection{Non-Overlap and General Position Condition (H2.3)}

To avoid systematic coincidence of distinct lattice points under $\pi_{\parallel}$, a general position condition is needed. This paper adopts the following replaceable assumption:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{(H2.3-a)} Window boundaries have zero measure;
  \item \textbf{(H2.3-b)} For almost every internal phase $u$, the restriction of $\pi_{\parallel}$ to the set of lattice points satisfying the window constraint is almost everywhere injective.
\end{itemize}
Related sufficient conditions and subsequent statistical properties are provided in Appendix \ref{app:general-position}.
