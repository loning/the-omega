\section{K4 spectral phase to delay audit (interface benchmark)}
\label{app:k4_spectral_phase_delay_audit}

\AuditTag This appendix records a minimal interface audit that turns a small phase sample $\phi(\omega)$ for a spectral response into a Wigner--Smith-type delay proxy.
It is designed to share the same stability-audit discipline as the scattering phase$\to$delay modules (Appendix~\ref{app:force_phase_delay_audit} and Appendix~\ref{app:k4_scattering_phase_delay_audit}).
It does not claim that atomic lines are scattering phase shifts; it only records what can be audited once a complex response phase $\phi(\omega)=\arg S(\omega)$ is supplied by a declared platform.

\subsection{Generated artifact}
\AuditTag Units: with $\omega$ in rad/s and $\phi$ in radians, the proxy $\tau=\dd\phi/\dd\omega$ has units of seconds.
\AuditTag Sign convention note: in signal-processing language the (positive) group delay of a transfer function is often defined as $\tau_g:=-\dd\phi/\dd\omega$. The present audit reports $\tau=\dd\phi/\dd\omega$ directly; therefore negative $\tau$ corresponds to positive group delay under the $\tau_g$ convention.
