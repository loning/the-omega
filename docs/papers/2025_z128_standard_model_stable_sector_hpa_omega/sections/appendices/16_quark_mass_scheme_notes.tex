\subsection{Quark reference masses: scheme dependence and confinement (matching layer)}
\label{app:quark_mass_scheme_notes}

\paragraph{Status and role.}
\MatchTag This appendix records a compact, audit-facing reason why quark reference rows are not treated on the same footing as the charged-lepton and electroweak anchor rows in Table~\ref{tab:mass_spectrum}.
Quark ``masses'' are renormalization-scheme and scale dependent parameters, and due to confinement they are not directly observable in isolation.
Accordingly, in the present paper we treat quark reference values as \emph{matching-layer} conventions used only to report an implied depth mismatch $\Delta r$ and the corresponding multiplicative factor $\mu/\mu_{\mathrm{pred}}=\varphi^{\Delta r}$ under the stated convention; see, e.g., standard PDG conventions \cite{PDGRPP2024} and textbook discussions of renormalization and QCD confinement \cite{PeskinSchroeder1995}.

\paragraph{Audit reading.}
The closed depth template $\widehat r$ is a discrete protocol output (Section~\ref{sec:mass_spectrum_closure}), while the shift
\[
\Delta r:=r(\mu)-\widehat r
\]
is interpreted as a matching-layer offset that absorbs scheme/scale dependence and threshold conventions.
This keeps the main-text anchor discussion focused on scheme-stable reference points (charged leptons and electroweak-scale anchors), while still recording quark rows as diagnostics in a reproducible format.
