\section{Induced Rotation Numbers and Möbius Updates on Two-Value Stable Windows}

Under the two-value degeneration condition of Theorem \ref{thm:two-value-criterion}, we give explicit formulas for the induced rotation number and fractional linear representation, and construct the window-induction renormalization operator.

\subsection{Induced Step Size and Rotation Number Read-Off}

Fix a two-value stable window $E=(0,b)$. Take indices $i$ and $c$ satisfying Theorem \ref{thm:two-value-criterion}. Define
\[
\rho(E)=\frac{\delta_i}{b}.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Induced Step Size is $\delta_i$]\label{prop:induced-step}
In the two-value degenerate case, the induced map $T_{\theta,E}$ on a certain return level is equivalent to a translation by $\pm\delta_i$. After linear rescaling of $E$ to length 1, it is conjugate to an irrational rotation with rotation number $\rho(E)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On the piece where return time is $q_i$,
\[
T_\theta^{q_i}(x)=x+q_i\theta \equiv x+(q_i\theta-p_i)\pmod 1,
\]
and $q_i\theta-p_i=(-1)^i\delta_i$. Under the coordinate rescaling $x\mapsto x/b$, the displacement ratio is $\delta_i/b$. Direction differences correspond to reversal conjugacy, so the conjugacy class is determined by $\rho(E)$. Proven.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Fractional Linear Expression}

Let
\[
\Delta_i(\theta)=q_i\theta-p_i,\qquad \Delta_{i-1}(\theta)=q_{i-1}\theta-p_{i-1}.
\]
In the two-value degenerate case, under consistent sign conventions we have
\[
\delta_i=\abs{\Delta_i(\theta)},\qquad \delta_{i-1}=\abs{\Delta_{i-1}(\theta)}.
\]
Moreover, $b=\delta_{i-1}-c\delta_i$ can be written under alternating signs as
\[
b=\abs{\Delta_{i-1}(\theta)+c\Delta_i(\theta)}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\rho(E)=\abs{\frac{\Delta_i(\theta)}{\Delta_{i-1}(\theta)+c\Delta_i(\theta)}}
=\abs{\frac{q_i\theta-p_i}{(c q_i+q_{i-1})\theta-(c p_i+p_{i-1})}}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Integer Möbius Form of Induced Rotation Number]\label{thm:mobius}
There exists $M_{i,c}\in \mathrm{GL}_2(\ZZ)$ such that
\[
\rho(E)=\left|\frac{a\theta+b}{c'\theta+d}\right|,
\qquad
M_{i,c}=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c'&d\end{pmatrix},
\]
with explicit representation
\[
M_{i,c}=
\begin{pmatrix}
q_i & -p_i\\
c q_i+q_{i-1} & -(c p_i+p_{i-1})
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The coefficients are read directly from the above formula. The absolute value eliminates endpoint conventions and alternating signs, without affecting the determination of the conjugacy class. Proven.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Continued-Fraction Tail Updates and Window Renormalization Operator}

Let $\theta=[0;a_1,a_2,\dots]$. Two-value stable windows exhibit a ``tail update'' phenomenon: under suitable conventions, the continued-fraction expansion of the induced rotation number can be built from the tail of $\theta$ plus a finite numerical correction.

\begin{theorem}[Tail Read-Off Formula]\label{thm:tail}
If $E=(0,b)$ satisfies two-value degeneration with $b=\delta_{i-1}-c\delta_i$ ($0\le c<a_{i+1}$), then there exists a choice of direction $\rho\in\{\rho(E),1-\rho(E)\}$ such that
\[
\rho=[0;\ a_{i+1}-c,\ a_{i+2},\ a_{i+3},\dots].
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof Outline]
From $\delta_{i-1}=a_{i+1}\delta_i+\delta_{i+1}$ we obtain
\[
b=(a_{i+1}-c)\delta_i+\delta_{i+1},
\]
hence
\[
\rho(E)=\frac{\delta_i}{(a_{i+1}-c)\delta_i+\delta_{i+1}}
=\frac{1}{(a_{i+1}-c)+\delta_{i+1}/\delta_i}.
\]
On the other hand, $\delta_{i+1}/\delta_i$ is equivalent to the continued-fraction tail $[0;a_{i+2},a_{i+3},\dots]$; the direction choice gives the binary choice between $\rho$ and $1-\rho$. Proven.
\end{proof}

We define the renormalization operator on two-value stable window families: on parameters $\theta$ and window data $(i,c)$, set
\[
\mathcal{R}(\theta;i,c)=[0;\ a_{i+1}-c,\ a_{i+2},a_{i+3},\dots],
\]
thus describing the dynamics of ``induction--rescaling'' at the parameter level.
