\paragraph{BH4 summary (record vs.\ hidden fiber information).} \AuditTag At fixed $m$, recording only the stable label $w\in X_m$ exposes at most $\log_2|X_m|$ bits per tick, while the microstate index $N\in\{0,\dots,2^m-1\}$ carries $m$ bits per tick. The fiber entropy $H(N|W)=\mathbb{E}_{\mu_m}[\log_2 g_m(W)]$ (with $\mu_m(w)=g_m(w)/2^m$) quantifies how much micro-information remains hidden inside folding fibers under coarse stable-label readout. Any unitarity/recovery claim stated purely at the coarse-record level must therefore specify how this hidden fiber information is encoded into externally accessible higher-order record correlations or auxiliary record components.
