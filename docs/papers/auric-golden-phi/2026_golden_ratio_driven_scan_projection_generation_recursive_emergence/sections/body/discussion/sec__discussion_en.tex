\begin{conjecture}[Prefix rigidity of zeros: external determinism of dyadic prefix counts]\label{conj:discussion-zeta-prefix-rigidity}
Model the high zeros in locally normalized scale as a stationary point process, and introduce the following external assumption: this process converges in distribution to \(\mathrm{Sine}_{2}\) (or more generally \(\mathrm{Sine}_{\beta}\)), and the equivalent correlation function/spacing convergence formulation holds (see \cite{AriasDeReynaRodgers2024ConvergencePointsZetaZeros} for the formalization given under ``equivalent formulations of the GUE hypothesis'').
\par
Under this assumption, it is conjectured that the following ``prefix rigidity'' strengthening holds: for any fixed window \(B=[-W,W]\) (\(W>0\)) and any resolution level \(m\ge 1\), let
\[
B=\bigsqcup_{j=0}^{2^{m}-1} I_{m,j},
\qquad
I_{m,j}:=\Bigl[-W+\frac{2Wj}{2^{m}},\ -W+\frac{2W(j+1)}{2^{m}}\Bigr),
\]
and denote the point process configuration as \(\mathcal X\subset\RR\) and the local counts as \(N_{m,j}:=\#(\mathcal X\cap I_{m,j})\).
Then there exists a family of measurable functions \(\{\mathcal F_m\}_{m\ge 1}\) such that almost surely
\[
\bigl(N_{m,0},\dots,N_{m,2^{m}-1}\bigr)
=\mathcal F_m\!\bigl(\mathcal X\cap B^{\mathrm c}\bigr).
\]
That is: the dyadic prefix count vector within the window (equivalently: finite-order Haar wavelet prefix coefficients) is determined by the configuration outside the window.
\par
\textbf{Falsifiable interface:} If there exists some \(m\) and some component \(j\) such that the conditional variance
\(
\mathrm{Var}(N_{m,j}\mid \mathcal X\cap B^{\mathrm c})>0
\)
holds on a positive probability event, or equivalently there exist two configurations with the same exterior that have different interior count vectors on a positive probability event, then this conjecture fails.
This refutation caliber translates ``whether addressing information can be carried by a single point'' into a hard criterion of measurability/conditional variance: once established, the above rigidity will systematically rule out the naive picture of ``single-point addressing,'' and instead interpret visible prefix information as a product of distributed external constraints.
\end{conjecture}

\subsection{Explanatory Positioning of Interface Semantics: Not a Proof Premise}\label{subsec:discussion-semantic-positioning}
The following statements serve only for positioning interface semantics and do not participate in the proof chain: in readout semantics, a ``number'' can be understood as the equivalence class of an iterated object and its stable encoding; ``addition/multiplication'' correspond respectively to the one-layer/two-layer readout of iterated composition in the encoding space. Their rigorous content is carried by the isomorphisms and computable realizations given in Section \ref{sec:emergent-arithmetic}, not by the explanatory statements themselves.
