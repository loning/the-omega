\begin{conclusion}[Affine cocycle of weight under doubling pullback: \texorpdfstring{$[2]^*y$}{[2]*y} is linear in \(y\) over \texorpdfstring{$\QQ(y)(\lambda)$}{Q(y)(lambda)}]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-y-doubling-affine-cocycle}
Using the weight function \(y=\lambda^{2}+Y-\tfrac12\) from Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-quadratic-discriminant}, and the doubling Latt\`es self-map from Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-lattes-4division-certificate},
\[
\Phi(\lambda)=\frac{\lambda^{4}+2\lambda^{2}-2\lambda+1}{4\lambda^{3}-4\lambda+1}.
\]
define
\[
D(\lambda):=4\lambda^{3}-4\lambda+1,\qquad
C_{\Phi}(\lambda):=2\lambda^{6}-10\lambda^{4}+10\lambda^{3}-10\lambda^{2}+2\lambda+1.
\]
Then in the function field \(\QQ(\mathcal{E})=\QQ(y)(\lambda)\), the doubling pullback satisfies the exact identity
\[
\boxed{\;
y\circ[2]=\frac{C_{\Phi}(\lambda)\,y+B(\lambda)}{D(\lambda)^{2}}\;},
\qquad
B(\lambda):=-\lambda^{8}+7\lambda^{6}-14\lambda^{5}+27\lambda^{4}-9\lambda^{3}-6\lambda^{2}+\lambda+1.
\]
Moreover, using \(\Phi'(\lambda)=2C_{\Phi}(\lambda)/D(\lambda)^{2}\) (the simplified derivative numerator from Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-lattes-4division-certificate}), this is equivalently
\[
\boxed{\;
y\circ[2]=\frac{\Phi'(\lambda)}{2}\,y+\frac{B(\lambda)}{D(\lambda)^{2}}\; }.
\]
Hence the weight variable exhibits an affine cocycle structure under doubling pullback, of the form "derivative multiplier + translation term."
In particular, on the critical layer \(C_{\Phi}(\lambda)=0\), the linear coefficient of \([2]^*y\) in the basis \(\{1,y\}\) vanishes, so \([2]^*y\in\QQ(\lambda)\), and the quadratic branch dependence of the weight undergoes a computable collapse degeneration on this layer.
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
From the doubling formula, write the closed rational expression for \([2](\lambda,Y)=(\Phi(\lambda),Y_2)\), and from
\(
y([2]P)=\Phi(\lambda)^{2}+Y_2-\tfrac12
\)
obtain an explicit expression for \(y\circ[2]\in\QQ(\lambda,Y)\).
Substitute \(Y=y-\lambda^{2}+\tfrac12\), then reduce in \(y\) under the quartic certificate \(\Pi(\lambda,y)=0\) from Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-quadratic-discriminant}; this uniquely normalizes the expression to a \(\QQ(\lambda)\)-linear form \(A(\lambda)\,y+K(\lambda)\).
Termwise simplification yields \(A(\lambda)=C_{\Phi}(\lambda)/D(\lambda)^{2}\) and \(K(\lambda)=B(\lambda)/D(\lambda)^{2}\).
The expression for \(\Phi'\) follows from differentiating \(\Phi=N/D\) and simplifying \(N'D-ND'=2C_{\Phi}\).
All identities above can be verified term-by-term in \(\QQ(\lambda)[Y]\) and \(\QQ(y)[\lambda]\).
Reproducibility audit script: \texttt{scripts/exp\_fold\_zm\_elliptic\_lattes\_rational\_points\_audit.py} (it verifies termwise on the physical principal branch \(y=\lambda^{2}-Y-\tfrac12\); then, using commutation between elliptic inversion \(\iota:(\lambda,Y)\mapsto(\lambda,-Y)\) and the doubling map, transfers the identity to the conjugate weight branch used in this conclusion). This completes the proof.
\end{proof}
