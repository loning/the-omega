\subsection{EFT remainder bounds under EG power counting (strong-closure interface)}
\label{app:strong_eft_remainder_bounds}

\AuditTag This appendix upgrades the truncation remainder term in the EFT error budget (Appendix~\ref{app:eft_error_bounds}) from a registry placeholder into a theorem-facing bound template using EG scaling-degree/power-counting control (Appendix~\ref{app:eg_causal_perturbation_framework}).
The objective is not to claim a universal nonperturbative remainder theorem, but to give an auditable bound form under explicit hypotheses.

\subsubsection{Observable class and truncation order}
\paragraph{Observable registry.}
Fix a finite observable registry $\mathfrak{Obs}_{\le N}$ as in Appendix~\ref{app:eft_error_bounds}, with observables built from smeared local fields/composites in the admissible EG control class.

\paragraph{Truncation.}
Let $D_{\max}$ denote the EFT operator-dimension truncation and $L_{\max}$ a loop-order truncation (if used).
Write $\langle \mathcal{O}\rangle_{\le N}$ for the EFT prediction truncated consistently with $(D_{\max},L_{\max})$.

\subsubsection{Power counting hypothesis and remainder envelope}
\begin{assumption}[Power-counting remainder envelope]
\label{ass:power_counting_remainder_envelope}
There exist constants $C_{\mathcal{O}}$, an effective expansion parameter $\lambda$ (dimensionless on the stated window), and an effective gap/scale ratio $\rho\in(0,1)$ such that the omitted contributions beyond the declared truncation satisfy
\[
\abs{\langle \mathcal{O}\rangle-\langle \mathcal{O}\rangle_{\le N}}
\le
C_{\mathcal{O}}\cdot \rho^{N+1},
\qquad \forall\,\mathcal{O}\in\mathfrak{Obs}_{\le N}.
\]
\end{assumption}

\AuditTag Assumption~\ref{ass:power_counting_remainder_envelope} is the explicit strong-closure hypothesis that replaces informal ``higher-order terms are small'' statements.
It is to be audited by (i) the EG scaling-degree/power counting, (ii) the declared scale window, and (iii) sensitivity sweeps over the finite scheme/threshold family.

\subsubsection{Theorem template: integrating remainder bounds into $\epsilon_N(m,n)$}
\begin{theorem}[Remainder term as a theorem-facing budget component (template)]
\label{thm:remainder_budget_template}
Under Assumption~\ref{ass:power_counting_remainder_envelope}, the truncation error component $E_{\mathrm{trunc}}$ in the decomposition of Appendix~\ref{app:eft_error_bounds} can be chosen as
\[
E_{\mathrm{trunc}}(N)\ :=\ \sup_{\mathcal{O}\in\mathfrak{Obs}_{\le N}} C_{\mathcal{O}}\rho^{N+1},
\]
and therefore contributes a quantitatively controlled term to the total bound $\epsilon_N(m,n)$.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
\AuditTag Theorem~\ref{thm:remainder_budget_template} is a theorem \emph{conditional} on the explicit remainder envelope.
Discharging this envelope in 4D interacting theories without additional assumptions is typically beyond current constructive control; when needed, it should be recorded as a conjecture/condition pack rather than silently assumed.
