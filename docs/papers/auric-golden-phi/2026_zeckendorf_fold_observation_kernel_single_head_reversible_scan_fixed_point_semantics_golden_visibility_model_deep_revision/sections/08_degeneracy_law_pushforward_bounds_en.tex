\section{Degeneracy Law: Pushforward Measure and Error Bounds}

\subsection{Degeneracy Prediction}

Let $\Pi=\mathrm{Fold}_6$. For a head register distribution $\pi$ (defined on $Y_6$), the visible distribution is $\nu=\Pi_*(\pi)$. Let $u$ be the uniform distribution on $Y_6$; then the degeneracy prediction distribution is
\[
q=\Pi_*(u),\qquad q(w)=\frac{\card{\mathcal{F}_6(w)}}{64}.
\]
By Theorem \ref{thm:deg-spectrum-6}, $q$ has three probability value classes: $4/64,3/64,2/64$. The boundary artifact set $B$ satisfies
\[
q(B)=\frac{6}{64}=0.09375.
\]

\subsection{Pushforward Non-Increase and Error Control}

For deterministic map pushforward operator $\Pi_*$, total variation does not increase:
\[
\norm{\Pi_*(\pi)-\Pi_*(u)}_{\TV}\le \norm{\pi-u}_{\TV}.
\]
Therefore, the degeneracy law deviation is bounded above by the microscopic marginal distribution's deviation from uniform.
