\section{Calibration of the Fibonacci resolution map}
\label{app:resolution_map_calibration}

This appendix records a data-facing calibration of the Fibonacci resolution--energy map in Axiom~\ref{ax:fib_resolution_map}. We fix the reference scale to the electron mass,
\begin{equation}
\mu_0=m_e,
\end{equation}
and define
\begin{equation}
r(\mu):=\frac{\log(\mu/m_e)}{\log\varphi}.
\end{equation}
With $r$ identified as Zeckendorf depth (maximal Fibonacci index), integer shifts $r\mapsto r+1$ correspond to multiplicative rescaling by $\varphi$.

Table~\ref{tab:resolution_map_calibration} places the muon and tau thresholds near $r\approx 11$ and $r\approx 17$, and the electroweak reference scale near $r\approx 25$. This anchors $r$ as a discrete depth coordinate with finite matching corrections at thresholds, matching leading-log running combined with decoupling/matching in effective field theory \cite{AppelquistCarazzone1975,PeskinSchroeder1995,PDGRPP2024}.

\begin{proposition}[Near-integer depth rigidity under a uniform null]\label{prop:resolution_map_uniform_null}
Fix reference scales $\{\mu_i\}_{i=1}^N$ and define $\delta_i:=r(\mu_i)-\mathrm{round}(r(\mu_i))\in[-1/2,1/2]$. If the fractional parts of $\{r(\mu_i)\}$ are independent and uniform on $[0,1]$, then
\begin{equation}
\mathbb{P}\!\left(\max_{1\le i\le N}|\delta_i|\le \delta_0\right)=(2\delta_0)^N.
\end{equation}
For $\{\mu_i\}=\{m_\mu,m_\tau,m_W,m_Z\}$ one has $\delta_0=0.134$ from Table~\ref{tab:resolution_map_calibration} and $N=4$, hence $(2\delta_0)^N\approx 5.2\times 10^{-3}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Under the null, each $\delta_i$ is uniform on $[-1/2,1/2]$, so $\mathbb{P}(|\delta_i|\le \delta_0)=2\delta_0$. Independence gives the product.
\end{proof}
