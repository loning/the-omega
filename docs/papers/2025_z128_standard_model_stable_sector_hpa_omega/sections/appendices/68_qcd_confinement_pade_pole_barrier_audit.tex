\section{Padé pole-barrier audit for the confinement proxy (HPA viewpoint; audit)}
\label{app:qcd_confinement_pade_pole_barrier_audit}

This appendix records an \AuditTag{} analytic-continuation audit in the spirit of the Abel-first / pole-barrier discipline used elsewhere in this paper.
It is intended as a computationally transparent consistency check and does not constitute a proof of confinement or of a Yang--Mills mass gap.

\paragraph{Input series.}
We extract the finite loop-size Wilson-loop style magnitudes $|W_k|$ from Table~\ref{tab:holonomy_wilson_loop} (Appendix~\ref{app:continuum_yang_mills_from_holonomy}).
We form a sparse power series
\[
F(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{36} c_n z^n,
\qquad
c_0=1,\ \ c_{k^2}=|W_k|\ (k\in\{1,\dots,6\}),
\]
with all other coefficients set to $0$.

\paragraph{Padé/pole-barrier heuristic.}
\AuditTag We compute a bounded family of Padé approximants $[L/M]$ to $F(z)$ and audit whether the resulting denominator polynomial $Q_{L,M}(z)$ has numerical roots in the open unit disk $|z|<1$.
Such roots correspond to interior poles of the rational approximant.
Because Padé approximants can introduce spurious poles/zeros for short or sparse series, this check is recorded only as an audit-facing diagnostic.
