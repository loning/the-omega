\section{Conclusion: The Geometric Renaissance}

We have presented \textbf{Omega Dynamics}, a new framework for quantum gravity rooted in Holographic Polar Arithmetic. In this framework, the classical $r=0$ curvature endpoint is not taken as a physical spacetime point: the Omega continuation rule replaces would-be radial termination by an inversion channel built on the isotropic Einstein--Rosen throat template.

Our key findings are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Gravity as Phase Pressure:} In the continuum limit, the phase potential $\Phi$ and its associated pressure field reproduce the Newtonian sector and close to the Schwarzschild exterior under standard uniqueness assumptions.
    \item \textbf{Singular Endpoints are Avoided by Inversion:} In isotropic holographic radius $\rho$, the Schwarzschild exterior exhibits an exact inversion symmetry $\rho\mapsto\rho_h^2/\rho$ and a minimal-surface throat at $\rho=\rho_h$. Omega Dynamics promotes this inversion to a continuation rule, replacing coordinate endpoints by a dual boundary channel.
    \item \textbf{Information is Conserved (Model Level):} Under the modular-scan and readout assumptions, evaporation is modeled as a unitary re-encoding of infalling data into phase correlations of the outgoing channel, consistent with Page-type entanglement turnover.
\end{enumerate}

These results give a concrete mathematical template (isotropic throat + inversion continuation) for replacing endpoint pathologies by a holographic continuation channel in Omega Dynamics. A next step is to extend the continuation rule from the time-symmetric slice to a fully causal spacetime description and to connect the symbolic readout model to quantitative semiclassical observables.

\subsection*{Limitations and Open Problems}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Microscopic origin of $\sigma(\mathbf{x})$.} In this paper the mismatch density sourcing $\Phi$ is introduced as a continuum coarse-graining assumption (Assumption~\ref{assump:mismatch_potential}). A derivation from an explicit microscopic readout model is left open.
    \item \textbf{Causal (4D) completion of inversion.} The inversion continuation axiom is stated on the scan-time slicing and the isotropic throat template (Axiom~\ref{ax:omega_inversion}). Constructing a fully causal Lorentzian spacetime realization is future work.
    \item \textbf{Semiclassical matching.} The radiation/readout model is presented as a minimal deterministic coding mechanism. Matching detailed spectra and backreaction to semiclassical Hawking calculations remains to be developed.
\end{enumerate}
