\paragraph{Audit summary (fusion: force propagation, scattering delay, and BH-like trapping).} \AuditTag We simulate particles on a periodic $64\times64$ screen under a scalar protocol field $\chi(x,t)$ with $\Phi=-\gamma c^2(\chi-\chi_0)$ and force $F=-\nabla\Phi=\gamma c^2\nabla\chi$. The field propagates via a damped telegraph/Klein--Gordon form $\chi_{tt}+\alpha\chi_t=c_f^2\Delta\chi-\omega^2(\chi-\chi_{\mathrm{eq}}(t))$, with a smooth equilibrium-center shift (new orbit center) and a persistent high-$\chi$ bump treated as a BH-like high-energy structure. A budget-triggered horizon proxy selects a top-$k$ region whose capacity satisfies $m|\mathcal R_\star|\ge cI_{\mathrm{obs}}$; particles inside are treated as trapped. Scattering delay is reported by a one-channel Breit--Wigner benchmark with an $\omega_0$ shift proportional to the local $\chi$ (operational delay channel). A deterministic leakage/evaporation toy reduces trapped kinetic energy and accumulates emitted energy $E_\mathrm{emit}(t)$ while injecting a small radiative pulse into the field. The table reports the energy ledger $E_\mathrm{tot}=E_\mathrm{part}+E_\mathrm{field}+E_\mathrm{emit}$, horizon occupancy fraction, mean delays (free vs trapped), and a relaxation proxy (radius stats) as the system converges to a new periodic orbit. Late-time period estimate: $T_{\mathrm{est}}=62.120$.
