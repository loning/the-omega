\begin{abstract}
We use the Cayley transform to identify the pullback of normalized Haar
measure on the circle with the Cauchy law on~$\RR$, and we show that
this property rigidifies the Cayley chart among normalized
orientation-preserving $C^{1}$ homeomorphisms.  On the algebraic side,
for a finitely generated abelian group~$G$ we define the circle
dimension by
\[
\cdim(G):=\dim\bigl((\widehat G)^0\bigr),
\]
the torus dimension of the identity component of its Pontryagin dual.
We prove that this invariant is characterized by natural axioms, is
additive on short exact sequences, obeys tensor and $\operatorname{Hom}$
product laws, vanishes on $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}$, and is recovered
from the phase-sampling counts $N\mapsto |\operatorname{Hom}(G,\mu_N)|$,
which in fact determine~$G$ up to isomorphism.  The same Cayley chart
also gives an operational interpretation of the half-circle dimension of
$\NN$ through dyadic prefix readout, with maximal injective scale of
order~$2^{b/2}$ at depth~$b$.

On the analytic side, the logarithmic Jacobian yields an entropy
identity and places the problem inside the Poisson semigroup on~$\RR$.
The functional-analytic core is a Cayley--Chebyshev mode calculus for
the comparison kernel
\[
R_\varepsilon(y)=\frac{1+y^{2}}{1+(y-\varepsilon)^{2}},
\]
which yields a uniform mode expansion, finite Fourier formulas for all
mode functions, and a parity principle forcing the large-time relative
entropy expansion to contain only even powers of~$t^{-1}$.  Using this
calculus we obtain a sharp eighth-order asymptotic formula for the
relative entropy between a Poisson average and the translated Poisson
kernel with matching mean.  The coefficients of order $t^{-6}$ and
$t^{-8}$ form the first two levels of a rigidity ladder, whose unique
zero-defect model is the symmetric two-point law.  The same mode
calculus has a canonical Hilbert completion: the secant family generated
by the comparison kernel has Gram kernel
$K(a,b)=2/(4+(a-b)^{2})$, and this reproducing-kernel space is
identified with the Poisson image space
$\sqrt{\pi}\,e^{-|D|}L^{2}(\RR)$ and obtain a concrete Fourier
realization of the associated reproducing-kernel Hilbert space.  We also
prove that the central Poisson trace together with its central
derivative determine the centred law through a Laplace transform of the
centred characteristic function, and with the mean they determine the
original measure.
\end{abstract}
