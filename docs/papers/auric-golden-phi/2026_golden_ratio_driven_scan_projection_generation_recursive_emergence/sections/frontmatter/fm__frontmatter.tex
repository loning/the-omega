\begin{abstract}
This paper studies stable syntax and folded normal forms induced by finite-window observations in a scan-projection readout model. Given a single-generator action \(T^t\) and binary readout \(s_t=\ind_W(T^t x)\), we introduce the window microstate space \(\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m\) and the golden-mean constrained stable-type space \(X_m\) (forbidding adjacent \(11\)), and construct the resolution-folding operator \(\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m\).

Under Zeckendorf syntax, we prove well-definedness, idempotence, and cross-resolution compatibility of \(\Fold_m\), and provide a finite-graph representation and a computable interface for the induced sliding-block code \(\Phi_m\). We then propose Projection Ontic Mathematics (POM) as a unifying language: within a three-projection/three-fiber framework, we establish a ledger identity for relative-entropy defects, construct projection-word rewrite systems, and prove termination, confluence, and decidable closure on bounded-complexity slices. For higher-order collision matrices, we provide finite-state moment-kernel compilation and a sufficient-and-necessary criterion under which modal constraints force Hankel rank collapse, thereby converting resonance mechanisms into auditable algebraic certificates. Under a square-root bound assumption in a kernelized GRH regime, we lift finite-layer Chebotarev counting along the profinite axis and derive equidistribution error bounds on cylinder sets. The paper also provides a script-solidified one-command reproducibility pipeline and baseline experiments for quantitatively validating folding histograms and spectral fingerprints.

Under the semantic convention ``address precedes concept value / null value \(\mathsf{NULL}\),''
we further compress the intuition ``deeper scans imply higher clarity'' into auditable error profiles and boundary exponent laws (Theorem \ref{thm:spg-clarity-boundary-dimension}), and decompose ``desynchronization (statistical \(\mathsf{NULL}\))'' and ``address collision'' into a dual-budget addressing-capacity law (Theorem \ref{thm:spg-double-budget-address-capacity}). At the same time, we absorb online synchronization budgets into explicit subtraction terms inside a Chebotarev-type capacity inequality, thereby separating definitional synchronization thresholds from spectral-gap homogenization budgets (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-sync-subtracted-chebotarev-capacity}).

In addition, we provide a non-abelian channelization framework for group-labeled kernels: Peter--Weyl block diagonalization yields an Artin-style factorization of coverage determinants, together with a class-function Chebotarev--Plancherel energy identity and quotient monotonicity. At the operator-algebra and spectral-realization levels, periodic values of Kronecker scans are identified as normalized traces in crossed-product algebras, and finite CMV certificates are shown to stabilize uniquely to an infinite-dimensional CMV unitary spectral object. The corresponding anti-linear self-duality defect is fully encoded by Tate/Maslov-type indices and boundary singular residues (degenerating to a single integer index under phase closure).

\end{abstract}
