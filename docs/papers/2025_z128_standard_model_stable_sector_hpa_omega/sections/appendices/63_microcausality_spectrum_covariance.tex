\subsection{Microcausality, spectrum, and covariance (AQFT consequences and audit boundaries)}
\label{app:microcausality_spectrum_covariance}

This appendix records a compact set of consequences and boundary statements around locality and spectral assumptions in the AQFT package.
The purpose is to keep the dependency footprint explicit: microcausality and spectrum are not derived from the finite folding core; they are interface-facing structural commitments for relativistic formulations.

\subsubsection{Microcausality as an operational non-disturbance constraint}
\paragraph{Mathematical statement.}
Microcausality is the locality axiom in Definition~\ref{def:z128_local_net}: observables in spacelike separated regions commute.

\paragraph{Interface reading.}
\InterfaceTag Commutativity is the algebraic encoding of a protocol-level non-disturbance/no-signaling stance between spacelike separated readout regions.
Any use of this axiom must be read as a declared interface constraint on admissible observable organizations, not as a theorem-level consequence of the tick-only folding core.

\subsubsection{Spectrum condition and positive energy}
\paragraph{Mathematical statement.}
The spectrum condition in Definition~\ref{def:z128_vacuum_spectrum_condition} is stated in the GNS picture: translations are represented by unitaries $U(a)$, and the joint spectrum of their generators lies in the forward light cone.

\paragraph{Interface reading.}
\InterfaceTag The spectrum condition encodes ``positive energy'' and rules out pathological time-translation behaviors.
In the tick\,+\,CAP discipline, it is a compatibility requirement for any attempted relativistic embedding of the protocol language, and it provides the standard directionality needed for scattering constructions.

\subsubsection{Covariance and locality in the GNS net}
Given a covariant state $\omega$ and its GNS representation (Appendix~\ref{app:aqft_states_representations_gns_nets}), covariance implies
\[
U_\omega(g)\,\mathcal{M}_\omega(\mathcal{O})\,U_\omega(g)^\dagger=\mathcal{M}_\omega(g\mathcal{O}),
\]
and microcausality implies $[\mathcal{M}_\omega(\mathcal{O}_1),\mathcal{M}_\omega(\mathcal{O}_2)]=0$ for spacelike separated regions.

\subsubsection{Open boundary items (explicitly not closed here)}
\paragraph{Domain and field reconstruction issues.}
\AuditTag Passing from nets of bounded operators to operator-valued distributions (fields) requires additional domain control and continuity assumptions.
These issues are handled in the hybrid bridge to Wightman formulations and are treated as explicit scope boundaries rather than implicit premises.

\paragraph{Interacting models and renormalization.}
\AuditTag AQFT axioms constrain the organization of observables and states, but do not by themselves produce a constructive interacting model in four dimensions.
Any constructive claims about interactions, renormalization, or nonperturbative phenomena must be accompanied by explicit additional inputs and a declared hypothesis space.
