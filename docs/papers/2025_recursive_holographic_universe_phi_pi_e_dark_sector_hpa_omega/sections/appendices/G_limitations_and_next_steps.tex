
\section{Limitations and next steps}
\label{app:limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Channel separation beyond the minimal window.}
At $m=6$, the $\e$-channel can coincide with the $\varphi$-channel when expressed as a local forbidden-word constraint.
A genuine three-channel separation requires higher resolution and weighted extensions (pressure mechanism).
\item \textbf{Wormholes as an extension layer.}
Pointer-jump wormholes are formulated as an address-level extension, but their minimal falsifiable signatures are not yet established at the same level as the $64\to 21$ folding table.
\item \textbf{Observer semantics and halting semantics are distinct.}
Readout/measurement semantics and halting/teleology questions should not substitute for a falsifiable dynamical closure.
\end{itemize}
