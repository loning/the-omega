\section{Arithmetic Statistical Mechanics: entropy as phase friction}
\label{sec:asm}

Arithmetic Statistical Mechanics (ASM) is the statistical mechanics induced by scan--projection readout. It does not assume ontic randomness. Instead, it counts \emph{readout equivalence classes} of a deterministic unitary orbit under finite resolution.

\subsection{Microstates, macrostates, and the readout lattice}

\begin{definition}[Microstate and macrostate in ASM]
Fix a local effective degree of freedom with pointer coordinate $x\in\mathbb{T}$. The \textbf{microstate} is the scan orbit $\{x_n\}_{n\ge 1}$ generated by O6. For a fixed readout resolution $\varepsilon$ (O5), the \textbf{macrostate} is the discrete output sequence $\{k_n\}$ (or an induced symbolic word $\{s_n\}$) produced by windowing the orbit, together with a finite set of statistics (histograms, correlations, coding depth) computed from a length-$N$ window.
\end{definition}

\noindent The finite readout resolution induces a discrete \emph{readout lattice}: a partition of $\mathbb{T}$ into finitely many distinguishable cells. In the golden branch, the natural notion of lattice depth is the Zeckendorf coding depth associated with the Ostrowski system; heuristically, ``spatial resolution'' becomes ``coding depth.''

\subsection{Phase friction as discrepancy}

To quantify mismatch without adding stochasticity, we use star discrepancy for the Kronecker sequence $\{x_n\}$.

\begin{definition}[Star discrepancy and accumulated mismatch]
Let $x_1,\dots,x_N\in[0,1)$ be the first $N$ points of the scan orbit. The one-dimensional star discrepancy is
\begin{equation}
D_N^\ast := \sup_{0\le a\le 1}\left|\frac{1}{N}\#\{1\le n\le N:\ x_n<a\}-a\right|.
\end{equation}
The accumulated mismatch (worst-case counting deviation) is
\begin{equation}
E_N := N D_N^\ast.
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Koksma's inequality: discrepancy controls readout error]
\label{thm:koksma}
Let $x_1,\dots,x_N\in[0,1)$ and let $f:[0,1)\to\RR$ have bounded variation $V(f)$ in the sense of Hardy--Krause (in one dimension this reduces to total variation). Then
\begin{equation}
  \left|\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=1}^N f(x_n) - \int_0^1 f(x)\,\dd x\right|
  \le V(f)\,D_N^\ast.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\noindent Theorem~\ref{thm:koksma} provides an operational meaning of phase friction: $D_N^\ast$ upper-bounds the worst-case readout bias for any bounded-variation observable at the readout layer. Standard references include \cite{Koksma1935,KuipersNiederreiter1974}.

\noindent In ASM we interpret:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $D_N^\ast$ as \textbf{phase friction strength} (a per-sample readout mismatch intensity);
  \item $E_N$ as \textbf{accumulated phase friction} (a finite-window ``heat'' generated by mismatch).
\end{itemize}

\begin{theorem}[Logarithmic bound for badly approximable slopes]
\label{thm:log_bound}
Let $x_n = x_0 + n\alpha \bmod 1$ with $\alpha$ irrational. If $\alpha$ has bounded continued-fraction coefficients (equivalently, $\alpha$ is badly approximable), then there exists a constant $C(\alpha)>0$ such that
\begin{equation}
D_N^\ast \le C(\alpha)\frac{\log N}{N}\qquad\text{for all } N\ge 2,
\end{equation}
and hence
\begin{equation}
E_N \le C(\alpha)\log N.
\end{equation}
The golden branch $\alpha=\varphi^{-1}$ is extremal among badly approximable slopes in the Hurwitz sense, providing near-minimal constants in such bounds \cite{Cassels1957,KuipersNiederreiter1974}.
\end{theorem}
\noindent An explicit continued-fraction bound controlling $D_N^\ast$ by partial quotients is recorded in Appendix~\ref{app:discrepancy_entropy}, Eq.~\eqref{eq:explicit_disc_bound}.

\begin{proposition}[Periodic ``thermal death'' for rational slopes]
\label{prop:rational_linear}
If $\alpha=p/q\in\QQ$ in lowest terms, then the orbit has period $q$ and the readout is eventually periodic (with period dividing $q$) under any fixed finite-resolution window protocol. Moreover, for every $N$ that is a multiple of $q$, one has the explicit star-discrepancy lower bound
\begin{equation}
  D_N^\ast \ge \frac{1}{2q},
\end{equation}
and therefore the accumulated mismatch grows at least linearly:
\begin{equation}
  E_N = N D_N^\ast \ge \frac{N}{2q}.
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\noindent A short proof of the universal $1/(2q)$ lower bound is given in Appendix~\ref{app:discrepancy_entropy}.

\noindent Proposition~\ref{prop:rational_linear} expresses an operational form of heat death: phase locking collapses accessible macrostates into a short periodic cycle, and mismatch accumulates at a constant per-step rate under fixed finite resolution.

\subsection{Entropy as arithmetic mismatch: two compatible definitions}

ASM matches the HPA layer-separation principle: additive thermodynamic quantities arise from log readout of multiplicative weights. This yields two compatible entropies.

\begin{definition}[Readout log-weight entropy]
Let $w\in(0,1]$ denote the operational success weight of a macro-constraint (e.g.\ a discrimination task) under a fixed readout protocol. Define
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{rd}} := k_B\,(-\log w).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Phase-friction entropy]
For a length-$N$ window define the phase-friction entropy
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{pf}}(N) := k_B\,E_N = k_B\,N D_N^\ast.
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\noindent $S_{\mathrm{rd}}$ and $S_{\mathrm{pf}}$ are calibrated differently but share the same structural meaning: entropy quantifies the operational cost of making a coarse-grained description consistent with a continuously scanned ontic orbit. In the golden branch, the cost can be normalized by Zeckendorf depth, making $S_{\mathrm{pf}}$ a directly computable proxy for thermodynamic entropy flow in toy models.

\subsection{Second law as monotonicity under readout coarse-graining}

Operational irreversibility is encoded by the unconditional readout channel $\Lambda^{(\varepsilon)}$ (O5), which forgets the outcome record. A key point is that for the POVM $\{E_k^{(\varepsilon)}\}$ induced by readout, there exists a canonical ``square-root'' (L\"uders-type) instrument with Kraus operators $M_k=\sqrt{E_k^{(\varepsilon)}}$ \cite{Kraus1983,NielsenChuang2000}. Its unconditional channel
\begin{equation}
  \Lambda^{(\varepsilon)}(\rho)=\sum_k \sqrt{E_k^{(\varepsilon)}}\,\rho\,\sqrt{E_k^{(\varepsilon)}}
\end{equation}
is automatically unital because $\Lambda^{(\varepsilon)}(\mathds{1})=\sum_k E_k^{(\varepsilon)}=\mathds{1}$. Therefore, entropy monotonicity follows without an extra assumption.

\begin{proposition}[Arithmetic second law (interface form)]
\label{prop:second_law}
Let $\rho$ be an effective state on the accessible sector, and let $\Lambda^{(\varepsilon)}$ be the outcome-discarding readout channel associated with the square-root instrument of the induced POVM (O5). Then $\Lambda^{(\varepsilon)}$ is unital and the von Neumann entropy satisfies
\begin{equation}
S\!\left(\Lambda^{(\varepsilon)}(\rho)\right)\ge S(\rho).
\end{equation}
Moreover, the phase-friction entropy $S_{\mathrm{pf}}$ provides a computable upper proxy for the coarse-grained entropy production in rotation models, controlled by discrepancy bounds such as Theorem~\ref{thm:log_bound}.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $d$ be the Hilbert-space dimension of the effective sector and let $\tau:=\mathds{1}/d$ be the maximally mixed state. For any CPTP map $\Lambda$, quantum relative entropy satisfies the data-processing inequality
\begin{equation}
  D(\rho\|\tau)\ \ge\ D(\Lambda(\rho)\|\Lambda(\tau)),
\end{equation}
see e.g.\ \cite{NielsenChuang2000}. If $\Lambda$ is unital then $\Lambda(\tau)=\tau$. Using $D(\rho\|\tau)=\log d - S(\rho)$, one obtains
\begin{equation}
  \log d - S(\rho)\ \ge\ \log d - S(\Lambda(\rho)),
\end{equation}
equivalently $S(\Lambda(\rho))\ge S(\rho)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why no external noise is needed]
Theorem~\ref{thm:log_bound} is purely arithmetic: even a perfectly deterministic unitary scan produces mismatch at finite readout resolution. In this sense, ``thermal noise'' is structural---it is the residual of projecting a Weyl orbit onto a discrete readout lattice.
\end{remark}
