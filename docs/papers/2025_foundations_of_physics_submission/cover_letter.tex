\opening{Dear Editor,}

I am pleased to submit my manuscript titled \textbf{``Omega Theory: Axiomatic Foundations of Holographic Spacetime and Interactive Evolution''} for consideration as an original research article (or Monograph) in \textit{Foundations of Physics}.

This paper presents a comprehensive attempt to axiomatize the foundations of cosmology and quantum mechanics from a purely information-theoretic perspective. Motivated by the tension between the unitarity of quantum evolution and the geometric nature of gravity, I propose a new framework---\textbf{Omega Theory}---which treats the entire universe as a single, static vector $|\Omega\rangle$ in an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space.

Key contributions of this work include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Omega Axiom}: A rigorous definition of the universe's ontology as a holographic projection of a high-dimensional vector, unifying the "block universe" view with dynamical evolution via phase re-alignment.
    \item \textbf{DQCA Continuum Limit}: A mathematical proof (Appendix C) demonstrating how the Dirac equation and Lorentz invariance naturally emerge from a discrete Quantum Cellular Automaton (DQCA) on a quasicrystal background, fulfilling the "It from Bit" program.
    \item \textbf{Geometric Derivation of Constants}: First-principles derivations (Appendix B) for the Proton-Electron Mass Ratio ($\mu = 6\pi^5$) and the Fine Structure Constant ($\alpha^{-1}$), treating them as geometric invariants of the internal Hilbert space fibration rather than arbitrary parameters.
    \item \textbf{The Observer Operator}: A resolution to the Gödelian incompleteness of closed unitary systems by introducing self-referential measurement as a fundamental driver of complexity (Part VII).
\end{enumerate}

Given the journal's prestigious history of publishing foundational works that challenge and expand our understanding of physical reality (such as those by Bell, Bohm, and ‘t Hooft), I believe \textit{Foundations of Physics} is the ideal venue for this work. The paper adopts a rigorous deductive structure similar to a monograph, aiming to provide a self-consistent "Source Code" for the physical laws we observe.

I confirm that this manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors (myself) have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to your response.
