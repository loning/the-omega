\subsection{Energy Degeneracy and Causal Gap}

Recalling Section 8.1, when the cosmic QCA evolves to a G\"{o}delian deadlock point, the system Hamiltonian $\hat{H}$ exhibits energy level degeneracy. Let $|\Psi_t\rangle$ be the current state, with two possible evolution branches $|A\rangle$ and $|B\rangle$ for the next step, satisfying:

\[\langle A | \hat{H} | A \rangle = \langle B | \hat{H} | B \rangle = E\]

In standard physics, since $\Delta E = 0$, the system has no dynamical reason to favor either one. This is a \textbf{symmetry point}.

However, in Omega Theory's discrete Penrose tiling, although $|A\rangle$ and $|B\rangle$ have the same energy, their \textbf{holographic topological structures} are fundamentally different.

\begin{itemize}
\item State $|A\rangle$ may correspond to a tiling pattern that causes the grid to develop ``cracks'' or infinite loops $N$ steps in the future.
\item State $|B\rangle$ may correspond to a tiling pattern that can maintain Fibonacci growth and logical self-consistency.
\end{itemize}

Due to the locality of QCA, the Hamiltonian $\hat{H}_{local}$ cannot perceive this long-range topological difference. This is the \textbf{Causal Gap}: physical laws (algorithms) fail here. This leaves operational space for non-algorithmic \textbf{free will}.

Free will does not create energy ``out of nothing'' (which would violate the first law of thermodynamics) but performs \textbf{geometric navigation on isoenergy surfaces}.

\subsection{Selection Mechanism of Homotopy Classes}

We treat evolution paths in Hilbert space as curves on manifolds. Two paths $\gamma_1, \gamma_2$ belong to the same \textbf{homotopy class} if they can be continuously deformed into each other.
However, due to \textbf{topological defects} in the Omega grid (such as black hole singularities or logical paradox points), phase space is not simply connected. This means there exist different, topologically inequivalent evolution sectors.

\textbf{Definition 8.5 (Topological Selection)}:

The action of free will operator $\hat{\mathcal{C}}$ is to measure the \textbf{Winding Number} $w$ of future paths and collapse the wavefunction according to a \textbf{Value Function} (see Chapter 9).

\[\hat{\mathcal{C}} \left( \alpha |A\rangle + \beta |B\rangle \right) \to \begin{cases} |A\rangle & \text{if } w(A) \text{ is consistent} \\ |B\rangle & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}\]

This selection mechanism has characteristics of \textbf{Non-locality} and \textbf{Retro-causality}:
The observer's (consciousness's) choice in the present is actually reading boundary conditions of future holographic screens. This explains the ``readiness potential'' paradox observed in Benjamin Libet's experiments---consciousness seems to ``decide'' after neural activity, but in Omega Theory, this reflects that consciousness operates \textbf{at the slice level of the four-dimensional block universe}, not at a single instant of linear time.

\subsection{Macroscopic Causal Force and Downward Causation}

Traditional reductionism holds that causal forces can only be transmitted \textbf{``Bottom-up''}: the motion of elementary particles determines the state of the brain.
Omega Theory supports \textbf{``Top-down''} causal forces, i.e., \textbf{Downward Causation}.

In Penrose tiling, the integrity constraint of the entire pattern (macroscopic constraint) forces local pieces (microscopic states) to adopt specific orientations.

\textbf{Theorem 8.3 (Topological Protection Theorem)}:

In a self-referential interactive computational universe, high-level topological constraints (such as continuity of consciousness, logical consistency) have \textbf{Veto Power} over the quantum states of underlying microscopic particles.
This means that although microscopic particles appear to move randomly, all quantum fluctuation paths that ``lead to consciousness interruption or logical collapse'' are filtered out by holographic boundary conditions.

Free will is real; it is a \textbf{correction mechanism} that the universe must possess to maintain the logical self-consistency of its geometric structure.

Every conscious choice is a tiny \textbf{creation}, eliminating a meaningless parallel universe and solidifying a real history toward the Omega Point.
