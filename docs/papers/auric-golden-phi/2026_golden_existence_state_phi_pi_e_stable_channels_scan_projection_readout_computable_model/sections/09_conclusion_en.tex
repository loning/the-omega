\section{Conclusion}

This paper formalizes the ``golden existence state'' in a computable manner and provides, under the scan--projection readout framework, a rigorous expression of ``existence/non-existence'' as a binary projection readout: the ontological state is conditioned to the stable subspace under finite resolution and stability filtering, and observation statistics are determined by the conditioned state. The stable type space $X_m$ obtained through the golden mean constraint exhibits a Fibonacci count law and topological entropy $\log\varph$, thereby providing a mathematical definition for ``golden dimension''. This paper further provides the $\varph$--$\pi$--$e$ three-channel stability decomposition: $\varph$ corresponds to syntactic legality and self-similar growth, $\pi$ corresponds to periodic closure and cyclic orbit structure, and $e$ corresponds to logarithmic readout cost and analytic stability domain; and locates primes as generative labels of the multiplicative skeleton and spectral organization indices. The mathematical layer output of the model is completely determined by finite objects, thereby possessing auditability and falsifiability; higher-layer semantic mappings can be tested or refuted on independent experiments and data interfaces.
