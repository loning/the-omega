\paragraph{KL Pythagorean decomposition: strict externalization of microscopic residuals as auditable fields}
At fixed resolution \(m\), the readout operator \(P_m\) and the fiber-uniform lift \(Q_m\) form an auditable ``macroscopic--microscopic'' interface (\S\ref{subsec:pom-information-ledger}).
Taking the uniform distribution \(u_{\Omega_m}\) on \(\Omega_m\) as the microstate baseline, its stable pushforward is
\(
w_m:=P_m u_{\Omega_m}
\)
with \(w_m(x)=d_m(x)/2^m\) (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-kl-pythagoras-uniform}).
For any microscopic distribution \(\mu\in\Delta(\Omega_m)\), let its macroscopic histogram be \(\pi:=P_m\mu\), and let \(\mu^e:=Q_m\pi\) be the fiberwise conditionally uniform lift under the same macroscopic constraint. Then KL divergence to the uniform baseline satisfies the exact identity
\[
\boxed{\;
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|u_{\Omega_m})
=D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\|w_m)\;+\;D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\mu^e)\;}
\]
(Theorem \ref{thm:pom-kl-pythagoras-uniform}).
This strictly splits the cost of ``deviation from uniform microstates'' into:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Macroscopic cost} \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\|w_m)\): depends only on the deviation of \(\pi\) from \(w_m\), the pushforward of uniform microstates;
  \item \textbf{Microscopic residual} \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\mu^e)\): the deviation of \(\mu\) from its fiber-uniformized version inside the constraint class with fixed \(\pi\) (fiberwise average nonuniformity; Lemma \ref{lem:pom-fiberwise-kl}).
\end{itemize}
Moreover, \(\mu^e\) admits strict characterizations as both maximum-entropy and I-projection:
it is the unique maximum-entropy element in the constraint set \(\mathcal{C}_\pi=\{\nu:P_m\nu=\pi\}\), and equivalently the KL-nearest point to \(u_{\Omega_m}\) (Corollary \ref{cor:pom-Iproj-maxent}).
Hence the KL Pythagorean decomposition serves as a minimal auditable definition of the ``orthogonal recording axis'': the axis is not an additional narrative coordinate but the residual term \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\mu^e)\) under projection \(\mu\mapsto\mu^e\), with exact gap characterization
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|u_{\Omega_m})-D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu^e\|u_{\Omega_m})
=D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|\mu^e)
\]
(Corollary \ref{cor:pom-Iproj-gap-exact}).

\paragraph{Chernoff quantile certificates: breakpoint phase diagrams driven by discrete curvature \texorpdfstring{$A_q$}{Aq}}
When the optimal design over tail failure rate \(\varepsilon\), resolution \(m\), and moment order \(q\) is written as an auditable certificate, the lower-envelope structure necessarily exhibits breakpoints.
Specifically, for audited \(r_q\), define secant slopes \(\beta_q=(\log r_q-\log2)/(q-1)\) and certificate family
\(
\gamma_m^{(q)}(\varepsilon)=\beta_q+\frac{1}{m(q-1)}\log\frac{1}{\varepsilon}
\),
whose lower envelope
\(
\gamma_m^{\mathrm{cert}}(\varepsilon)=\inf_{q\ge 2}\gamma_m^{(q)}(\varepsilon)
\)
is the piecewise lower envelope of a \(1/m\)-hyperbola family (Remark \ref{rem:pom-qstar-phase-diagram-breakpoints}).
Proposition \ref{prop:pom-breakpoint-curvature-law-Aq} shows that jumps of the optimal moment order are fully controlled by a discrete-curvature increment scalar \(A_q\), with closed-form breakpoints
\[
m_{q\to q+1}(\varepsilon)=\frac{\log(1/\varepsilon)}{A_q},
\qquad
A_q=(q-1)(\log r_{q+1}-\log2)-q(\log r_q-\log2),
\]
so \(q^\star(m,\varepsilon)\) decreases monotonically in unit steps as \(m\) increases, with two-way ties exactly at breakpoints (Remark \ref{rem:pom-qstar-phase-diagram-breakpoints}).
For \(\varepsilon=10^{-6}\) and DP-extended audits up to \(q\le 20\), all breakpoints are uniquely computable from \(\{r_q\}\) and summarized in Table \ref{tab:fold_collision_qstar_breakpoints_eps1e6_q2_20};
for example, \(q^\star=7\) for \(m\in[513.01,643.28)\), while \(q^\star=2\) for \(m\ge 5344.31\) (same remark and table).
Under continuous interpolation in pressure geometry, \(A_q\) can also be interpreted as discrete-curvature sampling of an analytic convex pressure surface (Remark \ref{rem:pom-breakpoint-curvature-thermo-realization}), fixing the kink mechanism of the ``budget-design phase diagram'' as a geometric consequence of curvature.

\paragraph{Ontology of the resonance window: near-spectral degeneracy, not primitive Hankel degeneracy}
In the audited resonance window \(q=9,\dots,17\), Hankel-null certificates are nonempty (Table \ref{tab:fold_collision_resonance_nullmodes_q9_17}),
but their ``primitive quotient-space dimension'' is identically zero:
\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{prim}}(q)=0
\]
(Corollary \ref{cor:pom-hankel-null-no-primitive-residual}; see also Remark \ref{rem:pom-nullmodes-recurrence-plus-signature} and the explicit multiplicative decomposition in Table \ref{tab:fold_collision_resonance_nullmodes_primitive_quotient_q9_17}).
Equivalently, every annihilating polynomial induced by a NullMode can be written as \(A^{(j)}(\lambda)=Q^{(j)}(\lambda)P_q(\lambda)\) (zero remainder), so there is no ``primitive'' null mode unexplained by the minimal recurrence characteristic polynomial \(P_q\).
Hence, in this window, resonance should not be interpreted as the appearance of ``additional Hankel constraint directions,'' but rather as near-degeneracy in spectral-root geometry:
the shadow spectral packet gives \(|\mu_q|/r_{q-2}\uparrow 1\) and narrow-angle-window phase drift (Remarks \ref{rem:pom-shadow-spectral-packet-q9-17}, \ref{rem:pom-resonance-trimodal-phase-lock}), and the algebraic phase-lock residual \((\delta\theta_q,\rho_q)\) yields a reproducible one-dimensional intensity coordinate (Table \ref{tab:fold_collision_shadow_spectral_packet_algebraic_phase_residual_q9_17}; see also linearization constants and quadratic error bounds in Proposition \ref{prop:pom-phase-residual-linearization-sqrt15}).
Conceptually, this is consistent with Remark \ref{rem:pom-resonance-spectral-not-hankel-degeneracy}: strong resonance can appear spectrally as near-edge slow modes and phase locking, but in the current window it does not generate new primitive Hankel constraints.
Therefore, if one seeks to ``dissolve resonance,'' one must change the generating kernel (thus changing \(P_q\)) or explicitly externalize new channels, rather than mining extra Hankel degeneracies under the same \(P_q\).
