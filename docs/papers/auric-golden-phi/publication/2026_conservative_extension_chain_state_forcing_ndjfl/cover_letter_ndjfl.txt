Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript,

"Homological Visibility of Gluing Obstructions in a State-Forcing Semantics,"

for publication in the Notre Dame Journal of Formal Logic.

The manuscript develops a visibility theory for finite abelian gluing obstructions within a conservative state-forcing semantics. It is intended as a focused application of the state-forcing fragment of a larger semantic architecture, not as a reconstruction of that full framework. Its first structural step is categorical: the paper introduces realization prestacks whose stackification identifies visible value classes with the global connected components of a realization stack. This shows that, in general, an admitted reference is governed not by a single gerbe but by a family of component gerbes indexed by visible branches. For each branch, a chosen cocycle presentation determines a strict visible quotient that is initial among coefficient reductions trivializing that presentation, while the intrinsic hidden subgroup is identified with the image of the universal-coefficient evaluation map from the second homology of a finite Cech nerve to the band, so that the intrinsic visible quotient is the corresponding cokernel. The paper then proves that visibility detects exactly the homologically visible part of a branch obstruction, exhibits purely Ext-type branch obstructions that are invisible to all character channels, and derives branch-sensitive and branch-uniform total visible quotients together with an exact multi-branch strictification budget. Two worked examples and a short section on refinement dynamics are included to clarify the range of the theory.

We believe the manuscript fits the journal's interest in original work in formal logic and the foundations of mathematics. The paper is mathematically self-contained, technically focused, and written for a logic audience.
