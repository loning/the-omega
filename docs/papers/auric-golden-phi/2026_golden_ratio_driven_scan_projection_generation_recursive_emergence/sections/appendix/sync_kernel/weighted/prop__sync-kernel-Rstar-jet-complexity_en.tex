\begin{proposition}[Prime-dependent analytic audit complexity law: minimal jet order deduced from \texorpdfstring{$R_\star$}{R*}]\label{prop:rate-center-Rstar-jet-complexity}
Adopting from Proposition \ref{prop:rate-center-Q-reciprocal-no-real} the notation
\(
R_\star:=\min\{|u-1|:\ Q(u)=0\}
\).
Suppose \(F(u)\) is some local audit quantity that is analytic in the disk
\(
D(1,R_\star)=\{u\in\CC:\ |u-1|<R_\star\}
\)
(e.g., pressure/spectral support/root disk prediction quantities induced by the central section certificate).
Denote its Taylor truncation at \(u=1\) by
\[
T_M(u):=\sum_{n=0}^{M}\frac{F^{(n)}(1)}{n!}(u-1)^n.
\]
Then there exists a constant \(C>0\) (depending only on the upper bound of \(F\) on \(\{|u-1|=R_\star\}\)) such that for any \(|u-1|<R_\star\), the remainder estimate holds
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rate-jet-error-bound}
\boxed{\;
\bigl|F(u)-T_M(u)\bigr|
\le
C\left(\frac{|u-1|}{R_\star}\right)^{M+1}. \;}
\end{equation}
In particular, for roots of unity \(u=\omega_p:=e^{2\pi i/p}\):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item If \(p\ge 7\) such that \(|\omega_p-1|<R_\star\) (Corollary \ref{cor:rate-center-omega-threshold}), then there exists a constant \(C\) such that
  \[
  \mathrm{err}_M(p):=\bigl|F(\omega_p)-T_M(\omega_p)\bigr|
  \le
  C\left(\frac{|\omega_p-1|}{R_\star}\right)^{M}.
  \]
  Thus to compress the error within \(\varepsilon\), the minimal order satisfies
  \[
  M_{\min}(p,\varepsilon)\ \gtrsim\ \frac{\log(\varepsilon^{-1})}{\log\!\bigl(R_\star/|\omega_p-1|\bigr)}\qquad(p\ge 7).
  \]
  \item If \(p=5\) such that \(|\omega_5-1|>R_\star\), then the local expansion centered at \(u=1\) generally does not converge at \(\omega_5\); hence any finite jet approximation with a fixed center cannot be guaranteed attainable by an analytic remainder bound.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Set \(M_\star:=\sup_{|z-1|=R_\star}|F(z)|<\infty\).
By Cauchy's integral formula and geometric series remainder, for any \(|u-1|<R_\star\), the standard bound holds
\[
\bigl|F(u)-T_M(u)\bigr|
\le
M_\star\cdot \frac{(|u-1|/R_\star)^{M+1}}{1-|u-1|/R_\star}.
\]
When \(|u-1|<R_\star\) is fixed, the right side can be absorbed into a constant \(C\) times \((|u-1|/R_\star)^{M+1}\), yielding \eqref{eq:rate-jet-error-bound}.
The conclusions for \(u=\omega_p\) follow directly by substituting the relationship between \(|\omega_p-1|\) and \(R_\star\) given by Corollary \ref{cor:rate-center-omega-threshold} and rearranging; the non-attainability for \(p=5\) is its converse statement. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}
