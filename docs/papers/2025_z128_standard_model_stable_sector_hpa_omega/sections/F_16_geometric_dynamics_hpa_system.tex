\subsection{X.1.\ Constitutive constants as boundary conditions (channel roles)}
\label{subsec:x1_constitutive_boundary_conditions}

\paragraph{$\varphi$ (capacity/growth rate).}
\MathTag The admissible stable-sector language $X_n$ is the block language of the golden-mean shift and has Fibonacci growth $|X_n|=F_{n+2}$, hence topological entropy $h_{\mathrm{top}}=\log\varphi$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:golden_mean_language_equals_Xn}).
This is the precise theorem-level meaning of ``$\varphi$ as a rigidity/capacity constant'' inside the folding layer.

\paragraph{$\pi$ (cyclic closure).}
\MathTag The $\pi$-channel closure predicate is the wrap-around admissibility condition $w_1w_m=0$ on Zeckendorf-admissible words (Section~\ref{subsec:pi_channel}),
which induces the canonical cyclic/boundary split $X_m=X_m^{\mathrm{cyc}}\sqcup X_m^{\mathrm{bdry}}$ and, at $m=6$, the rigid $18\oplus 3$ partition (Corollary~\ref{prop:cyc_bdry_6}).

\paragraph{$\mathrm{e}$ (Abel normalization and pole barrier).}
\MathTag The $\e$-channel records an analytic stability template by packaging periodic-orbit counts into an Artin--Mazur zeta function and then applying Abel normalization.
For the golden-mean grammar, the zeta function is rational and its leading Abel pole barrier is at $r=1$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:golden_zeta_matrix} and \eqref{eq:abel_normalized_zeta}).
For the general determinant/pressure viewpoint (shift of finite type), see Corollary~\ref{cor:pole_barrier_equals_exp_minus_pressure}.

\InterfaceTag Complex phases (unitary rotations) and their separation from semigroup weights are treated as a representation/dictionary choice (Section~\ref{subsec:interpretive_unification_complex_exp}); this does not add an axiom.

\subsubsection{X.2.2.\ The golden trajectory (Sturmian inside the admissible language)}
\label{subsubsec:x22_golden_trajectory_sturmian}

\paragraph{A theorem-level ``quasi-periodic'' carrier.}
\MathTag The scan-projection readout on the golden branch produces a Sturmian word (Appendix~\ref{app:sturmian_entropy}).
Independently, the admissible stable-sector language $X_n$ is the full block language of the golden-mean shift (Lemma~\ref{lem:golden_mean_language_equals_Xn}).
These are different symbolic objects: the Sturmian factor language is a strict subset of $X_n$ for all $n\ge 3$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:sturmian_language_strict_subset}).

\paragraph{A strict, checkable definition of ``quasi-periodic'' in this paper's finite language.}
\MathTag In the symbolic dynamics carrier used here, a practical theorem-facing proxy for ``quasi-periodic'' (as opposed to periodic resonance closure) is:
\emph{aperiodic with linear factor complexity}.
Concretely, for a bi-infinite binary word $w$, let $p_w(n)$ be its factor complexity.
If $p_w(n)=n+1$ for all $n\ge 1$, then $w$ is Sturmian and has minimal aperiodic complexity.
For the canonical rotation window coding used in the readout model, one has exactly
\[
p_w(n)=n+1\qquad (n\ge 1)
\]
(Theorem~\ref{thm:sturmian_complexity}).

\paragraph{Zero entropy rate (negentropy proxy at theorem level).}
\MathTag Linear factor complexity implies zero topological entropy rate:
\[
h_{\mathrm{top}}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}\log p_w(n)=0
\]
(Corollary~\ref{cor:sturmian_top_entropy}).
Moreover, for any shift-invariant measure supported on the orbit closure of such a Sturmian word, the block Shannon entropies satisfy $H_n\le \log(n+1)$ and hence have vanishing per-symbol rate (Corollary~\ref{cor:sturmian_shannon_entropy}).
These statements provide a theorem-level sense in which Sturmian readouts carry structured correlations but do not ``thermalize'' in an entropy-rate sense.

\paragraph{Frequency stability (unique ergodicity of the underlying rotation).}
\MathTag For the irrational-rotation scan $\Theta f(x)=f(x+\alpha)$ on $X=\RR/\ZZ$ with Haar measure $\mu$, unique ergodicity implies that for every continuous observable $f$ the orbit averages
\[
A_N(f;x_0)=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}f(x_0+k\alpha)
\]
converge to the space average $\int_X f\,\dd\mu$ independently of the starting point $x_0$ (Appendix~\ref{app:cyclic_calculus_theta_fp}).
In the readout language, this provides a theorem-facing notion of ``stable frequencies'' for any declared continuous probe of the scan orbit; it is the appropriate replacement for informal claims of ergodicity/thermalization in the present setting.

\paragraph{Persistent pattern return (uniform recurrence).}
\MathTag Beyond frequency stability, the canonical rotation-window coding yields a stronger persistence statement at the symbolic level:
every factor that occurs reappears with bounded gaps (uniform recurrence).
Formally, for each factor $u\in\mathcal{L}_m(w)$ the occurrence set $\mathrm{Occ}_w(u)$ is syndetic, hence has a finite gap bound (Theorem~\ref{thm:sturmian_uniform_recurrence} in Appendix~\ref{app:sturmian_uniform_recurrence}).
This is the theorem-facing replacement for informal claims of ``continued exploration without losing previously realized patterns''.

\paragraph{Quantitative gap bound (in terms of convergent denominators).}
\MathTag The bounded-gap statement can be made quantitative in an auditable way:
interval hitting times along the rotation scan admit an explicit bound at convergent lengths.
Concretely, for any interval of length $\ell$, choosing a convergent denominator $q_k\ge 32/\ell$ guarantees a hit within the first $q_k$ steps, uniformly in the intercept (Theorem~\ref{thm:rotation_interval_hitting_convergent} in Appendix~\ref{app:rotation_hitting_time_discrepancy}).
Applied to the open cylinder arcs underlying a factor's occurrence set in the canonical coding, this yields an explicit (though factor-dependent) numerical gap bound expressed by the continued-fraction denominators.

\paragraph{Two-scale bound purely in \texorpdfstring{$(m,q_k,q_{k+1})$}{(m,qk,qk+1)}.}
\MathTag A stronger Sturmian-specific bound avoids factor-dependent arc lengths:
if $q_k\le m<q_{k+1}$ then every length-$m$ factor has return gaps bounded by $q_k+q_{k+1}$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:sturmian_return_time_qk_bound} in Appendix~\ref{app:sturmian_return_times_qk}).
In the bounded-type class ($a_i\le A$), this implies the explicit linear bound $\tau\le (A+2)m$, and on the golden branch ($A=1$) the uniform constant is $\tau\le 3m$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:sturmian_return_bounded_type_linear}).

\paragraph{Robustness scope.}
\MathTag The distinguished role of the golden branch among irrational slopes is recorded as an auditable finite-depth minimality statement (Proposition~\ref{prop:golden_least_discrepancy} and Remark~\ref{rem:golden_branch_principled}).
Any additional KAM-type statements about invariant tori require extra dynamical assumptions beyond tick+CAP and must be recorded explicitly as \AuditTag or \textsf{[Open]} if invoked.

\paragraph{Interface caution (biological language).}
\InterfaceTag Any identification of ``life/negentropy'' with Sturmian (or Sturmian-like) symbolic structure is an interface-level hypothesis.
The closed theorem-level content provided here is the pair of checkable properties (linear factor complexity; zero entropy rate), which may be used as auditable proxies if a biological interface is pursued elsewhere.

\begin{proposition}[Four-way unification as a contract form (audit-only)]
\label{prop:four_way_unification_contract_form}
\AuditTag In this manuscript, ``black-hole-like'' and ``wormhole-like'' language is permitted to unify
\emph{micro/macro}, \emph{quantum/classical}, \emph{discrete/continuous}, and \emph{space/time} only in the following precise sense:
the unification is a \emph{rigidity contract form} (what may be claimed and how it must be audited), not a theorem-level ontological identification.
Concretely, under the declared protocol state and matching dictionaries:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Micro $\leftrightarrow$ macro (ledger form).}
Micro stabilization is read through delay/linewidth proxies (Section~\ref{sec:mass_latency_coordinate} and Appendix~\ref{app:time_mass_delay}),
while macro trapping/leakage is read through the full-fusion ledgers and counterfactual deltas (Section~\ref{sec:qg_interface_full_fusion}).
The unified statement is that both must be reported in finite windows as audited return/delay summaries with explicit baselines;
no identity of mechanisms is assumed.
\item \textbf{Quantum $\leftrightarrow$ classical (phase-to-delay dictionary).}
Quantum-facing phase response (unitary $S$-parameters) and classical-facing time-delay/lapse proxies are connected only by explicit interface dictionaries
(Appendix~\ref{app:time_mass_delay}), with failure-point downgrades when unitarity windows or reconstruction conditions are not discharged
(Appendix~\ref{app:minimal_failure_point_templates}, S1--S3 and related carriers).
\item \textbf{Discrete $\leftrightarrow$ continuous (windowed comparability).}
Discrete protocol objects (stable types, finite-family selection, pointer edges) may be compared to continuum representatives only under a declared bounded matching envelope
(Appendix~\ref{app:qg9_m1_windowed_comparability} and the matching-layer contracts referenced there);
no continuum-limit existence/uniqueness is used as a premise.
\item \textbf{Space $\leftrightarrow$ time (overhead/delay as the bridge variable).}
``Space'' enters through an addressing/screen dictionary, while ``time'' enters as tick index; their joint narrative is mediated by overhead/delay/lapse dictionaries
(Section~\ref{sec:tick_calculus} and Appendix~s~\ref{app:time_mass_delay} and \ref{app:overhead_to_gravity_closure}),
so any space--time reading is a statement about audited overhead fields rather than a theorem-level spacetime identity.
\end{enumerate}
Moreover, any use of wormhole-like shortcuts is admissible only as the protocol pointer-jump object of Definition~\ref{def:wormhole_pointer_jump} and only under the full-fusion hard gates
(energy ledger closure, complementarity, counterfactual wormhole-off baseline, and the local interface factorization gate (LF1) for any det/logdet/delay promotion;
Section~\ref{sec:qg_interface_full_fusion}; Appendix~\ref{app:minimal_failure_point_templates}).
\end{proposition}
