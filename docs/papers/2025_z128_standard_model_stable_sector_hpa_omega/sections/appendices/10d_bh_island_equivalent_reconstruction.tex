\section{An island-equivalent reconstruction template (BH6: no-double-counting in the record algebra)}
\label{app:bh_island_equiv}

\paragraph{Scope and status.}
\AuditTag This appendix records a protocol-level template for an ``island-equivalent'' mechanism:
an interior degree of freedom is \emph{not} treated as an additional independent Hilbert-space factor,
but as a reconstructible subalgebra of the exterior record algebra.
This avoids the double-counting pathology behind the naive contradiction
``causal inaccessibility + complete evaporation + unitarity''.
The present appendix is a CP/structure module: it states the target in algebraic terms and provides
finite diagnostics in a toy model; it does not claim a quantum-gravity replica derivation.
Scope contract and failure recording are in Appendix~\ref{app:bh_scope_contract}.

\subsection{Algebraic target: interior as a reconstructible subalgebra}
\label{subsec:bh6_algebraic_target}

\paragraph{Exterior record algebra.}
Fix a declared external record stream $R_{0:t}$ (Appendix~\ref{subsec:bh_contract_record_algebra}).
Let $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{out}}(t)$ denote the algebra generated by all observables of the record prefix up to time $t$
(for example, cylinder sets of a discrete record process, or the effect algebra of a finite readout).

\paragraph{Interior algebra (protocol-facing).}
\InterfaceTag At fixed $m$, the folding map induces fibers $P(w)=\mathrm{Fold}_m^{-1}(w)$.
Any ``interior micro-information'' beyond the coarse stable label $w$ is, in the paper's native finite objects,
fiber information (a choice of a preimage inside $P(w)$), potentially organized as a product over multiple ticks.
We denote by $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{in}}$ the algebra of these fiber degrees of freedom (at a chosen time slice),
under a declared protocol coarse graining.

\paragraph{Island-equivalent claim (template).}
\AuditTag An island-equivalent reconstruction template is the claim that there exists a (possibly approximate)
reconstruction map
\[
\mathrm{Rec}_t:\ \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{in}}\ \longrightarrow\ \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{out}}(t)
\]
such that, on a declared class of states and within a declared error tolerance, interior observables are reproduced
from the exterior record algebra.
In particular, the interior is not an extra independent factor: it is represented inside $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{out}}(t)$.

\paragraph{Failure point.}
\AuditTag If no such reconstruction map can exist without violating the declared protocol budget or without
introducing hidden knobs, the appropriate downgrade is to keep BH4 (record + resource ledger) and BH5 (Page surrogate)
as interface-only diagnostics and not to claim an island-equivalent mechanism.
