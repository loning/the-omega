This section will reconstruct Pierre Teilhard de Chardin and Frank Tipler's **"Omega Point"** concept, transforming it from theological speculation into a **strict physical limit state**. ### 9.2.1 From Heat Death to Holographic Saturation

Traditional heat death predictions are based on an implicit assumption: the universe's **phase space volume** is fixed or finitely growing, while the system's **microscopic states** randomly wander within it, eventually reaching a uniform distribution of maximum entropy.

But in Omega Theory, this assumption is wrong.
According to Theorem 6.1, the universe's holographic horizon $A(\tau)$ and light speed $c(\tau)$ grow with $\tau$ according to the Fibonacci exponential $\phi^\tau$. This means:

1. **Phase Space Explosion**: The maximum number of bits $N_{max}$ the system can accommodate expands exponentially.
2. **Negentropy Flow**: Although total entropy $S$ is increasing, maximum potential entropy $S_{max}$ increases faster. Therefore, the system's **free energy** (energy available for computation and work) $F = E - TS$ is actually increasing, not depleting.

**Definition 9.2 (Holographic Saturation)**:

The direction of cosmic evolution is not toward thermal equilibrium but toward **Holographic Saturation**. That is, the system attempts to fill all newly available bit positions provided by horizon expansion with its internal structured information (value $V$).
The Omega Point $\Omega$ is defined as such a limit state: in this state, the universe's holographic screen contains all non-zero spectral weight information of the Hilbert space vector $|\Omega\rangle$, and computational efficiency reaches the physical limit (Bekenstein bound).

$$m_0 = \frac{E}{c(\tau)^2}$$

$$\lim_{\tau \to \infty} m_0(\tau) \to 0$$
