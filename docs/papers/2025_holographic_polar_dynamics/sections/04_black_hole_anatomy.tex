\section{Anatomy of a Holographic Black Hole}

\subsection{The Event Horizon: Phase Locking Radius}
In the classical picture, the event horizon at $r_s = 2M$ is a null surface from which light cannot escape. In Omega Dynamics, we interpret this via the scanning operator $\Theta$.
The horizon corresponds to the \textbf{Phase Locking Radius} $\rho_{lock}$. At this radius, the radial phase accumulation rate matches the global scanning frequency:
\begin{equation}
    \frac{d\Phi}{d\rho} \bigg|_{\rho_{lock}} = \omega_{scan}
\end{equation}
At this resonance point, the local ``tick rate'' of the simulation becomes synchronized with the update cycle. This saturation logic is consistent with the \textbf{Unruh Effect} \cite{Unruh1976}, where an accelerated observer sees a thermal bath. Here, the scanning acceleration at the horizon defines a temperature barrier where classical information transfer is drowned out by phase noise. The effective information throughput drops to zero relative to an external observer, saturating the Bekenstein Bound \cite{Bekenstein1973}.

\subsection{The Interior: A Topological Inverter}
Crossing the horizon, we enter the region $\rho < \rho_{lock}$. Here, the HPA metric reveals its non-trivial topology. The coordinate $\rho$ does not simply count down to zero. Instead, the manifold undergoes an inversion $\mathcal{I}$:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{I}: \rho \to \frac{\rho_{lock}^2}{\rho}
\end{equation}
The interior geometry is conformal to the exterior but with inverted parity. A particle falling ``in'' towards $r=0$ is topologically equivalent to a particle moving ``out'' towards the holographic boundary in the dual space. This realizes the Holographic Principle \cite{Susskind1995, Maldacena1998} not just as a boundary condition, but as a dynamic map.

\subsection{The Throat at the Center}
The point $\rho=0$ is removed from the manifold. In its place sits a \textbf{Topological Throat} (or Einstein-Rosen bridge \cite{EinsteinRosen1935}) that connects the ``South Pole'' of the projection (the black hole center) directly to the ``North Pole'' (the holographic screen at infinity).
This connection supports the \textbf{ER=EPR} conjecture \cite{SusskindMaldacena2013}, implying that the entanglement between the bulk interior and the boundary radiation is mediated by this geometric throat. Matter reaching the center is thus delocalized and re-injected into the global boundary state vector $|\Omega\rangle$.
