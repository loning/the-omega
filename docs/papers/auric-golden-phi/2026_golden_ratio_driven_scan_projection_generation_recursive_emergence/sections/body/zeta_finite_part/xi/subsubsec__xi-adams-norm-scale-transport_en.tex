\subsubsection{Scale Exchange Law of Endpoint Compression: Adams--Norm Fold Gate and Fixed-Radius Certificates}\label{subsubsec:xi-adams-norm-scale-transport}
Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offcritical-quadratic-radial-compression} has established the structural compression of off-slice zeros
\(\rho=\beta+\mathrm{i}\gamma\) (\(0<\beta<\tfrac12\)) in disk coordinates:
let \(\delta:=\tfrac12-\beta>0\) and \(w_\rho=\mathcal{C}(\gamma+\mathrm{i}\delta)\in\mathbb{D}\), then
\[
1-|w_\rho|^2=\frac{4\delta}{\gamma^2+(1+\delta)^2},
\qquad
|w_\rho|^2=1-\frac{4\delta}{\gamma^2+(1+\delta)^2}.
\]
Therefore, when \(|\gamma|\to\infty\) with \(\delta\) fixed, the disk radius gap \(1-|w_\rho|^2\) is compressed into the boundary layer of the endpoint neighborhood at rate \(\Theta(\gamma^{-2})\), inducing the resolution/bit-budget threshold in Corollary \ref{cor:xi-offcritical-visibility-threshold-bit-budget}.
\par
Under the unitary slice locking regime (Definition \ref{def:xi-visible-read-null}, with \(X_{\mathrm{vis}}=\mathsf{Phase}\)), radial information is not permitted to be externalized as a continuous register; thus the intrinsic question is:
without introducing a radial continuous mode axis and while preserving the existing self-dual completion and fixed-slice regime, does there exist a purely algebraic/functorial scale gate that transports point defects in the endpoint boundary layer to a prescribed compact subdisk, so that ``high-height invisibility'' can be rewritten as ``visibility of a scale parameter''?
\par
This paragraph provides a rigorous realization: along the power covering \(w\mapsto w^m\), the Adams--Norm fold gate symmetrizes the branch degrees of freedom via the product, thereby transporting point defects from the endpoint neighborhood into fixed-radius verifiable Jensen defect lower bounds; the cost is an externalized scale parameter \(m\) (which can be explicitly estimated and admits integer-coefficient closure in invariant coordinates).

\paragraph{Power covering and Adams--Norm fold gate}
For any integer \(m\ge 1\), let
\[
\pi_m:\mathbb{D}\to\mathbb{D},\qquad \pi_m(\omega)=\omega^m
\]
be the \(m\)-fold covering (deck group \(\mu_m=\{\zeta_m^k:0\le k\le m-1\}\), \(\zeta_m=e^{2\pi\mathrm{i}/m}\)).

\begin{definition}[Adams--Norm fold gate]\label{def:xi-adams-norm-fold}
Let \(F\) be analytic in \(\mathbb{D}\). Define its \textbf{fold norm} (Norm) along \(\pi_m\) as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-adams-norm-def}
\boxed{\;
\mathrm{Nm}_m[F](w)
~:=~
\prod_{\omega^m=w}F(\omega),
\qquad w\in\mathbb{D}.
\;}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, by locally choosing an analytic branch of the \(m\)-th root \(w^{1/m}\), one can write
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-adams-norm-branch}
\mathrm{Nm}_m[F](w)
~=~
\prod_{k=0}^{m-1}F\!\bigl(\zeta_m^k\,w^{1/m}\bigr).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Single-valued analyticity]\label{prop:xi-adams-norm-holomorphic}
If \(F\) is analytic in \(\mathbb{D}\), then \(\mathrm{Nm}_m[F]\) is analytic and single-valued in \(\mathbb{D}\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On any simply connected open set \(U\subset\mathbb{D}\setminus\{0\}\), one can choose an analytic branch \(w^{1/m}\), and \eqref{eq:xi-adams-norm-branch} gives an analytic expression for \(\mathrm{Nm}_m[F]\) on \(U\).
Changing the branch amounts to multiplying \(w^{1/m}\) by some \(\zeta_m^\ell\), which merely permutes the factors and leaves the product invariant; hence the definition is independent of the branch choice and can be analytically continued to \(\mathbb{D}\setminus\{0\}\).
At \(w=0\), \(\mathrm{Nm}_m[F](0)=F(0)^m\), and by the Taylor expansion of \(F\) one sees that there is no essential singularity, so the continuation extends to an analytic function.
Therefore \(\mathrm{Nm}_m[F]\) is analytic and single-valued in \(\mathbb{D}\). \qedhere
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scale transport of point defects}
\begin{theorem}[Scale transport of point defects]\label{thm:xi-adams-norm-zero-transport}
Let \(F\) be analytic in \(\mathbb{D}\), and take \(m\ge 1\).
If \(F(a)=0\) (\(a\in\mathbb{D}\)), then \(\mathrm{Nm}_m[F](a^m)=0\).
More generally, if \(\mathrm{ord}_a(F)=r\), then
\[
\mathrm{ord}_{a^m}\bigl(\mathrm{Nm}_m[F]\bigr)\ \ge\ r.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If \(F(a)=0\), then the \(\pi_m\)-fiber over \(w=a^m\) contains the point \(a\), so the product in \eqref{eq:xi-adams-norm-def} includes the factor \(F(a)=0\), hence \(\mathrm{Nm}_m[F](a^m)=0\).
\par
If \(\mathrm{ord}_a(F)=r\), take a local parameter \(t\) so that \(F(\omega)=(\omega-a)^r g(\omega)\) with \(g(a)\neq 0\).
For the case \(a\neq 0\), choose in a neighborhood of \(w\) an analytic branch satisfying \(\omega(w)^m=w\), \(\omega(a^m)=a\); then
\((\omega(w)-a)\) is first-order nondegenerate in \(w-a^m\), so the factor \(F(\omega(w))\) contributes at least \(r\) orders of vanishing; the remaining factors are products of analytic functions, so the total order is at least \(r\).
For the case \(a=0\), write \(F(\omega)=\omega^{r}g(\omega)\) with \(g(0)\neq 0\).
By \eqref{eq:xi-adams-norm-branch},
\[
\mathrm{Nm}_m[F](w)
=\prod_{k=0}^{m-1}\bigl(\zeta_m^k w^{1/m}\bigr)^{r}\,g\!\bigl(\zeta_m^k w^{1/m}\bigr)
=\Bigl(\prod_{k=0}^{m-1}\zeta_m^{kr}\Bigr)\,w^{r}\cdot \prod_{k=0}^{m-1}g\!\bigl(\zeta_m^k w^{1/m}\bigr).
\]
The last factor is analytic at \(w=0\) and evaluates to \(g(0)^m\neq 0\), so \(\mathrm{ord}_{0}(\mathrm{Nm}_m[F])=r\).
\qedhere
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Invertibilization of endpoint compression: explicit scale threshold and fixed-radius Jensen certificate}
Following \eqref{eq:xi-ramanujan-horizon-Fzeta} for the diskified completion object \(F_\zeta\) (\(F_\zeta(w)=\Xi_\zeta(\frac12+\mathrm{i}\mathcal{C}^{-1}(w))\)), fix a target radius \(r_\star\in(0,1)\).
For any off-slice zero \(\rho=\beta+\mathrm{i}\gamma\) (\(0<\beta<\tfrac12\)), its disk image \(w_\rho\) satisfies \(|w_\rho|<1\) and \(F_\zeta(w_\rho)=0\) (Theorem \ref{thm:app-rh-iff-disk-zero-free} and the corresponding caliber of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offcritical-quadratic-radial-compression}).
\par
Define the explicit scale threshold
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-adams-norm-mstar-def}
m_\star(\gamma,\delta;r_\star)
:=\left\lceil \frac{\log(r_\star^{2})}{\log(|w_\rho|^{2})}\right\rceil,
\qquad
\delta=\tfrac12-\beta,
\end{equation}
then since \(|w_\rho|^2\in(0,1)\) one has \(\log(|w_\rho|^2)<0\), hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-adams-norm-mstar-radius}
|w_\rho|^{m_\star}\ \le\ r_\star.
\end{equation}
Moreover, when \(|\gamma|\to\infty\) with \(\delta\) bounded, using the first-order logarithmic expansion of
\(|w_\rho|^{2}=1-\frac{4\delta}{\gamma^{2}+(1+\delta)^{2}}\) one obtains the asymptotic
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-adams-norm-mstar-asymptotic}
\boxed{\;
m_\star(\gamma,\delta;r_\star)
\sim
\frac{\gamma^{2}+(1+\delta)^{2}}{4\delta}\,\log\!\frac{1}{r_\star^{2}}
\qquad(|\gamma|\to\infty).\;}
\end{equation}

\begin{corollary}[Fixed-radius defect certificate (scale exchange law)]\label{cor:xi-adams-norm-fixed-radius-jensen-certificate}
Take any \(R\in(r_\star,1)\).
If there exists an off-slice zero \(\rho=\beta+\mathrm{i}\gamma\) (\(0<\beta<\tfrac12\)) with corresponding disk zero \(w_\rho\), and one takes \(m_\star=m_\star(\gamma,\delta;r_\star)\) as in \eqref{eq:xi-adams-norm-mstar-def},
then \(\mathrm{Nm}_{m_\star}[F_\zeta]\) satisfies a deterministic lower bound on the Jensen defect (Definition \ref{def:app-jensen-defect}) at radius \(R\):
\[
D_{\mathrm{Nm}_{m_\star}[F_\zeta]}(R)\ \ge\ \log\!\frac{R}{|w_\rho|^{m_\star}}\ \ge\ \log\!\frac{R}{r_\star}\ >\ 0,
\]
thereby producing a positive defect lower bound at fixed radius \(R\) that is independent of the height parameter \(\gamma\); the cost is borne by the scale parameter \(m_\star\), which satisfies the quadratic growth law \eqref{eq:xi-adams-norm-mstar-asymptotic}.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem \ref{thm:xi-adams-norm-zero-transport}, \(F_\zeta(w_\rho)=0\) implies \(\mathrm{Nm}_{m_\star}[F_\zeta](w_\rho^{m_\star})=0\), and by \eqref{eq:xi-adams-norm-mstar-radius} one has \(|w_\rho^{m_\star}|\le r_\star<R\).
By the Jensen summation formula (Proposition \ref{prop:app-jensen-formula-defect}), any zero satisfying \(|a|<R\) contributes at least \(\log(R/|a|)\), hence
\[
D_{\mathrm{Nm}_{m_\star}[F_\zeta]}(R)\ \ge\ \log\!\frac{R}{|w_\rho|^{m_\star}}
\ \ge\ \log\!\frac{R}{r_\star}.
\]
\qedhere
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Integer-coefficient alignment with Chebyshev--Adams: algebraic closure of the scale gate}
It has been shown in the main text that under half-angle completion coordinates, the power semigroup \(u\mapsto u^m\) is equivalent in invariant trace coordinates to the integer-coefficient polynomial action \(S\mapsto C_m(S)\) (Remark \ref{rem:xi-half-angle-endpoint-paradigm}).
Therefore \(\pi_m\) is not a purely analytic device but is isomorphic to the Chebyshev--Adams dynamics in invariant coordinates: the scale parameter \(m\) can be addressed with integer coefficients in the \(\ZZ\)-structure.
In this sense, the fold gate \(\mathrm{Nm}_m\) can be viewed as the multiplicative pushforward along \(\pi_m\): it folds the branch degrees of freedom of the \(m\)-fold covering into a single-valued object, enabling point defects in the endpoint boundary layer to be transported to a prescribed compact subdisk and made manifest at a fixed radius via Jensen defect certificates (Corollary \ref{cor:xi-adams-norm-fixed-radius-jensen-certificate}).

\paragraph{Root-free audit interface and endpoint four-channel closure (minimal introduction of contravariant fiber)}
Under the Cayley rational parametrization, the power action on half-angle variables can be lifted to purely rational/integer-coefficient operations: parametrize the half-angle variable by
\(
r=\iota(x)=(1+\mathrm{i}x)/(1-\mathrm{i}x)
\),
then the completion trace coordinate \(S=r+r^{-1}\) yields the fully rational closed form \(S(x)=2(1-x^{2})/(1+x^{2})\), and there exists an integer-coefficient recursion \(x\mapsto\tau_d(x)\) such that \(C_d(S(x))=S(\tau_d(x))\) (Proposition \ref{prop:chebyshev-adams-fully-rational}).
Therefore, unitary-slice certificate constructions aligned with \(S\mapsto C_d(S)\) can be implemented at the integer level as a ``no trigonometric functions, no algebraic radicals'' computable audit interface.
\par
On the other hand, if the endpoint branch memory is to be lifted from the three-state ejection to a full \(\ZZ/4\) channel, one must introduce a minimal inversion contravariant fiber coordinate; to avoid confusion with the off-slice offset \(\delta=\tfrac12-\beta\) in this section, this is denoted
\(
\delta_{\mathrm{anti}}:=r-r^{-1}
\).
The appendix provides the exact minimal closure and the companion identity:
\(
\mathcal A^{-}=\delta_{\mathrm{anti}}\ZZ[S]
\)
and
\(
D_d(S,\delta_{\mathrm{anti}})=\delta_{\mathrm{anti}}\,U_{d-1}(S/2)
\)
(Remark \ref{rem:endpoint-inv-antiinv-min-closure}, Proposition \ref{prop:endpoint-odd-chebyshev-identity}), thereby producing the discrete index \(U_{d-1}(0)=\sin(d\pi/2)\) at the endpoint branch \(r=\pm\mathrm{i}\) and realizing the minimal \(\chi_4\)-type four-channel readout (Theorem \ref{thm:endpoint-four-channel-chi4}).

\paragraph{Multi-scale Toeplitz--PSD derived hierarchy: from Toeplitz--PSD to Adams--Toeplitz--PSD}
By Appendix Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-toeplitz-lmi}, if \(\mathscr{C}(w)=c_0+2\sum_{n\ge 1}c_n w^n\) belongs to the Carath\'eodory class, then there exists a positive measure \(\mu\) such that
\(
c_n=\int_{\TT}\xi^n\,d\mu(\xi)
\).
For each \(m\ge 1\), define the pushforward measure \(\mu_m:=(\xi\mapsto \xi^m)_*\mu\); its moments satisfy
\[
c^{(m)}_n:=\int_{\TT}\xi^n\,d\mu_m(\xi)=\int_{\TT}(\xi^m)^n\,d\mu(\xi)=c_{mn}.
\]
Therefore, for any \(N,m\ge 1\), the sparse Toeplitz principal submatrix family
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-adams-toeplitz-psd}
\boxed{\;
\mathbf{T}^{(m)}_N
:=\bigl(c_{m(j-k)}\bigr)_{0\le j,k\le N}\ \succeq\ 0\qquad(\forall\,N,m)\;}
\end{equation}
holds automatically.
This derived hierarchy couples endpoint compression directly with the externalized scale parameter \(m\): while preserving the positivity certificate caliber, it forms a family of multi-scale PSD constraints whose resolution can be selected via \(m\).

\begin{theorem}[Scaled theorem of the symmetry--auditability tension (scale exchange law)]\label{thm:xi-symmetry-audit-tension-scaled}
Under the self-dual completion and fixed-slice regime, the disk image \(w_\rho\) of an off-slice point defect \(\rho=\beta+\mathrm{i}\gamma\) is structurally compressed into the endpoint boundary layer by the Cayley--Busemann mechanism and satisfies the quadratic radial compression law of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offcritical-quadratic-radial-compression}.
The Adams--Norm fold gate \(\mathrm{Nm}_m\) along the power covering \(\pi_m(\omega)=\omega^m\) can transport point defects from this endpoint boundary layer to any prescribed compact subdisk: for any \(r_\star\in(0,1)\) there exists an explicit scale \(m_\star\) such that \(|w_\rho|^{m_\star}\le r_\star\) and \(\mathrm{Nm}_{m_\star}[F_\zeta]\) produces a height-independent positive Jensen defect lower bound at fixed radius \(R\in(r_\star,1)\) (Corollary \ref{cor:xi-adams-norm-fixed-radius-jensen-certificate}).
This scale gate is isomorphic to the integer-coefficient Chebyshev--Adams operation in invariant coordinates, and induces a multi-scale derived PSD hierarchy \eqref{eq:xi-adams-toeplitz-psd} compatible with the Toeplitz--PSD interface.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Linear gain in the endpoint channel: scale amplification of \texorpdfstring{$\Phi_{-1}$}{Phi-1} \texorpdfstring{$\perp$}{orthogonal to} Jensen reparametrization of radius}
It should be emphasized that under the power pullback \(F^{(m)}(w)=F(w^m)\), the Jensen defect undergoes only a deterministic reparametrization
\(
D_{F^{(m)}}(\varrho)=D_F(\varrho^m)
\)
(Proposition \ref{prop:app-jensen-defect-power-covariance}), so this operation itself does not produce new radial-average certificate gain.
In contrast, under the caliber where ``offset is carried solely by Blaschke data,'' the endpoint absorption coefficient \(\Phi_{-1}(B)\) (Proposition \ref{prop:xi-endpoint-absorption-coefficient}) satisfies a linear rescaling under the odd Adams gate
\(
B^{(m)}(w)=B(w^m)
\):
\(
\Phi_{-1}(B^{(m)})=m\Phi_{-1}(B)
\)
(Theorem \ref{thm:xi-endpoint-absorption-adams-rescaling}).
Therefore, the same scale parameter \(m\) merely reparametrizes the observation radius in the radial-average channel, while providing verifiable linear amplification in the endpoint channel; this contrast is compatible with the ``scale exchange law'' caliber of this subsection and provides an endpoint gain mechanism independent of the fixed-radius Jensen certificate for subsequent multi-scale audit chains.
