\section{Conclusion and Outlook}

This paper establishes a unified path from interval induction on one-dimensional rotations to algebraic constraints on higher-dimensional torus translations, and finally to the reflector--induction commutation template. The main conclusions show:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The return-time spectrum of interval windows has a rigid finite-value structure (at most three values); two-value degeneration provides an arithmetic criterion for induction preserving the rotation class.
  \item On two-value stable windows, the parameter update of induction can be written as an integer Möbius transformation and is equivalent to continued-fraction tail updates, thus organizing self-similar periodic points and quadratic irrationals into a unified $\mathrm{SL}_2(\ZZ)$ cocycle framework.
  \item The orbit closure dimension of higher-dimensional single-step translations is exactly controlled by the $\QQ$-linear rank; fixing a number field of low degree forces the closure dimension to decrease (quadratic fields limit to at most dimension 1).
  \item The reflector structure of factor systems provides categorical semantics for ``observable compression''; induction--reflection commutation admits a theorem statement under base-system closure and fiber-stability conditions.
\end{enumerate}

Further research directions include: classification of stable window families under more general windows (finite arc unions, fractal boundary sets); multi-dimensional continued fractions and $S$-adic structure in $\TT^d$ codings; and commutation theory on higher-order characteristic factors.
