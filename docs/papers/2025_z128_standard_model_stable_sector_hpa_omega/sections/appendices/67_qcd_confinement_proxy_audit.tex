\section{QCD confinement proxy from finite Wilson-loop diagnostics (audit)}
\label{app:qcd_confinement_proxy_audit}

This appendix records an \emph{audit-only} confinement proxy constructed from the finite holonomy diagnostics already used to close the continuum Yang--Mills representative (Appendix~\ref{app:continuum_yang_mills_from_holonomy}).
It does \emph{not} claim a nonperturbative confinement mechanism or a Yang--Mills mass gap.

\paragraph{Input.}
We use the loop-scale Wilson-loop style diagnostics $W=\frac13\RePart\Tr(Q)$ defined in \eqref{eq:wilson_loop_style_W} (Appendix~\ref{app:continuum_yang_mills_from_holonomy}), summarized for $k\times k$ square loops on the $n=3$ grid in Table~\ref{tab:holonomy_wilson_loop}.

\paragraph{Proxy model (area/perimeter).}
\AuditTag In standard lattice gauge theory, an area law has the schematic form $\langle W(C)\rangle\sim \exp(-\sigma\,\mathrm{Area}(C))$ (with perimeter corrections).
To keep the audit fully finite and to avoid introducing continuous fit freedom, we adopt the bounded two-parameter proxy
\[
|W_k|\approx \exp(-\sigma k^2-\mu k),
\]
where $k^2$ is the square-loop area in plaquette units and $k$ is a perimeter proxy.

\begin{proposition}[Finite confinement proxy selection (audit)]\label{prop:qcd_confinement_proxy_selection}
\AuditTag Let $|W_k|$ denote the absolute value of the mean Wilson-loop diagnostic at loop size $k$, extracted from Table~\ref{tab:holonomy_wilson_loop} for $k\in\{1,\dots,6\}$.
Within the bounded rational candidate family $\sigma=p/q$, $\mu=r/s$ with $1\le q,s\le 20$ and $0\le p,r\le 40$, selecting the unique minimizer of the mean absolute log mismatch
\[
E(\sigma,\mu):=\frac{1}{6}\sum_{k=1}^6\left|\log\!\left(\frac{\exp(-\sigma k^2-\mu k)}{|W_k|}\right)\right|
\]
with deterministic tie-break rules (smaller denominators, then lexicographic numerators) yields a stabilized proxy pair $(\widehat\sigma,\widehat\mu)$ reproduced by the generator script \texttt{scripts/exp\_qcd\_confinement\_proxy\_audit.py}.
\end{proposition}
