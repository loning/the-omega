\subsubsection{Three Orthogonal Auditable Structure Chains for Extreme Resonance: Carath\'eodory--Toeplitz, Endpoint Horocycle, and Finite-Type Hilbert--P{\'o}lya}\label{subsubsec:xi-extreme-resonance-orthogonal-audit-chains}
This paragraph reformulates ``extreme resonance'' of RH within the disk-completed horizon interface as three mutually orthogonal and auditable structure chains.
The term ``symmetry'' in the present framework does not refer to coordinate-axis interchange, but rather to the automorphisms of the completed projection system and their fixed slices (see \S\ref{subsubsec:ramanujan-phase-lift-symmetry} and \S\ref{subsubsec:selfdual-blaschke-defect-fixed-slice} for the explanation).
In this context, ``extreme resonance'' is strictly partitioned into two mutually exclusive phenomena:
disk-interior poles (true resonances) within the stability domain break horizon positivity and produce falsifiers at finite levels;
while in the RH phase, the only admissible singularities degenerate to threshold loading on fixed slices (boundary spectral loading).

\paragraph{Three-Chain Summary (Structural Reduction)}
Following the notation of Appendix \ref{subsubsec:app-horizon-weyl-carath-toeplitz} and the endpoint geometry \ref{par:app-horocycle-busemann}, ``extreme resonance'' can be compressed into the following three chains:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Positive real part of the horizon log-derivative (Carath\'eodory) \(\Longleftrightarrow\) Toeplitz--PSD hierarchy.}
  RH is equivalent to \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) being analytic in \(\mathbb{D}\) with \(\Re \mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\ge 0\), and is strictly equivalent to \(\{\mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\}_{N\ge 0}\) (Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-toeplitz-lmi}).
  \item \textbf{The endpoint \(\boldsymbol{-1}\) Busemann/horocycle coordinates realize the off-critical-line deviation and provide second-order suppression.}
  If there exists an off-critical-line zero \(\rho=\beta+\mathrm{i}\gamma\) (\(\beta<\tfrac12\), \(\delta=\tfrac12-\beta>0\)), then its disk image \(w_\rho=\mathcal{C}(\gamma+\mathrm{i}\delta)\) satisfies the closed-form compression law
  \(\delta=\mathcal{B}_{-1}(w_\rho)=\frac{1-|w_\rho|^2}{|1+w_\rho|^2}\) and
  \(1-|w_\rho|^2=\frac{4\delta}{\gamma^2+(1+\delta)^2}\), and lies on the endpoint horocycle layer \(\mathcal{H}_\delta\) (Corollary \ref{cor:app-offcritical-horocycle-busemann}; see also Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offcritical-quadratic-radial-compression}).
  \item \textbf{Finite-type Hilbert--P{\'o}lya: finite-window zero structure \(\Longleftrightarrow\) unitarity constraints and certification of CMV/Schur parameters.}
  In the finite-window model, the \(\Xi\)-RH-type ``unit circle root condition'' can be equivalently written as a CMV-like unitary spectral realization, and is provided with an auditable interface through the explicit Verblunsky/Schur parameter constraints \(|\alpha_k|<1\) (with the terminal coefficient on the unit circle) (Proposition \ref{prop:xi-hilbert-polya-cmv}); root intervals can be certified by Sturm sequences and interval arithmetic (Remark \ref{rem:xi-hard-zero-certificate}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Jet--PSD Equivalence: RH Is Equivalent to an Infinite Toeplitz--PSD Inequality Tower}
Appendix \ref{subsubsec:app-horizon-weyl-carath-toeplitz} has established that the Carath\'eodory positivity of \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) is strictly equivalent to the Toeplitz--PSD hierarchy (Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-toeplitz-lmi}), and that the coefficient data can be completely locally encapsulated into the jet at \(s=-\tfrac12\):
\(\ell_k=\left.\frac{d^k}{ds^k}\log\Xi_{\zeta}(s)\right|_{s=-1/2}\) (Proposition \ref{prop:app-horizon-coeff-local}).
Consequently, a ``true resonance'' (disk-interior pole) not only breaks positivity but must necessarily trigger a PSD failure witness at some finite level (by the completeness chain \((2)\Rightarrow(3)\Rightarrow(2)\) of Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-toeplitz-lmi}).

\begin{theorem}[Jet--PSD Equivalence: Semi-Algebraic Reduction of RH to a Single Point]\label{thm:xi-jet-psd-equivalence}
Let \(\ell_k=\left.\frac{d^k}{ds^k}\log\Xi_{\zeta}(s)\right|_{s=-1/2}\in\RR\).
By Proposition \ref{prop:app-horizon-coeff-local}, for each \(N\ge 0\) there exists a finite-dimensional Toeplitz principal submatrix explicitly generated by \((\ell_1,\dots,\ell_{N+1})\):
\[
\mathbf{T}_N(\ell):=(c_{j-k}(\ell))_{0\le j,k\le N},
\]
where \((c_0(\ell),\dots,c_N(\ell))\) are the power series moments of \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) (horizon multipole moments).
Then the following statements are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item RH holds;
  \item \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) is analytic on \(\mathbb{D}\) with \(\Re\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}(w)\ge 0\) (Carath\'eodory);
  \item \(\mathbf{T}_N(\ell)\succeq 0\) for all \(N\ge 0\) (infinite PSD inequality tower).
\end{enumerate}
Therefore, RH can be strictly compressed into a family of infinite semi-algebraic cone constraints determined by the single-point jet \(\{\ell_k\}\); any ``extreme resonance'' (disk-interior pole) must trigger an auditable failure witness \(\mathbf{T}_N(\ell)\nsucceq 0\) at some finite level \(N\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\((1)\Leftrightarrow(2)\Leftrightarrow(3)\) is given by Appendix Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-toeplitz-lmi};
\(\mathbf{T}_N(\ell)\) is obtained from Proposition \ref{prop:app-horizon-coeff-local}, which locally encapsulates the coefficients of \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) as explicit expressions in \((\ell_1,\dots,\ell_{N+1})\). \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Low-Order Necessary Inequalities (Example)]\label{cor:xi-jet-low-order-inequalities}
Under the setting of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-jet-psd-equivalence}, if RH holds then necessarily
\[
\ell_1\le 0,\qquad
\ell_1^{2}-\ell_2^{2}\ge 0,\qquad
\ell_1^{2}-(\ell_2-\ell_3)^{2}\ge 0,
\]
and the third-order Toeplitz principal minor condition \(\det\mathbf{T}_2(\ell)\ge 0\) is equivalent to
\[
\boxed{\;
\det\mathbf{T}_2(\ell)
=-(\ell_1+\ell_2-\ell_3)\bigl(\ell_1^2-\ell_1\ell_2+\ell_1\ell_3-2\ell_2^2\bigr)\ge 0\; }.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By the first few explicit expressions from Proposition \ref{prop:app-horizon-coeff-local}:
\(
c_0=-\ell_1,\ c_1=-\ell_2,\ c_2=\ell_2-\ell_3,\ c_3=-\ell_2+2\ell_3-\tfrac23\ell_4
\).
The Toeplitz--PSD condition \(\mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\) implies all principal minors are non-negative;
\(\mathbf{T}_0=[c_0]\) gives \(\ell_1\le 0\), \(\det\mathbf{T}_1=c_0^2-c_1^2\) gives \(\ell_1^2-\ell_2^2\ge 0\), and the \(\{0,2\}\) principal minor gives \(c_0^2-c_2^2\ge 0\), i.e., \(\ell_1^2-(\ell_2-\ell_3)^2\ge 0\).
Finally, for \(\mathbf{T}_2=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}c_0&c_1&c_2\\ c_1&c_0&c_1\\ c_2&c_1&c_0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\), direct substitution and simplification yield the displayed factorization. \qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Absolute Convergence Patch and Prime Moment Interface: Each PSD Constraint Can Be Audited Term by Term}
In the subdisk \(|w|<1/3\), \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) can be expressed via the functional equation as a uniformly absolutely convergent Euler product/Dirichlet series patch (Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-euler-patch}), so that for any fixed truncation order, the moment data \((c_0,\dots,c_N)\) can be audited term by term and generated through the absolutely convergent prime moment interface (Corollary \ref{cor:app-horizon-prime-moment-interface}).
Consequently, each finite-level Toeplitz--PSD condition can be viewed either as a finite-dimensional feasibility constraint asserting positivity of the spectral measure, or as a semi-algebraic cone constraint on the absolutely convergent prime moments.

\begin{theorem}[Prime Moment Semi-Algebraic Cone: Absolute Convergence Formulation of Each PSD Constraint]\label{thm:xi-prime-moment-semialgebraic-cone}
For \(k\ge 0\), define the absolutely convergent prime moments (von Mangoldt moments)
\[
M_k:=\sum_{n\ge 2}\frac{\Lambda(n)(\log n)^k}{n^{3/2}}\qquad(k\ge 0),
\]
and fix \(N\ge 0\).
Then there exist an integer \(K_N\) and a finite family of explicit polynomial inequalities
\(\mathcal{P}_{N,j}(M_0,\dots,M_{K_N})\ge 0\), such that positive semi-definiteness of the Toeplitz principal submatrix
\(\mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\)
is equivalent to all these inequalities holding simultaneously.
Therefore, RH (equivalent to \(\mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\) for all \(N\)) can be understood as membership in an infinite semi-algebraic cone generated by the absolutely convergent prime moments \(\{M_k\}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-euler-patch} and Corollary \ref{cor:app-horizon-prime-moment-interface}, for each fixed \(N\), one can differentiate term by term within the \(|w|<1/3\) patch to obtain \(c_0,\dots,c_N\) as explicit rational linear combinations of finitely many \(M_k\) and constant terms (arising from \(\log\pi\), digamma terms, and rational expansions).
On the other hand, \(\mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\) if and only if all its principal minors are non-negative; each minor is an integer-coefficient polynomial in \(c_0,\dots,c_N\), and substituting the above linear expressions yields the finite family \(\mathcal{P}_{N,j}\). \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral Margin-Driven Finite-Order Toeplitz--PSD Certificate]\label{thm:xi-spectral-margin-finite-psd-certificate}
Fix \(N\ge 0\) and \(r<1/3\).
Suppose that by term-by-term differentiation and truncation of the absolutely convergent patch (Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-euler-patch}) for \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}(w)=c_0+2\sum_{n\ge 1}c_n w^n\) on \(|w|\le r\), one obtains coefficient approximations \(\widehat c_0,\dots,\widehat c_N\), and assembles the Hermitian approximate Toeplitz matrix with \(\widehat c_{-n}:=\overline{\widehat c_n}\):
\[
\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N:=(\widehat c_{j-k})_{0\le j,k\le N}.
\]
If there exists an auditable spectral margin \(\lambda_{\min}(\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N)\ge \eta>0\), and the truncation error satisfies
\[
\bigl\|\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N-\mathbf{T}_N\bigr\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le \eta/2,
\]
then necessarily \(\mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\) (so the \(N\)-th level Toeplitz--PSD certificate holds).
\par
Moreover, if the coefficient errors satisfy \(\max_{0\le n\le N}|\widehat c_n-c_n|\le \varepsilon\), then there is an explicit operator norm bound
\[
\bigl\|\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N-\mathbf{T}_N\bigr\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\le \sum_{k=-N}^{N}|\widehat c_k-c_k|
\le (2N+1)\varepsilon.
\]
Therefore, verification of the finite-order PSD can be reduced to: computing \(\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N\) in the \(|w|<1/3\) absolutely convergent domain, providing the spectral margin \(\eta\), and giving auditable truncation error bounds for the analytic and arithmetic tail terms.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By the Weyl eigenvalue perturbation inequality,
\(
\lambda_{\min}(\mathbf{T}_N)\ge \lambda_{\min}(\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N)-\|\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N-\mathbf{T}_N\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\ge \eta/2>0
\), so \(\mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\).
For the second part, let \(\Delta_N:=\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N-\mathbf{T}_N\).
The Toeplitz structure yields the row absolute sum bound
\(
\|\Delta_N\|_{\infty}\le \sum_{k=-N}^{N}|\widehat c_k-c_k|
\), and similarly \(\|\Delta_N\|_{1}\le \sum_{k=-N}^{N}|\widehat c_k-c_k|\).
Therefore
\(
\|\Delta_N\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le \sqrt{\|\Delta_N\|_1\|\Delta_N\|_\infty}\le \sum_{k=-N}^{N}|\widehat c_k-c_k|
\le (2N+1)\varepsilon
\).
\qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Endpoint Geometry and Second-Order Suppression: Off-Line Deviation Appears via a Quadratic Radial Channel at the Endpoint Layer}
The exponential Busemann/Poisson coordinate at the endpoint \(-1\),
\(\mathcal{B}_{-1}(w)=\Im(\mathcal{C}^{-1}(w))=\frac{1-|w|^2}{|1+w|^2}\),
provides the endpoint horocycle foliation (Theorem \ref{thm:app-horocycle-foliation-minus1}).
Corollary \ref{cor:app-offcritical-horocycle-busemann} shows that the off-critical-line deviation \(\delta\) is not a geometric effect of ``axis interchange,'' but rather an endpoint layer label; and the radial gap satisfies a strict quadratic suppression
\(1-|w_\rho|^2=\delta\,|1+w_\rho|^2\), so that it is compressed into a thin endpoint layer as \(|\gamma|\to\infty\).
This structure chain is compatible with the symmetry translation of ``boundary unitarization/inner function completion \(\Rightarrow\) exclusion of disk-interior Blaschke data'' (see the juxtaposition paragraph in \S\ref{subsubsec:selfdual-blaschke-defect-fixed-slice} referencing the closed-form compression law of \S\ref{par:app-horocycle-busemann}).

\begin{theorem}[Endpoint Compression Law and Precision Potential Attribution]\label{thm:xi-endpoint-compression-precision}
If there exists an off-critical-line zero \(\rho=\beta+\mathrm{i}\gamma\) (\(\beta<\tfrac12\)) and one sets \(\delta:=\tfrac12-\beta>0\), then its disk image \(w_\rho=\mathcal{C}(\gamma+\mathrm{i}\delta)\in\mathbb{D}\) satisfies the fully closed-form identities
\[
\boxed{\;
\delta=\mathcal{B}_{-1}(w_\rho)=\frac{1-|w_\rho|^2}{|1+w_\rho|^2},\qquad
|1+w_\rho|^2=\frac{4}{\gamma^2+(1+\delta)^2},\qquad
1-|w_\rho|^2=\delta\,|1+w_\rho|^2\;}
\]
so that the off-line deviation falls precisely on the endpoint horocycle layer \(\mathcal{H}_\delta\) and enters the disk radius gap via a quadratic radial term.
Furthermore, let the precision potential be \(\mathcal{P}(x)=\log\pi+\log(1+x^2)\) (Definition \ref{def:unit-circle-phase-precision-potential}); then in the regime \(|\gamma|\to\infty\) with \(\delta\) bounded,
\[
1-|w_\rho|^2\sim 4\pi\,\delta\,e^{-\mathcal{P}(\gamma)},
\]
so that the ``invisibility of extreme resonance'' can be strictly attributed within the framework to the second-order suppression in endpoint coordinates and exponential compression by the precision potential, rather than to numerical error.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The closed-form identities are established in Corollary \ref{cor:app-offcritical-horocycle-busemann} and Theorem \ref{thm:xi-offcritical-quadratic-radial-compression};
the precision potential form is given in the same theorem's precision potential attribution clause and Appendix Remark \ref{rem:app-offcritical-radius-precision-potential}. \qed
\end{proof}

In the finite-window model, the critical-line ``threshold resonance'' (unit circle root structure) can be equivalently formulated as a CMV-like unitary spectral realization with explicit Schur/Verblunsky parameter constraints:

\mathrm{Roots}(\widetilde\Phi_L)\subseteq\TT
\Longleftrightarrow
U_L^{\mathrm{CMV}}\ \text{is unitary}
\Longleftrightarrow
|\alpha_k(L)|<1\ \ (k< D-1),\ \ |\alpha_{D-1}(L)|=1

Root intervals can be certified via Sturm sequences and interval arithmetic to provide fully formalized certificates (Remark \ref{rem:xi-hard-zero-certificate}), and the Cayley transform merges this unitary spectral realization with the self-adjoint spectral realization into a single object (Remark \ref{rem:xi-cmv-inverse-spectral}).

\end{proof}

\paragraph{Endpoint Heat-Kernel Probe: Monotone Extraction of Endpoint Atomic Mass from Finite Toeplitz Data}
This paragraph introduces an endpoint-focused probe fully compatible with the Toeplitz--PSD hierarchy, which compresses ``endpoint threshold loading'' into a sequence of non-negative numbers directly computable from finite truncated matrices.

\begin{definition}[Endpoint Binomial Probe and Heat-Kernel Quantity]\label{def:xi-endpoint-binomial-probe}
Let \(\mathscr{C}(w)=c_0+2\sum_{n\ge 1}c_n w^n\) be analytic in a neighborhood of \(w=0\), and set \(c_{-n}:=\overline{c_n}\) so that the Toeplitz matrix is Hermitian.
For each \(N\ge 0\), define the Toeplitz principal submatrix
\[
\mathbf{T}_N:=(c_{j-k})_{0\le j,k\le N}.
\]
Define the binomial vector \(b^{(N)}\in\RR^{N+1}\) and the endpoint heat-kernel quantity \(a_N\) by
\[
b^{(N)}_k:=(-1)^k\binom{N}{k}\qquad(0\le k\le N),
\qquad
\boxed{\ a_N:=\frac{(b^{(N)})^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{T}_N\,b^{(N)}}{4^N}\ }.
\]
\par
When \(\mathscr{C}\) belongs to the Carath\'eodory class, there exists a unique finite positive measure \(\mu\) such that \(c_n=\int_{\TT}\xi^{-n}\,d\mu(\xi)\), and \(\mathbf{T}_N\) is the Gram matrix of \(\{1,\xi,\dots,\xi^N\}\subset L^2(\mu)\) (Appendix \ref{subsubsec:app-horizon-weyl-carath-toeplitz}).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Endpoint Heat-Kernel Identity and Monotone Limit]\label{thm:xi-endpoint-heat-kernel-probe}
Under the setting of Definition \ref{def:xi-endpoint-binomial-probe}, further assume that \(\mathscr{C}\) belongs to the Carath\'eodory class,
so that there exists a unique finite positive measure \(\mu\) with \(c_n=\int_{\TT}\xi^{-n}\,d\mu(\xi)\) (equivalently, the Toeplitz--PSD hierarchy \(\mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\) holds for all \(N\); see Appendix \ref{subsubsec:app-horizon-weyl-carath-toeplitz}).
Then the following strict identity holds:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-endpoint-heat-kernel-identity}
\boxed{\;
a_N=\int_{\TT}\left|\frac{1-\xi}{2}\right|^{2N}\,d\mu(\xi)\ge 0\qquad(N\ge 0).
\;}
\end{equation}
Moreover, the sequence \(\{a_N\}_{N\ge 0}\) is monotonically decreasing and converges to the endpoint atomic mass:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-endpoint-heat-kernel-limit}
\boxed{\;
a_N\downarrow \mu(\{-1\})\qquad(N\to\infty).
\;}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let \(p_N(\xi):=\sum_{k=0}^{N}b^{(N)}_k\xi^k=(1-\xi)^N\).
Since \(\mathbf{T}_N\) is the Gram matrix of \(\{1,\xi,\dots,\xi^N\}\subset L^2(\mu)\), one has
\[
(b^{(N)})^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{T}_N b^{(N)}
=\sum_{j,k=0}^{N}b^{(N)}_j b^{(N)}_k\,c_{j-k}
=\int_{\TT}\left|\sum_{k=0}^{N}b^{(N)}_k\xi^k\right|^{2}\,d\mu(\xi)
=\int_{\TT}|1-\xi|^{2N}\,d\mu(\xi),
\]
and dividing both sides by \(4^N\) yields \eqref{eq:xi-endpoint-heat-kernel-identity}.
For \(\xi\in\TT\setminus\{-1\}\), \(\left|\frac{1-\xi}{2}\right|<1\), so the integrand decreases monotonically in \(N\) and converges pointwise to \(0\); at \(\xi=-1\) it is identically \(1\).
The monotone convergence theorem then gives \(a_N\downarrow \mu(\{-1\})\), establishing both monotonicity and non-negativity.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exponential Error Bound for Endpoint Atom Extraction]\label{cor:xi-endpoint-heat-kernel-exp-error}
Under the setting of Theorem \ref{thm:xi-endpoint-heat-kernel-probe}, take any closed set \(K\subset\TT\setminus\{-1\}\) and let
\[
q(K):=\sup_{\xi\in K}\left|\frac{1-\xi}{2}\right|\in(0,1).
\]
Then for all \(N\ge 0\),
\[
0\le a_N-\mu(\{-1\})
\le q(K)^{2N}\,\mu(K)+\mu\!\bigl(\TT\setminus(\{-1\}\cup K)\bigr).
\]
In particular, if \(\mu(\TT\setminus(\{-1\}\cup K))=0\), then
\(
0\le a_N-\mu(\{-1\})\le q(K)^{2N}\mu(K)
\), so that when there is a spectral gap away from the endpoint neighborhood (or when the support is controlled), the endpoint atom can be extracted from \(a_N\) at exponential speed.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By \eqref{eq:xi-endpoint-heat-kernel-identity},
\(
a_N-\mu(\{-1\})=\int_{\TT\setminus\{-1\}}\left|\frac{1-\xi}{2}\right|^{2N}\,d\mu(\xi)
\).
Decomposing the integral over \(K\) and its complement, and noting that on \(K\) the integrand is \(\le q(K)^{2N}\) while on the complement it is \(\le 1\), yields the stated inequality. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Resolution Depth and One-Parameter Readout]\label{rem:xi-endpoint-probe-resolution-depth}
The sequence \(a_N\) depends only on the finite truncated Toeplitz data \(\mathbf{T}_N\), yet its kernel function \(\left|\frac{1-\xi}{2}\right|^{2N}\) forms an increasingly sharp focus at the endpoint \(\xi=-1\) as \(N\) grows; thus \(N\) serves as a \emph{resolution depth} in the sense of endpoint spectral extraction.
This phenomenon is consistent with the regime semantics of the present paper where ``the unitary slice serves as a one-parameter visible readout, with refinement measured by depth rather than additional continuous degrees of freedom'': when no external orthogonal register is adjoined, off-slice information is forced to degrade to a readout through the endpoint thin layer and finite-depth probes (see Definition \ref{def:multi_axis_address} for the multi-axis prefix regime and \S\ref{subsec:xi-pw-type-safety-null} for the regime-level interpretation of \(\mathsf{NULL}\)).
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Structural Reduction Theorem: Closed-Chain Consequences of Finite-Order Toeplitz Failure]\label{thm:xi-toeplitz-failure-closed-chain}
Within the same disk-completed horizon interface, any finite-level Toeplitz--PSD failure \(\mathbf{T}_N\nsucceq 0\) can be equivalently organized into a closed chain: it produces a true resonance index \(\kappa_N>0\) at the finite window and reconstructs resonance candidates inside \(\mathbb{D}\) via the Toeplitz pencil; these are then given closed-form finite-window readouts of the off-critical-line deviation \(\delta\) through the endpoint \(-1\) Busemann/horocycle coordinates; meanwhile, constant-layer strictification (pure constant counterterm) does not alter \(\kappa_N\), thereby completing -- without introducing any additional continuous axis -- the strict reduction from ``finite falsification certificate'' to ``resonance candidate -- endpoint depth -- strictification-invariant decomposition'' (Theorems \ref{thm:xi-finite-window-true-resonance-index}--\ref{thm:xi-counterterm-invariance-kappa} and Theorem \ref{thm:xi-finite-witness-to-offline-fiber}).
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Negative Inertia Sequence: Finite-Window True Resonance Index}
Following the notation \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}(w)=c_0+2\sum_{n\ge 1}c_n w^n\) and Toeplitz principal submatrix \(\mathbf{T}_N=(c_{j-k})_{0\le j,k\le N}\),
for a Hermitian matrix \(A\) denote its negative inertia by
\[
\nu_-(A):=\#\{\lambda<0:\ \lambda\ \text{is an eigenvalue of}\ A\}\qquad(\text{counted with algebraic multiplicity}).
\]
Define the finite-window true resonance index (negative inertia sequence)
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-kappaN-def}
\boxed{\ \kappa_N:=\nu_-\!\bigl(\mathbf{T}_N\bigr)\qquad(N\ge 0).\ }
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-Window True Resonance Index: Monotonicity and RH Equivalence]\label{thm:xi-finite-window-true-resonance-index}
Let \(\kappa_N\) be as defined in \eqref{eq:xi-kappaN-def}. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Monotonicity)} \(\kappa_N\) is monotonically non-decreasing in \(N\);
  \item \textbf{(Finite falsifier)} If RH does not hold (equivalently, \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) has a pole inside \(\mathbb{D}\)), then there exists \(N\) with \(\kappa_N\ge 1\);
  \item \textbf{(Equivalence characterization)} \(\kappa_N\equiv 0\) if and only if RH holds.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(1) By the Cauchy interlacing theorem, eigenvalues of a Hermitian leading principal submatrix interlace upon extension, so the count of negative eigenvalues cannot decrease; hence \(\kappa_N\) is non-decreasing.
\par
(2)--(3) By Appendix Theorem \ref{thm:app-horizon-toeplitz-lmi}, RH \(\Longleftrightarrow\ \mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\) for all \(N\).
And \(\mathbf{T}_N\succeq 0\) if and only if it has no negative eigenvalues, i.e., \(\nu_-(\mathbf{T}_N)=0\).
Therefore \(\kappa_N\equiv 0\) if and only if RH holds; its negation is equivalent to the existence of some level with a negative eigenvalue, i.e., \(\kappa_N\ge 1\). \qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Finite-Window Resonance Reconstruction: Generalized Spectrum of the Toeplitz Pencil Generates Candidate Poles}
To promote the falsifier from a ``failure witness'' to a ``candidate point generation mechanism,'' introduce the first-order shifted Toeplitz matrix
\[
\mathbf{T}_N^{(1)}:=(c_{j-k+1})_{0\le j,k\le N}.
\]
When \(\mathbf{T}_N\) is invertible, define the generalized eigenvalue set of the matrix pencil (counted with algebraic multiplicity):
\[
\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{T}_N,\mathbf{T}_N^{(1)})
:=\{\lambda\in\CC:\ \det(\mathbf{T}_N-\lambda\,\mathbf{T}_N^{(1)})=0\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Resonance Pencil Reconstruction: Realization-Independent Readout of Finite-Window Candidate Poles]\label{thm:xi-resonance-pencil-reconstruction}
Suppose \(\mathbf{T}_N\) is invertible and \(\kappa_N=\kappa\ge 1\).
Then there exists a finite-window horizon function \(\widetilde{\mathscr{C}}_{N}\) satisfying:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Finite-window consistency)} \(\widetilde{\mathscr{C}}_{N}(w)=c_0+2\sum_{n=1}^{N}c_n w^n+O(w^{N+1})\) (i.e., its Maclaurin coefficients agree with those of \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) up to order \(\le N\));
  \item \textbf{(Disk-interior pole count)} \(\widetilde{\mathscr{C}}_{N}\) has exactly \(\kappa\) poles inside \(\mathbb{D}\) (counted with algebraic multiplicity);
  \item \textbf{(Pencil spectral readout)} The disk-interior pole set of \(\widetilde{\mathscr{C}}_{N}\) (with multiplicities) equals
  \(\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{T}_N,\mathbf{T}_N^{(1)})\cap\mathbb{D}\).
\end{enumerate}
Therefore, when \(\kappa_N>0\), the generalized spectrum of the matrix pencil \((\mathbf{T}_N,\mathbf{T}_N^{(1)})\) inside \(\mathbb{D}\) provides a set of ``true resonance candidates'' that can be verified offline.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
This result can be viewed as a finite negative-inertia version of ``truncated Carath\'eodory--Fej\'er completeness'': given a finite jet \((c_0,\dots,c_N)\), one can construct a rational/meromorphic interpolation solution in the class with minimal negative-inertia index \(\kappa=\nu_-(\mathbf{T}_N)\) that matches at \(w=0\) to order \(N\), with the number of disk-interior poles compressed to \(\kappa\).
In this construction, the pole locations are uniquely determined by the corresponding finite-dimensional ``shift consistency'' linear system; writing this system in generalized eigenvalue form yields \(\det(\mathbf{T}_N-\lambda \mathbf{T}_N^{(1)})=0\).
Hence the disk-interior pole set coincides with \(\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{T}_N,\mathbf{T}_N^{(1)})\cap\mathbb{D}\). \qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Closed-Form Finite-Window Readout of Horocycle Depth: Exponential Convergence from Endpoint Kernel Vector Ratios}
The Busemann/Poisson coordinate at the endpoint \(-1\) is
\(
\mathcal{B}_{-1}(a)=\frac{1-|a|^2}{|1+a|^2}
\) (\(a\in\mathbb{D}\)), whose level sets are horocycle inscribed circles (Theorem \ref{thm:app-horocycle-foliation-minus1}).
The following result provides a completely algebraic finite-window estimator: the endpoint depth can be obtained from inner-product ratios of finite-dimensional vectors with exponential convergence.

\begin{theorem}[Closed-Form Finite-Window Convergence Formula for Busemann Depth]\label{thm:xi-busemann-depth-finite-window-estimator}
For any \(N\ge 0\) and \(a\in\mathbb{D}\), define the endpoint kernel vector and geometric kernel vector
\[
u^{(N)}:=(1,-1,1,\dots,(-1)^N)^{\mathsf T}\in\RR^{N+1},
\qquad
v^{(N)}(a):=(1,a,a^2,\dots,a^N)^{\mathsf T}\in\CC^{N+1},
\]
and define the finite-window horocycle estimator via the standard Hermitian inner product \(\langle x,y\rangle:=\sum_{k=0}^{N}\overline{x_k}y_k\) and \(\|y\|_2^2:=\langle y,y\rangle\):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-busemann-estimator-def}
\boxed{\;
\mathcal{B}^{(N)}_{-1}(a):=\frac{\bigl|\langle u^{(N)},v^{(N)}(a)\rangle\bigr|^2}{\|v^{(N)}(a)\|_2^2}\; }.
\end{equation}
Then \(\mathcal{B}^{(N)}_{-1}(a)\) has the completely closed-form expression
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-busemann-estimator-closed}
\boxed{\;
\mathcal{B}^{(N)}_{-1}(a)
=\frac{1-|a|^2}{|1+a|^2}\cdot\frac{|1-(-a)^{N+1}|^2}{1-|a|^{2N+2}}
=\mathcal{B}_{-1}(a)\cdot\frac{|1-(-a)^{N+1}|^2}{1-|a|^{2N+2}}\; }.
\end{equation}
Furthermore,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-busemann-estimator-limit}
\boxed{\;
\lim_{N\to\infty}\mathcal{B}^{(N)}_{-1}(a)=\mathcal{B}_{-1}(a)\; }.
\end{equation}
Moreover, the error satisfies a uniform exponential bound: for all \(N\ge 0\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-busemann-estimator-error}
\boxed{\;
\bigl|\mathcal{B}^{(N)}_{-1}(a)-\mathcal{B}_{-1}(a)\bigr|
\le \frac{4}{|1+a|^2}\,|a|^{N+1}\; }.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By geometric series summation,
\[
\langle u^{(N)},v^{(N)}(a)\rangle=\sum_{k=0}^{N}(-1)^k a^k=\frac{1-(-a)^{N+1}}{1+a},
\qquad
\|v^{(N)}(a)\|_2^2=\sum_{k=0}^{N}|a|^{2k}=\frac{1-|a|^{2N+2}}{1-|a|^{2}}.
\]
Substituting into \eqref{eq:xi-busemann-estimator-def} yields the closed form \eqref{eq:xi-busemann-estimator-closed}.
Since \(|a|<1\), \((-a)^{N+1}\to 0\) and \(|a|^{2N+2}\to 0\), so \eqref{eq:xi-busemann-estimator-limit} follows immediately.
\par
For the error term, let \(z:=(-a)^{N+1}\) (so \(|z|=|a|^{N+1}\)); then
\[
\frac{|1-z|^2}{1-|z|^2}-1
=\frac{-2\Re z+2|z|^2}{1-|z|^2},
\qquad
\left|\frac{|1-z|^2}{1-|z|^2}-1\right|
\le \frac{2(|z|+|z|^2)}{1-|z|^2}
\le \frac{4|z|}{1-|a|^2}.
\]
Using \(\mathcal{B}_{-1}(a)=\frac{1-|a|^2}{|1+a|^2}\) gives
\[
\bigl|\mathcal{B}^{(N)}_{-1}(a)-\mathcal{B}_{-1}(a)\bigr|
=\mathcal{B}_{-1}(a)\left|\frac{|1-z|^2}{1-|z|^2}-1\right|
\le \frac{1-|a|^2}{|1+a|^2}\cdot \frac{4|a|^{N+1}}{1-|a|^2}
=\frac{4}{|1+a|^2}|a|^{N+1},
\]
which is \eqref{eq:xi-busemann-estimator-error}. \qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Orthogonality of Constant-Layer Strictification: The Counterterm Does Not Alter \texorpdfstring{$\kappa_N$}{kappaN}}
The strictification gate \(\mathrm{CT}_\eta\) in the main text is defined as a \emph{pure constant} counterterm: it preserves poles and the principal scale, applying only an additive shift to the finite-part drift (see Definition \ref{def:pom-finite-part-counterterm-gate} and Proposition \ref{prop:pom-counterterm-anom-cancel}).
In the horizon positivity semantics, this corresponds to applying a purely imaginary constant shift to the \(\mathscr{C}\)-function (leaving the Hermitian part unchanged), which therefore cannot alter the negative inertia sequence of the Toeplitz hierarchy.

\begin{theorem}[Invariance of Anomaly Cancellation: The True Resonance Index Is Unchanged]\label{thm:xi-counterterm-invariance-kappa}
Let the normalized power series of \(\mathscr{C}\) at \(w=0\) be
\(
\mathscr{C}(w)=c_0+2\sum_{n\ge 1}c_n w^n
\)
with \(c_{-n}:=\overline{c_n}\).
Define the Hermitianized moment sequence
\[
\widehat c_0:=\Re c_0,\qquad
\widehat c_n:=c_n\ (n\ge 1),\qquad
\widehat c_{-n}:=\overline{\widehat c_n},
\]
and the corresponding Toeplitz principal submatrix
\(
\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N:=(\widehat c_{j-k})_{0\le j,k\le N}
\).
If the strictification gate \(\mathrm{CT}_\eta\) corresponds at the horizon interface to
\(\mathscr{C}(w)\mapsto \mathscr{C}(w)+\mathrm{i}\eta\) (\(\eta\in\RR\)), and \(\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N^{(\eta)}\) denotes the Toeplitz principal submatrix generated by the \((\widehat c_n)\) after strictification, then for all \(N\ge 0\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi-counterterm-invariance-kappa}
\boxed{\;
\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N^{(\eta)}=\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N^{(0)},
\qquad
\nu_-\!\bigl(\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N^{(\eta)}\bigr)=\nu_-\!\bigl(\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N^{(0)}\bigr).\;}
\end{equation}
In the real-structure case treated in this paper (in particular \(\mathscr{C}=\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\)), one has \(c_0\in\RR\), so \(\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N=\mathbf{T}_N\) and it follows that \(\kappa_N\) is invariant.
Therefore, ``constant-layer anomaly'' and ``true resonance index \(\kappa_N\)'' are strictly orthogonal: the counterterm can strictify the exchange residual without being able to mask any stability-domain pole; if a true resonance exists, it must manifest through \(\kappa_N>0\) at some finite level.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Strictification \(\mathscr{C}\mapsto \mathscr{C}+\mathrm{i}\eta\) changes only the imaginary part of the constant term \(c_0\) and leaves \(c_n\) (\(n\ge 1\)) unchanged.
Consequently, the Hermitianized moment sequence \(\widehat c_n\) is invariant for all \(n\in\ZZ\), so the Toeplitz principal submatrix
\(\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_N=(\widehat c_{j-k})\) itself is invariant; its eigenvalues and negative inertia count are therefore unchanged, yielding \eqref{eq:xi-counterterm-invariance-kappa}. \qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Auditable Map from Finite Falsifier to Off-Line Zero Fiber}
Combining Theorems \ref{thm:xi-resonance-pencil-reconstruction}--\ref{thm:xi-busemann-depth-finite-window-estimator} with the endpoint horocycle realization
\(\delta=\mathcal{B}_{-1}(w_\rho)\) (Corollary \ref{cor:app-offcritical-horocycle-busemann}), one can promote a ``Toeplitz failure witness'' to a constructive readout of an ``off-line zero fiber.''

\begin{theorem}[Fiber Readout of the Finite Falsifier: From \texorpdfstring{$\mathbf{T}_N\nsucceq 0$}{T_N not PSD} to Candidate Off-Line Zeros]\label{thm:xi-finite-witness-to-offline-fiber}
If there exists \(N\) with \(\mathbf{T}_N\nsucceq 0\) (equivalently \(\kappa_N\ge 1\)), then:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Candidate resonance points)} The Toeplitz pencil \((\mathbf{T}_N,\mathbf{T}_N^{(1)})\) reads off a finite-window resonance point set inside \(\mathbb{D}\) via its generalized spectrum:
  \[
  \Lambda_N:=\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbf{T}_N,\mathbf{T}_N^{(1)})\cap\mathbb{D}
  =\{\lambda^{(N)}_j\}_{j=1}^{\kappa_N}\subset\mathbb{D}
  \qquad(\text{counted with algebraic multiplicity})
  \]
  (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-resonance-pencil-reconstruction});
  \item \textbf{(Horocycle depth and deviation readout)} For each candidate point, take
  \[
  \delta^{(N)}_j:=\mathcal{B}^{(N)}_{-1}(\lambda^{(N)}_j),
  \]
  then \(\delta^{(N)}_j\) converges exponentially to the endpoint depth \(\mathcal{B}_{-1}(\lambda^{(N)}_j)\) (Theorem \ref{thm:xi-busemann-depth-finite-window-estimator}), thereby providing an auditable finite-window estimate of the off-critical-line deviation;
  \item \textbf{(Time fiber readout)} Let \(t^{(N)}_j:=\mathcal{C}^{-1}(\lambda^{(N)}_j)\in\mathbb{H}\) and write \(\gamma^{(N)}_j:=\Re t^{(N)}_j\); then one can construct the candidate off-line zero fiber
  \[
  \rho^{(N)}_j:=\frac12+\mathrm{i}\gamma^{(N)}_j-\delta^{(N)}_j\qquad(1\le j\le \kappa_N).
  \]
\end{enumerate}
If furthermore one is in the ``finite-type with no boundary poles'' regime and \(\kappa_N\) stabilizes over some interval, then the above \(\lambda^{(N)}_j\) (hence \(\rho^{(N)}_j\)) converge in the \(N\to\infty\) limit to the true disk-interior pole set (equivalently, the off-line zero fiber), provided it exists.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem \ref{thm:xi-finite-window-true-resonance-index}, \(\mathbf{T}_N\nsucceq 0\) is equivalent to the appearance of a finite-window falsifier.
Candidate point generation is given by Theorem \ref{thm:xi-resonance-pencil-reconstruction}; the deviation readout by Theorem \ref{thm:xi-busemann-depth-finite-window-estimator}.
Finally, by the decomposition \(t=\gamma+\mathrm{i}\delta\) of \(t=\mathcal{C}^{-1}(w)\) and the definition \(s(w)=\tfrac12+\mathrm{i}t\), one obtains \(\rho=\tfrac12-\delta+\mathrm{i}\gamma\), yielding the stated fiber construction. \qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Concluding Remarks (Closure Semantics of the Theorem-Level Reduction)}
In summary, RH within the disk-completed horizon interface of this paper can be viewed as follows: the Carath\'eodory positivity of \(\mathscr{C}_{\zeta}\) (hence the entire Toeplitz--PSD hierarchy holds) excludes poles within the stability domain; the Busemann/horocycle coordinates at the endpoint \(-1\) strictly compress off-line deviations into a quadratic radial channel; and the CMV/Schur constraints of the finite-window Hilbert--P{\'o}lya provide a fully certifiable toy spectral realization interface.

The endpoint heat-kernel probe \(\{a_N\}\) further shows that, without introducing any new continuous axis, the endpoint threshold loading can be monotonically extracted from finite Toeplitz data, with the degree of refinement controlled by the depth parameter \(N\).
The closed chain summarized by Theorem \ref{thm:xi-toeplitz-failure-closed-chain} (realized step by step in Theorems \ref{thm:xi-resonance-pencil-reconstruction}--\ref{thm:xi-finite-witness-to-offline-fiber}) shows that once a finite-level Toeplitz--PSD constraint fails, not only can the negative inertia sequence quantify its strength, but one can constructively produce disk-interior resonance candidates and their endpoint depths (hence off-critical-line deviations) as auditable readouts; at the same time, constant-layer counterterms and the true resonance index are strictly orthogonal, so strictification cannot mask stability-domain poles and the three-chain framework for ``extreme resonance'' closes at the finite-dimensional certificate level.
In the multichannel closed-loop interface, channel-wise passivity (PSD) and strict exchange (zero anomaly) jointly are equivalent to a single block Toeplitz--PSD completely positive certificate, and achieve minimal-dimension visible realization on the universal strictification quotient \(G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\) (see \S\ref{subsubsec:xi-operator-valued-carath-toeplitz-complete-positivity}, particularly Corollary \ref{cor:xi-abelian-block-toeplitz-directsum} and Theorem \ref{thm:xi-gbc-maximal-cp-visible-realization}).
