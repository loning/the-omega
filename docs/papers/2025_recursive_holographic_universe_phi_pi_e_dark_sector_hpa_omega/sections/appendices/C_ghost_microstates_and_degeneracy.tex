
\section{Ghost microstates and folding degeneracy at \texorpdfstring{$m=6$}{m=6}}
\label{app:ghost_microstates}

At window length $6$, the microstate space has size $|\Omega_6|=64$ while the admissible (stable) type set has size $|X_6|=21$.
Two equivalent counts capture the same ``hidden'' degrees of freedom:
\[
|\Omega_6\setminus X_6|=64-21=43,
\qquad
\sum_{w\in X_6}\left(\bigl|\mathrm{Fold}_6^{-1}(w)\bigr|-1\right)=64-21=43.
\]

The second identity follows from the fact that $\mathrm{Fold}_6$ partitions $\{0,\dots,63\}$ into disjoint preimages indexed by $X_6$.
Each stable type must have at least one representative input; all additional preimage elements correspond to redundancy hidden by projection.

\subsection{Degeneracy histogram and a consistency check}
By Theorem~\ref{thm:fold6_preimages}, every preimage size is $2$, $3$, or $4$, with histogram
\[
\begin{aligned}
\#\{w\in X_6:\ |\mathrm{Fold}_6^{-1}(w)|=2\}&=8,\\
\#\{w\in X_6:\ |\mathrm{Fold}_6^{-1}(w)|=3\}&=4,\\
\#\{w\in X_6:\ |\mathrm{Fold}_6^{-1}(w)|=4\}&=9.
\end{aligned}
\]
This implies the hidden count directly:
\[
43=\sum_{w\in X_6}\left(|\mathrm{Fold}_6^{-1}(w)|-1\right)
=8(2-1)+4(3-1)+9(4-1)=8+8+27.
\]

\subsection{Interpretation as a dark-sector capacity}
In the paper's identification, the $43$ hidden degrees of freedom are interpreted as ghost microstates: they are not visible as stable types in the $\varphi$-projected readout, yet they occupy state space and can induce routing/compilation overhead.
At higher resolution, the mean degeneracy $d_m=2^m/F_{m+2}$ grows exponentially, suggesting that the dark-sector capacity generically increases with resolution (Section~\ref{subsec:degeneracy_growth}).
