\subsection{Minimal failure-point templates (audit $\rightarrow$ strong-closure normalization)}
\label{app:minimal_failure_point_templates}

\AuditTag This appendix normalizes the manuscript-wide representation of ``minimal failure points''.
Whenever a theorem-leaning statement depends on additional conditions (microcausality/spectrum, field reconstruction, scattering, renormalization, quantization), those conditions must be recorded as explicit failure points with:
\begin{itemize}
\item a \emph{trigger} (what can concretely fail),
\item a \emph{check} (what is to be audited/verified in the declared window),
\item an \emph{impact scope} (what downstream statements become invalid),
\item a \emph{fallback} (what the manuscript reverts to when the failure occurs).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Template}
\begin{definition}[Failure-point record (template)]
\label{def:failure_point_record_template}
\MathTag A \emph{failure-point record} is a quadruple
\[
(\mathrm{Trigger},\mathrm{Check},\mathrm{Impact},\mathrm{Fallback})
\]
attached to a named interface/strong-closure claim, with the semantics listed above.
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Wightman bridge (Appendix~\ref{app:wightman_bridge_and_reconstruction}).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(W1) Domain control.}
  Trigger: no common dense invariant domain for the needed unbounded generators.
  Check: existence of a stable domain $\mathcal{D}$ for the declared smeared fields/operators.
  Impact: Wightman$\to$net and net$\to$field maps are not well-defined at the stated regularity.
  Fallback: remain in AQFT net/state bookkeeping only.
\item \textbf{(W2) Field generation.}
  Trigger: the net has no pointlike generating fields in the declared class.
  Check: generating family exists with required energy bounds/regularity.
  Impact: reconstruction theorems cannot be invoked; correlator statements become external matching inputs.
  Fallback: treat field-level statements as interface/matching only.
\item \textbf{(W3) Locality encoding mismatch.}
  Trigger: graded/braided locality differs from naive commutativity.
  Check: confirm the correct locality notion for the sector and encode it consistently.
  Impact: microcausality identifications must be re-ledgered.
  Fallback: keep locality as a declared interface pack with explicit variant.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Scattering interface (Appendix~\ref{app:scattering_haag_ruelle_lsz_interface}).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(S1) Approximate unitarity window.}
  Trigger: $S(\omega)$ not (approximately) unitary on the declared band.
  Check: calibration/loss model and stability sweeps on the same band.
  Impact: Wigner--Smith delay becomes a proxy with explicit loss error.
  Fallback: revert to delay-as-proxy without asymptotic particle claims.
\item \textbf{(S2) One-particle stability/mass gap.}
  Trigger: no isolated stable one-particle sector.
  Check: spectral isolation / threshold dominance audit in the declared window.
  Impact: Haag--Ruelle/LSZ interpretation fails.
  Fallback: keep only operational phase/delay dictionary and AQFT bookkeeping.
\item \textbf{(S3) Asymptotic completeness.}
  Trigger: asymptotic completeness unknown/false.
  Check: declare it explicitly as hypothesis or restrict to a gapped subsector.
  Impact: $S$ cannot be interpreted as complete channel map.
  Fallback: treat $S$ as effective interface object only.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Renormalization boundary (Appendix~\ref{app:renormalization_dictionary_and_boundaries}).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(R1) Scheme dependence.}
  Trigger: changing scheme shifts numeric parameters.
  Check: keep a bounded finite scheme family and report sensitivity.
  Impact: any numeric matching must be reinterpreted as matching-layer shift.
  Fallback: restrict to scheme-invariant relations or bounded-family envelopes.
\item \textbf{(R2) Threshold conventions.}
  Trigger: decoupling/effective content changes running segments.
  Check: declare finite threshold family and audit variations.
  Impact: beta-function based claims become family-dependent.
  Fallback: keep only family-invariant comparisons.
\item \textbf{(R3) Nonperturbative boundary.}
  Trigger: claim requires constructive/nonperturbative control (4D existence, mass gap, asymptotic completeness).
  Check: explicitly mark as open/conditional and provide falsifiability hooks.
  Impact: strong-closure claims must not be asserted.
  Fallback: revert to proxy-level/audit-level statements.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Rigidity-bridge failure templates (cross-module constraints).}
\AuditTag The manuscript also uses several cross-module rigidity constraints as \emph{applicability boundaries} rather than as additional axioms.
When these constraints are violated, the appropriate response is not to ``tune a knob'' but to enlarge the declared bounded family or to downgrade the claim scope.
The following failure templates normalize these downgrade moves.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(IC1) Dyadic--cosmology consistency pin (Appendix~\ref{app:cosmology_resolution_flow}; Section~\ref{subsec:z128_label}).}
  Trigger: the discrete cosmology match selects $m_\ast$ that does not coincide with the dyadic-baseline pin $m_b^\ast=2p+1$ at the declared baseline $p=7$.
  Check: recompute the discrete match under the stated hypothesis class and verify whether the mismatch persists under the reported stability sweeps; confirm the baseline $p$ and the relation family are unchanged.
  Impact: the ``single low-complexity dyadic baseline supports the same resolution point'' narrative cannot be used.
  Fallback: explicitly enlarge (i) the dyadic baseline family, or (ii) the low-complexity relation family between $m$ and $p$, or (iii) the cosmology hypothesis class; keep the result as a bounded-family audit only (Corollary~\ref{cor:dyadic_cosmology_consistency_pin}).

\item \textbf{(IC2) Pole-barrier gate as analyticity certificate (Appendices~\ref{app:qcd_confinement_proxy_audit}, \ref{app:qcd_confinement_pade_pole_barrier_audit}).}
  Trigger: bounded Pad\'e order families produce interior-unit-disk denominator roots inconsistently across small order enlargements, or the gate flips under modest bounded extensions of the coefficient family.
  Check: stability under bounded order-family enlargement and under bounded loop-family/coefficient-family densification, with the same numerical root-finding tolerance.
  Impact: the pole-barrier verdict cannot be interpreted as a robust analyticity-discipline certificate even at the audit level.
  Fallback: treat pole-barrier outputs strictly as diagnostic rows with explicit instability flags (short/sparse-series ambiguity), and avoid any downstream promotion beyond ``audit hook''.

\item \textbf{(IC3) Invariant-verdict legality threshold (Proposition~\ref{prop:rigidity_legality_invariant_verdicts}).}
  Trigger: the primary objective is invariant under a declared equivalence, but the tie-break key (or an implicit secondary key) is representation-dependent.
  Check: explicitly list the tie-break keys and test invariance under the declared equivalence transformations.
  Impact: the reported ``unique minimizer/verdict'' is not well-defined on equivalence classes and violates the semantic contract.
  Fallback: downgrade the output to a representation-dependent diagnostic, or redefine the tie-break to use invariant keys only; otherwise report only the invariant objective envelope.

\item \textbf{(IC4) Identifiability boundary (cycle-type-only summaries) (Proposition~\ref{prop:cycle_type_identifiability_boundary}).}
  Trigger: an analysis claims to distinguish two internal realizations (e.g.\ CP-odd structure) while the available summaries reduce to cycle-type histograms or class-function aggregates on a fixed loop family.
  Check: verify whether the reported observables are class functions of the holonomy and whether the cycle-type histogram is the only retained information.
  Impact: the claimed distinction is not identifiable from the stated observable content and cannot be treated as falsifiable within that readout.
  Fallback: enlarge the loop family (more loop types/scales) and/or introduce a non-class-function readout; otherwise state the conclusion only as an interpretive overlay, not as an audit-facing discrimination.

\item \textbf{(IC6) Capacity bits $\leftrightarrow$ counting entropy compatibility (Remark~\ref{rem:rigidity_capacity_bits_entropy}).}
  Trigger: a module uses capacity bits as an entropy/regularity control parameter while implicitly changing the coarse-graining quotient, window length, or saturation calibration.
  Check: confirm the declared coarse-graining/quotient and the stated matching factors ($\ln 2$, $k_B$ and any area calibration), and verify that the corresponding squeezing/inequality certificate applies in the stated regime.
  Impact: any promoted entropy/typicality conclusion derived from capacity numbers is not audit-valid.
  Fallback: downgrade to matching-only capacity accounting with explicit hypothesis-class enlargement and/or explicit error budgets.

\item \textbf{(IC7) Scheme reparametrization as an origin shift in $r$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:rigidity_scheme_shift_origin_r}).}
  Trigger: numeric conclusions shift under scheme changes or reference-scale choices while being narrated as invariant predictions.
  Check: keep a bounded finite scheme family, make the $r$-origin convention explicit, and verify which quantities are invariants vs allowed non-invariants under the scheme contract.
  Impact: any claim that treats scheme-origin freedom as a hidden knob violates the equivalence-semantics discipline.
  Fallback: report bounded-family envelopes (sensitivity) and restrict promoted claims to scheme-invariant relations only.

\item \textbf{(IC8) WS delay $\leftrightarrow \log\det$ bridge requires a unitary window (Lemma~\ref{lem:wigner_smith_trace_logdet}; (S1)).}
  Trigger: the measured/scanned $S(\omega)$ is not approximately unitary and differentiable on the declared band, or phase unwrapping is unstable.
  Check: perform calibration/loss audits on the same band and verify stability of $\tau_{\mathrm{WS}}$ under bounded estimator-family variations.
  Impact: any upgrade of delay outputs into determinant/trace analyticity certificates is invalid.
  Fallback: treat delay as a proxy readout with explicit loss/error envelopes; do not promote it to pole-barrier/analytic-domain statements.

\item \textbf{(IC9) DPI/second-law certificate requires a declared channel family (Corollary~\ref{cor:rigidity_dpi_irreversibility_certificate}).}
  Trigger: the effective pipeline is not CPTP/Markov on the declared state space (e.g.\ uncontrolled postselection, non-trace-preserving steps, unmodeled leakage).
  Check: explicitly state the channel/instrument family and verify CPTP/Markov properties (or record the non-CPTP defect terms).
  Impact: monotonicity-based ``second law'' and irreversibility certificates cannot be claimed as theorem-level consequences.
  Fallback: downgrade to interface-only thermodynamic narratives with explicit defect/error bookkeeping.

\item \textbf{(IC10) Global MDL accounting for cross-family enlargement (Appendix~\ref{app:global_model_selection_mdl}).}
  Trigger: a comparison aggregates results across different hypothesis families while expanding or modifying the family registry without recording it.
  Check: ensure the family registry $\mathcal{R}$ is explicit and that any kernel scanning or joint $(m,n,K)$ scans are accounted for by explicit finite factors.
  Impact: global look-elsewhere interpretation inside the registry is not audit-valid.
  Fallback: restrict to within-family statements or re-run the global bound after updating the registry with the enlargement.

\item \textbf{(IC11) Minimal slot gauge is forced by $r_m$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:max_fiber_deg_forces_perm_gauge}).}
  Trigger: a construction assumes a uniform slot/gauge dimension smaller than $r_m$ without truncation, or uses representation-dependent keys while claiming gauge invariance.
  Check: verify $r\ge r_m$ at the stated resolution and that tie-break keys are invariants under the declared local relabelings.
  Impact: gauge-covariance and downstream holonomy readout claims are not well-defined on equivalence classes.
  Fallback: enlarge the slot dimension to $r_m$ (or declare truncation as an explicit approximation), and downgrade any invariant claims if representation dependence remains.

\item \textbf{(IC12) Kernel pressure/pole-barrier alignment (Corollary~\ref{cor:rigidity_kernel_pressure_polebarrier_alignment}).}
  Trigger: the weighted operator family violates the nonnegativity/monotonicity assumptions used in the PF/pressure proxy interpretation (e.g.\ sign-indefinite weights or inconsistent assembly).
  Check: verify entrywise nonnegativity and monotonicity in the stated $t$ grid, and reproduce the generated weighted-pressure/pole-barrier audit tables.
  Impact: the ``pressure'' language cannot be used as a rigidity certificate; pole-barrier locations lose their interpretation.
  Fallback: treat the outputs strictly as audit tables with explicit instability flags and avoid any promotion beyond ``diagnostic hook''.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Continuum-limit bridge (finite holonomy $\rightarrow$ continuum gauge dynamics).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(CL1) Refinement compatibility.}
  Trigger: discrete connection/holonomy constructions across a refinement chain are not compatible (no directed/projective family).
  Check: declare the refinement maps and verify consistency (or bound the defect) on the declared chain.
  Impact: no theorem-level continuum limit object can be claimed; only per-scale diagnostics remain.
  Fallback: treat holonomy outputs as finite protocol diagnostics only.
\item \textbf{(CL2) Scale map ambiguity.}
  Trigger: no declared mapping from protocol loop scale to continuum length $a$ (or mapping is unstable under bounded counterfactuals).
  Check: declare a bounded candidate family of scale maps and report sensitivity.
  Impact: Wilson-loop small-plaquette expansions cannot be used for numeric identification.
  Fallback: keep only dimensionless loop diagnostics and representative-level statements.
\item \textbf{(CL3) Regularity for small-loop expansions.}
  Trigger: the continuum representative lacks the smoothness/energy bounds required by the small-plaquette expansion.
  Check: verify the regularity/energy-bound bundle in the declared regime (or restrict to a smooth subsector).
  Impact: curvature/action-density proxy identification fails theorem-level.
  Fallback: downgrade to proxy-level curvature semantics with explicit error budgets.
\item \textbf{(CL4) Variational convergence / $\Gamma$-limit gap.}
  Trigger: no variational convergence result connecting the discrete loop action to the continuum Yang--Mills action.
  Check: supply a $\Gamma$-convergence (or equivalent) theorem under stated conditions.
  Impact: continuum Euler--Lagrange equations cannot be claimed as limits of discrete minimizers.
  Fallback: keep continuum Yang--Mills only as CAP-selected representative, not as a forced theorem-level limit.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Ward/BRST (Appendix~\ref{app:ward_brst_consistency} and EG strong-closure appendices).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(WBR1) Regularization/scheme breaking.}
  Trigger: a regulator or renormalization choice violates BRST.
  Check: restoration by admissible counterterms to the declared order.
  Impact: ST identities fail unless restored; gauge invariance is not theorem-usable.
  Fallback: treat BRST as interface assumption; track resulting error terms.
\item \textbf{(WBR2) Gauge anomaly.}
  Trigger: anomaly coefficients nonzero (Appendix~\ref{app:anomaly_theorem_filters}).
  Check: anomaly filters and cohomology obstruction test.
  Impact: ST cannot be restored by local counterterms.
  Fallback: modify field registry/scope; otherwise no strong-closure gauge claim.
\item \textbf{(WBR3) Truncation mismatch.}
  Trigger: enforcing identities only up to $D_{\max}$ leaves higher-order breaking.
  Check: encode remainder as explicit truncation budget term.
  Impact: Ward/BRST holds only up to the stated order.
  Fallback: enlarge truncation or weaken the claimed identity scope.
\end{itemize}
