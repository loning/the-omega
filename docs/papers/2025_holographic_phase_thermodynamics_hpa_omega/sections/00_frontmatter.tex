\begin{abstract}
In the HPA--$\Omega$ program, the universe is modeled ontically by a single normalized global state and an intrinsic unitary scan; ``time,'' ``probability,'' and macroscopic irreversibility are not postulated but induced by finite-resolution holographic readout \cite{Ma2025HPA,Ma2025FoP,Ma2025HPOT}. This paper develops the statistical mechanics of that interface. We introduce \emph{Arithmetic Statistical Mechanics} (ASM), in which microstates are deterministic phase orbits of a Weyl scan and macrostates are the finite-depth symbol statistics produced by a scan--projection readout protocol (O5). The central claim is that thermodynamic entropy is \emph{phase friction}: the accumulated arithmetic mismatch generated when a continuous unitary orbit is projected onto a discrete distinguishable readout lattice. In the golden branch, this lattice is canonically organized by Zeckendorf/Fibonacci coding.

We define phase friction using one-dimensional star discrepancy and its accumulated mismatch $E_N = N D_N^\ast$ for irrational rotations. For badly approximable scan slopes, $E_N$ admits an explicit logarithmic upper bound, while rational slopes exhibit linear growth corresponding to periodic ``thermal death'' by phase locking \cite{KuipersNiederreiter1974,Cassels1957}. We then reinterpret gravity as an entropic response: a coarse-grained mismatch density sources a phase potential $\Phi$ whose gradient is a phase pressure. Finally, by identifying gravitational redshift with a \emph{computational lapse} field $\lapse=\kappa_0/\kappa$ determined by routing overhead \cite{Ma2025CAP,Ma2025FoP}, we obtain a simple rescaling law for externally observed entropy production, $\dd S/\dd t \propto \sigma(x)\,\lapse(x)$, describing gravitational ``computational cooling.'' We propose a physical definition of life and intelligence as an \emph{active error-correction phase}: predictive feedback that pays a geometric Landauer cost to locally reduce phase friction and stabilize low-entropy structure.

We provide reproducible toy experiments (Python) validating the logarithmic mismatch bound for the golden branch, the periodicity of rational slopes, and the lapse rescaling of entropy flow.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords}: holographic readout; arithmetic statistical mechanics; star discrepancy; phase friction; Weyl pair; Zeckendorf coding; computational lapse; entropic gravity; geometric Landauer principle; intelligence as active error correction.

\paragraph{Conventions.}
Unless otherwise stated, $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm. ``mod $1$'' refers to reduction in $\RR/\ZZ \cong \mathbb{T}$. We set $c=1$ in theoretical derivations unless explicitly restored. Throughout, ``ontic'' refers to the global unitary scan layer, while ``operational'' refers to finite-resolution readout and implementation constraints.
