\paragraph{Holographic capacity bound (finite-dimensional PT surrogate).} \AuditTag Under the per-site convention $d_{\mathrm{site}}(m)=2^m$ and tensor-product locality, a region $R$ of $|R|$ sites has $\log_2\dim(\mathcal{H}_R)=m|R|$, hence $S_{\partial}(R)\le \log_2\dim(\mathcal{H}_R)=I_{\mathrm{prot}}(m,n;R)=m|R|$.
