\section{A modular toolbox: singularities, divergences, and lattice path sums}
\label{sec:modules}

\subsection{Topologizing singularities on the Riemann sphere}
On the extended complex plane $\widehat{\CC}:=\CC\cup\{\infty\}$, the inversion map
$$
\mathcal{I}(z):=\frac{1}{z}
$$
is a biholomorphic automorphism satisfying $\mathcal{I}(0)=\infty$ and $\mathcal{I}(\infty)=0$. Thus ``division by zero'' is replaced by a compactified pole within the object domain, shifting singularities from ``undefined'' to ``boundary points'' of a compactified geometry.

\subsection{Finite-part extraction: an Abel prototype}
Consider the Abel family
$$
S(\varepsilon):=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n\,\e^{-n\varepsilon},\qquad \varepsilon>0.
$$
A direct computation yields
$$
S(\varepsilon)=\frac{\e^{-\varepsilon}}{\bigl(1-\e^{-\varepsilon}\bigr)^2}.
$$
As $\varepsilon\to 0^+$,
$$
S(\varepsilon)=\frac{1}{\varepsilon^2}-\frac{1}{12}+O(\varepsilon^2).
$$
Therefore, in the finite-part sense (subtracting the pole terms and taking the limit),
$$
\FP_{\varepsilon\to 0^+} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n\,\e^{-n\varepsilon}=-\frac{1}{12}.
$$
This Abel-type finite-part extraction (and the appearance of $-1/12$ as the constant term) is classical; see e.g.~\cite{Hardy1949}.
More generally, any divergent object that admits a controlled Abel asymptotic can be treated within the orbit finite-part template of Section~\ref{sec:cyclic_calculus}.

\subsection{Lattice path sums over Zeckendorf-coded configuration spaces}
Suppose a class of configurations is coded by Zeckendorf bitstrings $(c_k)$ subject to the local constraint $c_k c_{k+1}=0$. Then a formally ill-posed continuum path integral may be replaced by a controlled sum over a discrete, locally constrained code space:
$$
\int \mathcal{D}[\mathrm{path}]\,\e^{\iu S[\mathrm{path}]}
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\sum_{(c_k)\in\mathcal{C}} W(c)\,\e^{\iu S(c)},
$$
where $\mathcal{C}$ is the set of legal bitstrings and $W(c)$ is a weight induced by the projection density or an application-specific measure.
