\begin{corollary}[Pontryagin dual classification of observable strictification]\label{cor:pom-bc-pontryagin-dual-classification}
In the setup of Definition \ref{def:pom-bc-quotient}, the restriction map
\[
\mathrm{res}:\widehat G\longrightarrow \widehat{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m},
\qquad
\chi\longmapsto \chi|_{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}
\]
is a surjective homomorphism, and its kernel is precisely
\(\widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\cong\{\chi\in\widehat G:\chi|_{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}=1\}\)
(the embedding calibration of Corollary \ref{cor:pom-anom-supported-on-bc-kernel}).
Therefore there exists a short exact sequence
\[
0\ \longrightarrow\ \widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\ \longrightarrow\ \widehat G\ \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{res}\ }\ \widehat{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\ \longrightarrow\ 0,
\]
and one obtains a natural isomorphism
\[
\boxed{\;
\widehat G/\widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\ \cong\ \widehat{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\; }.
\]
Thus, ``strictly observable distortion channels'' correspond bijectively with \(\widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\); the discrete part of potential anomaly directions is completely characterized by dual coordinates of \(\widehat{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For finite abelian groups, Pontryagin duality is compatible with short exact sequences: from \(0\to N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\to G\to G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\to 0\) one obtains the dual short exact sequence
\(
0\to \widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\to \widehat G\to \widehat{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\to 0
\);
here the image of \(\widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\) is precisely the characters trivial on \(N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\), so the kernel characterization holds, yielding the stated quotient isomorphism. \qed
\end{proof}
