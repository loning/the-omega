\section{K4 scattering phase to delay audit (interface benchmark)}
\label{app:k4_scattering_phase_delay_audit}

\AuditTag This appendix records a minimal interface audit that turns a small phase-shift point cloud into a Wigner--Smith-type delay proxy.
The goal is to keep the data-facing matching layer explicit: phase data $\delta(E)$ is a directly measurable quantity in scattering experiments, while $\tau_{\mathrm{WS}}$ is a delay readout proxy that can be compared to other delay channels.

\subsection{Scope}
\AuditTag The present artifact is a benchmark interface: the vendored dataset in \path{data/k4_matching/scattering_phase_registry.json} is a deterministic single-resonance phase model (non-empirical).
Replacing it by measured phase-shift points activates the same pipeline without changing the audit logic.

\AuditTag Important: the abscissa labeled $E$ in the registry is dataset-dependent. For NN-OnLine phase-shift tables, $E$ corresponds to the laboratory kinetic energy $T_{\mathrm{lab}}$ (MeV). Therefore the reported proxy series is $\widehat{\tau}_i:=2\,\mathrm{d}\delta/\mathrm{d}T_{\mathrm{lab}}$; its sign and magnitude are interpretable as a coordinate-specific delay proxy, but should not be confused with a fully physical Wigner--Smith time delay with respect to a uniquely defined center-of-mass energy coordinate.

\AuditTag Coordinate-change sign rule: for a monotone reparameterization $y=y(x)$ with $\mathrm{d}y/\mathrm{d}x>0$, we have
$$
\frac{\mathrm{d}\delta}{\mathrm{d}y}=\frac{\mathrm{d}\delta/\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}y/\mathrm{d}x},
$$
so the sign of $\mathrm{d}\delta/\mathrm{d}y$ matches the sign of $\mathrm{d}\delta/\mathrm{d}x$. The Jacobian ranges reported in the coordinate-transform table make this condition explicit for the transform hypotheses used here.
