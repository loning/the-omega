\subsection{Three Theorem Chains: Auditable Navigation from Protocol to Spectral Fingerprint}\label{subsec:three-theorem-chains-rewrite-skeleton}

This subsection compresses the key results of the entire paper into three verifiable theorem chains according to their dependency relations. The purpose is not to introduce new ``meta-theorems'' but to provide a reading path convenient for auditing: readers can follow each chain item by item back to the numbered conclusions in the original chapters for verification.

\paragraph{Chain I: Protocol rigidity chain (design space \texorpdfstring{$\Rightarrow$}{=>} golden endpoint \texorpdfstring{$\Rightarrow$}{=>} fold interface)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Protocol fixing of binary readout and timescale:} Scan--projection generator and prefix cylinder topology (Section \ref{sec:spg}, paragraph \ref{par:spg-prefix-ultrametric}).
  \item \textbf{Fold construction paradigm:} Fibonacci weighted values and Zeckendorf normal form define the binary folding \(\Fold_m\) (Definition \ref{def:fold-word}; Proposition \ref{prop:fold-basic}).
  \item \textbf{General design space:} General Ostrowski legitimate sets and general fold family \(\Fold_m^{(\alpha)}\) (Subsection \ref{subsec:ostrowski-generalized-fold}; Definitions \ref{def:Xm-alpha}, \ref{def:fold-alpha}).
  \item \textbf{Binary degeneration:} The binary alphabet degenerates the parameter axis to \(\alpha=\varphi^{-1}\), and the legitimate grammar to the golden mean grammar with forbidden word \((11)\) (Proposition \ref{prop:Xm-alpha-cardinality}; Subsection \ref{subsec:folding-fibonacci-stable-syntax}).
  \item \textbf{Finite-window completeness:} The prefix range bijection certificate for the stable grammar \(V_m:X_m\simeq\{0,\dots,F_{m+2}-1\}\) (Lemma \ref{lem:zeckendorf-range-bijection}; the value--address isomorphism interface in Section \ref{sec:emergent-arithmetic}).
  \item \textbf{Cross-resolution compatibility and naturality:} Functoriality of ``normal form first, then truncation'' in the general fold and its unique correction in the binary fold (Theorem \ref{thm:normalize-then-truncate-functorial}; Proposition \ref{prop:fold-omega-commute}).
  \item \textbf{Worst-case enforced non-locality:} Full-length flip of the gauge anomaly \(G_m\) at the worst-case end and tail patch incompleteness (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-gauge-anomaly-max}; Corollary \ref{cor:fold-tail-patch-incomplete}).
  \item \textbf{Symmetry auditing:} \(\ZZ_2\) rigidity of the geometric stabilizer at the window-\(6\) anchor and the mask \(\pm 34\) fingerprint (Theorem \ref{thm:foldbin6-geo-stabilizer-z2}; Corollary \ref{cor:foldbin6-geo-mask-34}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Chain II: Residual-as-time chain (quotient structure \texorpdfstring{$\Rightarrow$}{=>} fiber residual \texorpdfstring{$\Rightarrow$}{=>} online/synchronization depth)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Fold quotient structure and fibers:} Fiber multiplicity \(d_m(x)=|\Fold_m^{-1}(x)|\) and stable compression ratio (Subsection \ref{subsec:folding-stable-compression-resolution}).
  \item \textbf{Rigorous objectification of the residual:} Information ledger identity and fiberwise KL decomposition (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-kl-ledger}; Lemma \ref{lem:pom-fiberwise-kl}).
  \item \textbf{Resolution separation and prefix geometry:} Prefix ultrametric and the ``ball equals cylinder set'' decidability caliber (paragraph \ref{par:spg-prefix-ultrametric}; Definition \ref{def:xi-dyadic-prefix-depth-ultrametric}).
  \item \textbf{Sequence-level reversibility:} Conjugacy threshold for the sliding block factor \(\Phi_m\) and finite-memory inverse code (Theorem \ref{thm:Phi_m-conjugacy-threshold}).
  \item \textbf{Minimum online delay:} Realization of single-pass online delay 3 and its minimality (Theorem \ref{thm:online-delay-fold}; Proposition \ref{prop:pom-delay-min}).
  \item \textbf{Synchronization time and residual lifetime:} Definition of synchronization time \(T_m\) and exponential budget for the synchronization tail (Definition \ref{def:Ym-sync-time}; Theorem \ref{thm:Ym-sync-tail}).
  \item \textbf{Orthogonality of space/time defects:} Maximum fiber multiplicity and minimum delay provide space and time fingerprints respectively, which are not interchangeable (Remark \ref{rem:pom-space-time-orth}; Definition \ref{def:pom-fingerprint}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Chain III: Invariant and spectral chain (higher-order moments \texorpdfstring{$\Rightarrow$}{=>} MOM compilation \texorpdfstring{$\Rightarrow$}{=>} \texorpdfstring{$\zeta$}{zeta}/primitive interface)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Higher-order collision moments:} The fiber multiplicity spectrum is uniformly encoded by \(S_q(m)=\sum_x d_m(x)^q\) (e.g., Definition \ref{def:pom-s5} and its low-order analogues).
  \item \textbf{Moment-kernel compilation:} For any \(q\ge 2\), there exists a finite-state moment-kernel that reads off the recursion and Perron principal scale of \(S_q\) (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-collision-kernel-ak}).
  \item \textbf{Resonance and realization dimension:} Hankel rank collapse provides an auditable discriminant for the resonance mechanism (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-moment-resonance}; see also the chain consolidation in Theorem \ref{thm:pom-unified-closure}).
  \item \textbf{Time--space commuting diagram:} Strict alignment of trace sequences and primitive orbit spectra under the \(\zeta\)-determinant caliber (Proposition \ref{prop:time-space-commuting-diagram}).
  \item \textbf{Spectral/finite-part bridging:} Extraction of spectral fingerprints from the \(\zeta\) and finite-part constants of mechanism kernels, closing with group extensions/twisted channels (Section \ref{sec:zeta-finite-part}).
  \item \textbf{Unified closure:} The online kernel (time fingerprint)---collision moment kernel (spatial spectrum)---\(\zeta\)/primitive (spectral packaging)---geometric dimension spectrum close under the same interface (Theorem \ref{thm:pom-unified-closure}).
\end{enumerate}
