\section{Vacuum normalization from the \texorpdfstring{$\e$}{e}-channel: a pressure-based audit closure for \texorpdfstring{$\Lambda$}{Lambda}}
\label{app:lambda_pressure_closure}

\paragraph{Scope and status.}
\InterfaceTag The continuum representative action selected by CAP includes a cosmological-constant term as the lowest-complexity gravity-sector invariant (Remark~\ref{rem:lambda_status}).
This appendix records a compact \emph{audit-facing} closure route that links a vacuum normalization to the $\e$-channel pressure viewpoint, without importing any new theorem-level premises into the folding core.
\MatchTag Numerical values are fixed only through explicitly declared matching-layer targets and compact audit inputs (all listed in Appendix~\ref{app:inference_ledger}).
\AuditTag The goal is to provide a deterministic and reproducible mapping
\[
\text{(pressure normalization)}\ \longrightarrow\ \Omega_{\Lambda,0}\ \longrightarrow\ \Lambda,
\]
together with an explicit bounded candidate-family choice and a mismatch report.

\subsection{Pressure normalization and the entropy-gap dictionary}
\label{subsec:lambda_pressure_entropy_gap_dictionary}

The $\e$-channel is the analytic-stability channel: it packages admissibility and stability into generating functions and pressure-like normalizations (Appendix~\ref{app:thermodynamic_formalism_pressure}).
At the simplest level (zero potential), the relevant objects reduce to topological entropy/pressure constants:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Full dyadic baseline.}
\MathTag For the full two-shift $\Sigma_2=\{0,1\}^{\ZZ}$ one has $P_{\Sigma_2}(0)=\log 2$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:full_shift_entropy_gap}).
\item \textbf{Golden-mean admissible channel.}
\MathTag For the golden-mean shift $\Sigma_{\mathrm{GM}}$ one has $P_{\Sigma_{\mathrm{GM}}}(0)=\log\varphi$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:pressure_zero_potential_gm}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Normalized pressure share and gap share.}
\MathTag The zero-potential normalization already supplies the distinguished constants
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lambda_pressure_share_def}
s_0:=\log_2\varphi,
\qquad
1-s_0=\log_2(2/\varphi).
\end{equation}
\MathTag The identity $1-s_0=\log_2(2/\varphi)$ is the base-2 form of the entropy gap $\log(2/\varphi)$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:full_shift_entropy_gap}).

\subsection{Finite candidate family for \texorpdfstring{$\Omega_{\Lambda,0}$}{OmegaLambda0} and deterministic selection (audit)}
\label{subsec:lambda_pressure_candidate_family}

\paragraph{Candidate family.}
\AuditTag To keep the closure finite-family and auditable (as required by the CAP doctrine in Appendix~\ref{app:cap_audit_template}), we use a short dyadic-weighted pressure family.
Fix a finite index set $K:=\{0,1,2,\dots,8\}$ and define dyadic weights $w_k:=2^{-k}$.
\AuditTag The explicit bound on $K$ makes the ``look-elsewhere'' footprint transparent: the candidate family size is $|\mathcal{C}_\Lambda|=2|K|$.
Consider the one-parameter weighted golden-mean transfer matrix
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lambda_pressure_weighted_matrix}
A_k
:=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & w_k\\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\lambda_k:=\rho(A_k)=\frac{1+\sqrt{1+4w_k}}{2},
\qquad
s_k:=\log_2\lambda_k,
\end{equation}
so that $s_0=\log_2\varphi$.
We then declare the candidate family
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lambda_pressure_candidate_family}
\Omega_{\Lambda,0}\ \in\ \bigl\{s_k,\ 1-s_k:\ k\in K\bigr\}.
\end{equation}
\AuditTag This encodes a bounded set of minimal semantic identifications:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\Omega_{\Lambda,0}=s_k$: vacuum normalization identified with a dyadic-weighted admissible-channel pressure share.
\item $\Omega_{\Lambda,0}=1-s_k$: vacuum normalization identified with the corresponding complementary share relative to the full dyadic baseline.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Selection rule (finite-family closure) and mismatch report.}
\AuditTag The closure output is the \emph{finite-family} choice obtained by a deterministic complexity-first tie-break:
we select the smallest dyadic index $k_\ast:=\min K$ and pick the share $s_{k_\ast}$ rather than its complement.
Equivalently,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lambda_pressure_selection_rule}
\widehat\Omega_{\Lambda,0}
\;:=\;
s_{k_\ast},
\qquad
k_\ast:=\min K.
\end{equation}
\MatchTag Given a declared matching-layer target $\Omega_{\Lambda,0}^{\mathrm{ref}}$ (Planck-2018), we then report the mismatch
\[
\Delta_\Lambda(\Omega):=\left|\log\!\left(\frac{\Omega}{\Omega_{\Lambda,0}^{\mathrm{ref}}}\right)\right|
\]
for $\widehat\Omega_{\Lambda,0}$ and for the bounded counterfactual baselines in \eqref{eq:lambda_pressure_candidate_family}.
As a supplementary audit diagnostic, the generator also reports whether the mismatch-minimizer over the same finite family coincides with \eqref{eq:lambda_pressure_selection_rule}, and whether this agreement is stable under a $\pm1\sigma$ perturbation of $\Omega_{\Lambda,0}^{\mathrm{ref}}$.
\AuditTag As an additional bounded-complexity (MDL-style) audit, one can penalize larger $k$ values by a simple code-length proxy and report the resulting penalized minimizer within the same finite family; this is recorded by the generator as a diagnostic and does not change the closure output \eqref{eq:lambda_pressure_selection_rule}.
\AuditTag For the manuscript-wide, cross-family MDL accounting rule (when comparing across different candidate-family registries rather than within this single bounded family), see Appendix~\ref{app:global_model_selection_mdl}.

\subsection{From \texorpdfstring{$\Omega_{\Lambda,0}$}{OmegaLambda0} to \texorpdfstring{$\Lambda$}{Lambda} (matching dictionary)}
\label{subsec:lambda_pressure_to_lambda_dictionary}

\MatchTag We use the same GR convention referenced in Remark~\ref{rem:lambda_status}:
\[
\rho_\Lambda=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{8\pi G},
\qquad
\rho_{c,0}=\frac{3H_0^2}{8\pi G},
\qquad
\Omega_{\Lambda,0}:=\frac{\rho_\Lambda}{\rho_{c,0}}.
\]
Therefore,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lambda_from_omega_lambda_h0}
\Lambda
\;=\;
\frac{3H_0^2}{c^2}\,\Omega_{\Lambda,0}.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Finite \texorpdfstring{$H_0$}{H0} audit family (closure) and sensitivity.}
\AuditTag To keep the calibration auditable while acknowledging the well-known early/late tension, we treat $H_0$ as a bounded candidate family rather than a single hard-coded input.
Let $\mathcal{C}_{H_0}$ be the finite family recorded under \path{data/cosmology_lambda/h0_candidates.json} (Planck-2018, H0LiCOW XIII, SH0ES19).
We select $\widehat H_0\in\mathcal{C}_{H_0}$ by a deterministic precision rule (minimize the fractional uncertainty $\sigma_{H_0}/H_0$ with a fixed tie-break), and report the implied $\widehat\Lambda$ under \eqref{eq:lambda_from_omega_lambda_h0} together with the sensitivity across all candidates.

\MatchTag Reference values used for mismatch auditing (e.g.\ $\Omega_{\Lambda,0}^{\mathrm{ref}}$, $\Omega_{m,0}^{\mathrm{ref}}$, $\Omega_{b,0}^{\mathrm{ref}}$ and the Planck-2018 $H_0$ target) are recorded explicitly in the matching-input inventory (Appendix~\ref{app:inference_ledger}) and are vendored under \path{data/cosmology_lambda/planck2018_targets.json}.
