\begin{corollary}[Divisor-triggered triple reduction bound: collision/KL/peak suppression from zero-set coset union]\label{cor:fold-zero-divisor-triple-reduction}
Let \(M:=F_{m+2}\) and assume \(M\) is even.
If there exists a proper divisor \(d\mid (m+2)\), \(d<m+2\), such that \(F_d\mid (M/2)\),
then by Corollary~\ref{cor:fold-zero-divisor-lb} we have \(|\mathcal{Z}_m|\ge F_d\).
Therefore, by Theorem~\ref{thm:fold-zero-uncertainty}, the following deterministic improved bounds hold:
\[
\boxed{\;
\mathsf{Col}_m\ \le\ 1-\frac{F_d}{M},\qquad
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p_m\|u)\ \le\ \log(M-F_d),\qquad
S_m\ \le\ 2^m\sqrt{M-1-F_d}.\;}
\]
Moreover, the maximum fiber excess satisfies
\[
M_m-\overline d_m\ \le\ 2^m\sqrt{\frac{M-1-F_d}{M}}.
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By Corollary~\ref{cor:fold-zero-divisor-lb}, \(|\mathcal{Z}_m|\ge F_d\).
Substituting this into the collision upper bound \(\mathsf{Col}_m\le 1-|\mathcal{Z}_m|/M\),
the KL upper bound \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p_m\|u)\le \log(M-|\mathcal{Z}_m|)\),
and the peak upper bound \(S_m\le 2^m\sqrt{M-1-|\mathcal{Z}_m|}\) from Theorem~\ref{thm:fold-zero-uncertainty}, the first three inequalities follow.
The last inequality is obtained by substituting \(|\mathcal{Z}_m|\ge F_d\) into \(M_m-\overline d_m\le 2^m\sqrt{(M-1-|\mathcal{Z}_m|)/M}\) from the same theorem. \qed
\end{proof}
