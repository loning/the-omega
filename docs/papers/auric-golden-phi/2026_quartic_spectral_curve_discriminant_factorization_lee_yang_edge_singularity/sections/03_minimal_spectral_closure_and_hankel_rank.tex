\section{Minimal spectral closure and Hankel rank}\label{sec:hankel}

\subsection{Reduced rationality and minimal recurrence order}

The rational form of $Z(t,y)$ implies that $\{Z_m(y)\}$ satisfies a linear recurrence whose order is at most $\deg_t D=4$.
The key point is that, away from a finite degeneracy set in $y$, there is no cancellation between numerator and denominator, so the order-$4$ recurrence is minimal.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:minimal-order}
For $y\notin\{-1,0,1\}$, the polynomials $N(t,y)$ and $D(t,y)$ are coprime in $\CC[t]$.
Consequently, the minimal linear recurrence order of $\{Z_m(y)\}_{m\ge 0}$ is $4$, and its Hankel rank is $4$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Suppose $N(t,y)$ and $D(t,y)$ have a nontrivial common factor in $\CC[t]$.
Then there exists $t_0\in\CC$ with $N(t_0,y)=D(t_0,y)=0$, and the reduced denominator of $Z(t,y)$ has degree at most~$3$, yielding a linear recurrence of order at most~$3$.
We show in Appendix~\ref{app:degeneracies} that the only parameters where such cancellation occurs are $y\in\{-1,0,1\}$: at $y=-1$ one has $N(t,-1)\equiv D(t,-1)$; at $y=0$ and $y=1$ specific factors cancel.
For $y\notin\{-1,0,1\}$ the reduced denominator therefore has degree~$4$, and the minimal recurrence order is~$4$.

For the Hankel rank, we invoke the classical equivalence between reduced rational generating functions and finite-rank Hankel matrices (see Flajolet and Sedgewick~\cite[Theorem~IV.9]{FlajoletSedgewick2009} or Stanley~\cite[Proposition~4.2.3]{Stanley1999}): a sequence $\{Z_m\}$ has a reduced rational generating function with denominator of degree~$d$ if and only if the infinite Hankel matrix $(Z_{i+j})_{i,j\ge 0}$ has rank~$d$.
Applying this with $d=4$ yields the stated Hankel rank.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Degenerate parameters}

At $y=0$ and $y=1$, the denominator $D(t,y)$ has a reducible factor that cancels with $N(t,y)$, lowering the effective recurrence order.
At $y=-1$, one has $N(t,-1)=D(t,-1)$ and thus $Z(t,-1)\equiv 1$.
These points form a finite degeneracy set; a complete account of the cancellations is given in Appendix~\ref{app:degeneracies}.
