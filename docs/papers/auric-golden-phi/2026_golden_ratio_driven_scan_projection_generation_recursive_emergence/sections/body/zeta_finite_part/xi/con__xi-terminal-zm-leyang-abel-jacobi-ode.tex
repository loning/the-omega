\begin{conclusion}[Lee--Yang differential closure in Abel--Jacobi coordinates: a first-order algebraic equation for \texorpdfstring{$y(u)$}{y(u)}]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-abel-jacobi-ode}
Adopt the elliptic model from Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-normalization}:
\[
E:\quad Y^2=X^3-X+\frac14,
\qquad
y:=X^2+Y-\frac12\in\QQ(E),
\qquad
\omega:=\frac{\dd X}{2Y}.
\]
Take Abel--Jacobi coordinate \(u\) with \(\dd u=\omega\), and define
\[
p(u):=\frac{\dd y}{\dd u}.
\]
Then, on any branch-cut-free region, \((y(u),p(u))\) satisfies the fully explicit first-order algebraic differential closure
\[
\boxed{\;
p^{4}+(8y-3)p^{3}+(2y^{2}-34y-4)p^{2}
-y(y-1)\,P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)=0\;},
\qquad
P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y):=256y^{3}+411y^{2}+165y+32.
\]
Hence the constant term (the stationary spectrum \(p=0\)) gives exactly
\[
p(u)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
y(u)\in \{0,1\}\ \cup\ \{P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)=0\},
\]
so the Lee--Yang branch image appears intrinsically as a stationary set in Abel--Jacobi time, consistent with the branch-discriminant kernel in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-discriminant-leyang}.
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch (elimination)]
From the differential identity in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-ramification-critical-values},
\(
\dd y=(4XY+3X^2-1)\,\omega
\)
we obtain
\[
p=4XY+3X^2-1.
\]
Also, from \(y=X^2+Y-\tfrac12\), one has \(Y=y-X^2+\tfrac12\). Substituting into the equation above yields the cubic constraint in \(X\):
\[
4X^{3}-3X^{2}-(4y+2)X+(1+p)=0.
\]
On the other hand, the quartic plane model obtained by eliminating \(Y\) is
\[
F(X,y):=X^{4}-X^{3}-(2y+1)X^{2}+X+y(y+1)=0
\]
as in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-discriminant-norm-identity}.
Taking the resultant elimination in \(X\), \(\mathrm{Res}_{X}(F,\ 4X^{3}-3X^{2}-(4y+2)X+(1+p))\), and factoring in \(\ZZ[y,p]\), gives the displayed quartic equation.
Its constant term factors as \(-y(y-1)P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)\), in agreement with the branch-image characterization in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-discriminant-leyang}.

\end{proof}
