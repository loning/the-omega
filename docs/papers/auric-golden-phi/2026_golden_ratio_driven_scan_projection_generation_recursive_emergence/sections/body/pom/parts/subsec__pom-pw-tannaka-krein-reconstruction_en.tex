\subsection{Reconstruction Interface for the Projection-Word \texorpdfstring{$2$}{2}-Category: From Anomaly Cohomology to Compact Group Continuous Envelopes}\label{subsec:pom-pw-tannaka-krein-reconstruction}
This subsection, building on the established projection-word \(2\)-category \(\mathbf{PW}\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-projword-2cat}) and the anomaly cohomology caliber (\S\ref{subsec:pom-anom-cohomology}),
provides a reconstruction roadmap that promotes ``symmetry/gauge group'' from a narrative-level assumption to a \emph{strictly writable and finitely auditable/falsifiable} statement.
The key idea is: compress naturality/commutativity failures into computable \(2\)-cohomology classes, and equate ``comparable semantics'' with a finite kernel-matching problem on bounded slices.
This interface-level formulation can also be compared with the roadmap version in the internal document \cite{Internal2026PW2CategoryTannakaKreinSM}.

\paragraph{Normal form \texorpdfstring{$+$}{+} anomaly: compressing naturality failure into finite signatures}
Within the three-generator residual closure, commutativity failure is no longer treated as a syntactic defect but is defined as an auditable anomaly signature together with its cohomology class:
the residual commutativity certificate pushes the commutativity breakdown of \(E_m\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_G\) entirely into \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-signature}; Proposition \ref{prop:pom-em-lift-residual-swap}),
and further undergoes only boundary transformations when the reduction path changes (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-anom-additive-up-to-boundary}; Corollary \ref{cor:pom-anom-cohomology-class}).
Therefore \(\bigl(\mathrm{NF}(w),[\mathrm{Anom}(w)]\bigr)\) (Remark \ref{rem:pom-residual-2confluence}) becomes a path invariant,
promoting ``commutativity failure'' from a narrative input to a computable \(2\)-cohomological invariant.

\begin{corollary}[Finite decidability on bounded slices: semantic comparability \texorpdfstring{$\Longleftrightarrow$}{<->} finite signature agreement]\label{cor:pom-finite-audit-nf-anom}
On a bounded complexity slice (Definition \ref{def:pom-bounded-slice}),
for any two co-source co-target projection words \(w_1,w_2\in\mathbf{PW}^{\ast}_{\le}\), if their finite signatures (Remark \ref{rem:pom-finite-signature-sigmam})
\[
\Sigma_m(w)
=
\Bigl(
G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m(w),\
\{\mathsf{Anom}_{G}(K;\theta)\}_{\theta\in\Theta_{\mathrm{grid}}},\
\{r_q(u)\}_{(q,u)\in\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{grid}}}
\Bigr)
\]
agree coordinate-wise, then there necessarily exists a natural isomorphism \(w_1\simeq w_2\).
Hence within this slice caliber, ``semantic comparability'' can be strictly equated to a consistency-checking problem of a finite set of outputs (normal forms / group quotients / anomalies / spectral fingerprints).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By the conservativity of Theorem \ref{thm:pom-sem-conservative}, finite signature agreement implies \(\mathsf{Sem}(w_1)=\mathsf{Sem}(w_2)\), which in turn reflects to a natural isomorphism \(w_1\simeq w_2\). \qed
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Profinite carrier of visible group registers: alignment with the cylinder--Haar interface}
At fixed resolution \(m\), the universal abelianization quotient \(G\twoheadrightarrow G_m^{\mathrm{BC}}\) strips out the ``group directions erased by folding'' as the kernel \(N_m^{\mathrm{BC}}\), and strictifies the commutativity of \(E_m\) and \(\mathrm{LIFT}\) on \(G_m^{\mathrm{BC}}\) (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-bc-quotient-strictify} and Proposition \ref{prop:pom-bc-quotient-universal}).
The inverse system \(\{G_m^{\mathrm{BC}}\}\) over the resolution tower and its inverse limit
\(
G_\infty^{\mathrm{BC}}=\varprojlim_m G_m^{\mathrm{BC}}
\)
(Corollary \ref{cor:pom-bc-inverse-system}) therefore provides an \emph{intrinsic profinite carrier}: it remains strictly compatible with the folding tower in the infinite-resolution limit and naturally carries Haar measure and cylinder event coordinates (see \S\ref{subsec:op-algebra-cylinder-properdatum} and the ``cylinder projection---maximal entropy state---spectrum/\(\zeta\)'' operator-algebraic interface in \S\ref{subsec:operator-zeta-interface}; see also \cite{CuntzKrieger1980}).

\begin{remark}[Tannaka--Krein type reconstruction (roadmap; interface-level statement)]\label{rem:pom-tannaka-krein-roadmap}
In the present caliber, ``gauge symmetry'' can be understood as: the natural automorphism group that preserves cylinder semantics (the visible \(\sigma\)-algebra generated by cylinder projections/prefix coordinates) and is compatible with the folding tower (Remark \ref{rem:symmetry-projection-automorphism}).
Within the bounded slice, \(\mathbf{PW}^{\ast}_{\le}\) and its semantic functor \(\mathsf{Sem}\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-sem-functor}) provide an operationally viable substitute for a ``fiber functor'':
its natural automorphisms manifest at the certificate level as \(2\)-isomorphism classes in \(\mathbf{PW}\) that preserve visible output, and at the structural level must be compatible with the visible register inverse system \(\{G_m^{\mathrm{BC}}\}\), thereby falling in the limit into the profinite skeleton provided by \(G_\infty^{\mathrm{BC}}\).
\par
Furthermore, when the cylinder projection family is placed within the universal encapsulation provided by the Cuntz--Krieger algebra \(\mathcal{O}_A\) (or equivalently, the groupoid C*-algebra) (\S\ref{subsec:operator-zeta-interface}; \cite{CuntzKrieger1980}), its diagonal subalgebra aligns naturally with the cylinder event coordinates, and the Parry maximal entropy state provides a canonical state baseline;
therefore ``projection events---states---symmetries'' can be rigorously formulated in the operator-algebraic context as: the *-automorphism group preserving the diagonal and the state.
Under suitable rigidity/completeness assumptions, this path is compatible with the classical Tannaka--Krein duality picture: the tensor category (generated by auditable objects/certificate closures) and the tensor automorphism group of its fiber functor are mutual reconstruction targets.
The present paper uses this only as a \emph{strictification roadmap}: starting from discrete auditable objects and the profinite skeleton \(G_\infty^{\mathrm{BC}}\), one can advance under explicit interfaces and assumptions toward the continuous envelope reconstruction of compact groups (and even compact Lie groups).
\end{remark}

\paragraph{Falsifiable finite discriminator: anomaly cohomology vanishing \texorpdfstring{$\Rightarrow$}{=>} strictification}
The anomaly class provides a direct ``falsifier'': if a nonzero anomaly cohomology class appears in a given fragment, then any attempt to strictly close the corresponding naturality square via strictification will fail;
conversely, under the completion/critical pair enumeration caliber, if all necessary anomaly classes vanish, then ``commutativity failure'' can be entirely absorbed into boundary transformations and strictly closed on the visible group quotient (Remark \ref{rem:pom-anom-completion}; see also Corollary \ref{cor:pom-zero-anom-pullback-exactness} and the strictification interface of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-bc-quotient-strictify}).

\begin{proposition}[Finite discriminator (bounded slice): from anomaly vanishing to reproducible certificates/counterexamples]\label{prop:pom-finite-falsifier-anom}
Fix a bounded slice (Definition \ref{def:pom-bounded-slice}) and a generating fragment (for example, the three-generator fragment \(\langle E_m,\mathrm{PROJ}_u,\mathrm{LIFT}_G\rangle\), or its extension incorporating \(\mathrm{MOM}_q,\mathrm{TWIST}(\chi)\)).
Within this slice, the set of critical pairs required for completion is finite (Remark \ref{rem:pom-anom-completion}), so the necessary consistency conditions for the existence of ``strict naturality/commutativity closure'' can be compressed into \emph{finitely many} anomaly cohomology vanishing constraints:
\[
[\mathrm{Anom}(w)] = 0\qquad(\text{for all generator sub-fragments }w\text{ and their critical pairs requiring closure}).
\]
If this finite system of constraints is unsatisfiable, one can output a set of explicit counterexample paths (holonomy/anomaly labels) as a falsification certificate; if satisfiable, then under the corresponding strictification caliber, commutativity failure can be entirely absorbed and a strict closure interface compatible with the folding tower is obtained.
\par
In particular, at the minimal nontrivial anchor of window-\(6\), the abelianization of the dyadic fold-gauge yields a canonical binary charge lattice
\(
(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_6)^{\mathrm{ab}}\cong (\ZZ_2)^{21}
\)
(Corollary \ref{cor:fold-bin-gauge-m6}; see also Remark \ref{rem:fold-gauge-z2-21}).
Under the ``binary audit'' caliber, the anomaly label group can be further reduced to this finite bit lattice (and can be projected onto the boundary sheet-center \((\ZZ_2)^3\), Corollary \ref{cor:fold-bin-boundary-center-z2}),
thereby translating the above finite vanishing constraints into a finite linear satisfiability problem over \((\ZZ_2)^{21}\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[Decidability break (bounded slice): strictification existence is decidable]\label{cor:pom-strictification-existence-decidable-bounded-slice}
Under the bounded slice caliber of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-finite-falsifier-anom}, ``whether there exists a set of strictifications making all required naturality/commutativity squares strictly closed'' is decidable: it reduces to the satisfiability decision of finitely many anomaly cohomology vanishing constraints;
at the binary audit anchor of window-\(6\), this decision further degenerates to a finite linear satisfiability problem over \((\ZZ_2)^{21}\).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Within a bounded slice, the set of critical pairs required for completion is finite (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-finite-falsifier-anom}).
Therefore ``strict closure existence'' only requires simultaneously satisfying \([\mathrm{Anom}(w)]=0\) for finitely many generator sub-fragments and their critical pairs.
The satisfiability of this finite system of constraints can be completed by exhaustive enumeration/finite algebraic decision; under the binary charge lattice caliber, constraints are represented as finite linear constraints over \((\ZZ_2)^{21}\), hence decidable in finite time. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{conjecture}[Decidability break (global): strictification existence is undecidable (candidate)]\label{conj:pom-global-strictification-undecidable}
After removing the bounded slice constraint, consider the global strictification existence problem: given a set of extended generation fragments and their completion rule families, does there exist a set of (possibly infinite) strictification data such that all naturality/commutativity squares requiring closure hold strictly globally (anomaly cohomology classes vanish simultaneously)?
\par
Given that the deterministic control flow of FRACTRAN has been fully compiled into the first-fit sequence gate selection on \(\mathcal{P}\) fibers (subsection \ref{subsec:pom-fractran}), and inherits Turing-complete control expressivity, we conjecture that the above \emph{global} strictification existence problem is undecidable in the recursive sense: one can construct a reduction from FRACTRAN halting behavior such that ``strictification exists'' and ``halting/non-halting'' encode each other.
\end{conjecture}
