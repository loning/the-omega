\section{Appendix E: Statistical Protocols for Verifiable Predictions (Paradigms)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Syntactic stability verification:} Given sliding-window statistics of observed sequences, test whether the frequency of the prohibited word ``11'' tends to $0$ as resolution increases (or yields an interpretable nonzero steady state under weighted models).
  \item \textbf{Cyclic closure verification:} Under periodic concatenation or cyclic boundary conditions, test whether the number of permitted closed words satisfies the Lucas trace relation (or its weighted generalization).
  \item \textbf{Fold degeneracy verification:} Apply $\mathrm{Fold}_m$ to the empirical distribution of microscopic indices and compare whether the resulting stable-type distribution matches the theory-predicted degeneracy-induced distribution; if not, the model is falsified at this interface.
  \item \textbf{Channel separation verification:} After introducing weighted transfer operators or longer windows, test whether the analytic stability domain (characterized by spectral radius or pole location) exhibits measurable deviation from the local prohibition law; the presence and scale of this deviation serve as evidence for or against the independence of the $e$ channel.
\end{enumerate}
