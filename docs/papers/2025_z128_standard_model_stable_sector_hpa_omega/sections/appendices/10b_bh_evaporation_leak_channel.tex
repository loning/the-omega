\section{Evaporation and leakage as a protocol channel (BH4: record + resource ledger)}
\label{app:bh_evaporation_leak_channel}

\paragraph{Scope and status.}
\AuditTag This appendix isolates the minimal ingredients needed to talk about black-hole-like
evaporation in the protocol language: (i) a declared external record, and (ii) a resource ledger.
It is an interface/audit module and introduces no new theorem-level premise into the folding core.
For the broader leakage-kernel vocabulary (finite candidate families for survival laws, exit-channel
decompositions, and delay proxies), see Appendix~\ref{app:leakage_kernel}.

\subsection{External record: stable labels and fiber coordinates}
\label{subsec:bh4_external_record}

\paragraph{Paper-native micro/macro objects.}
Fix a window length $m$.
Microstates are dyadic indices $N\in\{0,\dots,2^m-1\}$.
The stable label is the folded word
\[
W:=\mathrm{Fold}_m(N)\in X_m,
\]
where $X_m$ is the no-consecutive-ones admissible language and $\mathrm{Fold}_m$ is the
Zeckendorf-truncation folding map (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol_primitives} and the fold-family uniqueness contract
Appendix~\ref{app:fold_family_uniqueness}).
The preimage fiber is
\[
P(w):=\mathrm{Fold}_m^{-1}(w),\qquad g_m(w):=|P(w)|.
\]

\paragraph{Two record granularities.}
\InterfaceTag We distinguish two externally recorded granularities:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Coarse record:} only the stable labels $w_t\in X_m$ are recorded.
\item \textbf{Refined record (single-stream).} the record remains a \emph{single} time-ordered stream of stable labels
$w_t\in X_m$, but late-time symbols are allowed to encode fiber information (e.g.\ via a finite digit alphabet derived
from $X_m$) so that the full record becomes information-complete at fixed $m$.
\end{itemize}
Recording only $w_t$ is a protocol coarse graining. A refined single-stream record is information-complete at fixed $m$
once enough late-time symbols have been emitted and the decoding rules are declared by a finite candidate family (CAP).

\subsection{Resource ledger: preventing perpetual emission without a source}
\label{subsec:bh4_resource_ledger}

\paragraph{Ledger requirement (audit).}
\AuditTag Any ``evaporation'' claim without external input must include a resource ledger that decreases
under emission. This prevents a perpetual-emission pathology in the tick-only language.
The present appendix keeps the ledger abstract: the concrete physical identification (mass/area/temperature)
belongs to matching dictionaries (Appendix~\ref{app:bh_planck_capacity_calibration} and Appendix~\ref{app:bh_wormholes_pointer}).
