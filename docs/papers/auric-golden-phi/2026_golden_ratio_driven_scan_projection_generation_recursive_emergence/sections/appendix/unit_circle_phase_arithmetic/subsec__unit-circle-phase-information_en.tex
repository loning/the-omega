\subsection{Partial Observation and Extension Preservation: Externalizing Irreversibility as a Record Axis}\label{subsec:unit-circle-phase-information}
The maps \(\upsilon\) and \(\iota\) are bijections on \(\RR\) (boundary points appear only as limits), so the ``lift--projection'' itself introduces no information loss.
When subsequent steps combine discretization, truncation, or folding---all irreversible read-outs---the following two principles make information conservation rigorous in terms of invertibility on an extended space.

\begin{definition}[Partial observation]\label{def:unit-circle-phase-partial-observation}
Given an observation map \(\pi:\Omega\to X\). If \(\pi\) is not injective, it is called a \textbf{partial observation}: distinct microstates are identified under the same observed value, manifesting as information compression at the visible level.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Extension to injectivity (trivial realization)]\label{prop:unit-circle-phase-extension-preserving}
For any sets \(\Omega,X\) and map \(\pi:\Omega\to X\), take the record space \(R:=\Omega\) and set \(r:=\mathrm{id}_\Omega\).
Then the extended observation
\(
\widetilde\pi(\omega):=(\pi(\omega),r(\omega))
\)
is injective; thus ``information conservation can be formulated as invertibility on the extended space'' is always realizable in this trivial set-theoretic manner.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If \(\widetilde\pi(\omega)=\widetilde\pi(\omega')\), the second component gives \(r(\omega)=r(\omega')\), i.e., \(\omega=\omega'\). Hence \(\widetilde\pi\) is injective. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Alignment with the fold conditional expectation/residual certificate caliber]
In Appendix~\ref{app:op-algebra}, the information compression of the fold \(\Fold_m\) is precisely characterized by the conditional expectation \(E_m:\mathcal{A}_m\to\mathcal{B}_m\) (fiber averaging; Proposition~\ref{prop:fold-conditional-expectation}).
The above ``extension-preserving'' principle may be viewed as the gate-language version of the same idea: the gap from irreversible compression is carried by an externalized record axis and can be further compiled into auditable residuals/certificates (cf.\ the \(2\)-morphism certificate caliber in \S\ref{subsec:pom-cost-functorial-calculus}).
\end{remark}
