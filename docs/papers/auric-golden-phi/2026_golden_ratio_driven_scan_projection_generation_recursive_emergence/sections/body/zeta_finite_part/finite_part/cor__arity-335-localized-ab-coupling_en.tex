
\begin{remark}[Three-dimensional coupling dependence on \texorpdfstring{$(a,b)$}{(a,b)} is strongly localized along the $c$ axis]\label{cor:arity-335-localized-ab-coupling}
Viewing each $c$-slice as a $3\times 3$ matrix (rows indexed by $b$, columns by $a$), let $\mathrm{sd}(c)$ denote the standard deviation of that matrix over its $9$ entries (averaged over entries). The reproducible output gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\bigl(\mathrm{sd}(c=0),\mathrm{sd}(c=1),\mathrm{sd}(c=2),\mathrm{sd}(c=3),\mathrm{sd}(c=4)\bigr)
&\approx\bigl(0.347585,0.0591959,0.00515942,\\
&\qquad 0.000787443,0.000137309\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$
In particular, for $c\ge 2$ one has $\mathrm{sd}(c)\le 0.00515942$, and $\mathrm{sd}(3),\mathrm{sd}(4)$ are at the $10^{-3}$ level and below. Hence at constant-level drift resolution, the nontrivial three-dimensional coupling in \(((3,3,5))\) is primarily concentrated on $c=0$ and $c=1$, while the tail congruence classes exhibit near-factorization behavior.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Three-phase stratification: the structural spike at $c=0$, the transition at $c=1$, and the near-factorized tail at $c\ge 2$]\label{cor:arity-335-phase-stratification}
Using $\max\mathrm{dev}(c)$ and $\Delta_c$ from the secondary summary output as diagnostic quantities, the \(((3,3,5))\) constant tensor along the collision congruence class axis $c\in\ZZ/5$ exhibits a three-phase stratification:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Spike phase ($c=0$):} the slice is highly nonuniform over $(a,b)$;
  \item \textbf{Transition phase ($c=1$):} the slice nonuniformity decreases but structure remains visible;
  \item \textbf{Tail phase ($c\ge 2$):} the slices approach rank-$0$ constant matrices (near-factorization).
\end{itemize}
This is stronger than the ``non-permutation-equivalence'' of Remark \ref{prop:arity-335-n2-nondegenerate}: not only does the third axis introduce new degrees of freedom, but the structure of that degree of freedom is mainly localized on small congruence classes.
\end{remark}
