\section{Conclusion}

This paper presents a unified framework with compact-Abelian-group translations as ontic scans, window kernels and conceptual observables as readout mechanisms, and substitution/blocking operators as renormalization operators. Within this framework we systematically clarify how the three structural constraints $\varphi$, $\pi$, and $e$ can be organized in a commutable manner. In the provable module, we provide a complete theorem chain based on scale conjugacy, unique ergodicity, Kronecker--Weyl equidistribution, Sturmian complexity, and Perron--Frobenius frequency closure. In the testable module, we provide an equidistribution theorem under gated sparsification together with computable experimental metrics. In the interface module, we provide a cut-and-project hidden-dimension window interpretation and an exponential-completion interpretation. The relation to HPA is that the present framework supplies a standard rigorous realization in which its protocol-layer ``scan--readout--coding--multichannel stability'' can be mathematically formalized.
