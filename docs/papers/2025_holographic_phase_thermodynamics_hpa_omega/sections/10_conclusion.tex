\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Holographic Phase Thermodynamics (HPT) provides a unified operational picture in the HPA--$\Omega$ framework:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Ontically, the universe is unitary and information-preserving.
  \item Operationally, finite-resolution scan--projection readout induces probability, thermodynamic time, and irreversibility.
  \item Entropy admits a micro-definitional origin as phase friction: accumulated arithmetic mismatch of a Weyl scan orbit under discrete readout.
  \item Gravitational redshift is computational slowdown governed by a lapse $\lapse=\kappa_0/\kappa$, implying computational cooling of externally observed entropy flow.
  \item Gravity can be modeled as an entropic/phase-pressure response sourced by mismatch density.
  \item Life and intelligence are active error-correction phases: predictive feedback that locally suppresses phase friction while paying a geometric Landauer cost.
\end{itemize}

The central shift is semantic but testable: instead of treating thermodynamics as an emergent average over unknown microstates, ASM treats it as an inevitable interface cost of finite readout acting on a deterministic unitary scan. The resulting scaling laws and spectral templates invite targeted tests once explicit microscopic realizations and measurement kernels are fixed.
