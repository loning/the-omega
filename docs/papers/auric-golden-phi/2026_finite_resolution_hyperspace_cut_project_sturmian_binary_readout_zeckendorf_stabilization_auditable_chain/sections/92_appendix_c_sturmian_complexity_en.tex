\section{Appendix C: Sturmian Mechanical Word Complexity Theorem (Proof Outline)}
\label{app:sturmian-outline}

\subsection{Form of Mechanical Word}

For irrational rotation $\theta_{t+1}=\theta_t+\alpha$ and two-interval partition $\mathcal{P}$, $w_t$ is a mechanical word. Its balance property arises from: in any two prefixes of equal length, the count of $1$'s differs by at most $1$.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Complexity $p(n)=n+1$}{Complexity p(n)=n+1}}

For each $n$, consider the set of factors of length $n$. From the density of orbits under irrational rotation on $\TT^1$ and the ``cutting point'' structure of partition endpoints, we can show that the number of factors is exactly $n+1$. The key point is: factors of length $n$ correspond to which of the $n+1$ partition intervals generated by $n$ cutting points the starting phase falls into; the number of intervals is $n+1$.
