\begin{abstract}
This paper investigates a model class of quasiperiodic structures of rank $6$ within the crystallographic superspace framework, and expresses three-dimensional quasiperiodic densities as restrictions of six-dimensional lattice-periodic densities to physical subspace. The observation process is formalized as a family of parameter-dependent readout operators: cross-section parameters (orientation, window, and internal phase) determine the three-dimensional realization, while realizable sampling under finite resources is characterized through one-dimensional ring rotation factors and a hierarchy of rational approximations, with verifiable invariants of Sturmian processes providing certificates at the symbolic level. To obtain stable representation, this work fixes bandwidth $m=6$ in the main text, introduces a stabilization kernel $\mathrm{Fold}_6:\Omega_6\twoheadrightarrow X_6^{\mathrm Z}$, and objectifies indistinguishable degrees of freedom via the preimage fiber structure of this kernel; the arrow of time is defined rigorously as the exponential contraction rate of posterior cylinder set measure, whose limit is characterized by KS entropy and the SMB theorem, with ``future'' objectified as conditional uncertainty (e.g., conditional entropy). Furthermore, this work provides an object-layer construction of fixed-bandwidth recursive readout (partition refinement) and characterizes the fiber-prioritized discovery process via Levi graphs; internal gauge degrees of freedom are incorporated through $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ fiber extension without increasing the superspace rank; ergodicity yields statistical holography: typical single-trajectory long-range statistics recover global invariant statistical laws.
\end{abstract}
