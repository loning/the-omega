\begin{remark}[Recovering \texorpdfstring{$P_{16},P_{17}$}{P16,P17} from recursion coefficients]\label{rem:pom-moment-charpoly-16-17}
Following the recursion format and characteristic polynomial convention of Theorem \ref{thm:pom-moment-resonance},
\(
P_q(\lambda)=\lambda^d-\sum_{i=1}^d c_i\lambda^{d-i}
\).
Substituting the \(q=16,17\) recursion coefficients from Table \ref{tab:fold_collision_moment_recursions_9_17} directly yields
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{16}(\lambda)={}&\lambda^{13}-2\lambda^{12}-1611\lambda^{11}-62312\lambda^{10}-2559407\lambda^9\\
&+24862788\lambda^8+585266591\lambda^7-62312\lambda^6+44606766\lambda^5\\
&-24862794\lambda^4-255692\lambda^3+124624\lambda^2-8\lambda+8,
\\
P_{17}(\lambda)={}&\lambda^{13}-2\lambda^{12}-2599\lambda^{11}-125872\lambda^{10}-6569850\lambda^9\\
&+96034590\lambda^8+2764163954\lambda^7+643026032\lambda^6+15022392733\lambda^5\\
&-769974566\lambda^4-15329386299\lambda^3-642908352\lambda^2-1347896340\lambda+673948170.
\end{aligned}
$$
These explicit characteristic polynomials can be directly used for root scanning/spectral packet structural analysis; for instance, the computations in Table \ref{tab:fold_collision_kernel_rh_scan_q2_17} take \(P_q\) as input (script \texttt{scripts/exp\_fold\_collision\_kernel\_rh\_scan\_q2\_17.py}).
\end{remark}
