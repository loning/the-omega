\begin{abstract}
We study the induced (first-return) dynamics of translation flows on compact abelian groups, particularly on the torus $\TT^d$, observed through a window. For one-dimensional irrational rotations with an interval window, we establish the three-value piecewise structure of return-time functions and provide necessary and sufficient conditions for two-value degeneration, proving that two-value degeneration is equivalent to the induced system being a 2-interval exchange transformation, hence conjugate to a circle rotation via measure. In the two-value degenerate case, we give explicit formulas for the induced rotation number expressed as a fractional linear action of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\ZZ)$, and construct a ``window-induced renormalization operator'' that characterizes periodic renormalization orbits for ultimately periodic continued-fraction parameters. The golden parameter yields the shortest periodic fixed point and Fibonacci scaling with self-induced closure. For higher-dimensional torus translations, we derive an exact formula for the closure dimension of orbits: the closure dimension equals the $\QQ$-linear rank of $\{1,\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_d\}$ minus one. Consequently, if the translation vector coordinates belong to a number field of degree $k$, the closure dimension is at most $k-1$; in quadratic fields (including the golden field), the closure is at most one-dimensional. Finally, using factor systems and categorical language, we propose a unified structure: viewing Kronecker factors as reflectors (idempotent comonads), window induction as a partial functor on the category of systems with sections, providing commutation theorems for induction and reflection, and formalizing ``inducible closure windows'' as stable objects in the reflector class. We propose generalization problems on higher-order characteristic factors.
\end{abstract}
