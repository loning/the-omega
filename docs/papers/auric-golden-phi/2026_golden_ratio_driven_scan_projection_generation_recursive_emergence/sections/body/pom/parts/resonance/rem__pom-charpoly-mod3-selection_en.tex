\begin{proposition}[Two-phase compression modulo \texorpdfstring{$3$}{3}: for \texorpdfstring{$q=13,15$}{q=13,15} only the \texorpdfstring{$1$}{1} and \texorpdfstring{$-1$}{-1} generalized phases survive]\label{prop:pom-moment-mod3-two-phase}

(E-1)^4(E+1)^5\,\widetilde S_q\equiv 0\pmod 3.

\widetilde S_q(m)\equiv
\sum_{j=0}^{3}a_j\binom{m}{j}
\ +\
(-1)^m\sum_{j=0}^{4}b_j\binom{m}{j}
\pmod 3,
\]

\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
From Table \ref{tab:fold_collision_charpoly_mod3_selection_q13_15}, for \(q\in\{13,15\}\) we have
\(
P_q(\lambda)\equiv \lambda^2(\lambda-1)^4(\lambda+1)^5\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 3)
\).
Replacing \(\lambda\) by \(E\) and applying to \(S_q\) yields
\[
E^2(E-1)^4(E+1)^5S_q\equiv 0\pmod 3,
\]
i.e., \((E-1)^4(E+1)^5\widetilde S_q\equiv 0\).
\par
Since \((x-1)^4\) and \((x+1)^5\) are coprime in \(\mathbb{F}_3[x]\), there exist \(U,V\in\mathbb{F}_3[x]\) such that
\(
U(x)(x-1)^4+V(x)(x+1)^5=1
\).
Set
\(
A:=V(E)(E+1)^5\widetilde S_q
\),
\(
B:=U(E)(E-1)^4\widetilde S_q

then \(A+B=\widetilde S_q\), and
\[
(E-1)^4A\equiv 0,\qquad (E+1)^5B\equiv 0\pmod 3.
\]
For \(A\): the operator \(E-1\) is the forward difference, and the solution space of \((E-1)^4A=0\) is spanned by
\(
\binom{m}{0},\binom{m}{1},\binom{m}{2},\binom{m}{3}

hence \(A(m)\equiv \sum_{j=0}^3 a_j\binom{m}{j}\).
\par
For \(B\): set \(C(m):=(-1)^mB(m)\), then
\(
(E-1)^5C(m)=(-1)^m(E+1)^5B(m)
\)
vanishes over \(\mathbb{F}_3\), so by the same reasoning
\(
C(m)\equiv \sum_{j=0}^4 b_j\binom{m}{j}
\),
and therefore \(B(m)\equiv (-1)^m\sum_{j=0}^4 b_j\binom{m}{j}\).
Combining yields the conclusion.
\end{proof}

\(
(E-1)^4(E+1)^5\widetilde S_q\equiv 0\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 3)
