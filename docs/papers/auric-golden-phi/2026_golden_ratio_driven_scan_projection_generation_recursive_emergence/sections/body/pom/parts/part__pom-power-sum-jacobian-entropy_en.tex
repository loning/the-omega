\begin{corollary}[Jacobian entropy of power-sum coordinates: unification of dimension-volume-entropy in the simplex coordinate chart]\label{cor:pom-power-sum-jacobian-entropy}
Following the notation of Theorem \ref{thm:pom-power-sum-vandermonde-jacobian}.
On the open dense set \(U\subset\Delta_{\circ}^{n-1}\), define the coordinate Jacobian entropy potential
\[
S_{\mathrm{coord}}(w):=-\log\left|\det \mathrm{D}\widetilde\Phi(w)\right|.
\]
Then there is a closed form
\[
\boxed{\;
S_{\mathrm{coord}}(w)
=-\log n!
-\sum_{1\le i<j\le n}\log|w_j-w_i|\;}
\]
so that when \(w\) approaches the collision hypersurface \(w_i=w_j\) on the boundary of the simplex,
\(S_{\mathrm{coord}}\) diverges logarithmically with the Vandermonde factor and exhibits a two-body logarithmic repulsion structure.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By the determinant formula of Theorem \ref{thm:pom-power-sum-vandermonde-jacobian}
\(
\det \mathrm{D}\widetilde\Phi(w)=n!\prod_{i<j}(w_j-w_i)
\),
taking absolute value and negative logarithm yields the conclusion. \qed
\end{proof}
