\section{Variational convergence: Wilson loop action $\Gamma$-limits to Yang--Mills (CL4)}
\label{app:gamma_convergence_wilson_to_yang_mills}

This appendix records the theorem-facing step that upgrades a small-loop expansion into a \emph{variational} statement: discrete loop actions must converge (in the $\Gamma$-sense or equivalent) to the continuum Yang--Mills action to justify limit claims about Euler--Lagrange equations.
This is the CL4 bridge in Appendix~\ref{app:minimal_failure_point_templates}.
The general $\Gamma$-convergence framework used for the bridge is standard \cite{DalMaso1993GammaConvergence,Braides2002GammaConvergence}.

\subsection{A canonical discrete loop action from protocol data}
\paragraph{Wilson-like plaquette scalar.}
\MathTag At each scale $(m,n)$, the protocol produces an effective unitary loop holonomy $Q_\square$ (Appendix~\ref{app:continuum_yang_mills_from_holonomy}).
Define the Wilson-like scalar
\[
W_\square:=\frac{1}{3}\RePart\Tr(Q_\square),\qquad A_\square:=1-W_\square.
\]

\begin{definition}[Protocol Wilson action functional (template)]
\label{def:protocol_wilson_action_functional_template}
\MathTag Fix a scale map $a$ (Definition~\ref{def:scale_map_protocol_to_continuum}) and let $\mathcal{P}_{m,n}$ denote the unit-plaquette family on the order-$n$ grid.
Define the discrete Wilson action (up to an overall coupling-scale factor) by
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:protocol_wilson_action}
S_{m,n}^{\mathrm{W}} \;:=\; \sum_{\square\in\mathcal{P}_{m,n}} A_\square\,a^{4}.
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\AuditTag The definition is deliberately minimal and uses only protocol-computable quantities.
Any additional normalization (e.g.\ $\beta=2N/g^2$) belongs to the matching layer unless it is closed within a declared finite coefficient family (Appendix~\ref{app:cap_continuum_action_closure}).

\subsection{$\Gamma$-convergence bridge (theorem-facing target)}
\paragraph{Continuum target action.}
\MathTag For a continuum connection $A$ with curvature $F$, define the Yang--Mills action
\[
S_{\mathrm{YM}}[A]:=\int \dd^4x\,\sqrt{-g}\,\frac{1}{4g^2}\Tr(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu})
\]
in the declared representative dictionary.

\begin{theorem}[$\Gamma$-limit of Wilson-type actions (template)]
\label{thm:gamma_limit_wilson_to_yang_mills_template}
\MathTag Assume the convergence bundle of Appendix~\ref{app:holonomy_to_connection_convergence_theorems} holds so that discrete holonomies converge to a smooth continuum connection, and assume the scale-map and regularity bundles of Appendix~\ref{app:scale_map_and_small_loop_regularity_contract} hold.
Assume further that the discrete plaquette family $\mathcal{P}_{m,n}$ samples the continuum volume in the sense that the Riemann-sum approximation induced by $a$ converges.
Then the discrete functionals $S_{m,n}^{\mathrm{W}}$ $\Gamma$-converge (after the standard coupling normalization) to $S_{\mathrm{YM}}$ on the declared configuration space.
\end{theorem}

\AuditTag Theorem~\ref{thm:gamma_limit_wilson_to_yang_mills_template} is the theorem-facing target statement for CL4.
Discharging it for gauge actions is nontrivial and is treated here as an explicit external-input point: one must specify the configuration space/topology, the equi-coercivity mechanism, and the precise discrete-to-continuum identification class.
When CL4 is not discharged, the manuscript must treat the continuum Yang--Mills term as a CAP-selected representative (Appendix~\ref{app:cap_continuum_action_closure}) and keep limit-Euler--Lagrange claims out of the theorem-level chain.
