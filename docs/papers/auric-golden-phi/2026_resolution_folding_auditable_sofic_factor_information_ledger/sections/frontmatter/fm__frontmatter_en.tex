\begin{abstract}
We introduce a finite-window binary readout model and isolate a single normalization map, the \emph{resolution folding} $\Fold_m$, as the organizing principle that closes a fully auditable loop:
\[
\Omega_m \xrightarrow{\ \Fold_m\ } X_m \xrightarrow{\ \Phi_m\ } Y_m
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{Markov cover invariants and an information ledger identity}.
\]
The microstate space is $\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m$. The folding $\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m$ is realized by a value-preserving local rewriting system, terminates and is confluent, and hence produces a unique canonical form in the stable language $X_m$; it is idempotent, surjective, and compatible with a fold-aware restriction across resolutions.
We quantify the obstruction to naive truncation by a \emph{gauge anomaly} functional, show it can be full length in the worst case, and compute its uniform-baseline density $g_\ast=4/9$ with an explicit finite-state certificate.
The induced sliding block code $\Phi_m$ yields a sofic image $Y_m$ with an explicit finite labeled graph that is \emph{bi-resolving} by a one-step distinguishability mechanism; as a consequence, $Y_m$ saturates the source entropy $h_{\mathrm{top}}(Y_m)=\log 2$ and the factor admits an injective finite-defect register of size $2^{m-1}$.
Standard cover theory then provides a computable interface to the right Fischer cover; from the corresponding adjacency matrix we obtain $h_{\mathrm{top}}$, Parry (PF) data, and a cover-level zeta determinant.
Finally, we prove an \emph{information ledger identity} for arbitrary $\mu\in\Delta(\Omega_m)$:
\[
H(\mu)=H(P_m\mu)+\mathbb{E}[\log d_m]-D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|Q_mP_m\mu),
\]
decomposing entropy into visible type entropy, fiber volume, and a unique residual (KL) term. This yields auditable certificates (constants, spectral radii, tail bounds) and a finite-sample verification protocol.
\end{abstract}

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Main contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Canonical folding as a multiscale interface.} We realize $\Fold_m$ as a confluent local rewriting normalizer producing a unique canonical form in $X_m$, and we prove idempotence, surjectivity, and fold-aware multiscale compatibility (Theorem~\ref{thm:fold-normal-form-multiscale}).
  \item \textbf{Quantified non-commutativity across resolutions.} We introduce the gauge anomaly $G_m$ measuring the mismatch between naive truncation and fold-aware restriction, prove a worst-case full-length obstruction, and compute its uniform-baseline density $g_\ast=4/9$ with a finite-state certificate (Propositions~\ref{prop:gauge-anomaly-max} and \ref{prop:gauge-anomaly-density-49}, Appendix~\ref{app:gauge-anomaly-transducer}).
  \item \textbf{Model-specific sofic factor with entropy saturation.} We construct $Y_m=\Phi_m(\{0,1\}^{\ZZ})$ via an explicit suffix-state labeled graph and prove one-step distinguishability (right- and left-resolving), which forces entropy saturation $h_{\mathrm{top}}(Y_m)=\log 2$ and yields an injective finite-defect register (Theorem~\ref{thm:phi-sofic-fischer}).
  \item \textbf{Information ledger identity with fiber-volume control.} We prove an entropy decomposition that isolates the residual KL term as the unique carrier of microstructure beyond folded types and fiber volumes (Theorem~\ref{thm:information-ledger}), and we record an explicit (crude) bound on fiber multiplicities $d_m$ to make the fiber-volume term auditable (Proposition~\ref{prop:fiber-multiplicity-bound}).
  \item \textbf{Auditable certificates.} We provide finite-sample total-variation certificates and a minimal reproducible pipeline interface for regenerating any numerical artifacts used in the paper (Theorems~\ref{thm:tv-certificate-hist} and \ref{thm:markov-tv-envelope}).
\end{itemize}
