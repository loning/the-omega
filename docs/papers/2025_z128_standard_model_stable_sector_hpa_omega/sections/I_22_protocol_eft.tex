\subsection{A minimal effective field theory embedding (protocol EFT)}
\label{subsec:protocol_eft}

This subsection records a concrete field-theoretic embedding whose purpose is \emph{consistency}, not a full derivation from the folding layer.
It answers a minimal technical question: given the three-channel stable-sector template and the closed labeling of Section~\ref{sec:sm_labeling_closure}, what is a well-defined local action whose gauge structure is $SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)$, whose matter content matches the $18$ cyclic labels, and for which anomaly cancellation holds in the standard sense?

\paragraph{Fields.}
Let $\{\psi_f\}_{f\in\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{SM}}}$ denote the $18$ left-handed Weyl fermion multiplets in one-to-one correspondence with the cyclic labels of Theorem~\ref{thm:labeling_unique}, with quantum numbers $(SU(3),SU(2))_Y$ as recorded in Table~\ref{tab:sm_labeling_table}.
Let $G_\mu=G_\mu^A T^A$ be an $SU(3)$ gauge field, $W_\mu=W_\mu^a \tau^a$ an $SU(2)$ gauge field, and $B_\mu$ a $U(1)$ gauge field.
Optionally (to form the usual renormalizable SM EFT), include a complex scalar Higgs doublet $H$ with $(SU(3),SU(2))_Y=(1,2)_{1/2}$.
In the present paper, this Higgs field is included only at the level of a standard EFT completion (Remark~\ref{rem:higgs_not_in_21}); it is not asserted to correspond to a distinct stable type at the minimal window length.
The corresponding protocol-level scalar-sector closure (scalar observables by coarse graining/uplift and an EFT coupling dictionary) is recorded in Proposition~\ref{prop:scalar_sector_closed}.

\paragraph{Gauge-invariant action.}
With standard normalizations \cite{PeskinSchroeder1995,WeinbergQFT1995,PDGRPP2024}, define the local Lagrangian density
\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{prot\text{-}EFT}}
&=
-\frac{1}{4}G_{\mu\nu}^A G^{A\,\mu\nu}
-\frac{1}{4}W_{\mu\nu}^a W^{a\,\mu\nu}
-\frac{1}{4}B_{\mu\nu} B^{\mu\nu}
\nonumber\\
&\quad
+\sum_{f\in\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{SM}}}\psi_f^\dagger\,\iu \bar{\sigma}^\mu D_\mu \psi_f
\nonumber\\
&\quad
+(D_\mu H)^\dagger (D^\mu H)-V(H)
\;+\;\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Yuk}}(H,\psi)\,,
\label{eq:protocol_eft_lagrangian}
\end{align}
where the covariant derivative is
\begin{equation}
D_\mu=\partial_\mu-\iu g_3\,G_\mu^A T^A-\iu g_2\,W_\mu^a \tau^a-\iu g_1\,Y\,B_\mu,
\label{eq:protocol_eft_covariant_derivative}
\end{equation}
with $T^A$ and $\tau^a$ the generators in the representation appropriate to the field and $Y$ its hypercharge.
The Yukawa sector $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Yuk}}$ is included only if one wishes to match the renormalizable Standard Model EFT; its detailed structure is not needed for the finite folding claims of this paper.

\paragraph{Equations of motion (standard).}
Varying \eqref{eq:protocol_eft_lagrangian} yields the standard gauge and matter field equations \cite{PeskinSchroeder1995,WeinbergQFT1995,PDGRPP2024}.
For the non-abelian sectors one obtains the Yang--Mills equations with currents determined by the chiral matter and (optionally) the Higgs field,
\begin{equation}
(D^\mu G_{\mu\nu})^A = g_3\,J^A_\nu,
\qquad
(D^\mu W_{\mu\nu})^a = g_2\,J^a_\nu,
\end{equation}
while the abelian hypercharge sector satisfies
\begin{equation}
\partial^\mu B_{\mu\nu} = g_1\,J^Y_\nu.
\end{equation}
The fermions obey the chiral Dirac equations $\iu \bar{\sigma}^\mu D_\mu\psi_f=\partial\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Yuk}}/\partial\psi_f^\dagger$, and, when included, the Higgs satisfies a covariant Klein--Gordon equation $D^\mu D_\mu H+\partial V/\partial H^\dagger = -\partial\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Yuk}}/\partial H^\dagger$.
Thus the protocol EFT provides an explicit dynamical embedding in standard field-theory language; the folding layer enters only through the finite stable-sector selection and labeling that determine which degrees of freedom are retained at the anchor.

\paragraph{Electroweak symmetry breaking and the Higgs mode (standard).}
If one chooses the usual Mexican-hat potential $V(H)=-\mu^2 H^\dagger H+\lambda (H^\dagger H)^2$ with $\mu^2>0$ and $\lambda>0$, then the vacuum selects $\langle H\rangle\neq 0$ and breaks $SU(2)\times U(1)$ to $U(1)_{\mathrm{em}}$.
Within the bounded low-complexity potential family declared at the scalar-interface audit layer, this potential form is also the unique CAP-minimal minimizer (Appendix~\ref{app:scalar_interface_audits}, Table~\ref{tab:higgs_ewsb_potential_closure}).
In unitary gauge one may write
\[
H(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}0\\ v+h(x)\end{pmatrix},
\]
so that three would-be Goldstone modes are absorbed as the longitudinal polarizations of $W^\pm$ and $Z$, while $h$ is the physical spin-$0$ Higgs excitation.
Expanding $(D_\mu H)^\dagger(D^\mu H)$ around $\langle H\rangle=(0,v/\sqrt{2})^T$ yields the standard tree-level electroweak mass relations
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ew_tree_masses}
m_W=\frac{1}{2}g_2\,v,\qquad
m_Z=\frac{1}{2}v\sqrt{g_1^2+g_2^2},\qquad
\sin^2\theta_W=\frac{g_1^2}{g_1^2+g_2^2},
\end{equation}
and the Higgs excitation has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:higgs_mass_lambda}
m_h^2=2\lambda v^2,
\end{equation}
while fermion masses arise from $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Yuk}}$ via $m_f=y_f v/\sqrt{2}$ in a mass eigenbasis.
\InterfaceTag In this paper, $g_1(\mu_Z)$ and $g_2(\mu_Z)$ are fixed at the matching scale by the closed electroweak normalization (Theorem~\ref{thm:weinberg_angle}), and $v$ is then fixed by the $Z$-pole reference mass $m_Z$ through \eqref{eq:ew_tree_masses} (Proposition~\ref{prop:vev_from_mz_closed_ew}).
Consequently, $m_W$ is determined at the same interface layer by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mw_from_mz_thetaW}
\frac{m_W}{m_Z}=\cos\theta_W=\sqrt{1-\sin^2\theta_W},
\end{equation}
so under the closed value $\sin^2\theta_W(\mu_Z)=3/13$ one has $m_W=m_Z\sqrt{10/13}$ as a parameter-free consistency check at the stated $Z$-scale conventions.
This standard mechanism supplies gauge-boson masses through $(D_\mu H)^\dagger(D^\mu H)$ and fermion masses through $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Yuk}}$, but it is not used as a premise for any of the finite-resolution folding or labeling statements in this paper.

\paragraph{Where the \texorpdfstring{$\varphi$--$\pi$--$\e$}{phi--pi--e} channels enter.}
The folding layer provides three commuting defect predicates/operators at finite window length (Section~\ref{sec:folding_core}).
In this EFT embedding, the three channels enter as \emph{protocol constraints} that restrict which local readout modes are treated as light degrees of freedom:
\begin{itemize}
\item the $\varphi$-channel selects admissible words (no adjacent ones), yielding a stable subspace of dimension $|X_m|=F_{m+2}$ at window length $m$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:xm_fib});
\item the $\pi$-channel further splits the stable space into cyclic/boundary sectors, with $|X_m^{\mathrm{cyc}}|\oplus |X_m^{\mathrm{bdry}}|$ given by Proposition~\ref{prop:cyc_bdry_size};
\item the $\e$-channel records the standard analytic normalization of the golden-mean shift via its Artin--Mazur zeta function (Lemma~\ref{lem:golden_zeta_matrix} and Lemma~\ref{lem:golden_normalization_factorization}).
\end{itemize}
Operationally, one may view \eqref{eq:protocol_eft_lagrangian} as the low-energy EFT \emph{after} this selection: the fields $\psi_f$ represent the cyclic stable labels, and the three boundary labels select the three gauge-factor classes (Section~\ref{sec:sm_labeling_closure}).
This does \emph{not} yet constitute a derivation of the SM gauge group from the finite combinatorics; it is an explicit consistent embedding compatible with the interface axioms.

\paragraph{Anomaly cancellation.}
Because the gauge sector is chiral, the EFT is consistent only if gauge and mixed anomalies cancel.
For the Standard Model hypercharge assignments under $Q=T_3+Y$, the anomaly sums vanish per generation \cite{BouchiatIliopoulosMeyer1972,PeskinSchroeder1995,PDGRPP2024}.
In our labeling closure, adding $\nu_R$ with $Y=0$ does not affect these sums (Proposition~\ref{prop:anomaly_nur}), so \eqref{eq:protocol_eft_lagrangian} is anomaly-free with the stated matter content.

\begin{remark}[Status]\label{rem:protocol_eft_status}
The purpose of the protocol EFT is to make the ``compensating connections'' claim mathematically precise at the level of a local gauge-invariant action with the correct group structure and anomaly constraints.
Within the stated interface rules (three compensation classes, dimension-as-complexity for gauge factors, and the auditable ordering closure), the gauge-factor assignment and the chiral representation content are uniquely fixed at the minimal anchor:
the Lie-algebra factor dimensions $(1,3,8)$ pin the compact factors to $\mathfrak{u}(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:su2_su3_unique_by_dim}), and the matter multiplet assignment is the unique closed labeling map (Theorem~\ref{thm:labeling_unique}).
Accordingly, \eqref{eq:protocol_eft_lagrangian} is recorded here as a closed EFT embedding consistent with those uniquely fixed interface identifications and with standard anomaly constraints.
\end{remark}
