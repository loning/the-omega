\begin{corollary}[Defect Degree as Entropy Slope in Logarithmic Scale: \texorpdfstring{$\frac{\dd}{\dd(\log m)}S_{\mathrm{gen}}(m)=\kappa$}{d/dlogm S_gen(m)=kappa}]\label{cor:xi-pick-poisson-entropy-derivative-visible-dimension}
Following the notation of Corollary \ref{cor:xi-pick-poisson-energy-stabilizer-covariance}, fix a set of upper-half-plane points \(t_1,\dots,t_\kappa\in\mathbb{H}\).
For any scale parameter \(m>0\), define the rescaled point set \(t^{(m)}_j:=t_j/m\), and let \(\mathsf P(m):=\mathsf P(t^{(m)}_1,\dots,t^{(m)}_\kappa)\) be the corresponding Pick--Poisson Gram matrix (\eqref{eq:xi-pick-poisson-gram}).
Define the generalized defect entropy
\[
S_{\mathrm{gen}}(m):=-\log\det\mathsf P(m).
\]
Then the strict covariance law holds
\[
\boxed{\;
\det\mathsf P(m)=m^{-\kappa}\det\mathsf P(1),\qquad
S_{\mathrm{gen}}(m)=S_{\mathrm{gen}}(1)+\kappa\log m\;}
\]
thus
\[
\boxed{\;\frac{\dd}{\dd(\log m)}S_{\mathrm{gen}}(m)=\kappa.\;}
\]
Therefore under the Pick--Poisson regime, the defect degree \(\kappa\) can be characterized as the strict slope of "boundary volume entropy" in the logarithmic scale direction: it is independent of geometric details, determined solely by the externalized dimension (defect degree).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By the scaling law of Corollary \ref{cor:xi-pick-poisson-energy-stabilizer-covariance}, taking \(\lambda=1/m\) yields
\[
S_{\mathrm{gen}}(t_1/m,\dots,t_\kappa/m)=S_{\mathrm{gen}}(t_1,\dots,t_\kappa)+\kappa\log m.
\]
Denoting the left-hand side as \(S_{\mathrm{gen}}(m)\) yields the shown linear law.
Differentiating both sides with respect to \(\log m\) immediately gives the derivative identity. QED.
\end{proof}
