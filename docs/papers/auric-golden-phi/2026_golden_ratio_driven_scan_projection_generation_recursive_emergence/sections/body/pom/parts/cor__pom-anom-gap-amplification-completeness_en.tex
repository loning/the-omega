\begin{corollary}[Completeness of MOM amplification: nonzero anomaly is always linearly amplifiable to threshold]\label{cor:pom-anom-gap-amplification-completeness}
Adopt the notation of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-anom-gap-amplification}, and write
\(
a:=[\mathrm{Anom}(w)]\in\mathcal{A}_G
\).
If \(a\neq 0\), then for any audit threshold \(\tau>0\) there exist an integer \(q\ge 2\) and some character coordinate \(\chi\in\widehat G\setminus\{\chi_0\}\) such that
\[
\bigl|[\mathrm{Anom}(w^{\boxtimes q})]_{\chi}\bigr|\ \ge\ \tau.
\]
Conversely, if there exists \(\tau>0\) such that for all \(q\ge 2\) and all \(\chi\neq\chi_0\)
\(
\bigl|[\mathrm{Anom}(w^{\boxtimes q})]_{\chi}\bigr|<\tau
\),
then necessarily \(a=0\).
Therefore, MOM amplification rewrites the ``anomaly too small to observe'' problem as a complete regression calibration: as long as the anomaly cohomology class is nonzero, there exists a finite order \(q\) such that it exceeds any preset threshold when read out at the constant layer.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By Proposition \ref{prop:pom-anom-gap-amplification},
\(
[\mathrm{Anom}(w^{\boxtimes q})]=q\cdot a
\).
If \(a\neq 0\), take a coordinate \(\chi\) such that \(a_\chi\neq 0\); then
\(
|[\mathrm{Anom}(w^{\boxtimes q})]_\chi|
=q|a_\chi|
\to\infty
\);
taking \(q\ge \tau/|a_\chi|\) yields the first statement.
The converse is its contrapositive. \qed
\end{proof}
