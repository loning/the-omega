\begin{conclusion}[Stable reduction of the Latt\`es map at the bad prime \(37\): degree drop caused by a common square factor]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-lattes-badprime-37-degree-drop}
Using the doubling Latt\`es self-map from Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-lattes-doubling},
\[
\Phi(X)=\frac{X^4+2X^2-2X+1}{4X^3-4X+1}\in\QQ(X).
\]
in \(\FF_{37}[X]\) there is a nontrivial common factor, with explicit congruence factorizations
\[
X^4+2X^2-2X+1\equiv (X-5)^2\,(X^2+10X+3)\pmod{37},
\]
\[
4X^3-4X+1\equiv (X-5)^2\,(4X+3)\pmod{37}.
\]
Hence, in the sense of stable reduction (after canceling the common factor), one obtains a reduced map with strict degree drop:
\[
\boxed{\;
\overline{\Phi}(X)=\frac{X^2+10X+3}{4X+3}\in\FF_{37}(X),\qquad \deg\overline{\Phi}=2<\deg\Phi=4\; } .
\]
\par
On the other hand, the cubic control polynomial for Lee--Yang branch points (Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-ramification-critical-values})
\[
g(X):=16X^3-9X^2+1
\]
undergoes double-root collapse in \(\FF_{37}[X]\):
\[
g(X)\equiv 16\,(X-16)(X-5)^2\pmod{37}.
\]
Likewise, the Lee--Yang cubic factor in \(\FF_{37}[y]\) shows repeated-root fusion (Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-badprime-37-common-double-root}):
\[
P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)\equiv -3\,(y-14)(y-6)^2\pmod{37}.
\]
Therefore, the special role of prime \(37\) is intrinsically characterized as follows: at the same bad prime, one simultaneously observes nontrivial branch-image collisions of the weight cover and stable-reduction degree drop of the doubling Latt\`es self-map.
This synchronized degeneration identifies the arithmetic singular source of this ellipticized system at its unique bad prime.
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]

\end{proof}
