This section fixes notation for the entire text at the object layer; unless explicitly stated otherwise, subsequent sections adopt these conventions by default.

\subsection*{Six-Dimensional Superspace and Cut-and-Project}
Let the six-dimensional Euclidean space be decomposed as
$$
E^{6}=E_{\parallel}^{3}\oplus E_{\perp}^{3},
$$
where $E_{\parallel}^{3}$ is the physical subspace and $E_{\perp}^{3}$ is the internal subspace. Let the superlattice be $\ZZ^{6}\subset E^{6}$, and denote the linear projections by
$$
P_{\parallel}:E^{6}\to E_{\parallel}^{3},\qquad P_{\perp}:E^{6}\to E_{\perp}^{3}.
$$
Given a window (acceptance domain) $W\subset E_{\perp}^{3}$ and internal phase $u\in E_{\perp}^{3}$, define the point set
$$
\Lambda(W,u)=\{P_{\parallel}n:\ n\in\ZZ^{6},\ P_{\perp}n\in W+u\}\subset E_{\parallel}^{3}.
$$

\subsection*{Ring Scan and Binary Symbolization (Sturmian Interface)}
The ring phase sequence $\theta_t\in \TT^1=\RR/\ZZ$ is advanced by
$$
\theta_{t+1}=\theta_t+\alpha\pmod 1,
$$
where $\alpha\in\RR/\ZZ$ is the scan parameter. Given a binary partition
$$
\mathcal{P}=\{I_0,I_1\},\qquad I_0\cap I_1=\varnothing,\qquad I_0\cup I_1=\TT^1,
$$
define the binary symbol
$$
w_t=\mathbf{1}_{I_1}(\theta_t)\in\{0,1\}.
$$

\subsection*{Finite Window, Stabilization Kernel, and Zeckendorf Legitimate Language}
For window length $m\in\NN$, denote
$$
\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^{m}.
$$
Define the Zeckendorf legitimate language (golden mean prototype)
$$
X_m^{\mathrm Z}=\{x\in\{0,1\}^{m}:\ x\ \text{contains no subword }11\}.
$$
The stabilization kernel (fold) is a surjection
$$
\mathrm{Fold}_m:\Omega_m\twoheadrightarrow X_m^{\mathrm Z}.
$$
For $x\in X_m^{\mathrm Z}$, the preimage fiber is defined as
$$
\mathcal{F}_m(x)=\mathrm{Fold}_m^{-1}(x)\subseteq \Omega_m.
$$

\subsection*{Readout Operators and Spacetime Image (Main Text Fixed $m=6$ Convention)}
In the main text, we fix the bandwidth $m=6$ by default, and denote $\mathrm{Fold}=\mathrm{Fold}_6$. If $\Pi_O$ denotes the geometric-symbolic factor map determined by observer parameters and $q_6$ is the finite window readout of length $6$, then the readout operator is written as
$$
\mathcal{R}_{O,6}=\mathrm{Fold}_6\circ q_6\circ \Pi_O.
$$
The corresponding ``spacetime image'' is denoted $\mathsf{ST}(O)$, with its definition and induced structure given in the respective chapters.
