\noindent Bounded-family CAP closure of the electroweak scalar potential (interface): within an explicit finite family of low-degree $V(H)$ templates, the unique minimizer is the renormalizable Mexican-hat form $V(H)=-\mu^2 H^\dagger H+\lambda (H^\dagger H)^2$ with $\mu^2>0$ and $\lambda>0$, as it is the minimal candidate that is simultaneously stable and admits EWSB ($\langle H\rangle\neq 0$) in the standard EFT embedding. Using the closed $Z$-scale electroweak normalization, $v=2m_Z\sqrt{15\pi/26}\approx 245.527235\,\mathrm{GeV}$, and with the paper's Higgs reference mass $m_H=125.2\,\mathrm{GeV}$ one obtains $\lambda=m_H^2/(2v^2)\approx 0.130115$ and $\mu^2=m_H^2/2\approx 7843.781250\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$.
