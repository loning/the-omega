\subsubsection{Invertible Fingerprinting of the Golden Constant: Inversion of \texorpdfstring{$\varphi$}{phi} from Constant-Level Nonuniformity and the Phase-Diagram Ledger of Resolution Deficiency}\label{subsubsec:discussion-phi-inversion-phase-ledger}
Most of the auditable interfaces in this manuscript do not rely on long-range fitting or full distributional details, but rather tend to compress structure into \emph{constant-level} verifiable objects: these serve both as minimal fingerprints of mechanism equivalence and as hard anchors for budget/compression narratives.
This paragraph consolidates and emphasizes five among these that are more structurally valuable: they elevate \(\varphi\) from a background constant to an \emph{identifiable parameter recoverable from a single scalar}, and rewrite resolution deficiency from an interpretive phrase into a computable phase diagram and ledger.

\paragraph{Inversion of \texorpdfstring{$\varphi$}{phi} from a Single Scalar: Strict Monotone Invertibility of Power-Divergence Limit Constants}
Let \(\mu_m\) denote the pushforward output distribution of the bin-fold, and \(u_m\) the uniform distribution over the stable layer (see notation in Corollary \ref{cor:fold-bin-two-point-limit-law} and Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-kl-constant}).
For any \(\alpha>1\), take the strictly convex function
\[
f_\alpha(t):=t^\alpha-1-\alpha(t-1)\qquad(t>0).
\]
Then its Csisz\'ar \(f\)-divergence limit
\(
D_{f_\alpha}^\infty=\lim_{m\to\infty}D_{f_\alpha}(\mu_m\Vert u_m)
\)
not only exists (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-f-divergence-collapse}), but yields a closed form with strict monotone invertibility: Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-phi-identifiable-power-divergence} shows
\[
D_{f_\alpha}^\infty
=\frac{1}{5^{\alpha/2}}\Bigl(\varphi^{2\alpha-1}+\varphi^{\alpha-2}\Bigr)-1
=:\mathcal{D}_\alpha(\varphi),
\]
where \(\mathcal{D}_\alpha:(1,\infty)\to\RR\) is strictly increasing.
Thus in this framework, \(\varphi\) is not an externally imposed constant: it can be uniquely recovered by inversion from the single scalar certificate \(D_{f_\alpha}^\infty\), constituting an \emph{auditable identifiable fingerprint} of the mechanism parameter.

\paragraph{Quantile Thresholds and R\'enyi Rate Collapse: Two-State Degeneracy at Exponential Scale, All Structure Condensed to \texorpdfstring{$O(1)$}{O(1)} Drift}
The bin-fold's two-state uniform asymptotics (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-bin-two-state-asymptotic}) and two-point limit law (Corollary \ref{cor:fold-bin-two-point-limit-law}) show that at exponential leading order, fiber sizes are determined solely by the final two states.
This induces piecewise-constant limits for tail quantile thresholds (Corollary \ref{cor:fold-bin-quantile-threshold-constant}): the rescaled quantile
\(
(\varphi/2)^m B^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(\varepsilon)
\)
jumps only between the two states \(\{1,\varphi^{-1}\}\) as \(\varepsilon\) crosses the threshold \(\varepsilon_c=\varphi/\sqrt5\).
\par
A harder compression fact is the R\'enyi rate collapse: for all orders \(q>0\) of the R\'enyi entropy \(H_q(\mu_m)\) (including the Shannon case), there is a uniform limit
\[
\frac{1}{m}H_q(\mu_m)\to\log\varphi
\qquad(q>0),
\]
with deviation only at constant level (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-bin-renyi-rate-collapse}; see also the KL constant law in Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-kl-constant}).
Thus at exponential scale, all fine details degenerate to the same slope \(\log\varphi\), and auditable structure remains only in the \(O(1)\) drift and two-state splitting; this provides a hard anchor for subsequent budget/compression explanations independent of narratives.

\paragraph{Rigid Kinks in the Visible-Capacity–Tail-Spectrum: Ineradicable Phase Transitions at \texorpdfstring{$s=\varphi^{-1}$}{s=phi^{-1}} and \texorpdfstring{$s=1$}{s=1}}
Writing "how much information can be seen under resolution deficiency" as a computable curve can be realized via the degeneracy tail count
\(
N^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(t)
\)
and the continuous capacity curve
\(
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{bin,cont}}_m(T)
\)
satisfying the strict identity
\(
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{bin,cont}}_m(T)=\int_0^T N^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(t)\,\dd t
\)
(Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-degeneracy-tail-capacity-kinks}).
In the rescaled variable \(s\), the tail-spectrum process and capacity curve converge to piecewise-constant/piecewise-linear limits, with two rigid kinks appearing at
\[
s=\varphi^{-1},\qquad s=1
\]
(same proposition).
These two kinks provide a minimal "phase-diagram certificate": they are ineradicable boundary points of the mechanism, capable of elevating visible capacity from an empirical phenomenon to an auditable piecewise structural object.

\paragraph{Two-Scale Expansion of the Gauge Volume Density \texorpdfstring{$g_m$}{gm}: Exponentially Small Corrections in the Volume–Information Ledger}
The bin-fold's fold-gauge group volume
\(
|\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m|=\prod_{w\in X_m}d_m^{\mathrm{bin}}(w)!
\)
induces the gauge volume density \(g_m:=2^{-m}\log|\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m|\);
on the other hand, the fiber information term
\(
\kappa_m=\mathbb{E}_{\mu_m}[\log d_m^{\mathrm{bin}}(W_m)]
\)
is the average logarithmic cost of resolution deficiency (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-kl-constant}).
The relation between the two is not merely heuristic: Stirling's formula with remainder yields a strict second-order expansion with explicit error bounds (Theorem \ref{thm:fold-bin-gauge-volume-stirling-second-order}), which can be further reduced to a two-scale leading term (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-bin-gauge-density-two-scale}):
\[
g_m=\kappa_m-1+\Theta\!\Bigl(\Bigl(\frac{\varphi}{2}\Bigr)^m\Bigr),
\]
with the error term remaining exponentially small and offline-verifiable.
Thus the discrepancy between combinatorial volume (factorial product) and information term (expectation of \(\log d\)) is itself an auditable ledger: the main difference is fixed at \(-1\), with all remaining structure residing in exponentially small corrections.

\paragraph{Functoriality of General \texorpdfstring{$\alpha$}{alpha}-Folds: Embedding the Golden Branch into an Auditable Parametrized Category}
This manuscript does not bind the folding mechanism to the single point \(\varphi\): the general Ostrowski fold
\(\Fold_m^{(\alpha)}\)
is defined by "weighted valuation \(N_\alpha\) \(\to\) Ostrowski normal form \(Z_\alpha\) \(\to\) truncation \(\pi_m^{(\alpha)}\)" (Definition \ref{def:fold-alpha}), satisfying idempotence, surjectivity, and multiscale compatibility
\[
\pi^{(\alpha)}_{m_2\to m_1}\circ \Fold^{(\alpha)}_{m_2}
=\Fold^{(\alpha)}_{m_1}\circ \pi^{(\alpha)}_{m_2\to m_1}
\qquad(m_2\ge m_1)
\]
(Proposition \ref{prop:fold-alpha-basic}-(4)).
More critically, this commutativity under the order "normalize then truncate" has definitional uniqueness (Theorem \ref{thm:normalize-then-truncate-functorial}), thereby fixing the canonical choice for cross-resolution comparison as a verifiable functorial constraint.
\par
Thus, the above constant certificates (divergence-limit inversion, quantile thresholds and R\'enyi rate collapse, tail-spectrum kinks, volume–information ledger) can be organized into a family of \(\alpha\)-parametrized audit kernel objects; the golden branch is merely the simplest and most analytically tractable specialization among them, and thus can serve as the canonical anchor point at the protocol layer.
