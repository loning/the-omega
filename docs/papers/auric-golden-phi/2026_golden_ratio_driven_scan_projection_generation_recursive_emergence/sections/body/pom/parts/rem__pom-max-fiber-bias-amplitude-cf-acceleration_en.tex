\begin{remark}[Three-step acceleration of the bias amplitude and periodic continued fraction locking at \texorpdfstring{$\sqrt5-2$}{sqrt5-2}]\label{rem:pom-max-fiber-bias-amplitude-cf-acceleration}
By the odd closed form of Corollary \ref{cor:pom-max-fiber-achievers-hidden-bit-imbalance},
for \(k=3n+2\) (equivalently \(m=6n+5\)) one has
\[
\Delta_{6n+5}^{(b)}=\Delta_{2(3n+2)+1}^{(b)}=\frac{F_{3n}}{F_{3n+3}}.
\]
Let \(\alpha:=\varphi^{-3}=\sqrt5-2\).
By the identity \(\alpha=\frac{1}{4+\alpha}\), the continued fraction of \(\alpha\) is \(\alpha=[0;\overline{4}]\), and its convergent subsequence \((p_n/q_n)\) satisfies the recursion
\(
p_{n+1}=4p_n+p_{n-1}
\), \(
q_{n+1}=4q_n+q_{n-1}
\).
On the other hand, the Fibonacci subsequences \((F_{3n})\), \((F_{3n+3})\) also satisfy the same second-order recursion
\[
F_{3(n+1)}=4F_{3n}+F_{3(n-1)},\qquad
F_{3(n+1)+3}=4F_{3n+3}+F_{3(n-1)+3},
\]
with initial values matching those of the convergent subsequence of \([0;\overline{4}]\). Hence \(\Delta_{6n+5}^{(b)}=F_{3n}/F_{3n+3}\) is precisely the standard convergent subsequence of \(\alpha\).
By continued fraction theory, the convergent subsequence satisfies the best-type error bound
\[
\boxed{\;
\left|\varphi^{-3}-\Delta_{6n+5}^{(b)}\right|
=\left|\alpha-\frac{F_{3n}}{F_{3n+3}}\right|
<\frac{1}{F_{3n+3}^2}.\;}
\]
Therefore, the ``finite-window observation of the maximum phase bias'' on this sparse subsequence is promoted to an auditable best-approximation certificate for \(\sqrt5-2\).
\end{remark}
