
\section{Langlands semantics: from Hecke spectra to Galois ``source code''}
\label{sec:langlands_semantics}

Up to this point, the chain
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
&\text{scan algebra }A_\alpha
\\
&\Longrightarrow\; \text{modular renormalization flow}
\\
&\Longrightarrow\; q\text{-expansion coefficients}
\\
&\Longrightarrow\; \text{Hecke prime skeleton}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
is closed within Layer~0/1 mathematics. The Langlands program provides a higher-level organizational semantics for the same integer spectra: Hecke eigenvalues are reinterpreted as automorphic parameters, and (in suitable settings) as traces of Frobenius under Galois representations.

\subsection{From modular forms to automorphic representations}
\label{sec:modular_to_automorphic}

For $GL_2/\QQ$, a normalized Hecke eigen cusp form $f$ corresponds to an automorphic representation $\pi_f$ of $\GL_2(\mathbb A_\QQ)$, with local parameters (Satake parameters) determined by the Hecke eigenvalues $a_p$ at almost all primes. The associated $L$-function
\begin{equation}
L(s,f)=\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{a_n}{n^s}
=\prod_p\left(1-a_p p^{-s}+p^{k-1}p^{-2s}\right)^{-1}
\end{equation}
is therefore an automorphic object encoding the full Hecke spectrum; see, e.g., \cite{Bump1997} for standard background on automorphic representations and Hecke parameters.

\subsection{Galois representations: making primes into operators}
\label{sec:galois_representations}

For suitable eigenforms (e.g.\ newforms), one can attach an $\ell$-adic Galois representation
\begin{equation}
\rho_f:\Gal(\overline{\QQ}/\QQ)\to \GL_2(\overline{\QQ}_\ell),
\end{equation}
such that for almost all primes $p$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{tr}\,\rho_f(\Frob_p)=a_p,\qquad \det \rho_f(\Frob_p)=p^{k-1}.
\end{equation}
In this sense, the visible Hecke spectrum is a projection of a deeper symmetry action carried by $\Gal(\overline{\QQ}/\QQ)$ (see, e.g., \cite{Deligne1971Bourbaki,Serre1973,Harris2014SatoTate} for standard accounts).

\subsection{A programmatic extension: a holographic Langlands functor}
\label{sec:holographic_langlands_functor}

The ``stairway'' viewpoint suggests a natural organizational target: promote the closed $GL_2$ chain to a functorial construction across a broader class of symmetry groups.
We record this as a programmatic definition goal.

\begin{definition}[Holographic Langlands functor (working definition)]
\label{def:holographic_langlands_functor}
Let $\mathsf{Scan}$ be a category of scan--readout protocols. An object is a triple
\begin{equation}
(\,A_\alpha,\ \omega_{\mathrm{eff}},\ \mathcal I^{(\varepsilon)}\,),
\end{equation}
where $A_\alpha$ is the rotation algebra, $\omega_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is an effective state, and $\mathcal I^{(\varepsilon)}$ is a finite-resolution instrument (a family of effects and post-measurement updates) at resolution $\varepsilon$. Morphisms are equivalences preserving the Weyl structure and readout statistics (e.g.\ implemented by Morita equivalence together with state pullback, under appropriate interface conditions).

Let $\mathsf{AutRep}(G)$ denote an appropriate category of automorphic representations for a reductive group $G$.
We seek a functor
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{HL}:\mathsf{Scan}\to \mathsf{AutRep}(G)
\end{equation}
such that:
\begin{itemize}
\item a renormalization flow (Gauss/geodesic flow) on $\mathsf{Scan}$ is sent to a natural Hecke/parameter flow on $\mathsf{AutRep}(G)$;
\item cusp discretization and its coefficient spectrum correspond to Hecke eigenvalues in the image;
\item prime-generated closure on the coefficient side corresponds to Euler-product factorization on the automorphic side.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
Definition~\ref{def:holographic_langlands_functor} is not used as a premise in any derivation in this paper. It is a clean mathematical task statement: to turn the closed $GL_2$ toy chain into a functorial framework that can, in principle, be lifted to general $G$.
\end{remark}
