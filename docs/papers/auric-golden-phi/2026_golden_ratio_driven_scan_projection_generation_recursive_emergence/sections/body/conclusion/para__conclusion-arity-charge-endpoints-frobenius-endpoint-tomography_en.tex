\paragraph{Zero-temperature endpoint branching constants, completed Frobenius towers, and finite certificate interfaces for endpoint tomography}
Beyond the emphasis throughout this work on ``main scales (Perron roots/pressure) determining exponential growth,'' further anchoring on endpoint expansions of algebraic branches, completed congruence towers, and endpoint tomographic inversion allows a unified compression of several seemingly analytic constant/stability conclusions into finite-dimensional, integer-coefficient, auditable certificate chains:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Arity-charge zero-temperature Puiseux refinement elevates leading terms to constant-term certificates.}
  For the maximal positive real root branch of \(P_7(\Lambda,q)=0\), the zero-temperature endpoint expansion not only yields \(\lambda(q)\sim \sqrt{3q}\); its constant term and higher-order corrections are rigidly locked by the branching structure of the algebraic curve at infinity (Proposition~\ref{prop:real-input-40-arity-charge-zero-temp-expansion}).
  The sub-leading positive real growth branch also possesses an auditable three-term closed form (Proposition~\ref{prop:real-input-40-arity-charge-subbranch-puiseux}), thereby refining the ``unified slow-mode baseline'' from asymptotic scaling to comparable endpoint-ejection fingerprints.

  \item \textbf{The analytic derivative at zero charge provides a fit-free perturbative strength constant.}
  By explicit factorization of \(P_7(\Lambda,0)\) and the implicit function theorem, the map \(q\mapsto\lambda(q)\) is analytic in a neighborhood of \(0\) and \(\lambda'(0)\) admits a closed-form algebraic expression (Proposition~\ref{prop:real-input-40-arity-charge-derivative-q0}), compressing the ``spectral response rate from zero to positive charge'' into a single scalar certificate verifiable offline.

  \item \textbf{Completed Dwork congruences close in Chebyshev form and collapse to pure Frobenius iteration \texorpdfstring{$\bmod p$}{mod p}.}
  The \(p^k\)-level congruence tower of the completed ghost strictly closes as the Chebyshev--Dwork congruence chain \(S\mapsto C_p(S)\) (Theorem~\ref{thm:discussion-completed-dwork-chebyshev}), and further collapses \(\bmod p\) to the Frobenius rigidity \(\widehat a_{p^k}(S)\equiv S^{p^k}\) (Corollary~\ref{cor:completed-dwork-frobenius-tower}).

  \item \textbf{Endpoint profiles are C-finite: Hankel rank and Prony inversion parametrize off-axis zeros as finite-sample certificates.}
  Under finite Blaschke caliber, the endpoint radial absorption profile is a rational function in the \(q\)-coordinate and yields explicit pole-pair locations (Theorem~\ref{thm:xi-endpoint-absorption-inversion-uniqueness}), so its Taylor coefficients satisfy constant-coefficient recursions and induce Hankel-rank certificates (Corollary~\ref{cor:xi-endpoint-profile-cfinite-hankel-rank}); furthermore, finite coefficient data can in principle recover all \((\gamma_k,\delta_k)\) via Prony/kernel methods (Corollary~\ref{cor:xi-endpoint-profile-prony-invertibility}).
\end{enumerate}
