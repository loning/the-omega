\section{Appendix A: Proof of the Scale-Conjugacy Proposition (Proposition \ref{prop:conjugacy})}

For any $x\in\RR$, let $[x]_L$ denote its equivalence class modulo $L$. Define $h_L([x]_L)=[x/L]_1$.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $h_L$ is well defined: if $x-y\in L\ZZ$, then $(x/L)-(y/L)\in\ZZ$, hence $[x/L]_1=[y/L]_1$.
  \item $h_L$ is a homeomorphism: its inverse is $h_L^{-1}([u]_1)=[Lu]_L$. Continuity follows from the quotient topology and continuity of linear maps.
  \item Conjugacy: note that
  \[
  h_L(R_\delta^{(L)}([x]_L))=h_L([x+\delta]_L)=[(x+\delta)/L]_1=[x/L+\delta/L]_1,
  \]
  while
  \[
  R_{\delta/L}^{(1)}(h_L([x]_L))=R_{\delta/L}^{(1)}([x/L]_1)=[x/L+\delta/L]_1.
  \]
  Therefore $h_L\circ R_\delta^{(L)}=R_{\delta/L}^{(1)}\circ h_L$.
\end{enumerate}
