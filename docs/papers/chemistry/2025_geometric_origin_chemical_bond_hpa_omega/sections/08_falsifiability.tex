\section{Interface corrections and falsifiability routes}
\label{sec:falsifiability}

The closed-theory values $\alpha_{\mathrm{geo}}$ and $\mu_{\mathrm{geo}}$ are not claimed to coincide identically with operational constants; instead, the HPA layer discipline requires that discrepancies be isolated as explicit interface inputs, not absorbed as hidden retuning. This section records the interface variables and outlines falsifiable spectroscopy routes.

\subsection{Two small mismatches and two matching inputs}
For $\alpha_{\mathrm{em}}$, define the inverse-impedance gap $\Delta\alpha^{-1}=\alpha_{\mathrm{geo}}^{-1}-\alpha_{\mathrm{exp}}^{-1}$ and the multiplicative matching input $s_\alpha=\exp(\Delta\alpha^{-1})$ (Section~\ref{sec:alpha_geo}). For $\mu$, define $s_\mu=\mu_{\mathrm{exp}}/\mu_{\mathrm{geo}}$ (Section~\ref{sec:mu_geo}). Numerically, the relative gaps are at the $10^{-6}$--$10^{-5}$ level \cite{Ma2025PCG,CODATA2022RMP}.

In chemistry-facing applications, these matching inputs propagate into:
\begin{itemize}
\item absolute unit conversions (energies in eV, lengths in Angstrom) through $E_h$ and $a_0$ (controlled by $s_\alpha$),
\item nuclear kinematics through $\mu$ (controlled by $s_\mu$),
\item any additional interface corrections through explicit, low-parameter matching inputs (e.g.\ resolution parameters) rather than hidden per-system retuning.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Precision spectroscopy as a falsifiability channel}
Beyond the scale-setting claims of the main text, one may ask whether finite-resolution readout consistency leaves additional, structured corrections to molecular energies. A mismatch-augmented interface template is recorded in Appendix~\ref{app:interface_phase_friction}. If such corrections are physically present, they must leave residual signatures in systems where standard theory is already extremely accurate. Suitable candidates include few-body molecules such as $\mathrm{H}_2^+$ and $\mathrm{HD}^+$, which are among the benchmark systems discussed in the CODATA constants review as inputs and cross-checks across theory and precision measurement \cite{CODATA2022RMP}.

Concretely, one compares measured transition frequencies $\nu_{\mathrm{exp}}$ with state-of-the-art theory predictions $\nu_{\mathrm{th}}(\alpha_{\mathrm{em}},\mu,\ldots)$ (including QED, relativistic, and finite-size corrections as appropriate) and studies the residual
\[
\Delta\nu := \nu_{\mathrm{exp}}-\nu_{\mathrm{th}}.
\]
In the HPA interface viewpoint, any additional readout-consistency correction must enter through explicit, low-parameter interface quantities (e.g.\ a resolution proxy $\varepsilon$ or an induced mismatch variable such as $E_N$ in Appendix~\ref{app:interface_phase_friction}), so that $\Delta\nu$ admits a structured decomposition rather than an unconstrained per-transition fit.

The falsifiability logic is:
\begin{itemize}
\item If experiments and standard theory agree within uncertainties, then either $\lambda$ is effectively negligible at the relevant resolution, or the functional form of $\mathcal{D}_\varepsilon$ and $S_{\mathrm{pf}}$ must be constrained to yield suppressed corrections.
\item If persistent residuals exist beyond known QED/nuclear corrections, then an interface model must explain not only magnitudes but also selection rules and systematic dependence on resolution-related proxies. Discrepancy-based corrections are highly structured and therefore restrictive.
\end{itemize}
In either case, the mismatch-cost proposal is testable.

\subsection{What this paper does not claim}
We do not claim that the closed-theory derivations alone replace quantum chemistry, nor that the interface functional \eqref{eq:chem_free_energy_template} is already uniquely fixed. The claim is narrower: once $(\alpha_{\mathrm{em}},\mu)$ are treated as protocol invariants, the two main structural prerequisites of chemistry (absolute scale and BO hierarchy) become necessary consequences of the same geometric data, and any additional readout-consistency physics must be encoded in explicit, falsifiable interface terms.
