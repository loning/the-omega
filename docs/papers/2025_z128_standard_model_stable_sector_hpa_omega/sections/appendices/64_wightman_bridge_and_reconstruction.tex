\subsection{Bridge to Wightman formulations (hybrid AQFT--Wightman interface)}
\label{app:wightman_bridge_and_reconstruction}

This appendix records a hybrid bridge between the algebraic-QFT (local net) package used in this manuscript and the Wightman (operator-valued distribution) formulation.
The bridge is recorded in an audit-facing way: we separate (i) clear implication directions that are structurally standard from (ii) technical prerequisites (domains, energy bounds, existence of generating fields) that are explicitly treated as scope boundaries or open items in the present manuscript.

\subsubsection{Wightman data (structural package)}
\paragraph{Fields as operator-valued distributions.}
Let $\Phi$ denote a collection of operator-valued tempered distributions on $\mathbb{M}^{1,3}$ acting on a Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$ with a distinguished vacuum vector $\Omega$.
Smeared fields $\Phi(f)$ are defined for test functions $f$ and act on a dense invariant domain $\mathcal{D}\subset\mathcal{H}$.

\paragraph{Axioms (informal structural list).}
The Wightman formulation typically includes: Poincar\'e covariance, spectrum condition, existence/uniqueness of the vacuum, locality (fields commute/anticommute at spacelike separation), and suitable regularity (temperedness) and cyclicity assumptions.

\AuditTag In this manuscript we do not attempt to reproduce a full Wightman-axiom development as a theorem-level consequence of the tick-only finite core.
Rather, we record the bridge as an interface map between two established mathematical packages, with explicit boundary conditions.

\subsubsection{From Wightman to local nets (one direction)}
\paragraph{Net generation (structural map).}
Given Wightman fields $\Phi$, one may define a local net by letting $\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O})$ be the $C^\ast$-algebra (or von Neumann algebra) generated by bounded functions of smeared fields $\Phi(f)$ with $\mathrm{supp}(f)\subset\mathcal{O}$.
Locality of fields induces microcausality of the net.

\AuditTag This direction depends on domain control and on how one passes from unbounded field operators to bounded operator algebras.
When invoked, these steps must be accompanied by explicit assumptions on domains and functional calculus.

\subsubsection{From local nets to Wightman fields (the other direction)}
\paragraph{Reconstruction problem.}
Starting from a local net and a vacuum state (Appendices~\ref{app:aqft_axioms_local_nets}--\ref{app:aqft_states_representations_gns_nets}), reconstructing pointlike fields is a nontrivial problem.
In general, additional assumptions are needed to ensure the existence of generating fields and to control their localization and domain properties.

\paragraph{Explicit scope boundary.}
\AuditTag In this manuscript, field reconstruction is treated as a bridge target rather than a closed theorem:
\begin{itemize}
\item Existence of pointlike fields that generate the net is not assumed unless explicitly stated in a scoped interface pack.
\item Any use of LSZ-type scattering formulas (Appendix~\ref{app:scattering_haag_ruelle_lsz_interface}) will explicitly state the required regularity (mass gap, asymptotic completeness assumptions, etc.) as audit-facing prerequisites.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Hybrid reading for the present paper}
\paragraph{Why the hybrid view is useful here.}
\InterfaceTag The algebraic net language is naturally compatible with the paper's protocol/readout viewpoint: ``observables'' are algebraic objects, states are linear functionals, and locality is expressed by commutativity constraints.
The Wightman language is useful as an interpretive and computational bridge to textbook field operators and correlators, but it carries additional domain and regularity subtleties that must remain explicit.

\subsubsection{Minimal failure points and falsifiability hooks (audit)}
\label{subsec:wightman_bridge_minimal_failure_points}
\AuditTag The bridge recorded here is \emph{not} closed from the tick-only folding core.
When the bridge is used as an interface pack, the minimal failure points are the canonical W1--W3 records in Appendix~\ref{app:minimal_failure_point_templates}.
In any of these failure modes, the paper reverts to the AQFT net language as the primary bookkeeping package (Appendix~\ref{app:aqft_axioms_local_nets}--\ref{app:aqft_states_representations_gns_nets}) and treats correlator/field statements as external matching inputs rather than as reconstructed objects.

\paragraph{Ledger discipline.}
\AuditTag Any later use of a Wightman-style statement will be ledger-tagged either as (i) a mathematical foundation theorem, or (ii) an explicitly scoped interface assumption pack, and will not be used as a premise for the finite folding core.
