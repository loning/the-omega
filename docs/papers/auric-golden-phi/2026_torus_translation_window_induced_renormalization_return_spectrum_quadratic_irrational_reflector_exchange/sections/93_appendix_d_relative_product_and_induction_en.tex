\section{Appendix D: Fiber Stability Under Window-Pullback Induction: Relative Direct Product and Relative Weak Mixing}
\label{app:relative-product}

\subsection*{D.1 Pushforward of Return Times}

Let $\pi:(X,\mu,T)\to(Y,\nu,S)$ be a factor map, take $F\subset Y$ with $\nu(F)>0$, and set $E=\pi^{-1}(F)$. Then for $\mu$-almost every $x\in E$, we have
\[
r_E(x)=r_F(\pi(x)).
\]
Thus induction yields a natural factor map
\[
\pi_E:(E,\mu_E,T_E)\to(F,\nu_F,S_F).
\]

\subsection*{D.2 Relative Direct Product and Induction Commutation}

Consider the relative direct product
\[
X\times_Y X=\{(x_1,x_2)\in X\times X:\pi(x_1)=\pi(x_2)\},
\]
with the relative direct product measure $\mu\times_\nu\mu$. Take $E\times_F E\subset X\times_Y X$ as the corresponding window. By D.1, the two points share the same base point on the fiber, so their return times coincide, and thus the induced map satisfies
\[
(T\times T)_{E\times_F E}\cong T_E\times T_E
\]
(in the sense of measure isomorphism). Moreover, the induced measure agrees with the relative direct product of the induced measures, i.e., after normalization becomes $\mu_E\times_{\nu_F}\mu_E$.

\subsection*{D.3 Preservation of Relative Weak Mixing}

If the extension $\pi:X\to Y$ is relatively weakly mixing, this is equivalent to $(T\times T)$ being ergodic on $(X\times_Y X,\mu\times_\nu\mu)$. By the commutation of D.2 and the standard argument that ``ergodicity on a positive-measure section under induction remains ergodic'', we can show that the induced extension
\[
\pi_E:\mathrm{Ind}(X,E)\to \mathrm{Ind}(Y,F)
\]
remains relatively weakly mixing. This conclusion provides a verifiable sufficient condition for condition 2 in the main text Theorem \ref{thm:reflector-induction-template}: if the original system is relatively weakly mixing over the Kronecker factor, then induction does not produce new isometric factors in the fiber direction.
