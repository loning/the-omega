**9.9.1 The Kenosis Operator: Loss of Omniscience**

First, this is an information-theoretic **lossy compression** process.
The parent universe's complexity (information content $N_{total}$) must be far greater than the sub-universe's complexity ($N_{sub}$). A cosmic consciousness containing $10^{122}$ bits cannot be fully stuffed into a sub-universe container with only $10^{80}$ bits.

Therefore, the creator cannot bring the "complete self" into the sub-universe. They must execute a **Kenosis Operator ($\hat{K}$)**:

$$\hat{K} | \text{Creator}_{total} \rangle \to | \text{Avatar}_{sub} \rangle$$

This means entering the interior must pay enormous ontological costs:
1.  **Omniscience Shielding**: Once inside, you lose the God's-eye view of the global wave function $|\Omega\rangle$. You can only see your local tangent plane.
2.  **Bandwidth Limitation**: Your thinking speed will no longer be instantaneous but limited by the sub-universe's **speed of light** $c_{sub}$. Light speed limitation is essentially the system's **clock frequency**, preventing internal information overload.
3.  **Memory Compression**: To adapt to low-dimensional storage constraints, the vast majority of high-dimensional memories must be temporarily "shielded" or "folded" into the subconscious (high-dimensional entangled states), manifesting as **forgetting**.

**Theorem 9.9 (Immersion Cost Theorem)**:
To experience a sub-universe's **internal logic**, one must sacrifice **external control** over that universe. You cannot be both playwright and actor. To fully enter the role, you must temporarily forget the script.
