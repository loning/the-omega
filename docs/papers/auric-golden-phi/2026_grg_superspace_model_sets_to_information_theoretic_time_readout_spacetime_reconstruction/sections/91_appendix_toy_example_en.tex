\section{A worked toy example: rotation readout, stabilization, and entropy time}\label{sec:appendix:toy}

This appendix sketches a minimal worked example illustrating the full chain ``geometry $\rightarrow$ readout $\rightarrow$ stabilization $\rightarrow$ fibers $\rightarrow$ entropy-rate time'' in a setting where all objects are explicit.

\subsection{Object layer: irrational rotation}
Let $\TT=\RR/\ZZ$ and $T_\alpha(x)=x+\alpha \pmod{1}$ with $\alpha\notin\QQ$. Equip $\TT$ with Haar measure $\mu$.

\subsection{Protocol layer: binary readout}
Fix an interval $I\subset\TT$ and define a readout $R:\TT\to\{0,1\}$ by $R(x)=\mathds{1}_I(x)$. Given an initial phase $x_0$, define the readout sequence $a_t=R(T_\alpha^t(x_0))$.

\subsection{Stabilization and fibers}
Fix a word length $m$ and consider the length-$m$ prefix $w=a_{0:m-1}\in\{0,1\}^m$. A stabilization map $\Fold_m$ (Definition~\ref{def:fold}) compresses $w$ into a legal word $\Fold_m(w)\in X_m$ (no adjacent ones). The fiber $\mathcal{F}_m(x)$ is the set of all raw prefixes that stabilize to the same type $x$.

At the level of internal phases, the stabilized fiber is
\[
\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}_m(x)=\{x_0\in\TT:\ \Fold_m(a_{0:m-1}(x_0))=x\},
\]
which is a measurable subset of $\TT$ (a finite union of intervals for simple stabilizations).

\subsection{Entropy-rate time}
Cylinder sets in this example are
\[
C(a_{0:t-1})=\{x_0\in\TT:\ R(T_\alpha^k(x_0))=a_k,\ k=0,\dots,t-1\}.
\]
If the partition induced by $R$ is generating, the SMB theorem applies and gives
\[
\tau(t)=-\log\mu\bigl(C(a_{0:t-1})\bigr)\sim t\,h_\mu(T_\alpha,\mathcal{P})
\]
for $\mu$-almost every $x_0$, where $\mathcal{P}=\{I,\TT\setminus I\}$. This makes explicit how an intrinsic arrow (linear growth of $\tau$) arises from refinement without introducing an external time parameter at the object layer.

In the particular case of irrational rotations, one has $h_\mu(T_\alpha)=0$ \cite{Walters1982}, so the same refinement functional still satisfies $\tau(t)/t\to 0$ and typically grows sublinearly (Section~\ref{sec:info-time:zero-ks}). For the Sturmian regime induced by a nondegenerate two-interval coding, the cylinder sets can be computed as explicit interval intersections on $\TT$, and the observed growth is consistent with $\tau(t)=O(\log t)$ (Figure~\ref{fig:demo1d-tau}), with empirical block-entropy rates decaying toward zero (Figure~\ref{fig:demo1d-entropy}). The numerical outputs are generated by \texttt{scripts/run\_all.py}.
