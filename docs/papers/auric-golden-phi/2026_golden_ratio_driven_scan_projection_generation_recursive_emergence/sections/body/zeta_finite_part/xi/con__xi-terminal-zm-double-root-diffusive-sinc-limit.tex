\begin{conclusion}[Diffusive scaling of a double characteristic root and the \texorpdfstring{$\operatorname{sinc}$}{sinc} limit law]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-double-root-diffusive-sinc-limit}
Retain the polynomial family \(Z_m(y)\) from Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-order4-rational}.
Equivalently, \(\{Z_m(y)\}_{m\ge 0}\) is uniquely determined by the initial values
\[
Z_0(y)=1,\quad
Z_1(y)=1+y,\quad
Z_2(y)=2+2y,\quad
Z_3(y)=2+5y+y^2
\]
and the fourth-order recurrence
\[
Z_m(y)
=Z_{m-1}(y)+(2y+1)Z_{m-2}(y)-Z_{m-3}(y)-y(y+1)Z_{m-4}(y)
\qquad(m\ge 4)
\]
Define
\[
\operatorname{sinc}(z):=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{\sin z}{z}, & z\neq 0,\\[1mm]
1, & z=0.
\end{cases}
\]
Then for any compact \(K\subset\CC\), as \(m\to\infty\) one has uniform convergence
\[
\boxed{\;
\sup_{u\in K}\left\lvert
\frac{2}{m}\,Z_m\!\left(-\frac{u}{m^{2}}\right)
-\operatorname{sinc}\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{u}{2}}\right)
\right\rvert\longrightarrow 0\;}
\]
where \(u\mapsto \operatorname{sinc}\!\left(\sqrt{u/2}\right)\) is uniquely determined by its power-series expansion, and therefore, as an entire function of \(u\), requires no square-root branch choice.

In particular, fix \(j\ge 1\).
For sufficiently large \(m\), \(Z_m\) has at least \(j\) distinct real roots in a neighborhood \((-\delta/m^2,0)\);
ordered from \(0\) toward \(-\infty\), write
\(
r_{m,1}>r_{m,2}>\cdots>r_{m,j}
\).
Then one has limiting quantization
\[
\boxed{\;
-m^{2}\,r_{m,j}\longrightarrow 2\pi^{2}j^{2}\;}
\qquad(m\to\infty),
\]
hence
\(
r_{m,j}=-\dfrac{2\pi^{2}j^{2}}{m^{2}}+o(m^{-2})
\).
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
From the rational generating function in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-order4-rational},
at \(y=0\) the denominator factors as \(D(t,0)=(1-t)^2(1+t)\), so \(t=1\) is a double pole,
corresponding to a double-root degeneration at \(\lambda=1\) in the recurrence characteristic equation.
When \(y=-u/m^2\), the adjacent pair of simple poles split from this double pole with spacing \(O(m^{-1})\);
local residue expansion and the limit
\(
(1\pm i\alpha/m)^{-m}\to e^{\mp i\alpha}
\)
yield the scaling limit whose leading term is \(\operatorname{sinc}(\sqrt{u/2})\);
uniformity follows from estimates uniform in \(u\) on compact sets.
Zero calibration follows by applying Hurwitz's theorem to the above uniform convergence and the simple zeros of
\(\operatorname{sinc}(\sqrt{u/2})\),
\(
u=2\pi^{2}j^{2}
\).

\end{proof}

\begin{conclusion}[Critical window width at \texorpdfstring{$y=0$}{y=0} is \texorpdfstring{$m^{-2}$}{m^{-2}}: the unique scale between linear and exponential growth]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-critical-window-m2}
At \(y=0\), the double characteristic root described in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-hankel-rank4} makes \(Z_m(0)\) grow only linearly;
for any fixed \(y>0\), the spectral radius is strictly greater than \(1\), so \(Z_m(y)\) has strict exponential growth.
The diffusive-scaling limit in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-double-root-diffusive-sinc-limit} shows that
the nontrivial transition between these growth mechanisms is rigidly locked in the unique critical window
\[
y=-\frac{u}{m^{2}},
\qquad
u\asymp 1,
\]
and is uniformly controlled there by the entire function \(u\mapsto \operatorname{sinc}(\sqrt{u/2})\).
Accordingly, the zero cluster near \(0\) exhibits \(m^{-2}\)-scale quantized calibration within this window
(Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-double-root-diffusive-sinc-limit}),
with leading-order spacing completely determined by the integer-multiple zeros of \(\sin\).
\end{conclusion}
