\section{Optional microscopic internal-fiber input (octonion/\texorpdfstring{$G_2$}{G2} route)}
\label{app:internal_fiber_g2_optional}

\AuditTag This appendix records an optional microscopic architecture input that may be adopted at the interface layer.
It is not used as a premise in the theorem-level folding core.
Its purpose is to supply a concrete microscopic route behind the gauge-factor candidate-family component that is otherwise left as a declared interface family in Proposition~\ref{prop:channel_to_gauge} (OP1).

\begin{assumption}[M2$^\star$ (On-site internal fiber and octonion/\texorpdfstring{$G_2$}{G2} organization)]
\label{ass:m2star_internal_fiber_g2}
Assume that, at the site level of the readout/addressing graph, the effective local Hilbert space admits a factorization
\[
\mathcal{H}_x \cong \mathcal{H}_{x,\mathrm{orb}}\otimes \mathcal{H}_{x,\mathrm{int}},
\qquad \dim \mathcal{H}_{x,\mathrm{int}}=8,
\]
and that the internal sector is organized by an octonionic/\texorpdfstring{$G_2$}{G2} structure as a microscopic ansatz for Standard-Model-like internal quantum numbers, in the sense recorded in \cite{Ma2025FoP}.
\end{assumption}

\begin{remark}[Interface role for OP1]
\AuditTag Assumption~\ref{ass:m2star_internal_fiber_g2} is a \emph{microscopic} route to the gauge candidate family: it provides an explicit internal-sector organization that can underwrite a three-factor gauge-dictionary viewpoint in which (i) a local rephasing sector yields an abelian $U(1)$ factor and (ii) non-abelian compact factors arise as internal probability-preserving redundancies tied to the internal organization.
Relative to the extended input set in which Assumption~\ref{ass:m2star_internal_fiber_g2} is adopted, the ``candidate family'' component of Proposition~\ref{prop:channel_to_gauge} is supplied by a declared microscopic architecture rather than by a hand-declared abstract hypothesis family.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[No equivalence claim]
\AuditTag The microscopic input here is not claimed to be equivalent to the physics-consensus matching inputs of Appendix~\ref{app:physics_consensus_inputs}.
It should be read as one optional implementation route that is compatible with those inputs at the level of effective description, not as a bidirectional derivation.
\end{remark}
