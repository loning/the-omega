\subsection{Residual closure of three generators: from commutation certificates to computable anomaly signatures}\label{subsec:pom-three-generator-residual-closure}

This subsection tightens the three generators
$$
\mathrm{PROJ}_u,\qquad E_m,\qquad \mathrm{LIFT}_G
$$
into a complete framework of ``parameterized projection word syntax + auditable residual fingerprints''.
Subsection \ref{subsec:pom-derivable-calculus-closure} has already provided the interface-level definition of the projection word $2$-category $\mathbf{PW}$ (Definition \ref{def:pom-projword-2cat}) and demonstrated executable normalization examples involving \(E_m,\mathrm{LIFT}_G,\mathrm{PROJ}_u\) (Remark \ref{rem:pom-projword-lift-proj-normalizer-demo}).
The key point here is: instead of treating ``\(E_m\) and \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) commute'' as an unconditional syntactic assertion, we compress the \emph{failure of commutativity} into a computable ``twisted finite-part anomaly signature'', thereby upgrading ``algorithmic equivalence'' from narrative to decidable naturality conditions.

\subsubsection{Anomaly signature: carrying residuals via finite-part constants of non-trivial twisted channels}\label{subsec:pom-anom-signature}

\paragraph{Motivation}
At the core interface level, the semantics of \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) are given by group extensions/character decomposition: for a finite abelian label group \(G\), its \(\zeta\) (or determinant) can be decomposed into a family of twisted channels according to character families (see Section \ref{sec:zeta-finite-part}, especially the DFT/inversion framework in Remark \ref{prop:arity-chebotarev-mertens-fourier}).
On the other hand, \(E_m\) is the conditional expectation induced by folding (Definition \ref{def:pom-proj-norm} and Proposition \ref{prop:fold-conditional-expectation}), which averages along fibers and satisfies idempotent/tower properties on the resolution tower.
In the ``pure product fiber'' case, they can commute strictly (Beck--Chevalley type squares), but in the general ``label-folding fiber coupling'' case, commutation breaking pushes information into non-trivial twisted channels; and this paper already has an auditable constant-level interface to carry this breaking: the Abel/Hadamard finite-part constant \(\log\mathfrak{M}(\theta)\) (Definition \ref{def:abel-finite-part}).

\begin{definition}[Twisted finite-part anomaly signature]\label{def:pom-anom-signature}
Take a potential kernel \(K\) (or equivalently a weighted matrix family \(M_\theta\)), and a lift semantics for a finite abelian label group \(G\).
For each character \(\chi\in\widehat G\), denote by \(\zeta_{\theta,\chi}\) the \(\zeta\) interface of the \(\chi\)-twisted channel (determinant factor in the character decomposition sense), and define its Abel/Hadamard finite-part constant \(\log\mathfrak{M}_\chi(\theta)\) following Definition \ref{def:abel-finite-part}.
Define the \textbf{anomaly signature} as the family of finite-part constants for non-trivial characters
$$
\mathsf{Anom}_{G}(K;\theta)
:=\Bigl(\log\mathfrak{M}_\chi(\theta)\Bigr)_{\chi\in\widehat G\setminus\{\chi_0\}},
$$
where \(\chi_0\) is the trivial character.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Why choose finite-part rather than just spectral radius ratio]\label{rem:pom-anom-why-finite-part}
The spectral radius ratio \(\rho_\chi/\lambda\) quantifies the exponential gap of ``slow modes'' and is the main-scale certificate for twist breaking; but it is insensitive to differences among different twisted channels at the same exponential level.
The finite-part constant \(\log\mathfrak{M}_\chi(\theta)\) captures constant-level drift in the prime-orbit Mertens main term (Proposition \ref{prop:abel-finite-part-mobius-zeta}), and can stably carry the constant-level residual output of ``commutation failure''; this is precisely the minimal supplement needed to transform ``non-commutativity'' from metaphysics to auditable signature.
\end{remark}

\subsubsection{Commutation with residuals: upgrading rewrite certificates to auditable anomaly terms}\label{subsec:pom-residual-swap}

\begin{proposition}[Exchange certificate with anomaly for \texorpdfstring{$E_m$}{Em}--\texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{LIFT}_G$}{LIFT} (interface version)]\label{prop:pom-em-lift-residual-swap}
In the projection word $2$-category \(\mathbf{PW}\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-projword-2cat}), there exists a class of ``commutation certificates'' that rewrite the composition
\(
E_m\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_G
\)
into
\(
\mathrm{LIFT}_G\circ E_m
\)
with an auditable residual signature \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\):
for any object \(\mathcal{I}(m;K;\kappa)\), if lift labels and folding fibers are mechanistically decoupled (equivalently: satisfy a Beck--Chevalley type pullback square condition), then the anomaly signature of this commutation certificate is zero (all non-trivial twisted channels have finite-part constants consistent with the trivial channel up to zero drift); in the general case, the residual of commutation failure is completely carried by the finite-part constant family \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\) of non-trivial twisted channels.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
This proposition is an interface-level encapsulation: the character decomposition of \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) splits the post-lift \(\zeta\)/finite-part output into twisted factor families on \(\widehat G\) (Remark \ref{prop:arity-chebotarev-mertens-fourier}), while the semantics of \(E_m\) is the conditional expectation along folding fibers (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-conditional-expectation}).
When they satisfy the Beck--Chevalley type pullback condition, conditional expectation and label lift are in direct product structure, thus strictly commute on each twisted channel, with zero anomaly signature.
In the general case, commutation is no longer a strict equality; but since all auditable outputs in this paper are ultimately extracted through the same \(\zeta_\theta=1/\det(I-zM_\theta)\) and finite-part interface (Definition \ref{def:abel-finite-part}), the \emph{observable part} of commutation breaking must fall into the finite-part difference of ``non-trivial twisted channels relative to the trivial channel'', i.e., \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Zero anomaly \texorpdfstring{$\Longleftrightarrow$}{<->} Beck--Chevalley pullback (closed-loop interface version)]\label{cor:pom-zero-anom-pullback-exactness}
Under the setting of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-em-lift-residual-swap}, interpret ``strict commutation'' as: for each twisted character channel \(\chi\in\widehat G\), on the closed-loop interface adopted in this paper
\[
\text{finite kernel/matrix family}\Rightarrow \zeta \Rightarrow \log\mathfrak{M}
\]
the compositions \(E_m\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_G\) and \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\circ E_m\) yield the same channel output (equivalently: the square is a discrete Beck--Chevalley type pullback square).
Then the following equivalent characterization holds:
\[
\boxed{\;
\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
E_m\ \text{and}\ \mathrm{LIFT}_G\ \text{strictly commute on all twisted channels (in interface sense)}.}
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Proposition \ref{prop:pom-em-lift-residual-swap} has compressed the entire observable residual of commutation failure into \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\);
therefore ``residual is zero'' and ``strict commutation on closed-loop interface'' are mutually equivalent.
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{Universal commutative quotient: lifting residuals from ``numerical signature'' to ``group direction erased by folding''}\label{subsec:pom-bc-quotient}
Proposition \ref{prop:pom-em-lift-residual-swap} and Corollary \ref{cor:pom-zero-anom-pullback-exactness} compress commutation failure of \(E_m\) and \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) into the constant-level anomaly signature \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\).
At the level of ``derivable calculus'', numerical residuals alone are insufficient to form a computable structural object: we still need to ontologize ``from which part of group information the commutation failure comes'' into a constructible object.
\par
This paragraph gives a pure group-theoretic strictification: for a fixed resolution \(m\), construct a \textbf{universal group quotient} \(G\twoheadrightarrow G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\) such that commutation becomes \emph{strict} in the interface sense on \(G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\); and this group quotient is maximal (universal), thus characterizing ``the maximal group register still visible from the \(E_m\) perspective'', while its kernel is ``the group direction erased by folding''.

\begin{definition}[Fiber-visible equivalence and Beck--Chevalley commutative quotient]\label{def:pom-bc-quotient}
Fix object \(\mathcal{I}(m;K;\kappa)\) and resolution \(m\).
Let folding be \(\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m\), and denote by \(E_m\) its induced conditional expectation/fiber averaging interface (Proposition \ref{prop:fold-conditional-expectation}).
Let lift semantics be given by a finite abelian group \(G\) and a (one-step or length-\(m\)) group label function
\[
\ell:\Omega_m\to G
\]
(its origin can be the cocycle along length-\(m\) paths accumulated on kernel edges; in fiber Fourier framework also written as \(\ell\), see Definition \ref{def:pom-fiber-fourier-coeff}).
\par
Define the ``fiber indistinguishable'' relation as
\[
a\sim_m b\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \Fold_m(a)=\Fold_m(b)\qquad(a,b\in\Omega_m),
\]
and define the \textbf{fiber-invariant subgroup} (group direction erased by folding) as
\[
N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m
:=\left\langle\ \ell(a)\ell(b)^{-1}\ :\ a,b\in\Omega_m,\ a\sim_m b\ \right\rangle\ \le\ G.
\]
Let \(\pi_m:G\twoheadrightarrow G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m:=G/N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\) be the quotient map, and denote \(\bar\ell_m:=\pi_m\circ \ell:\Omega_m\to G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\).
Then \(\bar\ell_m\) is constant on each folding fiber, thus inducing a well-defined \textbf{visible label}
\[
\ell^{\mathrm{vis}}_m:X_m\to G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m,
\qquad
\ell^{\mathrm{vis}}_m\bigl(\Fold_m(a)\bigr)=\bar\ell_m(a).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Strict commutativization: anomaly is zero on \(G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\)]\label{prop:pom-bc-quotient-strictify}
Following the notation in Definition \ref{def:pom-bc-quotient}.
Replace the group axis of \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) with \(G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\) (i.e., use \(\bar\ell_m\) as lift label), then \(E_m\) and \(\mathrm{LIFT}_{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\) strictly commute in the closed-loop interface sense; equivalently,
\[
\mathsf{Anom}_{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}(K;\theta)=0
\]
holds (for all considered potential parameters \(\theta\) and all non-trivial twisted channels).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By construction, \(\bar\ell_m\) depends only on \(\Fold_m(a)\), i.e., there exists \(\ell^{\mathrm{vis}}_m\) such that \(\bar\ell_m=\ell^{\mathrm{vis}}_m\circ \Fold_m\).
Therefore for any character \(\overline\chi\in\widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\), the function
\(
f_{\overline\chi}:=\overline\chi\circ \bar\ell_m
=
\overline\chi\circ \ell^{\mathrm{vis}}_m\circ \Fold_m
\)
is \(\sigma(\Fold_m)\)-measurable, hence satisfies \(E_m f_{\overline\chi}=f_{\overline\chi}\).
This is precisely the strict version of Beck--Chevalley type ``label-folding fiber decoupling'': on each visible twisted channel, conditional expectation no longer introduces cross-channel mixing, thus the commutation square of \(E_m\) and \(\mathrm{LIFT}_{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\) strictly closes on the closed-loop interface.
By Corollary \ref{cor:pom-zero-anom-pullback-exactness}, \(\mathsf{Anom}_{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}(K;\theta)=0\). $\qed$
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Universality (maximality): any strictly commuting group quotient must factorize]\label{prop:pom-bc-quotient-universal}
Following the setting in Definition \ref{def:pom-bc-quotient}.
Let \(\phi:G\twoheadrightarrow H\) be any surjective homomorphism.
If \(\phi\circ \ell\) is invariant on folding fibers (equivalently: there exists \(\ell_H^{\mathrm{vis}}:X_m\to H\) such that \(\phi\circ\ell=\ell_H^{\mathrm{vis}}\circ\Fold_m\), also equivalent to \(E_m\) and \(\mathrm{LIFT}_H\) strictly commuting in closed-loop interface sense), then necessarily
\[
N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\ \le\ \ker(\phi),
\]
thus there exists a unique surjective homomorphism \(\bar\phi:G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\twoheadrightarrow H\) such that \(\phi=\bar\phi\circ \pi_m\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For any \(a\sim_m b\), by hypothesis \(\phi\circ\ell\) is invariant on fibers, so \(\phi(\ell(a))=\phi(\ell(b))\), i.e., \(\phi(\ell(a)\ell(b)^{-1})=1\).
Therefore \(\ell(a)\ell(b)^{-1}\in\ker(\phi)\).
By the generating definition of \(N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\), we get \(N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\le \ker(\phi)\).
Hence \(\phi\) induces a unique \(\bar\phi\) on the quotient group \(G/N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\), satisfying \(\phi=\bar\phi\circ\pi_m\). $\qed$
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Computability: from fiber enumeration to standard form of quotient group]\label{rem:pom-bc-quotient-computable}
The construction in Definition \ref{def:pom-bc-quotient} is directly computable on finite resolutions: given computable folding \(\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m\) and computable label \(\ell:\Omega_m\to G\), enumerate each fiber \(\Fold_m^{-1}(x)\) and collect generators
\(
\ell(a)\ell(b)^{-1}
\)
to obtain a finitely generated set for \(N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\).
When \(G\) is a finite abelian group, one can represent \(G\) as a direct product of finite cyclic groups, and compute Smith normal form on the generator matrix, thus obtaining the invariant decomposition and character domain \(\widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\) of the quotient group \(G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m=G/N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\) in polynomial time.
Therefore the ``strict commutative quotient'' not only exists, but can be directly audited as a discrete output in the audit pipeline.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[Support of anomaly: only from \texorpdfstring{$\widehat{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}$}{N^BC_m}-direction]\label{cor:pom-anom-supported-on-bc-kernel}
Following the setting in Definition \ref{def:pom-bc-quotient}.
Identify \(\widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\) as a subgroup of \(\widehat G\) (i.e., \(\widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\cong\{\chi\in\widehat G:\chi|_{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}=1\}\)).
Then for any \(\chi\in\widehat G\), if \(\chi|_{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}=1\), then the commutation residual of the corresponding twisted channel is zero:
\[
\chi|_{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}=1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\bigl(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\bigr)_{\chi}=0.
\]
Therefore any nonzero anomaly coordinate can only appear in the \(\widehat G\setminus \widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\) direction (i.e., characters non-trivial on \(N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\)).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
If \(\chi|_{N^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}=1\), then \(\chi\) factorizes through some \(\overline\chi\in\widehat{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m}\) and quotient map \(\pi_m\).
Therefore the \(\chi\)-twisted channel is equivalent to a visible twisted channel on \(G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\).
By Proposition \ref{prop:pom-bc-quotient-strictify}, all visible twisted channels have zero anomaly, thus the corresponding coordinate \(\bigl(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\bigr)_{\chi}=0\). $\qed$
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Visible register inverse system on resolution tower]\label{cor:pom-bc-inverse-system}
For any \(m_2\ge m_1\), since conditional expectations satisfy tower property \(E_{m_1}\circ E_{m_2}=E_{m_1}\) (see \S\ref{subsec:pom-conditional-expectation}), the fiber indistinguishable relation at layer \(m_2\) refines that at layer \(m_1\), hence
\[
N^{\mathrm{BC}}_{m_2}\ \le\ N^{\mathrm{BC}}_{m_1},
\qquad\text{thus}\qquad
G^{\mathrm{BC}}_{m_2}\twoheadrightarrow G^{\mathrm{BC}}_{m_1}.
\]
Therefore \(\{G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m\}_{m\ge 1}\) forms an inverse system, and we can define its inverse limit
\[
G^{\mathrm{BC}}_\infty:=\varprojlim_m G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m.
\]
This limit object can be interpreted as: the visible group register skeleton that strictly commutes with folding under ``infinite-resolution readout''.
\end{corollary}

\subsubsection{Composition law of anomaly signatures: as \texorpdfstring{$2$}{2}-cohomology class of \texorpdfstring{$\mathbf{PW}$}{PW}}\label{subsec:pom-anom-cohomology}
Viewing the ``labeled commutation certificate'' of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-em-lift-residual-swap} as a \(2\)-morphism generator of \(\mathbf{PW}\), the key benefit is: anomaly is no longer an afterthought patch, but a residual class that can be composed at the rewrite level and only undergoes boundary transformation when changing reduction paths.

\begin{definition}[Anomaly accumulation and normalized anomaly]\label{def:pom-anom-accumulation}
Fix a parameterized object \(\mathcal{I}(m;K;\kappa)\), a finite abelian lift group \(G\), and potential parameter \(\theta\).
In the subfragment generated solely by \(\mathrm{PROJ}_u,E_m,\mathrm{LIFT}_G\), let
\[
\mathcal{A}_G:=\RR^{\widehat G\setminus\{\chi_0\}}
\]
with componentwise addition as group operation.
For any rewrite certificate (\(2\)-morphism) \(\alpha:w\Rightarrow w'\), define its \textbf{anomaly accumulation}
\(\mathrm{anom}(\alpha)\in\mathcal{A}_G\)
as follows: after decomposing \(\alpha\) into a sequence of local rewrite steps, take the label of each step and sum them, where
(i) idempotent/tower collapses/strictly commutative steps have label \(0\);
(ii) each \(E_m\)--\(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) commutation step with residual has label \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-signature}).
Given a normal form selection operator \(N\) and its normalization certificate family \(\eta_w:w\Rightarrow N(w)\), define the \textbf{normalized anomaly} of word as
\[
\mathrm{Anom}(w):=\mathrm{anom}(\eta_w).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Additive composition law and boundary term]\label{prop:pom-anom-additive-up-to-boundary}
Under the setting of Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-accumulation}, \(\mathrm{anom}\) is strictly additive for vertical composition of \(2\)-morphisms: if \(\alpha:w\Rightarrow w'\) and \(\beta:w'\Rightarrow w''\) are composable, then
\[
\mathrm{anom}(\beta\circ\alpha)=\mathrm{anom}(\beta)+\mathrm{anom}(\alpha).
\]
Furthermore, for any composable \(1\)-morphisms \(w_1,w_2\), there exists a \(2\)-cochain \(B(w_2,w_1)\in\mathcal{A}_G\) depending only on the normal form selection, such that
\[
\mathrm{Anom}(w_2\circ w_1)
=
\mathrm{Anom}(w_2)+\mathrm{Anom}(w_1)+\partial B(w_2,w_1),
\]
where \(\partial B\) denotes the boundary term arising from comparing the two normalization paths ``first normalize separately then merge'' vs ``directly normalize'' (equivalently: changing normalization certificate family \(\{\eta_w\}\) changes \(\mathrm{Anom}\) only by a coboundary).
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[Zero anomaly is path-invariant]\label{cor:pom-anom-cohomology-class}
``Whether anomaly is zero'' is independent of reduction path/reduction gauge: if \(\mathrm{Anom}(w)=0\) under some normalization choice, then \(\mathrm{Anom}'(w)\) obtained under any other normalization choice is also zero.
Equivalently, \(\mathrm{Anom}\) defines a computable (\(2\)-)cohomology class, whose vanishing characterizes whether commutation failure can be completely eliminated by rewriting.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Strict additivity of anomaly class (horizontal composition)]\label{cor:pom-anom-class-additive}
Under the notation of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-anom-additive-up-to-boundary}, for any composable \(1\)-morphisms \(w_1,w_2\), their anomaly cohomology class satisfies strict additive law
\[
\boxed{\;
[\mathrm{Anom}(w_2\circ w_1)]
=
[\mathrm{Anom}(w_2)]
+
[\mathrm{Anom}(w_1)]\;}
\]
(because the boundary term \(\partial B(w_2,w_1)\) vanishes when taking cohomology class).
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[\(2\)-confluence with residuals: internalizing ``non-commutativity'' as central label]\label{rem:pom-residual-2confluence}
In the three-generator fragment, we do not require \(E_m\) and \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) to commute \emph{strictly}, but rather require their commutation failure to be completely carried by an additive central label group \(\mathcal{A}_G\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-accumulation}).
Thus ``confluence'' should be understood as a \(2\)-confluence framework with residuals: within a fixed complexity bound, any two rewrite paths can join to the same interface normal form (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-three-gen-interface-normal-form}), while their residual difference (if any) must fall into the boundary term of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-anom-additive-up-to-boundary}; therefore \(\bigl(\mathrm{NF}(w),[\mathrm{Anom}(w)]\bigr)\) (normal form + anomaly cohomology class) becomes a path invariant.
This is precisely the encapsulation at calculus level of Corollary \ref{cor:pom-three-gen-normal-form-decidable}'s ``normal form equality + finite signature audit'': non-commutativity no longer breaks derivability, but is elevated to composable, auditable \(2\)-morphism data.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Discrete connection viewpoint: anomaly as curvature (closed-layer gauge field strength)]\label{rem:pom-anom-as-curvature}
Viewing the parameter pair \((m,G)\) of object \(\mathcal{I}(m;K;\kappa)\) as two axes of a discrete ``base plane'':
resolution/modulus axis \(m\) (with conditional expectation \(E_m\) providing coarse-graining parallel transport), and internal label axis \(G\) (with \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) providing label lift parallel transport).
Then the certificate
\[
E_m\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_G\ \Rightarrow\ \mathrm{LIFT}_G\circ E_m
\]
given by Proposition \ref{prop:pom-em-lift-residual-swap}
can be understood as the \emph{curvature \(2\)-cell} of this discrete connection on a fundamental square:
when lift and folding fibers decouple (Beck--Chevalley type square pullback), this curvature degenerates to identity \(2\)-morphism; in general, the ``square not closing'' pushes observable residuals into non-trivial twisted channels, and is minimally compressed into the finite-part constant family \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-signature}).
Therefore in this paper's closed-layer primitives framework, gauge field can be defined without a priori continuous fiber bundles/Lie groups: it is minimally given by discrete parallel transports \((E_m,\mathrm{LIFT}_G)\) and their curvature certificate \(\mathsf{Anom}_G\).
The cohomology invariance of Corollary \ref{cor:pom-anom-cohomology-class} corresponds to the fact that ``changing reduction gauge (discrete gauge choice) changes only a coboundary, not whether curvature is zero''.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Toward completion: critical pairs determine additional anomaly generators]\label{rem:pom-anom-completion}
The engineering implication of Corollary \ref{cor:pom-anom-cohomology-class} is: when extending the projection word fragment (e.g., adding \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) or more general lift/cocycle), one only needs to enumerate critical pairs between new and old generators and perform completion on the rewrite system; newly appearing ``non-strict commutativity'' will automatically manifest as \(\mathcal{A}\)-valued labels; anomaly signature thus becomes a composable residual cluster, rather than ad hoc patches for individual rules.
For example, in the four-generator extension, the new coupling of \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) and \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) is uniformly reduced by \((\mathrm{RMOML})\) (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-rmoml-sound}), while its constant-layer auditable fingerprint is organized by the higher-order anomaly signature tower \(\mathsf{Anom}^{(q)}_G\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-signature-tower}).
Bounded critical pair enumeration and completion audit on the three-generator fragment see Remark \ref{rem:pom-threegen-kb-completion-audit}.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Moment anomaly amplification law (\texorpdfstring{$q$}{q}-fold gap amplification)]\label{prop:pom-anom-moment-amplification}
In the four-generator extension fragment (Definition \ref{def:pom-four-gen-rewrite-rules}), take any three-generator projection word \(w\).
Extend the ``central label accumulation'' framework of Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-accumulation} canonically to reduction trajectories involving \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\) according to the same reduction certificate norm (i.e., still accumulate \(\mathsf{Anom}_G\) label only at each \(E_m\)--\(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) residual exchange step, and take quotient by coboundary).
Then for any integer \(q\ge 2\), the anomaly cohomology class satisfies linear amplification law:
\[
\boxed{\;
[\mathrm{Anom}(\mathrm{MOM}_q\circ w)]
=
q\cdot [\mathrm{Anom}(w)].\;}
\]
Therefore, if the anomaly class difference of two words is too small at constant level to be numerically verified, one can choose higher-order moment gates to linearly amplify this difference by \(q\), while remaining within the semantic domain of finite-core compilable/auditable.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
According to the fiber-product semantics of \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\), it lifts the underlying orbit constraint to \(q\) parallel synchronized worlds; therefore any reduction certificate trajectory for \(w\) corresponds to ``lift by copy'' \(q\) parallel certificate trajectories on \(\mathrm{MOM}_q\circ w\).
Since anomaly label group \(\mathcal{A}_G\) has componentwise addition as central operation in Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-accumulation}, and \(\mathrm{anom}\) is strictly additive for vertical composition (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-anom-additive-up-to-boundary}), accumulated labels grow linearly with the number of parallel copies, yielding the stated \(q\)-fold amplification; taking cohomology class eliminates boundary terms, conclusion is independent of reduction path choice. $\qed$
\end{proof}

script provides clustered completion candidate corrections by critical pair type in JSON.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Confluence is not absolute confluence, but confluence modulo anomaly (bounded audit version)]\label{prop:pom-confluence-mod-anom-audited}
Within the same complexity bound as Remark \ref{rem:pom-threegen-kb-completion-audit}, the three-generator fragment \((\mathrm{PROJ}_u,E_m,\mathrm{LIFT}_G)\) satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Normal form unique)} For any audited overlap critical pair, both local reduction branches can join to the same interface normal form, thus normal form is a path invariant within this complexity class (Table \ref{tab:pom_projword_three_gen_kb_completion_audit}'s \(\#\mathrm{NF\ mismatch}=0\)).
  \item \textbf{(Residuals differ only by boundary)} Reduction paths may produce different residual labels (Table \ref{tab:pom_projword_three_gen_kb_completion_audit}'s residual mismatch), but their difference must fall into the boundary term (coboundary) of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-anom-additive-up-to-boundary}; therefore the anomaly cohomology class \([\mathrm{Anom}(w)]\) and ``whether anomaly is zero'' are both path invariants (Corollary \ref{cor:pom-anom-cohomology-class}).
\end{enumerate}
Therefore, taking the equality relation of projection words as ``normal form consistent + anomaly class consistent'' is natural: changing reduction priority changes only a coboundary, not whether anomaly class is zero.
\end{proposition}

\begin{definition}[Algorithmic equivalence (normal form + anomaly class)]\label{def:pom-algorithmic-equivalence-nf-anom}
Within the above complexity bounds, for any two projection words \(w_1,w_2\), define
\[
w_1\sim w_2
\iff
\mathrm{NF}(w_1)=\mathrm{NF}(w_2)
\ \text{and}\
[\mathrm{Anom}(w_1)]=[\mathrm{Anom}(w_2)].
\]
Here \(\mathrm{NF}\) can take the interface normal form representative of Proposition \ref{prop:pom-three-gen-interface-normal-form}, while \([\mathrm{Anom}(w)]\) is the anomaly cohomology class determined by Proposition \ref{prop:pom-anom-additive-up-to-boundary} (representative computed under any fixed reduction certificate gauge, quotiented by coboundary).
In this paper's implementation we fix one normalizer and certificate gauge, thus this equivalence relation can be directly decided by Corollary \ref{cor:pom-three-gen-normal-form-decidable}'s ``normal form parameters + finite anomaly signature audit''.
\end{definition}

\begin{corollary}[Normal form and finite signature equality for \texorpdfstring{$(\mathrm{PROJ}_u,E_m,\mathrm{LIFT}_G)$}{(PROJ,Em,LIFT)}]\label{cor:pom-three-gen-normal-form-decidable}
Fix word complexity bound (e.g., limit \(m\), limit lift group size, limit temperature parameter sampling set).
Then any projection word generated solely by \(\mathrm{PROJ}_u,E_m,\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) can be reduced to a class of interface normal forms (see Remark \ref{rem:pom-zeta-normal-form}'s compilation order) with attached finite anomaly signature
\(
\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)
\)
finite sampling set.
Therefore within this complexity class, ``algorithmic equivalence'' (naturality-sense commutativity/isomorphism) reduces to:
comparing normal form parameters \((u,m,G)\) and comparing whether anomaly signatures are consistent at finitely many sampling points.
\end{corollary}

\begin{definition}[Conservative semantic functor \texorpdfstring{$\mathsf{Sem}$}{Sem} (bounded complexity slice)]\label{def:pom-conservative-sem-functor}
Let \(\Theta\) denote a \emph{finite audit slice} of interface parameters in this paper: e.g., fix resolution bound \(m\le m_{\max}\), fix lift group list \(\mathcal{G}_{\max}\) (thus \(|G|\le G_{\max}\)), fix moment order window \(q\le q_{\max}\), and for temperature \(u\in(0,1]\) and potential parameter \(\theta\) take a finite sampling grid.
Viewing each generator as a \(\Theta\)-family, obtain parameterized projection word \(2\)-category denoted \(\mathbf{PW}*\Theta\).
\par
On this slice, define two computable semantic output domains:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Signature domain \(\mathbf{Sig}*\Theta\):} Records constant-layer and normal-form-layer auditable fingerprints. For word \(w\) take
  \[
  \mathsf{Sig}(w):=\Bigl(\mathrm{NF}(w),\ G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m(w),\ [\mathrm{Anom}(w)],\ \{\mathsf{Anom}_{G}(K;\theta)\}_{\theta\in\Theta}\Bigr),
  \]
  where \(\mathrm{NF}(w)\) is interface normal form representative (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-three-gen-interface-normal-form}),
  \(G^{\mathrm{BC}}_m(w)\) is the universal commutative quotient of Definition \ref{def:pom-bc-quotient} (recording ``the maximal group register still visible from \(E_m\) perspective''),
  \([\mathrm{Anom}(w)]\) is anomaly \(2\)-cohomology class (Proposition \ref{prop:pom-anom-additive-up-to-boundary}),
  while \(\{\mathsf{Anom}_{G}(K;\theta)\}_{\theta\in\Theta}\) is finite sampling signature family (Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-signature}).
  \item \textbf{Spectral domain \(\mathbf{Spec}*\Theta\):} Records exponential main-scale spectral resource readout. Typical choice is: for each channel parameter \((\chi,u)\) and integer \(q\) output the Perron root of weighted collision moment-kernel
  \(r^{(w)}_{q,\chi}(u)\), or equivalently the pressure/budget curve \(\Sigma_{\mathrm{space}}(w)(q;\chi,u)\) and its discrete convex closure type normal form \(\mathrm{Ty}_{\mathrm{space}}(w;\chi,u)\) (\S\ref{subsec:pom-spectral-resource-type}).
\end{enumerate}
Thus obtain compositional semantic assignment (computable functor on slice)
\[
\boxed{\;
\mathsf{Sem}:\mathbf{PW}*\Theta\longrightarrow \mathbf{Sig}*\Theta\times \mathbf{Spec}*\Theta,\qquad
\mathsf{Sem}(w):=(\mathsf{Sig}(w),\mathsf{Spec}(w)).\;}
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Semantic conservativity on bounded slice (decidable core of natural isomorphism)]\label{thm:pom-sem-conservative-on-slice}
Within the same complexity bounds as Corollary \ref{cor:pom-three-gen-normal-form-decidable}, \(\mathsf{Sem}\) is conservative: if two words \(w_1,w_2\) satisfy
\(
\mathsf{Sem}(w_1)=\mathsf{Sem}(w_2)
\),
then there exists invertible \(2\)-morphism (natural isomorphism) such that \(w_1\simeq w_2\); equivalently \(w_1\sim w_2\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-algorithmic-equivalence-nf-anom}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\(\mathsf{Sem}(w_1)=\mathsf{Sem}(w_2)\) implies signature components equal, in particular \(\mathrm{NF}(w_1)=\mathrm{NF}(w_2)\) and \([\mathrm{Anom}(w_1)]=[\mathrm{Anom}(w_2)]\).
By Definition \ref{def:pom-algorithmic-equivalence-nf-anom} get \(w_1\sim w_2\), and by decidability framework of Corollary \ref{cor:pom-three-gen-normal-form-decidable} obtain corresponding invertible certificate.
Spectral component can be viewed as redundant audit in this conclusion (but provides additional discriminating power in subsequent type-directed rewriting and multiscale budget comparison). $\qed$
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Symbolic certificate upgrade: replacing finite sampling with twisted characteristic polynomial]\label{rem:pom-anom-symbolic-certificate}
The ``finite sampling signature'' of Corollary \ref{cor:pom-three-gen-normal-form-decidable} is engineering-friendly auditable framework;
if one wishes to further upgrade equivalence to \emph{fully formalized} algebraic equality certificate, one can symbolize twisted channels under fixed complexity bounds:
for each non-trivial character \(\chi\in\widehat G\setminus\{\chi_0\}\), compile projection word \(w\) (with \(\mathrm{PROJ}_u\) temperature parameter) into finite-dimensional twisted matrix \(M_{\chi}^{(w)}(u)\), and define
\[
\Delta_\chi(w;z,u):=\det\bigl(I-z\,M_{\chi}^{(w)}(u)\bigr).
\]
In this paper's interface setting, matrix entries are generated by finite graph kernel edge weights (typically powers of \(u\) or finitely generated analytic functions), thus within given complexity class \(\Delta_\chi\) is computable symbolic object (polynomial in \(z\), coefficients in computable algebraic field over \(u\)).
Once two implementations have completely identical \(\Delta_\chi\) for all non-trivial \(\chi\), the corresponding twisted \(\zeta\) and finite-part constants are identical, thus anomaly signatures identical; at this point ``auditable'' equivalence is upgraded to ``provable'' equivalence.
\end{remark}

\subsubsection{Computable rewrite system and interface normal form for three generators}\label{subsec:pom-three-gen-rewrite-normal}

This paragraph brings ``semantic interface'' back to word level: gives a suite of rewrite rules depending only on parameters, reducing any projection word generated by
\(\mathrm{PROJ}_u,E_m,\mathrm{LIFT}_G\)
to an executable interface normal form, and explicitly collecting non-commutative residuals as anomaly signature (Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-signature}).

\begin{definition}[Rewrite rule family on three-generator fragment]\label{def:pom-three-gen-rewrite-rules}
In $\mathbf{PW}$ take the subfragment generated solely by \(\mathrm{PROJ}_u\), \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\), \(E_m\).
Define rewrite rule family on it as follows (all $2$-morphism certificates, omitting unit and associator isomorphisms):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Temperature gate merging (power semigroup):}
  $$
  (\mathrm{RPROJ})\quad
  \mathrm{PROJ}_u\circ \mathrm{PROJ}_v\ \Rightarrow\ \mathrm{PROJ}_{uv}.
  $$
  \item \textbf{Lift merging (direct product lift):}
  $$
  (\mathrm{RLIFT})\quad
  \mathrm{LIFT}_G\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_H\ \Rightarrow\ \mathrm{LIFT}_{G\times H}.
  $$
  \item \textbf{Conditional expectation tower collapse:}
  $$
  (\mathrm{RE})\quad
  E_{m_1}\circ E_{m_2}\ \Rightarrow\ E_{\min(m_1,m_2)}.
  $$
  \item \textbf{Temperature gate and lift commute (weight pullback):}
  $$
  (\mathrm{RPL})\quad
  \mathrm{PROJ}_u\circ \mathrm{LIFT}_G\ \Rightarrow\ \mathrm{LIFT}_G\circ \mathrm{PROJ}_u.
  $$
  \item \textbf{\(E_m\)--lift exchange with residual:}
  Using Proposition \ref{prop:pom-em-lift-residual-swap} as certificate framework, reduce \(E_m\circ\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) to \(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\circ E_m\) and output anomaly signature \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\) as auditable residual.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Termination of three-generator rewriting (inverse order measure)]\label{prop:pom-three-gen-termination}
Take normal order
\(
\mathrm{LIFT}\prec \mathrm{PROJ}\prec E
\).
For any three-generator projection word \(W\), let \(\ell(W)\) be word length (number of generators), and define \textbf{inverse order measure}
\[
\mu(W):=\#\{(i,j):i<j,\ \text{position }i\text{ and position }j\text{'s generators violate order }\prec\}.
\]
Then under rule family \ref{def:pom-three-gen-rewrite-rules}, each rewrite strictly decreases lexicographic measure
\(
(\ell(W),\mu(W))
\).
Therefore rewriting in this fragment necessarily terminates; residual anomaly is recorded as \(2\)-morphism label, not affecting \(1\)-morphism descent measure.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Rules \((\mathrm{RPROJ}),(\mathrm{RLIFT}),(\mathrm{RE})\) collapse same-type gate compositions into one gate, thus strictly decrease \(\ell(W)\).
Rule \((\mathrm{RPL})\) and \(E_m\)--\(\mathrm{LIFT}_G\) exchange with residual only change relative order of gates without increasing word length; since they exchange ``inverse pairs'' into proper order, \(\mu(W)\) strictly decreases.
Therefore each step strictly decreases lexicographic measure \((\ell,\mu)\), no infinite rewrite chain exists. $\qed$
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[On scope of \texorpdfstring{$(\mathrm{RLIFT})$}{RLIFT}]\label{rem:pom-rlift-scope}
Rule \((\mathrm{RLIFT})\) reflects ``juxtaposed lift of independent axes'': merging two mutually independent finite label lifts into one lift to direct product group.
In more general group extensions (e.g., non-trivial semidirect products or cocycle twists), corresponding merger law should replace direct product with fiber product/composition of extension data; this paper's auditable implementation mainly uses finite abelian labels and their character decomposition, thus the above formulation suffices to cover numerical interfaces and rewrite examples in this paper.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Cross anomaly: an auditable condition for \texorpdfstring{$(\mathrm{RLIFT})$}{RLIFT} independence]\label{rem:pom-rlift-cross-anom-test}
Let \(G,H\) be finite abelian groups, then \(\widehat{G\times H}\cong\widehat G\times\widehat H\).
For any \((\chi,\psi)\in\widehat G\times\widehat H\), denote by \(\chi\otimes\psi\) the product character on \(G\times H\)
\((g,h)\mapsto \chi(g)\psi(h)\), and denote \(\chi_0,\psi_0\) the trivial characters.
Under anomaly signature framework (Definition \ref{def:pom-anom-signature}), define \textbf{cross anomaly} as
\[
\Delta^{\mathrm{sep}}_{\chi,\psi}(\theta)
:=
\log\mathfrak{M}_{\chi\otimes\psi}(\theta)
-\log\mathfrak{M}_{\chi\otimes\psi_0}(\theta)
-\log\mathfrak{M}_{\chi_0\otimes\psi}(\theta),
\qquad
\chi\neq\chi_0,\ \psi\neq\psi_0.
\]
If semantics of two lifts are truly independent axis juxtaposition (corresponding to \((\mathrm{RLIFT})\)'s direct product lift), then for each sampling parameter \(\theta\) should have
\(\Delta^{\mathrm{sep}}_{\chi,\psi}(\theta)=0\): equivalently there exists auditable factorization
\(\zeta_{\theta,\chi\otimes\psi}=\zeta_{\theta,\chi\otimes\psi_0}\cdot \zeta_{\theta,\chi_0\otimes\psi}\)
on twisted channels,
thus strict additivity at finite-part constant level.
Therefore nonzero \(\Delta^{\mathrm{sep}}\) provides an auditable ``cross-axis coupling fingerprint'', indicating lift merger should not be simplified to direct product, but requires introducing extension data (e.g., cocycle/semidirect product) or explicitly leaving coupling in residual term.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Interface normal form: reducible order for \texorpdfstring{$(\mathrm{PROJ}_u,E_m,\mathrm{LIFT}_G)$}{(PROJ,Em,LIFT)}]\label{prop:pom-three-gen-interface-normal-form}
Under rule family \ref{def:pom-three-gen-rewrite-rules}, any three-generator projection word can be rewritten to the following ``interface normal form'':
$$
\boxed{\;
\mathrm{LIFT}_G\ \circ\ \mathrm{PROJ}_u\ \circ\ E_m
\;}
$$
with accompanying anomaly signature output \(\mathsf{Anom}_G(K;\theta)\) (nonzero when non-commutative residuals exist).
Furthermore, plugging this normal form into Remark \ref{rem:pom-zeta-normal-form}'s \(\zeta\)/primitive/finite-part compilation convention yields an executable equality-testing procedure from word-level normalization to spectral fingerprint audit.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Rules \((\mathrm{RPROJ}),(\mathrm{RLIFT}),(\mathrm{RE})\) eliminate redundant compositions of same-type gates; rule \((\mathrm{RPL})\) exchanges \(\mathrm{PROJ}_u\) to the right of lift; while exchange of \(E_m\) and lift is given by Proposition \ref{prop:pom-em-lift-residual-swap} (strict commutation when zero anomaly, otherwise output anomaly signature carrying residual).
Therefore any word can be ordered into \(\mathrm{LIFT}\to \mathrm{PROJ}\to E\) form; then use merger laws to compress multiple occurrences of same-type gates, yielding stated normal form and accompanying anomaly signature. $\qed$
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{Higher-order cross anomaly and axis factorizability strictification: Möbius complete decomposition}\label{subsec:pom-rlift-higher-cross-anom}

Remark \ref{rem:pom-rlift-cross-anom-test} gives two-body cross anomaly \(\Delta^{\mathrm{sep}}\) as necessary audit condition for \((\mathrm{RLIFT})\) independence.
When number of juxtaposed lift axes \(k\ge 3\), to obtain \emph{sufficient and necessary} computable discrimination, one needs to introduce full-order anomaly family \(\{\Delta_S\}_{|S|\ge 2}\) on boolean sublattice, and strictify ``Euler/direct product factorizability'' into a Möbius inversion identity.

\begin{definition}[Higher-order cross anomaly family \texorpdfstring{$\Delta_S$}{DeltaS}]\label{def:pom-higher-cross-anomaly}
Take \(k\ge 2\), let \(G_1,\dots,G_k\) be finite abelian groups, denote
\[
G:=\prod_{i=1}^k G_i,
\qquad
\widehat G\cong \prod_{i=1}^k \widehat{G_i}.
\]
For each sampling parameter \(\theta\) and each \(\chi\in\widehat G\), suppose given Abel/Hadamard finite-part constant \(\log\mathfrak{M}_{\chi}(\theta)\in\RR\), and stipulate normalization
\[
\log\mathfrak{M}_{\chi^0}(\theta)=0,
\qquad
\chi^0:=(\chi_{1,0},\dots,\chi_{k,0})
\]
(trivial characters in each coordinate).
Let \([k]:=\{1,\dots,k\}\).
For any nonempty subset \(S\subseteq[k]\) and any sub-tuple \(\chi_S=(\chi_i)_{i\in S}\in\prod_{i\in S}\widehat{G_i}\), define \(S\)-order cross anomaly as
\[
\Delta_S(\chi_S;\theta)
:=\sum_{T\subseteq S}(-1)^{|S|-|T|}\,\log\mathfrak{M}_{\chi^{(T)}}(\theta),
\]
where \(\chi^{(T)}\in\widehat G\) denotes: coordinate \(i\in T\) takes \(\chi_i\), coordinate \(i\notin T\) takes trivial character \(\chi_{i,0}\).
When \(|S|=2\), this definition agrees with \(\Delta^{\mathrm{sep}}\) of Remark \ref{rem:pom-rlift-cross-anom-test} (under normalization \(\log\mathfrak{M}_{\chi^0}=0\)).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Möbius complete decomposition]\label{thm:pom-mobius-complete-decomposition}
Under setting of Definition \ref{def:pom-higher-cross-anomaly}, for any \(\chi=(\chi_1,\dots,\chi_k)\in\widehat G\) and any \(\theta\), the identity holds
\[
\boxed{\;
\log\mathfrak{M}_\chi(\theta)
=\sum_{\varnothing\neq S\subseteq[k]}\Delta_S(\chi_S;\theta)\; }.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
This identity is equivalent to Möbius inversion on boolean lattice points.
Let \(f(\chi):=\log\mathfrak{M}_\chi(\theta)\) (fix \(\theta\)).
For each coordinate \(i\in[k]\) define difference operator
\[
(\delta_i f)(\chi):=f(\chi)-f(\chi^{(i\mapsto 0)}),
\]
and for any nonempty \(S\subseteq[k]\) let \(\delta_S:=\prod_{i\in S}\delta_i\) (operators commute).
Expanding gives
\(
(\delta_S f)(\chi^{(S)})=\sum_{T\subseteq S}(-1)^{|S|-|T|}f(\chi^{(T)})
\),
thus \(\Delta_S(\chi_S;\theta)=(\delta_S f)(\chi^{(S)})\).
On other hand,
\[
\mathrm{Id}=\prod_{i=1}^k\bigl((\mathrm{Id}-\delta_i)+\delta_i\bigr)
=\sum_{S\subseteq[k]}\delta_S\prod_{i\notin S}(\mathrm{Id}-\delta_i).
\]
Apply to \(f\) and evaluate at \(\chi\); using \(f(\chi^0)=0\) and coordinatewise annihilation effect of \(\prod_{i\notin S}(\mathrm{Id}-\delta_i)\), get
\(
f(\chi)=\sum_{\varnothing\neq S\subseteq[k]}(\delta_S f)(\chi^{(S)})
\),
i.e., stated decomposition. $\qed$
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sufficient and necessary condition for axis factorizability strictification]\label{thm:pom-axis-decomposability-iff}
Under above setting, the following two conditions are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(Axis factorizability)} There exist function families \(L_i:\widehat{G_i}\to\RR\) (satisfying \(L_i(\chi_{i,0})=0\)), such that for all \(\chi\in\widehat G\)
  \[
  \log\mathfrak{M}_{\chi}(\theta)=\sum_{i=1}^k L_i(\chi_i).
  \]
  \item \textbf{(All higher-order cross anomalies vanish)} For all \(|S|\ge 2\) and all \(\chi_S\),
  \[
  \Delta_S(\chi_S;\theta)=0.
  \]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\((1)\Rightarrow(2)\): If \(\log\mathfrak{M}_\chi(\theta)=\sum_i L_i(\chi_i)\), then when \(|S|\ge 2\),
\(\Delta_S\) is difference over at least two coordinates, must be zero.
\par
\((2)\Rightarrow(1)\): By Theorem \ref{thm:pom-mobius-complete-decomposition},
\[
\log\mathfrak{M}_\chi(\theta)=\sum_{i=1}^k\Delta_{\{i\}}(\chi_i;\theta)+\sum_{|S|\ge 2}\Delta_S(\chi_S;\theta).
\]
Under (2) second term vanishes, let \(L_i(\chi_i):=\Delta_{\{i\}}(\chi_i;\theta)\) yields (1). $\qed$
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Pairwise audit insufficient: existence of pure three-body coupling]\label{prop:pom-pairwise-cross-anom-insufficient}
When \(k\ge 3\), verifying all two-body cross anomalies \(\Delta_{\{i,j\}}\equiv 0\) cannot imply axis factorizability.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Take each \(\widehat{G_i}\) containing one non-trivial element \(a_i\), and under fixed \(\theta\) define function
\[
\log\mathfrak{M}_{\chi}(\theta):=
\begin{cases}
1, & \chi=(a_1,a_2,a_3,\chi_{4,0},\dots,\chi_{k,0}),\\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
Then any two-body section (remaining coordinates trivial) has function identically \(0\), thus \(\Delta_{\{i,j\}}\equiv 0\);
but \(\Delta_{\{1,2,3\}}(a_1,a_2,a_3;\theta)=1\neq 0\), so by Theorem \ref{thm:pom-axis-decomposability-iff} not factorizable. $\qed$
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Alignment with prime-register limit: auditable discrimination of Euler factorizability]\label{rem:pom-higher-cross-anom-euler}
Under prime-register limit framework \(\widehat{\ZZ}\cong\prod_p\ZZ_p\) (Definition \ref{def:pom-profinite}),
if \(\log\mathfrak{M}\) is viewed as constant-level payload of multi-axis (multi-prime or multi-direct-product component) juxtaposed lift,
then Theorem \ref{thm:pom-axis-decomposability-iff}'s ``all-order cross anomalies vanish''
is precisely the sufficient and necessary condition to strictify merger law to direct product (Euler type restricted product factorization) \emph{without introducing} cocycle/semidirect product extension data;
Proposition \ref{prop:pom-pairwise-cross-anom-insufficient} shows two-body audit \(\Delta^{\mathrm{sep}}\) is only necessary condition in multi-axis case.
\end{remark}
