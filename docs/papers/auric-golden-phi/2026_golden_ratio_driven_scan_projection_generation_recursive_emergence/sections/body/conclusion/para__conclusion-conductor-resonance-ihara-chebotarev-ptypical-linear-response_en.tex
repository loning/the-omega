\paragraph{Derivation closure of modular obstructions, homological classification of resonant character groups, and the Ihara--Chebotarev error dictionary: from cyclotomic factors to \texorpdfstring{$p$}{p}-typical integrality and zero-temperature linear response}
In the twisted-spectral framework for finite-state weighted graphs/synchronization kernels, ``modular obstruction/resonance'' is not a scattered empirical phenomenon but can be compressed into a finite-dimensional, integer-coefficient, computable set of homological-spectral certificates: the homological derivation \(d\) determines all exceptional moduli; resonant characters form a finite abelian group computable once via Smith normal form; on non-resonant channels there exists an explicit phase-mismatch lower bound yielding a uniform twisted spectral gap; once resonance occurs, the determinant necessarily contains a cyclotomic factor inducing finite-order rotation modes, thereby producing non-cancellable strict periodic oscillation corrections in the time domain.
In parallel, the square-root-level error in the Ihara--Chebotarev theorem is equivalent to a spectral-gap condition, while the Ihara--Mertens constant and local \(p\)-factors can all be compressed via derivative/congruence closure into auditable finite-dimensional objects; at the zero-temperature endpoint, the first-order perturbation coefficient of the Perron root further provides a scalar order orthogonal to the spectral gap: ``excitability.''
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Finite derivation principle: all modular obstructions are completely controlled by a single integer \texorpdfstring{$d$}{d}.}
  For a primitive directed graph \(G\) with integer edge weights \(w\), the greatest common divisor of the cycle-weight-sum set \(\mathcal{C}\),
  \(d=\gcd(\mathcal{C})\),
  provides a necessary and sufficient criterion for modular coboundary: there exists \(g:V\to\ZZ/q\ZZ\) such that
  \(w(e)\equiv g(\mathrm{head}(e))-g(\mathrm{tail}(e))\ (\mathrm{mod}\ q)\)
  if and only if \(q\mid d\) (Proposition~\ref{prop:gm-cycle-gcd-mod-obstruction}).
  Thus the ``infinitely many moduli resonances'' must be equivalent to a fixed homological obstruction \(d>1\), not an analytic coincidence.

  \item \textbf{Quantitative lower bound for uniform twist gap: spectral gap on non-obstruction moduli explicitly given by minimal phase mismatch.}
  If \(q\nmid d\), then the twisted adjacency operator satisfies
  \[
  \boxed{\ \rho\!\bigl(A(\chi_q)\bigr)\ \le\ \bigl(1-\kappa_G\eta(q)\bigr)\rho\ },
  \qquad
  \eta(q)=\min_{1\le t<q}\sin^2\!\Bigl(\frac{\pi t}{q}\Bigr)\asymp q^{-2}
  \]
  where \(\kappa_G>0\) depends only on the primitivity constants of the underlying graph (Theorem~\ref{thm:gm-uniform-twist-gap-from-gcd}).
  This inequality compresses the origin of ``major-arc decay/spectral suppression'' into a combination of finite-dimensional spectral-radius comparison and explicit trigonometric lower bounds.

  \item \textbf{Complete homological classification and computability of resonant character groups: resonance is not scattered but a finite abelian group.}
  The set of resonant characters
  \(\mathcal{X}_{\mathrm{res}}=\{\chi:\rho(A(\chi))=\rho(A(1))\}\)
  forms a finite abelian group isomorphic to the Pontryagin dual of the torsion part of the homological quotient:
  \[
  \boxed{\ \mathcal{X}_{\mathrm{res}}\ \cong\ \mathrm{Hom}\!\Bigl(\mathrm{tors}\bigl(H_1(G,\ZZ)/\langle[w]\rangle\bigr),\ \QQ/\ZZ\Bigr)\ }
  \]
  (Theorem~\ref{thm:gm-resonant-character-group-homology}).
  Its invariant-factor decomposition and generator character table can be output in polynomial time via the Smith normal form of integer matrices (Proposition~\ref{prop:gm-smith-compute-obstructions}), elevating ``resonance structure'' to a once-auditable discrete output.

  \item \textbf{Cyclotomic factor \texorpdfstring{$\Longleftrightarrow$}{<->} equi-radius resonance: resonance necessarily corresponds to finite-order rotation modes and induces strict periodic oscillation terms.}
  In the graph-\(\zeta\)/Ihara--Artin \(\zeta\) determinant framework, non-trivial resonance channels exist if and only if the twisted \(\zeta\) (or equivalently \(\det(I-uA(\chi))\), \(\det(I-tB_\rho(u))\)) contains a cyclotomic factor; this is equivalent to some non-trivial twist satisfying equi-radius spectral equality
  \(\rho(A(\chi))=\rho(A(1))\)
  or
  \(\rho(B_\rho(u))=\lambda_1(u)\)
  (Theorems~\ref{thm:gm-graph-zeta-cyclotomic-classification},~\ref{thm:conclusion75-ihara-cyclotomic-factor-classification}).
  Cyclotomic factors correspond to roots of unity on the unit circle, forcing eigenvalues on the spectral boundary to be finite-order rotations; the time-domain consequence is: beyond the leading term, periodic oscillation corrections of the same order as \(\lambda_1(u)^n/n\) emerge, with period equal to the order of the corresponding root of unity.

  \item \textbf{Spectral-gap equivalence in Ihara--Chebotarev: square-root-level error and Ramanujan-type spectral bound are mutually equivalent criteria.}
  For Ihara-primitive counts \(q_{n,C}(u)\) stratified by Frobenius conjugacy classes, the uniform square-root-level error
  \[
  q_{n,C}(u)=\frac{|C|}{|G|}\cdot\frac{\lambda_1(u)^n}{n}
  +O\!\left(\frac{\lambda_1(u)^{n/2}}{n}\right)
  \qquad(\text{uniformly in }C)
  \]
  is equivalent to the spectral-gap condition \(\Lambda(u)\le \lambda_1(u)^{1/2}\) (Corollary~\ref{cor:ihara-chebotarev-grh-criterion}).
  Thus the error scale itself serves as a quality discriminator for the spectral gap, without requiring prior knowledge of all twisted spectra.

  \item \textbf{Möbius--Abel formula for Ihara--Mertens constant: leading-pole residue given by a finite-dimensional determinant derivative certificate.}
  Under the Perron--Frobenius spectral-gap condition, the Ihara-caliber Abel finite-part constant \(\log\mathfrak{M}^{\mathrm{Ih}}_K(u)\) admits a Möbius--Abel fast formula isomorphic to the non-backtracking constant level, and the leading-pole residue constant is completely determined by the finite-dimensional derivative certificate
  \(
  \partial_t\det(I-tB(u))|_{t=t_\star(u)}
  \)
  (Proposition~\ref{prop:ihara-mertens-constant}).

  \item \textbf{\texorpdfstring{$p$}{p}-typical \texorpdfstring{$p$}{p}-adic integrality closure: from Dwork congruence to local \texorpdfstring{$p$}{p}-factor, automatically stable under multi-potential parameters.}
  Dwork-type congruence and the Dieudonn\'e--Dwork integrality criterion yield the \(p\)-typical local factor
  \(
  Z_p(T;u)=\exp(\sum_{k\ge 0}a_{p^k}(u)T^{p^k}/p^k)
  \)
  closed in \(1+T\ZZ_p[u][[T]]\) (Theorem~\ref{thm:witt-dwork-zp-integrality}; Lemma~\ref{lem:witt-dieudonne-dwork}).
  Simultaneously, necklace divisibility and multi-dimensional Dwork congruences hold verbatim under multi-dimensional potentials (\S\ref{app:multi-dim-witt-guardrails}), so the ``local \(p\)-factor'' structure incurs no additional audit cost upon coefficient-ring upgrade.

  \item \textbf{Algebraic rigidity of zero-temperature linear response: first-order carry density completely determined by carry-free skeleton and yields comparable scalar order.}
  If the carry-free skeleton \(A_0\) is primitive with simple Perron root \(\lambda_0\), then the spectral radius is analytic at \(u=0\) and satisfies
  \[
  \lambda_K(u)=\lambda_0+c_1u+O(u^2),
  \qquad
  c_1=\frac{\ell^{\mathsf T}A_1 r}{\ell^{\mathsf T}r},
  \qquad
  \mathbb{E}_u[\kappa]=\frac{c_1}{\lambda_0}u+O(u^2)
  \]
  (Proposition~\ref{prop:lambda-linear-response} and Corollary~\ref{cor:kappa-linear-response-order}).
  In the \(K_9/K_{13}/K_{21}\) three-kernel case study, this first-order slope yields the ``carry resistance'' order near zero temperature, and the first-order response constant for \(K_{21}\) algebraically collapses to \(c_1=\sqrt5\) (Corollary~\ref{cor:k21-c1-sqrt5}).
\end{enumerate}
