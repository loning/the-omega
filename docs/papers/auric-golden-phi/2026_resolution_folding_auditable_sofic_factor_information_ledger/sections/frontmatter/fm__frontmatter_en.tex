\begin{abstract}
We introduce a finite-window binary readout model and isolate a single normalization map, the \emph{resolution folding} $\Fold_m$, as the organizing principle that closes a fully auditable loop:
\[
\Omega_m \xrightarrow{\ \Fold_m\ } X_m \xrightarrow{\ \Phi_m\ } Y_m
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{Markov cover invariants and an information ledger identity}.
\]
The microstate space is $\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m$. The folding $\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m$ is realized by a value-preserving local rewriting system, terminates and is confluent, and hence produces a unique canonical form in the stable language $X_m$; it is idempotent, surjective, and compatible with a fold-aware restriction across resolutions.
The induced sliding block code $\Phi_m$ yields a sofic image $Y_m$ with an explicit finite labeled graph, a right-resolving presentation, and a computable interface to the right Fischer cover; from the corresponding adjacency matrix we obtain $h_{\mathrm{top}}$, Parry (PF) data, and a cover-level zeta determinant.
Finally, we prove an \emph{information ledger identity} for arbitrary $\mu\in\Delta(\Omega_m)$:
\[
H(\mu)=H(P_m\mu)+\mathbb{E}[\log d_m]-D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|Q_mP_m\mu),
\]
decomposing entropy into visible type entropy, fiber volume, and a unique residual (KL) term. This yields auditable certificates (constants, spectral radii, tail bounds) and a finite-sample verification protocol.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.}
golden mean shift; Zeckendorf normal form; local rewriting system; sliding block code; sofic shift; Fischer cover; Perron--Frobenius measure; entropy decomposition; auditable certificate.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Main contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item We construct $\Fold_m$ as a local rewriting normalizer producing unique canonical forms and prove idempotence, surjectivity, and multiscale compatibility.
  \item We derive the induced factor $\Phi_m$ and give an explicit finite labeled graph for $Y_m$, together with a right-resolving / Fischer-cover interface for computing $h_{\mathrm{top}}$, Parry data, and cover zeta determinants.
  \item We prove an entropy ledger identity that isolates the residual KL term as the unique carrier of non-auditable microstructure beyond folded types and fiber volumes.
  \item We provide auditable certificates and a reproducible pipeline interface for verifying bounds and invariants from finite data.
\end{itemize}
