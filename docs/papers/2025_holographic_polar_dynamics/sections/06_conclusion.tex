\section{Conclusion: The Geometric Renaissance}

We have presented \textbf{Omega Dynamics}, a new framework for quantum gravity rooted in Holographic Polar Arithmetic \cite{Ma2025HPA}. In this framework, the classical $r=0$ curvature endpoint is not taken as a physical spacetime point: the Omega continuation rule replaces would-be radial termination by an inversion channel built on the isotropic Einstein--Rosen throat template.

Our key findings are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Gravity as Phase Pressure:} In the continuum limit, the phase potential $\Phi$ and its associated pressure field reproduce the Newtonian sector and close to the Schwarzschild exterior under standard uniqueness assumptions \cite{Lovelock1971, MisnerThorneWheeler1973, Wald1984}.
    \item \textbf{Singular Endpoints are Avoided by Inversion:} In isotropic holographic radius $\rho$, the Schwarzschild exterior exhibits an exact inversion symmetry $\rho\mapsto\rho_h^2/\rho$ and a minimal-surface throat at $\rho=\rho_h$ \cite{EinsteinRosen1935, Kruskal1960, Wald1984}. Omega Dynamics promotes this inversion to a continuation rule, replacing coordinate endpoints by a dual boundary channel.
    \item \textbf{Information is Conserved (Model Level):} Under the modular-scan and readout assumptions, evaporation is modeled as a unitary re-encoding of infalling data into phase correlations of the outgoing channel, consistent with Page-type entanglement turnover \cite{Page1993, HaydenPreskill2007}.
\end{enumerate}

These results give a concrete mathematical template (isotropic throat + inversion continuation) for replacing endpoint pathologies by a holographic continuation channel in Omega Dynamics. A next step is to extend the continuation rule from the time-symmetric slice to a fully causal spacetime description and to connect the symbolic readout model to quantitative semiclassical observables.

\subsection*{Limitations and Open Problems}
\begin{conjecture}[Mismatch-to-Poisson closure]
\label{conj:mismatch_poisson}
Fix a microscopic scan--projection readout model as in Paper~I \cite{Ma2025HPA}. Let $\sigma_\varepsilon(\mathbf{x})$ be the coarse-grained mismatch density induced by the local orbit/discrepancy functionals (window-symmetric-difference flips and prefix-count deviations) at spatial resolution $\varepsilon$. Suppose $\sigma_\varepsilon$ admits a continuum limit $\sigma$ (in a distributional or weak sense) and that the phase-potential functional converges to the Dirichlet form. Then the continuum phase potential $\Phi$ is characterized by the Poisson equation with source $\sigma$ (Appendix~\ref{app:dirichlet_poisson}) and reproduces the Newtonian sector.\par
\end{conjecture}
\paragraph{Minimal verification criteria.}
This closure reduces to: (i) define $\sigma_\varepsilon$ from explicit microscopic readout data (Paper~I), (ii) prove a scaling/compactness statement ensuring $\sigma_\varepsilon\Rightarrow\sigma$, and (iii) identify the continuum limit of the discrete orbit-cost functional with the Dirichlet energy (Appendix~\ref{app:dirichlet_poisson}; cf.\ standard potential theory \cite{Jackson1998} and quantitative discrepancy control \cite{Denjoy1932,Koksma1935,KuipersNiederreiter1974}).

\begin{conjecture}[Causal (4D) completion of inversion]
\label{conj:causal_inversion}
There exists a Lorentzian spacetime realization of Omega Dynamics whose exterior region is isometric to Schwarzschild, whose near-horizon geometry reduces to the Rindler$\times S^2$ form (Section~\ref{sec:black_hole_anatomy}), and whose continuation across the locking surface implements the inversion rule (Axiom~\ref{ax:omega_inversion}) as a causal gluing to a boundary readout channel.\par
\end{conjecture}
\paragraph{Minimal verification criteria.}
A causal completion can be formulated as a concrete construction problem: specify a Lorentzian metric and a gluing map that (i) matches the exterior uniquely up to diffeomorphism \cite{Wald1984}, (ii) respects the classical Einstein--Rosen/Kruskal template in the time-symmetric limit \cite{EinsteinRosen1935,Kruskal1960}, and (iii) yields a well-posed exterior initial-value problem with effective boundary/readout degrees of freedom (compatible with the membrane/ER=EPR organizing pictures \cite{ThornePrice1986,SusskindMaldacena2013}).

\begin{conjecture}[Semiclassical Hawking matching from modular/KMS structure]
\label{conj:hawking_kms}
Assume the modular-scan identification (Assumption~\ref{assump:modular_scan}) and the near-horizon Rindler form (Section~\ref{sec:black_hole_anatomy}). If the reduced state on the exterior observable algebra satisfies the corresponding KMS condition with respect to the boost/modular flow (as in the Bisognano--Wichmann paradigm for wedge algebras \cite{BisognanoWichmann1975,BisognanoWichmann1976,Haag1996}), then the readout marginals exhibit Hawking--Unruh thermality at $T_H=\kappa_{\mathrm{sg}}/(2\pi)$ up to model-dependent graybody and backreaction corrections \cite{Hawking1975,Unruh1976,Wald1984}.\par
\end{conjecture}
\paragraph{Minimal verification criteria.}
To match semiclassical observables one must: (i) compute detector response/two-point functions in the readout channel and verify KMS thermality at $T_H$, (ii) include the exterior potential-barrier transmission (graybody factors) to obtain the emitted spectrum, and (iii) control backreaction as a perturbation consistent with the unitary inversion/readout channel picture \cite{Wald1984}.
