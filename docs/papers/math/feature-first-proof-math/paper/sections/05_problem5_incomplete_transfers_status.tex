\textbf{Q5-V2 Proof Manuscript (synced into this file).}

\textit{Setup.}
Let $G$ be finite and $\mathcal O$ a reduced $N_\infty$ operad. Work in
$\mathrm{Ho}(\mathrm{Sp}^G_{\mathcal O})$ with restriction $\iota_H^*$, induction $G_+\otimes_H(-)$, and geometric fixed points built via isotropy separation.

\textit{Regular $\mathcal O$-slice filtration.}
For $H\le G$ and $k\ge 0$, define regular slice cells
\[
\mathsf{Cell}(H,k)=G_+\otimes_H\Sigma^\infty_{\iota_H^*\mathcal O}(S^{k\rho_H}).
\]
Define $\tau^{\mathcal O}_{\ge n}$ as the localizing subcategory generated by all $\mathsf{Cell}(H,k)$ with $k|H|\ge n$.

\textit{Main theorem (connective case).}
For connective $E\in\mathrm{Sp}^G_{\mathcal O}$ and $n\ge 0$:
\[
E\in\tau^{\mathcal O}_{\ge n}
\Longleftrightarrow
\forall H\le G,\ \Phi^H(E)\in\mathrm{Sp}_{\ge\lceil n/|H|\rceil}.
\]

\textit{Proof architecture.}

1. Restriction lemma:
$\iota_H^*(\tau^{\mathcal O}_{\ge n})\subseteq\tau^{\iota_H^*\mathcal O}_{\ge n}$.
Use Mackey decomposition plus
$\mathrm{Res}^{K}_{L}(\rho_K)\cong\rho_L^{\oplus[K:L]}$ to keep the inequality
$k[K:L]|L|=k|K|\ge n$.

2. Induction lemma:
$G_+\otimes_H(-)$ sends
$\tau^{\iota_H^*\mathcal O}_{\ge n}$ into $\tau^{\mathcal O}_{\ge n}$ by preservation on generators and localizing closure.

3. Isotropy separation:
for $\mathcal P=$ proper subgroups, cofiber sequence
\[
E\mathcal P_+\wedge E\to E\to \widetilde E\mathcal P\wedge E.
\]

4. Proper isotropy term:
\[
E\mathcal P_+\wedge E\in\tau^{\mathcal O}_{\ge n}
\Longleftrightarrow
\forall H<G,\ \iota_H^*E\in\tau^{\iota_H^*\mathcal O}_{\ge n}.
\]
(Restriction + induction + orbit-cell hocolim decomposition.)

5. Geometric term:
for $X=\widetilde E\mathcal P\wedge E$, only $H=G$-cells detect membership; under $\Phi^G$, $S^{k\rho_G}$ maps to $S^k$ with threshold $k\ge\lceil n/|G|\rceil$; hence
\[
\widetilde E\mathcal P\wedge E\in\tau^{\mathcal O}_{\ge n}
\Longleftrightarrow
\Phi^G(E)\in\mathrm{Sp}_{\ge\lceil n/|G|\rceil}.
\]

6. Induction on $|G|$ for $(2)\Rightarrow(1)$:
apply subgroup hypothesis to each proper $H$, get the proper-isotropy term in $\tau_{\ge n}$, combine with geometric term and closure under extensions.
