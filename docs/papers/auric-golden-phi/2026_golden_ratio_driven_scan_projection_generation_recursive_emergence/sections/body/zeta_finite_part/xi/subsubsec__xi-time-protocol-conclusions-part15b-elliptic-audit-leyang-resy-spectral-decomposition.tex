\begin{conclusion}[Elimination image of Lee--Yang cubic ramification in the spectral variable: cubic--sextic decomposition of \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{Res}_{y}(\Pi,P_{\mathrm{LY}})$}{Res\_y(Pi,P\_LY)}]\label{con:xi-terminal-zm-leyang-resy-spectral-decomposition}
Let the Lee--Yang cubic factor be
\[
P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y):=256y^{3}+411y^{2}+165y+32,
\]
and keep the quartic spectral equation \(\Pi(\lambda,y)=0\) from Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-quadratic-discriminant}.
Then in \(\QQ[\lambda]\), one has the exact factorization
\[
\boxed{\;
\mathrm{Res}_{y}\bigl(\Pi(\lambda,y),P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)\bigr)
=(16\lambda^{3}-9\lambda^{2}+1)^{2}\,
\bigl(256\lambda^{6}-480\lambda^{5}+201\lambda^{4}+750\lambda^{3}-921\lambda^{2}-78\lambda+704\bigr)\;}
\]
Hence the elimination image in the spectral variable \(\lambda\) of the three branch values from \(P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)=0\) splits into:
a cubic layer with squared multiplicity (corresponding to the double-root spectral branch), and a sextic layer (corresponding to the composite image of the remaining simple-root spectral branches).
\par
More finely, in the quotient ring \(\QQ[y,\lambda]/(P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y))\), the greatest common divisor of \(\Pi(\lambda,y)\) and \(\partial_{\lambda}\Pi(\lambda,y)\) is linear.
Equivalently, on the Lee--Yang ramification locus, the double-root spectral branch satisfies the linear rigidity relation
\[
\boxed{\;
279\lambda+512y^{2}+518y+5=0\quad(\mathrm{mod}\ P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y))\;}
\]
so this branch is uniquely determined by the rational function \(\lambda=-(512y^{2}+518y+5)/279\).
Eliminating \(y\) then exactly recovers the cubic certificate \(16\lambda^{3}-9\lambda^{2}+1=0\) (see the nontrivial branch-point \(x\)-coordinate factor in Conclusion \ref{con:xi-terminal-zm-elliptic-ramification-portrait}).
\end{conclusion}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Taking the resultant in \(y\) of \(\Pi(\lambda,y)\) and \(P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)\), and then factoring in \(\QQ[\lambda]\), yields the displayed cubic--sextic decomposition.
For the double-root spectral branch, solve jointly
\(
\Pi(\lambda,y)=0
\)
and
\(
\partial_{\lambda}\Pi(\lambda,y)=0
\)
then perform Gröbner elimination under the constraint \(P_{\mathrm{LY}}(y)=0\), obtaining the linear eliminant
\(
279\lambda+512y^{2}+518y+5
\);
this linear form uniquely characterizes the common root in the cubic quotient ring \(\QQ[y]/(P_{\mathrm{LY}})\), hence establishing linear rigidity of the double-root spectral branch.
Reproducibility audit script: \texttt{scripts/exp\_fold\_zm\_elliptic\_leyang\_resy\_spectral\_decomposition\_audit.py}. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}
