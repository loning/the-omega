\section{Golden-Branch Folding: Multi-Scale Projection from Microstates to Stable Types}\label{sec:folding}

This section constructs and analyzes the resolution-folding operator \(\Fold_m:\Omega_m\to X_m\), where \(\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m\) is the length-\(m\) window microstate space and \(X_m\subseteq\Omega_m\) is the golden-mean constrained stable-syntax space (forbidding adjacent \(11\)). The objective of folding is to compress exponentially growing microstate spaces into an auditable stable-type layer while preserving cross-resolution consistency, so that subsequent statistical auditing, addressing, and arithmetic structures close on a common normal-form representation.\par
The subsections below present, in order: counting structure for Fibonacci/stable syntax; Zeckendorf normal forms and well-definedness of folding; cross-resolution compatibility (inverse systems and inverse limits) with multi-scale residual interfaces; and finite-graph representation plus computable implementation of the sequence-level sliding-block code \(\Phi_m\) induced by \(\Fold_m\).
