\begin{abstract}
This paper constructs and audits a closed formal deduction chain: using a rank-6 hyperspace cut-and-project model class as the geometric closure representation and parameterizing the observer via internal phase and readout protocol parameters. Realizable sampling under finite resources is characterized through rotation of a one-dimensional factor on a torus and a hierarchy of rational approximations; binary partition readout induces a binary sequence that generates Sturmian processes in the irrational limit, with verifiable invariants (complexity, frequency, balance, etc.) providing symbolic certificates. To achieve stable representation, we introduce the Zeckendorf legal language $X_m^{\mathrm Z}$ and the stabilization kernel $\mathrm{Fold}_m$; under fixed bandwidth $m=6$ in the main body, $\mathrm{Fold}_6:\Omega_6\twoheadrightarrow X_6^{\mathrm Z}$ provides the minimal enumerable verification platform, with the preimage fiber $\mathcal{F}_6$ objectifying indistinguishable degrees of freedom. The arrow of time is rigorously defined as the exponential contraction rate of the posterior cylinder set measure, with limits characterized by SMB/KS theory; the ``future'' is objectified via conditional uncertainty (such as conditional entropy). We further provide an object-layer construction of fixed-bandwidth recursive readout (partition refinement) and characterize the fiber-prioritized discovery process and reproducible numerical protocol via Levi graphs.
\end{abstract}
