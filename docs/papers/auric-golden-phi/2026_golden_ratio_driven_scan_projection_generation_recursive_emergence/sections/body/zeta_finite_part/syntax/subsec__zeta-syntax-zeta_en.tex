\subsection{Dynamical \texorpdfstring{$\zeta$}{zeta} functions (stable syntax layer)}\label{subsec:zeta-syntax-zeta}

At the stable syntax layer, this paper uses exclusively the subshift of finite type (SFT) caliber: given adjacency matrix $A$, the dynamical $\zeta$ satisfies
\[
\zeta_A(z)=\det(I-zA)^{-1}
=\exp\!\left(\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{\Tr(A^n)}{n}z^n\right)
\]
(see \cite{LindMarcus1995}). For the golden mean constraint
\(
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
\)
one obtains
\[
\zeta(z)=\frac{1}{1-z-z^2},
\]
whose dominant pole is $z_\star=\varphi^{-1}$.

\begin{remark}[Discrepancy object: the degeneracy-$\zeta$ simultaneously aligns the trace side and the primitive side]\label{rem:degeneracy-zeta-bridge}
To compress the discrepancy between the ``unconstrained'' (full $2$-shift) and the ``golden stable constraint'' into a single computable object, write
\[
\zeta_{\mathrm{full}}(z)=\frac{1}{1-2z},
\qquad
\zeta(z)=\frac{1}{1-z-z^2},
\]
and define the discrepancy ratio (degeneracy-$\zeta$) as
\[
\zeta_{\mathrm{deg}}(z):=\frac{\zeta_{\mathrm{full}}(z)}{\zeta(z)}
=\frac{1-z-z^2}{1-2z}.
\]
Its logarithmic coefficients directly package the ``trace-side discrepancy'':
\[
\log \zeta_{\mathrm{deg}}(z)
=\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{2^n-\Tr(A^n)}{n}z^n
\]
In the periodic closure (cyclic/trace) caliber, the corresponding ``time-side degeneracy ratio'' can be taken as
\[
\delta_n^{\mathrm{cyc}}:=\frac{2^n}{\Tr(A^n)},
\]
whose exponential rate is given by the spectral radius ratio \(2/\varphi\).

Furthermore: the same discrepancy object also determines the ``prime-like (primitive periodic orbit) side discrepancy''. Writing the trace sequences as
\(
a_n^{\mathrm{full}}:=2^n,\ a_n^{\mathrm{gold}}:=\Tr(A^n)
\), the primitive counts are given by Witt/M\"obius inversion (Appendix~\S\ref{app:witt-bridge-package}):
\[
p_n=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)\,a_{n/d}.
\]
The discrepancy primitive counts
\(
p_n^{\mathrm{deg}}:=p_n^{\mathrm{full}}-p_n^{\mathrm{gold}}
\)
are therefore extracted from \(\{2^n-\Tr(A^n)\}\) by the same M\"obius/Witt transform. In other words, \(\zeta_{\mathrm{deg}}\) as a single object outputs both the trace-side discrepancy (periodic points/closed paths) and the primitive-side discrepancy (``prime-like closed loops''), thereby functorializing the ``trace \(\Rightarrow\) prime-like'' bridge into an auditable discrepancy encapsulation.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Relative object: the carry-excess primitive spectrum of \texorpdfstring{$\zeta_B/\zeta_A$}{zetaB/zetaA} and the relative finite-part shift]\label{rem:relative-zeta-sync-vs-grammar}
This paper simultaneously possesses \(\zeta\)-interfaces of two types of SFT: one is the golden mean adjacency matrix \(A\) at the stable syntax layer (above), and the other is the synchronization kernel adjacency matrix \(B\) induced by the online normalization mechanism (\S\ref{subsec:zeta-online-kernel} and Appendix~\ref{app:sync-kernel-basic}).
Both share the same chain of auditable identities:
\[
\zeta_M(z)=\det(I-zM)^{-1}
=\exp\!\left(\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{\Tr(M^n)}{n}z^n\right),
\qquad
a_n(M)=\Tr(M^n),
\]
and by Witt/M\"obius inversion the periodic trace sequence \(a_n\) is strictly extracted into primitive orbit counts \(p_n\) (counted by orbits):
\[
p_n(M)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)\,a_{n/d}(M).
\]

\paragraph{Relative ghost (trace) sequence and strict multiplicative factorization}
Define the ``mechanism exceeding syntax'' relative trace sequence (relative ghost coordinates)
\[
r_n:=a_n(B)-a_n(A)=\Tr(B^n)-\Tr(A^n).
\]
and define the relative \(\zeta\) via the same exponential/Fredholm caliber
\[
\zeta_{B/A}(z):=\exp\!\left(\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{r_n}{n}z^n\right).
\]
Since the trace in the exponent is linear, one immediately obtains the strict identity (not an approximation, not an analogy)
$$
\boxed{\ \zeta_B(z)=\zeta_A(z)\cdot \zeta_{B/A}(z)\ }.
$$
This yields a strict ``space (syntax)/time (mechanism)'' commutative encapsulation: the synchronization kernel's \(\zeta_B\) factorizes as the product of the stable syntax's \(\zeta_A\) and the pure mechanism excess term \(\zeta_{B/A}\).

\paragraph{Carry-excess prime-like: the relative primitive spectrum}
Since M\"obius inversion on trace sequences is linear, the relative primitive counts
\[
q_n:=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)\,r_{n/d}
\]
satisfy the strict difference identity
$$
\boxed{\ q_n=p_n(B)-p_n(A)\ }.
$$
Therefore the relative \(\zeta\) automatically possesses an Euler product (prime-like side):
$$
\boxed{\ \zeta_{B/A}(z)=\prod_{n\ge 1}(1-z^n)^{-q_n}\ }.
$$
Here \((q_n)\) can be interpreted as ``the primitive part of mechanism closed loops not accountable by syntax closed loops'' (carry-excess primes).
