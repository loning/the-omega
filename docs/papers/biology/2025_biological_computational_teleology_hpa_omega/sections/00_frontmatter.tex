\begin{abstract}
Life is often described as an emergent far-from-equilibrium structure sustained by chemical dissipation. In the HPA--$\Omega$ program, we adopt a strict layer discipline: the ontic layer is a global unitary scan, while irreversibility and thermodynamic entropy arise operationally from finite-resolution scan--projection readout \cite{Ma2025HPA,Ma2025FoP,Ma2025HPOT,Ma2025HPT}. This paper proposes a computable and auditable definition of life in that interface language:
\textbf{life is a predictive active error-correction (AEC) system}. Its core function is to use information acquisition, internal modeling, and feedback control to reduce its own \emph{phase-friction} entropy production (relative to a passive baseline) by exporting dissipation to external waste channels.

In arithmetic statistical mechanics (ASM) \cite{Ma2025HPT}, phase friction over a length-$N$ window is certified by star discrepancy $D_N^\ast$ of the induced phase-point set, with accumulated mismatch $E_N := N D_N^\ast$ and the phase-friction entropy certificate $S_{\mathrm{pf}}(N) := \kB E_N$. A geometric Landauer principle \cite{Landauer1961,Bennett1973} together with information-thermodynamic bounds \cite{SagawaUeda2010,ParrondoHorowitzSagawa2015} yields necessary survival inequalities:
\(
\dot F_{\mathrm{pred}}\le \kB T_c\, \dot I_{\mathrm{pred}}
\)
and
\(
\dot F_{\mathrm{pred}}>\dot W_{\mathrm{diss}},
\)
hence
\(
\dot I_{\mathrm{pred}}>\dot W_{\mathrm{diss}}/(\kB T_c).
\)
We define predictive efficiency $\eta_{\mathrm{pred}} := \dot F_{\mathrm{pred}}/\dot W_{\mathrm{diss}}$ and recast genetic coding, homeostasis, and evolution as multi-scale strategies that optimize $\eta_{\mathrm{pred}}$ under readout and architectural constraints. Finally, we propose a falsifiable control-law hypothesis for biological rhythms: to resist low-order phase locking, adaptive coupling ratios should be biased toward badly-approximable irrational numbers, with the golden branch $\varphi^{-1}$ as the Hurwitz extremal candidate \cite{Cassels1957,Strogatz1994,PikovskyRosenblumKurths2001}.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords}: HPA--$\Omega$; arithmetic statistical mechanics; phase friction; active error correction; reverse compilation; predictive information rate; geometric Landauer principle; coupled oscillators; golden ratio; computational teleology.
