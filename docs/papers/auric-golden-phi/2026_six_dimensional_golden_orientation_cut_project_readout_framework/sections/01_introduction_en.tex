\section{Introduction and Logical Audit}

\subsection{Research Objectives}

The objective of this paper is, under strictly stratified premises, to provide a computable, verifiable, and substitutable formal framework such that the following three classes of propositions obtain falsifiable interfaces:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Generation of three-dimensional phenomenal structure} can be obtained from high-dimensional periodic objects via cut--projection and finite-resolution readout;
  \item \textbf{Coherent superposition and interference fringes} can be reduced to intensity reconstruction from the readout kernel and multi-source projection superposition;
  \item \textbf{Temporal arrow} can be defined without postulating an objective temporal flow, as monotonic refinement and information rate contraction in the measurement algebra (or posterior cylinder sets) with respect to protocol time.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Stratification Rules}

To prevent interpretive language from reversing back into proof premises, this paper divides statements into four categories:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Definitions (D):} introduction of objects, mappings, and structures;
  \item \textbf{Theorems/Propositions (T):} conclusions provable under given definitions and standard mathematical facts;
  \item \textbf{Model Assumptions (H):} replaceable structural assumptions for bridging the geometric and readout layers;
  \item \textbf{Semantic Mappings (I):} interface descriptions mapping object-layer or protocol-layer quantities to physical semantics, not entering the theorem closure.
\end{itemize}
The core logical chain of the entire text depends only on (D) and (T), with required (H) explicitly marked.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Provide a unified definition chain of six-dimensional lattice--three-dimensional model set--finite-kernel readout, and give the structural source of coherent superposition at the intensity level (Sections \ref{sec:cut-project-readout}--\ref{sec:cut-project-readout}).
  \item Formalize the anti-resonance character of ``golden-branch orientation'' using Diophantine conditions, and establish equidistribution and recurrence scale templates for irrational scanning (Section \ref{sec:golden-branch}).
  \item Construct binary mechanical word readout at the symbolic level, prove its Sturmian complexity invariant, and obtain Fibonacci-type output at the golden branch (Section \ref{sec:symbolic}).
  \item Introduce $\Fold_m$ as Zeckendorf stabilization kernel, define time fibers and closure fixed-point semantics, and provide an auditable definition of information-theoretic temporal arrow (Sections \ref{sec:symbolic}--\ref{sec:info-time}).
  \item Provide falsifiable predictions and statistical verification pipelines, emphasizing control null models and identifiability (Section \ref{sec:falsifiable} and Appendix).
\end{enumerate}
