\section{Appendix A: Notation and Hierarchical Audit Table}
\label{app:audit-table}

\subsection{Hierarchical Convention}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Definition:} Introduction of objects, maps, and quantities.
  \item \textbf{Provable theorem:} Deducible proof under given definitions and explicit assumptions.
  \item \textbf{Model class assumption:} Choice of concrete realization (such as golden field, scale compatibility diagram) to obtain stronger structure or computability.
  \item \textbf{Interpretation-layer proposition:} Mapping formal objects to physical or philosophical semantics, not entering the theorem chain.
\end{itemize}
