
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a self-contained finite-resolution model of \emph{resolution folding} on a $64$-state local readout space.
The central mathematical outputs are:
\begin{itemize}
\item the $\varphi$-stable sector of $\mathcal{H}_6=\ell^2(\{0,1\}^6)$ has dimension $21$ (the golden mean admissible words);
\item the $\pi$-channel provides a canonical internal split $21=18\oplus 3$ into cyclically admissible states and boundary states;
\item the $\e$-channel is expressed by the Artin--Mazur determinant identity $\zeta(z)=1/\det(I-zA)=1/(1-z-z^2)$ and a spectral (Abel-type) normalization that places the dominant pole at the unit-circle boundary in the normalized variable;
\item a computable surjection $\mathrm{Fold}_6:\{0,\dots,63\}\twoheadrightarrow X_6$ realizes a concrete $64\to 21$ folding via Zeckendorf normalization and window truncation, with explicit preimage sets and degeneracy histogram (Theorem~\ref{thm:fold6_preimages}; Appendix~\ref{app:fold6_table});
\item beyond the base case, $\mathrm{Fold}_m$ admits a general preimage characterization and quantitative degeneracy control, including explicit bounds and observable scaling of the mean degeneracy (Section~\ref{sec:fold_map}).
\end{itemize}

The model intentionally separates what is proven at the minimal window length from what becomes structurally nontrivial only at higher resolution:
at window length $6$, $\varphi$-legality and $\e$-stability coincide as local constraints, while the $\pi$-channel refines the stable sector.
Higher-resolution generalizations (longer windows, weighted transfer operators, and infinite-state extensions) provide a natural arena where the three channels may become genuinely independent.
On the geometric side, the dihedral layout family acts by exact symmetries on Hilbert addressing, so adjacency-based statistics are rigid under layout changes (Section~\ref{sec:state_space_addressing}); on the syntactic side, the Zeckendorf recursion admits an explicit ``uplift'' decomposition at the level of languages and $\ell^2$ spaces (Section~\ref{sec:resolution_recursion}).

\paragraph{Selected open directions.}
The remaining nontrivial questions are structural rather than computational:
\begin{itemize}
\item realize a genuinely natural multi-channel decomposition compatible with recursion and layout symmetries (Definition~\ref{def:natural_decomp_interface});
\item formulate and analyze higher-resolution regimes where analytic stability is not equivalent to a single forbidden word, beyond finite-state rational zeta functions;
\item treat external mapping templates (Section~\ref{sec:applications}) as optimization/identifiability problems over finite encoding classes.
\end{itemize}
